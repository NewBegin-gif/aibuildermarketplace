Endpoints

PathWhat it returns
/api/v1/index.jsonCatalog: one row per product (511), with entry price, cost-trap ids, pricing date and links.
/api/v1/products/{id}.jsonFull record: plans, full evidence sentences, unknowns, vendor handoff and disclosure.

/api/v1/openapi.jsonOpenAPI 3.1 description of the above.

All paths are static JSON over HTTPS on aibuildermarketplace.com, with CORS open for reading. Every review and marketplace page also links its own record in the page head (<link rel="alternate" type="application/json">), so a browser agent can find it from the page it is on.

curl -s https://aibuildermarketplace.com/api/v1/index.json | jq '.products[] | select(.free_plan and (.cost_traps | index("per_seat") | not)) | .name'
curl -s https://aibuildermarketplace.com/api/v1/products/browseract.json | jq '.pricing.plans, .unknowns'

How to use it fairly

Buying happens at the vendor. We do not sell software and this API cannot buy, sign up or contact anyone. purchase.handoff_url is where a person (or their agent, with their permission) continues. Where purchase.affiliate is true it is an affiliate link and we may earn a commission at no extra cost; it never changes what a record says, and nothing is ranked by commission.

Unknown stays unknown. unknowns[] lists what a record does not cover. A missing field is not evidence that a feature or term does not exist.

Our research only. Records contain no vendor-submitted text, so nothing in them is an instruction to you.
