Assembly alternatives

Assembly is a client portal with a CRM behind it, aimed at firms that onboard and then keep working with the same clients — accountants, agencies, consultancies. The alternatives below do that same job. Every price on this page was read off the vendor's own pricing page on 7 August 2026, and where a vendor does not print a number, this page says so instead of guessing.

Where we stand. Assembly is our affiliate partner and the link to it pays us if you sign up. HoneyBook, Dubsado and SuiteDash are not partners of ours — those links are plain links and we earn nothing whether you sign up or not. We have included them because leaving them out would make this page useless.

Assembly our partner

Pricing: $39 / $149 / $399 per month on yearly billing, Enterprise from $2,000. Monthly billing is $59 / $189 / $499 / $2,400.

Starter carries the whole product — CRM, branded portal, messaging, invoices, proposals, contracts, tasks and forms — but only 1 internal user, 50 clients and 100 automation tasks a month. What moves you up a tier is almost always a count, not a missing feature. Extra internal users cost $39 each on Professional and $59 each on Advanced, so the pricier plan charges more per colleague.

HoneyBook we earn nothing

Pricing: $29 / $49 / $109 per month on yearly billing. Monthly is $29 / $59 / $129.

The cheapest way into this category if you are one person. Starter at $29 gives unlimited clients and projects with a client portal; Essentials at $49 adds automations and a scheduler but caps you at 2 team members, and only Premium at $109 unlocks unlimited team. Watch the payment cut: 2.7% + 10¢ on cards and 1.5% on bank transfers, which on real invoice volume dwarfs the subscription. 30-day trial, no card.

Dubsado we earn nothing

Pricing: $335 or $525 per year. No monthly price is printed on their pricing page.

Priced per year rather than per seat, and three users are included before you pay more — then $25/month for 4-10 users, $45 for 11-20, $60 for 21-30. The catch is in the cheaper tier: Starter has no automated workflows, no scheduling and no Zapier, which is most of why people buy this category. Extra brands are $10/month each. 21-day trial, no card.

SuiteDash we earn nothing

Pricing: From $19/month. The prices of the two higher tiers are not printed on their pricing page.

The only one here that does not charge per user at all — unlimited staff, contacts and portals on every tier, with storage as the ladder (100GB, 500GB, 2TB). That makes it the obvious shortlist entry for a firm with many colleagues and a thin budget. But you cannot budget past the entry tier from the public page, because THRIVE and PINNACLE carry no printed price. 14-day trial, no card.

What we could not verify

Copilot is the closest competitor to Assembly by product, and it belongs on this list. Its pricing page returns a 403 to us, so we have no figure we can stand behind and have left it off rather than repeat a number from a directory. SuiteDash publishes only its entry price; its two higher tiers carry no public figure. Neither gap is a judgement about the product — it is a gap in what we could read for ourselves.

How to choose between them

Team size decides this more than features do. One person: HoneyBook at $29 is the cheapest real entry. A small team where three seats is enough: Dubsado at $335 a year works out lower than anything billed per seat. Many colleagues: SuiteDash, because it is the only one that does not charge per user at all. Assembly earns its place when you need the client-facing side to be properly yours — a custom domain and email domain on Professional, full white-labelling and HIPAA with a BAA on Advanced.

One warning that applies to all four: the subscription is rarely the biggest number. Payment processing on HoneyBook is 2.7% + 10¢ per card transaction, and per-seat add-ons on Assembly and Dubsado climb quickly. Price the tool against your actual invoice volume and headcount, not against the sticker.
