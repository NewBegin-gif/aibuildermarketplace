What is the catch with AltText.ai?

The headline ‘5¢ per image’ only holds for a plain JPG in one language. The category caveat an honest review owes you: models describe what is visibly in a photo well, but alt text is a judgement call — whether an image is decorative or informative, and what your brand actually calls the thing in it, is context the model doesn’t have.
