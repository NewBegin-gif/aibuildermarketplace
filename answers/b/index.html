At VIP 0 (non-VIP): spot 0.1000% maker and 0.1000% taker; perpetuals and futures 0.0200% maker and 0.0550% taker; options 0.0200% maker and 0.0300% taker. The VIP ladder cuts those toward zero — at Supreme VIP spot is 0.0300% maker and 0.0450% taker, derivatives 0.0000% maker and 0.0300% taker, options 0.0050% and 0.0150%. Note that the top derivatives maker rate is zero rather than a rebate: we found no negative maker fee in the published table. That base table is not the only one, and the others start higher: fiat-crypto spot pairs cost 0.2000% taker and 0.1500% maker under $100,000 of 30-day volume, the Spot Adventure Zone and xStocks 0.2000% both ways at VIP 0, and Pre-Market Perpetuals and the Innovation Zone 0.0400% maker and 0.1000% taker — so a new account trading the newest listings pays about double the headline rate. Your VIP level is set by your asset balance, your 30-day average net borrowing or your trading volume over the past 30 days, whichever qualifies you higher, and it refreshes daily at 07:00 UTC. Bybit adds that actual rates can vary by region and points you to your own My Fee Rate page. Always verify the live price.

What is the catch with Bybit?

Derivatives are the draw and the danger: leverage cuts both ways, and funding rates on perpetuals are a real recurring cost the fee table doesn't show. Regulation-wise, check what Bybit may offer in YOUR jurisdiction before funding an account — availability and product range differ per country, and that's the part most fee comparisons skip.
