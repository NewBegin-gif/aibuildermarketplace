Bouncer

Is Bouncer worth it?

Bouncer is genuinely best for email marketers and outbound teams that clean lists regularly and want accurate verification with fair credit mechanics (no expiry, no double-charging). Skip it if you send rarely to a stable opt-in list (occasional pay-as-you-go beats any subscription), or your problem is content/reputation rather than list hygiene.

From the Bouncer review — hand-researched, sources on the page.
How much does Bouncer cost?

Pay-as-you-go credits that never expire (2026): from ~$8 per 1,000 verifications down to ~$2/1,000 at millions-volume; monthly plans from ~$50/mo (10k) for recurring needs. 100 free credits to test; duplicates and 'unknown' results aren't charged. Always verify the live price.

From the Bouncer review — hand-researched, sources on the page.
What is the catch with Bouncer?

Not charging for unknowns and duplicates is genuinely fair — many rivals do. The honest framing for any verifier: it removes invalid addresses, which protects deliverability, but it can't make a cold list warm or a purchased list legitimate. Verify before every big send (lists decay measurably per month), and treat 'accept-all' domain results as the gamble they are, not as verified.

What is the catch with Bread & Butter?

Two meters to watch: it prices per WEBSITE (multi-site setups multiply the $49) and the AI features run on compute-credit blocks that stack as usage grows.
