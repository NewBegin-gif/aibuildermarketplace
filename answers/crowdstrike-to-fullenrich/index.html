CrowdStrike

Is CrowdStrike worth it?

CrowdStrike is genuinely best for small and mid-size companies that want a serious endpoint agent without running a security team — Falcon Go is genuinely priced for businesses under 100 devices. Skip it if you are under ten devices and already run a maintained operating system with its built-in protection, because at that size the management overhead outweighs the gain; also skip Go if you actually need EDR, since that starts a tier up.

Falcon Pro $14.99 monthly or $99.99 annually per device. Falcon Enterprise $19.99 per device per month. Larger tiers and managed services are quote-only. A free trial is offered on every tier. Always verify the live price.

From the CrowdStrike review — hand-researched, sources on the page.
What is the catch with CrowdStrike?

Two numbers decide whether this is for you, and neither is the headline. The first is the 100-device ceiling on Falcon Go: it is not a soft limit you grow through, it is the point where you change product. The second is the annual rate, which is worth doing the division on — $59.99 a year against $7.99 a month is $95.88, so annual saves 37%, one of the larger annual discounts in security software. Beyond that, be honest with yourself about which tier you are comparing: Go is antivirus with a good agent, and the endpoint detection and response that CrowdStrike is famous for starts at Pro. Comparing Go against a full EDR product from a competitor is comparing the wrong things.
