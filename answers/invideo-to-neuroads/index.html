InVideo

Is InVideo worth it?

InVideo is genuinely best for marketing teams and creators who want to turn a prompt or script into a publish-ready first-draft video fast, using templates, AI voiceover and stock. Skip it if you need frame-level creative control, long-form or highly bespoke production, or your usage would blow past the monthly credit allowance.

Four paid tiers plus a free plan, read on invideo.io/pricing on 5 September 2026. On annual billing: Plus $17 a month (750 AI credits a year), Max $85 a month (3,900 credits), Generative $170 a month (8,000 credits) and Elite $900 a month (42,500 credits); month-to-month the same tiers are $20, $100, $200 and $1,000. From the InVideo review — hand-researched, sources on the page.
What is the catch with InVideo?

The honest catch is the credit model — each plan is a fixed monthly credit allowance that different models consume at different rates, and unused credits do not roll over, so a heavy month can exhaust an allowance long before it renews while a slow month simply wastes what you paid for. There is also no free way in any more: on 31 August 2026 the pricing page published no free tier at all, so the cheapest route to trying it is the Plus plan at $20 a month. AI video is fast but generic by default: templated output needs real editing and brand direction to not look like everyone else's. Treat it as a first-draft engine, not a finished-studio replacement.
