Kartra

Is Kartra worth it?

Kartra is genuinely best for creators and coaches who want funnels, email, checkout, memberships and scheduling under one roof instead of five subscriptions. Skip it if you only need one of those pieces — a dedicated tool does that piece better and cheaper than an all-in-one.

From the Kartra review — hand-researched, sources on the page.
How much does Kartra cost?

(re-verified 2026-09-05 on their own pricing page) Four tiers, monthly against yearly: Essentials $59 / $52, Starter $119 / $99, Growth $229 / $189 and Professional $549 / $429, with yearly billing advertised as saving up to 22% and a 14-day trial. The tier names moved since our August reading — the $119 plan is now called Starter and the $229 plan Growth — while every amount stayed the same. The line that changes the arithmetic and is missing from most summaries: Essentials charges a 5% transaction fee on your sales, and the tiers above it charge 0%. On $10,000 a month of sales that fee is $500, which makes the $60 step from Essentials to Starter trivially worth taking. Contacts are capped at 500, 2,500, 12,500 and 25,000 across the four tiers; Essentials sends 10,000 emails and texts a month against unlimited above it and includes 1 custom domain. Kartra never blocks new leads: it upgrades you automatically to the tier that fits and charges a mid-cycle reconciliation fee for the move — two answers above that one, the same FAQ says a Kartra subscription is flat-rate and there are never any extra fees. Always verify the live price.

What is the catch with Kartra?

If your core need is polished course delivery, Kajabi's creator features are stronger; Kartra wins on all-in-one breadth, not course depth.

What is the catch with Koongo?

The headline price is a base, not a bill. Each paid plan includes 1,000 items, 5 feeds, two marketplace wizards and 100 orders a month, and the listing says plainly that surcharges apply for extras used across all four of those dimensions. For a store with a real catalogue selling on several European marketplaces, every one of those limits is one you pass in week one. Price your own numbers on koongo.com's surcharge table before you take $39 seriously. The two pricing pages also do not quote the same bill. The App Store lists $39, $79 and $119 and bills in USD. koongo.com lists €35, €69 and €99 and states that all prices exclude VAT. Depending on where you trade and how you are registered, those are meaningfully different amounts for the same plan, and nothing on either page reconciles them. Then the record, which is the most useful thing on this listing. 4.4 from 98 reviews is the lowest average in this category among the apps Shopify shows beside it, and the breakdown explains why: 84 five-star ratings sit alongside six one-star ones. Neither is disqualifying on its own. Together they describe an app whose complexity is real and whose marketplace integrations sometimes need support to untangle — which is consistent with the vendor's own listing offering personal onboarding for complex setups. One more line to read before you install: the listing states the app views sensitive customer data as well as device and activity data, and can view and edit products, orders and custom data. Marketplace order sync needs customer details to work, so that is expected, but it makes this a processor of personal data on your behalf rather than a feed formatter.
