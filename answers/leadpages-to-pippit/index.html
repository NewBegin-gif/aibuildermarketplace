What is the catch with Livestorm?

This is a bet on attendance that you place a year in advance. A webinar that does better than expected runs you out; one nobody attends still cost you nothing back. The page will not show you a total: the slider moves but the price does not appear until you engage. Before you commit, count sessions times expected attendees for the whole year, and ask what happens to unused credits at the twelve-month mark, because the page does not say.
