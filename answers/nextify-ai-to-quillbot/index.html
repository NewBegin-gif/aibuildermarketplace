What is the catch with Nextify.ai?

The plans are sold in videos and images; the meter runs in credits per second, and the two do not reconcile. Every tier's headline count works out at 20 credits a video and 2 credits an image — 1,000 credits become “up to 50 videos” and “up to 500 images”, 3,000 become 150 and 1,500, 8,000 become 400 and 4,000. The 20-credit figure is reachable, but only on the 5-credit-a-second model at four seconds. The image claim has no such escape hatch: the cheapest image model in the table is 7 credits and the pre-selected one is 10, so nothing in the product costs the 2 credits those counts assume, and Starter's 500 images is nearer 100.

The sticker price buys a credit allowance, not finished video, and the two are easy to confuse.
