What is the catch with Tapita AI SEO Blog Builder?

Publishing volume is the trap this tool makes cheap. It generates posts quickly and schedules them daily or weekly in bulk. Used the other way round, as a drafting and structuring tool for posts you actually edit, it is sensible and cheap. Two practical points on the price: the $9.99 headline holds only on Shopify Basic, and the surcharge takes it to $69.99 a month on Shopify Plus, which is the sort of detail that belongs in a headline rather than a footnote. And the listing never says how many of the 250 monthly credits a single post costs, so the allowance cannot be planned against until you ask.
