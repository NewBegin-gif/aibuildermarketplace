VEA

Is VEA worth it?

VEA is genuinely best for hands-on entrepreneurs and small-business owners buried in operational detail who want AI that learns their specific business and helps surface what's actually holding it back — explicitly not enterprise software. Skip it if you run a larger organization with established teams and processes (VEA itself positions against that), or you need a task-specific tool rather than a business-level advisor.

How much does VEA cost?

The three-tier ladder we read in August ($31.95 / $24.95, $49.95 / $39.95, $77.95 / $59.95) was replaced between our 2 August and 1 September 2026 captures: the two lower tiers were rounded to whole dollars, the old top tier at $77.95 is gone, the new third tier costs roughly double it, and a fourth tier sits above that. The page's marketing line still says 'Starting at $24.95 per user per month', a figure no longer on the plan cards. Annual billing saves roughly 17% to 20%. Always verify the live price.

What is the catch with VEA?

The pitch is unusually bold: an AI that learns your business the way a great employee would, finds the constraint keeping you stuck, and helps you solve it. That ambition is also the honest caveat — it's a young product making a big promise, its price ladder was rebuilt in August 2026 with a top tier at $597 per user a month, and independent review coverage is still thin. Judge it in the trial on one measurable question: did it identify a constraint you recognize as painfully real? If yes, it earns the next month. If its advice stays generic — the thing its own positioning promises to avoid — walk.

What is the catch with Webflow?

Three separate bills, and the pricing page shows them on separate tabs. A site plan is what puts your site online: $15 a month for a static site, $25 with the CMS. A workspace plan is what lets your team work on it: $19 or $49 a month. And seats are charged on top of the workspace plan at $39 a month each for anyone who can actually design. And Optimize, the conversion tooling Webflow leads with in its marketing, starts at $299 a month on top of everything else and is priced by page views. Price your team, your site and your add-ons together, because no single tab on that page shows you a total.
