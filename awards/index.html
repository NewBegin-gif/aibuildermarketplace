The Honest Software Awards 2026

Most software awards are bought. These are earned the boring way: we hand-research the pricing of every tool we cover (455 and counting), flag every cost-trap with evidence — and once a year, we celebrate the vendors who do pricing right. 14 winners, 4 categories, and the receipt shown under every single award.

How winners are chosen. No submissions, no entry fees, no sponsored placements — vendors can’t apply and didn’t know in advance. Winners are drawn from the Honest Software Atlas, our hand-researched dossier corpus (also available as an open dataset). Each award quotes the dossier evidence it rests on. How we review →

From the dossier: “(re-verified 2026-08-17; the English page quotes USD while the same page served from a Dutch address quotes euros at the same numerals, so check your own currency) Four plans, billed annually: Build $24/month, Optimize $119/month, Scale from $229/month and Enterprise from $1,199/month.”

🪑Flat & Fair

No per-seat tax: pricing that doesn't punish you for growing your team.

Contractor Foreman
One flat fee per company instead of a per-seat meter: every tier has a user cap (1 on Basic at $49 up to 15 on Pro), and only Unlimited at $332/mo removes it — but no plan bills per head.
From the dossier: “Their own wording: the fee listed is the fee for the company as a whole.”

🧮The Honest Meter

Usage-based pricing done right: the meter is in the open, priced in plain sight.

Tax1099
Pay-as-you-go per form with public volume pricing — the page works a 600-form example to the cent, so you can compute your exact bill before you start.
From the dossier: “The page works the example itself for 600 forms — the first 20 at $2.99, forms 21-150 at $2.30, forms 151-500 at $1.31 and the last 100 at $0.68, totalling $885.30, an average of $1.48 a form.”
