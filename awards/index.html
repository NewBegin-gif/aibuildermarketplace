The Honest Software Awards 2026

Most software awards are bought. These are earned the boring way: we hand-research the pricing of every tool we cover (248 and counting), flag every cost-trap with evidence — and once a year, we celebrate the vendors who do pricing right. 17 winners, 4 categories, and the receipt shown under every single award.

How winners are chosen. No submissions, no entry fees, no sponsored placements — vendors can’t apply and didn’t know in advance. Winners are drawn from the Honest Software Atlas, our hand-researched dossier corpus (also available as an open dataset). Each award quotes the dossier evidence it rests on. How we review →
