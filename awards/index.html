AWARDS 2026 · NO PAY-TO-PLAY

The Honest Software Awards 2026

No entries, no fees, no sponsors. These awards are earned the boring way: we hand-research the pricing of every tool we cover (498 and counting), flag every cost-trap with evidence — and once a year, we celebrate the vendors who do pricing right. 14 winners, 4 categories, and the receipt shown under every single award.

How winners are chosen. No submissions, no entry fees, no sponsored placements — vendors can’t apply and didn’t know in advance. Winners are drawn from the Honest Software Atlas, our hand-researched dossier corpus (also available as an open dataset). Each award quotes the dossier evidence it rests on. The winners were picked by hand on 7 July 2026. Every rebuild of this page re-reads each winner’s evidence sentence in its dossier and stops if the sentence is gone; when a dossier no longer supports an award, the award is withdrawn (three were, on 15 September 2026: SignNow, AISQ and Lindy.ai). The list is reviewed once a year. How we review →

🆓Best Genuinely Free Tier

A free plan you can actually run on — not a teaser.

What it takes: a free plan with no time limit that a small business can run real work on, not a trial; the free limit is stated in the dossier. Pool today: 168 tools in our Atlas carry the free-tier label; 5 picked.

Free email marketing up to 10,000 subscribers with unlimited sends — exceptional in a market that usually caps free at a few hundred contacts.
From the dossier: “(re-verified 2026-09-02) The free plan is $0/mo and covers up to 10,000 email subscribers, with unlimited landing pages and forms, unlimited email broadcasts, audience tagging and segmentation, and digital products and subscriptions.”

🧾No-Tricks Pricing

Public prices, plain tiers, no quote games, no gotchas we could find.

What it takes: every self-serve plan priced on the vendor's own page, metered extras priced there too, and no gotcha we could find in the dossier. A clearly labelled promotion is allowed, and a custom enterprise plan alongside public tiers does not rule a tool out: the award is about the plans the page does publish. Pool today: 420 tools have a public price and no quote-only label; 5 picked.

Landingi
Four public tiers from $24 a month, with the metered items — extra domains and AI credits — priced on the page too.
From the dossier: “(re-verified 2026-08-17 and again on 2026-09-28; the English page quotes USD while the same page served from a Dutch address quotes euros at the same numerals, so check your own currency) Four plans, billed annually: Build $24/month, Optimize $119/month, Scale from $229/month and Enterprise from $1,199/month.”

🪑Flat & Fair

No per-seat tax: pricing that doesn't punish you for growing your team.

What it takes: the bill does not scale with the number of people who log in; user caps per plan are allowed and named. Pool today: 304 tools have a public price and no per-seat label; 3 picked.

Contractor Foreman
One flat fee per company instead of a per-seat meter: every tier has a user cap (1 on Basic at $49 up to 15 on Pro), and only Unlimited at $332/mo removes it — but no plan bills per head.
From the dossier: “Their own wording: the fee listed is the fee for the company as a whole.”

🧮The Honest Meter

Usage-based pricing done right: the meter is in the open, priced in plain sight.

What it takes: usage-based pricing where the unit price is published and you can work out the bill before you start. Pool today: 219 tools have a public price and a usage meter; 1 picked.

Tax1099
Pay-as-you-go per form with a public tier chart for every form type — sum the bands and you have your bill before you start. Budget from the chart, not the worked example above it.
From the dossier: “The Tiered Pricing Charts on the page, with the form-type selector on IRS eFile 1099, 1098, 1095, W-2, W-2C, W-2G, list the first 20 forms at $2.99 each, forms 21-150 at $2.59, 151-250 at $1.99, 251-500 at $1.42 and 501-1,000 at $0.72, with 1,000+ on request.”
