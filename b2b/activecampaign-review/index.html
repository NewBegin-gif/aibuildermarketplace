What ActiveCampaign does

Advanced marketing automation, email marketing, and CRM. Build complex, automated customer journeys that drive real revenue.

Pricing (2026)

No free plan (trial only): Starter from about $15/mo and Plus from roughly $49/mo at 1,000 contacts, scaling with your list — 10,000 contacts on Plus runs near $189/mo billed annually. CRM pipelines, SMS and extra users are paid add-ons (2026). Annual billing saves ~20%. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

Correction after re-checking: (re-verified 2026-07-23) ActiveCampaign's own pricing page shows no figures at all — every tier renders as "Starts at --/mo" alongside "Customize your plan"/"Request Pricing" — so every dollar amount in this review comes from third-party pages and none of it can be checked against the vendor. Both of those third parties are interested parties: sendx.io is a competing email marketing tool that positions itself as the cheaper alternative, and prospeo.io is a B2B data vendor. Their figures were re-fetched and quoted accurately on 2026-07-23, but they are not vendor-confirmed. Separately: on accounts created on or after 3 November 2025 unsubscribed, unconfirmed and bounced contacts count toward the billed contact limit (SendX; accounts created before that date stay on the old active-contacts-only rule), which makes older "you only pay for active contacts" guidance wrong for new accounts. SendX states this change was not publicly announced and it does not appear on the vendor pricing page.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

Pricing climbs with contact count and the add-ons (CRM, SMS, extra seats) stack up fast — the sticker Starter price is not what a serious automation setup actually costs. Powerful, but budget for the tier (Plus and up) you'll really use.

What buyers report about ActiveCampaign

Recurring themes from Capterra, Trustpilot and independent testing write-ups, read in August 2026. We have not run ActiveCampaign ourselves, so this is other buyers' experience rather than ours. We summarise it here because the complaints are usually the part a vendor page leaves out.

What they praise
  • • The automation builder is still the deepest in its price range, and that is what keeps reviewers on it despite everything below.
  • • CRM and email in one place, with a low entry price.
What they complain about
  • • Accounts created on or after 3 November 2025 are billed for all contacts — including unsubscribed, bounced and unconfirmed. That is the single most important thing to know here: your bill grows with a list you are not mailing. Older accounts should check which basis theirs is on.
  • • Crossing a contact tier re-prices the whole plan, not just the excess. Reviewers describe renewal jumps they had not budgeted for.
  • • Charges after a confirmed cancellation, unauthorised charges after closing an account, and difficult refunds are all recurring.
  • • Support leans on AI chat and reviewers report struggling to reach a person.
  • • Mobile rendering is inconsistent and you cannot design a separate mobile layout.

Where the ratings disagree. Trustpilot has sat between 2.8 and 3.0 across roughly 1,300-1,400 reviews through 2026 — low for this category — while the software directories rate it well. The directories are rating the automation; Trustpilot is rating the invoice.

When the paid plan is worth it

There is no free plan: after the 14-day trial (no credit card required) you pay, and ActiveCampaign backs that with a 30-day money-back guarantee. Judge the tiers on the feature grid rather than the price list, because the price list is no longer public — every tier on activecampaign.com/pricing renders as "Starts at --/mo" with no figure, so the amounts below come from third parties you cannot check against the vendor: SendX, a competing email marketing tool, and Prospeo, a B2B data vendor. On the published grid the sharpest break is automation depth: Starter is listed at 5 actions per automation, while Plus, Pro and Enterprise are listed as unlimited automation actions. That is the concrete thing you buy by leaving Starter. What you do not buy is a seat — Starter and Plus are both 1 user, Pro is 3 and Enterprise 5 — and you do not buy a CRM: Pipelines and Sales Engagement are separately priced add-ons, listed by Prospeo at $49 and $85 per month on top of your plan. Sending headroom moves slowly as well: the monthly send cap is 10x your contact limit on Starter and Plus, 12x on Pro and 15x on Enterprise. Billing is per contact regardless of how little you send, so a broadcast-only list gets expensive on volume alone. At 1,000 contacts on annual billing SendX lists Starter at $15, Plus at $49, Pro at $79 and Enterprise at $145 per month; Prospeo lists Starter at $19 per month if you pay month-to-month. At 10,000 contacts SendX puts Starter alone at $149 per month on annual billing.

How to actually use ActiveCampaign

First value is a campaign, not an automation: import a list, pick a template, send. That is a same-day job. Before your first real send, authenticate your sending domain in ActiveCampaign's settings and add the DNS records it gives you at your DNS host. If you are migrating in, ActiveCampaign says its free migration service moves lists, tags and custom fields, email templates and forms, and up to 10 automations, and that it includes account setup help; the page names no plan-tier or paid condition. Treat that as the vendor's own description of its own service and check it against your setup before you rely on it — the list stops at 10 automations and says nothing about third-party integrations. For the builder itself, ActiveCampaign says it ships 950+ automation recipes, which is a cheaper starting point than a blank canvas; that count is also the vendor's, not an independent one. Check your contact count early. SendX reports that on accounts created on or after 3 November 2025, unsubscribed, unconfirmed and bounced contacts all count toward the limit you are billed for, while accounts created before that date are grandfathered onto the old rule and pay for active contacts only. SendX also says the change was never publicly announced, and ActiveCampaign's pricing page does not state it, so confirm which rule applies to you against your own invoice. Frequently asked questions

Is ActiveCampaign worth it for a small team?

It depends on whether you have the bottleneck it solves. Small teams get the most out of this category when one clear problem is already costing real hours or revenue; buying ahead of that just adds cost and another login. Price it against the hours or lost deals it removes, not against its feature list, and start on the smallest plan that covers the job.

Who should not use ActiveCampaign?

Skip it if you just need to send a newsletter — a simpler, cheaper email tool does that without paying for automation you won't touch. Buying a tool to fix a problem you don't have yet just adds cost and another login to manage.
