The meter counts downloads, not campaigns — iterate seriously on ad variations and a 10-credit starter evaporates in an afternoon, which is exactly what iteration-heavy ad work does. Throughout this review we weigh it against Canva.

Best for: performance marketers and stores that test many ad variations across sizes and platforms and want on-brand creative volume without a designer bottleneck.
Who should skip it: if you run a handful of static ads a month (a designer or Canva covers it), or your bottleneck is offer/targeting rather than creative volume.

Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 13 Sep 2026

Three families, each with several credit steps. Starter is 1 user throughout: $39 a month for 10 credits and 1 brand, $89 for 15 credits and 2 brands, $139 for 30 credits and $189 for 50. Professional is 10 users and 10 brands: $249 for 50 credits, $339 for 75 and $400 for 100.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a working AdCreative.ai discount or promo code?

You probably do not need one, and that is the honest answer. Their pricing table has carried a banner reading FATHER'S DAY PREP SALE: 50% off yearly, 25% off quarterly plans on every reading we hold — 23 July 2026 and again on 15 August 2026, well past Father's Day. A discount present in every observation is the price, not an event. Note also that the page publishes no undiscounted yearly or quarterly rate, so there is nothing to measure the 50% against.

Is AdCreative.ai a scam?

No, it is a working product with a real complaint pattern, and the two are worth separating. What buyers actually report on the review platforms is difficulty getting money back: refund requests closed with a pointer to the terms, and in one account only the most recent charge refunded rather than the amount in dispute. The vendor's own policy is narrow — a refund requires that you did not use the service at all and that you ask within 7 days on a monthly plan or 30 days on an annual one. Combine that with credits that expire each cycle and a discounted yearly plan, and the risk is not fraud, it is committing 12 months up front to something you cannot unwind. Start monthly.

What does a second brand or a second person cost?

A second brand is cheap and a second seat is expensive. Starter carries 2 brands from its $89 step upward, so a second brand costs $89 a month rather than a tier change. But Starter is 1 user at every step, so the moment a colleague needs their own login you are on Professional at $249. Note the overlap that creates: 50 credits costs $189 in Starter and $249 in Professional, so that $60 buys brands and seats, not extra downloads.

What should I weigh before buying Growth & Revenue software?

It depends on whether you have the bottleneck it solves. Small teams get the most out of this category when one clear problem is already costing real hours or revenue; buying ahead of that just adds cost and another login. Price it against the hours or lost deals it removes, not against its feature list, and start on the smallest plan that covers the job.
