AfterSell Review (2026)

Growth & RevenueResearched assessment · by Daniel HaketUpdated 2026-09-25

Post-purchase upsells only move the needle with steady order volume and a sensible complementary-offer strategy — bolt it onto a store with little traffic and there's nothing to upsell. The no-rev-share model is a genuine plus over percentage-based rivals.

Best for: Shopify stores with steady order volume that want to lift average order value with one-click post-purchase and checkout upsells.
Who should skip it: if your store gets only a trickle of orders — fix traffic and conversion first; there's nothing to upsell against low volume.

Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 22 Sep 2026

Three plans, each metered by monthly order volume, read on aftersell.com/pricing and on the Shopify App Store listing on 22 September 2026 (re-verified 22 September 2026). The entry prices are quoted at 200 monthly orders: Post-Purchase $34.99 a month, capped at $999.99; Pre-Purchase $59.99, capped at $599.99; Complete $79.99, capped at $1,359.99. Complete is the other two together, which the vendor prices at 15% under buying them separately.

Go over a volume tier and the fee steps up to the matching one on its own, with that cap as the ceiling. Every plan carries a 30-day free trial, all charges are billed in USD, and the Free plan on the listing is for development and partner stores only. Checkout upsells are Shopify Plus only and now sit inside Post-Purchase and Complete rather than being billed separately.

Rokt Thanks, the third-party offers on the confirmation page, is free with no minimum order volume and pays up to $0.80 per order; for stores above 1,000 monthly orders whose primary market is the US, Canada, Australia or the UK those earnings are credited against the subscription dollar for dollar. Aftersell Max, for high-volume stores, is custom-priced and publishes no figure. One number we could not account for: a $19.99 sits above the $79.99 on the Complete card, in the same white heading type and not struck through, and neither the page nor the FAQ explains it — the FAQ says Complete starts at $79.99.

Check your own checkout total before you commit. This replaces the single order-volume ladder we recorded on 5 September 2026 ($34.99 for 0-500 orders, $54.99 for 501-1,000, $99.99 for 1,001-2,000); that ladder is on neither page any more. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

Correction after re-checking: (re-verified 22 September 2026) Aftersell has replaced the single order-volume ladder we recorded in July and September 2026 with three separately sold plans, each metered by order volume: Post-Purchase from $34.99 a month, Pre-Purchase from $59.99 and Complete from $79.99, quoted at 200 monthly orders and capped at $999.99, $599.99 and $1,359.99. The brackets we published ($54.99 at 501-1,000, $99.99 at 1,001-2,000, $149.99 plus $0.03 an order to a $799.99 ceiling) are on neither page any more. One charge has gone away: checkout upsells were billed separately at a flat $99 a month and are now included in Post-Purchase and Complete, still Shopify Plus only. The Shopify App Store listing shows only the three entry prices — not the volume scaling, not the caps and not the custom-priced Aftersell Max tier — so the listing on its own now understates what a busy store pays.

What AfterSell does

Post-purchase upsell and cross-sell app for Shopify (by Rokt): one-click upsells, downsells and thank-you-page offers plus checkout customization to lift average order value — no extra ad spend.

What buyers report about AfterSell

Recurring themes from the buyer-review sites listed at the foot of this page, read in August 2026. We have not run AfterSell ourselves, so this is other buyers' experience rather than ours. We summarise it here because the complaints are usually the part a vendor page leaves out.

When the paid plan is worth it

AfterSell has no free tier for a live store: the Free plan on its Shopify listing reads "ONLY For Development & Partner Stores". What the money buys is which half of the funnel you get, and how many orders you may run through it. Read 22 September 2026, at 200 monthly orders: Post-Purchase at $34.99 a month covers the one-click offers after checkout, the thank-you page and Smart Funnels; Pre-Purchase at $59.99 covers the cart — 18-plus drag-and-drop blocks, rewards bars, free gifts, product-page upsells and A/B testing; Complete at $79.99 is both, at 15% under buying them apart.

All three climb with your order volume and stop at a stated ceiling — $999.99, $599.99 and $1,359.99 respectively — so the ceiling, not the entry price, is the number to plan against. Checkout upsells no longer carry a separate fee: they are inside Post-Purchase and Complete, and still Shopify Plus only. Rokt Thanks is free with no minimum volume and pays up to $0.80 per order; above 1,000 monthly orders in the US, Canada, Australia or the UK it is credited against your subscription dollar for dollar, which is the one route by which more orders can mean a smaller bill rather than a bigger one.

Upgrading buys order headroom, not reach. Regardless of plan, Shopify blocks one-click offers on BNPL, digital wallets, gift cards, COD and bank transfers, on non-default-currency checkouts, and on orders worth less than $0.50 — and caps you at three accepted post-purchase offers per order.

How to actually use AfterSell

Install the app, then do the step people skip: Shopify itself has to be pointed at it. Under Settings → Checkout, Aftersell must be selected as the active post-purchase page app, or nothing renders. In the app, open Post-purchase Funnels, start from a template or add a funnel manually, set trigger conditions (they combine with AND logic), add your offers, then activate. Thank-you-page widgets are a separate job: the docs say the blocks must be placed on both the thank you page and the order status page in the Shopify editor, or they will not display.

Two practical traps. First, a test order paid with Apple Pay, Klarna or a gift card will show no offer at all — that is Shopify's restriction, not a broken install, and the documented fallback is thank-you-page offers. Second, the editor preview does not match live rendering, so real test orders are recommended. The vendor's best-practice page advises waiting 2-4 weeks or 100-200 impressions per layout before drawing conclusions.

Distilled from vendor documentation only: the Aftersell quickstart, post-purchase best-practices and "why isn't my post-purchase offer displaying" pages on docs.aftersell.com; no independent walkthrough met the bar (the two candidates were agency posts, one promoting a different app).

The natural comparison is Zipify OneClickUpsell or ReConvert — other Shopify upsell apps.
