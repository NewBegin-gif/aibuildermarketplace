What buyers report about AgencyAnalytics

Recurring themes from the buyer-review sites listed at the foot of this page, read in August 2026. We have not run AgencyAnalytics ourselves, so this is other buyers' experience rather than ours. We summarise it here because the complaints are usually the part a vendor page leaves out.

What they praise

  • • White-label delivery quality, and that the custom domain is on every plan including the cheapest.

What they complain about

  • • Integrations disconnect and need re-authorising.
  • • Several reviewers say the higher plans feel like worse value than the lower ones, so there is little pull to upgrade.

When the paid plan is worth it

AgencyAnalytics publishes one rate: $20 USD per client / month, billed annually, with "Save 20% annually" beside it. The card never prints the monthly figure; the page's own schema.org block does, as an AggregateOffer with lowPrice $20 and highPrice $25, so monthly is $25 per client. There is no free plan, only a 14-day trial with no credit card and a 30-day money-back guarantee. You pay per client, and staff and client users are unlimited, so cost tracks the roster you already invoice; putting a junior on reporting adds nothing.

It pays for itself when you rebuild the same reports for a stable roster every month. It works against you when your client count swings, because you pay per client whether or not that client got a report, and the annual rate carries a 1-year commitment while monthly has none. Budget the add-ons separately: Rank Tracker $41.67/month per 500 keywords, AI Tracker $20.83/month per 250 credits. Paying more fixes neither recurring complaint: integration outages are, in the vendor's words, "by far the most common reason for missing data", and same-name metrics calculated differently stay different at any price. Past 25 clients the vendor quotes rather than publishing a rate.

How to actually use AgencyAnalytics

The vendor's getting-started guide is eight videos. The first four are Welcome & Quick Start (4 minutes), Quick Account Tour (4.5), Add & Organize Clients (2) and Connect a Data Source (2.5). The rest are Create a Dashboard (7.5), Build and Schedule a Report (4.5), Set Up Branding / White Labeling (3), and Manage Users and Permissions (4). The vendor's own tutorial weighting suggests where the work sits: connecting a source gets 2.5 minutes, while building a dashboard gets 7.5 and scheduling a report another 4.5.

Read that as the vendor's emphasis, not as a measure of your time. A Client is "one business relationship you manage in AgencyAnalytics", and you connect its sources from the 85+ integrations. Plan for numbers that disagree with the native platform. The troubleshooting doc tells you to match the exact start and end dates used in the source platform, and warns that similarly named metrics are calculated differently — its "Clicks" pulls from the "Total Clicks" metric in Facebook Ads.

Integration outages are the vendor's stated most common cause of missing data, so expect reconnection work; the Connect Your Data help collection alone runs to 110 articles.

What does AgencyAnalytics cost for a real client roster?

One published rate: $20 USD per client per month, billed annually, with unlimited staff and client users (read on the vendor pricing page 11 September 2026). Twenty-four clients is $480 a month at that rate; from 25 clients up the vendor stops publishing a price and asks you to contact sales for a custom plan. Price the add-ons on top of that: Rank Tracker at $41.67 a month per 500 keywords and AI Tracker at $20.83 a month per 250 credits, both billed annually. Is there a month-to-month price?

What the page does spell out is what you trade: monthly plans carry no commitment and can be cancelled any time, while the discounted annual plan requires a one-year commitment. The 14-day trial needs no credit card, and there is a 30-day money-back guarantee (all read 11 September 2026).
