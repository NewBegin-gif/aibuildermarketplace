What AgencyAnalytics does

Client reporting and dashboards built for marketing agencies: aggregate 80+ marketing platforms into white-label dashboards and automated reports, with per-client KPI goals. White-labeling on every plan.

Pricing (2026)

The simplified Core plan is ~$20 per client/mo with no clients included in the base — so cost scales directly with your roster (a 50-client agency pays ~$1,000/mo). Legacy plans start around $59–79/mo for 5 clients (2026). Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

Correction after re-checking: (re-verified 2026-07-23) AgencyAnalytics lists a single per-client plan at $20 USD per client / month billed annually, with an Enterprise "contact us" route for agencies at 25+ clients. Rank Tracker and AI Tracker are separate add-ons priced on top of that rate, not included in it. The page advertises "Save 20% annually" but does not publish a month-to-month figure, so no monthly price is stated here.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

Two honest snags. Cost scales linearly with clients — great at 5, pricey at 50+. And the most-cited complaint is connector reliability: data sources disconnect and the platform doesn't always flag the failure clearly. Solo or small-data setups get little value from it.

What buyers report about AgencyAnalytics

Recurring themes from G2 and Capterra, read in August 2026. We have not run AgencyAnalytics ourselves, so this is other buyers' experience rather than ours. We summarise it here because the complaints are usually the part a vendor page leaves out.

What they praise
  • • Time saved on client reporting — automated reports are the single most-cited reason agencies keep it.
  • • White-label delivery quality, and that the custom domain is on every plan including the cheapest.
  • • Support response speed, rated above 9/10 by G2 reviewers.
What they complain about
  • • No global template management. You cannot push one change across all client reports at once — reviewers have flagged this for two years and it is still open.
  • • Integrations disconnect and need re-authorising.
  • • Several reviewers say the higher plans feel like worse value than the lower ones, so there is little pull to upgrade.

When the paid plan is worth it

AgencyAnalytics publishes one rate: $20 USD per client / month, billed annually, with "Save 20% annually" shown beside it — so paying month to month costs more, and the page does not print that figure. There is no free plan; the only free route in is a 14-day free trial with no credit card required, backed by a 30-day money-back guarantee. The economics are simple: you pay per client, and staff and client users are unlimited, so cost tracks the roster you already invoice and putting a junior on reporting adds nothing. It pays for itself when you rebuild the same reports for a stable roster every month. It works against you when your client count swings, because you pay per client whether or not that client got a report, and because the discounted rate carries a 1-year commitment while monthly plans have none. Budget the add-ons separately: Rank Tracker at $41.67 USD /month per 500 keywords and AI Tracker at $20.83 USD /month per 250 credits, both billed annually. Paying more fixes neither recurring complaint: integration outages are, in the vendor's words, "by far the most common reason for missing data", and same-name metrics that are calculated differently stay different at any price. Past 25 clients the vendor asks you to contact them for a custom plan rather than publishing a rate.

How to actually use AgencyAnalytics

The vendor's getting-started guide is eight videos: Welcome & Quick Start (4 minutes), Quick Account Tour (4.5), Add & Organize Clients (2), Connect a Data Source (2.5), Create a Dashboard (7.5), Build and Schedule a Report (4.5), Set Up Branding / White Labeling (3), and Manage Users and Permissions (4). The vendor's own tutorial weighting suggests where the work sits: connecting a source gets 2.5 minutes, while building a dashboard gets 7.5 and scheduling a report another 4.5. Read that as the vendor's emphasis, not as a measure of your time. A Client is "one business relationship you manage in AgencyAnalytics", and you connect its sources from the 85+ integrations. Plan for numbers that disagree with the native platform: the troubleshooting doc tells you to match the exact start and end dates used in the source platform, and warns that similarly named metrics are calculated differently — its "Clicks" pulls from the "Total Clicks" metric in Facebook Ads. Integration outages are the vendor's stated most common cause of missing data, so expect reconnection work; the Connect Your Data help collection alone runs to 110 articles. Distilled from vendor pricing page and vendor help-centre articles (getting-started guide, data-and-access-issues, data-mismatch troubleshooting, Connect Your Data collection), all fetched directly this session.

Frequently asked questions

Is AgencyAnalytics worth it for a small team?

It depends on whether you have the bottleneck it solves. Small teams get the most out of this category when one clear problem is already costing real hours or revenue; buying ahead of that just adds cost and another login. Price it against the hours or lost deals it removes, not against its feature list, and start on the smallest plan that covers the job.

Who should not use AgencyAnalytics?

Skip it if you're a solo operator with a couple of data sources, or you want rock-solid single-dashboard KPI tracking — Databox or a lighter tool may fit better. Buying a tool to fix a problem you don't have yet just adds cost and another login to manage.
