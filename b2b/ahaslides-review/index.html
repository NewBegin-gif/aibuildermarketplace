What AhaSlides does

Interactive presentation platform — live polls, quizzes, word clouds and audience Q&A, free for up to 50 participants.

Pricing (2026)

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

AhaSlides runs regional pricing: the number on the pricing page depends on where you browse from — so any review quoting one price (including ours) may not match yours. The advertised savings assume annual commitment, and the free tier's five-quiz-slide cap nudges real quiz use toward paid.

What buyers report about AhaSlides

Recurring themes from G2, Capterra, GetApp and Gartner Peer Insights, read in August 2026. We have not run AhaSlides ourselves, so this is other buyers' experience rather than ours. We summarise it here because the complaints are usually the part a vendor page leaves out.

What they praise
  • • Live polls, quizzes and Q&A that an audience actually joins — 4.7 on G2 across 317 reviews, and simplicity is the reason.
  • • Both presenter and audience are up and running in minutes, with no install.
What they complain about
  • • Limited customisation is the single most common complaint, in 42 of those 317 reviews.
  • • The free plan caps at 50 participants and allows only 5 quiz and 3 poll slides per presentation. Check that against your room size first.
  • • No undo button and no offline mode.
  • • From $7.95 a month, but reviewers on small teams still call it expensive for what it is.

When the paid plan is worth it

AhaSlides has a free tier that most people can actually run a session on: 50 live participants, unlimited presentations, and up to 5 Quiz or 3 Collect opinions/Q&A slides inside a single presentation. Read that slide cap carefully, because it does not degrade gently — AhaSlides' help centre states that if your presentation has more than 5 Quiz or 3 Poll-type slides, you will only be able to host an event with up to 3 participants. In practice it is one quiz-heavy deck, not the size of your audience, that pushes you onto a paid plan. On the paid side, AhaSlides' own comparison pages list yearly entry prices of $95.40/year for Essential (for non-educators) and $35.40/year for Edu Small (for educators). If you teach, check whether you qualify for the education rate before you pay the non-education one. Essential's audience ceiling is 100 participants according to AhaSlides' own pricing-change post, so what the tier above Essential mainly sells you is a bigger room rather than a different product. Three things to price in before you commit. Team plans are sold per licence: you choose the number of licences and AhaSlides shows the total, with the pricing page advertising up to 20% off team plans — so this scales with headcount, it is not a flat account fee. The yearly saving the pricing page advertises, up to 67% against monthly billing, only exists if you commit for a full year. And if you are still on a legacy Plus subscription, the help centre says you can move to Pro at a 50% discount, applicable only once and only while the legacy plan is active — worth using before you renew at list price. One limit on this section: AhaSlides' pricing page builds its price table in the browser and returned no plan prices as text, so the two prices above come from AhaSlides' own comparison pages rather than from the pricing page, and your checkout total may differ from them. We are not quoting a Pro price, because we could not read one on any AhaSlides page.

Best for: educators, trainers and event hosts running interactive sessions — polls, quizzes, word clouds and live Q&A — with audiences up to 50 free.
Who should skip it: if you need predictable, uniform pricing across a multi-country team, or your decks live in PowerPoint and a lightweight poll add-on covers it.

The natural comparison is Mentimeter — Mentimeter is the category standard with the polished template library; AhaSlides undercuts it with a more generous free tier and regional pricing — check both from your own location. Decide by which one fits the job above, not by the louder brand.

Frequently asked questions

Can AhaSlides match my brand style?

Up to a point, and that point is usually set by your plan. Check whether brand kits, custom fonts, colour locking and template controls sit on the tier you would actually buy, since these are common upsells. Test it with your own assets during the trial rather than the demo content, which is always chosen to flatter the output.

Who should not use AhaSlides?

Skip it if you need predictable, uniform pricing across a multi-country team, or your decks live in PowerPoint and a lightweight poll add-on covers it. Buying a tool to fix a problem you don't have yet just adds cost and another login to manage.
