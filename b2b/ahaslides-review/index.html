Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 6 Sep 2026

AhaSlides prices regionally, so the currency and the amount depend on where you buy from; the figures below are the reading from a Netherlands location on 6 September 2026, and your own country's page may show a different rate for the same tier. Free is 0 for up to 50 participants, with unlimited events and unlimited presentations but only 5 Quiz and 3 Unscored slides per deck.

Essential is €7.25 per month billed yearly and lifts the ceiling to 100 participants, adding unlimited interactive slides, analytics and exports, and folder management. Pro AI is €10.65 per month billed yearly, raises the ceiling to 2,500 participants and adds the AI features, custom branding, Q&A moderation and priority support. Enterprise is quote-only, listed at up to 100,000 participants with SSO and SCIM offered on demand.

Two discounts sit on top: yearly billing is advertised at up to 67% below monthly, and team licences bought together save up to a further 20%. AhaSlides' own USD listing puts Essential at $95.40 a year, which is the same tier at a different regional price rather than a second plan — so check the page from your own country before you budget. When the paid plan is worth it

AhaSlides has a free tier that most people can actually run a session on: 50 live participants, unlimited presentations, and up to 5 Quiz or 3 Collect opinions/Q&A slides inside a single presentation. Read that slide cap carefully, because it does not degrade gently — AhaSlides' help centre states that if your presentation has more than 5 Quiz or 3 Poll-type slides, you will only be able to host an event with up to 3 participants.

In practice it is one quiz-heavy deck, not the size of your audience, that pushes you onto a paid plan. On the paid side, AhaSlides' own comparison pages list yearly entry prices in US dollars of $95.40/year for Essential (for non-educators) and $35.40/year for Edu Small (for educators). If you teach, check whether you qualify for the education rate before you pay the non-education one. Essential's audience ceiling is 100 participants according to AhaSlides' own pricing-change post, so what the tier above Essential mainly sells you is a bigger room rather than a different product.

Three things to price in before you commit. Team plans are sold per licence: you choose the number of licences and AhaSlides shows the total, with the pricing page advertising up to 20% off team plans — so this scales with headcount, it is not a flat account fee. The yearly saving the pricing page advertises, up to 67% against monthly billing, only exists if you commit for a full year.

And if you are still on a legacy Plus subscription, the help centre says you can move to Pro at a 50% discount, applicable only once and only while the legacy plan is active — worth using before you renew at list price. One thing to know about the numbers above: AhaSlides builds its price table in the browser, so it only yields figures to a rendered read, and what it yields depends on where you browse from.

How to actually use AhaSlides

Sign-up is email, Google, Microsoft or enterprise SSO at presenter.ahaslides.com/pages/register, and the documentation says plainly that no credit card is required and the free plan is permanent with up to 50 live participants. You land on the presentations dashboard next to the template library. If a deck already exists, import the PPT, PPTX or PDF and slot interactive slides between the existing ones rather than rebuilding. Check the free-plan slide caps before you design around them: five Quiz slides and three unscored Collect opinions or Q&A slides inside a single presentation.

Presenting is where the settings that matter live. The audience joins from a URL or QR code with no app to install, and scored slides prompt for names automatically. Backstage gives you a private presenter window for slide control and Q&A moderation; self-paced mode removes the host entirely, and access can be gated with a passcode or Google and Microsoft verification.

What does AhaSlides cost?

How much does annual billing actually save?

The page advertises up to 67% off against monthly, which is unusually steep and means the monthly rate is closer to a convenience premium than a real option. Team licences add up to another 20% when several people buy together, so a department buying jointly pays materially less per seat.

Is the free tier enough for a classroom or workshop?

For most, yes: 50 participants covers a typical class or workshop and presentations are unlimited. The wall is per-deck content — five quiz slides and three unscored ones — so a long interactive session is what pushes you to pay, not audience size.
