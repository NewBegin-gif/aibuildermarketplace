What Aircall does

Cloud phone and call-center platform for sales and support teams. Smart call routing, CRM integrations and AI call insights — set up business numbers in 100+ countries in minutes.

Pricing (2026)

(2026) Essentials about $30/user/mo annual ($40 monthly), Professional ~$50/user/mo annual ($70 monthly); custom tier needs 25+ licenses. Real floor: a 3-license minimum applies, so entry is effectively ~$90/mo. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

Two structural catches: the 3-seat minimum makes the real starting price triple the sticker, and the annual-vs-monthly gap is steep (Professional: $50 vs $70). 'Unlimited calling' covers listed regions — check yours — and AI features increasingly live in add-ons or higher tiers. It's polished and integration-rich; just price the seats you'll actually have, on the billing cycle you'll actually choose.

What buyers report about Aircall

Recurring themes from G2, Trustpilot and Reddit, read in August 2026. We have not run Aircall ourselves, so this is other buyers' experience rather than ours. We summarise it here because the complaints are usually the part a vendor page leaves out.

What they praise
  • • CRM integration with HubSpot and Salesforce is what reviewers rate highest, along with how quickly a team gets running. 4.4 on G2.
  • • The interface is genuinely good — that part is not in dispute.
What they complain about
  • • Connection issues (71 mentions) and call quality (58) are consistently flagged on G2. For a phone system, weigh those first.
  • • Auto-renewal is the loudest complaint. Accounts over 10 seats need 30 days' written notice before renewal, and there are no refunds for early termination. Diarise it.
  • • Charges after cancellation and surprise invoices come up repeatedly on Trustpilot.
  • • A three-user minimum, and reviewers on small teams call it expensive regardless.
  • • Support is slow by most accounts.

Where the ratings disagree. 4.4 on G2 against 3.0 on Trustpilot across roughly 1,025 reviews. The G2 reviews are about the product; the Trustpilot ones are almost entirely renewal and billing.

When the paid plan is worth it

Aircall has no free tier. Its own pricing page does not display dollar figures (prices render dynamically per country and seat count), so the reference points come from a third-party marketplace, Vendr, which lists Essentials at $30 per user per month and Professional at $50, both on annual billing; your negotiated price may differ. The real decision is between those two tiers. Essentials covers a functioning cloud phone system for a team that mainly needs to place and receive calls. Professional earns its extra cost once your reps live inside a CRM all day and you need deeper routing, analytics and integration depth; at that point the per-seat gap is small next to the time saved. It does not pay for itself for a small team doing light call volume, and the 3-license minimum on both plans means you buy at least three seats regardless of headcount, so a solo operator overpays from day one.

Common Aircall setup mistakes

Where Aircall setups actually go wrong, and what it costs you when they do. Same sources as the setup notes above — not from a walkthrough we ran ourselves.

The friction starts when you try to bring your existing business line across.

  • Porting is blocked on trial accounts, so you must already be a paying customer, meaning you commit money before your real number works.
  • From there the Letter of Authorization has to be signed, and your losing carrier can reject the request over mismatched account details or because your number sits in a range that must all be ported together.
  • After a successful port, SMS can take up to 3 business days to switch on, and any reschedule or cancellation needs 2-3 business days' notice, so between the notice period and SMS reactivation a botched date can cost you the better part of a week.

Frequently asked questions

Will Aircall work for a remote or distributed team?

Generally yes, since this category is built for it, but the details that bite are timezone handling, how per-seat cost scales as the team spreads, and whether numbers, routing or coverage exist in the countries your people actually sit in. Availability varies far more by country than the marketing pages suggest, so check your specific regions before committing.

Who should not use Aircall?

Skip it if you're a solo operator or duo (the 3-license minimum prices you out), or you need a full contact-center suite with omnichannel routing. Buying a tool to fix a problem you don't have yet just adds cost and another login to manage.
