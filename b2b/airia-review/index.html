What Airia does

Enterprise AI orchestration and governance platform. Build, deploy and govern AI agents and automated workflows at scale — no-code/low-code/pro-code agent builder, connectors and MCP support, with security and governance built into the orchestration layer.

Pricing (2026)

(2026) A free tier, Individual about $50/mo and Team about $250/mo are published; the Enterprise tier — where most real deployments land — is custom-quoted (SSO/SAML, audit logs, dedicated support). Contact sales for enterprise pricing. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

Airia publishes some prices, which is refreshingly rare for enterprise AI — but the honest reality is that the low tiers are a taste, and serious multi-agent governance deployments land in custom-quote territory. Its value (orchestrating and governing AI agents at scale, with security built into the orchestration layer) only pays off at organizational scale; a small team that just wants one chatbot is buying far more platform than it needs. For an enterprise standardizing and governing AI, that's exactly the point.

How to actually use Airia

First value in Airia comes from shipping one working agent, and the path is guided. You accept an email invitation, set a password, then create a Project to hold your components. Inside it you add a model from the model list, add a Data Source (the docs steer you to the File Upload connector as the starting point), then drag the pieces onto a canvas, wiring Input to Data Source to Model to Output, save, and test. To actually use the agent, you attach an interface so it shows up in Airia Chat under Apps. Uploaded audio and video are not retrievable the moment you add them, because text extraction runs after speech-to-text processing rather than in real-time. The File Upload connector also rejects some file types outright, such as files containing script content like PHP or JS, and certain image formats fail through the Microsoft connectors. Frequently asked questions

Is Airia secure and reliable enough to depend on?

Judge it on evidence rather than adjectives. Look for a published security page, single sign-on and role controls on the plan you would actually buy (they are often gated to higher tiers), a status page with real incident history, and a clear answer on where your data is stored. If the tool will hold anything sensitive, those four answers matter more than any feature comparison.

Who should not use Airia?

Skip it if you're an individual or small team wanting a single AI assistant (this is far more platform than you need), or you can't commit to an enterprise rollout. Buying a tool to fix a problem you don't have yet just adds cost and another login to manage.
