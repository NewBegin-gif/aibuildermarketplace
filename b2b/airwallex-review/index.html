What Airwallex does

Multi-currency business accounts, corporate cards, international transfers and payment acceptance.

Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 1 Aug 2026

(2026-08-01, read on their own pricing page) FX conversion 0.5% above the interbank rate for major currencies and 1% for all others. Local transfers to 120+ countries free; SWIFT transfers $15–$25 each. Payment acceptance 2.8% + $0.30 on domestic cards and 4.30% + $0.30 on international cards; local payment methods $0.30 plus method-specific fees. Corporate cards carry no international fees and up to 2% cash rebate on local USD spend.

Account plans: Explore free, Grow $12 per user/month plus a platform fee that varies with team size, Accelerate custom. Platform API and embedded finance are quote-only. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

The honest headline here is that the FX markup is published at all — 0.5% and 1% above interbank is a real number, and most competitors will not print one. What the page does not settle is everything around it. The Grow plan's platform fee 'varies with team size' with no table, so the $12 a user is not the whole subscription. Chargeback and dispute fees are not priced anywhere, only mentioned as a feature.

And the page's own disclaimer says per-transaction fees apply to certain transactions with the detail in a separate fee schedule. So: use the published FX rate to compare against your bank, then ask for the fee schedule and the platform fee before you move money.

When the paid plan is worth it

Explore is genuinely free at $0 per user per month, and for most small teams it is the right answer — the paid tiers buy controls, not capability. Grow is $12 per user per month plus a platform fee that varies with team size; Accelerate is quote-only. Read on their own pricing page on 2 August 2026, and note their own line that the monthly fee is charged per business legal entity, once, however many accounts you hold under it.

The money is in the transaction fees, not the subscription, so price it there. FX conversion is 0.5% above the interbank rate on major currencies and 1% on everything else. Local transfers to 120+ countries are free; SWIFT is $15–$25 per transfer. Accepting card payments costs 2.80% + $0.30 domestic and 4.30% + $0.30 international, with subscription billing at 0.50% per successful card transaction.

That FX markup is the reason to look at Airwallex at all. Against a traditional bank charging 2–3% on conversion, 0.5% pays for itself on the first sizeable transfer — and the free Explore tier means there is no subscription to justify before it does.

How to actually use Airwallex

Open on Explore first. It costs nothing, it is the same rails as the paid tiers, and the fee schedule that determines whether Airwallex saves you money applies identically. Upgrade when you need approval workflows and spend controls, not before.

Set up local receiving accounts in the currencies your customers actually pay in, because that is what avoids the conversion fee entirely. Getting paid USD into a USD account costs nothing to convert; getting paid USD into a EUR account costs you 0.5% before anything else happens.

Route transfers locally rather than by SWIFT wherever the corridor allows it. Local is free to 120+ countries and SWIFT is $15–$25 — on a $500 supplier payment that is the difference between free and 5%. Check the corridor before you send, not after.

Frequently asked questions
