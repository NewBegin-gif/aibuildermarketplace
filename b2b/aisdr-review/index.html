What AISDR does

Deploy an autonomous AI Sales Development Rep. It qualifies leads, books meetings, and handles initial outreach conversations 24/7.

Pricing (2026)

(2026) Explore about $900/mo (1,200 AI messages + 1,200 lead-search credits) and Grow about $2,500/mo (4,500 each); billed on a quarterly commitment, ~20% off annual. Priced by message volume, not seats — unlimited users. Enterprise custom. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

Correction after re-checking: (re-verified 2026-07-24) AiSDR now lists a $250/mo Solo entry tier (200 AI-researched contacts, 1 user, 3 mailboxes) below the $900/mo Explore plan, and the top published tier is Scale at $2,500/mo for 2,500 contacts with 18 mailboxes — replacing the older single $900-minimum entry point and the higher per-plan "message" allowances. Solo is billed monthly and cancelable anytime; Explore and Scale are quarterly contracts paid in advance.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

It's priced like an SDR salary because it's meant to replace part of that function — so judge it on cost-per-meeting, not the sticker, and expect a quarterly commitment rather than a flip-the-switch monthly. Autonomous AI outreach is only as good as your targeting, list quality and messaging guardrails; point it at a bad list and you'll automate bad outreach (and risk your sending reputation). For teams with a solid ICP and offer, it can book meetings at a real cost-per-meeting; for those still figuring out messaging, it's premature.

When the paid plan is worth it

AiSDR has no free tier and no pay-per-use option, so the real decision is which paid step to buy. Solo at $250/mo (200 AI-researched contacts, 1 user, 3 mailboxes) fits a founder validating whether the AI's copy actually earns replies before committing budget; it is billed monthly and cancelable anytime. Explore at $900/mo pays for itself once more than one person needs access: it adds unlimited users and 6 mailboxes, and the extra mailboxes matter more than the higher 800-contact ceiling. Scale at $2,500/mo (2,500 contacts, 18 mailboxes) only earns its keep if you can genuinely feed that volume. Note that Explore and Scale are quarterly contracts paid in advance, not month-to-month like Solo. Upgrading buys reach, not results — it does not fix weak targeting, off-tone messages, or a cold domain. Because AiSDR runs domain warmup before full sending, you pay before peak volume arrives; and if replies are flat at Solo volume, a bigger tier just sends more of the same. Annual billing takes 20% off.

How to actually use AISDR

The self-serve route is five steps: request and assign mailboxes on new domains, invite your team and connect LinkedIn, build AI sales personas by answering a short questionnaire, connect your CRM, then launch campaigns from the Campaigns page. AiSDR frames this as minutes from sign-up to sending. The real gate to first value is infrastructure, not the software: new domains need a warmup period before you can safely send at volume, so being "launched" and "producing replies" are not the same milestone.
