Algomo Review (2026)

By Daan Haket · Researched and written with an AI assistant · who does what

The credit model is clear on paper with one opaque step: whether a conversation was 'useful', and so billed at five credits, is decided by a language model after it closes.

Best for

teams that want one AI agent on their own website for support and inbound sales, priced by the work it does rather than by seat, and who want to start self-serve on a free plan.

Skip it if

you need SOC 2 evidence, more than five users or a second agent without an enterprise contract, or you cannot accept the agent going quiet when its monthly credits run out.

Algomo now publishes a complete self-serve price list, and it replaces the single '$200 a month' starting price we had recorded since July. There are three plans. Free is $0 with 100 credits a month, one user, one workspace and one agent, and needs no credit card. Business is a ladder of fixed rungs rather than a slider: 2,000 credits for $50 a month, 4,000 for $100, 8,000 for $200, 16,000 for $400, 25,000 for $600, 40,000 for $950, 60,000 for $1,400, 80,000 for $1,850, 100,000 for $2,250, 150,000 for $3,300 and 200,000 for $4,300, each with up to five users on one workspace and one agent.

A budget between two rungs buys the lower one. Paid yearly, every rung costs ten months: $500 a year at the bottom and $43,000 at the top, which the table shows as a 17% to 28% saving against the $50-per-2,000 base rate once the volume discount (4% at 25,000 credits, rising to 14% at 200,000) is added. Enterprise is 'Contact us' and adds unlimited users and agents, several workspaces, SSO, SLAs, a DPA, a security review and, only when a buyer's procurement asks for it, a contract that runs for a year.

The unit behind every rung is the same on Free and Business: five credits for a conversation the agent helped with, five more for every further ten replies, nothing for a conversation that went nowhere, and a hundredth of a credit for each workflow step that calls an outside service or each knowledge-base lookup that finds something. Algomo's own estimate is that $50 covers about 400 useful conversations a month. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

What the price did

We capture Algomo’s own pricing page every month and keep the figures, so this is our record of the page — not a forecast, and not someone else’s summary.

The price model changed completely between our readings. From July to 3 October 2026 the only published figure, then served from newmode.ai under the Algomo name, was 'Plans start at $200/month', with two quote-only plans, Growth and Enterprise, built around personalised LinkedIn ads and website personalisation. On 22 October 2026 algomo.com/pricing carries a free plan and an eleven-rung Business ladder that starts at $50 a month, so the cheapest paid entry is a quarter of the old floor, and $200 now buys 8,000 credits on a monthly self-serve plan instead of opening a sales call. The newmode.ai pricing page still showed the $200 floor and the Growth and Enterprise cards on the same day, under an Algomo copyright line. Neither site says how the two offers relate; this review prices the algomo.com offer, which is where the vendor link on this page goes.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

Algomo shows the verdict and lets you contest it, but the bill rests on a classifier you do not control. The other edges are deliberate. You cannot buy top-ups, so a traffic spike ends with the agent switched off rather than with an overage invoice; nothing is prorated when you change rungs; and cancelling drops the account to Free, where any balance above 140 credits is trimmed. On security, Algomo states that it has not completed a SOC 2 audit, and conversations go to OpenAI by default, with Anthropic, Google and Mistral models reached through OpenRouter only if you select one.

When the paid plan is worth it

Free is a working product rather than a demo, but it is sized to show whether the agent works, not to run a live site. A hundred credits is about twenty useful conversations a month, the balance can bank no more than 140, and a Free account with no visitor conversation or agent run for 14 days is deleted, agent and all. Algomo's own FAQ spells out the trap in that rule: a paused account counts as quiet, so a Free site that spends its credits in the first days of the month can stay silent for more than a fortnight and be deleted before the credits return.

Business at $50 for 2,000 credits buys three things Free does not: up to five users, a balance that can bank 40% above the plan (2,800 credits on the bottom rung), and Algomo's help connecting systems behind a login and routing escalations to your team. The price per conversation is flat for the first four rungs: up to $400 a month, a useful short conversation costs $0.125 (five credits at $0.025).

The volume discount starts at 25,000 credits and reaches 14% at 200,000, where the same conversation costs about $0.1075. What Business does not buy is a second agent, a second site or single sign-on. Business is one workspace with one agent, so a site that wants a support agent and a sales agent side by side, or a buyer who needs SSO and SLAs in the contract, is on Enterprise, which has no published price.

Annual billing is two months free with the year's credits paid up front. The banking ceiling then becomes 40% above the year's quota rather than a month's, so a busy month can draw on credits saved in quiet ones, which is the main reason to choose yearly beyond the discount.

How to actually use Algomo

Start on Free: no card, one site, one agent. Algomo's claim is that the agent does its own integration work. It finds the data connections your pages already call, and a live lookup such as an order status runs in the visitor's own signed-in browser session with that visitor's permissions, so a pilot needs no back-end access and no IT project. You set it up by talking to it from Claude, ChatGPT, Claude Code, Codex or Cursor, or from the dashboard; changes are staged with a diff, saved as versions that can be restored, and can be previewed as drafts on the live site before they go out.

Testing does not draw on the plan: your own test threads use a separate balance of 200 credits a month on Free and 1,000 on Business. Settle two things in the first week. Read the verdicts: after each conversation closes, a language model decides whether it was useful (five credits) or went nowhere (free), and you see the verdict and its reason on every conversation and can contest it, so check a sample before you trust the bill.

Then decide what should happen at zero. Alerts arrive at 80% and 100% in the app and by email; at zero the agent pauses and the widget comes off your site until the month turns, unless you renew early or move up a rung. Systems that only your staff can reach, and escalation routing, are connected with Algomo's help and only from Business.

Frequently asked questions

What does Algomo cost now?

Free is $0 with 100 credits a month. Business runs from $50 a month for 2,000 credits to $4,300 for 200,000, in eleven fixed rungs, and yearly billing charges ten months for twelve. Enterprise is quoted. Read on algomo.com/pricing on 22 October 2026; until 3 October 2026 the only public figure had been a $200-a-month starting price on newmode.ai with no self-serve plan.

How many conversations does a Business rung buy?

Divide the credits by five for short conversations: 2,000 credits is about 400 useful conversations a month, which is Algomo's own estimate for the $50 rung. A long chat costs five more credits for every further ten replies. Enrichment on Algomo's keys is charged per call: 7 credits to enrich a person or company through Apollo, 2 for an Apollo search, 3 for a LinkedIn profile or company, 1 for an Exa web search. Custom agents add model tokens at 8 to 300 credits per million depending on the model. Conversations that go nowhere, and your own test threads, cost nothing.

Can a traffic spike run up an Algomo bill?

No; the agent stops instead. Algomo sells no top-ups and sends no overage invoice. You are warned at 80% and 100% of your credits; at zero the agent pauses and the widget leaves your site until the month turns. You can renew early, which starts a new month that day with fresh credits on top of what is left, up to the 40% ceiling, or move up a rung. Raising a plan is charged at once and restarts the month; lowering it takes effect at the next renewal and trims any balance above the new ceiling.

Where does Algomo keep and process data?

Per its security page (read 22 October 2026), application servers, database and file storage are hosted in the EU, while the AI model providers may process conversation content outside the EU. Personal data is kept no longer than five years, and deleting a workspace's conversations is requested by email to privacy@algomo.com. The subprocessor list dated 16 October 2026 names Railway, Neon, Amazon Web Services, Clerk, OpenAI, OpenRouter, Stripe and PostHog among others. Algomo has not completed a SOC 2 audit and offers answers to a security questionnaire on request. The contracting party is Algomo Limited, registered in England and Wales, under English law.

Can I use my own Apollo or Exa keys with Algomo?

Yes. With your own provider keys those enrichment steps cost no credits and no execution fee. LinkedIn is the exception and always runs on Algomo's keys, and a cached Apollo result is never charged twice.
