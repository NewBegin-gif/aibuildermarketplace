Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 14 Sep 2026

Plans start at $200 a month with no per-seat pricing - and that single sentence is still the entire public price list, unchanged since our July and August readings. The pricing page carries exactly two plans, Growth and Enterprise, and both buttons say 'Let's chat' and 'Book call': no amount, no contact slider, no self-serve tier. Growth lists 1:1 personalized LinkedIn ads, AI-generated ad copy, website personalization, CRM integration and email support; Enterprise adds unlimited campaigns, custom integrations, a dedicated success manager, priority support and advanced analytics.

What drives the price between the two is not published anywhere on the site, so the only figure a buyer can budget against is the $200 floor. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

When the paid plan is worth it

There is exactly one number on Algomo's site: plans start at $200 a month, with no per-seat pricing. Everything above that starting rate sits behind a book-a-demo call, so the honest answer to whether the paid plan is worth it depends on a conversation you have not had yet. What you CAN reason about before that call is the shape of the model, and the shape is unusually favourable in one specific case.

A flat platform fee behaves in the opposite direction from a per-seat one. At $200 a month, four people using the tool pay $50 each; twenty pay $10; forty pay $5. Per-seat tools do the reverse - they get more expensive exactly as you grow into them. So the more of your revenue team touches the platform, the better this pricing looks, and the worse it looks for a solo founder or a two-person team, for whom $200 a month is simply $200 a month.

Read that with the September 2026 positioning in mind: this is no longer sold as a support chatbot with agents to count, but as a website-to-pipeline system, so the seats you are comparing are marketing and sales seats rather than support agents. What upgrading does NOT solve is the part you cannot price. The step from Growth to Enterprise buys unlimited campaigns, custom integrations, a dedicated success manager, priority support and advanced analytics - and Algomo publishes no figure for any of it, so you cannot compare it with a rival's published tier before the call.

It also does not solve the dependency underneath: the personalization runs from a JavaScript snippet in your header, so the tailored copy and routing rules live exactly as long as the subscription does. Nor does any plan supply the inbound traffic the whole model needs - a tool that converts anonymous visitors is worth nothing if there are few.

How to actually use Algomo

First value depends on which piece you deploy. For the chat agent you sign up, then connect data sources — your documentation and product pages — with no coding. For the website-personalization side (the current newmode.ai focus), you paste a JavaScript snippet into your site header and define targeting rules in the dashboard, much like installing an analytics tag.

Frequently asked questions

What does Algomo cost?

Plans start at $200 a month with no per-seat pricing, and that is genuinely all Algomo publishes — confirmed against their own machine-readable pricing page on 17 August 2026. Anything above the starting rate comes from a demo call.

Why does 'no per-seat pricing' matter?

Because it inverts the usual arithmetic. Most support tools charge per agent, so a team of ten costs ten times a team of one; a flat platform fee does not. At $200 a month the break-even against a $39-per-agent competitor is around five agents — below that the per-seat tools are cheaper, above it Algomo is.

What should I ask on the demo call?

What drives the price above $200, since that is the part not published: conversation volume, channels, languages or integrations. Ask for the number at your current volume and at double it, so you can see the shape of the curve rather than one point on it.

What should I weigh before buying Sales & CRM software?

Most tools in this category plug in rather than replace. Check which direction the data flows and whether the sync is genuinely two-way, because a one-way push creates a second source of truth that your reps will quietly stop trusting. If it does aim to replace your CRM, price the migration honestly: history, custom fields and reporting rarely move across cleanly.
