What Alidrop does

End-to-end dropshipping automation for e-commerce entrepreneurs. Streamline product sourcing, fulfillment, and inventory management.

Pricing (2026)

(re-verified 2026-07-20) Four monthly plans: Starter $39/mo (50 unique / 25 premium products), Professional $59/mo (500 unique / 250 premium), Empire $99/mo (5,000 unique / 2,500 premium), Unicorn $299/mo (25,000 products); $1 for a 7-day trial, no transaction or hidden fees, yearly billing gives up to 8 months off. An optional one-time custom-store build service is available. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

Correction after re-checking: The 7-day trial is not free: it costs $1 for 7 days (a one-time trial charge), then rolls to the plan's monthly rate. The earlier "7-day free trial" and "$1/month for 7 days" wording was wrong per https://www.alidrop.co/pricing (each plan reads "$1 for 7 days, then $XX/month").

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

Alidrop's pitch includes faster-shipping (including US/EU) suppliers, but the core dropshipping economics still apply: you pay the subscription plus wholesale product cost, and margins live or die on product selection and ad efficiency, not the tool. The low Starter product cap nudges serious stores up to Pro quickly, and as with any dropshipping automation, the platform reduces but doesn't remove supplier-quality and shipping-time risk. Sample before you scale.

When the paid plan is worth it

Alidrop has no free tier — the only entry point is a $1, 7-day trial, after which every plan is paid, so the real question is which tier, not whether. The catalog-size ceiling is what forces the decision: Starter caps you at 50 unique products, Professional at 500, Empire at 5,000, and Unicorn at 25,000. Starter pays for itself only while you are validating a handful of products; the moment you run more than roughly 50 live SKUs, or want to scale a store that is already selling, you hit the wall and a higher tier becomes unavoidable. Paying up buys headroom, not capability — the tiers list the same winning-products access, product analyzer, and chat support, so upgrading raises quota, not features.

How to actually use Alidrop

First value comes from installing the Alidrop app from the Shopify App Store (or the WooCommerce connector), linking your AliExpress account, then importing products — either through the dashboard or a Chrome extension that pushes items one at a time while you browse supplier sites. You edit titles, images, and variants, publish to your store, and Alidrop places the supplier order and syncs tracking. Budget your first session for setup and reviewing each listing before you publish, not just clicking import. Distilled from https://www.alidrop.co/pricing

Frequently asked questions

Is Alidrop worth it for a small team?

It depends on whether you have the bottleneck it solves. Small teams get the most out of this category when one clear problem is already costing real hours or revenue; buying ahead of that just adds cost and another login. Price it against the hours or lost deals it removes, not against its feature list, and start on the smallest plan that covers the job.

Who should not use Alidrop?

Skip it if you want the absolute widest catalog at rock-bottom sourcing cost, or your margins can't absorb both the subscription and wholesale product cost. Buying a tool to fix a problem you don't have yet just adds cost and another login to manage.
