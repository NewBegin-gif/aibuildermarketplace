Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 17 Aug 2026

Four monthly plans: Starter $39/mo (50 unique / 25 premium products), Professional $59/mo (500 unique / 250 premium), Empire $99/mo (5,000 unique / 2,500 premium) and Unicorn $299/mo (25,000 products). A 7-day trial costs $1, there are no transaction or hidden fees, and yearly billing gives up to 8 months off. An optional one-time custom-store build service is available. The $1 trial converts to the full monthly rate of whichever plan you picked, and your card stays on file — set a reminder if you are only testing. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

Correction after re-checking: The 7-day trial is not free: it costs $1 for 7 days (a one-time trial charge), then rolls to the plan's monthly rate. The earlier "7-day free trial" and "$1/month for 7 days" wording was wrong per https://www.alidrop.co/pricing (each plan reads "$1 for 7 days, then $XX/month").

The catch

What Alidrop does

When the paid plan is worth it

Alidrop has no free tier — the only entry point is a $1, 7-day trial, after which every plan is paid, so the real question is which tier, not whether. The catalog-size ceiling is what forces the decision: Starter caps you at 50 unique products, Professional at 500, Empire at 5,000, and Unicorn at 25,000. Starter pays for itself only while you are validating a handful of products; the moment you run more than roughly 50 live SKUs, or want to scale a store that is already selling, you hit the wall and a higher tier becomes unavoidable.

Paying up buys headroom, not capability — the tiers list the same winning-products access, product analyzer, and chat support, so upgrading raises quota, not features.

How to actually use Alidrop

First value comes from installing the Alidrop app from the Shopify App Store (or the WooCommerce connector), linking your AliExpress account, then importing products — either through the dashboard or a Chrome extension that pushes items one at a time while you browse supplier sites. You edit titles, images, and variants, publish to your store, and Alidrop places the supplier order and syncs tracking. Budget your first session for setup and reviewing each listing before you publish, not just clicking import.

Frequently asked questions

What happens after the $1 trial?

It becomes the full price of the plan you chose — $39, $59, $99 or $299 a month — seven days after you start, and the card you used stays on file. Those figures were on the page on 17 August 2026 and in our July and August captures before that. The billing complaints on review platforms cluster on exactly this moment, so diary it before you sign up.

Which plan do I actually need?

The plans differ by catalogue size, not by feature class: 50 unique and 25 premium products on Starter, 500 and 250 on Professional, 5,000 and 2,500 on Empire, and 25,000 on Unicorn. Pick on the number of products you will really list, and note that annual billing is advertised as up to eight months off.

Does Alidrop keep supplier prices in sync?

No, and this is the operational risk rather than the sticker price. There is no automatic price sync, so a supplier raising their price does not raise yours — it eats your margin on every order until you notice. If you run thin margins, build a manual price check into your week.

What should I weigh before buying Growth & Revenue software?

It depends on whether you have the bottleneck it solves. Small teams get the most out of this category when one clear problem is already costing real hours or revenue; buying ahead of that just adds cost and another login. Price it against the hours or lost deals it removes, not against its feature list, and start on the smallest plan that covers the job.
