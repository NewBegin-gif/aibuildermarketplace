The catch

The entry is $299 a month — agency-grade automation priced accordingly — and every tier meters on FOUR axes at once (sites, seats, keywords, pages), so growth on any axis forces the upgrade. And the category caveat an honest review owes you: bulk automated on-page edits are powerful, but changes at scale deserve human review — unchecked automated SEO is how sites break.

  • • Independent reviews are close to non-existent at this price point. The case studies are Alli AI's own. That absence of third-party evidence is itself worth weighing.

When the paid plan is worth it

Alli AI has no free tier, only a 10-day trial, so the real decision is which paid plan, and the entry price is steep: the Business plan runs $249/month for 5 sites, 500 keywords and 1,250 pages. It starts paying for itself once you manage several sites and want on-page changes pushed through a single JavaScript snippet instead of routing every title tag and meta description through a developer; the Agency plan at $499/month (15 sites, 2,000 keywords) fits that agency workflow. It does not pay off for a single small site you could hand-edit yourself, where the monthly cost outruns the work saved. Upgrading tiers buys more sites, seats and keywords, but not better judgment. Users report off-target keyword suggestions, an auto-selected focus keyword you cannot set upfront, and false-positive technical flags. More quota does not fix recommendation quality.

How to actually use Alli AI

First value is quick to reach: add your domain, then paste an encrypted JavaScript snippet between your site's head tags. It works across WordPress, Shopify, Wix and others because changes are injected at render time rather than through each CMS. Install runs through a wizard in roughly 15 minutes, after which Alli AI crawls your site and returns on-page suggestions you approve or reject, with approved changes going live immediately. Where people get stuck: the tool auto-picks a focus keyword without asking, and correcting it triggers a full re-analysis one reviewer timed at about 45 minutes. Recommendations arrive in bulk with weak prioritization and are frequently off-target for your niche, with reviewers describing unrelated keyword and backlink suggestions, so you must vet each one rather than bulk-approve. Users also hit interface bugs, such as a task link returning a 404, and false-positive technical flags. Budget your time for reviewing suggestions, not just for installing the snippet.
