What Alli AI does

Agency-grade SEO automation — bulk on-page optimizations, rules and keyword tracking across multiple sites and CMSes from one dashboard.

Pricing (2026)

(2026) 10-day free trial, cancel anytime. Business $299/mo — 5 sites, 5 team members, 500 keywords, 1,250 pages. Agency $599/mo — 15 sites, 15 team members, 2,000 keywords, 5,000 pages. Annual billing saves up to 17%. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

The entry is $299 a month — agency-grade automation priced accordingly — and every tier meters on FOUR axes at once (sites, seats, keywords, pages), so growth on any axis forces the upgrade. And the category caveat an honest review owes you: bulk automated on-page edits are powerful, but changes at scale deserve human review — unchecked automated SEO is how sites break.

Best for: consultants and agencies managing SEO across a portfolio of client sites who want bulk on-page changes, optimization rules and keyword tracking from one dashboard.
Who should skip it: if you run a single site — $299 buys far more capacity than you need, and a one-site SEO suite costs a fraction — or you're not comfortable letting software edit pages in bulk.

The natural comparison is Rank Math — Rank Math optimizes WordPress one site at a time for a fraction of the price; Alli AI is the multi-site automation layer for agencies — pick by whether you manage one property or a portfolio. Decide by which one fits the job above, not by the louder brand.
