The honest knock

Growth on any axis costs extra, though at least the rates are published: from $39 per extra site, $24.99 per 100 extra keywords and $14.99 per 250 extra pages on monthly Business. And the category caveat an honest review owes you: bulk automated on-page edits are powerful, but changes at scale deserve human review — unchecked automated SEO is how sites break. One more thing the pricing page does not answer, and it matters at $299 a month: their own FAQ says your site, CMS and code stay untouched, so where changes are delivered through that snippet they last exactly as long as the subscription does.

Price this as renting an improvement, not buying one, and ask them directly what happens to applied changes after you cancel before you commit a year.

When the paid plan is worth it

Alli AI has no free tier, only a 10-day trial, so the real decision is which paid plan, and the entry price is steep: Business is $299 a month for 5 sites, 500 keywords and 1,250 pages, or $2,990 a year, which the page prints as $249 a month. It starts paying for itself once you manage several sites and want on-page changes pushed through a single JavaScript snippet instead of routing every title tag and meta description through a developer; Agency at $599 a month, or $499 on the annual term, fits that agency workflow.

It does not pay off for a single small site you could hand-edit yourself, where the monthly cost outruns the work saved. Upgrading tiers buys more sites, seats and keywords, plus API access, white label and snippet hosting — but not better judgment. Users report off-target keyword suggestions, an auto-selected focus keyword you cannot set upfront, and false-positive technical flags. More quota does not fix recommendation quality.

How to actually use Alli AI

First value is quick to reach: add your domain, then paste an encrypted JavaScript snippet between your site's head tags. It works across WordPress, Shopify, Wix and others because changes are injected at render time rather than through each CMS. Install runs through a wizard in roughly 15 minutes, after which Alli AI crawls your site and returns on-page suggestions you approve or reject, with approved changes going live immediately.

Frequently asked questions

Why does the same plan show $249 on one screenshot and $299 on another?

Because the pricing page carries two ladders behind a Monthly/Annual toggle, and both are on the page at the same time. Read on 19 September 2026: pay monthly and Business is $299 a month, Agency $599. Commit to a year and the page bills $2,990 and $5,990, which it prints as $249 and $499 a month with "You save $598 per year" and "You save $1,198 per year" beside them. Enterprise is quote-only on both. So $249 is not a lower price for the same terms, it is the annual term. Our own earlier text mixed those two up, which is why the correction note sits above this section.

What happens when I outgrow 5 sites or 500 keywords?

You buy the extra rather than jump a tier, and Alli AI publishes the rates, which is more than most tools in this category do. On monthly Business the page lists extra sites from $39 each, extra keywords from $24.99 per 100 and extra pages from $14.99 per 250. On the annual term those same add-ons drop to $32.50, $20.83 and $12.50. Agency is cheaper per unit again: $29 / $14.99 / $9.99 monthly, $24.17 / $12.50 / $8.33 annually. Do the arithmetic before upgrading — three extra sites on monthly Business is $117, while the step to Agency is $300 and brings ten more.

What does the extra $300 a month buy on Agency that Business does not have?

Three things, and only three, if you go by the feature lists: API access, white label and snippet hosting. Everything else in the Agency column is quota — 15 sites instead of 5, 15 team members instead of 5, 2,000 keywords instead of 500, 5,000 pages instead of 1,250. The engine itself is the same on both: the AI Search Visibility Engine, pre-rendering for 50-plus AI crawlers, the AI bot traffic monitor, LLM Mode, keyword tracking, the site speed optimizer, the link builder, standard reports and a weekly crawl are all listed under Business. Agency is a capacity-and-reselling plan, not a better product.

If I cancel, do the optimizations stay on my site?

The pricing page does not answer it directly, and the closest thing it says points the wrong way for you. Their own FAQ explains that Alli AI "serves AI crawlers a pre-rendered version of your existing pages" and that "your site, CMS, and code stay untouched" — which is the selling point while you pay, and the risk when you stop, because nothing was written into your CMS to survive the cancellation. The FAQ confirms you can cancel any time from the dashboard but says nothing about what happens to applied changes afterwards. Ask them in writing before you commit a year; at $2,990 that answer is the whole purchase.

What should I weigh before buying Marketing software?

Usually yes for the major platforms, but check yours specifically. Tools in this class typically ship native connectors for the big CMS, CRM and email platforms and fall back on Zapier, webhooks or an API for everything else. The practical question is whether your stack is on the native list or needs glue, because a missing native connector normally means either manual work or a paid middleware layer.
