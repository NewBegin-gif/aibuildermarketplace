Pricing (2026)

(2026) 10-day free trial, cancel anytime. Business $299/mo — 5 sites, 5 team members, 500 keywords, 1,250 pages. Agency $599/mo — 15 sites, 15 team members, 2,000 keywords, 5,000 pages. Annual billing saves up to 17%. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

Correction after re-checking: (re-verified 2026-07-23) Live alliai.com/pricing shows three tiers, Business at $249/month, Agency at $499/month, and custom-priced Enterprise, so any older figure listing a lower entry price or a separate small-business tier is out of date.

The honest knock

And the category caveat an honest review owes you: bulk automated on-page edits are powerful, but changes at scale deserve human review — unchecked automated SEO is how sites break. One more thing the pricing page does not answer, and it matters at $299 a month: where changes are delivered through that snippet rather than written into your CMS, they last exactly as long as the subscription does. Price this as renting an improvement, not buying one, and ask them directly what happens to applied changes after you cancel before you commit a year to it.

When the paid plan is worth it

Alli AI has no free tier, only a 10-day trial, so the real decision is which paid plan, and the entry price is steep: the Business plan runs $249/month for 5 sites, 500 keywords and 1,250 pages. It starts paying for itself once you manage several sites and want on-page changes pushed through a single JavaScript snippet instead of routing every title tag and meta description through a developer; the Agency plan at $499/month (15 sites, 2,000 keywords) fits that agency workflow.

It does not pay off for a single small site you could hand-edit yourself, where the monthly cost outruns the work saved. Upgrading tiers buys more sites, seats and keywords, but not better judgment. Users report off-target keyword suggestions, an auto-selected focus keyword you cannot set upfront, and false-positive technical flags. More quota does not fix recommendation quality.

How to actually use Alli AI

First value is quick to reach: add your domain, then paste an encrypted JavaScript snippet between your site's head tags. It works across WordPress, Shopify, Wix and others because changes are injected at render time rather than through each CMS. Install runs through a wizard in roughly 15 minutes, after which Alli AI crawls your site and returns on-page suggestions you approve or reject, with approved changes going live immediately.
