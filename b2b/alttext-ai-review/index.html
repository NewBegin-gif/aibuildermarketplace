AltText.ai Review (2026)

MarketingResearched assessment · by Daniel HaketUpdated 2026-09-25

The headline ‘5¢ per image’ only holds for a plain JPG in one language.

Best for: image-heavy sites that need alt text written back into the CMS automatically: e-commerce catalogues, publishers and long-running blogs on WordPress, Shopify, WooCommerce, Magento, BigCommerce, Contentful, DatoCMS or Storyblok. The native integrations, page crawler, CSV import/export and developer API let you clear thousands of missing alt attributes without touching them by hand.
Who should skip it: if you have a few dozen images. Write those yourself for free and you will describe them better than any model, because you know why each image is on the page. Also skip it if your visuals are diagram-, product- or brand-specific enough that every generated line needs rewriting anyway — in which case the credits buy you a draft you were going to redo.
