The thing to test before you commit a year

Deliverability on exported lists

Reviewers report bounce rates of 20–30% on exported lists. That is high enough to damage your sending domain, and a damaged domain costs more to repair than the subscription costs to cancel.

Data accuracy also drops noticeably outside the US. If your market is European or APAC-heavy, sample a list and verify it independently before signing an annual deal.

What the money buys

The AI-written messages are the reason people pay this. Reviewers say they read as human rather than templated, pulling from LinkedIn profiles, company news and job postings. Customer service rates 5.0 on Capterra — close to unheard of.

Against that: 4.6 on G2 across 598 reviews with 76% five-star and no one- or two-star ratings at all, but 32 reviewers name a steep learning curve, particularly configuring buying signals. Budget onboarding time as well as money.
