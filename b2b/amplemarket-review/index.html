What Amplemarket does

An all-in-one AI sales platform: an AI 'Searcher' that turns a plain-English ICP into verified contacts, 100+ contact-level buying-intent signals, and social prospecting from LinkedIn, plus multichannel outreach and deliverability tooling — drawing on a large multi-source contact database it refreshes continuously. Its Duo copilot proactively surfaces prioritized leads from 20+ real-time signal types, and an MCP server puts the platform inside ChatGPT or Claude — where it can triage the Unibox, update or remove leads, and since July run a whole account-based play end to end: research the account, pull enriched profiles, and enrol every contact into one sequence with copy written per person and per step, without leaving the chat. A GTM Skills Library exposes the rest of the platform the same way. Since June you can also describe a workflow in plain English and have it built for you in the normal editor, and meeting notes from Fathom and Attention flow into the activity feed. Deliverability is a named toolkit rather than a footnote: a Domain Health Center for SPF/DKIM/DMARC, inbox-to-outbox ratio, spam-list and mailbox-age monitoring; automated warm-up and recovery for flagged Gmail or Outlook mailboxes; weekly spam-placement tests before a campaign goes out; and Duo rotating the sending mailbox per email against that same health data.

Pricing (2026)

Premium and annual-only — pricing starts around $600+/mo and is negotiated per account with their sales team (no public monthly option); real costs run higher once you add seats, credits and LinkedIn Sales Navigator (2026). Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

It's premium and built for teams that will use the whole all-in-one stack. The independent picture is genuinely strong overall (around 4.6/5 across ~600 G2 reviews, with repeated praise for unusually human-sounding AI messages, ease of use and hands-on support) — but data accuracy and email deliverability are the most-cited complaints, and they cluster on European contacts (North American data holds up better than EMEA/APAC). Amplemarket points to its own benchmarks showing competitive accuracy, so weigh both sides and test their data against your own target region in a trial before you commit. Under ~10 reps you'll also pay for features you never touch. The genuinely new bit: it now works inside ChatGPT and Claude, and since June that integration can triage the inbox and update or remove leads rather than just search, so reps can run more of the workflow without leaving the chat. Since July that stretches further: from the same chat a rep can research an account, pull enriched profiles and enrol every contact into one sequence with copy written per person and per step, and a GTM Skills Library exposes the rest of the platform the same way. Amplemarket says setup takes under two minutes — that is their figure, not one we timed.

When the paid plan is worth it

There is no free tier. The only published price is Startup: $600/mo on an annual term, covering 2 users, 13,500 email address credits and 600 phone number credits per user per year, 2 mailboxes per user and 27,000 contacts. Growth and Elite are listed as "Custom", so those numbers come from sales, not the site. What you buy by moving up is volume, not a different product. Growth lists 4 users, 35,000 email and 5,000 phone credits per user per year, 4 mailboxes per user and 140,000 contacts; Elite lists 10 users, 40,000 email and 9,600 phone credits, 8 mailboxes per user and 400,000 contacts, and is where Duo Inbox is included rather than sold as an add-on. The upgrade pays back if calling and multi-mailbox sending are your constraint — phone credits going from 600 to 5,000 is the biggest proportional jump in the table. It does not pay back if your problem is data accuracy in a given region, and it does not shorten the annual commitment. Note that Amplemarket's own pricing post states different Startup allowances (15,000 email, 480 phone credits) than the pricing page, and prices phone top-ups at $0.50 each: get your allowance written into the contract.

How to actually use Amplemarket

The vendor's own route to first value is short. Amplemarket University's Getting Started course is 6 videos and about 10 minutes total: connect a mailbox, install the Chrome extension, link LinkedIn, then Searcher, save a search, build a sequence from that saved search, launch it, and watch the sequence overview and tasks page. Their onboarding page says customers can "start producing real results in days" — their claim, not our timing. Where teams stall is the layer underneath. The knowledge base collection for domains and mailboxes is dominated by SPF/DKIM/DMARC recipes, one per registrar-and-mail-host pair (GoDaddy, Cloudflare, Namecheap, Squarespace, Amazon, DNSimple; Google Workspace or Microsoft 365). Amplemarket recommends a minimum of 2 domains for a sales team, and Startup includes 2 mailboxes per user, so DNS work and warm-up time set your real start date, not the software. Some knowledge-base articles, safety settings among them, sit behind a customer login, so you cannot read those limits before signing. Distilled from vendor material only: Amplemarket University (amplemarket.com/university), the knowledge base collection on domains and mailboxes (knowledge.amplemarket.com) and Amplemarket's onboarding page — we found no independent hands-on walkthrough worth citing; the third-party pages that surfaced were competitor blogs or affiliate comparison pages, not independent reviews.
