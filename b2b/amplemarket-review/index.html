What Amplemarket does

An all-in-one AI-powered sales platform. Discover leads, personalize outreach, and close deals faster with intelligent workflows.

Pricing (2026)

Premium and annual-only — starts around $600+/mo, and real costs run higher once you add seats, credits and LinkedIn Sales Navigator. There's no monthly option (2026). Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

Best for: mid-market sales teams (20+ reps) who'll actually adopt the full AI-prospecting, multichannel and deliverability stack in one vendor.
Who should skip it: if you're a small team or solo — Apollo starts around $49/user/mo with a free plan and covers the basics for a fraction of the price.

The honest knock

It's expensive and built for teams that will use the whole all-in-one stack. Independent reviews still flag data-accuracy gaps and occasional deliverability issues despite the marketing, and under ~10 reps you'll pay for features you never touch — a focused data tool plus a standalone sequencer often gets most of the value for far less. The genuinely new bit: it now works inside ChatGPT and Claude, so reps can prospect and prep without leaving the chat.
