(2026-09-17, re-read on their own pricing page on 2026-10-04 and again on 2026-10-21, unchanged) Free $0 with 2 million events a month, permanently, no card required and no time limit. Plus starts at $0 with the first 2 million events free and scales up to 70 million events; a card is required. The plan card prints no rate, but the bill estimator on the same page does: on 17 September and 4 October 2026 it priced 3 million events at $94 a month, 10 million at $720, 30 million at $2,450 and 70 million, the Plus ceiling, at $5,865, with annual billing 20% lower at every step.

Growth and Enterprise are quote-only, both described as event-based pricing. The page states every plan includes the full platform and unlimited seats, and that expanded packages are priced as a percentage of your platform plan. Startups with under $10 million in funding and fewer than 20 employees are offered one free year on Growth, applied for rather than self-served. The published caps that do differ by tier are session replays (10,000 a month on Free and Plus, 20,000 on Growth, 50,000 on Enterprise), active experiments (one on Free and Plus, two on Growth and Enterprise, more as an add-on) and agent sessions (5,000, 10,000 and 20,000).

Plus can also be metered on monthly tracked users instead of events: the comparison table caps Plus at 700K MTU or 70M events, and the page's estimator prices that ladder from $52 a month for 1,000 MTU to $5,338 for 700,000 (read 4 October 2026). Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

The cost lands on events instead, and events are the thing you control least: an instrumentation change or a growth spurt moves your bill without anyone choosing to upgrade. What the plan cards do not print, the page's own bill estimator does, and we read the whole Plus ladder there on 17 September and again, unchanged, on 4 October 2026. What it shows is how little volume buys you: the marginal million events costs $94 a month at the bottom of the ladder and still $86.25 at the 70-million ceiling where Plus stops, and above that ceiling nothing is published.

And 'expanded packages are priced as a percentage of your platform plan' means the add-ons scale with your bill rather than with your usage of them — the more events you send, the more the extras cost, whether or not you use them more. Get the per-event rate in writing before you instrument.

When the paid plan is worth it

The free tier is the reason to look at all: 2 million events a month, no card, no time limit and unlimited seats, and their own FAQ insists it is a plan rather than a trial. Plus is the volume step. It starts at $0 with the first 2 million events free, requires a card, scales to 70 million events, and adds custom events and formulas, 20 behavioural cohorts, alerts, heatmaps and two-year retention.

What Plus does not buy is depth. SSO and project permissions start at Growth; role-based access control is an add-on on Growth and included only on Enterprise, and advanced permissions with SCIM are Enterprise-only. Feature Experiment, Web Experiment, Guides and Surveys, Activation and account-level analytics are each marked "Available for Growth and Enterprise", so paying to leave the free tier does not unlock experimentation or B2B reporting. And neither paid-by-quote tier publishes a rate: Growth and Enterprise both read "Custom, event based pricing", with expanded packages priced as a percentage of your platform plan.

    How to actually use Amplitude

    The docs put four foundation steps before any chart. Create an account to open the workspace, then create a project for the product or environment you want to analyse. Plan the implementation so business questions map to the events you track, and identify users so activity joins up across sessions and devices. Only then does the first workflow start — select the events that represent meaningful actions, build a chart to answer one question or start from a template, and share the result in a space so teammates find the related charts, dashboards and notebooks.

    Amplitude Academy carries a "Getting Started with Amplitude Analytics" course that covers cohorts. The planning step is the one with a bill attached: the tracking plan decides how many events you send, events are the billing unit, and loose instrumentation on the free 2 million is what makes the next tier arrive sooner than expected.

    What does Amplitude cost above the free 2 million events?

    The plan card prints only “Starts at $0”, but the bill estimator on that same page carries the whole Plus ladder, and we read it there on 17 September and again, unchanged, on 4 October 2026. Billed monthly: 3M events $94, 5M $281, 7.5M $495, 10M $720, 12.5M $945, 15M $1,170, 20M $1,575, 30M $2,450, 50M $4,140 and 70M — where Plus stops — $5,865. Billed annually those same steps are $900, $2,700, $4,752, $6,912, $9,072, $11,232, $15,120, $23,520, $39,744 and $56,304 a year, exactly 20% below the monthly rate throughout. Read as a rate it is $94 for the first extra million events and $86.25 at the top of the plan, so growing into Amplitude buys almost no volume discount. Past 70 million events there is no published number at all.

    Is the free plan a real plan or a trial?

    A plan, and Amplitude answers it in its own FAQ: “It's not a trial. There's no time limit.” Free carries 2 million events a month with no credit card and unlimited seats, plus 10,000 session replays, 5,000 agent sessions, 2,000 AI feedback records and one active experiment on each of feature and web experimentation. Read 17 September 2026. The care goes into the step above it rather than the free tier itself: Plus is also badged “Starts at $0” with the first 2 million events free, but it needs a card on file and the meter starts at event 2,000,001. So the question is not whether the free plan expires — it does not — but how tightly your instrumentation holds under 2 million events, because that number is set by how you tag, not by how many people you employ.

    What does paying actually unlock?

    Less than the ladder implies, and the parts most teams upgrade for sit two steps up. Plus buys volume plus custom events and formulas, 20 behavioural cohorts, alerts, heatmaps and two-year retention. SSO and project permissions do not arrive until Growth; role-based access control is an add-on on Growth and included only on Enterprise, and advanced permissions with SCIM are Enterprise-only. The published caps move in single steps too: session replays 10,000 a month on Free and Plus, 20,000 on Growth, 50,000 on Enterprise; active experiments one, then two; agent sessions 5,000, then 10,000, then 20,000. Read on their comparison table on 17 September and 4 October 2026. Both upper tiers read “Custom, event based pricing”, and the same table adds that expanded packages are priced as a percentage of your platform plan — so the extras scale with your bill rather than with your use of them.

    What happens if the free plan goes over 2 million events?

    The account gets blocked, then deleted. Amplitude's own Billing and plans FAQ, read 21 October 2026 (page dated 30 September 2026): exceed the monthly event limit three times and Amplitude blocks the account, so charts and dashboards stop working while admin functions such as the User API stay available for compliance; keep exceeding it without upgrading and the account is deleted six months after the first block. Paid plans get email and in-product alerts at 80, 90, 100 and 110% of the limit instead (Limits and quotas FAQ, same day). Everything ingested counts, including hidden and backfilled events; only blocked or deleted events are excluded, and Identify and Group Identify calls do not count. On Free, block noisy events before the ceiling, not after the first strike.

    What happens on Plus if we go over quota, and how do we cancel?

    Plus is pay-as-you-go. The Billing and plans FAQ, read 21 October 2026, says usage above your included events or MTUs is charged at the same per-unit rate as your plan price: no penalty rate, but no hard stop either. Monthly Plus renews on the first of each month and annual Plus every 12 months; you can cancel any time before the next renewal under Settings > Organization Settings > Plans & Billing > Manage Billing and keep Plus until the period ends. The original purchaser and every org admin can do it. A refund for a part-used period is not mentioned and not confirmed. Seats do not drive the bill: the Manage users page lists unlimited team members on Free, Plus, Growth and Enterprise.

    Is there a cheaper route for an early-stage startup?

    Yes, if you qualify, and the order of steps matters. Per the Billing and plans FAQ read 21 October 2026, the Startups plan gives one year of Growth at no cost to companies with less than $10 million in funding and fewer than 20 employees, with 100 million events or 200,000 MTUs. From Free you apply directly; on monthly Plus you first cancel, wait for the period to end and apply once the account is back on Free; on annual Plus or Growth you email the scholarship team, who cancel the contract and refund the remaining months. After the year you choose Growth or Free, with 30 extra days to trial Growth, and Amplitude grants no extensions past one year.
