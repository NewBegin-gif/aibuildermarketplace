What Amplitude does

Product analytics: funnels, retention and behavioural cohorts, plus session replay and experimentation.

Pricing (2026)

(2026-08-01, read on their own pricing page) Free $0 with 2 million events a month, permanently, no card required. Plus starts at $0 with the first 2 million events free and scales up to 70 million events; a card is required and the page offers an estimator rather than a rate. Growth and Enterprise are quote-only, both described as event-based pricing. The page states every plan includes the full platform and unlimited seats, and that expanded packages are priced as a percentage of your platform plan. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

The honest knock

Unlimited seats is genuinely unusual and worth the credit — you are not taxed for letting the whole team look. The cost lands on events instead, and events are the thing you control least: an instrumentation change or a growth spurt moves your bill without anyone choosing to upgrade. The page gives no rate per event beyond the free 2 million, only an estimator, so you cannot work out what 20 million events costs without entering the funnel. And 'expanded packages are priced as a percentage of your platform plan' means the add-ons scale with your bill rather than with your usage of them — the more events you send, the more the extras cost, whether or not you use them more. Get the per-event rate in writing before you instrument.

What buyers report about Amplitude

Recurring themes from G2, Gartner Peer Insights, Reddit and Hacker News, read in August 2026. We have not run Amplitude ourselves, so this is other buyers' experience rather than ours. We summarise it here because the complaints are usually the part a vendor page leaves out.

What they praise
  • • Non-technical people genuinely get answers out of it, and the dashboards are what reviewers name.
  • • Free Starter tier, and Plus at $49/month annually covers up to 300,000 monthly tracked users.
What they complain about
  • • Expensive at scale is the number one complaint across G2, Reddit and Hacker News — 2 to 5 times Mixpanel for equivalent usage on Growth and Enterprise. Buyers report a median enterprise contract around $64,000 a year.
  • • Pricing is per monthly tracked user, so a viral moment or a seasonal campaign inflates the bill for a month you did not plan.
  • • The full depth still needs engineers: backend event tracking, cross-device identity, schema maintenance. Any non-trivial question becomes a ticket.
  • • Setup time and technical prerequisites are flagged regardless of company size.
Best for: product teams who want everyone looking at the same behavioural data — the free 2 million events a month is a real working tier, not a demo.
Who should skip it: if you need page-and-session web analytics rather than product events, because that is a different tool and much cheaper; also skip it if your event volume is unpredictable and you cannot get a rate card.

The natural comparison is Mixpanel or PostHog — the direct product-analytics peers — compare the per-event rate above the free tier, since that is the only number that moves. Decide by which one fits the job above, not by the louder brand.

Frequently asked questions

Is Amplitude secure and reliable enough to depend on?

Judge it on evidence rather than adjectives. Look for a published security page, single sign-on and role controls on the plan you would actually buy (they are often gated to higher tiers), a status page with real incident history, and a clear answer on where your data is stored. If the tool will hold anything sensitive, those four answers matter more than any feature comparison.

Who should not use Amplitude?

Skip it if you need page-and-session web analytics rather than product events, because that is a different tool and much cheaper; also skip it if your event volume is unpredictable and you cannot get a rate card. Buying a tool to fix a problem you don't have yet just adds cost and another login to manage.
