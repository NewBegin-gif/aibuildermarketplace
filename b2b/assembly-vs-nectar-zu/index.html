Imibuzo Ejwayele Ukubuzwa (FAQ)

Iyiphi entengo eshibhile ku-startups amancane?

I-Nectar Standard ($2.75 ngesisebenzi ngenyanga) intengo eshibhile ku-startups amancane. I-Assembly Core ($2 ngesisebenzi) ishibhile nakakhulu kodwa inekhono elinganiselwe kakhulu.

Iyiphi elula kakhulu ukuyisetha?

I-Nectar elula kakhulu ukuyisetha. Ungakwazi ukuqala usebenze ngaphakathi namahora ambalwa. I-Assembly idinga ukusetha okwengeziwe nokuqeqeshwa.

Ingabe lezi zinhlelo zihlangana ne-Slack kanye ne-Microsoft Teams?

Yebo, zombili i-Assembly ne-Nectar zihlangana ne-Slack ne-Microsoft Teams. I-Assembly inikeza ukuhlanganiswa okwengeziwe nezinye izinhlelo eziningi.

Ingabe zinezimpahla zangempela ezinikeziwe?

Yebo, zombili zinikeza izimpahla zangempela. I-Nectar inezitolo zempahla ezihlelwe kahle, kuyilapho i-Assembly inikeza izinketho eziningi zezimpahla nezimpahla ezizenzekelayo.
