What Atlas AI Store Builder does

Shopify app that builds a full store or product page from a single product link — layout, copy and images generated automatically, plus bundle and cart upsells. $39/month covers 5 pages, $1,000 of bundle revenue and 250 orders; past that the meter tracks your revenue.

Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 19 Aug 2026

(prices read 2026-08-18 on apps.shopify.com/dropshipt and helloatlas.io) The listing says “Free to install”, but that is the development-store tier: a real store runs on the All-in-One plan at $39 a month, billed in USD with a 7-day free trial — their own partners page says 5 days, so check which you get. What $39 buys is capped rather than unlimited: 5 published pages, $1,000 of bundle revenue, 250 cart orders and 50 AI photos a month.

Their pricing page states it plainly: “you’ll be charged the base plan fees and given access to features to a limit, any additional uses for those features can be purchased as shown in the per feature pricing”. The App Store listing adds that “additional charges apply as your store generates revenue and impact”. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

The meter runs on your success, not your usage. $1,000 of bundle revenue and 250 cart orders is a rounding error for a store that is actually working. So the $39 headline describes the bill of a store that has not started selling yet. Price the tier you expect to be on in month three, not the one you sign up on. Two other things worth knowing before you install.

The rating is 4.7 from 389 reviews, but 17 of those are one star and the recent ones are about billing rather than the product: one seller writes “cancelled it, uninstalled it, but still getting charged” after two months. And the listing carries a line Shopify does not require of most apps: “Atlas must not be used to copy, duplicate, or reproduce products, product listings, images, or store content from any website, or marketplace without the explicit permission of the rights holder”. That tells you what a paste-a-product-link builder gets used for.

When the paid plan is worth it

There is only one paid plan, so the real question is not which tier but what the tier leaves open. All-in-one is $39 a month and the listing adds that further charges apply as your store generates revenue and impact, without publishing what those charges are. That is an open end on the only price they do quote, and it is the number to ask about in writing before you install.

The free tier is for development stores only, so it is a sandbox rather than a trial; the actual trial is 7 days. Read on the App Store listing on 19 August 2026, where the rating is 4.7 from 390 reviews -- but note the shape of that average: 338 five-star ratings sit beside 17 one-star ones, and the one-star reviews visible on the day we read them describe being charged after cancelling and uninstalling.

A high average with a heavy tail at the bottom is worth more attention than the average alone.

How to actually use Atlas AI Store Builder

Install on a development store first, which is free and lets you see what the AI actually produces before any billing starts. Paste one product link and compare the generated store against what you would have built: the speed is real, the question is whether the copy and layout survive your own editing. Watch two counters from day one rather than at renewal — published pages (5) and cart orders (250) — because those are what convert a flat $39 into a variable bill.

If you cancel, cancel inside Shopify and check the next invoice: the recurring one-star theme in the reviews is charges continuing after uninstall, and Shopify billing for apps is easier to dispute early than late.
