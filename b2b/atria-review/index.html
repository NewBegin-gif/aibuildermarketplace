Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 17 Aug 2026

Core is $129/mo, a stated $360 saving a year. That covers 5 seats, 4,000 AI credits a month, 50 brands followed without AI insights, 5 connected ad accounts, $500K of ad spend analysed a month, 5GB of asset storage and 3 brand profiles. Plus $479/mo (a stated $1,440 saving): 8 seats, 10,000 credits, 100 brands with AI insights, 10 ad accounts, $1M of ad spend, 1TB storage, 8 brand profiles.

Business $959/mo (a stated $2,880 saving): unlimited ad spend. Enterprise is custom. Extra seats are $20 each a month on every tier. Note the step from Core to Plus is far steeper than the tier names suggest — it is roughly 3.7x the price for 2.5x the credits. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

The catch

It's credit-metered — AI credits and brand-tracking slots cap what you can generate and monitor, so a heavy user burns through Core's allowance and steps up to Plus. And it's specialized for Meta/TikTok ad creative research and generation, so it's a performance-marketing tool, not a general design suite. Its edge is competitor ad intelligence plus creative generation in one place; if you only need one of those, a cheaper single-purpose tool may fit.

What Atria does

Competitor ad research and AI creative generation for Meta and TikTok in one platform. Credit-metered: Core is about $159/month ($129 annual) with 4,000 AI credits and 50 brands tracked, and heavy users step up to Plus at roughly twice that.

What buyers report about Atria

Recurring themes from the buyer-review sites listed at the foot of this page, read in August 2026. We have not run Atria ourselves, so this is other buyers' experience rather than ours. We summarise it here because the complaints are usually the part a vendor page leaves out.

What they praise
  • • A 25M+ ad library with semantic search and an AI agent trained on over $5B of ad spend — for creative research that corpus is the product.
  • • Radar analytics with letter-grade creative scoring gives a concrete read rather than a vague one.
What they complain about
  • • 2.6 out of 5 across 7 Trustpilot reviews (read 25 August 2026), with billing complaints and support problems the pattern. Seven reviews is far too small a sample to lean on, but the theme is consistent enough to check your own cancellation route first.
  • • There is no video generation, and multiple users flag it as a gap they expected to be covered.
  • • Core starts around $129-$159 per cycle, Plus $479, Business $959. Solo creators name the entry price as the barrier.

When the paid plan is worth it

Atria's entry point is free but thin: the homepage offers "No credit card required" with "1,000 free credits included", while the help centre separately documents a trial lasting 7 days from signup that does ask for a card for verification. Neither is a working tier. The real decision is Core against Plus. Core, listed at $129/mo on annual billing, gives 4,000 AI credits / month, "Follow 50 brands, no AI insights", "Connect 5 ad accounts" and a $500K monthly ad-spend ceiling.

Plus, at $479/mo on annual billing, adds 10,000 credits, "Follow 100 brands + AI insights", "Connect 10 ad accounts" and $1M spend. Upgrading pays back where the AI layer on tracked brands earns its keep, or where the spend cap binds — cross $500K and you have no choice. What it does not fix is the meter itself. Credits "don't carry over to the next month", and generation, transcription, URL analysis and Radar all draw on the same pool, so a heavy generator on Plus still buys top-ups. Custom metrics sit above Plus, on Business.

How to actually use Atria

First value comes from connecting Meta, not from browsing the library. Open the workspace menu, go to Settings > My Connections, click Connect next to Meta, approve the Pages and Businesses, and check that the review screen lists both ads_management and business_management. Then add the account under Ad accounts in the Analytics & Launch sidebar. TikTok populates once authorised.

Common Atria setup mistakes

Where Atria setups actually go wrong, and what it costs you when they do. Same sources as the setup notes above — not from a walkthrough we ran ourselves.

Where people stall: data is not there on day one.

  • The vendor writes "First-time sync can take a few hours — this is normal", and only treats it as broken "If after 24 hours data still isn't showing up" — their timing, not ours.
  • Permissions are the other wall.
  • If your Meta Business Manager role is too narrow you hit "Launch Permission Not Granted", and an admin has to grant you "Manage ad accounts", or at minimum Manage campaigns.
  • Scope catches people too: Atria says "Launching ads to TikTok directly from Atria is not available yet", and the Instagram DM-to-save flow can show "Coming soon" during an Instagram API change, leaving the Chrome extension as the fallback.

Frequently asked questions

What does Atria cost?

On annual billing, read on 17 August 2026: Core $129 a month, Plus $479 and Business $959, with Enterprise on quote and extra seats at $20 each. Atria advertises annual as 20% off. Those figures were identical in our July and August captures, so a quote you were given earlier this summer should still hold.

What do you get for the jump from Core to Plus?

Ask this before upgrading, because the step is steep: $129 to $479 a month is about 3.7x the price for 2.5x the AI credits (4,000 to 10,000), double the brands followed (50 to 100), twice the ad accounts and twice the ad spend ceiling ($500K to $1M). The feature that actually changes rather than scales is AI insights on the brands you follow, which Core does not include at all. If insights are the reason you are buying, Core is not the plan.

What is missing that people expect?

Video generation. Multiple users flag it as a gap they assumed would be covered by a creative-intelligence platform, and it is the thing to check against your own workflow before you commit. The ad library and semantic search over it are the part reviewers rate; producing the creative is not what this tool does.

What should I weigh before buying Growth & Revenue software?

It depends on whether you have the bottleneck it solves. Small teams get the most out of this category when one clear problem is already costing real hours or revenue; buying ahead of that just adds cost and another login. Price it against the hours or lost deals it removes, not against its feature list, and start on the smallest plan that covers the job.
