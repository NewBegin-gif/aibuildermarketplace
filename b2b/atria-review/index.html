Atria Review (2026)

Atria is credit-metered — AI credits and brand-tracking slots cap what you can generate and monitor, so a heavy user burns through Core's allowance and steps up to Plus.

Best for

performance marketers and creative teams that want competitor ad research and AI ad-creative generation for Meta and TikTok in one platform.

Skip it if

you don't run paid social at volume, or you only need ad inspiration (a cheaper ad-library tool) or only design (a general creative tool).

Frequently asked questions

What does Atria cost?

On annual billing, read on 7 October 2026: Core $129 a month, Plus $479 and Business $959, with Enterprise on quote and extra seats at $20 each. Billed monthly the same plans cost $159, $599 and $1,199. Atria labels annual billing 'Save 20%'; worked out, that is 18.9% on Core and 20% on Plus and Business. The annual figures were the same in our July and August captures.

What do you get for the jump from Core to Plus?

Ask this before upgrading, because the step is steep: $129 to $479 a month is about 3.7x the price for 2.5x the AI credits (4,000 to 10,000), double the brands followed (50 to 100), twice the ad accounts and twice the ad spend ceiling ($500K to $1M). The feature that actually changes rather than scales is AI insights on the brands you follow, which Core does not include at all. If insights are the reason you are buying, Core is not the plan.

What is missing that people expect?

Video generation. Multiple users flag it as a gap they assumed would be covered by a creative-intelligence platform, and it is the thing to check against your own workflow before you commit. The ad library and semantic search over it are the part reviewers rate; producing the creative is not what this tool does.

Who can see billing, and who needs a paid seat?

Only workspace Admins see billing, change plans, top up credits and cancel. Members use the whole workspace but cannot manage ad accounts, team membership or billing (Atria Accounts and Admin Roles, 7 September 2026, and Manage billing and plans, both read 7 October 2026). Guests, who get view-only access to the ad accounts you pick, are a Business-plan feature. A reporting snapshot, a shareable link to a frozen report, uses no seat and needs no login, so a client who only needs results does not need a seat. Core, Plus and Business include 5, 8 and 15 seats; each extra seat is $20 a month. Atria recommends keeping at least two Admins: if the only Admin leaves, billing and ad accounts stay frozen until Atria has verified ownership.

What happens if I downgrade, cancel or want a refund?

Upgrades are self-serve and switch on at once. A downgrade is arranged through support and takes effect at the end of the current billing period; you keep your features and credit balance until then. Only Admins can cancel, and some plans route you to a short call before the cancellation completes. Refunds are reviewed case by case; the cases Atria names as most often approved are a charge after cancelling and a double charge (Manage billing and plans; How to cancel your subscription or request a refund; read 7 October 2026). What you can export when you leave is not described in the help centre: not confirmed.

What should I weigh before buying Growth & Revenue software?

It depends on whether you have the bottleneck it solves. Small teams get the most out of this category when one clear problem is already costing real hours or revenue; buying ahead of that just adds cost and another login. Price it against the hours or lost deals it removes, not against its feature list, and start on the smallest plan that covers the job.
