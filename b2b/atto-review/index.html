What Atto does

Time tracking and timesheets for hourly and mobile teams. Track hours, breaks and locations with GPS and turn timesheets into payroll-ready reports.

Pricing (2026)

Per-user (2026): from about $6.95/user/mo (Starter) up to ~$19.95/user for tiers with GPS, kiosk mode and API; unlimited jobs/locations on all plans; free trial, annual discounts. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

Simple and honest pricing — the real conversation with any field-team tracker is the human one: GPS tracking of employees needs transparency and often consent (rules differ by country), and adoption dies the day the crew feels surveilled rather than served. Roll it out as 'accurate paychecks and fewer timesheet fights', involve the team, and the tool pays for itself; roll it out as surveillance and no feature list saves it.

What they complain about
  • • You are billed per user even for a manager who only views hours and never tracks their own. Reviewers use the word greedy about it — count your seats accordingly.

When the paid plan is worth it

Atto has no free tier: after the trial you pick from a paid ladder, and the gate that matters is GPS. Starter, $6.95 per user/month billed monthly or $5.95 billed annually, covers clock-in/out, job details and overtime calculations but not real-time GPS tracking. That starts on Plus, $9.95 monthly or $7.95 annually. If you came to Atto for GPS-verified timesheets, which is the reason most field crews look at it, Starter is not the product you had in mind and Plus is the honest entry price. Advanced at $12.95 monthly adds the tablet Kiosk, mileage tracking and job-specific pay rates. Expert at $19.95 monthly, $16.55 annually, is mostly service — priority support, personalized onboarding — plus API access, so it only pays back if you actually need to push data somewhere Atto does not already export to. Upgrading fixes none of the things that break rollouts: every phone still needs continuous location permission, and the crew still has to accept being located. Annual billing saves 20% on every tier.

How to actually use Atto

Sign up at dashboard.attotime.com, fill in name, company, industry and password, and the 14-day free trial starts at signup with no card. The documented order is: invite the team from Settings > Invite New Members, by email or by sharing the account link; set company settings for location tracking, workweek and overtime rules, breaks and mileage; then assign Admin, Manager or Employee roles and pay rates under Settings > Manage Team. Atto says you can invite first and tune the rest later, so a crew can be clocking in the same day. Distilled from Vendor documentation only, all from Atto's own Zendesk help centre (help.attotime.com). Specifically: the "Atto Pricing Plans" article (tier names, prices, per-tier feature lists) for the paid ladder; the "Getting Started" category, in particular the "Initial Setup Guide", for the signup-to-first-clock-in sequence and the 14-day trial; "Common Atto Clock-In Issues and Solutions" for the iOS/Android location-permission wording, the Job Code requirement and the job-site boundary block; "Why 'Location Permissions OFF' Appears in Team Activity & How to Solve It" for the manager-side symptom; "Setting Up the Atto Kiosk" for the iPad/Android tablet requirement; "Downloading Timesheet Reports (Web and Mobile)" for PDF/CSV export. Common Atto setup mistakes

Where Atto setups actually go wrong, and what it costs you when they do. Same sources as the setup notes above — not from a walkthrough we ran ourselves.

The friction is on the phone, not the dashboard.

  • Atto's clock-in troubleshooting lists iOS needing Always or While Using the App with precise location on, and Android Allow all the time or while-in-use, depending on whether your account runs continuous or clock-in-only tracking.
  • Miss it and managers just see Location Permissions OFF in Team Activity.
  • Other quiet blockers: an account can require a Job Code before clock-in, and job-site boundaries will refuse a clock-in from outside the perimeter.
Best for: hourly and mobile crews (construction, cleaning, field service) that want dead-simple clock-in/out, GPS-verified timesheets and payroll-ready exports.
Who should skip it: if office/knowledge teams (project-based trackers like Toggl fit better), or anywhere the team hasn't been brought along on why.

Frequently asked questions

Does Atto replace my current setup or sit on top of it?

Usually it sits on top rather than replacing everything. Tools in this class tend to become the layer where work is tracked and handed off, while your existing systems stay the source of truth for their own data. Before you buy, map which tools it would replace outright and which it would merely sync with. If the answer is that it syncs with all of them, you are adding a layer rather than removing one.

Who should not use Atto?

Skip it if office/knowledge teams (project-based trackers like Toggl fit better), or anywhere the team hasn't been brought along on why. Buying a tool to fix a problem you don't have yet just adds cost and another login to manage.
