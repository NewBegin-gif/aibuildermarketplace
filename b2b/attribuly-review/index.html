Attribuly Review (2026)

Growth & RevenueResearched assessment · by Daniel HaketUpdated 2026-09-13

This is the most expensive app in its category on the Shopify store by a wide margin, and the jump is not gradual — free to $450 a month with nothing in between.

Best for: stores already running Klaviyo at volume, where a few percent more recovered carts is worth $450 a month and the identity-resolution layer has enough traffic to work with.
Who should skip it: if your monthly email revenue is below roughly $5,000 — the entry price eats the gain — or you do not run Klaviyo, since the product is built around it.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

It is also an add-on rather than a replacement: the pitch is explicitly that it recovers carts “Klaviyo misses” and syncs identity signals back into Klaviyo, so you are paying for this on top of an email platform you already pay for. Work out what $450 a month needs to recover before it breaks even, and compare that against simply improving the flows you already have.

What Attribuly does

Recovers abandoned carts Klaviyo misses — identifies visitors, syncs intent signals back into Klaviyo and lets an AI agent send a personalised recovery email. Free until $1,000 of recovered revenue, then $450 or $500 a month.

What buyers report about Attribuly

Recurring themes from the buyer-review sites listed at the foot of this page, read in August 2026. We have not run Attribuly ourselves, so this is other buyers' experience rather than ours. We summarise it here because the complaints are usually the part a vendor page leaves out.

What they praise
  • • The attribution itself is what merchants credit: reviewers describe finally getting a clear view of where customers actually come from, and re-weighting their channel spend on the back of it rather than on platform-reported numbers.
  • • Cost against the alternatives comes up explicitly — a merchant of more than two years says it costs far less than Triple Whale and Hyros for the same job.
  • • Support is described as fast and hands-on through setup, with the account manager named in several reviews.
  • • The newer email-capture side is credited with faster list growth, which is the part of the product closest to what the app is now named for.
What they complain about
  • • Setup is real work, and the Klaviyo side is where it bites: one merchant reports having to duplicate several Klaviyo flows to make the integration behave, and the vendor's own reply says the Klaviyo integration is being improved.
  • • The absolute price is high enough that reviewers question the return — the complaint is not that the data is wrong but that the monthly cost has to be earned back before the insight pays for itself.

Where the ratings disagree. Everything public sits on one platform. The Shopify App Store listing carries 155 reviews at 4.8 and is also where the vendor replies, and no comparable body of buyer reviews exists elsewhere for this app. That is a thin base for a tool whose paid plans start in the hundreds per month, so weigh the setup complaints heavily: they are the part a trial will show you and the pricing question is the part it will not.

When the paid plan is worth it

The gap between the two paid plans is $50 a month and it decides whether you own the product this app is named after. Capture, at $450, identifies anonymous visitors, enriches US emails and syncs those identities into Klaviyo, Meta and Google -- it finds people. Recapture, at $500, is the plan that actually recovers the abandoned carts Klaviyo misses, adds server-side tracking, Omnisend alongside Klaviyo, AI attribution across both views and clicks, and carries the 4x ROI guarantee.

Paying $450 to identify shoppers you then have to act on yourself is the worse of the two deals by some distance. Both come with a 14-day trial. Read on the App Store listing on 19 August 2026; rating 4.8 from 154 reviews.

How to actually use Attribuly

Use the free tier deliberately, because it is a real test rather than a demo: it runs until $1,000 of recovered email revenue, which is enough to measure the lift against your existing Klaviyo flows. Record your baseline recovery rate before you install, not after, or you will have nothing to compare against. When the free tier ends you will have the one number that matters — how much revenue it recovered per month — and $450 either clears that or it does not.
