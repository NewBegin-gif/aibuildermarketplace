What Aura does

Dynamic repricing for Amazon and Walmart sellers — automated price strategy across marketplaces.

Pricing (2026)

(2026-08-01, read on their own pricing page) Flat per account, not per seat, with unlimited listings on every tier. Starter $37/month ($451 a year), Essential $77 ($931), Plus $157 ($1,891), Pro $237 ($2,851) — annual saves 20%. The ladder is set by monthly sales volume, not by features: Starter up to $15K, Essential up to $50K, Plus up to $250K, Pro up to $1M. Marketplaces included: 2, 3, 4 and 6. Aura AI covers 250, 500, 1,000 and unlimited listings; Hyperdrive 10, 25, 50 and 100 allocations; user seats 1, 2, 5 and unlimited. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

The tier is decided by how much you sell, not by what you need. That is unusually honest as a model — a seller doing $10K a month is not subsidising one doing $900K — but it means your bill moves when your business does, in the same month, without you choosing to upgrade. Pass $15K and Starter stops being the plan you are on. Work out which band your best month lands in rather than your average, because that is the one that will trip it. Two smaller things: the marketplace count is a real cap (two on Starter, so Amazon plus one), and the AI listing allowance is per listing rather than per repricing action, so a large catalogue eats it faster than a busy one.

Frequently asked questions

Does Aura work with my store platform?

Check your platform and version specifically. Shopify support is close to universal in this category, while WooCommerce, BigCommerce, Magento and headless setups vary a lot, and 'supported' sometimes means a generic API rather than a maintained app. A listing in your own platform's app store, with recent reviews there, is a better signal than the vendor's logo wall.

Who should not use Aura?

Skip it if your catalogue is small or your prices rarely need to move — repricing pays back on competition and volume, and at low volume the $37 is buying automation you could do by hand. Buying a tool to fix a problem you don't have yet just adds cost and another login to manage.
