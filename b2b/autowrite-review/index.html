When the paid plan is worth it

The tiers differ on volume, not on capability, with one exception worth knowing about. Every step multiplies posts, images and workflows by roughly three: 10 to 30 to 100 to 300 posts a month. The one real feature gate sits between Free and Starter, and it is the gate that decides whether this is a tool or a toy. Brand voices and auto-publish are Starter-and-up, so on the free plan you generate drafts and paste them yourself, in whatever voice the model picked.

Above Starter you are only buying volume, plus generation priority on Pro and support priority on Business. Do the arithmetic on your actual publishing rhythm before stepping up. At $29 for 100 posts you pay about 29 cents a post, at $79 for 300 about 26 cents — a discount of roughly a tenth, which only pays for itself if you genuinely publish at that rate. And the meter is per month, so an unused allowance is gone.

How to actually use AutoWrite

Because there are no reviews to learn from, do the checking yourself and do it early. Generate three posts on subjects you actually know, and read them for invented specifics rather than for style: AI writing tools fail on facts long before they fail on grammar. Check what the free plan meters on before you build a publishing schedule around it, since the listing does not say. And keep the drafts somewhere outside the app, because a young app with no reviews is also an app with no track record for staying online.
