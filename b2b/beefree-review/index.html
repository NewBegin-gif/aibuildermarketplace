Beefree Review (2026)

Growth & RevenueResearched assessment · by Daniel HaketUpdated 2026-07-29

Growth tools only earn their keep if they move a real number — leads, conversions or revenue — not just activity. So where does Beefree actually fit?

The honest bottom line. Worth it for agencies and in-house teams shipping email at volume across brands or ESPs. Skip it once solo senders whose ESP editor already copes.

What Beefree does

RGE Studio (formerly Beefree — renamed 14 May 2026): drag-and-drop email and landing-page design. Build responsive, on-brand emails fast and export clean HTML to any major ESP, no coding required. Beefree remains the company name and the name of its separate developer SDK; the builder itself now lives under reallygoodemails.com.

Pricing (2026)

(verified 21 Jul 2026 on reallygoodemails.com/pricing) Sold as RGE Studio since the 14 May 2026 rebrand. Starter free — up to 10 designs, 6 exports/mo, landing-page builder, custom subdomain, 20GB/mo image traffic, unlimited users but no roles or permissions. Professional $25/mo billed yearly ($30/mo monthly) — unlimited designs and folders, unlimited reusable blocks, 2 editing users plus 4 view-only, AI writing assistant. Business $134/mo billed yearly ($160/mo monthly) — 2 editing users plus 10 view-only, 2 workspaces, approval workflows, advanced brand controls, QA assistant. Enterprise: custom, quoted by sales. The free trial is 15 days on the Business plan. The embeddable Beefree SDK is a separate product on its own price list (developers.beefree.io) and is not covered by these numbers. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

The honest knock

Read the seat row before the price row: Professional and Business both cap at two full editing seats, so a "mid-sized team" plan at $134/mo annual still buys two people who can actually build — everyone else lands in view-only slots (four on Professional, ten on Business) or an Enterprise quote. The free Starter is the odd one out: unlimited users, but with no roles or permissions at all, and exports metered at six a month rather than the unlimited free tier its old reputation suggests. And the naming is a live tripwire: since 14 May 2026 the builder is RGE Studio at reallygoodemails.com, while Beefree remains the company name and the name of the separate developer SDK — so check which product a plan or invoice actually refers to. It is purely a creation environment that never sends, so it always sits on top of your ESP: worth it when the ESP's native editor is the bottleneck (agencies, multi-brand teams, ESP migrations), duplicated effort when it isn't.

When the paid plan is worth it

The pricing page lists Starter at $0, Professional at $30/mo ($25/mo billed yearly), Business at $160/mo ($134/mo billed yearly), and Enterprise on request. Starter caps you at 10 stored designs, 3 custom templates and 6 exports per month, and stamps a "Designed with RGE Studio" footer on every design, so Professional pays back quickly for anyone shipping more than a handful of emails a month. The steepest jump in the table is Professional to Business: $30 to $160 for co-editing, a custom domain on published landing pages, four simultaneous review requests per workspace, 10 HTML imports a month instead of 4, and 1 TB of CDN traffic instead of 250 GB. That is an approval-and-governance purchase, not a design-quality one; the builder itself is the same. Upgrading does not buy seats either: both paid tiers include 2 member seats, and extra members cost $15/month on Professional and $35/month on Business. Nor does any tier send email — RGE Studio builds and exports, so an ESP stays a separate bill.

How to actually use Beefree

The vendor docs describe a short path: log in, land on the Projects page, click Create new, and choose a blank email, a template from a catalog of 1,600+ templates, a page, or a folder — folders prompt Starter users to upgrade. After saving, the details page is where you send a test or export via connector, raw HTML, ZIP or PDF. Starting a Professional or Business trial is a six-step sequence from the Subscription page and needs no payment details. The friction comes later. Publishing pages on your own domain is Business-and-up: it needs a CNAME pointing to bdn.beefree.io and, if you run a CAA record, an entry allowing letsencrypt.org, after which the domain sits pending with no timeline the docs will commit to, and unconfirmed requests are deleted after 15 days. Migration is rationed too — 4 HTML imports a month on Professional, 10 on Business, 1 MB per file, with background images, dividers and menus unsupported — and Unlayer JSON conversion is a manual support-ticket service. Best for: agencies and in-house teams shipping email at volume across brands or ESPs — a dedicated builder with approvals, brand controls and one-click push to Salesforce Marketing Cloud, Pardot and HubSpot, sitting above whatever you send with.
Who should skip it: if solo senders whose ESP editor already copes — you would be paying for a second tool to do a job your sending platform already does, and you still need the ESP because RGE Studio sends nothing itself.

The natural comparison is Stripo or your ESP's own editor — Stripo is the head-on builder rival — compare template depth, export connectors into YOUR sending stack, and how many real editing seats each plan actually gives you. Decide by which one fits the job above, not by the louder brand.
