FAQ

How much does Beehiiv cost per month?

Beehiiv costs $0/month for Launch (up to 2,500 subscribers), $39/month for Grow (up to 10,000 subscribers), and $99/month for Scale (up to 100,000 subscribers). Enterprise plans start around $500/month with custom pricing based on subscriber count and feature requirements.

Does Beehiiv charge overage fees?

Yes, Beehiiv charges $0.30 per additional 100 subscribers on paid plans when you exceed your tier limits. For example, if you have 12,000 subscribers on the Grow plan (10K limit), you'll pay an extra $6/month for the 2,000 additional subscribers.

Is there a free version of Beehiiv?

Yes, Beehiiv offers a free Launch plan supporting up to 2,500 subscribers with basic newsletter features, automation, and analytics. However, it includes Beehiiv branding and lacks advanced features like A/B testing, premium integrations, and priority support.

What's the difference between Beehiiv Grow and Scale plans?

Grow ($39/month) supports 10K subscribers with basic automation and polls, while Scale ($99/month) supports 100K subscribers and adds advanced segmentation, A/B testing, custom domains, 3D analytics, and referral programs. Scale also includes priority support and advanced integrations.

Can you negotiate Beehiiv Enterprise pricing?

Yes, Beehiiv Enterprise pricing is negotiable. Companies with 100K+ subscribers can often secure 15-30% discounts through annual contracts, volume commitments, or bundled services. Custom feature development and dedicated support are also negotiable for large accounts.

What Each Plan Actually Includes

Launch Plan ($0/month) - Real Feature Breakdown

The free Launch plan supports up to 2,500 subscribers and includes:
  • Newsletter editor: Basic drag-and-drop editor with standard templates
  • Website builder: Simple landing pages with Beehiiv subdomain
  • Analytics: Basic open rates, click rates, and subscriber growth
  • API access: Limited to 1,000 requests per month
  • Branding: "Powered by Beehiiv" footer required
  • Support: Community forum only, no direct support
What's missing: Automation sequences, A/B testing, custom domains, advanced analytics, and priority support.

Grow Plan ($39/month) - Mid-Tier Analysis

The Grow plan targets newsletters with 2,500-10,000 subscribers:
  • Automation: Welcome sequences, drip campaigns, trigger-based emails
  • Polls & surveys: Interactive content with response analytics
  • Custom signup forms: Embeddable forms with basic customization
  • Integrations: Zapier, Stripe, basic e-commerce connections
  • Support: Email support with 24-48 hour response time
  • Branding removal: Clean newsletters without Beehiiv footer
Real usage scenario: A newsletter with 8,000 subscribers sending 4 emails monthly pays $39/month base cost. If they grow to 12,000 subscribers, they pay an additional $6/month overage fee.

Scale Plan ($99/month) - Advanced Features

The Scale plan serves growing publications with serious monetization goals:
  • A/B testing: Subject lines, send times, content variations
  • Advanced segmentation: Behavioral targeting, custom fields, dynamic content
