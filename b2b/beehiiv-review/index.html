What Beehiiv does

Newsletter platform with growth, automation, SEO and monetisation tooling, priced by subscriber count. You keep 100% of paid subscriptions where Substack takes 10%; the trade is a steeper start than Substack's near-zero setup and built-in discovery.

Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 16 Aug 2026

Subscriber-based, and you keep 100% of paid subscriptions — beehiiv takes 0%, against Substack's 10%, leaving only Stripe's 2.9% + $0.30. Launch is $0 up to 2,500 subscribers with unlimited sends. At up to 1,000 subscribers Scale is $49/mo billed monthly or $517 a year (about $43/mo), Max $109/mo or $1,151 a year (about $96/mo). At 10,000 subscribers Scale is $109/mo or $1,151 a year; at 50,000 it is $249/mo or $2,629 a year.

The ladder tops out at 100,000 subscribers ($329/mo Scale, $459 Max) and Enterprise is custom above that. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

What they complain about

When the paid plan is worth it

beehiiv's Launch plan is $0 per month and covers up to 2,500 subscribers, and it already includes custom domains and unlimited email sends, so the free tier is a real publishing tier rather than a demo. At the pricing page's default setting of 1,000 subscribers with annual billing selected, Scale is $517 billed annually ($43 per month) and Max is $1,151 billed annually ($96 per month); those per-month figures are the annual-billing rates, not the month-to-month price.

The biggest jump in the table is Launch to Scale, because that is where monetization moves. The Ad Network, paid recommendations and a 0% take rate on paid subscriptions are all Scale-and-above. The help center states the Ad Network is available to anyone on the Scale plan or above who is actively sending. Scale to Max is a narrower step: removing beehiiv branding, up to 10 publications, unlimited seats, priority support and a sponsorship storefront.

Upgrading does not solve list size: both Scale and Max stop at 100,000 subscribers, above which beehiiv requires Enterprise. It also does not solve deliverability or domain setup, which is the same DNS work on every tier.

How to actually use Beehiiv

beehiiv's own platform walkthrough sets the order: create and confirm the account, then set up the workspace and publication; each post moves through 5 tabs in the Post Builder — Compose, Audience, Email, Web and Review.

Common Beehiiv setup mistakes

Where Beehiiv setups actually go wrong, and what it costs you when they do. Same sources as the setup notes above — not from a walkthrough we ran ourselves.

The friction is not in that list.

  • A custom domain means adding 12 DNS records, and beehiiv says verification can take up to 72 hours and warns against removing or re-adding those records while it runs; Cloudflare users must set each record to DNS Only, since proxied records cannot be detected.
  • A valid DMARC record has been mandatory for custom domains since February 2024, and DNS changes can take 24-72 hours to fully propagate.
  • Migration is the other slow part: beehiiv's Substack guide puts content migration at 5-10 minutes and the Stripe paid-subscriber transfer at 5-15 minutes, but recommends excluding subscribers with an activity rating of 0 for the first 2-4 weeks of sending.
  • The free trial sits on a Launch account with temporary higher-tier access, and locks the Ad Network, paid subscriptions, community and additional users; no trial length is stated.

The natural comparison is Substack — the simpler, network-driven newsletter platform. Weigh it against Substack on the job you actually need done.

Frequently asked questions

What are the limits on beehiiv's free plan?

Launch is free with up to 2,500 subscribers and unlimited email sends, and beehiiv states no credit card is required. On the free tier you get one team member, three publications, custom domains, and API access excluding the Send API; monetisation, automations and the ad network start on Scale. Read on 16 August 2026.

What does beehiiv cost once I pass the free tier?

At up to 1,000 subscribers Scale is $49 a month billed monthly, or $517 for a year — about $43 a month. Max is $109 monthly or $1,151 a year, about $96 a month. The price then steps with list size rather than sliding: 10,000 subscribers puts Scale at $109/mo, 50,000 puts it at $249/mo.

Does beehiiv take a cut of paid subscriptions?

No. beehiiv states 0% take rate on paid subscription revenue on every paid plan; what you lose is Stripe's processing fee of 2.9% + $0.30 per transaction. That is the difference their own pages draw against Substack's 10%.

What should I weigh before buying a tool like this?

It depends on whether you have the bottleneck it solves. Small teams get the most out of this category when one clear problem is already costing real hours or revenue; buying ahead of that just adds cost and another login. Price it against the hours or lost deals it removes, not against its feature list, and start on the smallest plan that covers the job.
