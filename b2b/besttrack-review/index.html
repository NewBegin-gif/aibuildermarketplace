We tested it · 22 November 2026

Our AI assistant ran the test on 22 November 2026 on a Shopify development store, with the interface in English. We installed BestTrack on the free plan, fulfilled a test order with UPS's public sample tracking number and looked at the dashboard, billing page, notification settings, dropshipping mode and CSV export. We turned no emails on, upgraded nothing and removed the app afterwards.

How we tested

  1. Our AI assistant ran the test in the Shopify admin and the app in the browser, with the interface in English; nothing was paid
  2. The notes record screens and wording; screenshots are shown
  • Install: the permission screen lists customer data, staff data and store data such as orders. The app then opened straight on its dashboard, with no plan screen, no charge and no card. The dashboard said 300 free tracked orders every month, renewing monthly and not a one time trial.
  • Tracking: after we fulfilled the test order, it showed up in BestTrack within about 10 seconds with UPS detected. Within about a minute the status was Delivered, with the carrier's full history.
  • Warning gap: that history ran from a pickup on 12 June 2025 to a delivery on 5 December 2025, for an order placed on 16 November 2026. The app showed it as Delivered and gave no warning.

Frequently asked questions

How much does BestTrack cost?

The Shopify App Store listing, read on 27 November 2026, shows a free plan with 300 tracked orders a month and Pro at $9 a month for 1,000 tracked orders, then $0.03 per extra tracked order. In our test the free plan showed no base fee, and our one test order counted as 1 of 300 at $0.00.

What happens when I pass the 300 free orders?

The help centre says new orders pause and the dashboard shows a usage warning until the monthly reset or an upgrade. We did not reach the limit in our test.

Does it find the carrier by itself?

In our test it did. We fulfilled an order with UPS's sample number and the app detected UPS and showed the carrier history within about a minute.

Does it warn about a wrong or reused tracking number?

Not in our test. The sample number carried a history from 2025 and the app showed it as Delivered for an order placed in November 2026, with no warning.

Are customers emailed automatically?

Not until you switch the emails on. In our test every status email was disabled by default, and mail sends from BestTrack's own address until you verify your domain. The help centre says it supports email only, not SMS.

Can I export my tracking data?

Yes. In our test a CSV with 19 columns, including tracking number, status, pickup, fulfilment and delivery dates and the customer email, downloaded at once.

What do we not know?

How the customer facing tracking page looks, how the emails read, how dropshipping mode behaves on a real shipment and what Pro adds in practice, because our test store is password protected and we ran only the free plan.
