Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 13 Oct 2026

Amazon PPC is sold in three tiers, and every one of them is a monthly fee plus an unspecified percentage of connected ad spend. They are Self Service Platform €495/month, Managed Platform €1,995/month and Managed Service €4,995/month. The tiers differ mainly in how much of a human you get.

Frequently asked questions

Can I manage several Amazon accounts or clients under one subscription?

By default, one Amazon account per region. The pricing page FAQ (bidx.io/en/pricing, read 13 October 2026) says agencies and users with multiple accounts should contact support. BidX's knowledge-base article on managing multiple accounts as an agency (go.bidx.io, read the same day) describes Administrator, Account Manager and Advertiser roles with Full Access, View Only or No Access per page, but neither page says what extra accounts cost, so that is not confirmed.

Can I cancel after a few months?

Not on a normal plan: the pricing page (read 13 October 2026) says plans are based on an annual commitment paid monthly. The FAQ offers new customers a paid three-month proof-of-concept period first, and cancellation itself is done under Settings > Billing & Payments > Manage Subscription. Prices are net, with VAT added.
