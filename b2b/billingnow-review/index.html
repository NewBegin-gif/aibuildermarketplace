What BillingNow does

Invoicing and expense tracking with AI — unlimited invoices, expenses, clients and users on one flat $9.99/month plan, made in Geneva.

Pricing (2026)

(2026) One flat plan: $9.99/mo for unlimited invoices, expenses and clients with an AI assistant, no tiers and no per-user fees. A 3-day free trial (cancel anytime) converts to automatic billing, so set a reminder the day you sign up. SOC 2 Type II certified with European-standard compliant invoices; the site publishes no per-tier pricing page above the single flat plan. (re-verified 18 Jul 2026) Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

The honest knock

The pricing is genuinely refreshing — one flat plan, no tiers, unlimited users. Two honest watch-outs: the trial is just THREE days and converts to automatic billing (set a reminder the day you sign up), and it’s a young product from a small Geneva maker — weigh ecosystem depth and longevity before migrating your billing history in.

How to actually use BillingNow

You sign up with an email address and sign in either through Continue with Google or by clicking a magic link emailed to you with the subject Sign in to BillingNow; the docs say you never need a password. First login forces a two-step company wizard: step one takes organization name, tax ID, address, default currency and timezone; step two takes a logo (PNG or JPG, max 2MB), an invoice model, a primary colour and standard text before and after the line items. Common BillingNow setup mistakes

Where BillingNow setups actually go wrong, and what it costs you when they do. Same sources as the setup notes above — not from a walkthrough we ran ourselves.

The friction sits after that.

  • Relations are created individually; the help centre documents CSV and Excel export but no import, so an existing client list must be retyped.
  • Bank accounts and tax rates live in Organization Settings rather than in the wizard, so IBAN and tax-rate setup is a separate trip after the wizard finishes.
  • Receipt scanning is not hands-off: you upload a PDF or image, click Analyze with AI, then review or edit the extracted fields.
  • The homepage advertises Stripe-powered payment links, but the Payments article documents only manually recorded payments.

Frequently asked questions

Does BillingNow work with my accountant's setup?

Ask your accountant before you buy; it is the cheapest step in this whole decision. What matters is whether it exports in the format they already work in, whether the ledger mapping survives that export, and whether they can access it directly. A tool your accountant has to re-key by hand costs more than it saves.

Who should not use BillingNow?

Skip it if you need accountant-grade features, payroll or deep integrations (the incumbents’ ecosystems are bigger), or you dislike auto-billing after a 3-day trial. Buying a tool to fix a problem you don't have yet just adds cost and another login to manage.
