Detailed Pricing Breakdown: Real Numbers for Real Decisions

Bitvavo employs a maker-taker fee model that rewards liquidity providers while maintaining competitive rates for all users. Understanding these fees is crucial for calculating the true cost of automated strategies.

Trading Fees:

  • Maker fees: 0.15% (orders that add liquidity to the order book)
  • Taker fees: 0.25% (orders that remove liquidity from the order book)
  • Volume-based discounts available, dropping to 0.05%/0.15% for monthly volumes exceeding €2.5 million

Deposit and Withdrawal Costs:

  • SEPA bank deposits: Free
  • SEPA bank withdrawals: Free
  • Cryptocurrency withdrawals: Network fees only (typically €1-15 depending on blockchain congestion)
  • No deposit fees for cryptocurrency transfers

Real-World Cost Example: A founder implementing a €10,000 monthly DCA strategy across Bitcoin and Ethereum would pay approximately €25 in taker fees per month (0.25% of €10,000). Over a year, total fees would be €300 on €120,000 in purchases—a 0.25% annual cost that's significantly lower than traditional investment management fees.

API Usage: Bitvavo provides generous API rate limits at no additional cost: 1,000 requests per minute for REST API calls and unlimited WebSocket connections. This allowance supports sophisticated automation strategies without triggering usage fees that plague other platforms.

Staking Rewards: Bitvavo offers staking services for proof-of-stake cryptocurrencies, with yields ranging from 3-12% annually depending on the asset. These rewards are automatically compounded, providing passive income on holdings without manual intervention.
