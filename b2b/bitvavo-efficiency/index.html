Pricing Breakdown and Cost Analysis

Understanding Bitvavo's fee structure proves essential for calculating integration costs and ongoing operational expenses:

Trading Fees

Bitvavo employs a maker-taker fee model with volume-based discounts:

  • Tier 1 (€0 - €1,000 monthly volume): 0.25% maker, 0.25% taker
  • Tier 2 (€1,000 - €2,500): 0.20% maker, 0.25% taker
  • Tier 3 (€2,500 - €10,000): 0.15% maker, 0.25% taker
  • Tier 4 (€10,000 - €25,000): 0.10% maker, 0.20% taker
  • Tier 5 (€25,000 - €100,000): 0.05% maker, 0.15% taker
  • Tier 6 (€100,000+): 0.03% maker, 0.10% taker

For a B2B SaaS processing €50,000 monthly in crypto transactions, fees would total approximately €75-100 per month at Tier 5 rates, significantly lower than traditional payment processors charging 2.9% + €0.30 per transaction.

Deposit and Withdrawal Costs

SEPA deposits and withdrawals remain free for EUR transactions, while cryptocurrency withdrawals incur network fees:

  • Bitcoin: 0.0005 BTC (approximately €15-25 depending on BTC price)
  • Ethereum: 0.005 ETH (approximately €10-20)
  • Stablecoins (USDC/USDT): €2-5 depending on network congestion

These withdrawal fees align with industry standards and prove reasonable for business operations requiring periodic fund movements.

API Access Costs

Standard API access comes free with basic accounts, while professional API access requires verification and minimum trading volumes. No additional subscription fees apply for API usage within rate limits, making integration cost-effective for most B2B applications.

Total Cost Example

Consider a SaaS platform processing €25,000 monthly crypto volume with 10 withdrawals:

  • Trading fees (Tier 5): €37.50 (0.15% average)
  • Withdrawal fees: €150 (10 × €15 average)
  • Monthly total: €187.50
  • Annual cost: €2,250

This represents a 0.75% total cost on transaction volume, competitive with traditional payment processing when factoring in reduced chargeback risks and faster settlement times.
