Detailed Pricing Breakdown for B2B Operations

Trading Fees Structure

Bitvavo uses a maker-taker fee model with volume-based discounts. For B2B operations, understanding these fees is crucial for cost optimization:

  • Tier 1 (€0 - €100,000 monthly volume): 0.25% maker fee, 0.25% taker fee
  • Tier 2 (€100,000 - €500,000): 0.20% maker fee, 0.24% taker fee
  • Tier 3 (€500,000 - €1,000,000): 0.15% maker fee, 0.22% taker fee
  • Tier 4 (€1,000,000 - €2,500,000): 0.10% maker fee, 0.20% taker fee
  • Tier 5 (€2,500,000+): 0.05% maker fee, 0.18% taker fee

For a B2B SaaS company processing €500,000 monthly in crypto transactions, this translates to approximately €1,100 in monthly trading fees at Tier 3 rates. Compared to traditional payment processors charging 2.9% + €0.30 per transaction, the cost savings become substantial at scale.

Deposit and Withdrawal Costs

SEPA bank deposits and withdrawals are free for EUR transactions, a significant advantage for European B2B operations. Cryptocurrency deposits are free, while withdrawal fees vary by asset:

  • Bitcoin: 0.0005 BTC (approximately €15-20)
  • Ethereum: 0.003 ETH (approximately €6-10)
  • Stablecoins (USDC, USDT): €2-5 depending on network

For businesses making frequent crypto payments to suppliers or customers, these fixed withdrawal fees provide predictable cost structures compared to percentage-based alternatives.

API and Integration Costs

API access is included at no additional cost, unlike some enterprise platforms that charge monthly API fees ranging from €500 to €2,000. This inclusion makes Bitvavo particularly attractive for startups and scale-ups building crypto-integrated products without substantial upfront infrastructure costs.
