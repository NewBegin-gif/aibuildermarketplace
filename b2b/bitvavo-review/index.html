Bitvavo Review (2026)MiCA-Regulated, 0.25% Taker Fee

Financial OperationsResearched assessment · by Daniel HaketUpdated 2026-09-25

The honest caveats are crypto-wide, not Bitvavo-specific: fees are only part of the cost (spread matters on small orders), and no exchange removes market risk — the platform being cheap and MiCA-regulated doesn't make the assets safe. Throughout this review we weigh it against Coinbase.

Best for: EU-based traders and builders who want a MiCA-regulated, euro-native exchange with low tiered fees, deep EUR pairs and a solid API for automation.
Who should skip it: if you're outside the EU (it's euro-centric), you trade exotic altcoins it doesn't list, or you need derivatives-first infrastructure.

Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 18 Aug 2026

No subscription — you pay per trade. EUR markets (Category A) run ten volume tiers. It is 0.15% maker / 0.25% taker below €100,000 of 30-day volume, then 0.10%/0.20% from €100,000, 0.09%/0.18% from €250,000, 0.08%/0.16% from €500,000 and 0.05%/0.14% from €1,000,000.

Frequently asked questions

What does trading actually cost on Bitvavo?

Read on their fees page on 18 August 2026: on EUR markets you start at 0.15% maker and 0.25% taker, and the schedule steps down through ten volume tiers — 0.10%/0.20% from €100,000 of 30-day volume, 0.05%/0.14% from €1m, 0.00%/0.08% from €10m and 0.00%/0.02% above €100m. USDC markets run on their own schedule from 0.05% maker and taker. Funding is where the other cost hides: SEPA, iDEAL/Wero and Bancontact deposits are free, but a credit card costs 1% and PayPal 2% — more than the trade itself at the entry tier.

Can I run a trading bot on it, and what does the API require?

Yes, and the friction is documented rather than hidden. Bitvavo's own docs put the default rate limit at 1,000 weight points per minute and require an operatorId field on every create, update and cancel order request, so a bot written before that requirement gets rejected. Keys are made under Settings > API with an IP whitelist and a choice of six permissions, two-factor authentication has to be on first, and the secret is shown once and cannot be retrieved later. None of that changes with volume.

Do stablecoin trades help me reach a cheaper fee tier?

No. The fees page states that trades on stablecoin pairs do not contribute to the fee-schedule 30-day volume, and prices them on a separate Category C schedule starting at 0.10% maker and taker rather than on the EUR schedule. High stablecoin turnover can leave you paying entry-tier rates on everything else.

What should I weigh before buying Financial Operations software?

The bar here is higher than features. Check who actually holds the money and under what licence, whether client funds are segregated, what audit trail and permission controls you get, and how support behaves when something goes wrong mid-transaction. Anything touching payments should be able to answer those in writing; if it cannot, that is your answer.
