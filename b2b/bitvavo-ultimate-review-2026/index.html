Risk Management and Compliance Considerations

B2B cryptocurrency adoption requires robust risk management frameworks. Key considerations include:

Market Risk: Cryptocurrency volatility can significantly impact treasury values. Limit allocations to amounts the company can afford to lose entirely (typically 5-15% of cash reserves). Implement stop-loss orders for larger positions and maintain sufficient fiat reserves for operational needs.

Operational Risk: Private key management becomes critical for business operations. Consider multi-signature wallets for larger holdings and implement clear procedures for key backup and recovery. Regular security audits and employee training reduce human error risks.

Regulatory Risk: Cryptocurrency regulations continue evolving across jurisdictions. Maintain detailed records of all transactions, work with crypto-experienced accountants, and stay informed about regulatory changes in operating jurisdictions.

Counterparty Risk: While Bitvavo provides insurance coverage, exchange failures remain possible. Diversify across multiple platforms for larger holdings and consider self-custody solutions for long-term positions.
