Four tiers, each paid one with a 7-day free trial. Development Stores is free but only for development and partner stores; it carries all Pro features and starts billing on upgrade. Basic $9.99 a month or $83.92 a year, a 30% saving: enforcement inside the checkout page, unlimited country blocking, a fully customisable blocked page and online support. Advanced $16.99 a month or $142.71 a year adds unlimited IP blocking for IPv4 and IPv6, an IP whitelist and a geolocation country redirector.

Pro $19.99 a month or $167.92 a year adds blocking by email, name, address and phone, the Bot Killer for TOR, proxy and VPN traffic, an add-ons blocker, a right-click blocker and unlimited visitor analytics traffic logs. All three paid tiers carry the same restriction in their own plan text: all stores except for Shopify Plus stores. All charges are billed in USD and recur every 30 days.

Developer Effective Apps, 13 Max Nordau, Ashdod, Israel; the app launched 7 April 2021, carries Shopify's “Built for Shopify” badge, is available in eleven languages, and had 324 reviews at the time of writing, 5% of them one-star. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

The App Store header says a free plan is available; that free plan is Development Stores, for development and partner stores only, and it starts charging the moment the store goes live. All three paid tiers then carry the line 'all stores except for Shopify Plus stores', so if you are on Plus this app is not sold to you. Check that with the developer before you plan around it.

A blocker is a blunt instrument, and the review distribution shows it: 324 reviews, 89% of them at the top rating and 5% at the bottom — fifteen one-star reviews. Rules that stop fraud also stop customers. A blocked country catches the expat who genuinely wanted to buy; a VPN rule catches the privacy-minded shopper. The tuning is yours to do, continuously, using visitor analytics that only the Pro tier includes.

And it prevents rather than recovers. Nothing here disputes a chargeback that has already landed or claws back an order that already shipped. For that you need a separate dispute tool, which is a second subscription on top of this one.

What Blocky does

Shopify fraud filter that blocks visitors and orders by country, IP (v4 and v6), email, name, address and phone, with an AI bot blocker for TOR, proxy and VPN traffic that gets past reCAPTCHA, country redirects, a right-click blocker and visitor analytics. The free tier is for development stores only; live stores run $9.99 for country blocking, $16.99 with IP blocking or $19.99 for the identity and bot filters, 30% off annually. Not sold to Shopify Plus stores.

What buyers report about Blocky

Recurring themes from the buyer-review sites listed at the foot of this page, read in August 2026. We have not run Blocky ourselves, so this is other buyers' experience rather than ours. We summarise it here because the complaints are usually the part a vendor page leaves out.

What they praise
  • • The numbers merchants quote are the concrete part: over 4,800 blocked hits for a US store a year in (July 2026), and over 5,000 bots inside a week for a Spanish store (April 2026).
  • • Card-testing fraud is the specific win. A US soap maker seven months in (July 2026) credits it with stopping card testing, and another US store (August 2026) values being able to block a particular person who had tried to defraud them rather than only a country.
  • • It reads cheap against the category: a US lighting store (June 2026) switched from a competitor at $139 a month and rates both the blocking and the support higher.
What they complain about
  • • It is not airtight against a determined evader, and the sharpest reviews come from the longest-tenured merchants. A US store two years in (September 2025) had blocked every country except the US and switched VPN blocking on, and still took fraudulent orders from blocked countries — paying the non-refundable card processing fee on each one they cancelled. A Canadian store three years in (July 2025) reports bots still getting through and the right-click protection not working at all, and a Pakistani merchant (February 2026) says VPN traffic beat it on all three of their stores.
  • • Feature gating irritates people on install day: two merchants (July 2025 and August 2026) uninstalled within the day or the week because each capability they wanted sat on a higher tier. The developer replied to one of them that country blocking is included in Basic.
  • • The advertised free plan is the other install-day complaint (Australia, December 2025; US, July 2026): merchants expected one and found only paid options. That matches the listing rather than contradicting it — the free tier is for development and partner stores, and it starts billing when the store goes live.
  • • One US merchant (January 2026, seven days in) says they were charged close to $200 in the first week and asked for a refund. That does not square with published tiers of $9.99 to $19.99 a month and the developer left no public reply, so read it as unexplained rather than as the rate.

Where the ratings disagree. 324 reviews averaging 4.7, and the distribution says more than the average does: 89% at five stars, 5% at one, and almost nothing in between. That is the signature of a blunt instrument. For most stores the rules work on the first day, which is when a lot of the five-star reviews are written; for the minority facing someone who rotates IP addresses and VPNs it does not, and those reviews are written years in. Decide which of the two you are before you read the average.

When the paid plan is worth it

Ten dollars separates the cheapest paid tier from the dearest, and that is the whole pricing story. Basic at $9.99 blocks countries. Advanced at $16.99 adds IP blocking and an IP whitelist. Pro at $19.99 adds the parts most people install a fraud filter for: blocking by email, name, address and phone, and the Bot Killer aimed at TOR, proxy and VPN traffic that reCAPTCHA lets through. So the tiers do not scale with your size, they scale with how many ways you want to say no.

If your problem is genuinely one region, Basic is the honest answer and it is cheap. If your problem is bots placing orders or one determined person changing IP addresses, Basic and Advanced will not touch it and you are on Pro, where the difference from Basic is ten dollars a month — less than the fee on a single chargeback. Annual billing takes 30% off at every level: $167.92 against $239.88 for a year of Pro.

How to actually use Blocky

Trial the tier that matches your actual problem, not the cheapest one. If bots or repeat fraudsters are the issue, the plan you want to test is Pro, because the Bot Killer and the email, phone and address filters do not exist below it. Then spend the seven days watching before you block. Turn the visitor analytics on first and read a week of your own traffic — country, IP, browser — so your first rules come from your logs rather than a hunch about which country is trouble.

Block the narrowest thing that fixes it: one IP range before one country, one email domain before all of them. Write every rule down with the reason you added it, and re-read that list once a month. The reason blockers collect angry reviews is that nobody remembers the rule that quietly turned away real customers for a year.
