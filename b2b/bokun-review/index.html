What Bokun does

Booking management for tours and activities, by Tripadvisor. Manage availability, channel sales and online bookings in one place — built for experience businesses.

Pricing (2026)

(2026) A free version plus paid plans from about $49/mo (Start). Service fee on direct bookings varies by plan (~1.5% Start, 1.25% Plus, 1% Premium); crucially, zero Bokun booking fee on Viator reservations. Free trial available. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

Bokun is a Tripadvisor/Viator company, and that's both the draw and the lock-in: distribution to Viator and 2,600+ resellers is excellent, but you're building your booking backbone inside one OTA's ecosystem, and the per-booking service fee on direct sales is a real cut on top of the subscription. If most of your business is direct, weigh that fee; if you live on OTA distribution, the Viator advantage is hard to beat.

What buyers report about Bokun

Recurring themes from Capterra, GetApp and Software Advice, read in August 2026. We have not run Bokun ourselves, so this is other buyers' experience rather than ours. We summarise it here because the complaints are usually the part a vendor page leaves out.

What they praise
  • • 1.5% booking fees are the lowest in the category, Viator bookings carry none, and offline bookings are never charged. From $49/month.
  • • It genuinely centralises bookings, availability, channel management and payments in one place.
What they complain about
  • • It charges a service fee on cancelled bookings, on top of payment processing. On a high-cancellation product that is a real cost.
  • • OTA synchronisation fails in ways that cost money — reviewers report Viator showing slots as unavailable when they were free, losing bookings.
  • • Support response times are the most frequent complaint.
  • • The interface and the pricing-catalogue setup are both described as time-consuming to learn.
  • • Long-term users report pricing for new customers has doubled, narrowing the cost advantage.

When the paid plan is worth it

Bókun's pricing page lists FREE, START, PLUS and PREMIUM. FREE covers booking management and manual bookings and is capped at 1 user. START is $49 per month plus a 1.5% fee for applicable bookings, up to 5 users. PLUS is $149 per month plus 1.25%, up to 10 users. PREMIUM is $499 per month plus 1%, with unlimited users. The decisive step is FREE to START, because selling online via booking widgets, OTAs and the Marketplace only starts at START; if you take bookings on your own site, that first $49 is not optional. The largest jump in the table is PLUS to PREMIUM, $149 to $499, and it buys the least visible return: the fee drops from 1.25% to 1%, subvendors go from 5 to 15, and free Viator submission checks from 24 per year to 48 per year. Upgrading does not make Bókun your merchant of record: the help center states Bókun does not process payments directly on the platform, and that you have to invoice OTAs and Marketplace resellers yourself to get paid.

Common Bokun setup mistakes

Where Bokun setups actually go wrong, and what it costs you when they do. Same sources as the setup notes above — not from a walkthrough we ran ourselves.

The friction is elsewhere.

  • Bókun does not process payments, so taking money online means registering separately with a supported provider such as Braintree, Stripe or PayPal; the help center adds that you do not need a payment provider at all if you accept payment upon arrival on one of your booking channels.
  • Only one Viator account can be connected to one Bókun account, products in Viator must already be active and set to accept bookings instantly, and from initiation the docs say products are fully connected within 24 hours.
  • New Viator listings still go through a Launch Assist evaluation with a non-refundable 29USD submission fee.
  • Pointing your own domain at a Bókun website needs A records to 35.172.94.1 and 100.24.208.97 plus a www CNAME, and can take up to 48 hours.
Best for: tour, activity and experience operators who want booking management plus wide OTA distribution (Viator, GetYourGuide and more) from one dashboard.
Who should skip it: if you're a tiny operator taking a handful of direct bookings where the fees and setup outweigh the reach, or you want to stay off OTA rails entirely.

The natural comparison is FareHarbor or Rezdy — close tour-booking peers — compare direct-booking fees and how much you rely on Viator/Tripadvisor distribution. Decide by which one fits the job above, not by the louder brand.

Frequently asked questions

Does Bokun replace my current setup or sit on top of it?

Usually it sits on top rather than replacing everything. Tools in this class tend to become the layer where work is tracked and handed off, while your existing systems stay the source of truth for their own data. Before you buy, map which tools it would replace outright and which it would merely sync with. If the answer is that it syncs with all of them, you are adding a layer rather than removing one.

Who should not use Bokun?

Skip it if you're a tiny operator taking a handful of direct bookings where the fees and setup outweigh the reach, or you want to stay off OTA rails entirely. Buying a tool to fix a problem you don't have yet just adds cost and another login to manage.
