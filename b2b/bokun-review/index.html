Bokun Review (2026)

Operations & WorkflowResearched assessment · by Daniel HaketUpdated 2026-09-25

Bokun is a Tripadvisor/Viator company, and that's both the draw and the lock-in.

Best for: tour, activity and experience operators who want booking management plus wide OTA distribution (Viator, GetYourGuide and more) from one dashboard.
Who should skip it: if you're a tiny operator taking a handful of direct bookings where the fees and setup outweigh the reach, or you want to stay off OTA rails entirely.

When the paid plan is worth it

Bókun's pricing page lists FREE, START, PLUS and PREMIUM. FREE covers booking management and manual bookings and is capped at 1 user. START is $49 per month plus a 1.5% fee for applicable bookings, up to 5 users. PLUS is $149 per month plus 1.25%, up to 10 users. PREMIUM is $499 per month plus 1%, with unlimited users. The decisive step is FREE to START, because selling online via booking widgets, OTAs and the Marketplace only starts at START; if you take bookings on your own site, that first $49 is not optional.

The largest jump in the table is PLUS to PREMIUM, $149 to $499, and it buys the least visible return. The fee drops from 1.25% to 1%, subvendors go from 5 to 15, and free Viator submission checks from 24 a year to 48 — which the page values at $696 and $1,392, so $4,200 of extra annual subscription buys $696 of listed checks plus a quarter-point off your fee.

Upgrading does not make Bókun your merchant of record: the help center states Bókun does not process payments directly on the platform, and that you have to invoice OTAs and Marketplace resellers yourself to get paid.

Common Bokun setup mistakes

Where Bokun setups actually go wrong, and what it costs you when they do. Same sources as the setup notes above — not from a walkthrough we ran ourselves.

The friction is elsewhere.

  • Bókun does not process payments, so taking money online means registering separately with a supported provider such as Braintree, Stripe or PayPal.
  • The help center adds that you do not need a payment provider at all if you accept payment upon arrival on one of your booking channels.
  • Only one Viator account can be connected to one Bókun account, products in Viator must already be active and set to accept bookings instantly, and from initiation the docs say products are fully connected within 24 hours.
  • New Viator listings still go through a Launch Assist evaluation with a non-refundable 29USD submission fee.

Frequently asked questions

Can I actually sell on the free plan?

Not in the ordinary sense. Read the comparison table rather than the FREE card: on 20 September 2026 the free plan carries two ticks in the whole table — manage availability, and import products directly from Viator. The 'Book now' button for direct online bookings, access to the Marketplace of OTAs and resellers, Google Things to do, the website builder, the mobile app, gift cards, payment links and point of sale are all marked absent on FREE. So the free plan is a back office for bookings that reach you through Viator, not a way to take a booking of your own. If you want a booking button on your site, the entry price is $49 a month plus 1.5%.

Does the free plan really carry zero Viator fees?

Bokun's own pricing page says both things on 20 September 2026 and does not reconcile them. The FREE card lists 'Zero fees on Viator' under what is included. Further down the same page the comparison row '0% booking fees on Viator bookings' is blank on FREE and ticked on START, PLUS and PREMIUM. The banner over the cards promises 'zero Bokun fees for Viator and offline bookings' in general, while the START card lists 'Zero fees on offline bookings' as something START adds over FREE. Get the answer in writing before you build a business on the free tier. And note the exception that is in none of these places: Channel Manager customers, who connect their own reservation system, pay 0.5-1.5% on Viator reservations.

Is the step up to a lower booking fee worth the higher subscription?

Only at real volume, and the page gives you everything you need to work it out. START is $49 a month plus 1.5% of applicable bookings; PLUS is $149 plus 1.25%. That is $100 more a month for a quarter of a percentage point, so the move pays for itself at $40,000 a month in applicable bookings and not before. PLUS to PREMIUM is $350 more for the same quarter point: $140,000 a month. Below those lines you are buying features, not a cheaper fee — Zapier, webhooks and the allocation manager at PLUS, unlimited users and priority support at PREMIUM. And any paid plan lets you pass the booking fee to the guest, which changes the arithmetic entirely. Prices read on bokun.io/pricing, 20 September 2026; the break-even figures are ours.

Is there anything in the paid plans with a published dollar value?

One row, and it is easy to miss. Viator product submission checks are compulsory once you are listed on Viator, and Bokun does not charge for them: 12 a year on START, 24 on PLUS, 48 on PREMIUM, none on FREE. The page prices those allowances itself — $348, $696 and $1,392 a year — which works out at $29 a check. If you submit a dozen products a year to Viator, that row alone covers more than half of START's $588 annual cost. Read on bokun.io/pricing, 20 September 2026.

What should I weigh before buying Operations & Workflow software?

Usually it sits on top rather than replacing everything. Tools in this class tend to become the layer where work is tracked and handed off, while your existing systems stay the source of truth for their own data. Before you buy, map which tools it would replace outright and which it would merely sync with. If the answer is that it syncs with all of them, you are adding a layer rather than removing one.
