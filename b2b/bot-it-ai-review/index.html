Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 24 Sep 2026

Free is free forever, with no trial involved: the full catalogue audit and Agent Readiness Score across 13 checks per product, the store gates check (password, robots.txt, UCP profile, sitemap), native llms.txt, agents.md and robots.txt theme files, a brand voice editor, unlimited product and collection narratives, and email support. Category-attribute proposals are shown on Free but not applied. Pro is $29 a month with a 30-day free trial and adds the automated AI fixes for alt text, SEO fields, attributes and narratives under three automation levels with one-click revert, the nightly AI Rank Tracker against named competitors, IndexNow broadcast on product changes, the MCP Enrichment Proxy, a bring-your-own-LLM connector, review integration, AI-agent traffic analytics, knowledge-base sync, 90-day log retention and priority support.

Frequently asked questions

What does Bot It AI cost?

The audit is free forever: every product scored on 13 checks, the store gates check and the llms.txt, agents.md and robots.txt theme files. Pro, which writes the fixes and adds the rank tracker, the enrichment proxy and analytics, is $29 a month with a 30-day free trial, billed through Shopify. Read on 24 September 2026.

Is there a limit on the AI fixes?

Yes, but it is not published. Pro runs on a monthly token budget the vendor calls generous; its FAQ says a typical product costs about a thousand tokens to enrich once and that the app stops proposing, never mid-write, at the ceiling. The Automation tab shows your usage. For a very large catalogue, ask for the number before you commit.

What happens to the changes if I uninstall?

According to the vendor's FAQ they stay. Bot It writes ordinary Shopify data, meaning product fields, standard metafields and metaobjects, so nothing depends on the app remaining installed. Downgrading to Free stops the automation but keeps the audits running.

What should I weigh before buying E-commerce software?

Check your platform and version specifically. Shopify support is close to universal in this category, while WooCommerce, BigCommerce, Magento and headless setups vary a lot, and 'supported' sometimes means a generic API rather than a maintained app. A listing in your own platform's app store, with recent reviews there, is a better signal than the vendor's logo wall.
