Boulevard Review (2026)From $140 Per Location, Add-Ons Too

Business OperationsResearched assessment · by Daniel HaketUpdated 2026-09-12

Boulevard is per location, so the arithmetic for a second salon is not a volume discount, it is double — and the add-ons are per location too, which compounds it.

Best for: salons, spas and clinics with staff to schedule and a front desk to run — the per-location pricing works out fine for one busy site.
Who should skip it: if you are a solo practitioner with a calendar and a card reader, because the entry tier at $140 a month is priced for a team, and multi-location owners should price the add-ons per site before comparing.

Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 18 Aug 2026

(2026-08-01, read on their own pricing page) Priced per location per month: Essentials $140 (shown as discounted from $176), Premier $234 (from $293), Prestige $328 (from $410). Enterprise is a bespoke quote. Paid add-ons sit on top, also per location — the page lists items at $45 and $65 per month per location, ePrescribe at $45 per prescriber, and email marketing at $0.01 per email beyond the included allowance.

Every tier's button is 'Get a demo': there is no self-serve checkout at any price. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

Two things about this price list. And every listed price is shown struck through against a higher 'normally' figure, footnoted on 18 August 2026 as a limited-time summer offer for new customers only. Treat the higher number as the list price a renewal may return to, and the lower one as an introductory rate with an end date you should ask about. The third thing is not a price at all: you cannot buy this without booking a demo.

That is a signal about how the deal is done, and it means the published numbers are a starting point rather than a checkout.

What they complain about
  • • You sign a 12-month contract. There is no month-to-month, so this is a year's commitment from day one.
  • • Each additional location pays full price, which makes scaling expensive rather than cheaper.
  • • Essentials is $140 per location a month (shown as discounted from $176) and Prestige $328, with add-ons at $45-$65 a location on top — reviewers call it expensive.
  • • Frequent updates are reported to affect performance, and reporting is limited relative to the price.

When the paid plan is worth it

The step from Essentials to Premier buys headcount and volume, not a different product. Essentials caps you at five professionals with 100 texts, 500 email blasts and 25 GB of storage; Premier lifts professionals to unlimited with 250 texts, 1,000 blasts and 50 GB; Prestige bundles the Forms add-on and raises the allowances to 2,500 texts and 10,000 blasts. Upgrade when you are hiring past five, then, not because a feature is missing.

What no tier settles is the money side. Card processing is a separate negotiation: their FAQ offers volume discounts against your current merchant statement, or an Offset programme where clients pay a 3% fee and you pay 1%. The Duo point-of-sale has to be bought before you can start processing payments. And upgrading never changes the per-location arithmetic — a second site pays the full plan again, with QuickBooks at $45 and Forms from $65 per month per location on top.

Read the sticker too: $140, $234 and $328 are flagged as limited-time summer pricing for new customers only, against "normally" figures of $176, $293 and $410.

How to actually use Boulevard

There is no self-serve checkout: the three published tiers all say Get a demo and Enterprise says Book a consultation. The FAQ says they are in touch within a day or two to set up a call covering your needs, a product demo and next steps. After signing, an Onboarding Specialist helps create the locations, service menu and staff accounts, and the on-demand training site Boulevard Academy takes the team through going live.

Start the data question early — the vendor's own answer is that what comes across depends on what your previous system releases, with client lists and inventory the types most platforms export. On hardware you probably have the computer or iPad already, but the Boulevard Duo point-of-sale must be purchased to process payments; a cash drawer, barcode scanner and receipt printer are optional. Payments batch into one business day and the deposit is initiated the next, usually reaching the bank after two business days.

Distilled from Vendor pricing page including its own FAQ on setup, onboarding, data migration, equipment and payouts (joinblvd.com/pricing), read 18 August 2026.

The natural comparison is Mindbody or Vagaro — the standing comparison in salon and spa software — compare per-location totals with your add-ons switched on, not the entry sticker.

Frequently asked questions

What should I weigh before buying Business Operations software?

Judge it on evidence rather than adjectives. Look for a published security page, single sign-on and role controls on the plan you would actually buy (they are often gated to higher tiers), a status page with real incident history, and a clear answer on where your data is stored. If the tool will hold anything sensitive, those four answers matter more than any feature comparison.
