What Bread & Butter does

Lead-intelligence platform with server-side tracking: captures the full visitor journey from the first visit, then enriches, scores and nurtures the moment someone submits an email — verified identities instead of IP-based company guesses. 30-day full trial, then a genuinely usable freemium tier; Pro from $49 per website.

Pricing (2026)

(2026) Fully functional 30-day free trial that moves to a freemium tier after (200 lead profiles, unlimited traffic). AI Enabled Pro $49 per month PER WEBSITE: full AI lead intelligence, enrichment, intent scoring, nurture and attribution with 5,000 lead profiles. AI capacity scales via add-on blocks: +$49/mo per Scale-AI-Enrichment block (+10,000 compute credits) and +$89/mo per deep-profile-research block (100 → 300 profiles/month). Enterprise is custom. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

Best for: marketing and revenue teams that want engaged visitors — people who filled a form or left an email — automatically enriched, scored and nurtured, on a genuinely usable freemium tier.
Who should skip it: if you want IP-based company guessing at anonymous visitors — that’s a different tool class (Leadfeeder-style) with a different accuracy and compliance profile — or you run many small sites, where per-website pricing adds up.

The honest knock

Two meters to watch: it prices per WEBSITE (multi-site setups multiply the $49) and the AI features run on compute-credit blocks that stack as usage grows. On privacy — refined twice in open dialogue with the vendor (16 Jul 2026, verified against their docs): the anonymous journey IS tracked from the first visit, server-side via their site plugin — so ad-blockers and Safari’s ITP don’t strip it the way they kill client-side scripts (the vendor estimates 30-50% of high-value traffic is lost to client-side blocking). What stays dormant until someone submits an email is identity resolution — the ‘hand-raise’ that connects the captured history to a verified person. You still owe normal GDPR care on enriched data, and the real trade-off is simply this: no IP-based guessing at which company an anonymous visitor works for.

The natural comparison is Leadfeeder — Leadfeeder guesses the company behind anonymous IPs — useful when it lands, though remote work and VPNs often return an ISP instead; Bread & Butter tracks behaviour server-side from the first visit and resolves identity when a visitor hands over an email. Pick by what your sales team acts on: company guesses or verified people. Decide by which one fits the job above, not by the louder brand.

Frequently asked questions

Will Bread & Butter integrate with my existing stack?

Yes. It is built to sit on top of your existing stack rather than replace it. It installs through a lightweight, server-side site plugin, so ad-blockers and Safari ITP do not strip the tracking, and its Automation and Integrations panel then pushes enriched, scored lead activity into the tools you already run, triggered by rules such as a lead crossing a score threshold or an email being verified. Google Analytics (GA4) is a documented native integration, and there is an in-app integrations library plus conversion tools; check that library for your specific CRM and marketing platforms before you commit.

Who should not use Bread & Butter?

Skip it if you want IP-based company guessing at anonymous visitors — that’s a different tool class (Leadfeeder-style) with a different accuracy and compliance profile — or you run many small sites, where per-website pricing adds up. Buying a tool to fix a problem you don't have yet just adds cost and another login to manage.
