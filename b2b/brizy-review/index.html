What Brizy does

Drag-and-drop website builder with an AI builder, hosted on Brizy Cloud or as a WordPress plugin, with multi-site agency plans and a white-label version for resellers.

Personal is one site; the agency plans are priced for ten

Personal at $19 a month covers one hosted site, and each extra site costs another $17 a month, so a second site nearly doubles the bill. From four sites upward Agency ($69 for ten) is cheaper than Personal plus extras. White Label at $159 is for agencies that resell websites under their own brand: it adds client management, a rebranded builder and Stripe billing of clients, and drops the price of each extra site to $8.

Yearly billing is $190 instead of $228 on Personal, a saving of about 17%. If you want to sell products, look at Brizy Shops pricing instead, because e-commerce is not part of these Cloud plans. Against Webflow, the designer's builder in our files, the one-site price is close and the gap opens with volume. Webflow's site plans start at $15 a month on yearly billing in our 29 September 2026 reading; Brizy Personal on yearly billing comes to about $15.83 a month with hosting included.

Ten client sites cost $690 a year on Brizy Agency, or $1,590 if they must carry your own brand. The WordPress plugin is the other route, for people who already host their own sites: a Free Forever version with non-premium templates needs no card, and paid licences are yearly. The page source we read on 24 October 2026 carries two different sets of WordPress licence prices ($59, $99, $199 and $299 a year in the comparison table; $60, $108, $192 and $300 on the plan cards), so we quote no WordPress figure until it is read in a rendered page.

Lapsing on WordPress has a defined cost: Brizy's help article on not renewing PRO (updated 29 September 2026) says the site stays online and looks the same, but form submissions stop working, PRO elements already on a page can no longer be edited, and the theme builder, dynamic content and WooCommerce builder become inaccessible.

    How to actually use Brizy

    Use the 14-day trial to build one real page with a template or the AI builder. Count the sites you will run in the next year: one stays on Personal, four or more belong on Agency. Choose between Cloud (hosted by Brizy) and the WordPress plugin before you start, because the two are priced and built differently. During the 14-day trial every PRO feature is open, including unlimited AI website generations.

    Brizy's help article on free use (updated 28 July 2026) says that once the trial ends you must buy a plan to keep access to the Cloud dashboard and to keep managing and updating your projects. Buy the domain elsewhere, because Brizy does not sell domains. The custom-domain article (updated 26 August 2026) asks for a www CNAME pointing to your project's brizy.site name and an A record on the root pointing to 34.237.47.210.

    Any other A or AAAA records must be deleted, and on Cloudflare the DNS proxy must be off for both records. Free SSL comes with custom domains and brizy.site subdomains and can take up to 24 hours to install. If you want the files on your own server, there are two routes. Server Sync keeps a copy on your server updated as you edit in Brizy Cloud. Export HTML downloads the project as a ZIP; the export article (updated 3 June 2025) ties it to the Agency plan and says generating the file takes 15 to 60 minutes, after which you upload it with an .htaccess rewrite rule. An API route can also push the export to your own Amazon S3 bucket.

    Can I build an online store with Brizy Cloud?

    Not on these plans: e-commerce sites are priced separately under Brizy Shops.

    Can I keep a Brizy Cloud site without paying?

    Not as a site you can still edit. After the 14-day trial, Brizy's help article (updated 28 July 2026) says a paid plan is needed to keep using the Cloud dashboard and to update projects. The terms (7 October 2026) let Brizy modify, suspend or end free accounts, or delete their content, at any time with or without notice, and allow deletion of free or inactive accounts after 12 months of inactivity, subject to reasonable notice. Whether a published Cloud site stays online after a paid plan ends is not stated in what we read, so export the HTML first if your plan allows it.

    Who handles billing and support for my clients on White Label?

    You do. Brizy's terms (7 October 2026) say your end users contract with you, not with Brizy, that you set their prices and are responsible for billing, refunds, customer service, onboarding and account administration, and that you must provide your own terms, privacy and cookie notices. The White Label plan includes client billing through Stripe; how that payout and dispute handling works is not described on the pricing page.

    How many custom domains can I connect?

    The help centre and the pricing page do not use the same plan names. The publishing article (updated 29 July 2026) lists no custom domain on Free, 1 on Personal, 20 on a Freelancer plan and 100 on Agency, and notes that deals such as an AppSumo purchase can change the count. The current pricing page sells Personal with 1 website and Agency and White Label with 10 websites each, at $12 or $8 a month per extra site, and has no Freelancer plan. Publishing on a brizy.site subdomain is free and unlimited.

    What has changed in Brizy Cloud this year?

    The Cloud changelog (read 24 October 2026) lists version 3.5.16 on 16 July 2026, which added image fetch priority and a configurable default country for phone fields in forms and removed the Twitter/X element; 3.5.15 on 20 May 2026, with accessibility work across more than twenty elements, Pinterest and Google Search widgets and italic font support; and 3.5.14 on 1 April 2026, with further accessibility improvements. No pricing changes are recorded there.
