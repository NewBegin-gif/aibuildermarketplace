What Buzz.ai does

AI-powered outbound platform: prompt it and it finds intent-ready buyers, writes multichannel sequences and books the meetings — per-seat plans with monthly credit allowances and a free start.

Pricing (2026)

(re-verified 16 Jul 2026) Core $125/mo per seat with 500 credits/seat/mo — a launch offer runs $75/mo for the first 3 months (and $75/mo per seat billed annually); AI Employee $795/mo per seat with 5,000 credits/seat/mo, and the pricing page itself warns it ‘moves to $1,000/mo soon’; custom tier via sales. Free start available. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

Two meters run at once: seats AND credits — the $125 seat includes 500 credits a month, and real outbound volume means credit packages on top, so budget past the sticker. Watch the launch math too: $75 is a 3-month teaser that steps up to $125, and the top tier openly signposts a rise from $795 to $1,000 — decide on the post-rise price, not the promo. And the honest fundamental for any AI-SDR: it only books meetings as good as your list and offer, so keep a human reviewing what goes out in your name.

When the paid plan is worth it

The limit that bites first is credits, not features. Core includes 500 credits per seat per month, and that single pool pays for data enrichment, AI functions and dialer calls alike. At 1 credit per enriched email, a seat that prospects a few hundred fresh contacts a month will drain the balance before it gets much use out of the AI, and "Run out of credits" is one of the documented reasons a campaign step stalls. If you work lists you already own and run social sequences without enrichment, Core at $125 per seat is enough, and the AI Employee tier is wasted money: it buys 5,000 credits plus AI targeting and messaging, which only repays you if you enrich and dial at volume. What upgrading does not fix at any tier: Buzz drives your own social account, so InMails still need a separate Sales Navigator subscription, and the platform's own caps still apply, including a 1,900-character ceiling on an InMail. Cancelling also requires 31 days of written notice regardless of plan.

How to actually use Buzz.ai

Start with the free seat, which needs no card, then connect a sending account before anything else. Buzz has you add the account manually: choose a geographic location (optionally your own proxy), set timezone and working hours, then enter the account email and password. Since April 2025 two-step verification is required for Sales Navigator accounts, so keep your phone to hand, because you get prompted for the code inside the workspace. Sending then ramps: Warming Up raises daily actions from your minimum toward your maximum, and clicking Confirm restarts that ramp. Click "+ Create New Campaign", pick Simple, Smart or Magic, name it, paste the post or event you want to target, then add steps such as Send Connection Request and Send Message and set the delays between them. Where it goes wrong in practice: delays are a floor, not a promise, and the documentation warns that a one-hour delay does not guarantee the step runs in one hour. Steps also stall quietly on an invalid email, a disconnected email or CRM integration, or an empty credit balance. Frequently asked questions

Does Buzz.ai replace my CRM or plug into it?

Most tools in this category plug in rather than replace. Check which direction the data flows and whether the sync is genuinely two-way, because a one-way push creates a second source of truth that your reps will quietly stop trusting. If it does aim to replace your CRM, price the migration honestly: history, custom fields and reporting rarely move across cleanly.

Who should not use Buzz.ai?

Skip it if you send low-volume, hand-crafted outreach (per-seat-plus-credits is overkill), or you're not ready to QA an AI that writes to your market on your behalf. Buying a tool to fix a problem you don't have yet just adds cost and another login to manage.
