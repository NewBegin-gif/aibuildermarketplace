What Bybit does

Crypto exchange covering spot, perpetuals and options. No subscription — spot is 0.1% maker and taker for non-VIP, derivatives around 0.02% and 0.055%, with volume tiers cutting lower. Funding rates on perpetuals are a recurring cost the fee table does not show, and availability differs per country.

Pricing (2026)

No subscription — per-trade fees (2026): spot base 0.1% maker/taker for non-VIP; derivatives around 0.02% maker / 0.055% taker; options ~0.03%. VIP tiers (by volume or balance) cut rates toward zero, with maker rebates at the top. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

Correction after re-checking: Bybit publishes no negative maker fee. The standard table runs from VIP 0 — 0.1000% maker and taker on spot, 0.0200% maker and 0.0550% taker on Perpetual & Futures — down to Supreme VIP, whose Perpetual & Futures maker rate is 0.0000% and whose Options maker rate is 0.0050%. That is zero, not a rebate. The Pro Users & Market Makers tab ends at the same floor: Pro 6 pays 0.0000% maker on derivatives across all five symbol groups. That tab does use rebate wording — it describes Group 1 as carrying "adjusted Maker rebates" and Groups 4-5 as carrying "increased Maker rebates" — but it attaches no rate below 0.0000% to any group. The only rebate percentage Bybit publishes sits outside both tables, in the Market Maker Incentive Program, which the fee page describes as "up to 0.01% maker fee rebate" and which requires a separate application. A rebate figure is therefore something you apply for, not a number any fee tier hands you. Note also that Bybit states actual rates may vary by region and directs users to their own My Fee Rate page. (re-verified 31 Jul 2026)

The honest knock

Derivatives are the draw and the danger: leverage cuts both ways, and funding rates on perpetuals are a real recurring cost the fee table doesn't show. Regulation-wise, check what Bybit may offer in YOUR jurisdiction before funding an account — availability and product range differ per country, and that's the part most fee comparisons skip.

How to actually use Bybit

Bybit's own onboarding runs five steps: register with an email or phone number, complete Identity Verification, deposit crypto, buy crypto, then explore its beginner-recommended products. Step two is not optional. Bybit's KYC table marks crypto deposit, spot trading, derivatives and Earn as unavailable without verification, so an unverified account can only withdraw — capped at 20K USDT daily and 100K USDT monthly. Standard verification (identity assessment plus proof of identity) is what makes a first trade possible and raises the daily withdrawal ceiling to 1M USDT. Bybit puts the check at about 15 minutes but says it can take up to 48 hours.
