The subscription is the platform, not the talking: numbers, minutes and SMS meter separately from a credit balance, so an active calling team's real bill sits meaningfully above the per-seat sticker — model a month of your actual call volume before comparing vendors. Frequently asked questions

What does Calilio cost?

$15 per user a month for Standard, or $12 billed annually ($144 a year); $35 for Premium, or $28 annually ($336). Annual saves 20%. Read on 17 August 2026.

What is included before the credit balance starts?

Each plan carries a stated value of free calls and SMS/MMS to US and Canada numbers — $6.75 a month on Standard and $33.74 on Premium. After that, calls and messages draw from a credit balance you top up, and phone numbers are billed separately from the seat. That is the part that makes the effective bill higher than the plan price.

Has the pricing changed recently?

The way it is presented has. Our July 2026 capture of the page quoted per-minute rates from $0.0029 a minute; by August those were gone and the same plans were described in terms of included call value instead. The $15 entry price was the same in both. If you are comparing against a quote from earlier in the summer, the structure moved even where the headline did not.
