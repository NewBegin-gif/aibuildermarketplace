What Calilio does

Modern, reliable business VoIP communications. Set up clear, automated phone menus and virtual numbers in minutes.

Pricing (2026)

(2026) Standard about $12/user/mo annual ($15 monthly, up to 10 users, 1 local number, ~300 free minutes); Premium ~$28/user/mo (unlimited users, live monitoring); Enterprise custom. Numbers are billed separately; calls/SMS draw from credit. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

The subscription is the platform, not the talking: numbers, minutes and SMS meter separately from a credit balance, so an active calling team's real bill sits meaningfully above the per-seat sticker — model a month of your actual call volume before comparing vendors. It's a young player in a crowded VoIP field; the price is keen, so test call quality on your real routes in the trial.

When the paid plan is worth it

Standard is the entry dialer; Premium is where the call-handling machinery sits. Calilio's own plan-restriction guide places Phone Trees (IVR), Call Transfer, Live Call Monitoring, Call Whispering, Power Dialer, Business Hours, Teams, Caller ID, AI-Powered Call Summary and Call Sentiment Analysis behind Premium. The seat cap makes the crossover arithmetic simple: Standard stops at 10 users, so growing past that cap forces the move from $12 to $28 per user per month on annual billing, or $15 to $35 monthly. Bundled usage scales with it, from 200 outgoing and 100 incoming minutes on Standard to 1,000 and 500 on Premium, and from 100 outbound and 100 inbound SMS to 500 of each. Below ten seats the seat cap itself forces nothing; the move is a feature and bundle decision. What upgrading does not fix: the same guide names the integration rows "Salesforce (Coming Soon)", "Zoho (Coming Soon)", "HubSpot (Coming Soon)" and "Slack (Coming Soon)" while ticking them for Premium, calling and messaging still meter against a prepaid wallet, additional numbers are charged at $2 per number/month.

How to actually use Calilio

Calilio is paid from the first step. Manual signup collects name, email, password and phone number, and Calilio may then send an OTP code to the email for verification; a Google or Apple sign-in skips those fields. The next screens ask for plan choice, plan duration (Monthly or Annual) and billing details before a Pay Now button. Payment confirmation ends in a "Congratulations!" message and a "Go to your Dashboard" link; no trial step appears in that flow. One local US or Canada number is free for each user after sign-up; further numbers are bought under Settings > My Numbers > Purchase Number. For an additional number the help centre states that numbers become available once the sales team has verified payment and, if necessary, documentation; "Verification documents vary from country to country". A USA toll-free number needs no verification, though sending SMS from it requires A2P 10DLC registration. Budget for the wallet too, since calling, messaging and other usage-based features draw down prepaid balance; auto-recharge adds a Recharge Amount whenever the balance hits a Trigger Amount you set. Frequently asked questions

Will Calilio work for a remote or distributed team?

Generally yes, since this category is built for it, but the details that bite are timezone handling, how per-seat cost scales as the team spreads, and whether numbers, routing or coverage exist in the countries your people actually sit in. Availability varies far more by country than the marketing pages suggest, so check your specific regions before committing.

Who should not use Calilio?

Skip it if you need a battle-tested enterprise contact center with deep integrations and SLAs, or your call volume makes metered credits unpredictable. Buying a tool to fix a problem you don't have yet just adds cost and another login to manage.
