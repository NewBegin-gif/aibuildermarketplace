CallHippo Review (2026)

Communication & VoiceResearched assessment · by Daniel HaketUpdated 2026-09-25

'Unlimited calling' is unlimited to the US and Canada — international minutes meter separately, so teams calling Europe or Asia should price their real destinations first. Throughout this review we weigh it against RingCentral.

Best for: budget-conscious sales/support teams wanting virtual numbers in 50+ countries with a free entry point and CRM integrations.
Who should skip it: if your calling is mostly international at volume (metered minutes stack up), or you want one simple plan without add-on shopping.

Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 15 Sep 2026

Read the tab before the number: the grid opens on yearly, so the lower column is what most write-ups quote, and the toggle beside it advertises "Save 3 Months' Cost" -- paying annually is a third cheaper per user. The $18 that a plain fetch kept printing for Starter was the placeholder sitting in the HTML before the pricing script runs, not a column any visitor is shown. Basic still costs nothing, but fair-usage note 8 lower on that page says it is free only for the first 6 months from signup, after which the account must move to Starter to keep working - so Basic is a long runway, not a permanent free tier.
