What CallHippo does

AI-driven business phone system. Virtual numbers in 50+ countries, power dialer, call analytics and AI agents — set up your sales and support calling in minutes. Its omnichannel inbox threads voice, WhatsApp, SMS, email, Instagram and Telegram into one conversation per customer.

(2026-08-15, read on their own pricing page in euros) Starter €19/user/mo, Professional €29 and Ultimate €39 on monthly billing for a 1-4 user team, above a genuinely free Basic tier where you pay only for the numbers. Unlimited landline and mobile minutes start at Starter with 100 SMS; call recordings, AI voicemail transcription and multilingual IVR at Professional with 500 SMS; smart DID, multilevel and holiday routing at Ultimate with 1,000 SMS. 10-day free trial on the paid tiers.

When the paid plan is worth it

Basic is a number-rental shell: you pay only for the numbers, and it carries click-to-dial, voicemail, business hours, SMS/MMS and the WhatsApp Business API. It is a free tier, not a trial — the plan grid read on 15 August 2026 shows it as Free with no time limit. Starter at €19/user/month is where unlimited landline and mobile minutes begin, capped on messaging rather than calling at 100 SMS, so a rep on the phone all day is fine but a texting workflow is not.

The first threshold that matters for most teams is call recording, AI voicemail transcription and multilingual IVR, which start at Professional (€29/user/month, 500 SMS). Ultimate at €39 buys routing depth — multilevel IVR, smart DID routing, holiday routing, CRM-based VIP routing, 1,000 SMS — which pays back only once there are queues to route. Enterprise requires a minimum of 50 users. Add-ons are priced apart from all of this: an AI copilot, an AI voice agent, a dashboard-user seat and CNAM.

What no upgrade fixes: 'unlimited' minutes are unlimited within the number region you select, so international destinations still meter. The pricing page caps outbound calls to the same number at 10 calls per hour, and the terms cap messaging at 2,000 SMS/MMS segments per day for an approved brand.

How to actually use CallHippo

A signup opens a 10-day free trial with no credit card, but nothing dials until a number is attached. Numbers are bought inside the app — Numbers, then Add Number, choose a country, filter, upload documents, pay — and the documents are where the delay lives. US local and Australia mobile numbers are the only ones CallHippo lists as available without submitting documentation; everything else needs country-specific paperwork, with UK local numbers requiring UK-issued documents.

Support states numbers typically activate within 24 to 48 business hours after document verification, and that regulator rules allow no exceptions, so a new account can sit unusable while paperwork clears. Each user includes 1 free standard number; premium numbers are chargeable. Texting is a slower, separate track: SMS services carry a one-time setup fee of $20 per account and a documented approval window of 2 to 4 weeks, so US business texting has to be started well before launch rather than switched on.
