What CallHippo does

AI-driven business phone system. Virtual numbers in 50+ countries, power dialer, call analytics and AI agents — set up your sales and support calling in minutes. Its omnichannel inbox threads voice, WhatsApp, SMS, email, Instagram and Telegram into one conversation per customer.

Pricing (2026)

(2026) A Basic plan, free for the first 6 months from signup (re-verified 2026-07-31); Starter about $18/user/mo (1,000 US/CA minutes, 100 SMS), Professional ~$30 (unlimited US/CA calling), Ultimate ~$42; call-center 'Pro Suite' tiers $32-48/user/mo annual; Enterprise custom at 50+ users. AI copilot, AI voice agent and CNAM are paid add-ons. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

'Unlimited calling' is unlimited to the US and Canada — international minutes meter separately, so teams calling Europe or Asia should price their real destinations first. The plan grid is busy (two suites, add-ons for the dialer and AI), which makes comparisons slippery: list the five features you actually need and find the cheapest tier that truly contains all five.

When the paid plan is worth it

Basic is a number-rental shell — you pay only for the numbers and get click-to-dial, voicemail and business hours — and it is free only for the first 6 months from signup, after which an upgrade is required. Starter at $18/user/month caps you at 1,000 US/CA calling minutes and 100 SMS, which a rep on the phone for a few hours a day exhausts, after which metered rates apply. The first threshold that matters for most teams is call recording and AI voicemail transcription, which start at Professional, $30/user/month. Ultimate at $42 buys routing depth — multilevel IVR, smart DID routing, holiday routing — which pays back only once there are queues to route. Enterprise requires a minimum of 50 users. Add-ons are priced apart from all of this: AI Copilot at $10/user/month, with an AI Voice Agent, a dashboard-user seat and CNAM priced separately. What no upgrade fixes: unlimited minutes start only at Professional and stop at the US and Canada, 48-country calling appears only at Enterprise, the pricing page caps outbound calls to the same number at 10 calls per hour, and the terms cap messaging at 2,000 SMS/MMS segments per day for an approved brand.

How to actually use CallHippo

A signup opens a 10-day free trial with no credit card, but nothing dials until a number is attached. Numbers are bought inside the app — Numbers, then Add Number, choose a country, filter, upload documents, pay — and the documents are where the delay lives. US local and Australia mobile numbers are the only ones CallHippo lists as available without submitting documentation; everything else needs country-specific paperwork, with UK local numbers requiring UK-issued documents. Support states numbers typically activate within 24 to 48 business hours after document verification, and that regulator rules allow no exceptions, so a new account can sit unusable while paperwork clears. Each user includes 1 free standard number; premium numbers are chargeable. Texting is a slower, separate track: SMS services carry a one-time setup fee of $20 per account and a documented approval window of 2 to 4 weeks, so US business texting has to be started well before launch rather than switched on. Frequently asked questions

Will CallHippo work for a remote or distributed team?

Generally yes, since this category is built for it, but the details that bite are timezone handling, how per-seat cost scales as the team spreads, and whether numbers, routing or coverage exist in the countries your people actually sit in. Availability varies far more by country than the marketing pages suggest, so check your specific regions before committing.

Who should not use CallHippo?

Skip it if your calling is mostly international at volume (metered minutes stack up), or you want one simple plan without add-on shopping. Buying a tool to fix a problem you don't have yet just adds cost and another login to manage.
