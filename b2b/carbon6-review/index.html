The honest knock

The honest headline is the pricing maze: a suite of separately-priced tools means your real monthly needs its own spreadsheet, and the flagship reimbursement service takes a quarter of what it recovers (fair — it's found money — but know the split). Buy it tool-by-tool for problems you measurably have, starting with reimbursements where the commission model means you only pay on results.

The natural comparison is Helium 10 or Jungle Scout — the flat-suite rivals — simpler pricing, broader research tools; Carbon6's edge is depth in reimbursements and operations at scale. Decide by which one fits the job above, not by the louder brand.
