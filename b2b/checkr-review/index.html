What Checkr does

AI-powered background check and screening platform for hiring: fast criminal, employment and identity verification with automated adjudication and ATS integrations — speeds up onboarding while staying FCRA-compliant.

Pricing (2026)

(re-verified 2026-07-23) Pay-as-you-go per report, no subscription: Basic $29.99, Essential $59.99 and Complete $94.99 per report, with Enterprise quote-only. Tenant screening is priced separately at $24.99, $34.99 and $44.99 per report, and personal checks start from $49.99. Add-ons stack on top: MVR report $9.50/check, identity verification $4.99/check, drug screening from $10/test, FACIS from $6/check and employment verification from $12.50/check. On top of that, standard third-party court and database access fees are passed through at cost with no markup, so the real cost per hire lands above the sticker price. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

The honest knock

The headline per-check price is rarely what you actually pay — passthrough fees vary wildly by county and search type, so budget for the effective cost, not the advertised one. It's overkill unless you hire at real volume.

What buyers report about Checkr

Recurring themes from Trustpilot, BBB and ComplaintsBoard, read in August 2026. We have not run Checkr ourselves, so this is other buyers' experience rather than ours. We summarise it here because the complaints are usually the part a vendor page leaves out.

What they praise
  • • Fast and heavily automated, which is why gig platforms and high-volume employers use it. A+ rating with the BBB.
  • • For straightforward checks at volume, reviewers report it does the job without friction.
What they complain about
  • • Report errors are frequent enough that past class actions over file accuracy ran to multi-million dollar settlements. Verify anything flagged before you act on it — that is a legal exposure, not just an inconvenience.
  • • Disputes and corrections take a long time. Candidates report pending status stretching for months, with one account describing a 5-month delay.
  • • Support is chatbot-first, and reviewers describe being unable to reach a person while a dispute is open.
  • • 3.4 out of 5 on Trustpilot — low for a product whose whole value is accuracy.

When the paid plan is worth it

Checkr charges per report, not per seat, so "premium" means moving up a package rather than unlocking a dashboard. Basic at $29.99 buys an SSN trace, sex offender registry, global watchlist and a national criminal search — a database sweep, not a courthouse one. Essential at $59.99 adds identity verification and unlimited county criminal search, and county records are where actual convictions sit; that is the step that changes what you learn, not the one above it. Complete at $94.99 adds unlimited state criminal search plus a federal criminal search, which earns its keep for roles touching finance, federal contracts or interstate work and is otherwise redundant. Enterprise is quote-only and gates automated adjudication, 200+ ATS integrations and custom workflows — worth it once a human reviewing every report is the bottleneck rather than the check itself. What upgrading never fixes: third-party court and database access fees are passed through at cost on every tier, so the package price is a floor and not an invoice, and Checkr states it does not make the hiring determination on the customer's behalf. Individualized assessment and adverse action remain your legal work at every price point.

How to actually use Checkr

Nothing runs until Checkr credentials the account. Dashboard and API access require establishing permissible purpose through an Account Executive, backed by legal business name, EIN, state of incorporation and articles of incorporation. That gate, not the integration work, is where timelines usually slip, and Checkr publishes no duration for it. Developers then request a staging account by form — Checkr documents that new staging accounts are typically created within 1-2 business days — and pull secret and publishable keys from Account Settings, Developer Settings. Staging is deliberately partial: post adverse action, invoices and analytics are unavailable, and mocked reports finish in minutes rather than realistically. First live value comes from an invitation carrying candidate ID, package name and work location, with the candidate supplying SSN and zip code for criminal packages, or driver license number and state for motor vehicle records. Invitations stay valid for 7 days with a follow-up notice every 24 hours. Checkr documents webhooks as the recommended way to receive report status. Distilled from Checkr developer documentation, getting-started and credentialing sections: https://docs.checkr.com/ and https://docs.checkr.com/#section/Getting-started (both read 2026-07-31). Package and add-on gating cross-checked against the vendor pricing page: https://checkr.com/pricing (read 2026-07-31).

Frequently asked questions

Does Checkr replace my current setup or sit on top of it?

Usually it sits on top rather than replacing everything. Tools in this class tend to become the layer where work is tracked and handed off, while your existing systems stay the source of truth for their own data. Before you buy, map which tools it would replace outright and which it would merely sync with. If the answer is that it syncs with all of them, you are adding a layer rather than removing one.

Who should not use Checkr?

Skip it if you hire only a handful of people a year — a one-off check from a simpler provider costs less hassle than onboarding a screening platform. Buying a tool to fix a problem you don't have yet just adds cost and another login to manage.
