We tested it · 2026-11-18

We created the account and our AI assistant ran the test in our browser. On 18 November 2026 the session tested the free GEO scan and the citation probe of CiteRoute on a site of our own, in our existing free account. The scan took about 3.5 minutes in 3 steps and gave 81/100 Strong technical readiness, although Google shows the site hardly at all. It reported 0 JSON-LD detected while the homepage has 3 JSON-LD blocks, and some tips are made up (a placeholder wikidata address and an endpoint /api/agent/catalog). The citation probe gave Verified Citation three times on a question that contains the domain name, with n=1 per engine; Claude said it had no verified information and still got 92/100. The prices match our research; our research says CiteRoute does not measure citations while the app has a probe, and the scan page says rescans every 7 days while Free has daily re-scans struck through. The tracking tag and the AI bot log were not tested, because they need verification by meta tag or DNS. No plan was bought. Account and start: the test used the existing CiteRoute our account, on the Free plan, with the interface in English (no language setting was found). The test started at 09:22 in our own tab. The My sites page showed FREE PLAN and No sites yet. Adding a site needs the CiteRoute Tag and a check of ownership with a meta tag or a DNS TXT record, and the page says that CiteRoute verifies that you control the domain before adding it. That was NOT done, so the tracking tag and the AI bot log were not tested. The scanned site is a site of our own; its domain is not named in this delivery. Core action: the GEO Scanner (the audit page) works without verification. The domain of our own site was typed in at 09:23, the scan was started, and the result came after about 3.5 minutes (the progress bar stayed at 98% for a long time). It took 3 steps: type the domain, start the scan, start the probe. Result: Technical Readiness Score 81 / 100, labelled Strong technical readiness, with sub scores Tabular and FAQ 77, Content depth 86, Schema 73 and Section hierarchy 88. The scan reported JSON-LD Schemas: 0 detected, 1 H1 and 31 H2, 4,703 words, 0 tables and 0 of 15 points for JSON-LD Schema.org declarations. Check of the scan: the homepage has 3 application/ld+json blocks (Organization, WebSite with a SearchAction and an ItemList with 12 items), so 0 detected is wrong. The 0 tables is right; the llms.txt file exists (HTTP 200) and /.well-known/agent.json gives 404, as the scan says. The Schema sub score of 73/100 stands next to 0 of 15 points for JSON-LD, so the scores contradict each other. Google shows our own site hardly at all, so a label of Strong technical readiness is suspiciously optimistic; readiness is not visibility. The recommendations: add Organization, FAQPage and Dataset JSON-LD and a price table. The Organization example uses sameAs links to our own pages; the second patch example has a placeholder wikidata address ending in Q... and a made-up Crunchbase address; the agent.json example has a made-up endpoint /api/agent/catalog. One tip names the Dataset schema as the fix for HTML data tables and says Embed this JSON-LD on a table tip. These tips must not be copied blindly. The live citation probe (via OpenRouter, preset Brand Authority, no verification needed) asked What are the core capabilities and verified offerings of the domain of our own site; the question itself contains the domain name, so a mention is almost certain. Perplexity Sonar: 92/100, Verified Citation, 100% (n=1), 20 source URLs; the answer mostly fits our site. OpenAI GPT-4o Search: 92/100, Verified Citation, 1 source URL; the answer is a generic invention (pre-built AI applications). Anthropic Claude 3.5: 92/100, Verified Citation, while the answer says I don't have verified information about this site in my training data; it still counts as a citation because it named the URL. Google Gemini: 68/100, Brand Mention Only; the description is wrong (a no-code AI app marketplace). A citation on a question that contains the domain name proves nothing, and n=1 per engine is not a measurement. Deviation from our research: our research says CiteRoute does not promise or measure specific citations, while the app has a citation probe, with n=1 per engine; no guarantee was seen. Second, the scan page says CiteRoute rescans domains every 7 days automatically, while the Free plan has Daily automated re-scans struck through and Pro has them. Plans, read up to the payment step: Free $0 forever (10 GEO scans per month, GEO Radar 1 monitored buyer prompt, 1-on-1 competitor comparison of 2 domains, 3 watchlist domains, community leaderboard, agent.json Studio, basic score report; struck through: Tracking tag and AI bot traffic detection, API key access, Daily automated re-scans, Weekly email performance digest); Pro $79 a month (unlimited GEO scans, GEO Radar 10 prompts 24/7, 5-way competitor benchmark, 1 verified site with tracking tag, 20 watchlist domains, an AI bot and crawler analytics dashboard, API key with 500 requests a day, history and trend charts, Start 14-day Trial, 14 days free, cancel anytime); Agency $249 a month, Most popular (10 verified client sites, white-label PDF, API 10,000 requests a day, 14 days free); Enterprise Custom. API pricing per call: geo-score $0.02, verify $0.01 and ai-traffic $0.05, and requests beyond the daily plan limits are billed at standard usage rates. The prices and the 14-day trial match our research. The Free limit of 10 scans a month was not tested (the counter is not visible on the scan page). No price field is changed here. Checkout: NOT reached. The Pro trial was not started and no payment screen was seen. Cancelling: Free has no subscription. The account menu has API Keys, Third-Party AI Keys and Sign Out, and no button for deleting the account or cancelling a plan; the FAQ says you can upgrade, downgrade or cancel (text cut off). No site was added, so nothing had to be removed. Not tested: the tracking tag and the AI bot log (Pro, 1 verified site; they need verification by meta tag or DNS and a script on the site, which was not allowed), so the recorded group of founders running a single content site is not tested; also API keys, the watchlist, the white-label PDF, share, export, GEO Radar, competitor comparison and Third-Party AI Keys (links external accounts). Clean up: all own tabs were closed; no test site, no script, no DNS or meta change and no external link were made. The scan stays in the Score History of our account; the watchlist was not touched.

Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 18 Nov 2026

Free costs nothing and includes 10 GEO scans a month, one monitored buyer prompt in GEO Radar, a two-domain competitor comparison, 3 watchlist domains, the agent.json studio, a basic score report and community support. Pro is $79 a month with a 14-day trial: unlimited scans, 10 monitored prompts, a five-way competitor benchmark, one verified site with the tracking tag and a dashboard of real AI bot and crawler visits, 20 watchlist domains, an API key for 500 requests a day, history charts and priority support. Agency is $249 a month with a 14-day trial: 50 monitored prompts, 10 verified client sites, unlimited watchlist, white-label PDF reports, an embeddable badge and 10,000 API requests a day.

Does CiteRoute have a free plan?

Yes. Read on 13 October 2026, the Free plan includes 10 GEO scans a month, one monitored buyer prompt, a two-domain comparison, 3 watchlist domains and the agent.json studio. Real AI-crawler analytics on your own site start on Pro at $79 a month, which has a 14-day trial. Daily automated re-scans are not included on Free; in our test on 18 November 2026 the scan page said it rescans every 7 days.

Can I get a refund from CiteRoute?

The terms of service, last updated 10 September 2026 and read 13 October 2026, offer a full refund within 14 calendar days of your initial subscription purchase, requested by email with your account email and receipt number. After cancelling you keep access until the end of the paid month.

Will CiteRoute get my site cited by ChatGPT or Perplexity?

It cannot promise that. Its own terms say rankings, citation frequency and indexing are decided by the AI engines and that CiteRoute does not guarantee specific results. What it offers is scoring, recommendations and, on paid plans, a log of AI crawler visits to your site. Since our first reading the home page has added citation probes. In our test on 18 November 2026 a probe scored 92/100 'Verified Citation' on an answer that said 'I don't have verified information', so check the answer text yourself.
