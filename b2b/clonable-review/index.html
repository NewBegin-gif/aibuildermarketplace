Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 5 Sep 2026

Read in a browser on clonable.net/pricing on 5 September 2026 (the page blocks plain fetches), monthly view in euros: Free at €0 for 1 clone and up to 20 translated pages (50 pages for €25 a month), Business €49 for 3 clones and 400 pages, Pro €79 for 5 clones and 1,200 pages, Expert €249 for 10 clones and 5,000 pages, Premium €999 for 20 clones and 50,000 pages.

An extra clone with its pages is €25 a month (€50 on Expert and Premium); optional setup assistance is €250 (€1,000 on Premium). Annual billing is cheaper and there is a separate ladder for online stores. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

The honest knock

Two honest caveats. Second, it's machine translation under the hood, so quality varies by language pair; always have a native speaker spot-check high-stakes pages. (Public pricing was gated at research time — confirm current tiers yourself.)

What Clonable does

Creates a continuously-translated clone of your existing website — fast multilingual versions that stay synced as your site changes, without rebuilding it.

What buyers report about Clonable

Recurring themes from the buyer-review sites listed at the foot of this page, read in August 2026. We have not run Clonable ourselves, so this is other buyers' experience rather than ours. We summarise it here because the complaints are usually the part a vendor page leaves out.

What they praise
  • • Updates to your original site translate and sync automatically — reviewers name that as the thing that saves the ongoing work, not the initial setup.
  • • Reviewers report standing up German, French, English and Danish clones in a single day, without hiring anyone or using an agency.
  • • Translation quality is described as clearly better than Google Translate, cheap enough that a human can review the pages that matter.
What they complain about
  • • The independent review base is thin and heavily weighted toward positive experiences. There is not much critical evidence either way — trial it on your own site.
  • • It is a translated clone of your site, not a native multilingual build. Weigh what that means for your SEO setup before committing.

When the paid plan is worth it

The free tier caps at 1 clone and 20 translated pages (a one-time upgrade to 50 is offered). Business is EUR 49 per month invoiced annually (EUR 59 billed monthly) for 3 clones and up to 400 translated pages; Pro is EUR 79 or EUR 95 for 5 clones and 1,200 pages. The pricing page FAQ states the page total applies to all clones combined, so extra languages draw on the same allowance.

Higher tiers buy tooling as well as volume: the feature table lists AI proofreading as 1-2 texts on Free and 50-100 texts on Business, switches on site search from Pro, and backups from Expert. No subscription covers installation assistance (EUR 250 optional on Business, Pro and Expert), the feeds themselves (EUR 30 to EUR 75 per feed per month depending on package), or a non-translating clone (EUR 49 to EUR 249 on the e-commerce plans).

How to actually use Clonable

Create an account, then a clone: enter your domain without https:// or www, let Clonable detect the platform (the docs allow up to 10 seconds), and pick subfolder, subdomain or separate domain. In about a minute the clone is ready to preview; the vendor FAQ says it is ready to use within 10 minutes. What follows is DNS. Subdomain and separate-domain clones need A and AAAA records, with and without www, and the DNS article warns records are generally active an hour after setting but can take up to 24 hours depending on your ISP.

Subfolder clones need no DNS change, but the docs say one can currently only be created with the Clonable WordPress plugin (WordPress 6.0 or higher, PHP 8.1 or higher); other platforms are told to ask for a custom solution. Documented snags: Error 421 when a subfolder is not correctly registered in Clonable, an SSL name mismatch after deleting a clone in the dashboard but not the plugin, and a language switcher off by default.
