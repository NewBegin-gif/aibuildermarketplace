What Clonable does

Creates a continuously-translated clone of your existing website — fast multilingual versions that stay synced as your site changes, without rebuilding it.

What buyers report about Clonable

Recurring themes from the buyer-review sites listed at the foot of this page, read in August 2026. We have not run Clonable ourselves, so this is other buyers' experience rather than ours. We summarise it here because the complaints are usually the part a vendor page leaves out.

What they praise
  • • Updates to your original site translate and sync automatically — reviewers name that as the thing that saves the ongoing work, not the initial setup.
  • • Reviewers report standing up German, French, English and Danish clones in a single day, without hiring anyone or using an agency.
  • • Translation quality is described as clearly better than Google Translate, cheap enough that a human can review the pages that matter.
What they complain about
  • • The independent review base is thin and heavily weighted toward positive experiences. There is not much critical evidence either way — trial it on your own site.
  • • It is a translated clone of your site, not a native multilingual build. Weigh what that means for your SEO setup before committing.

When the paid plan is worth it

The free tier caps at 1 clone and 20 translated pages (a one-time upgrade to 50 is offered). Business is EUR 49 per month invoiced annually (EUR 59 billed monthly) for 3 clones and up to 400 translated pages; Pro is EUR 79 or EUR 95 for 5 clones and 1,200 pages. The pricing page FAQ states the page total applies to all clones combined, so extra languages draw on the same allowance.

Higher tiers buy tooling as well as volume: the feature table lists AI proofreading as 1-2 texts on Free and 50-100 texts on Business, switches on site search from Pro, and backups from Expert. No subscription covers installation assistance (EUR 250 optional on Business, Pro and Expert), the feeds themselves (EUR 30 to EUR 75 per feed per month depending on package), or a non-translating clone (EUR 49 to EUR 249 on the e-commerce plans).

How to actually use Clonable

Create an account, then a clone: enter your domain without https:// or www, let Clonable detect the platform (the docs allow up to 10 seconds), and pick subfolder, subdomain or separate domain. In about a minute the clone is ready to preview; the vendor FAQ says it is ready to use within 10 minutes. What follows is DNS. Subdomain and separate-domain clones need A and AAAA records, with and without www, and the DNS article warns records are generally active an hour after setting but can take up to 24 hours depending on your ISP.

Subfolder clones need no DNS change, but the docs say one can currently only be created with the Clonable WordPress plugin (WordPress 6.0 or higher, PHP 8.1 or higher); other platforms are told to ask for a custom solution. Documented snags: Error 421 when a subfolder is not correctly registered in Clonable, an SSL name mismatch after deleting a clone in the dashboard but not the plugin, and a language switcher off by default.

Frequently asked questions

Is €49 a month really what Business costs?

Only if you pay for a year up front. The pricing page carries a Per month / Annually switch and it opens on Annually, so the first figure you see is already the discounted one. Read on 19 September 2026, the website ladder is €49 for Business, €79 for Pro, €249 for Expert and €999 for Premium on the annual setting, against €59, €95, €299 and €1,199 when you click Per month. Free is €0 either way. That is roughly a sixth off for committing to a year, and it is the same trap in both directions: a screenshot of the default page understates a monthly subscription, while the monthly column is what you actually pay if you want to leave after three months.

Do extra languages eat into my page allowance?

Yes, and that is the number that decides your tier. Each plan states a ceiling of translated pages — 20 on Free, 400 on Business, 1,200 on Pro, 5,000 on Expert and 50,000 on Premium — and their own price calculator on the same page asks for your page count and your number of languages before it returns a plan, so the two multiply. A 300-page site in two languages is already 600 translated pages and therefore Pro, not Business, even though the site itself fits Business twice over. Count pages first with their page counter, multiply by the languages you actually intend to launch, and only then read the ladder.

Website or Online Store — which ladder applies to me?

They are separate ladders with separate entry prices, and a webshop is the expensive one. The Website tab starts free and includes several clones per plan: three on Business, five on Pro. The Online Store tab starts at €99 a month annually for Ecommerce Small and includes exactly one clone, then €299 for Medium and €499 for Large, with 1,000, 5,000 and 15,000 translated pages respectively. Product feeds are billed on top at €30, €50 and €75 a month per XML feed. Setup assistance is €250 on Small but €950 on Medium and Large. If you sell products, price the feed and the setup in from the start — the headline €99 is not the whole shop.

What does adding a clone or a language cost once I am on a plan?

The cards publish it, which is more than most tools in this category do. An additional clone with its pages is €25 a month on Business and Pro, and €50 a month on Expert and Premium; on Free the only step is an upgrade from 20 to 50 pages for €25 a month. Optional setup assistance is €250 on Business, Pro and Expert, and €1,000 on Premium. On the webshop ladder each extra XML feed is €30 to €75 a month depending on tier, and a non-translation clone starts at €49, €149 or €249. So the cheap way up is usually one more clone on your current plan rather than the next tier — until the page ceiling, not the clone count, is what you are hitting.

How many clones does Expert include?

Ten, and the page now says so in both places. Until 19 September 2026 the Expert card read '10 clones included' while the comparison grid lower down the same page listed 'Max. number of clones' as 1, 3, 5, 5, 20 across Free, Business, Pro, Expert and Premium — giving Expert five. We asked Clonable which figure was authoritative; they corrected the grid, and read again on 21 September 2026 it shows 1, 3, 5, 10, 20. At €249 a month against Pro's €79 the clone count is most of what the step up buys, so it was worth settling. Extra clones are sold at €50 a month each on Expert.

What should I weigh before buying Design & Content software?

Up to a point, and that point is usually set by your plan. Check whether brand kits, custom fonts, colour locking and template controls sit on the tier you would actually buy, since these are common upsells. Test it with your own assets during the trial rather than the demo content, which is always chosen to flatter the output.
