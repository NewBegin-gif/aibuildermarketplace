CloudTalk Review (2026)

Communication & VoiceResearched assessment · by Daniel HaketUpdated 2026-08-17

Communication tools win on reliability and coverage — every missed call or message is a customer who quietly moves on. So where does CloudTalk actually fit? Most people land here while weighing it against RingCentral, so that is the comparison this review keeps coming back to: does it do the job you actually need, without charging you for the parts you don't?

The honest bottom line. Worth it for support and sales teams that want a cloud call center with strong CRM integrations and call analytics. Skip it once you make only occasional calls — a basic VoIP line or your CRM's built-in dialer costs far less.

What CloudTalk does

Cloud call centre with call routing, analytics and CRM integrations, from €19/user/month on annual billing (Expert €49 needs at least three licences). AI voice agents are separate subscriptions from €99/month for 200 minutes, and number rental plus outbound minutes stack on top of the seat price.

The catch

What buyers report about CloudTalk

Recurring themes from G2, Capterra, Trustpilot and TrustRadius, read in August 2026. We have not run CloudTalk ourselves, so this is other buyers' experience rather than ours. We summarise it here because the complaints are usually the part a vendor page leaves out.

What they praise
  • • Local numbers in 160+ countries is the single most-cited benefit, and for distributed sales teams it is the reason to pick it. 4.4 on G2 across 1,700+ reviews.
  • • Reporting and general usability get consistently good marks.
  • • TrustRadius reviewers keep landing on the same three things: the IVR builder is visual and quick to change, call recording and monitoring are dependable, and setup takes minutes rather than a project.
What they complain about
  • • Call quality and dropped calls are the top G2 theme at 176+ mentions, with connection issues at 81+. For a phone system that is the complaint that matters most.
  • • The $19/month headline is not the bill. Add the dialer, AI features and CRM access and reviewers report $45-55 per user per month.
  • • The iPhone app cannot transfer calls or send SMS and quits in the background; iOS trails desktop.
  • • Annual billing is rigid — monthly signups occasionally billed for twelve months, and no refunds on unused term. Read the term before you sign.
  • • Live phone support is gated to higher tiers, and chat runs 2-4 hours against an advertised 5-minute SLA.
  • • The specific gaps TrustRadius reviewers name are narrower than the G2 complaints and worth checking against your setup: three-way calling, features for physical IP phones, call summary notes, thin documentation, and an admin level between owner and ordinary operator.
  • • Onboarding, requesting phone numbers and initial training come up as the rough edges of an otherwise fast setup.

Where the ratings disagree. TrustRadius is the platform to read last, not first. Its CloudTalk reviews are almost uniformly enthusiastic, but the newest written one we could find dates from February 2023 — the sample is from before the AI voice agents and the current packaging existed. G2 and Trustpilot are noisier and much more current, which is where the call-quality and billing complaints live. Where they agree is the mobile app.

When the paid plan is worth it

CloudTalk has no free tier: the 14-day trial is the only unpaid route, so the real decision is which paid plan. On annual billing the ladder is Lite €19, Starter €25, Essential €29 and Expert €49 per user per month, and Expert carries a minimum of 3 licences — awkward for a two-person desk. The clearest hinge is outbound dialling: the Power Dialer is a €15 per user per month add-on on the lower plans and comes bundled with Expert, which absorbs much of the step up from Essential. The Parallel Dialer stays an add-on at €39 per user per month. Upgrading the seat tier does not make the AI included. Conversation Intelligence renews at €9 per agent per month after a 30-day AI Analytics trial, and AI voice agents are separate subscriptions: AI Receptionist from €99 per month, AI Specialist €349 per month for 1,000 minutes, or — above 10,000 minutes a month — custom pricing CloudTalk advertises as low as €0.15 per minute. Telephony stays separate too: CloudTalk prices a US local or mobile port at €30 per request plus €2 per number, and a US toll-free port at €30 per request plus €4 per number.

How to actually use CloudTalk

The 14-day trial includes 50 AI Voice Agent minutes; buying a number is the fast path: CloudTalk documents at least one number type in 95 countries with no local address requirement or documentation; where a number type does require documents, carrier verification can add 1-2 days to processing time. The help centre prices a US local or mobile port at €30 per request plus €2 per number over 10-14 business days, lists German national numbers at 37 business days, calls a name/address mismatch with the losing carrier the #1 cause of rejected ports, and requires the old line to stay active until the port completes. CloudTalk documents up to 100 kbps per call, calls 1 Mbps per person sharing the connection a good start, and asks you to exclude *.cloudtalk.io from firewall inspection on ports 80 and 443. CloudTalk Go requires Android 12 (API Level 31) or higher and at least 3 GB of RAM, needs the 'Calling accounts' setting enabled or calls may not ring, and is not distributed via AppGallery, so Huawei users cannot install it. Frequently asked questions

What does CloudTalk actually cost per user?

The seat is €19 to €49 a month on annual billing (Lite €19, Starter €25, Essential €29, Expert €49), and Expert has a three-licence minimum. The bill reviewers describe is higher than the headline because the dialers and AI are priced separately: Power Dialer €15 per user a month, Parallel Dialer €39, AI Conversation Intelligence €9. Power Dialer is already in Expert. Read on 16 August 2026.

How much are CloudTalk's AI voice agents?

They are a separate subscription from the seat. AI Receptionist starts at €99 a month for 200 minutes; AI Specialist is €349 a month with 1,000 minutes included; above 10,000 minutes a month CloudTalk quotes custom volume pricing it advertises as low as €0.15 a minute. The 14-day trial includes 50 AI voice agent minutes, which is enough to test the flow and not enough to test the load.

Are those prices in euros or dollars?

Both appear on the same page. Read from an EU address on 16 August 2026 the plans and add-ons quote in euros, while the AI bundle also shows $99 for what the euro block prices at €99. Treat the currency as part of the quote and confirm it in your own checkout rather than converting ours.

Will CloudTalk work for a remote or distributed team?

Generally yes, since this category is built for it, but the details that bite are timezone handling, how per-seat cost scales as the team spreads, and whether numbers, routing or coverage exist in the countries your people actually sit in. Availability varies far more by country than the marketing pages suggest, so check your specific regions before committing.

Who should not use CloudTalk?

Skip it if you make only occasional calls — a basic VoIP line or your CRM's built-in dialer costs far less. Buying a tool to fix a problem you don't have yet just adds cost and another login to manage.
