What CloudTask does

Managed marketplace for LATAM go-to-market hires — SDRs, BDRs and similar — with payroll and compliance handled. $299/month membership, plus an all-in monthly rate per hire that is not published. Direct Hire instead costs 30% of first-year salary.

Pricing (2026)

(2026-08-15, read on their own pricing page) Three models, all published. The Hiring Membership is $299/month with no contract and cancel-anytime, and when you hire, each team member is billed as a single all-in monthly rate covering salary, compliance, payroll and support — that rate is not published, and it is the number that matters. Direct Hire has no access fee and costs 30% of first-year salary as a one-time payment, with a 6-month replacement guarantee against 24 months on the membership. Graduating a managed hire onto your own payroll is a flat $7,500. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

The $299 is the honest part and also the small part. What you will actually spend is the all-in monthly rate per hire, and that figure appears nowhere on the pricing page — you get it after you talk to them, once you are already invested in the process. Work out before you start what a comparable LATAM salary is on your own, so you can see what the managed layer costs on top. The $7,500 graduation fee is worth reading twice too: it is the price of ending the arrangement while keeping the person, which is exactly the situation a good hire creates.

Best for: companies hiring GTM roles — SDRs, BDRs and similar — who want LATAM talent without setting up their own payroll and compliance in another country, and who value the 24-month replacement guarantee.
Who should skip it: if you only need one hire ever, where Direct Hire's 30% one-time fee is cleaner than a subscription; also skip it if you cannot get the all-in monthly rate in writing before you commit.

Frequently asked questions

Is CloudTask compliant with local employment rules?

Compliance is country by country, so the honest answer is: only where the vendor says it is, and only for the entity type you actually have. Ask which countries are covered by its own entities versus a local partner, who carries the liability if something is filed wrong, and how changes in local law reach you. Get those answers in the contract rather than the sales deck.

Who should not use CloudTask?

Skip it if you only need one hire ever, where Direct Hire's 30% one-time fee is cleaner than a subscription; also skip it if you cannot get the all-in monthly rate in writing before you commit. Buying a tool to fix a problem you don't have yet just adds cost and another login to manage.
