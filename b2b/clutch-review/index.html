What Clutch does

B2B services marketplace \u2014 submit a free project brief and get matched with verified, best-fit agencies for your project, no research required.

Pricing (2026)

(2026) Free for buyers: submitting a project brief costs nothing and carries no obligation to hire. Clutch is a B2B services marketplace — you describe a project and get matched with about five verified, best-fit agencies. Agencies invest to be listed and matched; the buyer pays nothing. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

Best for: businesses that want to skip manual agency research and get matched quickly with vetted, best-fit service providers for a specific project.
Who should skip it: if you already have trusted agency relationships, or you'd rather research independently without agencies contacting you.

The honest knock

It's genuinely free to submit a brief — but be clear on what it is: a lead-generation marketplace, not a neutral directory. Your brief goes straight to agencies who will reach out to pitch you, and providers pay to be visible and matched. It's a fast way to shortlist vetted agencies; just expect the outreach that comes with it.

The natural comparison is G2 — G2 and traditional directories make you scroll and compare yourself; Clutch's brief-matching narrows it to a handful of vetted providers fast — the trade-off is that it's a matching marketplace where you'll field agency outreach. Decide by which one fits the job above, not by the louder brand.
