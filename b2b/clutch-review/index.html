What Clutch does

B2B services marketplace — submit a free project brief and get matched with verified, best-fit agencies for your project, no research required.

Pricing (2026)

(2026) Free for buyers: submitting a project brief costs nothing and carries no obligation to hire. Clutch is a B2B services marketplace — you describe a project and get matched with about five verified, best-fit agencies. Agencies invest to be listed and matched; the buyer pays nothing. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

It's genuinely free to submit a brief — but be clear on what it is: a lead-generation marketplace, not a neutral directory. Your brief goes straight to agencies who will reach out to pitch you, and providers pay to be visible and matched. It's a fast way to shortlist vetted agencies; just expect the outreach that comes with it.

What buyers report about Clutch

Recurring themes from Trustpilot, G2 and agency write-ups, read in August 2026. We have not run Clutch ourselves, so this is other buyers' experience rather than ours. We summarise it here because the complaints are usually the part a vendor page leaves out.

What they praise
  • • The phone verification process is genuinely the best in the category, and it is why buyers trust the reviews. 4.1 out of 5 on Trustpilot across 259 reviews.
  • • The buyer audience has high intent, and a free listing carries credibility even if it sends few direct leads.
What they complain about
  • • The revenue model puts paid placement and objective ranking in the same product. Reviewers increasingly say the rankings read as sponsorship.
  • • Sponsorship means a 12-month commitment from $12,000 to $60,000. Agencies are openly questioning whether the leads justify it.
  • • The strongest verification sits behind the paywall.
  • • Billing complaints and verification-badge subscription problems come up on Trustpilot.

When the paid plan is worth it

Clutch's help center documents free features for service providers, with premium advertising options on top: Sponsorship and Featured Listings. Clutch Verified is a separate paid upgrade. All three are sold to the providers being listed. A free profile covers a listing, client review collection, badges and widgets and a profile on sister-site The Manifest, so paying buys placement and depth. Clutch Verified raises the free profile's limit of 3 sharable case studies to what Clutch calls unlimited and adds up to 10 featured team members. Sponsorship and Featured Listings buy position: 9 advertising tiers, Starter through Quadruple Diamond, set by how contested a directory page or keyword is, and one of 15 Featured Provider positions shown in groups of 3. Clutch documents a 12-month agreement for both, Verified billed for the full 12 months upfront, no downgrade or cancellation inside the term, and cancellation notice at least 5 business days before renewal. If you cannot absorb a full year, skip it. Upgrading does not manufacture credibility either: the Premier Verified badge requires 3+ reviews, one within the past 12 months, a 3.0+ average and low to moderate credit risk.

How to actually use Clutch

As a buyer, the Project Brief walks you through your requirements and then recommends up to 9 service providers. Submissions can also feed the provider-side Pay Per Lead program, where Clutch builds a shortlist of up to five providers, matched on their bid amount and targeting criteria, their services and their organic rankings, with priority for active Pay Per Lead customers. As a provider, Clutch says profile setup takes 20 minutes or less, but the real gate is acceptance: the help center lists rejection grounds including a company that primarily sells software, tools or platforms, primarily sells to consumers, duplicates an existing or parent-company profile, or runs a blog or publication site. Reviewers need a LinkedIn account, Google account or company email address; Clutch says it manually verifies every submission, usually within 5 business days, and warns that a recent increase in submissions can stretch that. Reviews it cannot verify publish as Not Verified and do not affect the Clutch score or directory rankings. Frequently asked questions

Does Clutch replace my current setup or sit on top of it?

Usually it sits on top rather than replacing everything. Tools in this class tend to become the layer where work is tracked and handed off, while your existing systems stay the source of truth for their own data. Before you buy, map which tools it would replace outright and which it would merely sync with. If the answer is that it syncs with all of them, you are adding a layer rather than removing one.

Who should not use Clutch?

Skip it if you already have trusted agency relationships, or you'd rather research independently without agencies contacting you. Buying a tool to fix a problem you don't have yet just adds cost and another login to manage.
