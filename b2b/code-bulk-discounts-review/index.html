Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 16 Sep 2026

Two plans, billed in USD every 30 days. Free costs nothing and covers 500 lifetime codes with CSV export. Pro is $14.99 a month and lifts that to unlimited codes per month, with the same CSV export; both plans say they work with most discount applications. The plan table lists no annual rate, no free trial on Pro and no per-code charge, so the monthly figure is the whole cost. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

One influencer drop or one email segment can spend the whole allowance in an afternoon, and after that the only way on is $14.99 a month — so the free tier is a sample, not a plan. What the app does is also narrower than the word 'discounts' suggests. It takes a discount you have already created and multiplies it into unique codes you export as a CSV. It does not create the discount rule, does not send the codes to anyone, and does not report which ones were redeemed, so it sits between your discount settings and your email or SMS tool and needs both of those to be worth anything.

The one critical review on the listing shows what that costs in practice: when a merchant's parent discount broke after an unrelated change to their stack, every code generated from it went inactive and they learned about it from customers rather than from the app. The developer's answer is the right one to remember — the rules and logic belong to the parent discount, not to Code Bulk — but it means nothing in this app will tell you that a batch has stopped working.

The 5.0 rating also rests on 41 reviews, read 16 September 2026, which is a thin record next to the app's peers.

What Code Bulk Discounts does

Shopify app (by Optizio) that turns a discount you have already created into thousands of unique codes and exports them as CSV for email and SMS campaigns. Free covers 500 lifetime codes; Pro is $14.99 a month for unlimited codes.

What the $14.99 tier actually adds

The two plans differ in exactly one line: 500 lifetime codes becomes unlimited codes per month. Every other feature the table names — CSV export, working with most discount applications — is already on the free plan. So this is not a ladder with more product at the top; it is a meter, and Pro switches it off. That makes the sum unusually easy. Work out how many unique codes one campaign burns, and if the answer clears 500 over the life of the store, the free plan was never going to hold and $14.99 a month is the price of the app.

If you send one bulk campaign a quarter and each takes a couple of hundred codes, the free tier may carry you for a year before you have to decide anything.

How to actually use Code Bulk Discounts

Installation runs from the App Store, so setup starts inside your Shopify admin rather than at a separate signup. Read the permission screen before you accept it: alongside viewing customer data — name, email address, phone number, physical address, plus device, activity and geolocation data — and the store owner's record, the app asks to view and edit orders and discounts, including draft orders and discount codes. That is a write permission on the two things it touches rather than a read-only integration, which is what you would expect from something that creates codes for you, but it is worth knowing what you granted.

Verified on the listing's own data-access section, 16 September 2026. The order of operations matters more here than the setup does: the discount has to exist first. Create the discount you want in Shopify (or in whichever discount app already makes it), then point Code Bulk at it and generate the batch. Start with a small run of a few dozen codes and push them through your email or SMS tool end to end before you generate thousands, because the free plan's 500 are gone for good once you spend them and there is no trial on Pro to fall back on.

And because nothing here reports on a batch after it leaves, re-test a code yourself whenever you change the parent discount or the app that owns it.
