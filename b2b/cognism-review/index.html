What Cognism does

B2B contact data with a GDPR focus — phone-verified mobile numbers and intent signals.

Pricing (2026)

(2026-08-01, read on their own pricing page) No prices are shown. Two named plans, Standard and Pro, both stated to include 5 seats, with everything routed to 'Talk to sales' and 'Get a personalised quote'. The model is credit-based — their own wording is that 1 credit equals 1 revealed contact — with credits included in the subscription per seat, but no allowance is published. CRM Enrichment is sold standalone or as an add-on; Data-as-a-Service is priced by volume and delivery method. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

The honest knock

No price, no credit allowance, and a five-seat minimum on both named plans. That combination means the smallest deal is five seats of something you cannot price, so budget conversations start with a sales call rather than a number. The credit model is the part to nail down: one credit reveals one contact, so your real cost per usable lead is the credit price divided by your connect rate, and neither figure is published. Before the call, work out how many contacts a month you actually reveal — that is the only number that lets you judge the quote you get, and it is worth asking whether unused credits roll over, because nothing on the page says.

What buyers report about Cognism

Recurring themes from G2, Capterra and Trustpilot, read in August 2026. We have not run Cognism ourselves, so this is other buyers' experience rather than ours. We summarise it here because the complaints are usually the part a vendor page leaves out.

What they praise
  • • European coverage and GDPR-compliant sourcing is what reviewers pick it for over the American alternatives — 4.5 on G2 across 1,337+ reviews, 4.7 on Capterra.
  • • Phone-verified mobile numbers are the specific feature reviewers say justifies the price when they land.
What they complain about
  • • Data accuracy is the number one complaint by volume: 99 review mentions of inaccurate data, 62 of incorrect numbers, 58 of outdated contacts, 57 of incorrect information.
  • • North American coverage, especially mid-market, is noticeably thinner than European.
  • • Nothing is published. Platform fees start around $15,000 before per-user licences; a ten-rep team runs roughly $25,000-$30,000 a year on Grow and $40,000-$50,000 on Elevate.
  • • Annual contracts only, with reviewers describing no flexibility on billing terms and difficulty cancelling. Get the exit terms in writing before you sign.

Where the ratings disagree. 4.5 on G2 against 3.0 on Trustpilot. The G2 reviews are about the data; the Trustpilot ones are about opaque pricing, credit limits and renewal practice.

Best for: outbound sales teams in Europe who need mobile numbers that are actually verified and care about GDPR provenance — that is the specific thing this is better at.
Who should skip it: if you are one or two people prospecting, because both named plans include five seats; also skip it if your market is US-only, where the data advantage is smaller and cheaper options exist.

Frequently asked questions

Does Cognism replace my CRM or plug into it?

Most tools in this category plug in rather than replace. Check which direction the data flows and whether the sync is genuinely two-way, because a one-way push creates a second source of truth that your reps will quietly stop trusting. If it does aim to replace your CRM, price the migration honestly: history, custom fields and reporting rarely move across cleanly.

Who should not use Cognism?

Skip it if you are one or two people prospecting, because both named plans include five seats; also skip it if your market is US-only, where the data advantage is smaller and cheaper options exist. Buying a tool to fix a problem you don't have yet just adds cost and another login to manage.
