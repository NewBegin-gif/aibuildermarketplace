The free switch most people miss

Before changing exchange at all: if you are on Coinbase’s simple buy/sell mode, switching to Advanced Trade on the same account takes your fee from up to 3.99% down to 0.40–0.60%. Same platform, same account, no migration. For a lot of people that is the whole saving, without moving anything.
