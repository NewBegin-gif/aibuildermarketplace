Billed in USD every 30 days, with a 7-day free trial on each paid tier. Banner impressions are unlimited on every tier, the free one included, so the ladder gates features rather than traffic. Free costs nothing and covers the cookie banner and preferences, the data-request and Do-Not-Sell pages, one banner language and one cookie scan a month, with minimal Consentmo branding on the banner.

Standard is $10 a month or $100 a year (17% off) and adds unlimited cookie scans, multi-region compliance (GDPR, CCPA, PIPEDA and others), Google Consent Mode v2 and Meta, Microsoft and TikTok integrations, US opt-out signals, script blocking, multilingual banners with automatic detection, automatic Google Drive backups and removal of the branding. Plus is $37 or $370 a year and is where the accessibility side starts: web accessibility (ADA/WCAG), smart geotargeting, the EU withdrawal page for Directive 2023/2673, automated monitoring, scheduled scans, AI cookie categorisation, advanced analytics and full CSS customisation.

Enterprise is $64 or $640 a year for IAB TCF 2.3, cross-domain consent sharing, a checkout banner for Shopify Plus, Hydrogen and headless support, A/B testing, an alt-text manager, bot and crawler protection and priority support. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

The two things the listing leads with — web accessibility and a smart, region-specific banner — are not on Standard; they start at Plus, $37 a month, which is 3.7 times the entry price. A Shopify Plus store that wants a banner on the checkout needs Enterprise at $64, and so does anything headless or on Hydrogen. The accessibility side is a widget and a scanner: it overlays controls and flags issues, but it does not change the theme markup underneath, which is where an accessibility audit actually looks — treat it as a first layer, not as a remediation.

And the free tier puts Consentmo's own branding on the banner your visitors see first.

What Consentmo does

Shopify consent app that geolocates the visitor and shows the right GDPR, CCPA, PIPEDA or LGPD banner, with Google Consent Mode v2 and Meta, Microsoft and TikTok signals, cookie scanning and data-request pages — plus an accessibility widget and scanner from the Plus tier. Banner impressions are unlimited on every tier. Free plan; Standard $10 a month or $100 a year, Plus $37 or $370, Enterprise $64 or $640. Read 8 Sep 2026.

That is consent working as designed: blocked trackers collect nothing until a visitor agrees. No tier buys that data back.

How to actually use Consentmo

The vendor's own compliance checklist, last updated in March 2026, runs in a fixed order, and one step in it is easy to miss. Set your regions first, under the Cookie banner tab in the Geolocation section — but the app's Shopify Customer Privacy API integration applies only to stores that installed after 12 December 2024. An older install sets its regions under Settings, Customer Privacy in Shopify instead. Then set the initial state: Consent banner, Behavior, Default mode of checkboxes, every cookie group unchecked before consent.

Then link your privacy policy under Consent banner, Content. The step that catches people out is the tracking scripts you pasted into theme files yourself. Those fire before consent and are not blocked for you; the setup guide has a section for adding that blocking by hand. Free includes one cookie scan a month, which is enough to see what is actually loading, and Support, Diagnose site tells you whether the banner is live.
