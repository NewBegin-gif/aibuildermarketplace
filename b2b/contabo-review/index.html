Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 8 Oct 2026

The Core Cloud VPS line is priced for a 24-month term: Cloud VPS 4 (4 vCPU, 8 GB RAM, 100 GB SSD, 200 Mbit/s port) €4.40 a month, Cloud VPS 6 (6 vCPU, 12 GB, 200 GB SSD or 100 GB NVMe, 300 Mbit/s) €6.00, Cloud VPS 8 (8 vCPU, 24 GB) €11.20, Cloud VPS 12 (12 vCPU, 48 GB) €20.00, Cloud VPS 16 (16 vCPU, 64 GB) €29.60 and Cloud VPS 18 (18 vCPU, 96 GB) €39.20.

In the configurator for Cloud VPS 12 the term changes the price: 1 month €25.00, 12 months €21.25 (save 15%), 24 months €20.00 (save 20%), with no setup fee on any term; the 24-month term is prepaid, €480.00 due today. Add-ons in that configurator: a United Kingdom location €5.80 a month (EU free), Windows Server €53.00, cPanel €21.75, Plesk with Linux €12.00, Auto Backup €6.70 a month and double storage €6.00.

Every Cloud VPS has unlimited traffic under a fair-use policy (Contabo may throttle servers whose traffic is exceptionally high), DDoS protection, a firewall and IPv4 and IPv6. The pricing page also lists Performance VPS (Cloud VPS Plus 4 from €10.80), virtual dedicated servers (Cloud VDS S from €39.20) and dedicated servers (from €109.65 as a limited offer), all quoted for a 24-month term. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

Month-to-month costs about 25% more. Backups (€6.70 a month on that size), Windows Server, cPanel and Plesk are paid add-ons, and the servers are unmanaged. Traffic is unlimited only under a fair-use policy that lets Contabo throttle heavy users. Prices on the page exclude VAT. We have not used Contabo ourselves.

What Contabo does

Unmanaged cloud VPS, virtual dedicated and bare-metal servers with a lot of RAM and CPU per plan, unlimited traffic under fair use, and data centres in Europe, the US, Asia and Australia.

The cheap price is a two-year prepayment

Contabo's headline prices are the 24-month rate. On the Cloud VPS 12 the configurator showed €20.00 a month on 24 months, €21.25 on 12 months and €25.00 month to month, and the 24-month term is paid upfront: €480.00 due at checkout. The hardware per euro is the reason people buy it: 8 GB of RAM and 4 vCPUs for €4.40 a month on the smallest plan is far more memory than most cloud providers give at that price.

What you do not get is managed hosting: backups are a €6.70 add-on, Windows Server costs €53.00 a month on that size, and a control panel such as cPanel or Plesk is extra. Traffic is unlimited under fair use, and port speed rises with the plan from 200 Mbit/s to 1 Gbit/s. If you need a server for a month or two, compare the monthly-term price, not the headline.

How to actually use Contabo

Pick the plan by RAM and storage first; vCPUs are generous on every tier. Choose the term deliberately: the headline price needs 24 months prepaid, while 1 month costs about a quarter more and lets you leave. Add Auto Backup or plan your own backups before you put anything important on the server; it is not included. Select the region at checkout: EU locations are free, other regions such as the UK add a monthly fee.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a Contabo VPS cost?

Read on 8 October 2026 (EUR, excluding VAT): Cloud VPS 4 is €4.40 a month on a 24-month term; Cloud VPS 12 was €25.00 month to month, €21.25 on 12 months and €20.00 on 24 months.

Is there a setup fee?

The Cloud VPS 12 configurator showed no setup fee on the 1-, 12- and 24-month terms.

Are backups included?

No. Auto Backup was a €6.70 a month add-on on the Cloud VPS 12; snapshots are included (1 to 3 depending on the plan).

Does a Contabo VPS renew automatically, and how much notice do I give?

Yes. Contabo's terms (version effective December 2025, read 8 October 2026) say the contract extends by the same length as the term you picked unless either side cancels with four weeks' notice before the end of the term. Cancel in the customer panel at my.contabo.com or in writing (e.g. email). The terms list initial terms of 1, 3, 6 or 12 months; the 24-month term sold in the configurator is not named there, so check the renewal length on your order confirmation.

Who is responsible for backups on Contabo?

You are. The terms oblige the customer to back up data regularly, even when a backup add-on was agreed. Auto Backup is a paid add-on (see the price question above).
