Convert Review (2026)

What Convert does

Privacy-first A/B testing platform — experiments, personalization and feature rollouts, priced by tested users. Via our link: an exclusive 30-day full-access trial (standard is 15 days).

Pricing (2026)

(2026) 15-day standard free trial — through our link it's an exclusive 30-day full-access trial (verified 16 Jul 2026, no credit card). Growth $399/mo, or $299/mo paid annually ($3,588/yr), for 100K tested users a month; Pro $599/mo, or $420/mo annually ($5,040/yr); Enterprise on request. Overuse is metered — $399 per extra 100K on Growth, $699 per 250K on Pro (it can be switched off) — and each additional custom domain costs $1,000/year. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

The meter is tested users, and it keeps running: blow past your monthly allowance and overuse blocks land at $399-699 unless you hard-cap them — and every extra custom domain is $1,000 a year. The friendly $299 headline assumes annual prepay; month-to-month is $399.

What buyers report about Convert

Recurring themes from G2, Capterra, TrustRadius and Gartner Peer Insights, read in August 2026. We have not run Convert ourselves, so this is other buyers' experience rather than ours. We summarise it here because the complaints are usually the part a vendor page leaves out.

What they praise
  • • It bills per unique tested visitor, not per page load — one person viewing five pages counts once. That is a materially better meter than the competition uses.
  • • Reviewers get direct access to the people who build it, up to and including the CEO. For a testing tool that is unusual and it shows in the support ratings.
What they complain about
  • • $299/month annually on Growth for 100,000 tested users, $420 on Pro. Overage is $399 per extra 100,000. Reviewers call it expensive and that is the main complaint.
  • • The interface is simpler than Optimizely and VWO, which cuts both ways — less to learn, less to do.
  • • The newer visual editor uses a layout unlike the rest of the category and reviewers stumble over it.
  • • Onboarding below the Growth plan is thin.
Best for: CRO teams running serious A/B and multivariate testing who want a privacy-first, GDPR-friendly alternative to the big experimentation suites.
Who should skip it: if your traffic is modest — statistical significance needs volume, and 100K tested users a month is more than most small sites can use — or your landing-page tool's built-in testing covers it.

The natural comparison is VWO — VWO is the broad experimentation suite where testing is one module; Convert focuses squarely on A/B testing with GDPR-first positioning — compare the add-on costs (API, domains) on both before deciding. Decide by which one fits the job above, not by the louder brand.

Usually yes for the major platforms, but check yours specifically. Who should not use Convert?
