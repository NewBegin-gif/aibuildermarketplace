CrankWheel Review (2026)

Communication & VoiceResearched assessment · by Daniel HaketUpdated 2026-09-25

Mind which plan caps what: the free plan drops to 15 sessions a month after the first fortnight and Team puts everyone on a shared pool of 100, while Solo is the one paid tier with unlimited usage.

Best for: sales reps, insurance agents and consultants who need prospects to view a screen instantly with no download or install, on any device.
Who should skip it: if you need full video conferencing, webinars or team collaboration (Zoom/Meet fit better), or your call volume stays under the free tier.
Price: Free (15 sessions/mo after a 15-day unlimited trial); Solo $29/month, 1 user, unlimited usage · full pricing as we read it on 21 Aug 2026 ↓

Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 21 Aug 2026

A free plan with no payment details required — unlimited usage for the first 15 days, then up to 15 sessions a month for life. Solo is $29/month for one user with unlimited usage, up to 30 viewers per share and up to 10 video-conferencing participants. Team starts at $99/month for unlimited users sharing 100 meetings a month, rising in steps: Team 200 $194, Team 500 $467, Team 1000 $882, Team 2000 $1,562.
