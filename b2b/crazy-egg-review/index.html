Crazy Egg Review (2026)

Growth & RevenueResearched assessment · by Daniel HaketUpdated 2026-09-25

The entry plan is thin on recordings — 50 a month against Hotjar's far more generous free tier. Throughout this review we weigh it against Hotjar.

Best for: lean marketing teams that want heatmaps, recordings and built-in A/B testing in one consolidated tool — noting that testing starts on the $99 plan, not the $29 one.
Who should skip it: if you want generous recordings, automatic heatmaps and qualitative feedback — Hotjar's free tier and product depth suit product teams better.

Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 21 Aug 2026

Analytics starts at $29/month and scales on tracked pageviews across 5,000, 50,000, 150,000, 500,000 and 1,000,000 a month, starting at 50 recordings. Growth starts at $99/month, but its slider starts at 50,000 pageviews rather than 5,000, and includes 500 recordings plus A/B testing, CTAs and targeting by device type. Enterprise is quoted.

Frequently asked questions

What should I weigh before buying Growth & Revenue software?

It depends on whether you have the bottleneck it solves. Small teams get the most out of this category when one clear problem is already costing real hours or revenue; buying ahead of that just adds cost and another login. Price it against the hours or lost deals it removes, not against its feature list, and start on the smallest plan that covers the job.
