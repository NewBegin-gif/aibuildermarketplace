Credit Repair Cloud Review (2026)$49 Solo, $179 for Client Work

Financial OperationsResearched assessment · by Daniel HaketUpdated 2026-09-22

This is business-in-a-box software for starting a credit-repair company, not a tool that repairs your own credit — the price only makes sense if you're actually building a client business on it.

Best for: entrepreneurs building or running a credit-repair business who need client management, dispute automation and done-for-you letter workflows.
Who should skip it: if you just want to fix your own personal credit (you don't need this), or you're not prepared for the sales and compliance work a client business demands.

Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 21 Aug 2026

There are five plans, priced monthly. Grow is $299 for 6 users and 600 clients, and Scale $399 for 12 users and 1,200 clients. Enterprise is $599 for 24 users and 2,400 clients. Annual billing takes 20% off and is shown as a monthly equivalent on the four business tiers: $143.20, $239.20, $319.20 and $479.20.

Frequently asked questions

What should I weigh before buying Financial Operations software?

The bar here is higher than features. Check who actually holds the money and under what licence, whether client funds are segregated, what audit trail and permission controls you get, and how support behaves when something goes wrong mid-transaction. Anything touching payments should be able to answer those in writing; if it cannot, that is your answer.
