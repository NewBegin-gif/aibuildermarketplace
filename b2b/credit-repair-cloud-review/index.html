What Credit Repair Cloud does

The leading software to start, run, and grow a highly profitable credit repair business from anywhere in the world.

Pricing (2026)

(2026) Five plans from Personal about $49/mo up to Enterprise about $599/mo (Start ~$179, Grow ~$299, Scale ~$399), scaling by active clients and team seats; roughly 20% off annual, 30-day trial, no contract. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

This is business-in-a-box software for starting a credit-repair company, not a tool that repairs your own credit — the price only makes sense if you're actually building a client business on it. The middle tiers jump fast as you add clients and seats, and success depends far more on your sales and compliance discipline (credit repair is a regulated space) than on the software. Buy it as an operator, not a consumer.

What buyers report about Credit Repair Cloud

Recurring themes from the BBB, Trustpilot and CFPB filings, read in August 2026. We have not run Credit Repair Cloud ourselves, so this is other buyers' experience rather than ours. We summarise it here because the complaints are usually the part a vendor page leaves out.

What they praise
  • • 2,346 Trustpilot reviewers, with positive comments on service quality and support, and it remains the best-known software for running a credit repair business.
  • • Flat-rate pricing with no per-client fee is genuinely unusual in this category.
What they complain about
  • • An F rating at the BBB, for failing to respond to four of seven complaints. Not BBB-accredited.
  • • Complaints describe false representations about service outcomes, data import failures that disrupted customers' own client work, and unresponsive support on refunds.
  • • The CFPB reached an agreement with Credit Repair Cloud over allegations that it helped other credit repair businesses charge consumers illegal fees. If you plan to build a business on this, that regulatory history is your context too, not just theirs.

Where the ratings disagree. Trustpilot is broadly positive while the BBB rating is an F and a regulator has acted. Those describe different things: the software experience on one side, the company's conduct and the legal frame around the industry on the other. Read both.

Frequently asked questions

Is Credit Repair Cloud safe to run my finances through?

The bar here is higher than features. Check who actually holds the money and under what licence, whether client funds are segregated, what audit trail and permission controls you get, and how support behaves when something goes wrong mid-transaction. Anything touching payments should be able to answer those in writing; if it cannot, that is your answer.

Who should not use Credit Repair Cloud?

Skip it if you just want to fix your own personal credit (you don't need this), or you're not prepared for the sales and compliance work a client business demands. Buying a tool to fix a problem you don't have yet just adds cost and another login to manage.
