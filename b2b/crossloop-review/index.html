We tested it · 2026-11-13

We made one launch film in CrossLoop on 13 November 2026 from our own homepage address, using one of the two credits the account started with. The account is Daan's, and our AI assistant did the clicking. We downloaded, posted and shared nothing. We did not open Buy credits, so checkout, support and cancellation were not tested.

How we tested

  1. Used the 2 credits the account started with, no card
  2. Made one Launch Film from the homepage address and timed it
  3. Tried a description with an exact closing line twice
  4. Read the result page and stopped before downloads and checkout
  • The dashboard offers a Launch Film at $9 for 1 credit and a Launch Film Studio at $19 for 2 credits. It asks for a page address and a film style, and has an optional box of 400 characters to describe the film. The account started with 2 credits and no card was asked for.
  • We pasted our homepage address and chose Launch Film in English with the Match my brand style. The status moved from Queued to Reading your site to Rendering videos, and the film was ready after about 16 minutes. The vendor says about 15. Credits went from 2 to 1.
  • The result was a 61-second narrated film, offered as a 16:9 landscape cut, a 9:16 reel and story cut, a 4:5 portrait cut and a captions file, plus ready-made post text for LinkedIn and Instagram. The pictures and numbers in the film came from our own site. Films are kept for 30 days.

The catch

What CrossLoop does

Turns a public web page into a narrated launch video in three formats (16:9, 9:16, 4:5) with captions and suggested social copy, sold as one-time credit packs instead of a subscription.

Studio buys a different script

Launch Film at $9 turns your page into a narrated film; Studio at $19 uses two credits and adds Trend Pulse, which writes the script around current news and TikTok trends in your niche. That is a choice about the script, not about video quality, so try one Launch film first and see whether the plain version already says what you need. The packs only pay off if you make films regularly: 20 Studio films for $129 is about $6.45 a film against $19 for a single one (our arithmetic).

Because credits never expire, buying a pack is not a monthly commitment, but the money-back guarantee covers only your first purchase.

    How to actually use CrossLoop

    Pick the page that explains your product best, usually the home page or a product or launch page; it must be public and yours to use, and it should not contain private or personal data. CrossLoop reads the copy, colours, fonts and product shots and writes the script, voice-over and edit itself, so the quality of the film follows the quality of the page. The home page offers a free 10-second teaser, one per account with no card needed (read 24 October 2026), so you can see the style first.

    Then start with a single $9 Launch film, which falls under the 7-day guarantee on the first purchase. Download all three formats and the captions straight away: the terms say films are deleted after 30 days.

    Frequently asked questions

    Can you export your data from CrossLoop?

    Exit check 2026-11-13; route excerpt: No export or reimport was performed; came along (excerpts): not tested; lost (excerpts): not tested; re-import: not tested.

    Is CrossLoop a subscription?

    No. Read on 24 October 2026, credits are sold in one-time packs from $9 and never expire; the terms say there is no subscription.

    Can I edit the film CrossLoop makes?

    The pages we read describe what you get (three formats, captions, post copy) and that a failed render can be retried with one click, but they do not describe an editor or how many revisions you get. Ask hs@crossloop.ai before buying a pack if you need to change the script.

    Can I get a refund?

    There is a 7-day money-back guarantee on your first purchase only, and the credits from a refunded pack are removed. If a film fails to render, the credit comes back automatically.

    Who owns the films?

    The terms say you own the films made from your pages and can use them anywhere. They are deleted after 30 days, so download what you want to keep.
