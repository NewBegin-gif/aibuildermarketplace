We tested it · 2026-09-25

We opened a new Databox account through the 'Get started' button under the Free plan on databox.com/pricing, connected Google Analytics 4 and Google Search Console for our own site, asked Genie two questions about that data, let Genie try to build a dashboard, and read the credit meter under Settings → Billing after every step. One account, one evening, our own traffic data, not a month of team use.

  • The Free button does not give you Free. The account opened as a 14-day trial of Team Core at $249 a month, billed monthly. Free was listed in the app as a 'Downgrade'. Databox's own FAQ says that if no payment details are added by the end of the trial, syncing stops and reports pause, so anyone who wanted Free has to downgrade by hand before day 14.
  • The downgrade was blocked until we deleted Databox's own sample data. The message: 'You have 1 connected Spreadsheets, but the Free plan is limited to 0.' That one spreadsheet was the Sample dataset Databox adds at sign-up. Free allows no spreadsheets at all: Excel and Google Sheets both open an 'Upgrade to Analyst' wall at $89 a month, something the pricing page does not say.
  • Your one free dashboard is already taken. Databox's own Sample Dashboard stays after the downgrade and fills the plan's single dashboard slot. 'Create New Databoard' opened the $89 upgrade wall, and so did the button that would hang the sample dashboard on your own data.

Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 20 Oct 2026

Databox renamed and repriced its whole ladder since our August reading, so the Analyst/Pro/Growth tiers we described are gone. Free is $0 forever: 1 user, 3 data sources, 1 dashboard, 10 custom metrics, 50 AI credits a month, daily sync. Billed annually: Analyst $71/mo (1 user, 5 sources, unlimited dashboards and reports, 150 credits, hourly sync), Team Core $199/mo (3 users, 10 sources, 500 credits), Team Scale $319/mo (10 users, 30 sources, 1,000 credits, sub-accounts) and Agency from $79/mo (unlimited users, 4 clients, 20 sources, 300 credits), plus a quoted Custom tier.

Frequently asked questions

Is the free plan usable, or is it really a trial?

Both exist, separately. Read on 12 September and again on 2 October 2026: Free is $0 forever with 1 user, 3 data sources, 1 dashboard, 10 custom metrics, 50 AI credits a month and daily sync: workable for one person watching a handful of channels. Separately you can trial any paid plan for 14 days without a card. Do not assume the trial lapses quietly into Free: Databox's own FAQ says that if no payment details are added by the end of the trial, your data stops syncing and scheduled reports and notifications are paused.

What will it cost with our actual tool stack?

Count sources, not seats: Databox states it does not charge per seat. Read on 12 September and again on 2 October 2026, on annual billing: Analyst $71 a month covers 1 user and 5 sources; Team Core $199 covers 3 users and 10 sources; Team Scale $319 covers 10 users and 30 sources. Going past your source count means moving up a tier rather than paying per source, because the $5.60-per-source line we recorded in August is gone. The exception is Agency, from $79 a month with unlimited users and 20 sources, where client packs add 1 client, 5 sources and 50 credits for $20 a month each.

Will a higher plan make the data fresher?

Barely, and less than it used to. Sync is daily on Free and hourly on every paid plan, so the 15-minute interval that came with the old Growth tier is no longer something a plan buys: it is a $14 per-source add-on. Databox's own troubleshooting documentation also says data sources do not sync independently and only pull when metrics are actively in use, and that upstream provider limits shape the real refresh rate. Upgrading cannot backfill history that was never collected. Read 12 September 2026.

What counts as one data source?

Each property, account or store, not each tool. Databox's help article Add a data source (read 6 October 2026) separates the integration from the data source: Google Analytics 4 is the integration and each GA4 property is a data source, and the same goes for each HubSpot account, each Shopify store and each GitHub repository. Two GA4 properties and one Shopify store therefore use three of the five sources on Analyst. The AI credits are one pool for the whole account: every user draws from it and unused credits do not roll over to the next billing cycle, according to Understanding AI credits. Databox publishes a typical-consumption table there, with a help question at around 1 credit and a deep analysis or a new Databoard at around 11 (medians), so check it against your own use before choosing a tier.

What happens if I downgrade or cancel?

A downgrade takes effect at once: features and limits outside the new plan are disabled immediately, and the unused part of what you prepaid is credited toward your next invoice rather than refunded (Change your subscription plan, read 6 October 2026). Cancelling keeps data syncing until the subscription ends; then syncing is put on hold, some plans lose the ability to add connections or data sources, and AI credits keep renewing monthly until the end of a quarterly or annual term. Synced data stays readable while the account exists; once the account is closed it is kept for 30 days and then deleted. A full export is not self-service: Databox handles it through support case by case, where technically feasible, and the per-dataset CSV export is limited to select plans. Databox also warns that pausing sync can leave permanent gaps in metrics with historical data limits.
