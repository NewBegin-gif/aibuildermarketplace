We tested it · 2026-09-25

We opened a new Databox account through the 'Get started' button under the Free plan on databox.com/pricing, connected Google Analytics 4 and Google Search Console for our own site, asked Genie two questions about that data, let Genie try to build a dashboard, and read the credit meter under Settings → Billing after every step. One account, one evening, our own traffic data, not a month of team use.

  • The Free button does not give you Free. The account opened as a 14-day trial of Team Core at $249 a month, billed monthly. Free was listed in the app as a 'Downgrade'. Databox's own FAQ says that if no payment details are added by the end of the trial, syncing stops and reports pause, so anyone who wanted Free has to downgrade by hand before day 14.
  • The downgrade was blocked until we deleted Databox's own sample data. The message: 'You have 1 connected Spreadsheets, but the Free plan is limited to 0.' That one spreadsheet was the Sample dataset Databox adds at sign-up. Free allows no spreadsheets at all: Excel and Google Sheets both open an 'Upgrade to Analyst' wall at $89 a month, something the pricing page does not say.
  • Your one free dashboard is already taken. Databox's own Sample Dashboard stays after the downgrade and fills the plan's single dashboard slot. 'Create New Databoard' opened the $89 upgrade wall, and so did the button that would hang the sample dashboard on your own data.

Frequently asked questions

Is the free plan usable, or is it really a trial?

Not any more. On 12 November 2026 the pricing page lists no free plan and its FAQ says every account starts with a 14-day trial of the Platform plan instead. Earlier readings (12 September and 2 October 2026) had Free at $0 forever with 1 user, 3 data sources, 1 dashboard, 10 custom metrics, 50 AI credits a month and daily sync: workable for one person watching a handful of channels. The trial is 14 days without a card, and it does not lapse into a free plan: Databox's own FAQ says that if no payment details are added by the end of the trial, your data stops syncing and scheduled reports and notifications are paused.

What will it cost with our actual tool stack?

Count sources, not seats: Databox states it does not charge per seat. Read on 12 November 2026: the one self-serve plan, Business, is $399 a month billed annually ($499 monthly) and covers up to 10 users and 30 sources, so the question is whether you fit inside 30. The exception is Agency, from $79 a month with unlimited users, where each $20 account pack adds 1 account, 5 sources and 50 AI credits. In September and October the page also had Analyst at $71 (1 user, 5 sources), Team Core at $199 (3 users, 10 sources) and Team Scale at $319 (10 users, 30 sources).

Will a higher plan make the data fresher?

Barely, and less than it used to. In our September reading sync was daily on Free and hourly on every paid plan, so the 15-minute interval that came with the old Growth tier is no longer something a plan buys: it is a $14 per-source add-on. Databox's own troubleshooting documentation also says data sources do not sync independently and only pull when metrics are actively in use, and that upstream provider limits shape the real refresh rate. Upgrading cannot backfill history that was never collected. Read 12 September 2026.

What counts as one data source?

Each property, account or store, not each tool. Databox's help article Add a data source (read 6 October 2026) separates the integration from the data source: Google Analytics 4 is the integration and each GA4 property is a data source, and the same goes for each HubSpot account, each Shopify store and each GitHub repository. Two GA4 properties and one Shopify store therefore use three of the 30 sources on Business. The AI credits are one pool for the whole account: every user draws from it and unused credits do not roll over to the next billing cycle, according to Understanding AI credits. Databox publishes a typical-consumption table there, with a help question at around 1 credit and a deep analysis or a new Databoard at around 11 (medians), so check it against your own use before choosing a tier.

What happens if I downgrade or cancel?

A downgrade takes effect at once: features and limits outside the new plan are disabled immediately, and the unused part of what you prepaid is credited toward your next invoice rather than refunded (Change your subscription plan, read 6 October 2026). Cancelling keeps data syncing until the subscription ends; then syncing is put on hold, some plans lose the ability to add connections or data sources, and AI credits keep renewing monthly until the end of a quarterly or annual term. Synced data stays readable while the account exists; once the account is closed it is kept for 30 days and then deleted. A full export is not self-service: Databox handles it through support case by case, where technically feasible, and the per-dataset CSV export is limited to select plans. Databox also warns that pausing sync can leave permanent gaps in metrics with historical data limits.
