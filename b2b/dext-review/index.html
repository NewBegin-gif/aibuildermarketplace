Dext Review (2026)

Financial OperationsResearched assessment · by Daniel HaketUpdated 2026-07-03

Finance tools are trust-critical: the bar is accuracy, compliance and real support, not flashy features. So where does Dext actually fit?

What Dext does

Bookkeeping automation: capture receipts and invoices, extract the data automatically and sync to your accounting software — less manual entry.

Pricing (2026)

From ~£24/mo (Starter) to £42 (Growing) and £84 (Business) — roughly $31–239 depending on plan and region (2026). Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

Best for: accountants, bookkeepers and growing businesses drowning in receipts who want fast, accurate capture flowing straight into Xero, QuickBooks or Sage.
Who should skip it: if you're a sole trader with few receipts (pricey for low volume), or you want all-in-one accounting — Dext only does the capture layer.

The honest knock

It's a data-capture layer, not accounting software — it feeds Xero, QuickBooks or Sage, so you still need one of those. Extraction accuracy varies and sometimes needs manual fixes, the UI is tricky for beginners, the mobile app is more limited than desktop, and sole traders find it pricey.

The natural comparison is Hubdoc or manual entry — the free-with-Xero and the do-it-yourself routes. Decide by which one fits the job above, not by the louder brand.
