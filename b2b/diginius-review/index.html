Frequently asked questions

How many seats do I get for $75 a month?

The page does not say any more, and that is a change rather than an oversight on our part. When we read diginius.com/pricing/ on 21 August 2026 the plan cards sold Essential as 2 users, Pro as 5 and Core as 10. Reading the same page on 22 September 2026, the word user does not occur once — not in the comparison table, not on the four plan cards, not in the FAQ and not anywhere in the page source. Every other number survived the month unchanged, so this is not a repricing: the amounts are still $75, $250 and $500, and the keyword, page, lead and credit limits all match to the digit. What the page now publishes per tier is limits and support level, and the only account line left in the table reads Integrations: Unlimited. Treat the old 2 / 5 / 10 as what we saw in August rather than as a current commitment, and if more than one person needs a login, ask sales to put the seat count in the order before you sign. An unpublished limit is the easiest one to discover after you have paid.

Is Essential a real tool or a demo of the real tool?

Hold it against your own site and the answer is usually quick. Essential's 25 SEO keywords is the binding figure — most sites worth tracking have more than 25 terms that matter — while the 1,000 site pages will cover a small business site comfortably. Pro at $250 moves those to 500 keywords and 5,000 pages, which is a twentyfold jump in keywords for roughly triple the money, and that ratio is the clearest argument on the page for skipping the entry tier if SEO is why you are here. Lead intelligence tells the same story: 10 identified companies and 10 Bombora intent leads on Essential against 25 and 50 on Pro, plus 25 contact credits a month against 50. One caution on those lead numbers: the comparison table says weekly and the mobile plan cards say monthly for the Lead Intelligence row, a factor-of-four difference on the figure most likely to justify the subscription (read 22 September 2026). If the lead module is the reason you are buying, get that cadence confirmed.

Is the free trial 14 days or 30?

Both numbers are on the page at the same time, and they have been for at least a month. The banner above the plans offers a 30-day free trial with 100 intent leads a week and 100 contact info credits, after which your plan limits apply, and the desktop Essential card repeats the 30 days. Scroll to the stacked mobile cards on the same page and the Essential card says 14 day free trial. We recorded the conflict on 21 August 2026 and it is still there on 22 September 2026, unreconciled. The same pair of layouts disagrees about support as well: the table gives Core a Dedicated CS Rep, the mobile Core card says email support. None of that makes the trial a bad deal — 100 intent leads a week is ten times the Essential allowance — but it does mean the trial length is something to have in writing before you plan a fortnight of testing around it.

What should I weigh before buying Growth & Revenue software?

It depends on whether you have the bottleneck it solves. Small teams get the most out of this category when one clear problem is already costing real hours or revenue; buying ahead of that just adds cost and another login. Price it against the hours or lost deals it removes, not against its feature list, and start on the smallest plan that covers the job.
