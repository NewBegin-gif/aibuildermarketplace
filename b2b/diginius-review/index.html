What Diginius does

Digital marketing and e-commerce performance platform. Manage PPC, analytics and product feeds to improve online sales and visibility.

Pricing (2026)

(2026) Essential about $20/mo (single user); Pro ~$99/mo (5 users); Core ~$249/mo (10 users, most features); Business/custom above. ~20% off annual; 14-day trial. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

All-in-one marketing dashboards live or die on data depth per module: Diginius bundles PPC, SEO, lead intelligence and reporting affordably, but specialists (a dedicated SEO suite, a dedicated PPC optimizer) go deeper in their lanes — so buy it as consolidation for a lean team, not as a replacement for expert-grade tooling in your primary channel. Verify the integrations you depend on (especially Microsoft Ads, its historical strength) in the trial.

What buyers report about Diginius

Recurring themes from G2, Capterra and marketing-tool reviews, read in August 2026. We have not run Diginius ourselves, so this is other buyers' experience rather than ours. We summarise it here because the complaints are usually the part a vendor page leaves out.

What they praise
  • • Google, Microsoft, Meta and LinkedIn campaigns from one interface, with Bombora intent data folded in — reviewers name the single view across marketing and sales as the reason they use it.
  • • A 30-day trial with 100 intent leads a week, and 20% off annual across all plans.
What they complain about
  • • The integration set is smaller than the bigger platforms, so check yours is covered.
  • • Most users are UK agencies. In North America it carries little recognition, which matters if procurement needs references.
  • • The interface is functional rather than modern, custom calculations are limited and mobile dashboards are basic.
  • • Essential $75/month covers 2 users and 25 keywords; Pro $250, Core $500. Lead volumes are capped per tier.
Best for: lean teams and agencies that want PPC, SEO and lead intelligence consolidated in one affordable dashboard instead of five subscriptions.
Who should skip it: if your growth lives in one channel where a specialist tool's depth pays for itself, or you need enterprise analytics rigor.

The natural comparison is Semrush or channel-native dashboards — the specialist suites — deeper per channel at higher cost; Diginius wins on breadth-per-dollar for generalists. Decide by which one fits the job above, not by the louder brand.

Frequently asked questions

Is Diginius worth it for a small team?

It depends on whether you have the bottleneck it solves. Small teams get the most out of this category when one clear problem is already costing real hours or revenue; buying ahead of that just adds cost and another login. Price it against the hours or lost deals it removes, not against its feature list, and start on the smallest plan that covers the job.

Who should not use Diginius?
