Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 22 Sep 2026

Essential $75 USD a month, Pro $250, Core $500 and marked Most Popular, Business by contact with sales. Every amount held to the dollar against our 21 August 2026 reading. The FAQ on the same page confirms a 20% discount for annual payment. The plan limits scale with the price: SEO keywords 25 / 500 / 1000 / custom, SEO site pages 1000 / 5000 / 10000 / custom, Lead Intelligence leads 10 / 25 / 50, Bombora intent leads 10 / 50 / 100 per week and contact info credits 25 / 50 / 100 per month.

The comparison table labels the Lead Intelligence figure weekly where the mobile plan cards label the same numbers monthly. Support runs from email only to a Customer Success Rep on Pro and a Dedicated CS Rep on Core and Business, although the mobile Core card contradicts the table and says email support. The trial banner advertises 30 days including 100 intent leads a week and 100 contact credits and the desktop Essential card agrees, while the mobile Essential card still says 14 days.

Seat counts are no longer published: the 2 / 5 / 10 users we recorded on 21 August 2026 are gone and the word user does not appear anywhere in the page or its source. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

Correction after re-checking: Our earlier text quoted Essential at about $20 a month for a single user, Pro at $99 and Core at $249. Those figures are still in the page source on 21 August 2026, but only inside commented-out JavaScript that no longer runs. What the page renders is $75, $250 and $500 for 2, 5 and 10 users. The annual toggle belongs to that same dead code, so the switch is on the page but the amounts do not move when you use it; the 20% annual discount survives only as an answer in the FAQ.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

Diginius bundles PPC, SEO, lead intelligence and reporting affordably, but specialists — a dedicated SEO suite, a dedicated PPC optimizer — go deeper in their lanes. So buy it as consolidation for a lean team, not as a replacement for expert-grade tooling in your primary channel. Verify the integrations you depend on (especially Microsoft Ads, its historical strength) in the trial.

When the paid plan is worth it

The ladder is steep and it is bought with volume rather than with features: Essential is $75 a month, Pro $250 and Core $500, with Business quote-only. Each step is roughly triple the last, and what moves are the counters. SEO keywords go 25, then 500, then 1,000. Site pages go 1,000, 5,000, 10,000. Identified leads go 10, 25, 50 — the comparison table calls those weekly and the mobile plan cards call the same figures monthly — while Bombora intent leads go 10, 50, 100 a week and contact credits 25, 50, 100 a month.

Essential's 25 keywords is the figure to hold against your own site first, because it is the one that decides whether the entry plan is a tool or a demonstration. Two things paying up does not change. The first is the nature of the lead data. Diginius describes it as GDPR-compliant information the company has made publicly available, with no personal emails or mobile numbers, and that description is the same on every tier.

Core buys more of those records, not richer ones, and the annual option takes 20% off the same counters rather than raising them. The second is seats, and only because the page has gone quiet on them: on 21 August 2026 it sold Essential as 2 users, Pro as 5 and Core as 10, and on 22 September 2026 the word user appears nowhere on the page or in its source.

Nothing says the seat counts changed; what changed is that they are no longer published, so the number of logins you are buying is the thing to get in writing before you pay.

How to actually use Diginius

Every plan card routes to a trial or to sales, so the first decision is which door you take. The banner offers a 30-day free trial with 100 intent leads a week and 100 contact-info credits, after which your plan limits apply. Get the length confirmed in writing before you plan around it: the mobile plan card on the same page says 14 days where the desktop card says 30, and the page does not reconcile the two.

Once inside, Diginius says the platform opens on the Traffic Insights tab, with Digital PPC KPIs for advertising performance aggregated across networks, Sales and Goals for conversion, source, device and time-of-day breakdowns, and a Google Ads Explorer. Connect the sources before judging any of it. The listed integrations are Google, Microsoft, Facebook and LinkedIn ads, plus X, GA4 and your own website for SEO and site health, and consolidating those is the entire premise of the product.

Distilled from Diginius's own pages: the business pricing page (diginius.com/pricing/) and the Diginius Insight Software product page (diginius.com/diginius-insight-software/), read 21 August 2026; the business pricing page re-read 22 September 2026 for the price check, the trial and support discrepancies and the disappearance of the seat counts.

The natural comparison is Semrush or channel-native dashboards — the specialist suites — deeper per channel at higher cost; Diginius wins on breadth-per-dollar for generalists.

Frequently asked questions

How many seats do I get for $75 a month?

The page does not say any more, and that is a change rather than an oversight on our part. When we read diginius.com/pricing/ on 21 August 2026 the plan cards sold Essential as 2 users, Pro as 5 and Core as 10. Reading the same page on 22 September 2026, the word user does not occur once — not in the comparison table, not on the four plan cards, not in the FAQ and not anywhere in the page source. Every other number survived the month unchanged, so this is not a repricing: the amounts are still $75, $250 and $500, and the keyword, page, lead and credit limits all match to the digit. What the page now publishes per tier is limits and support level, and the only account line left in the table reads Integrations: Unlimited. Treat the old 2 / 5 / 10 as what we saw in August rather than as a current commitment, and if more than one person needs a login, ask sales to put the seat count in the order before you sign. An unpublished limit is the easiest one to discover after you have paid.

Is Essential a real tool or a demo of the real tool?

Hold it against your own site and the answer is usually quick. Essential's 25 SEO keywords is the binding figure — most sites worth tracking have more than 25 terms that matter — while the 1,000 site pages will cover a small business site comfortably. Pro at $250 moves those to 500 keywords and 5,000 pages, which is a twentyfold jump in keywords for roughly triple the money, and that ratio is the clearest argument on the page for skipping the entry tier if SEO is why you are here. Lead intelligence tells the same story: 10 identified companies and 10 Bombora intent leads on Essential against 25 and 50 on Pro, plus 25 contact credits a month against 50. One caution on those lead numbers: the comparison table says weekly and the mobile plan cards say monthly for the Lead Intelligence row, a factor-of-four difference on the figure most likely to justify the subscription (read 22 September 2026). If the lead module is the reason you are buying, get that cadence confirmed.

Is the free trial 14 days or 30?

Both numbers are on the page at the same time, and they have been for at least a month. The banner above the plans offers a 30-day free trial with 100 intent leads a week and 100 contact info credits, after which your plan limits apply, and the desktop Essential card repeats the 30 days. Scroll to the stacked mobile cards on the same page and the Essential card says 14 day free trial. We recorded the conflict on 21 August 2026 and it is still there on 22 September 2026, unreconciled. The same pair of layouts disagrees about support as well: the table gives Core a Dedicated CS Rep, the mobile Core card says email support. None of that makes the trial a bad deal — 100 intent leads a week is ten times the Essential allowance — but it does mean the trial length is something to have in writing before you plan a fortnight of testing around it.

What should I weigh before buying Growth & Revenue software?

It depends on whether you have the bottleneck it solves. Small teams get the most out of this category when one clear problem is already costing real hours or revenue; buying ahead of that just adds cost and another login. Price it against the hours or lost deals it removes, not against its feature list, and start on the smallest plan that covers the job.
