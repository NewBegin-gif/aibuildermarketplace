What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

All-in-one marketing dashboards live or die on data depth per module. Diginius bundles PPC, SEO, lead intelligence and reporting affordably, but specialists — a dedicated SEO suite, a dedicated PPC optimizer — go deeper in their lanes. So buy it as consolidation for a lean team, not as a replacement for expert-grade tooling in your primary channel. Verify the integrations you depend on (especially Microsoft Ads, its historical strength) in the trial.

When the paid plan is worth it

The ladder is steep and it is bought with volume rather than with features: Essential is $75 a month for 2 users, Pro $250 for 5 and Core $500 for 10, with Business quote-only. Each step is roughly triple the last, and what moves are the counters. SEO keywords go 25, then 500, then 1,000. Site pages go 1,000, 5,000, 10,000. Identified leads go 10, 25, 50 a week; Bombora intent leads 10, 50, 100 a week; contact credits 25, 50, 100 a month. Essential's 25 keywords is the figure to hold against your own site first, because it is the one that decides whether the entry plan is a tool or a demonstration.

What paying up does not change is the nature of the lead data. Diginius describes it as GDPR-compliant information the company has made publicly available, with no personal emails or mobile numbers, and that description is the same on every tier. Core buys more of those records, not richer ones. The annual option takes 20% off the same counters rather than raising them.

How to actually use Diginius

Every plan card routes to a trial or to sales, so the first decision is which door you take. The banner offers a 30-day free trial with 100 intent leads a week and 100 contact-info credits, after which your plan limits apply. Get the length confirmed in writing before you plan around it: the mobile plan card on the same page says 14 days where the desktop card says 30, and the page does not reconcile the two.

Once inside, Diginius says the platform opens on the Traffic Insights tab, with Digital PPC KPIs for advertising performance aggregated across networks, Sales and Goals for conversion, source, device and time-of-day breakdowns, and a Google Ads Explorer. Connect the sources before judging any of it. The listed integrations are Google, Microsoft, Facebook and LinkedIn ads, plus X, GA4 and your own website for SEO and site health, and consolidating those is the entire premise of the product.

Distilled from Diginius's own pages: the business pricing page (diginius.com/pricing/) and the Diginius Insight Software product page (diginius.com/diginius-insight-software/), read 21 August 2026.

Best for: lean teams and agencies that want PPC, SEO and lead intelligence consolidated in one affordable dashboard instead of five subscriptions.
Who should skip it: if your growth lives in one channel where a specialist tool's depth pays for itself, or you need enterprise analytics rigor.

The natural comparison is Semrush or channel-native dashboards — the specialist suites — deeper per channel at higher cost; Diginius wins on breadth-per-dollar for generalists. Decide by which one fits the job above, not by the louder brand.
