What Digit does

Inventory, production, purchasing and sales in one system for manufacturers and distributors. Starter $400/month with 5 users, Growth $750, Professional $1,300, extra users $63 each. Watch the add-ons: integrations beyond two, API and MCP access are $100/month apiece.

Pricing (2026)

(2026-08-15, read on their own pricing page) Four tiers with three published: Starter $400/month including 5 users, Growth $750 including 10, Professional $1,300 including 15, and Enterprise on quote. Extra users are $63/month each on every tier. Paying quarterly takes 10% off and annually 20%. Two integrations are included; each one after that is $100/month, and API access, MCP access and webhooks are $100/month each on top. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

The tier price is a floor, not a ceiling, and the add-ons are where an inventory system quietly doubles. Two integrations are included — but a working setup usually means an accounting package and a webshop, which is your two, and then Amazon or a second sales channel is another $100/month. Want the API, or your data inside an AI assistant? $100/month each. A Starter customer on QuickBooks, Shopify and Amazon with API access is at $600/month before adding a sixth user. Price the whole configuration you actually need, not the tier.

Best for: manufacturers and distributors outgrowing spreadsheets who need inventory, production, purchasing and sales in one system, and who genuinely need lot tracking and traceability rather than just wanting them.
Who should skip it: if you are a small operation where $400/month for five users is heavy against what a warehouse module in your existing accounting package would cost; also skip it if your integration list is long, because the $100-per-connection model punishes exactly that.

The natural comparison is Katana — the natural head-to-head for manufacturers leaving spreadsheets. Both cover inventory and production; the difference shows up in how they charge for the edges. Price the full configuration you need on each — every integration, the API if you want it — because Digit's $100-per-connection add-ons are where the tier price stops being the price. Decide by which one fits the job above, not by the louder brand.

Frequently asked questions

Does Digit replace my current setup or sit on top of it?

Usually it sits on top rather than replacing everything. Tools in this class tend to become the layer where work is tracked and handed off, while your existing systems stay the source of truth for their own data. Before you buy, map which tools it would replace outright and which it would merely sync with. If the answer is that it syncs with all of them, you are adding a layer rather than removing one.

Who should not use Digit?

Skip it if you are a small operation where $400/month for five users is heavy against what a warehouse module in your existing accounting package would cost; also skip it if your integration list is long, because the $100-per-connection model punishes exactly that. Buying a tool to fix a problem you don't have yet just adds cost and another login to manage.
