What doola does

Business formation and compliance made easy. Launch your US LLC or C-Corp from anywhere globally, complete with banking and tax management.

When the paid plan is worth it

doola's entry point, Starter at $297/year plus state fees, covers LLC formation, EIN application, a registered agent, and a US address — enough for a solo founder who already handles bookkeeping and taxes elsewhere. The concrete push toward paying more is tax filing: Starter explicitly excludes federal and state tax filing and bookkeeping tools, so anyone who needs those must jump to Tax and Compliance at $1,599/year (down from $1,999). A dedicated bookkeeper and monthly financial statements are gated further, at Business-in-a-Box, $2,399/year (down from $2,999) or $329/month. What no tier changes: EIN turnaround is set by the IRS, not by doola's plan — without a US Social Security Number, doola's own materials cite 4-6 weeks, extending to 8-10 weeks during tax season, regardless of which package you buy. State filing fees are also billed separately from every tier, so upgrading doesn't roll those into the subscription price.

How to actually use doola

Getting started means creating a doola account and submitting company name, state, member details, address, phone, and email — a step doola describes as taking about 15 minutes with no documents required up front. doola then files the LLC, averaging about a week depending on state. The formation documents needed for banking and tax purposes become available in 1-2 business days for US residents; opening a US bank account through doola's partner portal then takes another 3-5 business days, requiring a passport for international founders or a government ID for US residents. The recurring bottleneck reported is the EIN for founders without a US Social Security Number: doola's own guidance cites 4-6 weeks, extending to 8-10 weeks during tax season, since that step depends on IRS processing rather than doola's own speed. Until the EIN arrives, both bank account approval and tax filing stay blocked. Distilled from doola.com pricing and process pages, doola blog, and help.doola.com, read 6 Aug 2026

Frequently asked questions

Is doola safe to run my finances through?

doola isn't a bank and it doesn't hold or move your money. It forms the entity, acts as registered agent and keeps the filings and books straight; where it helps you open a US business account, the account and the balance sit with the partner bank, not with doola. So judge it on filing accuracy and accountability rather than custody: who is registered agent of record, what happens — and who pays — if a filing or return is late or wrong, how your EIN and identity documents are stored, and whether you can move your entity and records elsewhere without a fight. Get those answers in writing before you commit to a multi-year bundle.

Who should not use doola?

Skip it if you're a US resident comfortable filing formation yourself — the state fee plus a cheap registered agent costs far less than the bundle. Buying a tool to fix a problem you don't have yet just adds cost and another login to manage.
