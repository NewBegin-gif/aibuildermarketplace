Dripshipper Review (2026)

E-commerceResearched assessment · by Daniel HaketUpdated 2026-09-27View Dripshipper in the Atlas →

Read the ladder before you climb it.

Best for: someone who wants a turnkey private-label coffee or tea brand on Shopify — their roasting and packaging, your label on the bag — and is content to stay inside one product category.
Who should skip it: if you want product breadth, fulfilment outside the US, or control over sourcing and packaging cost — this is a single-supplier pipeline and the higher tiers only add coaching.

Frequently asked questions

What does Dripshipper cost, and is the annual plan worth taking?

Three monthly tiers, read on apps.shopify.com/dripshipper on 17 September 2026 and unchanged since 19 August: Starter $30, Professional $99, Elite $197, each with a 14-day free trial. Starter is also sold as an annual plan at $240 a year, which works out at $20 a month for exactly the same tier — a third off, rather than the usual ten or twenty per cent. It is also the only tier with a published annual rate, so if you want a year of Professional or Elite up front you will have to ask. The subscription is not the whole bill, though, and on this app it is not even the big line: product and shipping cost is charged per order on top, in USD, recurring every 30 days. Coffee is heavy, so work out the margin on a single bag before the plan price matters at all.

What do Professional and Elite actually add?

People, not fulfilment. The listing is explicit about it as read on 17 September 2026. Starter already carries unlimited orders, unlimited products, automatic order fulfilment, a 1-click product add and a dedicated support team — that is the working product. Professional at $99 adds live monthly training, priority support and a dedicated success manager. Elite at $197 adds monthly one-on-one coaching on top of that. Nothing above Starter changes how the coffee is roasted, bagged or shipped, and nothing changes the per-order cost you pay them. So the honest way to read the ladder is as a coaching subscription bolted onto a fulfilment service: worth it if you want someone walking you through building the store, and dead weight if you already know what you are doing. Moving Starter to the annual $240 saves more than either step up adds.

What kind of track record does the app have on Shopify?

Its own listing is the only public record, which is worth naming as a limit: this is the vendor's sales channel, not an independent panel. As read on 17 September 2026 it carries 159 reviews averaging 4.8, up from 142 averaging 4.7 a month earlier, so the recent direction is more reviews and a slightly better average. The spread is 130 at the top, then 14, 7, 4 and 4 — about one in twenty reviewers sits at the bottom two levels. What recent reviewers actually praise is the support and the hands-off fulfilment; the concrete gripe in the current top three is a discontinued blend, from a shop that has run the app over three years and says its customers missed it. That is the real constraint of this model in one line: a single supplier decides your catalogue. The app launched on 11 April 2018.

What should I weigh before buying E-commerce software?

Check your platform and version specifically. Shopify support is close to universal in this category, while WooCommerce, BigCommerce, Magento and headless setups vary a lot, and 'supported' sometimes means a generic API rather than a maintained app. A listing in your own platform's app store, with recent reviews there, is a better signal than the vendor's logo wall.
