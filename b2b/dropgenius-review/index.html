What DropGenius does

Generates a Shopify dropshipping store — products, branding and copy — as a starting template, not a finished business. Monthly runs $39 to $299, and annual billing drops Starter. Trustpilot sat near 1.6/5 at research time, with recurring complaints about charges after cancellation.

Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 5 Aug 2026

(re-verified 5 Aug 2026) Monthly: Starter $39, Pro $59, Empire $99, Unicorn $299, each with a 14-day free trial. Switch to annual billing and Starter disappears from the table; the rest become Pro $24/mo (billed annually at $288), Empire $57/mo ($684) and Unicorn $79/mo ($948). Their own FAQ still quotes an older $39.99-$299.99 range plus a $1.99 one-time store-creation fee, so the marketing copy and the checkout do not agree.

The store it builds runs on Shopify, which bills you separately — DropGenius does not state that cost anywhere on its pages. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

When the paid plan is worth it

The cheapest way to buy this is not the cheapest plan. Monthly the ladder reads Starter $39, Pro $59, Empire $99 and Unicorn $299. Switch to annual and Starter vanishes from the table entirely, while Pro drops to $24 a month ($288 a year), Empire to $57 ($684) and Unicorn to $79 ($948). So an annual Pro subscription costs $15 a month LESS than paying monthly for Starter, the tier below it -- and you get the better plan.

Those annual discounts are unusually steep: 59% on Pro and 74% on Unicorn. A discount that large normally means the monthly price is set high to make the annual look good rather than the annual being a bargain, so treat $24 as the real price of Pro and $59 as the penalty for staying flexible.

Two things that are not on the pricing table. Their own FAQ still quotes an older $39.99 to $299.99 range plus a $1.99 one-time store-creation fee, so the marketing copy and the checkout disagree -- ask which one the card will be charged. And the store this builds runs on Shopify, which bills you separately; DropGenius does not state that cost anywhere on its pages, so the real monthly outlay is this subscription plus a Shopify plan you have to price yourself. Every monthly tier carries a 14-day free trial.

How to actually use DropGenius

Know how to stop the charges before you start them. This carries a very poor Trustpilot rating — around 1.6 out of 5 at research time — with recurring reports of billing continuing after cancellation and refunds being difficult. Find the cancellation route in the account settings on day one, screenshot it, and screenshot the price you were actually charged.

Check that price carefully, because their own pages disagree. The FAQ still quotes an older $39.99-$299.99 range plus a $1.99 one-time store-creation fee, while the checkout shows Starter $39, Pro $59, Empire $99 and Unicorn $299 monthly. On annual billing Starter disappears entirely and the rest become $24, $57 and $79 a month — discounts of 59%, 42% and 74%, which is three different offers under one annual toggle.

Budget Shopify separately. The store this builds runs on Shopify, which bills you on its own, and DropGenius does not state that cost anywhere on its pages — so the subscription you are comparing is not the cost of having a store.

And treat the output as a starting template rather than a business. An AI-generated store in thirty seconds still leaves product selection, marketing and margin entirely to you, and those three decide whether any of it works.
