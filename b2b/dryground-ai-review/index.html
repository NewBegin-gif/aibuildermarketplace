What Dryground AI does

Full-stack AI implementation services: helps businesses design and integrate AI across their tech stack — for teams that want AI built and shipped, not just advised.

Pricing (2026)

(2026-08-17, read in a browser because the site renders client-side and a plain fetch returns only the title) No public pricing anywhere, and we checked before saying so: /pricing, /plans, /services, /about and /contact all return 404, the words “pricing”, “price” and “$” appear zero times on the homepage, and the navigation carries no pricing entry at all — it runs Solutions, Products, Verticals, Case Studies, Research, Book a Consultation.

Tiers do exist with no numbers attached: the AI Velocity line is described as “six tiers from pilot to full enterprise transformation” on 90-day outcome cycles. Budget this as a consulting engagement scoped per project, not a subscription you can read off a page. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

The honest knock

This is consulting, not a tool you log into: best when you want AI designed and built for you, not when you'd rather DIY with software. Read the headline numbers carefully too — the 20-50% efficiency and 10-20% revenue figures on their homepage carry the caption “2025 industry benchmarks”, so they describe the market rather than this firm's own results, and the case studies underneath them name no clients.

What actually drives the quote

There is no plan to weigh, and on 21 August 2026 the absence is documented rather than assumed: /pricing and /plans both return 404, and the llms.txt the firm now publishes for AI crawlers, 7,978 bytes covering products, services, verticals, research and key facts, carries no pricing section. The words pricing and price appear nowhere in it. Its single dollar figure is the $480M revenue the founder oversaw at Rackspace.

So what you negotiate is scope, and that file names the levers. Engagements are structured as 90-day outcome cycles rather than multi-year programmes. Inference is self-hosted on dedicated NVIDIA A100 80GB GPUs, a cost line a firm reselling API capacity does not carry and the reason a data-sovereignty requirement will move your number. The four service lines are not one purchase: a Custom AI Engineering build, the AI Velocity transformation programme and Adaptive AI Management, described as an ongoing centre of excellence combining consulting, engineering and managed services, sit at very different commitments. The free 45-minute consultation is the only price-discovery step on offer.

How to actually use Dryground AI

There is no signup and no dashboard, so getting started means a sales conversation: the only conversion path on the entire site is the Book a Consultation button, which hands you to Calendly (calendly.com/dg-keith/consultation). What they say happens after that is a Lean Six Sigma pass over the process first and the AI second, delivered in 90-day outcome cycles measured against business metrics. The four service lines are AI Consulting, AI Velocity, Adaptive AI Management and Custom AI Solutions; the products underneath are CompanyClaw (an autonomous operator built on OpenClaw), Nexa, and the Dry Ground Stack. You arrive at a scoped quote, not a plan you pick.
