Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 28 Aug 2026

Four tiers, all monthly. Free installs at no cost and gives you unlimited AI if you bring your own OpenAI or DeepSeek key, or free built-in AI for a small quota (50 products, 50 metafields, 100 variants, 5 articles). Starter is $4.99, Basic $14.99 and Pro $29.99, each raising the free-AI quota (100, 400 and 800 products respectively) — or, again, unlimited if you supply your own key.

The model's real pivot is that key: bring your own and you pay the AI provider's token costs directly for unlimited volume; rely on the built-in free AI and you are capped per tier. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

The honest knock

Auto-translation of new or changed content is reserved for the higher plans. And the track record is thin: 5.0 stars but only nine reviews, the app launched in July 2025, and it carries no 'Built for Shopify' badge — cheap and promising, but unproven.

What Easy Advanced Translations does

AI Shopify store translation (by Northwest): translate products, collections, theme text, metafields and even hard-coded and third-party-app text across languages, with a choice of engine (its own, Google, OpenAI or DeepSeek), image translation, protected terms and Shopify Markets support.

What the money actually buys

This is one of the rare translation apps where the tiers gate the AI quota more than the features — and where the cheapest path can be the free one. On every plan you can bring your own OpenAI or DeepSeek key and translate unlimited content; the subscription then buys convenience and the built-in free-AI allowance, which grows from 50 products on Free to 800 on Pro at $29.99. The upper plans also unlock auto-translation of new and changed content, which is the difference between a one-time translation and a store that stays translated as you add products.

So the honest calculation has two routes. Bring your own key and you pay the AI provider's token costs directly, with the subscription almost incidental — pick the tier for the auto-translation and convenience, not the quota. Lean on the built-in free AI and the tier is a straightforward quota ladder you climb as your catalogue grows. Either way the app translates deeper than most — theme text, metafields, hard-coded strings and even third-party-app text, with per-language images — which is where it earns its place against pricier incumbents, provided you accept a young app with a nine-review track record.

How to actually use Easy Advanced Translations

The app installs from the Shopify App Store (apps.shopify.com/advanced-translation) and works with any theme, no code required. The first decision is the engine: its own Basic Engine, or Google, OpenAI or DeepSeek — and if you pick OpenAI or DeepSeek you can add your own API key for unlimited volume, which is the app's cheapest route for a large store. From there you translate in bulk across the store's surfaces — products, collections, theme text, metafields, and even hard-coded strings and text from third-party apps — and can hand-edit any string afterward.

'Preset Terms' let you protect brand names or words that should not be translated, and you can replace images per language or generate them with AI. One expectation to set: automatic translation of new and changed content — so the store stays translated as you add products — is a higher-plan feature, so on Free or Starter you run translations manually. It pairs with Shopify Markets for serving the right language per market. Read on apps.shopify.com/advanced-translation, 28 August 2026.

Frequently asked questions

What does Easy Advanced Translations cost?

Four monthly tiers: Free (install-free), Starter $4.99, Basic $14.99 and Pro $29.99 (read 28 August 2026). Every tier gives unlimited AI if you bring your own OpenAI or DeepSeek key; otherwise you get a free-AI quota that grows from 50 products on Free to 800 on Pro.

What does 'bring your own key' mean for cost?

If you add your own OpenAI or DeepSeek API key, translation volume is unlimited but you pay that provider's token costs directly, separately from the subscription. Use the built-in free AI instead and you are capped by your tier's quota. For a large catalogue, your own key is usually the cheaper route.

How much does it translate?

More than most — products, collections, theme text, metafields, hard-coded strings and even text from third-party apps, plus per-language images (replaced or AI-generated). It works with any theme and pairs with Shopify Markets, with manual editing and protected 'Preset Terms' for words that should not change.

Is it a safe long-term bet?

It is cheap and capable but young: 5.0 stars across only nine reviews, launched July 2025, and no 'Built for Shopify' badge. If you need a proven, large-scale track record, a pricier incumbent like Weglot is safer; if price and translation depth matter more, this is a strong-value option with a short history.

What should I weigh before buying E-commerce software?
