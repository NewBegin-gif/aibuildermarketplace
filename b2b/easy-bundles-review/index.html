Easy Bundles Review (2026)

E-commerceResearched assessment · by Daniel HaketUpdated 2026-09-04

The pricing is tied to the thing the app is supposed to grow.

Best for: Shopify merchants who want build-your-own-box, gift-box, mix-and-match, fixed-price or volume bundles live in days rather than sprints, with bundle inventory syncing across Shopify and 3PLs and bundles that also sell through POS — and whose bundle revenue is still small enough to sit comfortably inside a published tier.

Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 4 Sep 2026

Four tiers billed in USD every 30 days, and the meter is the part that matters: you are charged on bundle sales, not on orders, products or staff. The free plan is free to install and covers up to $500 of bundle sales a month. Essential is $29 a month or $299 a year and lifts that to $3,000; Growth is $49 or $499 a year up to $5,000; Scale is $99 or $999 a year up to $10,000.

Annual billing saves 14% to 16% depending on the tier, and every paid tier has a 15-day free trial. Features climb the same ladder: free gives mix-and-match, fixed and volume bundles with templates and a custom or template design; Essential adds the single-step and multi-step bundle builders, landing-page templates, search and filtering, and multi-currency and multi-language support; Growth adds advanced discount logic, custom personalisation fields, add-ons, free gifts and subscription bundles; Scale adds AI-generated designs, advanced analytics, one-to-one onboarding and an account manager on Slack.

Two things the block does not say are worth noting before you commit. It does not publish what happens when you pass $10,000 of bundle sales in a month, and it does not publish an overage rate; the plan table simply ends there and links out to see all pricing options. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

The honest knock

A revenue meter reads as generous at the bottom — $500 of bundle sales a month free is a real trial, not a crippled one — but it means every tier upgrade is triggered by the app working, and the published ladder stops at $10,000 a month with no stated overage. A store doing well on bundles has to ask what comes next rather than read it, and that is the wrong moment to be negotiating.

Maturity is not the concern here: the app has been on the App Store since 15 September 2022 and carries 4.9 stars over 1,216 reviews as of 4 September 2026. The concern is where the money goes as you succeed. Two smaller things: the features most merchants picture when they think bundles — guided build-your-own-box, subscription bundles, add-ons and free gifts — sit on Growth at $49, not on the free plan, and the recent reviews that praise the app most warmly also praise the support team by name, which is a compliment to people rather than to a product that never needs them.

What Easy Bundles does

Shopify bundle builder for build-your-own-box, gift-box, mix-and-match, fixed-price, volume and subscription bundles, with bundle inventory synced across Shopify and 3PLs and POS support. Priced on bundle sales rather than orders: free up to $500 a month, Essential $29 up to $3,000, Growth $49 up to $5,000, Scale $99 up to $10,000, with 14-16% off on annual billing. 4.9 stars over 1,216 reviews as of 4 Sep 2026.

The natural comparison is Shopify's own Bundles app — Shopify ships a free, first-party bundles app that handles fixed and mix-and-match bundles with no meter on your revenue, and for a store that wants one simple bundle that is the cheaper answer and always will be. Easy Bundles is worth the $29 to $99 when the bundle is an experience rather than a SKU — a guided build-your-own-box or gift-box flow, subscription bundles, add-ons and free gifts, bundle landing pages in several currencies and languages — and when you want the bundle stock to stay in step with your 3PL without anyone reconciling it by hand.
