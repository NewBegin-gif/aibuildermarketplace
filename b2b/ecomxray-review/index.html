When the paid plan is worth it

The split between the two tiers is unusually clean, and once you see it the decision takes about a minute. Everything this app is sold on is on the FREE plan: cost price and gross margin, net units sold over 30, 90 and 365 days with refunds, cancellations and test orders stripped out, 30-day restock recommendations, and a revenue rank for your top products. The $29 a month buys editing, not insight: changing product name, description and price in place, SEO fields with automatic URL redirects, publishing to channels, managing collections and a full product activity log.

There is also a Chrome extension that shows stock value and lets you edit from your own storefront pages. So the question is not whether the analysis is worth paying for. It is whether you would rather make the changes here than in the Shopify admin you already have open. For a merchant working through a long restock list that is a real saving of clicks; for one who edits a handful of products a week it is $348 a year for a convenience layer.

How to actually use EcomXray

This is a Shopify app, so the install is the Shopify one: Install from the listing, approve the data access, and the app appears in your admin. There is a free plan and a 14-day trial on Pro, so nothing forces a payment decision on day one. The step that actually decides whether the app is useful happens before you open it. Gross margin is computed from Shopify's own cost-per-item field, which is empty by default on most catalogues.

If you install first and look second, you will see a margin column full of blanks and conclude the app does not work. Export your products, fill in cost per item, re-import, and only then judge the numbers. After that the reading order is the one the listing implies: revenue rank to find your top products, net units over 30, 90 and 365 days to see the trend with refunds and test orders already excluded, and the 30-day restock recommendation as the action.

On Pro the Chrome extension puts stock value and inline editing on your own storefront pages, which is the piece worth trying during the trial — it is the part that cannot be replicated in the admin.
