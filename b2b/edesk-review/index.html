What eDesk does

E-commerce helpdesk: one unified inbox for Amazon, eBay, Shopify and email, with order data and an AI Agent that bills per resolved outcome. Free plan; paid from $39/agent/mo (annual).

Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 17 Aug 2026

(re-verified 2026-08-17) Free plan to start; Essential $39 per agent/mo, Growth $89 (recommended), Professional $119, Enterprise custom — all billed annually, with monthly billing 20% higher ($49 against $39 on Essential). The AI Agent is included on every paid tier, but its automated resolutions bill separately at $0.99 per outcome: you pay when it actually resolves a query, not per attempt. eDesk claims the agent can automate up to 65% of support across channels, so model the outcome fee against your ticket volume — at 1,000 resolved tickets a month that is $990 on top of the seats. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

Two meters, and the second one is easy to miss: the per-agent price is the entry ticket, but the AI Agent charges $0.99 for every automated resolution on top — great when it deflects real tickets, but a variable line item that scales with your support volume, so model it against your ticket count before assuming a flat bill. And the honest fundamental for any e-commerce helpdesk: the value is only as good as your marketplace integrations actually syncing — test Amazon/eBay/Shopify connections with your real accounts during the free trial.

When the paid plan is worth it

eDesk's entry-level Essential plan ($39 per user/month billed annually, $49 monthly) covers a seller running a single online store or marketplace, with centralized messaging, live chat, and rule-based automation included. The concrete threshold is the store/marketplace count: Essential caps out at 1 connected channel, so anyone selling on multiple marketplaces (Amazon plus a Shopify store, for example) is pushed to Growth (5 stores, $89/user/month annual) or Professional (10 stores, $119/user/month annual, plus 2 free read-only users).

What upgrading does not solve is the AI Agent cost: eDesk charges $0.99 per resolved outcome on every plan, including Enterprise, so moving up tiers adds seats and channel capacity but does not reduce or cap the per-resolution AI fee, which scales linearly with support volume regardless of subscription level. API access is also gated separately: it requires the custom-priced Enterprise plan, not just a higher per-user tier.

How to actually use eDesk

Getting first value from eDesk means connecting at least one channel — a marketplace like Amazon or eBay, or a webstore like Shopify — through the App Store: browse channel tiles, click Install App, then complete that channel's authorization flow so its messages start flowing into the eDesk Mailbox. This requires an Admin login; team members without Admin rights have to request access before they can add anything. eDesk offers a 14-day full-feature free trial with no credit card required, so testing channel connections costs nothing upfront.

Where people get stuck is the API: the documentation flags that calls "can be quite complex to set up," and more importantly, API access to build custom integrations (orders, tracking, custom fields) is only available on the Enterprise plan — teams on Essential, Growth, or Professional who want programmatic access have to upgrade first, regardless of how many stores they've already connected.

Frequently asked questions

What does eDesk cost per agent?

$39 per agent a month on Essential billed annually ($49 monthly), $89 on Growth, $119 on Professional, with Enterprise quoted. Annual billing saves 20%. Read on 17 August 2026.

How does the $0.99 AI charge actually work?

It is per resolved outcome, not per attempt or per message: if the agent answers a 'where is my order' query end to end, that is $0.99. The AI Agent itself is included on every paid tier. The honest way to budget it is against your own deflection rate — eDesk claims up to 65% automation, and at a thousand resolved tickets a month the fee alone is $990.

Is the free plan enough to evaluate it?

It is enough to see the shared inbox and the channel integrations, which is the core of the product. What it will not tell you is the thing that decides the bill: how many of your tickets the AI Agent can close on its own. Run a paid month against real volume before committing to a year.

Will eDesk work for a remote or distributed team?

Generally yes, since this category is built for it, but the details that bite are timezone handling, how per-seat cost scales as the team spreads, and whether numbers, routing or coverage exist in the countries your people actually sit in. Availability varies far more by country than the marketing pages suggest, so check your specific regions before committing.

Who should not use eDesk?

Skip it if you run a non-commerce SaaS or service business (general helpdesks fit better and cheaper), or your ticket volume is low enough that a shared inbox already copes. Buying a tool to fix a problem you don't have yet just adds cost and another login to manage.
