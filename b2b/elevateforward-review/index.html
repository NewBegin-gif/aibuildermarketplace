Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 21 Aug 2026

The prices are published, despite the consultative first impression, and the report figures have not moved since our 12 August reading. An Elevate Insight Report is $500, a bundle of three is $1,350 at a 10% discount, and the Elevate Studio tiers render at $250, $500 and $750 a month. Those three are Elevate Strategy, Elevate Execution and Business Enablement, the last still marked coming soon.

A report is scoped to one focus area you choose, delivered within five business days of submission, with up to two rounds of feedback-based adjustments and a review consultation included. Treat the monthly figures as provisional: each price sits in a pair of spans whose second value reads $1,500 per report. For the three monthly tiers those second values are $8, $16 and $149.

The page carries no visible label for either state, so which figure a given buyer is shown cannot be settled from the markup. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

What ElevateForward does

An operational execution and alignment platform (Elevate Studio) for scaling companies, structured in three layers — Strategy (set priorities and initiatives), Execution (track initiatives, workstreams, ownership and KPIs), and Momentum (adoption, change management and follow-through). It turns strategy into structured, visible execution rather than task lists.

When the paid plan is worth it

The choice here is not a plan ladder but two different purchases. A single Elevate Insight Report is $500, and a bundle of three is $1,350 — a 10% discount. That buys a deliverable rather than access. You get one focus area you select, delivery within five business days, up to two rounds of feedback-based adjustments, an optional consultation to review the findings, and a licence to share it internally with leadership.

The subscription is the other product, and it is priced per month: Elevate Strategy at $250, Elevate Execution at $500, and Business Enablement at $750 still labelled Coming Soon. Read the cards before you budget. Each price sits in a pair of spans, and the second value of each pair is $1,500 per report, then $8, $16 and $149 a month. The page carries no visible label for either state, so which figure a given buyer sees is not something the markup lets us pin down.

That ambiguity was in our note of 12 August 2026 and it is still there. What the report does not buy is adoption: it is a diagnosis with permission to circulate it.

How to actually use ElevateForward

Buying is unusually direct for a product that presents itself as consultative. The Elevate Strategy and Elevate Execution cards, and the Single Insight and Bundled Package cards further down, link straight to Stripe-hosted checkouts on pay.elevateforward.ai: card details, no call, no quote. Two cards do not. The Elevate Insight Reports card at the top and the Business Enablement card both link back to the pricing page itself, so the headline Purchase Now is a loop and the real report checkout is the pair of cards lower down.

Where you land after paying is worth knowing first. The My Account / Log In link in the site header points at elevate-os-5a6324d2.base44.app over plain http, so the product runs on a Base44 subdomain rather than on elevateforward.ai. That is a fair thing to raise, along with the terms of the internal-use licence, on the consultation included with each report.

Distilled from ElevateForward's own pricing page (elevateforward.ai/pricing), including the checkout targets behind each plan card and the account link in the site header, read 21 August 2026.

The natural comparison is an EOS/Traction-style operating system plus your existing PM and BI tools — the pieces EF tries to combine into one.

Frequently asked questions

Does ElevateForward publish its prices?

Yes — and we had this wrong until 12 August 2026, so it is worth saying plainly. Their pricing page lists $500 for a single Elevate Insight Report and $1,350 for a bundle of three, a 10% discount, alongside the Elevate Studio subscription tiers. The consultative framing of the site makes it read like a book-a-call product, which is how we had it down as unpriced.

What do you actually get for $500?

One report on one focus area you select, not an engagement. Their page states delivery within five business days of submission, up to two rounds of feedback-based adjustments, a consultation to walk through the findings, and an internal-use licence so you can share it with leadership. If you want the ongoing product rather than the diagnostic, that is Elevate Studio and it is priced separately.

Are there independent reviews of ElevateForward?

Effectively none, and you should weigh that. In August 2026 we checked Capterra, GetApp, TrustRadius, G2, Product Hunt and SourceForge: Capterra's own listing states it is based on zero user reviews, and the others carry no listing for the product at all. What circulates instead is directory copy. That is not a mark against the product — it is a young, narrow B2B tool — but it means there is no buyer experience to check it against, so treat the $500 report as the trial.

What should I weigh before buying Operations & Workflow software?

Usually it sits on top rather than replacing everything. Tools in this class tend to become the layer where work is tracked and handed off, while your existing systems stay the source of truth for their own data. Before you buy, map which tools it would replace outright and which it would merely sync with. If the answer is that it syncs with all of them, you are adding a layer rather than removing one.
