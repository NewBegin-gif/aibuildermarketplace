What ElevenLabs does

Market-leading AI voice platform: hyper-realistic text-to-speech, voice cloning, dubbing, sound effects and conversational agents — with a developer API and a free tier to test voices before you pay.

Pricing (2026)

(re-verified 16 Jul 2026) Free $0 with 10k credits/mo; Starter $6/mo (30k credits, commercial license); Creator $22/mo — first month 50% off at $11 — with ~121k credits; Pro $99/mo (~600k credits, 44.1kHz PCM); Scale $299/mo (~1.8M credits, 3 workspace seats); Business $990/mo adds low-latency TTS. Extra usage runs roughly $0.36 down to $0.17 per additional minute depending on tier. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

Credits are the meter and retakes are the tax — regenerate a paragraph three times and you pay three times, so real usage lands well above the calculator estimate. And read the free-tier fine print: $0 gets you 10k credits to play with, but no commercial license — anything you publish commercially needs at least the $6 Starter. Voice realism genuinely leads the market; niche names and jargon can still need manual retakes, so run your hardest paragraph through the free tier before subscribing.

What buyers report about ElevenLabs

Recurring themes from G2 and Trustpilot, read in August 2026. We have not run ElevenLabs ourselves, so this is other buyers' experience rather than ours. We summarise it here because the complaints are usually the part a vendor page leaves out.

What they praise
  • • Voice quality is the reason it dominates — G2 ratings sit at 4.5+ and reviewers name output quality and ease of use first.
  • • The $11/month Creator plan carries most serious content production without needing a bigger tier.
What they complain about
  • • Credits forfeited on downgrade or cancellation is the single most repeated complaint. If you scale back, you lose what you already paid for.
  • • Failed generations are charged.
  • • Credits roll over on paid plans for two billing cycles only, then expire.
  • • Long calls and the Multilingual v2 model burn quota faster than teams expect — overage typically bites in month two or three after deploying an agent.

Where the ratings disagree. 4.5+ on G2 against 3.0 on Trustpilot across roughly 1,005 reviews. The gap is billing, not the product — nobody disputes that the voices are good.

Best for: creators, developers and product teams that want the most realistic AI voices available — voiceovers, audiobooks, dubbing, conversational agents and a well-documented API.
Who should skip it: if you generate hours of long-form audio on a tight budget (credit math punishes volume — flat-hour tools or the TTS built into your video editor may be enough), or you need acted emotional range only a human voice actor delivers.

Frequently asked questions

Does ElevenLabs fit my publishing workflow?

That depends less on features than on handoffs. Map where it sits between idea, production, approval and publishing, then count the manual steps that remain. A tool that adds an export-and-re-upload step is one you will quietly stop using. Run one real piece through it end to end before you standardise on it.

Who should not use ElevenLabs?

Skip it if you generate hours of long-form audio on a tight budget (credit math punishes volume — flat-hour tools or the TTS built into your video editor may be enough), or you need acted emotional range only a human voice actor delivers. Buying a tool to fix a problem you don't have yet just adds cost and another login to manage.
