HR & PeopleResearched assessment · by Daniel HaketUpdated 2026-08-08

The honest bottom line. Worth it for small and mid-size employers who want HR and payroll from one vendor and will genuinely use both. What Employment Hero does

Modular HR, payroll and recruitment platform for small and mid-size employers.

Pricing (2026)

(2026-08-01, read on their own pricing page) Sold as separate modules rather than one plan. Recruitment: a free ATS tier at $0, then $199, $359, $639 and $959 a month for 50, 100, 200 and 300 AI video interviews — their page notes no HR or payroll subscription is required for these. HR Essentials $10 per employee per month, HR Engage $14, Payroll $10. Chat is $2 per employee per month with a stated $20 a month minimum. Every price on the page carries an asterisk. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

There is no such thing as the Employment Hero price. HR, payroll, recruitment and chat are four separate subscriptions and three of them are per employee, so the number you want is a sum you have to build yourself. A twenty-person company on HR Engage plus Payroll is $14 plus $10 per employee, which is $480 a month before recruitment. The recruitment tiers are the odd one out: they are priced by AI video interviews rather than by people, so a hiring push moves that line independently of your headcount. And every figure on the page has an asterisk against it — find out what those say before you compare this with anything.

What buyers report about Employment Hero

Recurring themes from G2, Capterra, GetApp and Research.com, read in August 2026. We have not run Employment Hero ourselves, so this is other buyers' experience rather than ours. We summarise it here because the complaints are usually the part a vendor page leaves out.

What they praise
  • • Onboarding automates contracts, checklists and e-signatures properly — that is the part reviewers say saves the most manual chasing.
  • • From $8 per employee per month, with HR, payroll and benefits in one system.
What they complain about
  • • Support runs on AEST hours only, with slow responses and limited phone access. If you are not in an Australian timezone, that is a daily problem rather than an occasional one.
  • • Implementation runs from a few hundred to several thousand, and customisation, training or data migration can add $1,000-$10,000+.
  • • Minimum charges make it expensive for genuinely small teams.
  • • Help articles are poorly formatted with outdated instructions and in-app videos that do not play. Complex pay and leave scenarios cause difficulty.

Frequently asked questions

Is Employment Hero compliant with local employment rules?

Compliance is country by country, so the honest answer is: only where the vendor says it is, and only for the entity type you actually have. Ask which countries are covered by its own entities versus a local partner, who carries the liability if something is filed wrong, and how changes in local law reach you. Get those answers in the contract rather than the sales deck.

Who should not use Employment Hero?

Skip it if you only need one of the four modules — each is sold separately and priced as though you are buying the platform, so a dedicated payroll or ATS tool is usually cheaper on its own. Buying a tool to fix a problem you don't have yet just adds cost and another login to manage.
