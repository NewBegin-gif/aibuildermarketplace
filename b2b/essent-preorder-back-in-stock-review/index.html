What does Essent Preorder Back in Stock cost?

Free covers 25 pre-orders and 300 back-in-stock notifications a month. Lite is $4.95 a month for 100 pre-orders, Starter $8.95 for 300 and Essentials $24.95 for 500, with a 14-day free trial; a Professional plan with unlimited volume is $59 a month. Read on the Shopify App Store on 4 October 2026. On 17 November 2026 the app's own plan screen showed 30% off the first month of each paid plan (Lite $3.47, then $4.95) and no Free card, although the app started without asking us to pick or pay for a plan.

What happens when I go over my plan's pre-orders?

On a paid plan you pay per extra pre-order: $0.25 on Lite, $0.20 on Starter and $0.10 on Essentials, plus $1.00 per 100 extra notifications. For a free store the listing gives no rule after the 25th pre-order of the month, so check in the app before a launch.

Can the free plan take deposits?

Yes. The listing puts full and partial payments on the free plan. Mixed-cart validation and market-specific campaigns start on Starter. We could not confirm the free allowances inside the app on 17 November 2026: it showed no Free plan card.

Do I need Shopify Payments to hold or schedule pre-orders?

For two of the three fulfilment statuses, yes. The FAQ on essential-apps.com/preorder, read 22 November 2026, says Unfulfilled, Scheduled and On hold are all on the free plan, but Scheduled and On hold run through Shopify purchase options and need Shopify Payments. Without it you use Unfulfilled and manage the timing yourself. The same FAQ notes that putting a product on pre-order does not email its back-in-stock waitlist; that alert only goes out on a real restock.
