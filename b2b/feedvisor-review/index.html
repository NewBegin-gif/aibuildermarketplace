Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 3 Sep 2026

Feedvisor publishes no price, and we checked that again on 2026-09-03. feedvisor.com/pricing returns a 404. Their own llms.txt lists every public page and contains no pricing page and no amount. Treat those as indicative only, and expect a sales call. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

How to actually use Feedvisor

Bring three numbers to the call, because the quote is built from them: SKU count, monthly ad spend and monthly Amazon revenue. Feedvisor publishes no price at all — /pricing returns a 404 and its own llms.txt lists no pricing page — and the plan comparison it does publish ends every column in Request Demo rather than a figure. There is no way to arrive at a figure without that conversation, so arrive prepared for it.

Ask one specific question early: is the algorithmic Buy-Box strategy included in the tier you are being quoted? That is the part Feedvisor is famous for — the repricing that holds prices up rather than racing them down — and it sits on the higher pricing tiers rather than the entry one. A quote for the on-ramp is a quote for a different product. Then sanity-check the figure as a percentage of your Amazon revenue rather than in absolute terms.

Enterprise repricing and ad management are justified as a small share of what they manage; if the quote is not a small share of yours, a simpler repricer is the rational choice and there are several. And treat any figure you have read elsewhere, including ours, as third-party indication rather than a price.

Frequently asked questions

What does Feedvisor cost?

Feedvisor does not say. We checked the pricing URL (404), their own machine-readable site index (no pricing page) and the plans page itself (tiers named, no amounts) on 17 August 2026 and again on 3 September 2026. Figures circulating elsewhere — around $100 a month at the entry level, around $1,500 a month for the full platform — do not come from Feedvisor, so verify anything you are quoted against your own catalogue rather than against a number you read online.

How does it compare with Informed.co and the cheaper repricers?

The comparison people search for, and the honest split is scale rather than features. Feedvisor's algorithmic repricer optimises Buy Box share against margin in a way rule-based tools cannot, and reviewers above roughly $1M of Amazon revenue say the results justify the fee. Below that, one reviewer found it only marginally better than free Amazon tooling with the fee eating the incremental margin. With no published price, the only way to settle it is to get the quote and run it against your own gross margin.

Is Feedvisor only for Amazon?

Yes, and it matters for the total bill. Feedvisor is an Amazon platform; Shopify, eBay and other channels need separate tooling, so a multi-channel seller is budgeting for Feedvisor plus something else rather than for one system.

What should I weigh before buying Growth & Revenue software?

It depends on whether you have the bottleneck it solves. Small teams get the most out of this category when one clear problem is already costing real hours or revenue; buying ahead of that just adds cost and another login. Price it against the hours or lost deals it removes, not against its feature list, and start on the smallest plan that covers the job.
