What Feedvisor does

AI-driven Amazon and retail-media optimization: dynamic repricing, ad automation and analytics for brands and larger sellers on Amazon and Walmart.

Pricing (2026)

(2026) Essentials from about $100/mo (entry repricing for smaller catalogs); the real platform — Feedvisor360 with AI repricing, advertising optimization and dedicated support — starts around $1,500/mo. 14-day trial. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

The honest knock

The honest structure: the $100 tier is a limited on-ramp, and most of what Feedvisor is famous for (the algorithmic Buy-Box strategy that holds prices UP, ad management, analysts) lives behind the $1,500+ 360 tier — this is enterprise software for serious catalogs, priced accordingly. If your Amazon revenue can't absorb that as a small percentage, simpler repricers are the rational choice.

What buyers report about Feedvisor

Recurring themes from G2, Capterra and Amazon-seller reviews, read in August 2026. We have not run Feedvisor ourselves, so this is other buyers' experience rather than ours. We summarise it here because the complaints are usually the part a vendor page leaves out.

What they praise
  • • The AI repricer is best-in-class — balancing Buy Box share against margin targets in a way rule-based repricers cannot match.
  • • For sellers above $1M annual Amazon revenue, reviewers say the results justify it.
What they complain about
  • • A $1,500/month minimum for the full platform, on hybrid pricing that varies with SKU count, ad spend and complexity.
  • • One reviewer found it only marginally better than free Amazon tools, with the fee consuming most of the incremental gross margin. Do that arithmetic on your own numbers.
  • • Amazon-only. Shopify and eBay need separate tools.
  • • Independent reviews on Capterra and G2 both document difficulty cancelling, and the interface needs too many clicks.

Frequently asked questions

Is Feedvisor worth it for a small team?

It depends on whether you have the bottleneck it solves. Small teams get the most out of this category when one clear problem is already costing real hours or revenue; buying ahead of that just adds cost and another login. Price it against the hours or lost deals it removes, not against its feature list, and start on the smallest plan that covers the job.

Who should not use Feedvisor?

Skip it if you're a small seller (rule-based repricers cost a tenth), or your margins are set by factors repricing can't touch. Buying a tool to fix a problem you don't have yet just adds cost and another login to manage.
