Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 29 Aug 2026

(2026-08-29, read on their own pricing page) No prices are shown. Their own wording: all packages are custom built for your team's needs. The page describes five products — LOIS (AI features), Matters, Intake, Depositions and Signatures — and routes everything to a sales team for a custom quote. No per-user cost, no minimum seats, no contract length and no implementation fee is published, which is unchanged since 1 August 2026.

What the page does name now is a free way in: LOIS Explore, described as a free tier of LOIS for AI-powered legal research and drafting, with a trial that takes no credit card. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

The honest knock

In legal software the number that usually surprises people is not the per-seat rate but implementation: data migration from your existing case system, template configuration and training, quoted separately and often as a one-off in the thousands. That figure is absent here along with everything else. Ask for it as its own line, ask what the contract length is, and ask what happens to your matter data if you leave.

A custom quote is fine; a custom quote you cannot compare against a rate card is what you are trying to avoid.

When the paid plan is worth it

The pricing page publishes no rates: Filevine states that all packages are custom built for a team's needs and points buyers to its sales team for a custom quote. Filevine's help center states that case management features such as task assignment, deadline scheduling and operational analytics are not included in the LOIS free trial and require a separate subscription. Vinesign is where a tier boundary is visible. The documented free plan is three documents per month with single signatures, the Solo plan 30 documents sent per month with 20 signers per document, and Bulk Document Sends appears only in the Business column.

Depositions is separate again: since January 1, 2026 an active subscription is required to schedule proceedings or upload files. Firms choose a set number of hours for the year and pay a flat fee each month based on that number, instead of paying after each proceeding. Filevine documents that hosted proceedings include in-app scheduling, participant reminders and page:line and narrative summaries at no extra cost. But it notes some services may still require additional fees after the proceeding: certified transcripts, cancellations/reschedules, certificates of non-appearance or third-party vendor costs.

How to actually use Filevine

The LOIS Console trial signup takes a business email. Filevine documents that account setup can take up to one business day, and that the welcome mails come from reminders@filevine.com and freetrial@filevine.com and should be allowlisted. An address already attached to an existing Filevine Org is not eligible for this trial; existing customers are pointed to their Filevine representative instead. The trial's specialties are document analysis (up to 50 documents per chat, each file up to 5 GB), drafting and legal research.

A good-law check on a highlighted opinion passage is documented as taking about 20 minutes, sometimes up to 1-2 hours. Vinesign has its own route: the help center directs you to vinesign.com, again with a business email, and excludes Bulk Send during the trial. The platform route starts with an invite email, then Setup for profile, calendar sync and notifications; org admins get a separate Setup Guide for teams, members and roles.

Filevine's implementation checklist lists Customs Editor, Doc Generation, Taskflow and Deadline Chains as advanced tools Support has to enable.

Frequently asked questions

What should I weigh before buying Business Operations software?

Judge it on evidence rather than adjectives. Look for a published security page, single sign-on and role controls on the plan you would actually buy (they are often gated to higher tiers), a status page with real incident history, and a clear answer on where your data is stored. If the tool will hold anything sensitive, those four answers matter more than any feature comparison.
