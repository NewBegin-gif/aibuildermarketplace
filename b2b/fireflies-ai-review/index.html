What Fireflies.ai does

AI meeting assistant that records, transcribes and summarizes your calls across Zoom, Google Meet and Teams — searchable notes, action items and CRM sync, so nobody takes minutes by hand.

Pricing (2026)

Free plan with unlimited transcription (limited AI); Pro ~$10/seat/mo and Business ~$19/seat/mo in 2026. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

The honest knock

Paid tiers cap AI credits, and if you mainly want real-time in-Zoom captioning and simple English notes, Otter is the simpler pick.

Frequently asked questions

Will Fireflies.ai work for a remote or distributed team?

Generally yes, since this category is built for it, but the details that bite are timezone handling, how per-seat cost scales as the team spreads, and whether numbers, routing or coverage exist in the countries your people actually sit in. Availability varies far more by country than the marketing pages suggest, so check your specific regions before committing.

Who should not use Fireflies.ai?

Skip it if your meetings involve sensitive topics where an AI notetaker joining calls raises consent or confidentiality questions — check policy first. Buying a tool to fix a problem you don't have yet just adds cost and another login to manage.
