What FlexiQuiz does

Online quiz and test maker for educators, trainers and businesses: build graded exams, assessments and simple courses with certificates, timed tests, question banks and analytics — from a genuinely free tier.

Pricing (2026)

(2026) A genuinely free plan (up to 20 responses a month); paid tiers run roughly $17/mo (Essentials) to about $60/mo (Premium), with Enterprise above that (contact sales for Enterprise+). Annual billing saves 25%+. The response caps are the real ladder: roughly 500/month on Essentials versus 2,000 on Premium. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

The monthly response caps are the business model: 20 on free, roughly 500 on Essentials and 2,000 on Premium — one viral quiz or one big exam cohort and you're shopping a tier up. Price the cap you'll actually hit, not the sticker.

When the paid plan is worth it

The monthly response caps are the business model, and pricing this product on the sticker instead of the cap is how people end up on the wrong plan. Free stops at 20 responses a month. Essentials is roughly $17 a month for about 500, and Premium roughly $60 for 2,000, with Enterprise above that.

Work out the cost per response and the ladder turns out to be almost flat: about $0.034 on Essentials against $0.030 on Premium, and the 1,500 extra responses between them cost about $0.029 each. That is a 12% improvement for a plan that costs three and a half times as much. So there is no volume argument for climbing — you climb only when you hit the ceiling, and not a moment before.

Which makes the real question how lumpy your usage is. A trainer running a steady 400 responses a month sits comfortably on Essentials. One exam cohort of 600, or one quiz that gets shared further than expected, and you are shopping a tier up for a single month. Price the peak you will actually hit rather than the average, because the caps are monthly and a single spike is what breaks them.

Annual billing saves 25% or more, which is a larger discount than most tools offer and the one place where committing genuinely pays. Weigh it against the lumpiness above: if your volume is seasonal, an annual commitment on the tier that covers your peak may still beat paying monthly and upgrading twice a year.

How to actually use FlexiQuiz

The vendor describes the flow in three steps — Create, Publish, Analyze — and the useful detail is what sits inside each. You build the quiz from question types and a question bank, set test options such as timing and grading, then publish and share it; auto-grading is what removes the manual work afterwards, and the analytics come back per respondent and per question.

What to check on the free plan before committing is not the features but the ceiling. Free allows 20 responses a month, which is enough to prove the quiz works and nowhere near enough to run a cohort, so treat it as a build-and-test environment rather than a trial of the real thing. The vendor also notes that uploading media and having unlimited questions are premium capabilities — if your assessment leans on images, audio or a long question bank, that is a paid feature and worth confirming before you design around it.

The practical order that saves the most rework: build one complete quiz on Free, including certificates and timing if you need them, run it past a handful of real respondents, and only then count how many responses a normal month will produce. That number, not the feature list, is what picks your plan.

Best for: educators, trainers and small teams that want professional graded quizzes, exams and simple courses — with certificates, timed tests, question banks and analytics — without building out a full LMS.
Who should skip it: if you need a complete learning platform with deep integrations, or your volumes run past a few thousand responses a month — the caps make it the wrong shape.

The natural comparison is Typeform or Google Forms — the generic form tools win on price and ubiquity for simple surveys; FlexiQuiz wins on exam features — grading, certificates, timed tests and question banks. Decide by which one fits the job above, not by the louder brand.

Frequently asked questions

Does FlexiQuiz replace my current setup or sit on top of it?

Usually it sits on top rather than replacing everything. Tools in this class tend to become the layer where work is tracked and handed off, while your existing systems stay the source of truth for their own data. Before you buy, map which tools it would replace outright and which it would merely sync with. If the answer is that it syncs with all of them, you are adding a layer rather than removing one.
