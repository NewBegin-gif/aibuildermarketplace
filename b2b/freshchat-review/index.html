On this page
We tested it · 2026-10-08

The Freddy AI Agent in a Freshchat trial account, built in AI Agent Studio. We wrote a short help page for a made-up headset shop (shipping, returns, warranty, two products, opening hours: about 250 words) and gave the agent its URL as the only knowledge source; learning finished in about two minutes. We then ran 13 customer questions through the studio's Test screen: 10 whose answer is on the page, one that needs a small inference (shipping to Japan when the page lists only three countries) and two whose answer is not on the page at all (PayPal, and battery life with noise cancelling on). We scored every reply against the page.

  • 11 of the 13 replies were correct, and every factual reply linked the help page as its source. That includes the trickier ones: an order at 4pm on a Tuesday ships on Wednesday because of the 15:00 cut-off, a cable broken by pulling is excluded from the warranty, and 'Do you ship to Japan?' got a clear no with the three countries we do ship to.
  • It did not invent anything where the page was silent. Asked about PayPal, it said the help centre has no information on payment methods. Asked for battery life with noise cancelling on, it gave the documented 40 hours, said that figure is with noise cancelling off, and declined to give a number for the other case.
  • The one wrong reply was 'Can I call you?'. The page says there is no phone support; the agent answered 'Sure! Assigning your chat to our support agent.' It treated the question as a request for a human and started a handover, and the 'Sure!' reads as a yes. The Test dashboard still counted this reply as Answered.

Other add-ons on the same page: Freshcaller at $15/$39/$69 per agent per month annually ($18/$47/$83 monthly) on Growth/Pro/Enterprise, campaign contacts at $100, connector app tasks at $80, and day passes at $2/$7/$12.

Frequently asked questions

What does the AI agent cost on top of the seat price?

Freddy AI is billed separately from seats. On the pricing page read 1 September 2026, AI Agent sessions cost $49 per 100 sessions beyond the 500 complimentary sessions each paid account receives once, and the Freddy AI Copilot add-on is $29 per agent a month billed annually ($35 monthly). Seats start at $19 per agent a month on Growth billed annually ($23 monthly); the Free plan covers up to 10 agents.

Does the AI agent stick to my own help content?

Mostly. In our test on 8 October 2026, built on a single help page of our own, 11 of 13 replies were correct and each factual reply linked the page. It declined to guess about PayPal and about a battery figure the page does not give. It went wrong on 'Can I call you?', answering 'Sure!' and starting a handover although the page says there is no phone support, and once offered help the page never mentions.

Can I rely on the Test screen's answered count?

Only as a first filter. In the same 8 October 2026 test the dashboard showed 12 answered and 1 unanswered: the wrong handover reply counted as answered, and the correct 'no information about payment methods' reply counted as unanswered. Read the replies themselves before going live.
