Pricing (2026)

A free plan for up to 10 agents; paid Growth about $19, Pro $49, Enterprise $79 per agent/mo billed annually (a little more monthly), with Freddy AI billed as a usage-based add-on (2026). Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

What Freshchat does

AI-powered customer messaging from Freshworks: live chat and chatbots across your website, app, WhatsApp and Instagram, with a unified agent inbox and automation that deflects repetitive questions.

When the paid plan is worth it

Start with what the free plan is worth rather than what the paid ones cost, because ten agents free is unusually generous for live chat. On Growth pricing at about $19 an agent a month, those same ten seats would be around $190 a month — so the free tier is not a demo, it is roughly $2,280 a year of product for teams that fit inside it. The paid ladder runs about $19 for Growth, $49 for Pro and $79 for Enterprise per agent per month on annual billing, a little more monthly.

Both steps cost the same $30 a seat, but they are not equally sized: Growth to Pro is a 2.6x jump in rate, Pro to Enterprise 1.6x. On a ten-agent team that is $300 a month either way, which is the number to hold against whatever the tier adds. What pushes teams off free is rarely agent count. It is routing and AI: the higher-tier routing rules sit behind paid plans, and Freddy AI is billed separately as a usage-based add-on rather than being included in any tier.

That last point is the one that breaks budgets built from seat counts, because usage does not scale with headcount — it scales with conversation volume. Get the Freddy AI unit and rate in writing before you plan around a per-agent figure. One structural thing to weigh that has nothing to do with price: Freshchat is one channel inside the wider Freshworks suite. If you need a full help desk with ticketing, you will be nudged toward the platform, and buying chat alone can turn into buying the suite.

That is fine if it was going that way anyway, and expensive if it was not.

How to actually use Freshchat

Run the free plan first and do not treat it as a trial. It covers up to ten agents, which is more people than most small support teams have, so there is no clock — the sensible approach is to work in it until something specific is missing rather than until a date passes. When something is missing, name it before you upgrade. The paid tiers are mostly about routing and AI, so the honest question is whether your queue is complex enough to need rules.

If conversations currently reach the right person because your team is small enough to see them all, Growth at about $19 an agent buys you nothing yet. Budget Freddy AI separately whatever tier you land on. It is a usage-based add-on rather than a feature of any plan, so ask what a session costs and estimate your monthly volume before you build a workflow around it. And decide in advance whether you want the wider Freshworks suite: being part of it means you will be nudged toward Freshdesk, Freshsales and the rest, and each is a separate subscription.
