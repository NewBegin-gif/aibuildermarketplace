Pricing (2026)

On Freshdesk: Growth $19 per agent per month, Pro $55, Enterprise $89, all requiring annual billing, with a stated 20% annual saving and no monthly rate shown. Freddy AI Agent is included with the first 500 sessions.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

Freshworks is not a product, it is five of them, and the page you land on when you search for its price does not contain a price. Once you find Freshdesk's own page, two things sit on top of the number. The listed rates require an annual commitment, and Freddy AI Copilot — the AI the marketing leads with — is $29 per agent per month extra, which is 53% on top of a Pro seat.

The Freddy AI Agent that is included stops after 500 sessions, with no published rate for what comes next. If you want support and IT and CRM, price three subscriptions, not one.

What buyers report about Freshworks

Recurring themes from G2, Capterra, TrustRadius and Trustpilot, read in August 2026. We have not run Freshworks ourselves, so this is other buyers' experience rather than ours. We summarise it here because the complaints are usually the part a vendor page leaves out.

What they praise
  • • It is one of the fastest help desks to stand up, and reviewers rate the interface as clean and easy to navigate — about 4.4 on G2 across roughly 3,700 reviews.
  • • Strong value at the small and mid-market end, which is where the positive reviews concentrate.
What they complain about
  • • Reporting depth and serious AI both sit behind a bigger bill. Teams either love the speed or hit that wall.
  • • Costs and limits creep up as you scale — reviewers describe it as sneaking up rather than jumping.
  • • Reports of support email processing failing for days at a time.

Where the ratings disagree. 4.4 on G2 against 2.5 on Trustpilot across 367 reviews, and the Trustpilot side is dominated by auto-renewal billing. Diarise the renewal date before you sign.

When the paid plan is worth it

Two things to settle before comparing tiers. First, Freshworks is not one subscription: the main pricing page carries no prices at all and links out to each product, and Freshdesk, Freshservice, Freshsales, Freshchat and Freshcaller are separate subscriptions with their own ladders. If you want two of them, you are buying two products. Second, on Freshdesk every tier requires annual billing -- Growth $19 per agent, Pro $55, Enterprise $89 -- and no monthly rate is shown at all. The page advertises a 20% annual saving against a monthly price it never prints, so there is no flexible option to weigh.

The step from Growth to Pro is the expensive one at nearly three times the price, and it is worth asking exactly which of your requirements sit above that line before accepting it. The AI story complicates it further: Freddy AI Agent is included with the first 500 sessions, but Freddy AI Copilot is a separate add-on at $29 per agent per month and only on Pro and Enterprise. Adding Copilot to Pro takes an agent from $55 to $84 -- a 53% increase -- and it is not available on Growth at all, so “we want the AI assistant” is really a decision to buy Pro plus an add-on.

Count your agents carefully. Everything here is per agent per year with no monthly escape, so an over-estimate is locked in for twelve months.

How to actually use Freshworks

Start on the product's own page, not the Freshworks one. The page you land on when you search for Freshworks pricing carries no prices at all — it lists the products and links out — so decide first which product you are buying: Freshdesk for support, Freshservice for IT, Freshsales for CRM, Freshchat, Freshcaller. Each is a separate subscription with its own page, and wanting two of them means budgeting two bills.

On Freshdesk, budget three things beyond the seat price. The listed rates — Growth $19, Pro $55, Enterprise $89 per agent — all require an annual commitment and no monthly rate is shown at all. Freddy AI Copilot, the AI the marketing leads with, is $29 per agent a month extra on Pro and Enterprise, which is 53% on top of a Pro seat. And the Freddy AI Agent that is included stops after 500 sessions, with no published rate for what follows — so ask what session 501 costs before you design a workflow around it.

Then work out your total across products before comparing Freshworks to an all-in-one competitor. Support plus IT plus CRM is three subscriptions, three annual commitments and three sets of add-ons, and no page on their site will add that up for you.

Is Freshworks secure and reliable enough to depend on?

Judge it on evidence rather than adjectives. Look for a published security page, single sign-on and role controls on the plan you would actually buy (they are often gated to higher tiers), a status page with real incident history, and a clear answer on where your data is stored. If the tool will hold anything sensitive, those four answers matter more than any feature comparison.
