Gamma offers four pricing tiers: Free ($0), Plus ($8/user/month), Pro ($16/user/month), and Enterprise (custom pricing). Annual billing provides 20% savings. The Plus plan includes advanced templates and collaboration features, while Pro adds priority support and advanced analytics. Enterprise offers custom integrations and dedicated account management.

FAQ

Is Gamma really free to use?

Yes, Gamma's free tier includes 400 AI credits per month, unlimited presentations, and basic templates. However, it lacks advanced features like custom branding, priority support, and team collaboration tools that most businesses need.

What's the difference between Gamma Plus and Pro plans?

Plus ($8/month) includes 800 AI credits, advanced templates, and basic collaboration. Pro ($16/month) adds 1,600 AI credits, priority support, advanced analytics, custom themes, and enhanced export options for professional use.

Does Gamma charge overage fees for AI credits?

Yes, additional AI credits cost $0.025 per credit after your monthly allocation. Heavy users can exceed their limit quickly, potentially doubling monthly costs. Monitor usage through the dashboard to avoid surprises.

Can I negotiate Gamma Enterprise pricing?

Enterprise pricing starts around $35/user/month for 50+ users. You can negotiate volume discounts (10-30% off), annual payment terms, and custom feature additions. Leverage competitor quotes for better positioning.

When should I upgrade from Gamma's free plan?

Upgrade when you hit the 400 AI credit limit, need team collaboration, require custom branding, or want priority support. Most active users upgrade within 2-3 months of regular use.

Hidden Costs Analysis

AI Credit Overages

Cost: $0.025 per additional credit

Heavy users frequently exceed monthly allocations. A complex presentation with multiple AI-generated slides can consume 50+ credits. If you exceed by 1,000 credits monthly, that's an additional $25.

Premium Integrations

  • Advanced Salesforce integration: $5/user/month
  • Custom API access (below Enterprise): $50/month base fee
  • White-label branding (Pro add-on): $100/month

Support Upgrades

  • Phone support for Plus users: $10/user/month
  • Dedicated training sessions: $500 per session
  • Custom template creation: $200-500 per template

Storage Overages

Plans include 10GB (Plus) to 100GB (Pro) storage. Additional storage costs $2/GB/month. Teams uploading high-resolution images and videos can hit limits quickly.

Cost Optimization Strategies

Choose Annual Billing

Save 20% immediately by paying annually. For a 10-user Pro team, this saves $384 yearly ($1,920 annual vs. $2,304 monthly billing).

Monitor AI Credit Usage

Track usage through the dashboard. Optimize by:

  • Using templates instead of generating from scratch
  • Editing AI suggestions rather than regenerating
  • Batch creating similar presentations
  • Training team on efficient AI prompt writing

Right-Size Your Plan

Start with Plus for most teams. Only upgrade to Pro when you need priority support, advanced analytics, or enhanced export options. Many teams over-buy initially.

Leverage Free Tier Users

Keep occasional users on the free tier. Only upgrade active users who need collaboration features. A mixed approach can save 30-50% on total costs.

Enterprise Negotiation Tips

Volume Discounts

Request 10-15% discounts for 50+ users, 20-25% for 100+ users, and 30%+ for 500+ users. Gamma typically offers tiered volume pricing.

Contract Terms

  • Push for 30-day termination clauses instead of annual locks
  • Negotiate price protection for 2-3 years
  • Include user growth allowances (25% overage buffer)
  • Request service level agreements (SLAs) for uptime and support

Custom Features

Enterprise plans can include custom integrations, additional storage, extended version history, and specialized training at no extra cost if negotiated upfront.
