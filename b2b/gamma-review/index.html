We tested it · 2026-09-28

On 28 September 2026 we gave Gamma, Beautiful.ai, Prezi and Canva the same brief for a ten-slide investor deck: eleven facts about a made-up bakery chain and the rule to add no other numbers. We checked every deck against 15 tests written down before the first one was generated. Gamma ran on a new Free account.

  • Gamma asked us to pick a style, then its outline pane stayed empty until we wrote 'Please draft the outline.'; the finished deck was ready about seven minutes after the prompt.
  • It added no numbers of its own, kept both charts to scale and marked the missing market figures as [MARKET SIZE] and [COMPETITIVE LANDSCAPE]. It left out the founding year.
  • Asked once to 'Change the ask to €450,000 for 12%', it updated all three places the amount appeared and showed the old and new slide side by side. It was the only one of the four to change the deck completely from one prompt.

When the paid plan is worth it

Gamma's free tier works as a sampler rather than a workspace: the vendor's help centre states each new free user receives 400 credits at signup, and those credits do not refresh, so the meter simply stops. A paid plan repays itself for anyone publishing decks or sites on a routine basis, since credits refill every month. The pricing page lists 1,000 monthly credits on Plus, 4,000 on Pro, 20,000 on Ultra and 10,000 per seat on Business, and unused credits roll over up to twice the monthly amount.

Plus also raises the single-prompt ceiling from 10 slides to 100 and removes Gamma branding. Annual billing is advertised as saving up to 25%. What upgrading pointedly does not buy is unlimited AI. The developer docs price generation at 1-3 credits per card and 34-75 credits for a premium image, and estimate a doc with 20 cards and 15 premium images at roughly 320-1070 credits, so image-heavy output eats a monthly allowance fast.

Nor does it solve seat economics: on Plus, Pro and Ultra every member who accepts an invitation is charged as a seat, and Business is €420 a seat a year (€35 a month, billed annually) with seats bought upfront. Read 29 September 2026.

How to actually use Gamma

The shortest route to a usable result starts on the Gamma dashboard: click Create new AI and choose Generate, Paste or Import. Generate takes a topic prompt, Paste reformats text written elsewhere, and Import accepts a PowerPoint, a document or a URL. Pasting existing text tends to reach something presentable fastest, since the model is styling material instead of inventing it. The common stumbling block is the free workspace, which caps a single prompt at 10 slides where Plus and above allow up to 100, so long outlines quietly arrive truncated.

Refinement happens per card through the sparkle icon at the top-left of a slide. Exporting runs from the Share button at the top right, then Export, in PDF, PNG or PPTX. The other trap is the watermark: the "Made with Gamma" logo appears on exports from free accounts. Removal depends on the gamma originating inside the paid workspace, so decks first created on a free account have to be copied into that workspace before export.

Frequently asked questions

What does Gamma cost in 2026?

Re-read on 29 September 2026 in the browser, in euros from a Dutch address: Free €0, Plus €10 a month (€8 annual, €96 a year), Pro €24 (€18 annual, €216) and Ultra €86 (€77.33 annual, billed €928 per seat a year) — the same as in August. Business is €35 per seat a month, billed annually at €420; the Team plan at €18 a seat that stood beside it in August is no longer listed. Gamma prices per seat and quotes in the currency of your location, so treat these as the shape of the ladder and confirm your own figure at checkout.

Are Gamma's free credits monthly or one-off?

One-off. The free plan gives 400 credits at signup and does not refill them; the monthly credit allowance starts on the paid plans — 1,000 a month on Plus, 4,000 on Pro, 20,000 on Ultra, and 10,000 per seat on Business; unused credits roll over up to twice the monthly amount. This is the single most common surprise reviewers report, because every AI edit spends credits, not just the first generation.

Is there a plan with unlimited AI use?

No. Every paid tier is metered in credits, including the €86 Ultra plan, which Gamma positions as roughly 20x the usage rather than as unlimited. If your team generates all day, price the credit ceiling rather than the seat.
