Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 16 Oct 2026

Three packages, each priced per background check, with no setup fees, no minimums and no long-term contracts. Standard, marked Most Popular, is $39.95 per check and adds biometric identity verification and county criminal searches in every county on the candidate's full address history. Advanced, marked Recommended, is $49.95 per check and adds a federal criminal search across every federal district on that same address history.

Frequently asked questions

What does a GCheck background check cost?

Basic is $18.95, Standard $39.95 and Advanced $49.95 per check, with no setup fee, minimum or contract. Third-party fees may be added at cost and are not listed. Read on gcheck.com/pricing-packages on 29 September 2026.

What is the difference between GCheck's Basic and Standard packages?

Basic searches county court records only in the candidate's current county; Standard searches every county on their full address history and adds biometric identity verification. Advanced adds federal criminal searches.

Can I order a GCheck check online without talking to sales?

Not from the pricing page: on 29 September 2026 every Get Started button there led to the same contact form as Book a Demo.
