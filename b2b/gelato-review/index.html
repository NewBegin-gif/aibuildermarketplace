What Gelato does

Global print-on-demand platform: design, produce and ship custom products (apparel, wall art, mugs, photo books) locally in 30+ countries with no inventory — integrates with Shopify, Etsy and WooCommerce.

Pricing (2026)

(2026) A free plan ($0 forever) with core print-on-demand; Gelato+ is about $29.99/mo (or $19.99 annual) adding premium mockups, a stock-image library and year-round product discounts (up to ~25–35%); Platinum is custom for high volume. Gelato+ tends to pay for itself past ~10–20 orders/month. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

The honest knock

The free plan is genuinely usable, so the honest question is only whether Gelato+ earns its fee — and that depends entirely on volume: below ~10 orders a month the discounts won't cover the subscription. Its real edge is local production in 30+ countries (fast, cheaper international shipping), but that means quality and product availability can vary slightly by region and print partner. Sample your key products before scaling.

Recurring themes from the Shopify App Store and print-on-demand reviews, read in August 2026. We have not run Gelato ourselves, so this is other buyers' experience rather than ours. We summarise it here because the complaints are usually the part a vendor page leaves out.

What they praise
  • • 4.8 out of 5 with 90% five-star on Shopify. It routes each order to the nearest print facility, so international customers get local shipping speeds and costs.
  • • Wall art quality is called best-in-class, and t-shirt prints hold colour with minimal fading after five washes. Support replies within about an hour.
What they complain about
  • • Quality varies between facilities — the same design can come out differently in different countries. That is the trade-off for local production.
  • • The apparel range is narrower than the specialists.
  • • No phone support and no high-touch service.
  • • On razor-thin margins the per-item cost does not work.

Frequently asked questions

Is Gelato worth it for a small team?

It depends on whether you have the bottleneck it solves. Small teams get the most out of this category when one clear problem is already costing real hours or revenue; buying ahead of that just adds cost and another login. Price it against the hours or lost deals it removes, not against its feature list, and start on the smallest plan that covers the job.

Who should not use Gelato?

Skip it if you sell only a handful of orders a month (stay on the free plan) or your customers are concentrated in one country where a single-hub competitor is simpler. Buying a tool to fix a problem you don't have yet just adds cost and another login to manage.
