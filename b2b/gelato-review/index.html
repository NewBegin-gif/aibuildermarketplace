What Gelato does

Global print-on-demand platform: design, produce and ship custom products (apparel, wall art, mugs, photo books) locally in 30+ countries with no inventory — integrates with Shopify, Etsy and WooCommerce.

Pricing (2026)

(2026) A free plan ($0 forever) with core print-on-demand; Gelato+ is about $29.99/mo (or $19.99 annual) adding premium mockups, a stock-image library and year-round product discounts (up to ~25–35%); Platinum is custom for high volume. Gelato+ tends to pay for itself past ~10–20 orders/month. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

The honest knock

Its real edge is local production in 30+ countries (fast, cheaper international shipping), but that means quality and product availability can vary slightly by region and print partner. Sample your key products before scaling.

When the paid plan is worth it

The free plan is $0 forever and genuinely usable for print-on-demand, so Gelato+ has to earn its fee out of savings rather than access. At about $29.99 a month, or $19.99 on annual billing — a third less — what you are buying is premium mockups, a stock-image library, and year-round product discounts of roughly 25 to 35%. Those discounts are the whole calculation, and it is a volume question with an arithmetic answer.

To cover $19.99 a month at a 25% discount you need about $80 of monthly product cost; at 35% you need about $57. On typical print-on-demand item costs that lands where the vendor itself puts it: somewhere around ten to twenty orders a month. Below that the subscription costs more than it saves, and the free plan is simply the better deal — not a lesser one. So the honest sequence is to start free, run your real orders through it for a month, and add up what a 25 to 35% discount would have returned.

If that number is under $20 you have your answer, and it is not a close call. What does not appear in either price is the thing that actually makes Gelato different: local production in more than thirty countries, which cuts international shipping time and cost. That also means print quality and product availability can vary by region and print partner, so order a sample of your key products from the regions your customers actually sit in before you scale.

That sampling costs a few dollars and it is the one test neither plan can do for you.

How to actually use Gelato

Start on the free plan, which is free forever rather than a trial, and order samples of your key products before you scale anything. Gelato's real edge is local production in more than thirty countries — faster and cheaper international shipping — and the direct consequence is that quality and product availability vary a little by region and print partner. Sample from the partner that will actually serve your main market.

Then do the Gelato+ arithmetic with your own numbers rather than accepting a rule of thumb. The subscription is about $19.99 a month on annual billing and buys product discounts of up to 25 to 35%. Divide the fee by your average saving per order: on a $10 product cost at 30% off, that is $3 an order, so it pays for itself at about seven orders a month. On thinner discounts or cheaper products the break-even moves out toward twenty.

Below that volume, stay free. The free plan carries core print-on-demand properly, so the only thing you are giving up is premium mockups, the stock library and discounts you are not yet ordering enough to use.
