When the paid plan is worth it

The free plan is $0 forever and genuinely usable for print-on-demand, so Gelato+ has to earn its fee out of savings rather than access. At about $29.99 a month, or $19.99 on annual billing — a third less — what you are buying is the 800+ premium mockups, the 100+ million stock photos, the AI tools (Magic Mockups, Personalization Studio, Velocity Switch, Price Navigator, Instant Collections) and year-round product discounts that the plan table caps at "Up to 33%".

All five AI tools carry a cross on the free column, as do both media libraries. Those discounts are the whole calculation, and it is a volume question with an arithmetic answer. Gelato publishes only the ceiling, so the arithmetic runs from there: covering $19.99 a month at the full 33% takes about $61 of monthly product cost, and every point below the ceiling pushes that figure up — at 20% it is about $100.

On typical print-on-demand item costs that lands somewhere around ten to twenty orders a month. Below that the subscription costs more than it saves, and the free plan is simply the better deal — not a lesser one. So the honest sequence is to start free, run your real orders through it for a month, and add up what a 25 to 35% discount would have returned. If that number is under $20 you have your answer, and it is not a close call.

What does not appear in either price is the thing that actually makes Gelato different: local production in 250+ facilities across 32 countries, which cuts international shipping time and cost, and which the free plan carries in full. That also means print quality and product availability can vary by region and print partner, so order a sample of your key products from the regions your customers actually sit in before you scale.

That sampling costs a few dollars and it is the one test neither plan can do for you.

How to actually use Gelato

Start on the free plan, which is free forever rather than a trial, and order samples of your key products before you scale anything. Gelato's real edge is local production in 250+ facilities across 32 countries — faster and cheaper international shipping — and the direct consequence is that quality and product availability vary a little by region and print partner. Sample from the partner that will actually serve your main market.

Then do the Gelato+ arithmetic with your own numbers rather than accepting a rule of thumb. The subscription is $19.99 a month on annual billing, $29.99 monthly, and buys product discounts the plan table caps at "Up to 33%". That is a ceiling with no published floor, so treat it as the best case rather than the rate. Divide the fee by your average saving per order: on a $10 product cost at the full 33%, that is $3.30 an order and the subscription pays for itself at about six orders a month.

At half that discount, or on cheaper items, the break-even moves out past fifteen. Below that volume, stay free. The free plan carries core print-on-demand properly, so the only thing you are giving up is premium mockups, the stock library and discounts you are not yet ordering enough to use.
