What GeoStock does

Shows per-location stock on the product page plus a store-locator map, so shoppers can see which branch has the item. From $9.99 a month, Plus $29.99. It displays inventory rather than managing it.

The honest knock

Know what this is before you buy it: despite the name it is a storefront widget, not an inventory system. It displays the stock you already track in Shopify per location, and adds a store-locator map — it does not manage, forecast or reconcile anything. If you came looking for multi-location inventory management, that is a different category and a different budget. What it does do well is close a specific gap: a shopper who can see that a nearby store has the item is a shopper who does not abandon.

When the paid plan is worth it

Read the Plus plan carefully before you triple your spend, because it adds no functionality whatsoever. Basic at $9.99 already carries every feature: stock per location per product, unlimited locations and variants, stock thresholds with custom colours, nearest-store detection by IP, country include and exclude filters, pickup, and widgets on product, cart and collection pages. Plus at $29.99 lists exactly one functional line -- “everything in Basic” -- and then seven service lines: a dedicated onboarding session, priority support with video calls, help integrating a custom theme, same-day fixes, direct access to the dev team and ongoing optimisation guidance. That can be worth $20 a month if you are on Shopify Plus with a bespoke theme and no in-house developer. It is worth nothing if your theme is standard. Both plans include a 14-day trial. How to actually use GeoStock

Get your Shopify location data clean before you install, because the widget shows exactly what is in there and wrong stock numbers on a product page are worse than no numbers. Set the stock threshold colours per variant deliberately: showing “2 left” is useful, showing it when the real figure is unreliable trains shoppers to distrust the widget. Start on the $9.99 Base plan and only move to Plus once you know which of the extra controls you actually use — the difference is features, not capacity. Does GeoStock work with my store platform?

Check your platform and version specifically. Who should not use GeoStock?

Skip it if you are online-only — there are no locations to show — or you need actual inventory management, which this is not. Buying a tool to fix a problem you don't have yet just adds cost and another login to manage.
