The catch

And the dependency worth pricing in: the rules run from a script you drop into your site — their own wording is “add one line of code” — so the redirects and content swaps exist only while that script is being served.

When the paid plan is worth it

There is no feature decision to make here, which simplifies things considerably: all six tiers include country, state and city-level targeting, and what scales is your visitor quota. Dev $12 a month, Startup $24, Growth $49, Business $99, High Traffic $199 and Enterprise from $349, with yearly billing bringing the first four to $9, $19, $39 and $79. That annual discount runs at a consistent 20 to 25% across the ladder rather than getting better at the top, so there is no volume incentive hidden in the billing frequency.

Because capability does not change, pick purely on measured traffic and pick honestly. Under-buying means hitting a quota mid-month on the exact pages you added targeting to; over-buying is money spent on headroom you can add later at the same rate. The 14-day free trial is enough to see your real volume flowing through it, which is a better basis than an analytics estimate.

The six-step ladder is itself worth noting: with steps this fine you are rarely paying much for headroom you do not use, which is unusual in this category.

How to actually use Geotargetly

Understand what you are renting before you build anything on it. The rules run from a script you add to your site — their own wording is 'add one line of code' — so your redirects and content swaps exist only while that script is being served. Stop the subscription and every visitor sees the same page again, wherever they are. That is fine if you are renting geo-targeting deliberately for campaigns; for something permanent, like a country redirect you always want, build it server-side and use this for the temporary work.

Size the plan on visitors rather than features. All six tiers include country, state and city-level targeting, so the only thing separating Dev at $12 from Enterprise at $349 is the visitor quota — and a traffic spike moves you up a tier whether you planned it or not. Estimate a good month, not an average one.

Note which prices you are reading, too: the friendlier numbers are the yearly rates ($9, $19, $39, $79 and up), and month-to-month runs roughly a quarter to a third higher. Use the 14-day trial to check the one thing that matters technically — whether the script adds visible delay to your pages, since it runs before the visitor sees the right version.

Best for: marketers personalizing redirects, content blocks and popups by visitor location — country to city level — without engineering work.
Who should skip it: if your traffic is small enough for a hand-built redirect, or your CDN/edge stack already covers the basic geo-cases you need.
