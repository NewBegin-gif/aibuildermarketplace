What Geotargetly does

Geo-targeting toolkit — location-based redirects, content swaps, popups and personalization by country, state and city, priced by traffic volume.

Pricing (2026)

(2026) 14-day free trial. Six tiers by traffic volume: Dev $12/mo, Startup $24, Growth $49, Business $99, High Traffic $199, Enterprise from $349 — billed yearly that becomes $9/$19/$39/$79 and up. All plans include country, state and city-level targeting; visitor quotas scale per tier. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

The meter is your traffic: tiers gate on visitor quotas, so a growth spike pushes you up a tier whether you planned it or not — and the friendlier advertised numbers are the yearly-billing rates; month-to-month runs roughly a third higher.

What buyers report about Geotargetly

Recurring themes from Capterra, GetApp, Software Advice and SourceForge, read in August 2026. We have not run Geotargetly ourselves, so this is other buyers' experience rather than ours. We summarise it here because the complaints are usually the part a vendor page leaves out.

What they praise
  • • From $9/month with every feature in one plan — no tier puzzle, which reviewers name directly.
  • • Quick to set up, and it drops into Squarespace and similar platforms without a developer.
What they complain about
  • • Chat support runs CET work hours only, with email outside that. The company acknowledges it, but reviewers describe an unanswered chat window.
  • • There are no tags or folders. Past a few dozen redirects the dashboard becomes unmanageable, and anyone creating thousands of links will feel it immediately.
  • • Google declines ads pointed at redirect pages, which limits one obvious use case.
  • • Pageview brackets are coarse — reviewers ask for finer scaling.
Best for: marketers personalizing redirects, content blocks and popups by visitor location — country to city level — without engineering work.
Who should skip it: if your traffic is small enough for a hand-built redirect, or your CDN/edge stack already covers the basic geo-cases you need.
