Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 14 Sep 2026

Starter €16/mo or €13.12/mo billed annually: 1 automation workflow, up to 3 users. Marketer €54/mo or €44.28 annual: unlimited workflows, ecommerce tools, advanced segmentation, abandoned-cart recovery. Creator €62/mo or €50.84 annual: website builder, course creator up to 500 students, webinars. Enterprise is quote-only and adds SMS, a dedicated IP and sending domain, SSO and unlimited users. Annual billing saves a stated 18%, and prices step up at 1k, 2.5k, 5k, 10k, 25k, 50k and 100k contacts.

What changed since our August 2026 reading is the free tier: the pricing page no longer sells one. It opens with a 14-day trial that needs no card, and nine fresh loads on 14 September 2026 each showed the same four plan cards with no free option among them. Free accounts have not been withdrawn altogether — the FAQ on that same page still describes a Free account that keeps working once the trial lapses, with limits on webinars, messages, landing pages, marketing automation and chats — but it is no longer a tier you can pick from the grid, so treat it as what the trial decays into rather than as a plan.

One currency warning survives unchanged: that same FAQ quotes the entry plan at $19 a month for 1,000 subscribers while the plan grid served us euros, so check which currency your own checkout uses before you budget. Charities and NGOs are offered 50% off every monthly plan. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

What buyers report about GetResponse

Recurring themes from the buyer-review sites listed at the foot of this page, read in August 2026. We have not run GetResponse ourselves, so this is other buyers' experience rather than ours. We summarise it here because the complaints are usually the part a vendor page leaves out.

What they praise
  • • It replaces several tools with one bill — email, automation, webinars and landing pages together — which is why owners of small businesses stay. 4.3 on G2 across 1,127 reviews.
  • • Easy to learn, and cheaper than the venture-backed competitors at comparable features.
  • • On TrustRadius the visual campaign builder and the funnel builder get named repeatedly as best-in-class for the money, with 24/7 chat support treated as a genuine differentiator by small teams.
What they complain about
  • • Price escalation by contact count is the most common complaint on every platform. The $19 Starter figure is at 1,000 contacts; check the price at the list size you expect in a year.
  • • One automation workflow on Starter — if automation is why you are buying, that tier is not the product.
  • • A strict no-refund policy that reviewers say applies even when the fault is GetResponse's.
  • • Difficulty cancelling, and charges continuing after a cancellation request.
  • • The technical limits TrustRadius reviewers name are ones the marketing pages do not: the API was described as needing hacking around during a migration from another platform, and integration coverage is still thinner than reviewers want.

Where the ratings disagree. 4.2 on Trustpilot across 955 reviews, but the shape tells you more than the average: 63% five-star and 27% one-star, with almost nothing between. The one-star cluster is entirely billing and cancellation — not the product. The two platforms disagree because they were written at different times. TrustRadius's newest written GetResponse review dates from January 2024 and reads as a product conversation — builders, templates, API. Trustpilot's is current and splits 63% five-star against 27% one-star, with the one-star cluster almost entirely about billing and cancellation. Neither is wrong; the older platform simply predates the complaint.

When the paid plan is worth it

The free plan is a 14-day full-feature trial wearing a free-plan badge. After those 14 days the premium features stop, and you are left with a genuinely thin tier. It gives you 1,000 landing-page visitors a month, AI tools capped at 3 uses, no dynamic segmentation, and restricted contact tagging and scoring. Webinars cap at 10 attendees, and GetResponse branding goes on everything you send. Read on their own pricing page on 2 August 2026, at 1,000 contacts: Starter €16 a month or €13.12 billed annually; Marketer €54 or €44.28; Creator €62 or €50.84; Enterprise quote-only.

Annual saves 18%. Prices step up at 1k, 2.5k, 5k, 10k, 25k, 50k and 100k contacts, so your bill moves with list growth rather than with headcount. The limit that bites is automation. Starter gives one workflow and three users. That is enough for a newsletter and nothing else — the moment you want a welcome series and an abandoned-cart flow at the same time you are on Marketer, which is 3.4× the price.

Decide whether you are buying a newsletter tool or a marketing automation platform, because the pricing treats those as different products.

How to actually use GetResponse

Use the 14 days on the automation, not on the email design. The templates will look fine on every plan; what you cannot judge later is whether one workflow is enough for you, and that is the €16-versus-€54 decision. Import your list before you build anything, because the price is set by contact count and the tier boundaries are at 1k, 2.5k, 5k and 10k. A list of 1,050 costs the same as one of 2,500 — clean out the dead addresses first and you may land a tier lower for a year.

If webinars are why you are here, check the attendee caps before the tier price. Free allows 10, Creator 100, Enterprise 1,000. That ceiling, not the monthly fee, is what usually forces the upgrade.
