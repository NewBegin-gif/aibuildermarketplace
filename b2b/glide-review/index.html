Glide Review (2026)Two Products, Two Pricing Pages

Glide meters updates, not just seats.

Best for

teams that already run operations out of spreadsheets and want a real app on top — internal tools, field data capture, client portals — without hiring a developer.

Skip it if

you need heavy custom logic or a high-write consumer app: the update metering punishes exactly that shape of workload, and anything beyond the published ceilings is a quote-only conversation.

On this page
We tested it · 2026-09-07

We already knew from their pricing pages that Glide sells two products. What we had never done is the thing a reader does: go to glideapps.com and press the free signup button. Daan created the account; everything after that was measured in the product.

  • The free signup button does not lead to the product our pricing section describes. Both “Sign up” and “Get started” on glideapps.com point at os.glideapps.dev — GlideOS. We signed up and landed there. The classic builder, with the free tier of unlimited drafts, one editor and 25k rows, sits behind glideapps.com/classic, which you only reach if you already know to look for it.
  • The two ladders are an order of magnitude apart. Classic business pricing starts at $199 a month billed yearly; GlideOS Basic is $25 a month. Same brand, same homepage, two different products with two different price floors.
  • And the meter is different. Classic bills updates — 5,000 included, 2 cents each after that. GlideOS bills credits: Basic 100 a month, Plus 250, Pro 500 and upward with team size. So the “updates, not seats” warning that applies to Classic does not describe the product a new signup actually lands in.

Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 7 Sep 2026

Its paid plans are unchanged, but they now live on glideapps.com/classic/pricing instead of the main pricing page. On the individual tab, Explorer starts at $19 per month billed yearly or $25 billed monthly (one app, 100 personal users, 250 updates, up to 25k rows) and Maker at $49 per month billed yearly or $60 monthly. On the business tab, Business starts at $199 per month billed yearly or $249 monthly, with unlimited apps, 30 users included, 5,000 updates and up to 100k rows.

Frequently asked questions

What should I weigh before buying IT & Productivity software?

Judge it on evidence rather than adjectives. Look for a published security page, single sign-on and role controls on the plan you would actually buy (they are often gated to higher tiers), a status page with real incident history, and a clear answer on where your data is stored. If the tool will hold anything sensitive, those four answers matter more than any feature comparison.
