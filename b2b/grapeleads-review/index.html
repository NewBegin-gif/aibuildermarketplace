What GrapeLeads does

Lead-finding for web developers and agencies — surfaces local businesses without websites from Google Maps, with contact details, on signup credits.

Pricing (2026)

(2026) Credit-based, with 20 free credits at signup. At research time the site's own pricing link returned a 404 — there is no public price list; verify current credit-pack rates in-app before buying. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

The catch

For a lead tool aimed at web professionals, a broken pricing page is an ironic look — check in-app rates before committing, and know that the niche (Google-Maps businesses without websites) is the real product.

Frequently asked questions

Does GrapeLeads replace my CRM or plug into it?

Most tools in this category plug in rather than replace. Check which direction the data flows and whether the sync is genuinely two-way, because a one-way push creates a second source of truth that your reps will quietly stop trusting. If it does aim to replace your CRM, price the migration honestly: history, custom fields and reporting rarely move across cleanly.

Who should not use GrapeLeads?

Skip it if you sell to companies that already have an online presence, or you want transparent pricing before creating an account. Buying a tool to fix a problem you don't have yet just adds cost and another login to manage.
