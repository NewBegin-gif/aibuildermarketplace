What Guesty does

Property management for short-term and vacation rentals: bookings, guest messaging, channel distribution and pricing in one dashboard. Guesty Lite is roughly $9/month plus about 1% per reservation for one to three listings; Pro and Enterprise are custom-quoted. Heavy for a single listing.

Pricing (2026)

Guesty Lite is roughly $9/mo plus ~1% per reservation for 1–3 listings; Pro and Enterprise are custom-quoted (2026). Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

Pricing runs higher than leaner rivals, onboarding has a learning curve, and some users flag support — it earns its keep at scale, but it's heavy for a single listing.

What buyers report about Guesty

Recurring themes from G2, GetApp and host-community reviews, read in August 2026. We have not run Guesty ourselves, so this is other buyers' experience rather than ours. We summarise it here because the complaints are usually the part a vendor page leaves out.

What they praise
  • • The automation engine, multi-calendar and unified inbox are best-in-class, and the Airbnb Preferred Plus integration is a genuine advantage.
  • • For a portfolio manager rather than a single host, the depth is what nothing cheaper matches.
What they complain about
  • • Price is the top complaint, with extra fees on top of an already high base. Reviewers use the word exorbitant.
  • • Billing errors are reported as recurring rather than isolated, with slow resolution and neither support nor account managers taking ownership.
  • • Support is inconsistent: fine on simple questions, escalation-heavy on billing disputes, integrations and bugs.
  • • One host reports the software making internal scheduling edits they never requested, costing them thousands.
  • • Mobile app limitations and message automation bugs.
Best for: hosts with 1-3 listings starting on Guesty Lite (~$9/mo + ~1% per reservation) who may grow into the full property-management platform.
Who should skip it: if you want quote-free scaling — Pro and Enterprise are custom-priced, so growth means a sales conversation.

The natural comparison is Hostaway / Hospitable / Lodgify — leaner, cheaper rivals. Decide by which one fits the job above, not by the louder brand.

Frequently asked questions

Does Guesty replace my current setup or sit on top of it?

Usually it sits on top rather than replacing everything. Tools in this class tend to become the layer where work is tracked and handed off, while your existing systems stay the source of truth for their own data. Before you buy, map which tools it would replace outright and which it would merely sync with. If the answer is that it syncs with all of them, you are adding a layer rather than removing one.

Who should not use Guesty?

Skip it if you want quote-free scaling — Pro and Enterprise are custom-priced, so growth means a sales conversation. Buying a tool to fix a problem you don't have yet just adds cost and another login to manage.
