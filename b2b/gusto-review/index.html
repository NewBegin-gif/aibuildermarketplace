What Gusto does

Payroll, benefits and HR in one friendly platform. Automated tax filings, employee onboarding and time tracking — run payroll for your whole team in just a few clicks.

Pricing (2026)

Simple about $49/mo base + $6/employee, Plus $80 + $12/employee, Premium $180 + $22/employee; a contractor-only plan from about $35 + $6/contractor (2026). US-focused. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

Best for: US small businesses that want genuinely easy full-service payroll, benefits and tax compliance in one place.
Who should skip it: if you employ people outside the US or need enterprise, multi-entity HR — Gusto is built for US SMB payroll.

The honest knock

The base-plus-per-employee model is transparent but adds up, and Gusto is US-only — excellent for straightforward US payroll and benefits, but not the pick for international teams or complex multi-entity HR.

Frequently asked questions

Is Gusto safe to run my finances through?

It depends on the job. Gusto is best for US small businesses that want genuinely easy full-service payroll, benefits and tax compliance in one place; if that's you, it tends to pay for itself in saved time. If not, hold off. We don't publish fixed prices because they change — check Gusto's live pricing before deciding.

Who should not use Gusto?

Skip it if you employ people outside the US or need enterprise, multi-entity HR — Gusto is built for US SMB payroll. Buying a tool to fix a problem you don't have yet just adds cost and another login to manage.
