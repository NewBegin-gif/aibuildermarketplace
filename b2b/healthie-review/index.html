Healthie Review (2026)

By Daan Haket · Tested hands-on · written with an AI assistant · who does what

Two honest limits: it's pricier than alternatives with thin mid-tier options, and several users report slow customer service (sometimes a week or more to reply). It really only pays off at scale — solo or tiny practices get more value, more simply, elsewhere. Throughout this review we weigh it against SimplePractice.

Best for

growing group practices (10+ clinicians) in health, wellness and nutrition that need multi-provider scheduling, telehealth and a real API to automate operations.

Skip it if

you're a solo practitioner or tiny team who wants the simplest, cheapest setup — SimplePractice is easier and lighter for that.

What Healthie does

All-in-one practice management and EHR for health, wellness and nutrition providers: scheduling, telehealth, secure messaging, billing and a branded client portal — plus a full API (GraphQL + webhooks) for automation. Built to scale with group practices.

Themes from the buyer-review sites listed at the foot of this page, read in August 2026. We tested Healthie ourselves on 2026-10-30, but one test is a narrow sample, so here is what other buyers report. We summarise it here because the complaints are usually the part a vendor page leaves out.

What they praise
  • • Practitioners get started fast — the interface and the tutorial videos are what reviewers name first.
  • • Support is called proactive, which is rare in clinical software.
What they complain about
  • • The strongest negative reviews are from providers, and they are specific: billing problems, bugs they end up explaining to their own patients, and charting that spills outside the working day. If you are a solo provider, read those before you commit.
  • • You cannot schedule a follow-up from inside a telehealth call — the calendar is not reachable during the session.
  • • Calendar customisation is limited, and client packages cannot be posted without pricing details.
  • • Implementation cost is the barrier reviewers name for smaller clinics.

When the paid plan is worth it

Healthie has no free tier, so the question is which paid step you need. Core at $19.99 a month caps you at 10 active clients, and archived clients do not count against that, so a low-volume practice that closes files as it goes can stay there. The cap is soft on an externally linked calendar or package: a 10th client can still book or buy, and you are notified that the account moves up a level unless you archive back under the limit.

Essentials at $49.99 raises the ceiling to 250 active clients and adds custom form building, CMS 1500s and data transfer support. Plus at $129.99 removes the cap and adds group telehealth and online programs, so it earns its keep only if you run groups. Group starts at $149.99 and then adds $50 per clinician per month, so the headline understates a multi-provider practice. Annual billing takes 10% off. Upgrading does not fix the payment cut, since Stripe takes 2.9% plus $0.30 at every tier.

ePrescribing through DoseSpot is a separate $40 per clinician per month. Outside Enterprise, Healthie imports patient demographic data only, so no tier buys you a full chart migration.

How to actually use Healthie

Signup gets you 14 days on the Plus plan with no credit card, so the trial exposes features most people will not have on the tier they actually buy. Set the account up in the web app, not the mobile app. Before an import Healthie tells you to set up intake forms and intake flow, appointment types, and calendar and availability first. Add a test client with a second email address, since it cannot be the same as your provider email.

Clients cannot self-register at all; they only get in through an invite, your unique link, or a package purchase or appointment booking from your website. The two places people stall are the calendar and billing. Calendar setup alone runs seven steps and over 35 settings. Billing needs a linked bank account before a package can take money. Migration is not a self-serve upload either: you file an Imports Questionnaire and Healthie runs a one-time import for you as part of implementation.

Frequently asked questions

Can you export your data from Healthie?

What does Healthie cost to start?

$19.99 a month on Core billed monthly, or $18 billed yearly — considerably lower than the figure most write-ups quote, ours included until this update. Essentials is $49.99 or $45, Plus $129.99 or $115 and Group $149.99+ or $135+. Read on 17 August 2026 and again, unchanged, on 28 September 2026.

What does it cost for a group practice?

The Group tier starts at $149.99 a month ($135 yearly), and then each additional clinician is $50 a month on top. A five-clinician practice is therefore the tier plus four times $50, so about $350 a month yearly-billed — work from the clinician count, not the tier price.

Which add-ons should I budget for?

Two in particular: the ClaimMD integration for insurance billing starts at $30 a month, and extra clinicians are $50 each. Full API access sits above the standard group tier, so if you plan to build against Healthie, check which tier unlocks it before pricing the project.
