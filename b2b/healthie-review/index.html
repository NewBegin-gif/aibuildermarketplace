What Healthie does

All-in-one practice management and EHR for health, wellness and nutrition providers: scheduling, telehealth, secure messaging, billing and a branded client portal — plus a full API (GraphQL + webhooks) for automation. Built to scale with group practices.

Pricing (2026)

Tiered and it climbs: entry plans from ~$49/mo, mid-tiers around $129–149/mo, and group/enterprise plans scale with team size into four figures a month. Full API access sits above the standard group tier; 14-day free trial (2026). Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

The honest knock

Two honest limits: it's pricier than alternatives with thin mid-tier options, and several users report slow customer service (sometimes a week or more to reply). It really only pays off at scale — solo or tiny practices get more value, more simply, elsewhere.

Best for: growing group practices (10+ clinicians) in health, wellness and nutrition that need multi-provider scheduling, telehealth and a real API to automate operations.
Who should skip it: if you're a solo practitioner or tiny team who wants the simplest, cheapest setup — SimplePractice is easier and lighter for that.

Frequently asked questions

Does Healthie replace my current setup or sit on top of it?

Usually it sits on top rather than replacing everything. Tools in this class tend to become the layer where work is tracked and handed off, while your existing systems stay the source of truth for their own data. Before you buy, map which tools it would replace outright and which it would merely sync with. If the answer is that it syncs with all of them, you are adding a layer rather than removing one.

Who should not use Healthie?

Skip it if you're a solo practitioner or tiny team who wants the simplest, cheapest setup — SimplePractice is easier and lighter for that. Buying a tool to fix a problem you don't have yet just adds cost and another login to manage.
