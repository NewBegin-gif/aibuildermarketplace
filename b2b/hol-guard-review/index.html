    Frequently asked questions

    Can you export your data from HOL Guard?

    Exit check 2026-11-19; route excerpt: No export or reimport was performed; came along (excerpts): not tested; lost (excerpts): not tested; re-import: not tested.

    How much does HOL Guard cost?

    Per the Guard pricing page read on 18 November 2026, shown monthly in US dollars: Free is $0 forever, Solo is $4.99, Pro is $15 and Team is $30 per seat. Enterprise is contact sales. The page says annual billing gives 2 months free.

    Does it work without an account?

    Yes. The vendor says Guard is free forever on one machine and, on supported integrations, evaluates actions locally without an account or internet connection.

    What does Guard check?

    On supported integrations it evaluates shell commands, secret access, MCP changes and plugin or skill installs before they run, and can allow, ask or block under your policy.

    What does it not do?

    The vendor says Guard is not a cloud MCP gateway, not a network firewall and not a complete prompt-injection preventer. Coverage depends on the tool and the event.

    What leaves my machine?

    By default nothing, says the vendor. With Guard Cloud only a short record of what was allowed or blocked is synced, not files, prompts or passwords.

    Is there a trial?

    The Guard page mentions a 3-day Solo trial then $4.99 a month. The pricing page says trial and payment terms are shown at checkout, so check the length before you start.

    What does Pro add over Solo?

    5 devices, 180 days of retention, 5 GB of storage, real-time activity alerts, policy versioning with rollback, and advanced search with full evidence.

    What do we not know?

    How well Guard catches real attacks, how often it blocks something harmless, and how it behaves on your agent. We are affiliates of HOL but have not installed it or paid for a plan.
