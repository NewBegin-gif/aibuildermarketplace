What Hopp does

Digital business cards and smart networking for teams. Share contact details instantly and capture leads, with branded cards your whole team can use.

Pricing (2026)

A free tier exists (2026); public pricing beyond that is fragmented across Hopp-branded products — verify the exact product and current plans on their site before committing. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

The honest knock

The honest note is brand confusion: multiple products share the Hopp name (Wix's link-in-bio tool among them), so make sure the thing you're evaluating is the digital-card/networking product you mean — and given thin public pricing, get current rates in writing. Digital cards themselves are a proven, low-cost category; the durable value is contact-sync into your CRM, not the tap-to-share moment.

What buyers report about Hopp

Recurring themes from Trustpilot and link-in-bio comparisons, read in August 2026. We have not run Hopp ourselves, so this is other buyers' experience rather than ours. We summarise it here because the complaints are usually the part a vendor page leaves out.

What they praise
  • • Clean design and simple setup — 4.4 on Trustpilot, and Pro at $8.50-$9.99 a month includes branding removal, a custom domain, premium templates and short links.
  • • There is a free plan, and being part of Wix means the infrastructure behind it is not a startup risk.
What they complain about
  • • There is no white-label on any tier. You can remove Hopp branding on Pro, but you cannot resell under your own brand or manage client pages from one account — that rules out agency use entirely.
  • • Analytics are surface-level even on Pro. No per-block performance breakdown, no audience geography.
  • • Hopp no longer publishes a pricing page; rates are regional and only visible from inside your account.
  • • 18 Trustpilot reviews is a small base, and the mixed ones cite Facebook link integration problems and slow support.
Best for: teams that want modern digital business cards with centralized branding, starting free and verifying paid tiers directly.

Frequently asked questions

Is Hopp secure and reliable enough to depend on?

Judge it on evidence rather than adjectives. Look for a published security page, single sign-on and role controls on the plan you would actually buy (they are often gated to higher tiers), a status page with real incident history, and a clear answer on where your data is stored. If the tool will hold anything sensitive, those four answers matter more than any feature comparison.

Who should not use Hopp?

Skip it if you need a battle-tested team deployment today with published pricing (Blinq-class tools publish theirs). Buying a tool to fix a problem you don't have yet just adds cost and another login to manage.
