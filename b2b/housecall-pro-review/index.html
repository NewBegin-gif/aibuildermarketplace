What Housecall Pro does

Field service management for home-service businesses. Schedule jobs, dispatch techs, send quotes and invoices, and take payments from one app.

Pricing (2026)

Basic about $59/mo (1 user, no GPS or QuickBooks), Essentials about $149/mo (up to 5 users), MAX about $299-329/mo; extra users ~$35/mo each, plus card processing around 2.59% (2026). Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

The honest knock

The Basic tier is a trap for real field teams — it omits GPS tracking and QuickBooks sync, so most businesses need Essentials or up, and add-on users plus processing fees push the true cost higher. Price the tier you'll actually need.

What buyers report about Housecall Pro

Recurring themes from Capterra, GetApp and field-service reviews, read in August 2026. We have not run Housecall Pro ourselves, so this is other buyers' experience rather than ours. We summarise it here because the complaints are usually the part a vendor page leaves out.

What they praise
  • • No long-term contract, and a 14-day trial of the top plan with no card required — you can evaluate the whole thing before paying.
  • • For a small field-service business it covers scheduling, dispatch, invoicing and payments in one place.
What they complain about
  • • The base price is low because the features come as paid add-ons. Basic is $59/month annually but Essentials is $149 and MAX $299, with extra MAX users at $35 each.
  • • No custom fields, and reporting is basic.
  • • Cancellation is reported as difficult despite the absence of a contract.
  • • Recent adopters describe the last year or two skewing negative: payment processing failures, unauthorised recurring charges, disruptive updates and limited support access. Support is the most polarising part — excellent for some, unresolved escalations for others.
Best for: home-service businesses (HVAC, plumbing, cleaning) that want scheduling, dispatch, invoicing and payments in one app.
Who should skip it: if you're a solo operator who just needs simple invoicing — the field-service features are more than you'll use.

Frequently asked questions

Does Housecall Pro replace my current setup or sit on top of it?

Usually it sits on top rather than replacing everything. Tools in this class tend to become the layer where work is tracked and handed off, while your existing systems stay the source of truth for their own data. Before you buy, map which tools it would replace outright and which it would merely sync with. If the answer is that it syncs with all of them, you are adding a layer rather than removing one.

Who should not use Housecall Pro?

Skip it if you're a solo operator who just needs simple invoicing — the field-service features are more than you'll use. Buying a tool to fix a problem you don't have yet just adds cost and another login to manage.
