IDrive Review (2026)

IT & ProductivityResearched assessment · by Daniel HaketUpdated 2026-07-03

The IT and productivity layer is where small, reliable tools quietly give you hours back — or, done wrong, add another login to babysit. So where does IDrive actually fit? It's most often picked as a leaner, cheaper alternative to Backblaze — so the real question is whether it does the job you actually need without the bloat.

What IDrive does

Automated disaster recovery and cloud backup. Securely mirror unlimited devices to the cloud to protect against ransomware.

Pricing (2026)

(2026) Aggressively priced first year: Mini from ~$3/yr (small storage), Personal ~5TB around $69-99/yr promotional, Team/Business tiers upward; physical data shipping (IDrive Express) included once (Personal) or 3x (Business) per year. The honest catch: renewal rates jump 43-100% after year one. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

Best for: individuals and small businesses that want cheap multi-device cloud backup with versioning, snapshots and physical seed/restore shipping.
Who should skip it: if you need real-time sync-and-collaborate storage (that's Drive/Dropbox territory, not backup), or you won't tolerate the year-two renewal jump.

The honest knock

The first-year price is a teaser and the renewal is the price — budget on year-two rates, not the banner. Beyond that, the honest backup fundamentals apply: a backup you never test-restore is a hope, not a backup, and upload speed for the first full backup of multi-TB data takes weeks unless you use their physical Express shipping (which is genuinely useful and included).

The natural comparison is Backblaze or Acronis — Backblaze is simpler flat-rate per computer; Acronis adds security suites — IDrive wins on multi-device flexibility per dollar (year one). Weigh the honest alternatives on our Backblaze alternatives page.
