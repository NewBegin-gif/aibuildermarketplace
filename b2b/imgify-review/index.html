Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 20 Sep 2026

Billed in USD, with recurring charges billed every 30 days. There are two plans and the cheaper one is almost certainly not for you: the Development plan is free, unlocks every feature, and the listing states its limit plainly — development and partner stores only, with billing activated on upgrade. A live shop is therefore on the paid plan from day one.

Monthly Plan is $2.99 a month, or $28.70 a year with the annual saving the listing puts at 20%, and it carries the falling effect, the AI logo maker, custom image upload, appearance and density controls, the marquee logo slider, dozens of holiday presets, support for all themes and live chat support. A 7-day free trial applies to the paid plan. Effective Apps' own site, effectify.io, publishes no pricing page at all — /pricing and /apps both return 404, checked the same day, and the home page carries no prices — so unlike most Shopify apps there is no upper half of the ladder hiding on a vendor site. What the listing shows is the whole price record. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

The free plan is development and partner stores only, and billing activates the moment you upgrade — a real storefront pays $2.99 from the first day, after a 7-day trial. Then the harder question: the nearest app in the same Shopify category costs nothing. Dakaas Seasonal Effects carries the same Built for Shopify badge, lists 200+ holiday themes plus cursor effects, music and scheduling, and has 1,567 reviews at 4.9 since May 2021 against Imgify's 32 at 4.8 since January 2020.

So you are not weighing $2.99 against nothing at all — you are weighing it against a free app with a public record roughly fifty times deeper. What the money actually answers is narrow: the AI logo maker and the marquee slider for partner, payment and press logos, neither of which is on the free app's feature list. That is a real difference and it is the whole case for paying.

And treat the sales pitch as a pitch. The listing promises effects that 'boost sales' and logo rows that build 'instant trust'; no conversion data is published for either claim, by this vendor or by the free ones. Note too what the recent reviews are built from — three of the newest come from stores that had the app installed for ten minutes, about three hours and nine months respectively, so most of that 4.8 says the install went smoothly, not that the thing kept working.

What Imgify does

Shopify app that drops your own logo — or snow, hearts and dozens of holiday presets — down the storefront, with a marquee slider for partner, payment and press logos and an AI logo maker for the artwork. $2.99 a month or $28.70 a year; the free plan is development and partner stores only, so a live shop pays from day one.

What the money actually buys

There is only one paid plan, so the question is never which tier but whether $2.99 a month beats free. What it buys over the free competition is two things, and both are about your brand rather than the season: the AI logo maker, which generates falling artwork from a short prompt when you have no image ready, and the marquee logo slider for partner, payment and press logos. Neither appears on the feature list of Dakaas Seasonal Effects, the free app that leads this category with 1,567 reviews.

Everything else on the paid plan — falling effects, custom image upload, appearance and density control, page targeting, dozens of holiday presets, support for all themes — has a free equivalent one listing away. At $2.99 a month or $28.70 a year the sum is small enough that the real calculation is whether you want your own logo falling rather than snowflakes. If you do not, you are paying for presets you can have for nothing.

How to actually use Imgify

If you have a development store, install there first. The Development plan is free, unlocks every feature and only starts billing when you upgrade, which is the cheapest way to see whether a falling effect actually suits your theme. On a live store you are on the paid plan immediately, with a 7-day trial to decide. Switch on one surface and one occasion rather than everything at once. The app targets specific pages — home, product, cart, landing and custom pages — so the usual first test is the homepage for a single holiday using one of the presets.

Keep density and speed low: what reads as festive at a handful of images on screen reads as broken at fifty. If you have no artwork, run the AI logo maker before you go looking for an image.
