What Instapage does

Enterprise landing-page and conversion platform: build, personalize and A/B-test post-click landing pages at scale, with ad-to-page mapping, heatmaps and fast AMP pages — built for paid-ads and demand-gen teams.

Pricing (2026)

(re-verified 16 Jul 2026) Instapage no longer publishes plan prices: the pricing page now only offers ‘Try for free’ (14-day trial) and ‘Get a demo’. Last published rates (2026) were Create from $99/mo (~$79 annual, ~15k visitors) and Optimize $199-299/mo with visitor caps and overage fees; Convert was custom. Budget on a sales conversation for current numbers. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

The honest knock

It's premium-priced and only justified by serious ad spend — small budgets will feel it. Visitor caps mean overage fees, there's no 24/7 support, and like Unbounce it still can't generate a full landing page from one prompt.

Best for: marketing teams running significant paid ad campaigns who need post-click optimisation — AdMap ad-to-page personalisation, A/B testing and heatmaps.
Who should skip it: if you run limited ad campaigns or want budget pricing — at $99+/mo it's overkill below real paid-traffic volume.

The natural comparison is Unbounce or Landingi — the other premium and the budget landing-page builders. Decide by which one fits the job above, not by the louder brand.
