What Intelis does

Monitors competitor prices on Google Shopping and direct competitor sites and reprices your Shopify catalogue automatically with AI matching rules. Priced by tracked products or variants: $49 a month for 100, $179 for 1,000, $249 for 2,000 and $399 for 5,000, each with a 14-day trial.

Pricing (2026)

(read 2026-08-21 on apps.shopify.com/ecoprice-analytics and on intelis.ai/pricing) Four tiers on the Shopify listing, priced by how many products or variants you track, each with a 14-day free trial. Basic $49 a month or $490 a year, a 17% saving: up to 100 products or variants, Google Shopping competitor monitoring, AI automatch for unlimited competitors, dynamic pricing, unlimited competitor URL tracking, reports, pricing history and chat support.

Professional $179 a month or $1,790 a year: up to 1,000, adding variant tracking, sales analysis, live feed export, pricing strategy monitoring and premium chat support. Premium $249 a month or $2,490 a year: up to 2,000, adding Shopify Markets support per market, Google Analytics integration and API access. Business $399 a month or $3,990 a year: up to 5,000, adding a custom labels feed for Google Merchant Center, MAP monitoring, a customised AI matching algorithm, pricing consultation and A+ support.

Intelis's own pricing page does not agree with the listing above Premium. It shows Business at $499 a month and $4,990 a year on its annual toggle, $399 on its monthly toggle, and an Enterprise tier for up to 10,000 SKUs at $849 a month. The company homepage prints that same Enterprise tier as $749. The listing is what Shopify billing charges you, so treat everything above Premium as unsettled and get the tier confirmed in writing.

All charges are billed in USD and recur every 30 days. Developer Intelis; the app launched 6 July 2021. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

The ladder counts variants, not products, and that is where stores get caught. A catalogue of forty garments in five sizes and three colours is six hundred variants, so a shop most people would call small lands on the $179 tier rather than the $49 one. Count your variants, not your line items, before you read the price.

Above Premium the vendor cannot keep its own numbers straight. The Shopify listing says Business is $399 a month. Intelis's pricing page says $499 with a $4,990 annual rate, and its monthly toggle on the same page says $399. Its homepage prices Enterprise at $749 while the pricing page says $849. Three published figures for the same tier usually means the tier is negotiated rather than listed, which is fine, but it means the number you were quoted is the only one that counts.

And automatic repricing is not a switch you leave alone. The matching is AI-driven across Google Shopping and competitor sites, and a mismatched listing quietly reprices the wrong product, while an unbounded rule set on a busy category is how a store races itself to the bottom. Set floor prices before you turn dynamic pricing on, and audit what the automatch decided in the first fortnight.

Check your platform and version specifically. Who should not use Intelis?
