Key Features That Actually Move the Needle

After extensive testing, several InVideo features stand out as genuine differentiators for B2B SaaS companies:

One of InVideo's strongest features is its ability to automatically resize and reformat content for different platforms. The platform goes beyond simple resizing, adjusting text placement, animation timing, and even suggesting different hooks for different platforms. A professional product demo might lead with features and benefits on LinkedIn but start with a problem statement on Instagram.

The search functionality is intelligent, suggesting relevant media based on your script content and video theme. Brand Consistency Tools

InVideo allows you to create brand kits that automatically apply your company's colors, fonts, logos, and style guidelines to any template. This ensures consistency across all video content—crucial for B2B companies where brand recognition directly impacts trust and conversion rates.

The brand kit feature extends to animation styles and transitions, creating a cohesive visual language across all your video content. This level of brand consistency typically requires expensive custom templates or dedicated design resources.
