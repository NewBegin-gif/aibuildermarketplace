There is a free plan, and this is what it actually gives: 2 live funnels, publishing on a daanV.involve.me subdomain (or one of ten alternatives such as quizzes.cx, forms.ac and calculators.cx), the AI funnel builder, and involve.me branding on every page. Paid, per person per month, annual against monthly: Start $29 / $49 with 3 live funnels and 1 user, Grow $69 / $99 with 5 funnels and 2 users, Scale $139 / $199 with 25 funnels, 5 users, A/B testing and lead verification.

Enterprise is from $499/mo on annual contracts only. In the app the same tiers are priced in euros — €29, €69, €139 — and the annual toggle says “save up to 41%” where the public page says 40%. The tier pages also hide three meters that decide more than the funnel count does: funnel visits a month (10,000 / 25,000 / 100,000), contacts collected a month (300 / 1,000 / 3,000) and AI credits a month (50 / 100 / 300).

Each is toppable: +5,000 visits €8.33, +200 contacts €8.33, +250 AI credits €7.50, +1 seat €7.50, +5 live funnels €7.50. Prices exclude EU VAT. Every tier is higher than at the 17 Jul 2026 reading, when Start was $19/mo annual and $29 month-to-month. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

Start collects 300 contacts a month against 10,000 funnel visits — so a funnel converting at 3% hits the contact ceiling and the visit ceiling in the same week, and the next 200 contacts cost €8.33 on top. That trio (visits, contacts, AI credits) appears only in the in-app comparison table, not on the public pricing page. The free plan is worse than undocumented: involve.me offers it ten times across the pricing page, the comparison table and the in-app upgrade screen, and specifies it nowhere.

We measured it — 2 live funnels, involve.me branding, no team seats — and you only learn any of that by hitting the wall, because a free account has no usage panel at all: “Plans & Billing” goes straight to the upgrade page. And the honest fundamental for any interactive-funnel tool: quizzes and calculators convert traffic you already have — they don't create it, so validate on the free plan before the subscription outruns the pipeline.

When the paid plan is worth it

Read the ladder as funnels and seats, not features. Start is $29 a month billed annually for 3 live funnels and 1 user, Grow $69 for 5 funnels and 2 users, Scale $139 for 25 funnels and 5 users, and Enterprise starts at $499 on annual contracts only. Month-to-month costs $49, $99 and $199, so paying a year up front saves 41% on Start and 30% on the two tiers above it (re-verified 4 Sep 2026).

The step that catches people is the seat. Extra seats are $10 a month, but the comparison table marks them unavailable on Start — so a second user is not a $10 add-on there, it is the $40 jump to Grow. Per funnel, Grow is the worst rung on the ladder: $13.80 against $9.67 on Start and $5.56 on Scale. What upgrading does not fix is traffic. Every tier meters funnels, seats and AI credits; not one of them sends visitors to the quiz.

If submissions are thin because too few people arrive, Scale collects the same silence at nearly five times the price.

What we found running involve.me ourselves

Hands-on · 2026-09-07

We ran the free plan ourselves rather than describing the paid tiers from the pricing page. Daan created the account; everything after that was measured in the product — funnels built and published one by one until the walls appeared, with the wall text copied out word for word. involve.me contributed nothing to this test: no account, no briefing, no sight of the draft. We do carry an affiliate link to them, disclosed on this page, and the test was run on the free plan we signed up for ourselves.

  • The free plan allows exactly 2 live funnels. We published two, and the third stopped with: “You have reached your limit of 2 live funnels. Please end any live funnels or upgrade your plan to publish more funnels.” The dialog puts an Upgrade plan button next to Publish now.
  • A second user is not a paid extra on free, it is nothing at all: “You don't have any team member seats on your plan. Upgrade for more.” There is no referral credit or trial seat.
  • The AI funnel builder works on the free plan, and it is the real product. One sentence — “build a 4-question quiz that helps a marketer pick a CRM” — produced a five-page funnel with a theme applied, questions, scoring and a result page, and it asked for approval before each risky edit. The comparison table lists “Create funnels with AI” as a Start-plan benefit, so this is more generous than the tier pages suggest.
  • A free account has no usage panel. “Plans & Billing” in the account menu goes straight to the upgrade page: no current plan, no counters, no AI-credit balance. You cannot find out what you have left; you discover a limit by hitting it.
  • Free publishing gives you a real subdomain and a choice of ten: daanV.involve.me plus quizzes.cx, forms.ac, calculators.cx, assessments.cx, tests.cx, surveys.so, feedback.ac, funnels.cx, researched.cx and paynow.cx. Every page carries involve.me branding; removing it is a paid step the share screen labels PRO, while the plan is actually called Grow.
  • The in-app comparison table publishes limits the public pricing page does not: funnel visits a month (10,000 / 25,000 / 100,000), contacts collected a month (300 / 1,000 / 3,000), file storage, embeds, payment accounts, and the price of every top-up. If you are sizing a plan, that table is the one to read.
  • Prices differ by surface: the public page quotes $29 / $69 / $139 and “Save up to 40%”, the in-app upgrade screen quotes €29 / €69 / €139 and “Save up to 41%”.
  • Onboarding is compulsory and is itself an involve.me funnel embedded in the app — four questions, carrying the “Made with involve.me” badge. You cannot reach the builder until you finish it. One of the answer options for “How did you discover involve.me?” is “AI - Claude”.

How to actually use involve.me

Count live funnels before you compare features, because that is what the ladder meters: 3 on Start, 5 on Grow, 25 on Scale. Three disappears the moment you run campaigns in parallel — one quiz, one calculator and one survey is already the allowance — and archiving one to publish another is friction you will feel every week. Then do the seat arithmetic, because it does not run the way you would expect.

Additional seats are $10 a month, but the pricing table marks them unavailable on Start, so a second user costs you the step to Grow: $69 a month annually against $29. Start on the free plan rather than the trial where you can. The 14-day trial unlocks paid features but asks for billing details first; the free plan does not, and it answers the question that matters early — how many funnels you actually keep live.

Note too that the advertised figures are the annual rates, month-to-month runs $20 to $60 higher per tier, and EU buyers pay VAT on top.
