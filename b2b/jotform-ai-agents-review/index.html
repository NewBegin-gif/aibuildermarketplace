What Jotform AI Agents does

Build AI chatbots and customer-service agents in minutes — no code required. Handles leads, support tickets and bookings 24/7. Integrates with your existing forms and CRMs.

Pricing (2026)

Starter is free with 5 forms and 100 monthly submissions. Bronze is $34 a month, Silver $39 and Gold $99, each billed annually; Enterprise is quoted. The ladder is drawn in submissions and storage — 1,000, 2,500 and 10,000 monthly submissions, 1 GB to 100 GB — and the agent limits ride on the same plans as the forms, so an agent build competes with form usage for the same allowance.

Our earlier note mixed one annual figure with two monthly ones; these four are all read off the same yearly view. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

When the paid plan is worth it

The gate is conversations, not features, and the free tier makes that obvious once you price it. Starter gives five agents and about a hundred conversations a month; at Silver's rate those hundred conversations are worth roughly two dollars of product. The free plan is a demonstration, not a tier you operate on. Above it, Bronze runs about $34 a month, Silver about $49 for 2,500 conversations and Gold about $129 for 10,000.

Per conversation that is about $0.020 on Silver and $0.013 on Gold — the top tier is roughly a third cheaper per conversation, which is a real volume discount rather than a token one. So if you are consistently near Silver's ceiling, Gold is better value per unit and not merely bigger. The number that breaks budgets is not the tier, though. Voice minutes and SMS are metered separately on top, and neither scales with the conversation count you used to pick your plan.

An agent that answers by text costs what the tier says; the same agent taking calls does not. Get those two unit rates before you model anything, because a voice-enabled agent is a different product commercially. One structural point that decides fit more than price: this is tightly bound to the Jotform ecosystem. If your forms already live there, an AI agent on top of them is a fast, low-code win and the integration is the whole value.

As a standalone chatbot competing with dedicated conversational-AI platforms, it is a harder sell at any tier.

How to actually use Jotform AI Agents

Estimate conversations before you look at features, because conversations are the gate. The free Starter tier includes around 100 a month, which on a live site is a few days rather than a month, and an agent that actually gets used will push you up a tier regardless of which features you need. Silver's 2,500 conversations work out at about two cents each and Gold's 10,000 at about one and a third — so if you expect volume, the higher tier is genuinely cheaper per conversation, which is not always true on this site.

Budget voice minutes and SMS separately. They meter on top of your conversation allowance, as does knowledge-base size, so an agent that answers by phone or text has a second bill that the tier price does not cover. Check the ecosystem fit honestly. This is tightly bound to Jotform: if your forms already live there, the agent is a fast, low-code win and the integration is the point. If they do not, you are evaluating it as a standalone chatbot against tools built to be standalone, and it is less compelling on that ground.

Then spend the free conversations on your five most awkward real enquiries rather than on testing that it replies. What you need to know is whether it is right about your policies, not whether it is polite.
