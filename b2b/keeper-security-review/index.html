What Keeper Security does

Password manager and privileged access management for teams, with a zero-knowledge architecture.

Pricing (2026)

(2026-08-01, read on their own business pricing page) No per-user prices are shown for any business tier. Three plans are listed — Business Starter, Business and Enterprise — all billed annually, with Business Starter stated as 5 to 10 users. A long list of capabilities is sold separately rather than included: Secrets Manager, Advanced Reporting & Alerts, Compliance Reporting, remote access and privileged session management, endpoint privilege management and threat detection. KeeperPAM is described as available through sales only, priced to organisation size, and KeeperMSP requires contacting sales. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

The honest knock

The business pricing page does not price the business plans. What it does do is list, at length, what is not included — Secrets Manager, compliance reporting, session management, endpoint privilege management are all separate purchases — so the tier you pick is a base and the security posture you were sold on is an assembly. Annual billing is stated on every tier, so there is no month-to-month option to test with, and Business Starter has a floor of five users. Ask for the per-user price of the base plan and each add-on you need as separate lines, because a bundled quote here hides which part is actually costing you.

What buyers report about Keeper Security

Recurring themes from G2, Capterra, Trustpilot and security-press reviews, read in August 2026. We have not run Keeper Security ourselves, so this is other buyers' experience rather than ours. We summarise it here because the complaints are usually the part a vendor page leaves out.

What they praise
  • • 256-bit AES with a strict zero-knowledge model, never breached, and third-party security audits published regularly. That is the record you want from a password manager.
  • • One of the most feature-rich options available, and easy to use with it.
What they complain about
  • • Auto-renewal is the recurring complaint: reviewers report being charged before the renewal date, and at least one reports it renewing again after they had switched auto-renewal off.
  • • There is no money-back guarantee and no refunds. Combined with the renewal behaviour above, that is the thing to manage.
  • • Breach scanning and secure file storage are paid add-ons rather than included — unusual in a security-first package.
  • • Pricing and discounting have shifted over time, making renewal quotes hard to reconcile with what you were originally told.
Best for: teams that need shared credentials with real admin control and audit, and that will genuinely use the provisioning and role-based access that separate this from a consumer password manager.
Who should skip it: if you are under five people wanting a shared vault, since Business Starter has a five-user floor and consumer or small-team plans elsewhere are cheaper and published.

Frequently asked questions

Is Keeper Security secure and reliable enough to depend on?

Judge it on evidence rather than adjectives. Look for a published security page, single sign-on and role controls on the plan you would actually buy (they are often gated to higher tiers), a status page with real incident history, and a clear answer on where your data is stored. If the tool will hold anything sensitive, those four answers matter more than any feature comparison.

Who should not use Keeper Security?

Skip it if you are under five people wanting a shared vault, since Business Starter has a five-user floor and consumer or small-team plans elsewhere are cheaper and published. Buying a tool to fix a problem you don't have yet just adds cost and another login to manage.
