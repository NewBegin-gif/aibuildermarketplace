How overages actually behave

The hard stop

Exceeding your visit cap bills per 1,000 additional visits. You get email notifications at 80% and 100% of the cap, which is fair warning.

The part to know: if your overages approach the cost of your plan or hit $500, whichever is less, Kinsta may require immediate payment or a plan upgrade. On a client site during a traffic spike that is a decision you make under pressure. Ask about the bandwidth-based plan option instead if your traffic is spiky — it exists precisely for this.

What agencies get and what they pay for it

4.8 on both Trustpilot (1,000+ reviews) and G2. Performance and support are what reviewers rate, consistently.

Reviews from 2025–2026 also mention longer support response times as the customer base grew, and specifically difficulty reaching the billing team.
