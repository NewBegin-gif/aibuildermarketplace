Frequently Asked Questions

Can JotForm forms slow down my Kinsta-hosted site?

In testing, embedded JotForm forms added 200-400ms to page load times. However, using JotForm's popup or redirect methods eliminated this impact entirely. Kinsta's CDN actually helped improve JotForm's performance for international visitors.

Is Kinsta worth the premium price compared to shared hosting?

For businesses generating over $3,000 monthly revenue, the 23% improvement in conversion rates (due to faster load times) typically pays for the hosting cost difference. For hobby sites or startups, the premium may not justify the benefits.

How does JotForm's free plan compare to Google Forms?

JotForm's free plan offers significantly more customization and professional appearance than Google Forms. However, the 100 submission limit means most businesses will need to upgrade within 2-3 months. Google Forms remains better for internal/unlimited simple surveys.

What happens if I exceed JotForm's submission limits?

JotForm stops accepting new submissions once you hit monthly limits, potentially losing leads. Unlike some competitors, they don't offer pay-per-overage options. Monitor usage closely or upgrade proactively to avoid business disruption.
