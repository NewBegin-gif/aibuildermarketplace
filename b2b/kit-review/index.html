Kit Review (2026)

Growth & RevenueResearched assessment · by Daniel HaketUpdated 2026-07-15

Growth tools only earn their keep if they move a real number — leads, conversions or revenue — not just activity. So where does Kit actually fit? It's most often picked as a leaner, cheaper alternative to Mailchimp — so the real question is whether it does the job you actually need without the bloat.

The honest bottom line. Worth it for creators, writers and coaches building an audience-first business who want email, landing pages. Skip it once you're an e-commerce store or your list is big-but-cold.

What Kit does

Email marketing built for creators (formerly ConvertKit). Grow your list with landing pages and forms, then automate newsletters and sell digital products from one place; a beta Kit MCP connects AI assistants like Claude directly to your account. Recent additions: a new landing-page editor, Dynamic Content, Newsletter Sponsorships, and App Store integrations on paid plans — SMS via SlickText, Patreon member sync (tier and support data in automations), Luma/Eventbrite event sync, and two-way HubSpot CRM sync on Pro.

Pricing (2026)

(2026) A genuinely generous free Newsletter tier (up to 10,000 subscribers, unlimited sends, 1 automation); Creator from about $39/mo at 1,000 subscribers scaling to ~$199/mo at 25k; Creator Pro ~$79-279/mo adds scoring and advanced reporting. Annual saves ~16%. Note: prices rose roughly 35% in late 2025. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

Best for: creators, writers and coaches building an audience-first business who want email, landing pages, digital products and a creator network in one.
Who should skip it: if you're an e-commerce store (commerce-native email tools fit better) or your list is big-but-cold — clean it before paying per subscriber.

The honest knock

Two honest notes. Subscriber-based pricing counts everyone on your list — cold subscribers cost the same as fans, so prune before you price. And the ~35% price hike in 2025 changed the value math: the free tier is still arguably the best in creator email, but paid tiers now deserve comparison shopping rather than default loyalty. The creator-first features (tip jars, paid newsletters, recommendations) remain the real differentiator.

The natural comparison is Beehiiv or MailerLite — the creator-email rivals — Beehiiv leans newsletter-growth mechanics, MailerLite undercuts on price; Kit wins on creator-commerce maturity. Weigh the honest alternatives on our Mailchimp alternatives page.
