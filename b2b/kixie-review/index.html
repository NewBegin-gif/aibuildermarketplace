What Kixie does

Sales engagement with a power dialer: automated calling and SMS, CRM integration and call analytics to help reps connect with more leads, faster.

Pricing (2026)

(2026) Billed quarterly: Integrated about $35/user/mo, Professional ~$65, Outbound PowerCall ~$95 — minutes NOT included (per-minute or ~$30/user unlimited bundle); multi-line dialer and AI voice detection are add-ons. Realistic all-in: ~$110-220/user/mo. 7-day trial. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

The honest knock

Plan for 30-60% above the sticker: minutes, the multi-line dialer and AI human-voice-detection all bill separately, and the quarterly auto-renewing contract only allows downgrades in its final 30 days — read that clause before signing. The dialer itself is genuinely fast and CRM-native; just buy it with the full basket priced and the calendar reminder set (our Renewal Radar exists for exactly this).

What buyers report about Kixie

Recurring themes from G2 and Trustpilot, read in August 2026. We have not run Kixie ourselves, so this is other buyers' experience rather than ours. We summarise it here because the complaints are usually the part a vendor page leaves out.

What they praise
  • • 4.8 on G2 across 860+ reviews — the power dialer and the CRM integration are what sales teams rate.
  • • Connect rates improve measurably with local presence dialling, which is the reason to buy it.
What they complain about
  • • Annual contracts with no mid-cycle downgrade and no refunds. Downgrades are only possible in the last 30 days, and Trustpilot reviewers describe being locked in when a quarterly renewal landed before they could cancel.
  • • The official pricing page lists plans but not per-user prices. Integrated is $35, Professional $65, Outbound Power Dialer $95 — but real-world cost reaches $109-220 per user once AI Voice Detection ($30/user) and SMS volume are added.
  • • Support responsiveness and mobile app bugs recur on Trustpilot, at 3.8 out of 5 with auto-renewal the dominant theme.
  • • HubSpot users report team SMS and contact sync quirks, strict phone-number formatting and missed logs.
Best for: outbound sales teams living in their CRM that want a serious power dialer with local presence, SMS and automation.
Who should skip it: if low call volumes (per-seat + minutes math won't pay back), or you want monthly flexibility without contract fine print.

The natural comparison is PhoneBurner or JustCall — dialer peers with simpler pricing — compare all-in per-seat including minutes, and the contract terms. Decide by which one fits the job above, not by the louder brand.

Frequently asked questions

Will Kixie work for a remote or distributed team?

Generally yes, since this category is built for it, but the details that bite are timezone handling, how per-seat cost scales as the team spreads, and whether numbers, routing or coverage exist in the countries your people actually sit in. Availability varies far more by country than the marketing pages suggest, so check your specific regions before committing.

Who should not use Kixie?

Skip it if low call volumes (per-seat + minutes math won't pay back), or you want monthly flexibility without contract fine print. Buying a tool to fix a problem you don't have yet just adds cost and another login to manage.
