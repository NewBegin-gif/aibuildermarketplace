Kopage sells two things. Kopage Studio, the cloud white-label builder for agencies, has four monthly plans priced by the number of active websites: Solo 49 € a month for 5 websites with 10K AI tokens; Pro 149 € for 25 websites with 100K AI tokens and custom branding of the admin area; Scale 349 € for 100 websites, 100K AI tokens and a separate cloud instance; Unlimited 699 € for unlimited websites with the same 100K AI tokens and separate instance.

Every plan is white-label and lets you connect your own domains. No yearly prices were shown for these plans. On Scale and Unlimited a client site can also be self-hosted on the client's own server, for additional fees the page does not state, and fair-use limits on disk space and bandwidth may apply. For a single site, a Single Website plan (a service provided by Website•IM) is 9 € a month billed annually or 19 € monthly for 1 static website.

Full pricing notes (2 more paragraphs)

What Kopage does

White-label AI website builder for freelancers and agencies that build sites for local clients: start from AI or a template, hosting in European or US datacenters, own domains, plans priced by the number of active websites, plus a free self-hosted PHP version.

You pay for a count of sites, not for features

Every cloud plan is white-label, so client sites carry no Kopage branding even on Solo. What the tiers change is how many websites you may publish and a few agency extras. At full use Solo works out at about 9.80 € a site a month, Pro at about 5.96 € and Scale at about 3.49 €, our arithmetic from the plan prices and site limits. Pro is the first plan that puts your own logo and colours in the admin area your clients log in to, and it raises the AI tokens from 10K to 100K.

Above Pro the token figure stays at 100K, so Scale and Unlimited buy site count, a separate cloud instance with your own technical address (client.your-domain.com) and the option to host a site on a client's server for an extra fee Kopage does not publish. If you only need one website, the agency plans are the wrong product: the Single Website plan or the free self-hosted version is the cheaper route.

The plans also leave the client billing to you. In the default agency setup Kopage does no billing towards your clients: you build in your dashboard, share access when the site is ready and charge the client the way you prefer, by bank transfer, invoice or cash (white-label page, read 25 October 2026). The DIY Platform setup instead gives clients a sign-up site on your subdomain where they create a site in a 14-day trial and pay online through Stripe at a price you set.

Every site comes with the same built-in modules: a shopping cart with PayPal by default and gateways such as Stripe, Square, Mollie, Klarna, iDEAL and PayU, a blog with comments, forms, a newsletter, an events calendar and live chat through Smartsupp or tawk.to. That is the clearest difference with Brizy, where an online store was a separate Brizy Shops add-on in our 24 October 2026 reading.

How to actually use Kopage

Start the free trial, which the pricing page puts at 7 days with most features available, and build one real client site from start to finish: let the AI generate it from a short description or start from a template, connect a test domain and look at the admin area your client would use. Count the websites you expect to have live in the next year, because the plan limit is a count of active sites: 5 on Solo, 25 on Pro, 100 on Scale.

Keep an eye on the AI tokens while you build, since Kopage does not say how fast 10K tokens on Solo run out, when they reset or what more costs. Pick a datacenter in Europe or the US when you set up. And treat the first payment as final: the terms say Kopage generally does not refund. The agency plan starts with a domain of your own parked on Kopage's name servers as a technical address; each client site is created as a subdomain of it until you connect the client's real domain.

Set aside a domain for that before you start, since its DNS moves to Kopage. On Scale and Unlimited the separate cloud instance gives new sites a client.your-domain.com address. Support sits inside the product: the support page says to log in and use the HELP button in the left sidebar for answers, videos and tickets, and it sends people who got their site from an agency or host back to that provider. If you resell Kopage, you are your clients' first line of support.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Kopage cost?

Read 16 October 2026, in euros: Solo 49 € a month for 5 websites, Pro 149 € for 25, Scale 349 € for 100 and Unlimited 699 € for unlimited websites, all white-label. A Single Website plan run by Website•IM is 9 € a month billed annually or 19 € monthly. The self-hosted version is free, with Basic at $50 and Business at $150 a year. Local sales tax may apply.

Can I get a refund?

Probably not from Kopage directly. Section 10 of the terms says Kopage generally does not provide refunds, with reasoning written around downloaded licences that cannot be deactivated remotely. Section 9 says orders are processed by Paddle.com as merchant of record, which provides customer service and handles returns, so a refund request on a cloud plan goes to Paddle. The terms point to the online demo and the free self-hosted version as ways to test first.

How long is the free trial?

Seven days for your own account, on the pricing page and the white-label page (read 25 October 2026). The 14 days that appear on the white-label page belong to the DIY Platform setup: there your clients start their own sites in a 14-day trial before they pay you. The self-hosted page's FAQ also says 14 days, but that FAQ describes an older Agency Plan.

Can my clients' sites run on their own hosting?

On Scale and Unlimited, yes: Kopage says you can self-host a client website on the client's own server, for additional fees it does not publish. On Solo and Pro the sites stay in Kopage's cloud, in a European or US datacenter you choose.

Which payment gateways does a Kopage shop support?

The shopping-cart page (read 25 October 2026) says the cart is built into every Kopage website, works with PayPal by default and lists 2Checkout, Authorize.net, Paytrail, iDEAL, MercadoPago, Stripe, Square, Mollie, DotPay, TPay, PayU, Billplz, PayFast, Redsys, Klarna, PayFlex, ЮKassa, Payson, Rapyd, QuickPay and LiqPay. It can also run as a plain catalogue. Transaction fees are not mentioned.

Who is behind Kopage?

Sablike Sàrl, a company in Switzerland, which operates kopage.com according to the terms and the privacy policy (effective 15 May 2024). Claims are governed by Swiss law. The privacy policy says personal data may be transferred outside Europe, including to Canada and the United States, and names PayPal, Paddle and Google Analytics among the services involved.
