What KrispCall does

Virtual numbers across 100+ countries with a shared dashboard, dialer and CRM hooks, from about $12/user/month on annual billing. Calls and SMS are pay-as-you-go from a separate credit balance, so compare per-country call rates at your volume rather than the seat price.

When the paid plan is worth it

KrispCall's Essential tier ($12/user/month billed annually, $15 month-to-month) is workable for a small team of up to 5 users that needs basic calling, multi-level IVR, and 100+ listed integrations, with one number per seat included in the price. The concrete wall is that 5-user cap: exceeding it forces a move to Standard ($32/user/month annually, up to 50 users), which is also the only tier that unlocks call recording, power dialer, bulk SMS campaigns, and Salesforce integration — all marked unavailable on Essential's own comparison table.

What upgrading does not fix is usage billing. Calls and SMS are charged pay-as-you-go on top of the subscription at every tier. And running US bulk SMS campaigns still requires separate A2P 10DLC registration costs regardless of plan: a one-time brand fee, a one-time campaign vetting fee, plus a small recurring monthly campaign fee. A Standard or Enterprise subscription buys seats and features, not lower or bundled calling and messaging costs.

How to actually use KrispCall

KrispCall's own guide describes sign-up as a 7-step flow: create the account, verify identity, then buy or port a number and add team members. New accounts start unverified, and the helpdesk states Basic KYC document review finishes within 1-2 hours, with email confirmation once it clears. Until then, an account is capped at 20 outbound SMS total and 5 phone number purchases, which is enough to test the product but not to run a real campaign.

If you already have a number and want to port it in rather than buy new, KrispCall's own porting guide puts the timeline at 1-2 weeks for most numbers, narrowing to 1-10 days for individual local numbers — this is the step most likely to delay an actual go-live date. CRM connections are also tier-gated: Salesforce integration is absent from the Essential plan and only appears on Standard, so a team planning to wire KrispCall into its CRM from day one needs to budget for that tier before starting onboarding, not after.

Frequently asked questions

What does KrispCall cost per user?

$12 per user a month on annual billing ($15 monthly) for Essential, and $32 ($40 monthly) for Standard. Annual billing saves 20%, which their page states as $36 and $96 per user a year. Read on 17 August 2026.

Is there a KrispCall promo code?

We have found none, and it is a common search. The published discount is the annual one — 20%, worth $36 or $96 per user a year — plus $1 of free calling and SMS credit on signup. Treat any code you find elsewhere with suspicion and check it at checkout.

What are the user limits on each plan?

This is the detail to check before you buy: Essential is aimed at solo agents and teams of up to 5 people, and Standard at teams scaling up to 50. Above that you are into Enterprise and a quote. Calls and SMS are billed separately from a credit balance on every plan, so the seat price is a floor rather than the bill.

Will KrispCall work for a remote or distributed team?

Generally yes, since this category is built for it, but the details that bite are timezone handling, how per-seat cost scales as the team spreads, and whether numbers, routing or coverage exist in the countries your people actually sit in. Availability varies far more by country than the marketing pages suggest, so check your specific regions before committing.

Who should not use KrispCall?

Skip it if your calling is domestic-only at low volume (simpler tools suffice), or you need enterprise reliability guarantees over price. Buying a tool to fix a problem you don't have yet just adds cost and another login to manage.
