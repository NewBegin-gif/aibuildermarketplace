What Leadfeeder does

Website visitor identification for B2B sales. See which companies visit your site and what they viewed, and turn anonymous traffic into a warm pipeline.

Pricing (2026)

(2026-08-01, read on their own pricing page) Lite €0 forever, after a 14-day full-access trial: the last 100 identified companies, 7 days of visitor history, 20 credits a month. Discover from €79/month billed annually, or €113 month-to-month. Activate from €369 annually, or €527 monthly. Scale €599, annual billing only. Enterprise quote-only. Users are unlimited on every tier. Credits are consumed by enrichment and activation actions, not by identifying a company. The identified-company allowance per paid tier is shown only as 'Tiered', with no numbers. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

The words doing the work are 'starting at'. What actually sets your price is how many companies you identify a month, and the page shows that allowance as 'Tiered' with no figures at any level — so €79 is the floor of the smallest bucket and you cannot tell from the page which bucket your traffic puts you in. Get that table before you talk price. Two other things: month-to-month costs 43% more than annual on the same plan (€113 against €79), and Scale is annual-only, so the tier most growing teams land on cannot be tried for a month.

What buyers report about Leadfeeder

Recurring themes from G2, GetApp and Software Advice, read in August 2026. We have not run Leadfeeder ourselves, so this is other buyers' experience rather than ours. We summarise it here because the complaints are usually the part a vendor page leaves out.

What they praise
  • • Visitor identification that is genuinely simple to read, with solid CRM integrations across Salesforce, HubSpot, Pipedrive, Zoho and Dynamics. 4.3 on G2 across 860 reviews.
  • • For European traffic the post-merger Dealfront data is a real strength.
What they complain about
  • • Annual plans auto-renew and you must cancel 30 days before the date or face another twelve months. Reviewers name this as the number one trust complaint across all three platforms, made worse by the absence of renewal reminders.
  • • The identification is IP-based, and remote work breaks it. Anyone browsing from home, a café or a consumer ISP is invisible, and 80%+ of identified visitors come back with incomplete records. Judge the tool on your own traffic before committing.
  • • Post-merger price rises of 20-40% were reported after the Dealfront rebrand, with free-plan users facing $99-$200/month for equivalent function.
  • • Dealfront pricing is modular and unpublished — estimating cost without a sales call is close to impossible.
Best for: B2B teams with real website traffic and a sales team that will actually work the list — the free tier is enough to find out whether your traffic contains anyone worth calling.
Who should skip it: if your traffic is mostly consumer or very low volume, because company identification needs business networks to match against and thin traffic produces a thin list.

The natural comparison is Albacross or Clearbit — the direct visitor-identification peers — compare the identified-company allowance at your traffic level, since that is the number that sets the bill. Decide by which one fits the job above, not by the louder brand.

Frequently asked questions

Is Leadfeeder worth it for a small team?

It depends on whether you have the bottleneck it solves. Small teams get the most out of this category when one clear problem is already costing real hours or revenue; buying ahead of that just adds cost and another login. Price it against the hours or lost deals it removes, not against its feature list, and start on the smallest plan that covers the job.

Who should not use Leadfeeder?

Skip it if your traffic is mostly consumer or very low volume, because company identification needs business networks to match against and thin traffic produces a thin list. Buying a tool to fix a problem you don't have yet just adds cost and another login to manage.
