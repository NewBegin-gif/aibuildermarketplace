When the paid plan is worth it

One number decides this ladder and it is not a plan price: Starter is $29 a month ($24 annually) plus $5 for every paid enrollment, while Pro Trainer at $99 charges no enrollment fee at all. That makes the maths concrete. The gap between the two is $70, so fourteen paid enrollments a month is where they meet. Below fourteen, Starter is cheaper; above it, Starter is the more expensive plan and gets worse with every sale.

At thirty enrollments Starter costs $179 against Pro Trainer's $99. On annual billing the crossover comes earlier, at about eleven. Upgrading does not settle the rest of the bill. Every plan price excludes VAT, added for EU buyers without a valid EU VAT number, and your payment gateway charges on top on every tier — Stripe at about 2.9% plus $0.30, so roughly $6.10 on a $200 course before LearnWorlds takes anything.

The branded mobile app is a separate add-on that the page lists as having multiple price options, with no amount attached.

How to actually use LearnWorlds

Work out your monthly paid enrollments before you pick a tier, because Starter's $5-per-paid-enrollment fee is the number that decides this. Starter at $29 plus $5 a paid enrollment costs the same as Pro Trainer's flat $99 at fourteen a month — above that, the cheap tier is the expensive one. On annual billing the crossover falls to about eleven. Add two things to whatever you conclude. Your payment gateway charges on top regardless of tier, Stripe at roughly 2.9% plus $0.30, and every plan price excludes VAT, which is added for EU buyers unless a valid EU VAT number is given.

Ask for the branded mobile app's price rather than budgeting a figure for it: the page lists it as an add-on with multiple price options and prints no amount. Use the 30-day trial to build one real course end to end, checkout included. Build it on the tier you intend to buy rather than the one you are trialling, and check your admin count while you are there — Starter allows one.

Frequently asked questions

What does LearnWorlds cost?

$29 a month on Starter (or $24 annually), $99 on Pro Trainer and $299 on Learning Center, with a 30-day trial and 20% off annual billing. But read the enrollment fee before comparing: Starter charges $5 per enrollment and Pro Trainer charges none.

When does the $5 enrollment fee overtake the plan price?

At fourteen enrollments a month. Starter at $29 plus fourteen enrollments is $99 — exactly Pro Trainer, which has no transaction fee. So if you sell more than about a dozen course seats a month, the cheaper plan is the more expensive one.

What else is not included?

Two things that catch course creators: the branded mobile app is a separate add-on at roughly $149 a month, and your payment gateway takes its own cut — Stripe at about 2.9% plus $0.30 per transaction — on every plan including the ones advertising no transaction fee. The 'no fee' refers to LearnWorlds, not to the whole chain.

What should I weigh before buying Growth & Revenue software?

It depends on whether you have the bottleneck it solves. Small teams get the most out of this category when one clear problem is already costing real hours or revenue; buying ahead of that just adds cost and another login. Price it against the hours or lost deals it removes, not against its feature list, and start on the smallest plan that covers the job.
