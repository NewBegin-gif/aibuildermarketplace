Growth tools only earn their keep if they move a real number — leads, conversions or revenue — not just activity. So where does lemlist actually fit?

What lemlist does

Cold email outreach and sales engagement. Build personalized multichannel sequences (email, LinkedIn, calls) with built-in deliverability tools to book more meetings.

Pricing (2026)

No free plan; paid runs roughly $39/user/mo (Email Starter) up to ~$159/user/mo (Outreach Scale) as of 2026. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

Best for: teams that have a clear growth bottleneck this tool directly addresses.

The honest knock

The per-seat plus per-email-account model makes total cost unpredictable as you scale, and reviews flag support response times and the LinkedIn Chrome-extension's reliability.

The natural comparison is Instantly or Smartlead — cheaper, unlimited-mailbox cold-email tools. Decide by which one fits the job above, not by the louder brand.
