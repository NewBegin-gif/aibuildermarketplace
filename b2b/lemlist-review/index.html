What lemlist does

Cold email outreach and sales engagement. Build personalized multichannel sequences (email, LinkedIn, calls) with built-in deliverability tools to book more meetings.

Pricing (2026)

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

The per-seat plus per-email-account model makes total cost unpredictable as you scale, and reviews flag support response times and the LinkedIn Chrome-extension's reliability.

When the paid plan is worth it

lemlist has no free plan — you get 14 days of full Multichannel access, no credit card required. The real decision is Email ($69/month, $55 on annual billing) versus Multichannel ($109/month, $87 annual, billed per user). Email covers 50,000 emails a month, unlimited users, the email and phone finder, and the 650M+ lead database. Multichannel lifts that cap to unlimited emails and messages, and adds LinkedIn, SMS and WhatsApp automation, a call dialer, and 5 senders per user. Upgrade if you run LinkedIn or phone touches inside the same sequences as your email, or if you send more than 50,000 emails a month; below that volume the higher tier buys channels, not better email. The seat model also flips: Email is unlimited users, Multichannel is charged per user. Upgrading does not change enrichment either, which stays pay-per-credit on every plan — 1,000 credits for $10, 5 credits per email verification, 20 credits per phone number — and unused credits expire every month with no rollover. Saleshandy, which sells a competing sequencer, puts a five-person annual Multichannel team at $435/month listed and says the real cost can reach $700-800 once extra senders ($9/month each), credit top-ups and add-ons are counted; that same article closes by telling readers to start with Saleshandy. Check that figure against your own seat and credit use — it is a calculation by a competitor, not a measured bill.

How to actually use lemlist

lemlist's setup guide lists three steps: connect your email provider (Gmail, Outlook, or custom SMTP), set up your email signature, then add SPF, DKIM and a custom tracking domain to your DNS. The guide estimates 15-30 minutes depending on DNS propagation, and notes records can take up to 48 hours to show as verified in your lemlist settings — do not launch before they do. Where people get stuck: the review on Saleshandy's blog, written by a competing sequencer, found connecting email and LinkedIn accounts overly complicated for new users before their first campaign, and reports users periodically removing and reinstalling the LinkedIn Chrome extension. LinkedIn volume ramps automatically: newly connected accounts start at 2 invitations a day and increase by 2 per day up to your configured cap, with 20 a day the recommended setup. Teams that get results build and verify lead lists in external tools, then import clean contacts. Distilled from lemlist's own technical-setup help article for the steps and timings. The friction points come from two reviews published by companies that sell competing products: one on the blog of Saleshandy, a competing sequencer, and one from Prospeo, which sells competing lead data and aggregates G2, Trustpilot and Reddit reports.

Best for: outbound teams that live in cold email and want multichannel sequences, warm-up and a big template community in one per-seat tool.
Who should skip it: if you send occasional one-off campaigns — per-seat pricing from ~$39/user only pays off with steady outbound volume.

The natural comparison is Instantly or Smartlead — cheaper, unlimited-mailbox cold-email tools. Decide by which one fits the job above, not by the louder brand.
