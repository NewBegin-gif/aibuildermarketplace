Detailed Pricing Analysis: What You Actually Pay

Lindy's pricing structure reflects its positioning as a premium AI automation platform, with costs that scale based on agent activity rather than simple seat-based models.

Starter Plan: $39/month

The entry-level plan includes one active agent with up to 1,000 actions per month. Actions include sending emails, making API calls, updating records, or processing data. For small teams testing AI automation, this provides sufficient capacity to handle routine tasks like email responses or basic lead qualification. However, the single-agent limitation means you can't run parallel workflows for different business functions.

Professional Plan: $99/month

This plan supports up to 5 active agents with 5,000 monthly actions. The Professional tier also includes advanced integrations, custom webhooks, and priority support. For most B2B SaaS companies, this plan provides the flexibility to automate multiple workflows simultaneously. Real-world usage typically sees 3,000-4,000 actions monthly for a team of 10-15 people with moderate automation needs.

Business Plan: $299/month

The Business plan offers 15 active agents with 20,000 monthly actions, plus advanced analytics and team collaboration features. This tier suits growing companies that want to automate customer support, sales processes, and operational workflows simultaneously. The higher action limit accommodates more complex, multi-step workflows that generate numerous API calls and data updates.

Enterprise: Custom Pricing

Enterprise plans start around $1,000/month and include unlimited agents, custom action limits, dedicated support, and on-premise deployment options. Enterprise customers also get access to beta features and can request custom integrations. For companies processing over 50,000 actions monthly or requiring specific compliance features, enterprise pricing becomes necessary.

Implementation Best Practices for B2B Founders

Successfully implementing Lindy requires strategic planning and gradual rollout. Start by identifying high-volume, repetitive tasks that follow predictable patterns. Customer support inquiries, lead qualification, and data entry workflows typically provide the best initial automation opportunities.

Begin with a single agent focused on one specific workflow. This allows your team to understand Lindy's capabilities and limitations without overwhelming existing processes. Monitor agent performance closely during the first month, making adjustments based on actual results rather than assumptions.

Train agents using real examples from your business rather than generic templates. The more specific and contextual your training data, the better agents will perform in production scenarios. Document common edge cases and provide clear escalation procedures for situations agents cannot handle independently.

Establish clear metrics for measuring automation success. Track time savings, error rates, customer satisfaction scores, and process completion times. This data helps optimize agent performance and demonstrates ROI to stakeholders.
