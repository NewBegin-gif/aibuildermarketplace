What Logome.ai does

Generates a logo and basic brand assets from a prompt. Designing and previewing is free, but downloading requires a paid plan — Basic at around $29/month gives one file without a transparent background. Their public site including pricing returned 404 at last check, so verify what you are charged.

Frequently asked questions

What does Logome.ai cost?

Designing and previewing is free without signing up; downloading is not. Our dated reading puts Basic at about $29 a month ($19 annually), Premium at $99 ($49) and the Logo + Brand Kit tier at $199 ($99). We could not re-confirm those on 17 August 2026, so check before paying.

Do I own the logo?

On the paid plans, yes — commercial rights are included, which is the whole point of paying rather than screenshotting the preview. That is also the reason the free preview is genuinely free: the file, not the design, is the product.

Which tier do I actually need?

Most businesses need one logo in transparent, high-resolution formats, which is what the middle tier provides. The brand-kit tier is for a full identity rollout rather than a mark, and the entry tier's single file will frustrate you the first time you need a different background.

Is Logome.ai worth it for a small team?

It depends on whether you have the bottleneck it solves. Small teams get the most out of this category when one clear problem is already costing real hours or revenue; buying ahead of that just adds cost and another login. Price it against the hours or lost deals it removes, not against its feature list, and start on the smallest plan that covers the job.

Who should not use Logome.ai?

Skip it if you need a truly bespoke, trademark-safe brand identity (hire a designer), or the single-file Basic tier won't give you the formats you actually need. Buying a tool to fix a problem you don't have yet just adds cost and another login to manage.
