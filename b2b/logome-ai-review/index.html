What buyers report about Logome.ai

Recurring themes from the buyer-review sites listed at the foot of this page, read in August 2026. We have not run Logome.ai ourselves, so this is other buyers' experience rather than ours. We summarise it here because the complaints are usually the part a vendor page leaves out.

What they praise
  • • It produces more varied initial concepts than Looka, Hatchful or Canva's logo maker, and the brand kit integration is better.
  • • Fast and cheap for a startup that needs branding without hiring a designer. Trustpilot reviews are largely positive.
What they complain about
  • • Unauthorised charges continuing after cancellation, and difficulty cancelling, are reported by multiple users. Watch your statement.
  • • The $19 Basic tier ships one logo file with no transparent background — a basic professional need — so you are effectively pushed to Premium just to get a usable PNG.
  • • Pricing is inconsistent across sources ($49/month or $24 annually for Premium, $99 or $57 for the brand kit). Those figures could never be reconciled, and now they cannot be checked at all: the vendor's site is gone.

Did Logome move to a new domain?

Not that we can find. I am already being charged. What now?

Cancel through the payment processor rather than the vendor: find the charge on your card or PayPal statement and stop it there, since the site that would host an account page does not load. Export anything you still have access to, and do not count on a support address answering.

Do I own the logo I already downloaded?

On the paid plans commercial rights were included, which was the point of paying rather than screenshotting the preview. What you cannot do now is obtain fresh written proof of that grant from a vendor whose site is gone — so if the mark matters to your business, get an independent licence or a new one you can document.
