Frequently asked questions

What does Lucrovox cost?

Our dated reading gives $199 a month for up to 100 AI-handled calls, $499 for up to 500 with CRM integration, and $999 for up to 2,000 with white-label and multi-location. On 17 August 2026 we could not re-confirm those from the page itself: it now leads with an ROI calculator rather than a plan table. Ask for the current tier list in writing.

What are the numbers on their page actually measuring?

Not price. The figures that dominate the page — $750 a week, $39,000 a year — come from a calculator that multiplies your missed calls by an assumed $500 job value and a 30% close rate. Those are defaults you can change, not measurements of your business, so treat the output as an illustration rather than a forecast.

What decides which tier I need?

Call volume: the tiers are bounded at roughly 100, 500 and 2,000 AI-handled calls a month. Count the calls you currently miss rather than the calls you receive, since that is the volume the service is meant to catch.

What should I weigh before buying Communication & Voice software?

Generally yes, since this category is built for it, but the details that bite are timezone handling, how per-seat cost scales as the team spreads, and whether numbers, routing or coverage exist in the countries your people actually sit in. Availability varies far more by country than the marketing pages suggest, so check your specific regions before committing.
