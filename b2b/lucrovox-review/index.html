What Lucrovox does

AI phone agent for local service businesses — answers missed calls, books jobs and captures leads 24/7 for trades like HVAC, plumbing and roofing.

Pricing (2026)

(2026) Published pricing, 14-day free trial on every plan (Enterprise 30-day; you are not charged until day 15). Starter $199/mo for up to 100 AI-handled calls; Pro $499/mo for up to 500 calls with CRM integration and advanced lead qualification; Enterprise $999/mo for up to 2,000 calls, white-label and multi-location. Higher volume is custom. (Correction 14 Jul 2026: an earlier version of this review said pricing wasn’t public — it is, in the pricing section of their homepage; their /pricing URL 404s, which is what tripped us up.) Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

The meter is AI-handled calls: Starter’s 100 calls a month is roughly three a day, so a genuinely busy shop outgrows it quickly — and the steps are steep ($199 → $499 → $999). The ‘callers can’t tell it’s AI’ claim is the vendor’s own: they offer a live demo number, so test that before you commit.

When the paid plan is worth it

LucroVox does not publish flat dollar prices; tiers are differentiated purely by monthly call volume, and an actual price only appears once you start checkout. The Starter tier covers up to 100 AI-handled calls a month, which suits a business whose current inbound volume sits near or below that, since exceeding the cap is the concrete trigger to move up. Pro, capped at 500 calls a month, also adds CRM integration and advanced lead qualification, features Starter does not include at all, so a business that wants call data flowing into an existing CRM needs Pro regardless of how many calls it actually takes. Enterprise raises the ceiling to 2,000 calls a month and adds white-label branding and multi-location support. What upgrading does not solve: any call the AI cannot fully resolve still gets transferred to a live person or left as a flagged message on every tier, so a higher plan buys more automated call capacity, not less reliance on your own staff for complex or edge-case calls.

How to actually use Lucrovox

Setup starts with signing up for a plan: Starter and Pro carry a 14-day free trial, Enterprise a 30-day trial. You provide business details, a preferred call script and qualifying questions, and calendar access, connecting Google Calendar, Calendly, HousecallPro, ServiceTitan, Jobber, or another CRM, with anything else needing a custom API integration. No coding is required. LucroVox states most businesses go live within 48 hours of signing up, with billing on the 14-day plans starting on day 15. The practical snag: CRM integration and advanced lead qualification sit behind the Pro tier, not Starter, so a business that signs up for Starter to test the waters and then finds it needs CRM sync has to upgrade mid-trial. Businesses running a system outside the listed integrations are pointed to a custom API build rather than a ready-made connector, which adds development work before the AI can actually book into that calendar.
