When the paid plan is worth it

The meter here is audience size, not engagement, and that distinction is the whole thing to understand. Bands run $14 a month for 250 active contacts, $29 for 2,500, $69 for 7,500 and $139 for 25,000, with per-contact billing above each allowance. Inactive contacts still count toward your band unless you remove them, so a Reel that goes wide moves you up a band and keeps you there long after those people stopped replying.

Pruning inactive contacts is a billing task, not just housekeeping. Per contact the ladder rewards scale heavily: 5.6 cents at 250, 1.2 cents at 2,500, 0.9 at 7,500 and 0.56 at 25,000 -- a tenfold improvement from bottom to top. But the band edges are brutal in the other direction: crossing from 250 to 251 contacts doubles your bill to $29, and you get 2,250 contacts of headroom you did not ask for.

If you are near a boundary, the cheaper move is often to clean the list rather than to upgrade. One structural line to note: two channels on the lower tiers, unlimited from $69. If you need a third channel, that is a jump to the $69 band regardless of how few contacts you have. The free tier is no longer a working plan -- since the March 2026 change it covers 25 active contacts, two channels and one user. Treat it as a demo. Annual billing takes 18 to 30% off.

How to actually use ManyChat

Put a monthly prune in the calendar before you launch anything, because inactive contacts count toward your band unless you remove them. The bill tracks audience size rather than engagement — which is why a viral Reel can move you up a band — and clearing out people who have not interacted in months is the single most effective cost control here. Check the free tier as it stands rather than as it is remembered.

Since the March 2026 change it covers 25 active contacts with two channels and one user, so anything you have read describing a generous ManyChat free plan is out of date. The paid bands run $14 for 250 contacts, $29 for 2,500, $69 for 7,500 and $139 for 25,000, with per-contact billing above each allowance and channels limited to two below $69. Build the fallback path deliberately, because it will be used.

Flows here are button-and-script based rather than natural language, so anyone who types something unexpected drops out of the flow — decide now whether that person gets a human, a menu or a link, and test it as a stranger would. The AI add-on is closer to keyword matching than conversational AI, so do not design around it doing more. And plan not to need support during a campaign: it is email-only and slow, so launch on a quiet week rather than the morning of a promotion.

The natural comparison is Chatfuel or newer AI chatbots — the other chat-automation routes.
