Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 4 Nov 2026

Two plans, and the free one is a real plan rather than a demo. Basic is free to install and, on the listing as read on 4 November 2026, covers 100 emails and 300 push notifications, which the listing itself translates as roughly 100 recovered carts; in September the same card named push notifications only. It carries unlimited subscribers and segmentation, the abandoned-cart recovery campaign, scheduled promotional campaigns, AI-generated campaign content, intelligent delivery timing and basic email support, and it takes no commission on recovered revenue.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need Meeri Pro, or is the free plan enough?

Start on Basic, because the free plan is sized to answer the only question that matters before you pay. Read on 19 September 2026 and unchanged since August, the free tier hands you 300 notifications a month, which the listing itself translates as roughly 100 recovered carts, together with unlimited subscribers and segmentation, the abandoned-cart campaign, scheduled promotions, AI-generated campaign content and intelligent delivery. Upgrading multiplies that allowance tenfold for less than five dollars, throws in unlimited campaigns and automations, and swaps basic email support for premium; neither tier takes a cut of what it recovers, and the paid one opens with a week you do not pay for. What decides the upgrade is not the feature list but your opt-in rate: web push only reaches browsers that accepted the prompt, so a month on Basic tells you how many of your visitors that actually is. If 300 notifications a month is more than you can send because too few people opted in, the paid plan buys you nothing. If you run out in the first week, it costs less than one recovered order.

What are the usage-based charges on top of the $4.99?

Nobody outside the vendor knows, and that is worth one email before you enable the plan. The Pro card says “Upgrades as you grow. Additional usage-based charges are billed every 30 days”, and the footer repeats that recurring and usage-based charges are billed every 30 days in USD. No rate appears anywhere on the listing, and meeritech.com does not print one either: both re-read on 19 September 2026. There is a second ambiguity stacked on the first: Basic is measured in 300 push notifications and Pro in 3,000 push notification impressions. Those are different words, and neither page defines the second, so one campaign sent to a thousand subscribers might count as one notification or as a thousand impressions. Between them, those two unpublished details decide whether Pro is ten times the free plan or roughly the same size, and what happens when you pass it. Ask support for the impression definition and the overage rate in writing, and keep the reply.

Is the 5.0 rating on Meeri’s website real?

No, and the gap is easy to check yourself. On 19 September 2026 meeritech.com advertises “5.0 on Shopify App Store”, “100% five-star reviews” and “5.0 average — 8 verified reviews on Shopify”. The App Store listing the same day shows 4.7 from 11 reviews, with ten at five stars and one at one star. The vendor’s number is the average you get by omitting the only bad review, and its count has been frozen at eight since at least 23 August while the listing added two more. Treat both numbers as very thin evidence rather than as a dispute worth settling: eleven reviews on an app that launched in February 2024 cannot tell you much either way, and one of the three the listing shows first was written after seven days of use. Judge this on your own opt-in rate over a free month, not on anyone’s star average.

Is Meeri web push only, or does it send email too?

Both, according to the Shopify App Store listing read on 28 October 2026: it describes recovering abandoned carts and checkouts via email and web push notifications, and custom email automation campaigns. The plan cards count push notifications and impressions; the listing does not state an email sending limit per plan or whether email uses the same allowance, so treat that as not confirmed and ask support before you rely on email volume.
