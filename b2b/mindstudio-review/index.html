Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 18 Sep 2026

The subscription is a floor, not the bill: every tier reads “+ usage”, so model calls are charged on top. Free is $0/month plus usage and caps you at one agent and 1,000 runs a month. Individual is $20/month plus usage, or $16/month plus usage billed yearly, a stated 20% saving, and lifts both caps to unlimited agents and unlimited runs. Business is quote-based.

Because the metered part is uncapped by the plan, the tier you pick says little about what you will actually pay — the run volume does. The metered half is more governable than that makes it sound, and the page is specific about it. A run is one execution of an agent, triggered manually or automatically. MindStudio states it does not mark up model cost — its FAQ says you pay exactly the same price as bringing your own API keys from the providers — and that you can set per-agent and total spend limits and track run cost per agent.

All tiers reach 200+ models through its Service Router without API keys of your own, with bring-your-own keys as an option, and hitting a run limit triggers a notification with the choice of upgrading or buying additional runs rather than a hard stop mid-month. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

When the paid plan is worth it

The free tier works for anyone testing a single workflow: one agent, up to 1,000 runs a month, and access to the same pool of 200+ underlying AI models as paid tiers, according to MindStudio's pricing page. The concrete trigger for upgrading is hitting either limit — needing another agent or exceeding 1,000 monthly runs — at which point the Individual tier, $20/month or $16/month billed yearly, removes both caps entirely rather than raising them incrementally.

What upgrading to Individual does not solve is anything related to team use: granular collaborator permissions, single sign-on, audit logs, custom SLAs, self-hosting, and custom domains are reserved for the Business tier, which is quote-only with no published price. So a solo builder outgrowing the free cap gets unlimited agents and runs for a flat monthly fee. A small team that needs shared workspace governance or compliance features still has to go through a sales conversation, regardless of how much it already pays at the Individual level.

How to actually use MindStudio

Getting started means creating a new agent, which drops you into the MindStudio Editor on an Automations Canvas with a default Start and End block. From there you add blocks — User Input for form fields, Generate Text for model prompts, Display Content for output — wire them into a sequence, and use the Preview button to run it before clicking Publish to deploy. No API key is required for this core loop; you just need a MindStudio account.

Where people get stuck, per reports on MindStudio's own community forum: the official "build your first agent in minutes" tutorial video predates a UI overhaul, so new signups following it land in a noticeably different editor than what's shown on screen. A separate forum thread describes a deployed agent that couldn't summarize outside content and instead replied that it "cannot access external websites," pointing to a block or configuration step for external fetching that isn't obvious from the basic tutorial flow.

Frequently asked questions

What will MindStudio actually cost me a month?

Two numbers added together, and only one of them is published. The subscription is $0 on Free, $20 a month on Individual, or $16 a month billed yearly, with Business on quote — read on mindstudio.ai/pricing on 18 September 2026. Every one of those carries “+ usage”, meaning model calls are billed on top. So the plan sets your floor and your run volume sets your bill, and an agent that loops over long documents can cost more in a week than the subscription does in a year.

Can I stop an agent running up a bill?

Yes, and this is the part worth reading before you judge the pricing. MindStudio's own FAQ says you can set per-agent and total spend limits and are in full control, and that you can track run cost per agent (read 18 September 2026). It also states plainly that it does not mark up model cost — you pay what you would pay bringing your own API keys. That combination is unusual for a usage-priced builder: the meter is uncapped by the plan, but you are handed the cap.

What is a ‘run’, and what happens at 1,000?

A run is one execution of an agent, whether you trigger it yourself or something else does, per MindStudio's FAQ read on 18 September 2026. Free allows 1,000 a month and one agent. Hitting the ceiling is not a hard stop: the page says you are notified as you approach it and can either upgrade or buy additional runs. Individual removes both caps — unlimited agents and unlimited runs — which is what the $20 mainly buys, since the model spend is metered either way.

What should I weigh before buying IT & Productivity software?

Judge it on evidence rather than adjectives. Look for a published security page, single sign-on and role controls on the plan you would actually buy (they are often gated to higher tiers), a status page with real incident history, and a clear answer on where your data is stored. If the tool will hold anything sensitive, those four answers matter more than any feature comparison.
