What Miro does

Visual collaboration platform: an infinite online whiteboard for brainstorms, diagrams, workshops and planning — with templates, voting and AI that generates diagrams and summaries, built for real-time and async teamwork.

What buyers report about Miro

Recurring themes from the buyer-review sites listed at the foot of this page, read in August 2026. We have not run Miro ourselves, so this is other buyers' experience rather than ours. We summarise it here because the complaints are usually the part a vendor page leaves out.

What they praise
  • • The canvas is fast, there are 7,000+ templates, and real-time collaboration simply works — 4.6 on G2 across 12,000+ reviews.

When the paid plan is worth it

Starter is $8 per member a month billed yearly, Business $20, Enterprise custom from 30 members; the yearly toggle is marked save 20%, putting the monthly rates at $10 and $25. The number that actually decides whether you can stay free is easy to miss: the free plan allows 3 editable boards for the WHOLE workspace, not 3 per member, while members themselves are unlimited. That is generous on people and strict on projects -- so the limit binds when you start a fourth thing, not when you add a fourth person.

Teams often misjudge this and assume they are near a seat limit when they are actually near a board limit. Business is two and a half times Starter, which is a large step for a collaboration tool, so identify the specific capability you need from it rather than treating it as “the proper version”. And note the Enterprise floor of 30 seats: below that headcount it is not available at any price, which caps what you can negotiate.

Miro AI credits are published, not quoted on request: 25 per member a month on Starter, 50 on Business, a limited trial on Free. MCP calls are a second meter, 500 a day and 2,000. Those allowances, not the seat price, decide your next upgrade.

How to actually use Miro

Model who genuinely needs to edit before you buy seats, because per-member pricing charges for everyone added to the workspace. Most people in a workshop are reading, reacting and voting rather than building, and an org-wide rollout multiplies at full headcount — so decide the editor list deliberately rather than adding everyone by default. Know where the free plan's wall is before you build a process on it. Three editable boards is workspace-wide rather than per person, so a real team reaches it in days rather than months.

Unlimited members make it a genuine trial; Free's AI is a limited trial: the table prints no number, the FAQ says 10 credits a month. It is not a plan. Read the AI allowance off the plan table rather than asking: 25 credits per member a month on Starter, 50 on Business, with MCP calls metered at 500 and 2,000 a day. Those allowances, not the seat price, force your next upgrade.

Annual billing saves roughly 20% ($8 against $10 on Starter, $20 against $25 on Business), and Enterprise starts at 30 seats.

Frequently asked questions

Do clients I invite to a Miro board need a paid seat?

Not if you are on Business or Enterprise and invite them as guests. Read 23 September 2026, the pricing FAQ says members consume a paid seat, guests have a free Miro account and do not, but can only reach the boards they are invited to, and visitors open a board through a public link without an account. The plan table gives guests only to Business ($20 per member a month billed yearly) and Enterprise. On Starter you can still share boards with visitors, but on Free there are no guest or visitor roles at all and anyone you invite becomes a member. So for an agency or consultant working with outside clients, the real comparison is Business at $20 against Starter plus clients on view-only links.

Do unused Miro AI credits roll over?

No. Read 23 September 2026, the FAQ says credits are pooled across your organisation, consumed per AI action with usage varying by feature, and reset monthly without rolling over. The allowance is 25 per member a month on Starter and 50 on Business; Free shows a limited trial in the table and 10 credits a month in the FAQ; Enterprise starts from 2,500 a month, scaling with seats. Because the pool is shared, a handful of heavy AI users can drain it for everyone, and the FAQ says additional credit packages can be bought when you run out, without printing their price. Budget on the pool, not per person.

Is Miro's free plan really limited to three boards?

To three editable boards. Read 23 September 2026, the Free card says you can create and share unlimited boards, but only the three most recent stay editable. That limit is for the whole workspace, while members are unlimited, so a team hits it on its fourth project rather than its fourth person. Free also has no guest or visitor roles and makes every board visible to all team members by default. Starter at $8 per member a month billed yearly removes the board cap and adds private boards; Business at $20 has a 14-day trial if you want to test guests and AI workflows first.

What should I weigh before buying Operations & Workflow software?

Usually it sits on top rather than replacing everything. Tools in this class tend to become the layer where work is tracked and handed off, while your existing systems stay the source of truth for their own data. Before you buy, map which tools it would replace outright and which it would merely sync with. If the answer is that it syncs with all of them, you are adding a layer rather than removing one.
