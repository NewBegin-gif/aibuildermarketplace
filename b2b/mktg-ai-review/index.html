What buyers report about Mktg.ai

Recurring themes from the buyer-review sites listed at the foot of this page, read in August 2026. We have not run Mktg.ai ourselves, so this is other buyers' experience rather than ours. We summarise it here because the complaints are usually the part a vendor page leaves out.

What they praise

What they complain about
  • • Those performance figures are the vendor's own. We found no independent verification and no user reviews on G2, Capterra or Trustpilot.
  • • Pricing is quote-only, scaled to team size and creative volume. Nothing is published.
  • • It competes with Bynder, Tenovos and Tableau — bigger names with real review histories. Weigh the absence of an independent track record accordingly.

What actually drives the quote

There is still no published rate — mktg.ai/pricing returned 404 on 6 September 2026 and the navigation carries no pricing item — but the tier structure is no longer hidden, and that is new. The FAQ names two plans above the entry level, Growth and Enterprise, and says what each buys: dedicated onboarding on both, plus a dedicated customer success manager, SLA-backed response times and quarterly business reviews. Email support comes with every plan.

Custom integrations are Enterprise-only, and so is EU data residency — US data centres are the default, which for a European buyer is a contract question rather than a preference. Worth noticing before the call: the FAQ's own introduction says it covers what the platform costs, and then no answer in it names a figure. Its own homepage promises you will see what is working before the spend is gone; the one figure it does not publish is its own.

So ask what the licence is counted on — media spend, seats, connected platforms or data volume. A tool priced against your ad spend grows with the budget you hired it to shrink, and that is the one shape you cannot renegotiate later.

How to actually use Mktg.ai

Bring your own media-spend figure to the demo, because every number the vendor publishes is anchored to a $5M budget that is probably not yours. The homepage business-case tile sets out four: $5M assumed annual spend, $150K as a 3% efficiency gain, $70K of labour saved per year based on 1,050 hours, and $100K of reallocated underperforming media spend. Two describe the same budget at different rates — $150K is 3%, $100K is 2% — and the labour figure implies about $67 an hour.

The FAQ adds a third scale: 15–20% of ad spend often reallocated within the first few months. Ask which of those a customer at your spend actually reached. Ask about onboarding in the same breath. The FAQ puts real-time data in week one and full onboarding — connectors, KPI configuration, training — at two to three weeks. Then get the contract shape in writing: term, notice period, renewal, and whether your data sits in the US or the EU, since residency is an Enterprise-only option.
