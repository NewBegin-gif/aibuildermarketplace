Modge Review (2026)$4 a Seat, Screenshots Off by Default

HR & PeopleResearched assessment · by Daniel HaketContent updated 2026-09-26Prices checked 2026-09-03View Modge in the Atlas →

The numbers on Modge's own pages do not agree with each other.

Best for: desk-bound remote or hybrid teams of a few to a few hundred people who want time tracking, app classification, shift adherence, leave and payroll-ready timesheets in one seat priced between $4 and $7 a month, and who are happy to run screenshots as an opt-in rather than a default.
Who should skip it: if you need SSO or a SOC 2 report today, both are marked soon; your people work in the field, since the agent is desktop-only with no mobile or GPS on the page; or your security team will not accept a compliance claim without a document.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Modge cost?

Per seat, per month: Basic $4 on annual billing or $5 monthly, Pro $5.50 or $7, Premium $7 or $9, Enterprise on quote. Every plan has a 14-day trial without a card, and two $2-a-seat add-ons cover extra screenshots and six-year data retention (read 3 September 2026).

Does Modge take screenshots of employees?

Only if you turn it on. The pricing page states screenshots are optional, enabled per organisation or per person, only during tracked sessions, with configurable interval and blur. Default capture is up to 18 an hour; the More Screenshots add-on raises that to 60. Retention is 3, 12 or 24 months by tier.

Is Modge a keylogger?

The comparison table says keyboard and mouse activity is recorded as aggregated intensity counts only, never what was typed, and calls that out explicitly as not a keylogger. App and URL tracking does record the exact URL open in the browser during a tracked session.

Does Modge work on mobile or track GPS?

Not according to its pricing page. The agent is described as a desktop app for macOS and Windows; mobile tracking and GPS are not mentioned, so hourly field teams should look at Hubstaff or a time-clock product instead.

What should I weigh before buying HR & People software?

Compliance is country by country, so the honest answer is: only where the vendor says it is, and only for the entity type you actually have. Ask which countries are covered by its own entities versus a local partner, who carries the liability if something is filed wrong, and how changes in local law reach you. Get those answers in the contract rather than the sales deck.
