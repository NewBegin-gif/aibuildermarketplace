Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 25 Sep 2026

Quote-only since September 2026. Re-read 25 September 2026, momence.com/pricing (now www.momence.com/pricing/, with the footer reading 'Xplor Momence') no longer shows plan cards: it carries three customer quotes and a 'Let's talk pricing' form asking first and last name, business name, business website and email, with no amount, commission rate or processing fee anywhere on the page. The last ladder Momence published itself, read 6 September 2026: Basic $0/mo with a 5% Momence fee on every transaction plus 4% passed to the client; Pro $60/mo with the fee halved to 2.5%; Custom $199/mo with no Momence fee, unlimited add-ons and dedicated support.

Basic and Pro then carried an identical exclusion list — no add-ons, no phone number, no inbox, no retail products, no reporting or insights, no staff members, no locations. Card processing was published separately per country: US 3.9% + $0.30 online, 3.7% + $0.05 in person, 1.8% ACH; UK 2.5% + 20p, 2.4% + 10p, 2% + 20p BACS; Australia 2.75% + A$0.30, 2.75% + A$0.10, 2% + A$0.30 BECS; Canada 3.9% + C$0.30, 3.7% + C$0.05, 2% + C$0.40.

Treat those figures as the yardstick to hold a quote against, not as a current offer. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

The honest knock

The last public ladder (6 September) made the 'free' plan the most expensive one at real volume — 5% of every sale overtook Pro's $60 flat fee around $29k a year, and Pro lost to the $199 Custom plan above about $67k — while Pro added no features over Basic. Ask whether your quote is a flat fee or a percentage before you ask anything else.

What Momence does

Booking and business management for class-based businesses — yoga and fitness studios, gyms, salons and schools: scheduling, memberships, payments, marketing automations and a member app in one place.

What buyers report about Momence

What they praise

What they complain about

How to actually use Momence

Get the fee structure in writing before you move a single class. Since September 2026 Momence prices every business by quote: the pricing page is a form (name, business, website, email) with no amounts on it, and 'Start free trial' is the other way in. Bring your processed volume to that conversation, because the last published ladder (6 September 2026) charged by it: 5% of every transaction on the free Basic plan, 2.5% on Pro at $60 a month, none on Custom at $199.

Basic overtook Pro at about $2,400 a month and Pro overtook Custom at about $5,560 — use those crossovers to test whatever flat fee or percentage you are quoted. Then ask which features the quote includes. On the old ladder add-ons, a phone number, an inbox, retail, reporting, staff members and multiple locations arrived only at Custom. Frequently asked questions

Is there still a free Momence plan?

Not one you can see. On 6 September 2026 the pricing page listed Basic at $0 a month, paid for by a 5% fee on every transaction plus 4% passed to the client. Re-read on 25 September 2026, the page shows no plans at all, only a 'Let's talk pricing' form, and 'Start free trial' is the only no-cost entry the site names. Ask in the call whether a commission-only plan still exists and at what rate; do not assume the old 5% still applies, in either direction.

How do I know whether a Momence quote is fair?

Hold it against the last ladder Momence published itself, on 6 September 2026: Basic $0 plus 5% of every transaction, Pro $60 a month plus 2.5%, Custom $199 with no Momence fee. At $10,000 a month of processed revenue that ladder cost $500 on Basic, $310 on Pro and $199 on Custom, before card processing. A quote above those figures for your volume is more than Momence used to charge in public, and worth asking about.

Are card processing fees included in the price?

They were not on the last published ladder, and the current page says nothing either way. On 6 September 2026 Momence listed processing separately per country: in the US 3.9% + $0.30 online, 3.7% + $0.05 in person and 1.8% for ACH; in the UK 2.5% + 20p online; in Australia 2.75% + A$0.30; in Canada 3.9% + C$0.30. On 25 September 2026 no fee table is on the pricing page, so ask for your own country's rates as part of the quote.

What should I weigh before buying Operations & Workflow software?

Usually it sits on top rather than replacing everything. Tools in this class tend to become the layer where work is tracked and handed off, while your existing systems stay the source of truth for their own data. Before you buy, map which tools it would replace outright and which it would merely sync with. If the answer is that it syncs with all of them, you are adding a layer rather than removing one.
