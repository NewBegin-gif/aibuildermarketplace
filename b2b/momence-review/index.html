Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 6 Sep 2026

The tiers render in plain text again: Basic $0/mo with a 5% Momence fee on every transaction plus 4% passed to the client; Pro $60/mo with your fee halved to 2.5%; Custom $199/mo with no Momence fee, unlimited add-ons and dedicated support. Basic and Pro carry an identical exclusion list in their card tooltips — no add-ons, no phone number, no inbox, no retail products, no reporting or insights, no staff members, no locations — so Pro buys a lower commission and nothing else.

Card processing is charged separately and published per country: US 3.9% + $0.30 online, 3.7% + $0.05 in person, 1.8% ACH; UK 2.5% + 20p, 2.4% + 10p, 2% + 20p BACS; Australia 2.75% + A$0.30, 2.75% + A$0.10, 2% + A$0.30 BECS; Canada 3.9% + C$0.30, 3.7% + C$0.05, 2% + C$0.40. One change since our August readings: on 15 and 19 August 2026 these cards were rendered and then covered by a lead form and a blur filter, so the figures existed in the page but were unreadable until you handed over your details. That gate is gone. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

When the paid plan is worth it

The free plan is the most expensive one, and there is a precise point where that flips. Basic is $0 a month but takes 5% of every transaction from you and passes another 4% to your client. Pro is $60 with your share halved to 2.5%. Custom is $199 with no Momence fee at all. Card processing sits on top of all three. Set them against each other and the crossovers are exact.

Basic and Pro cost the same at $2,400 of monthly processed revenue — about $28,800 a year — and above that Basic is simply worse: at $10,000 a month Basic costs $500 against Pro's $310. Pro and Custom meet at $5,560 a month; at $10,000 of volume Custom costs $111 less than Pro and $301 less than Basic. What $60 does not buy is the thing most people expect it to.

The tooltip on the Pro card carries the same exclusion list as the free one: no add-ons, no phone number, no inbox, no retail products, no reporting or insights, no staff members, no locations. Pro halves a commission and adds no features whatsoever. Every feature gate on this page opens at Custom.

How to actually use Momence

Pick your tier by processed volume, not by sticker, because the free plan is the most expensive one at any real volume. Basic's 5% on every transaction overtakes Pro's $60 flat fee plus 2.5% at around $2,400 a month in processed revenue — roughly $29,000 a year — and Pro in turn overtakes Custom's $199 flat fee at about $5,560 a month, or $67,000 a year. Then check whether you need any feature beyond booking and payments, because that decides the tier as hard as volume does.

Add-ons, a phone number, an inbox, retail products, reporting, staff members and multiple locations are absent from Basic and from Pro alike, and arrive only at Custom. Add card processing last, and read your own country's row rather than the American one. Online card payments cost 3.9% plus $0.30 in the US, 2.5% plus 20p in the UK, 2.75% plus A$0.30 in Australia and 3.9% plus C$0.30 in Canada. On $10,000 of online volume the gap between the US and UK rates alone is about $140 — more than twice the Pro subscription.

Read as plain text: unlike our 15 and 19 August 2026 readings, the cards are no longer covered by a lead form and a blur filter. momence.com/llms.txt returns 404.

The natural comparison is Mindbody or Acuity Scheduling — Mindbody wins on marketplace reach for fitness, Acuity on cheap simple scheduling; Momence wins on modern all-in-one value for class businesses.

What should I weigh before buying Operations & Workflow software?

Usually it sits on top rather than replacing everything.
