What Moosend does

Email marketing and automation platform — drag-and-drop campaigns, automation workflows, landing pages and subscription forms, priced by contact-list size with a 30-day free trial and pay-as-you-go email credits as an alternative.

Pricing (2026)

(2026, re-verified 26 Jul 2026) 30-day free trial, no credit card. The Pro email-marketing plan is priced by contact-list size: at 500 contacts it is $9/month billed monthly or $7/month billed annually (save 20%; biannual saves 15%), and the price climbs as you move up the contact slider (which runs to 1,000,000). Instead of a subscription you can buy email credits with no time limit: 350,000 credits for $350, 500,000 for $500, 750,000 for $750, 1,000,000 for $1,000. Moosend+ (Pro plus the add-ons you pick) and Enterprise are quote-based. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

The friendly $7-9 headline is the entry rate at the smallest list size (500 contacts) — Moosend's pricing scales with your contact count, so as your list grows the monthly bill climbs with it, and the lowest rate assumes you pay a year up front. Read the comparison table before you assume Pro covers you, too: transactional emails, SSO and SAML, a dedicated IP, hosted files and an account manager are all marked as Moosend+ add-ons rather than included.

What buyers report about Moosend

Recurring themes from G2, Capterra and Trustpilot, read in August 2026. We have not run Moosend ourselves, so this is other buyers' experience rather than ours. We summarise it here because the complaints are usually the part a vendor page leaves out.

What they praise
  • • Price against feature set is the reason people choose it — 50-70% below Mailchimp, from $9/month, and the automation builder handles cart abandonment and behaviour flows properly. 4.7 on G2 across 717+ reviews.
  • • Live chat in under five minutes is reported by name.
What they complain about
  • • Auto-renewal without notice and refused refunds are what the one-star reviews describe.
  • • Constant Contact acquired Moosend in June 2025, which leaves genuine uncertainty about the roadmap. Worth weighing if you are committing to it long-term.
  • • Above about 50 users the custom pricing turns opaque and the app ecosystem runs thin.

Where the ratings disagree. 4.7 on G2 against 3.4 on Trustpilot across roughly 79 reviews. Small Trustpilot sample, and the theme in it is renewal rather than the product.

Best for: small businesses and marketers who want affordable email marketing and automation with a low entry price, a real free trial and a clean drag-and-drop builder.
Who should skip it: if you run a very large list where per-subscriber pricing adds up, or you only need transactional/API email — a developer-focused sender is cheaper for that.

The natural comparison is Mailchimp — Mailchimp is the recognized incumbent that gets pricey as your list grows; Moosend undercuts it on entry price with comparable automation — just watch how the per-subscriber tiers climb as you scale. Decide by which one fits the job above, not by the louder brand.
