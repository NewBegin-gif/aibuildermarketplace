MRPeasy Review (2026)

Operations & WorkflowResearched assessment · by Daniel HaketUpdated 2026-07-14

The best operations tools remove repetitive work quietly — the win is fewer dropped balls and less manual oversight, not more dashboards. So where does MRPeasy actually fit?

The honest bottom line. Worth it for small manufacturers. Skip it once you're a large or highly complex manufacturer.

What MRPeasy does

Cloud manufacturing ERP for small manufacturers. Plan production, track inventory and manage orders and procurement so a growing workshop runs without spreadsheets.

Pricing (2026)

From about $49/user/mo across four tiers; the first 10 users pay the tier rate, then each extra block of 10 users is a flat ~$79/mo — all plans include unlimited orders and items (2026). Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

Best for: small manufacturers, wholesalers and distributors who want dependable production planning and inventory control without a heavy ERP or a dedicated IT team.
Who should skip it: if you're a large or highly complex manufacturer, or you need deep customisation, advanced analytics and forecasting — MRPeasy is built for simplicity, not complexity.

The honest knock

Paying ~$50 per user just so a shop-floor worker can clock in and out feels steep when they'll touch a fraction of the features. Customisation and reporting are limited, and there's no advanced demand forecasting or AI — so larger or highly complex manufacturers will outgrow it.

The natural comparison is Katana — the other small-manufacturer MRP. Decide by which one fits the job above, not by the louder brand.
