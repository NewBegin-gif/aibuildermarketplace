Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 24 Sep 2026

Navan Business is free for companies of up to 300 employees, with no limit on trips, and Navan Expense is free for the first 5 monthly expensing users and then $15 per user per month. An expensing user is anyone who submits a transaction in the month, so the expense bill follows who actually files, not headcount. The travel side is free because it is funded by travel-supplier commissions, which makes the question not what it costs but what it steers; note that corporate negotiated rates sit in the Enterprise column of the plan table, not the Business one.

Frequently asked questions

Is Navan really free?

The travel product is, for companies of up to 300 employees: Navan Business is funded by commissions from travel suppliers rather than by a subscription, and there is no limit on trips. The Expense product is free for the first 5 monthly expensing users and then $15 per user per month (navan.com/pricing, read 24 September 2026). Above 300 employees the plan is Navan Enterprise, priced by quote.

What is the catch with commission-funded travel software?

Not a hidden fee — an incentive. The platform earns when you book through it, so the thing to test is whether your own negotiated hotel and airline rates are loaded and surfaced, and whether the policy engine steers travellers toward inventory that pays Navan. Ask for that in the demo with your own routes.

When does it stop being free?

In two places. On Expense, at the sixth monthly expensing user, where $15 per user per month starts; the count is people who submit a transaction that month, not headcount. On travel, when the company passes 300 employees, where Navan Business gives way to Enterprise pricing by quote. If you only need travel booking and policy below that size, the free plan genuinely covers it.

What should I weigh before buying Financial Operations software?

The bar here is higher than features. Check who actually holds the money and under what licence, whether client funds are segregated, what audit trail and permission controls you get, and how support behaves when something goes wrong mid-transaction. Anything touching payments should be able to answer those in writing; if it cannot, that is your answer.
