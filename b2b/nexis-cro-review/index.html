Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 15 Sep 2026

Free, then Pro $9 a month or $99 a year, Growth $21 or $121, and Agency $44 or $344. All charges are in USD and recur every 30 days, and the paid tiers carry a 2-day free trial — short for judging a tool whose whole promise is a change in how AI engines see you. The meter is AI credits: Free gives 5 a month and a single audit, Pro 100 with unlimited audits and history, Growth 300, Agency 500.

Read Agency's “500 AI credits (unlimited usage)” against that number rather than past it: the credits are the ceiling. The yearly discounts the vendor advertises do hold up when you check them — 8% on Pro, 52% on Growth, 35% on Agency. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

The app launched on 9 June 2026 and its App Store listing still showed a 0.0 rating from zero reviews when we read it on 14 September 2026 — so every claim about what the audit catches, how good the AI rewrites are and whether the citation tracking is accurate rests on the vendor's own description. That matters more than usual here, because AEO tracking is a young field: nobody has yet shown that being cited by ChatGPT or Perplexity moves revenue, and the numbers a tracker reports are hard for a merchant to check independently.

The free tier is also thin — 5 AI credits and one audit a month is enough to see the interface, not to test the product.

What Nexis CRO does

Shopify app that runs a 34-check SEO and AEO audit across products, collections, pages and policies, rewrites the gaps with AI, deploys JSON-LD schema and an llms.txt file, and tracks whether ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity cite your store.

What buyers report about Nexis CRO

Nothing, and we would rather say so than fill the space. We looked for independent user reviews of Nexis CRO on the buyer-review sites listed at the foot of this page, read in September 2026, and found none at all. That points to a young product rather than a badly reviewed one, but it does mean there is no crowd verdict to weigh: judge Nexis CRO on your own trial, not on anyone's score.

When the paid plan is worth it

The step that matters is Pro to Growth, and only if you pay yearly. Pro yearly is $99 and Growth yearly is $121: $22 more for the whole year buys three times the AI credits (300 against 100), twenty tracked prompts instead of five, three AI models instead of one, plus citation-gap alerts and competitor benchmarking. That is the cheapest real upgrade on the page. The same step month to month is $9 against $21, which is a different proposition entirely — and Growth billed monthly costs $252 a year, more than double the $121 yearly rate for the identical plan.

So the honest advice is to decide the tier on the yearly column and ignore the monthly one unless you genuinely intend to cancel within a few months. Agency at $44 (or $344 a year) is a different product: it manages up to five child stores. If you run one shop, there is nothing above Growth to buy.

How to actually use Nexis CRO

Run the free audit before you pay anything: it scores the whole store across 34 checks and hands you a prioritised fix list, which is enough to judge whether it is finding real problems or generic ones. Fix a handful by hand first and see whether the score moves for the right reason — the AI Quick Fix will rewrite meta tags and thin content in one click, and a tool that rewrites at scale is exactly the kind you want to watch closely at small scale first.

Note the audit cap: one a month on Free, so plan that single run for after you have made changes, not before. If you go on to track citations, write down where you stand on your key prompts on day one, because the tracker only tells you something once it has a baseline to compare against. And treat the 2-day trial on paid plans as a tour of the interface rather than a test of results.
