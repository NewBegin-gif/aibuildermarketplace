What Nexis CRO does

Shopify app that runs a 34-check SEO and AEO audit across products, collections, pages and policies, rewrites the gaps with AI, deploys JSON-LD schema and an llms.txt file, and tracks whether ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity cite your store. Free plan; paid $9 to $44 a month.

When the paid plan is worth it

The step that matters is Pro to Growth, and only if you pay yearly. Pro yearly is $99 and Growth yearly is $121: $22 more for the whole year buys three times the AI credits (300 against 100), twenty tracked prompts instead of five, three AI models instead of one, plus citation-gap alerts and competitor benchmarking. That is the cheapest real upgrade on the page. The same step month to month is $9 against $21, which is a different proposition entirely — and Growth billed monthly costs $252 a year, more than double the $121 yearly rate for the identical plan.

So the honest advice is to decide the tier on the yearly column and ignore the monthly one unless you genuinely intend to cancel within a few months. Agency at $44 (or $344 a year) is a different product: it manages up to five child stores. If you run one shop, there is nothing above Growth to buy.

How to actually use Nexis CRO

Run the free audit before you pay anything: it scores the whole store across 34 checks and hands you a prioritised fix list, which is enough to judge whether it is finding real problems or generic ones. Fix a handful by hand first and see whether the score moves for the right reason — the AI Quick Fix will rewrite meta tags and thin content in one click, and a tool that rewrites at scale is exactly the kind you want to watch closely at small scale first.

Note the audit cap: one a month on Free, so plan that single run for after you have made changes, not before. If you go on to track citations, write down where you stand on your key prompts on day one, because the tracker only tells you something once it has a baseline to compare against. And treat the 2-day trial on paid plans as a tour of the interface rather than a test of results.
