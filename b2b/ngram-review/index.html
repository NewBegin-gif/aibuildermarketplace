Ngram Review (2026)

IT & ProductivityResearched assessment · by Daniel HaketUpdated 2026-07-05

The IT and productivity layer is where small, reliable tools quietly give you hours back — or, done wrong, add another login to babysit. So where does Ngram actually fit?

What Ngram does

AI video creation for product and marketing teams. Turn docs, screenshots and recordings into polished, on-brand videos — scripted, storyboarded and produced with AI on a credit-based model.

Pricing (2026)

(2026) Credit-based: every export consumes ~50 credits and AI video generation uses ~10 credits per second, across Free, Basic, Plus and Pro tiers; top-up credits scale by plan (roughly $40 per 1,000 on Free down to $10 per 1,000 on Pro). Annual billing saves ~20%; enterprise is custom. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

Best for: product and marketing teams that want to turn docs, screenshots and recordings into polished, on-brand videos quickly and repeatably.
Who should skip it: if you need bespoke, high-end studio production, or your video volume would make the per-export credit model expensive versus a flat-fee tool.

The honest knock

The credit model is the honest catch — because every export costs credits and video eats ~10 per second, anyone producing a lot of clips tops up beyond their plan, so the real monthly cost tracks your output, not the sticker. And AI video is fast but templated by default: turning docs and screenshots into a decent clip is genuinely useful, but it needs your brand direction to not look generic, and it won't replace bespoke studio production. For quick, repeatable product and marketing videos, it removes real work.

The natural comparison is Synthesia or Pictory — close AI-video tools — Ngram leans product/marketing clips from your own assets; compare credit economics against your real export volume. Decide by which one fits the job above, not by the louder brand.

My ex-banker filter is simple: does Ngram remove a real cost — time, errors, missed revenue — bigger than what it charges? If the job above is genuinely yours, it's worth a look. We never publish fake or “exclusive” prices, so always confirm the current plan on their site.
