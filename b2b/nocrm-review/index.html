noCRM Review (2026)The Team Features Are All on Dream

Sales & CRMResearched assessment · by Daniel HaketUpdated 2026-09-22

The pitch is that salespeople hate CRMs, and the answer is a tool built around the next action rather than the form.

Best for: solo sellers, small outbound teams and telesales operations that want a list of leads each carrying a next step, rather than a CRM to configure — and European buyers who care that it is built and hosted in the EU, ISO 27001 certified and billed in euros.
Who should skip it: if you want email to live inside the tool without paying for the top tier, because Starter and Expert are BCC-only; you need WhatsApp, VoIP dialling or call recording, all of which are Dream-only; you are running a team and expected roles, permissions and performance reports below €33 a user a month; or you wanted a free plan rather than a 15-day trial.

Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 16 Sep 2026

Three per-seat tiers and no free plan. That makes the annual saving 42% on Starter, 24% on Expert and 15% on Dream, so the "save up to 40%" badge is carried entirely by the cheapest tier. Starter is capped at 500 leads in a single pipeline with at most four custom fields, and it is sold to new customers only.

Frequently asked questions

What does noCRM cost?

There are three tiers, all priced per seat, and no free plan behind them. On annual billing that is €11, €22 and €33 per user a month for Starter, Expert and Dream; paying month to month costs €19, €29 and €39 instead. The tier prices are only half the answer, because two rules decide the real bill. Everyone on one account has to sit on the same plan, so a single colleague who needs two-way email sync moves the whole team to €33. And Starter is capped at 500 leads in one pipeline and sold to new customers only, which makes it a way in rather than somewhere to retreat to when budgets tighten.

Which tier do I need if email has to live inside the CRM?

Dream, at €33 a user a month on annual billing. Email synchronisation is BCC only on both Starter and Expert, so on those tiers you copy noCRM on outgoing mail and read the replies elsewhere; two-way sync, templates, shared inboxes and email performance tracking all start at Dream. The same tier carries WhatsApp, the VoIP integrations, internal automations and the whole team layer. And because all users on one account must sit on the same plan, one person needing any of that puts everybody on €33.

Is there a free plan, and what happens when the trial ends?

There is no free plan. Every tier comes with a 15-day free trial that needs no credit card, and adding payment details extends it to 30 days. When it runs out the account is paused rather than downgraded or deleted, so nothing is lost by taking the shorter trial first. Note one restriction before you plan a retreat path: Starter is sold to new customers only, so it is a starting point rather than a tier you can fall back to later.

What should I weigh before buying Sales & CRM software?

Most tools in this category plug in rather than replace. Check which direction the data flows and whether the sync is genuinely two-way, because a one-way push creates a second source of truth that your reps will quietly stop trusting. If it does aim to replace your CRM, price the migration honestly: history, custom fields and reporting rarely move across cleanly.
