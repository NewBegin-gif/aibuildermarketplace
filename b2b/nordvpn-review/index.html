What NordVPN is

VPN with audited no-logs, fast NordLynx protocol and threat protection.

  • Encrypted tunnel — secures your connection on public and untrusted networks.
  • NordLynx protocol — a WireGuard-based protocol built for speed.
  • Threat Protection — blocks trackers, malicious sites and ads at the network level.
  • Large network & extras — thousands of servers across 100+ countries, kill switch, double VPN, plus password manager and encrypted storage in higher tiers.

What makes it stand out

Speed and trust. NordLynx consistently benchmarks among the fastest protocols, so the VPN doesn't feel like a tax on your connection — the main reason people quit VPNs. And its no-logs policy has been independently audited more than once, which matters more than any marketing claim for a privacy product.

Who it's for

For remote workers, frequent travellers and anyone regularly on public Wi-Fi, NordVPN is an easy recommendation. It's also useful for accessing your normal services while abroad. If you never leave a trusted home network and don't care about tracker-blocking, you may not need one — be honest with yourself about your actual usage.

Why not just use a free VPN

The tempting alternative is a free VPN, and that's exactly where I'd push back. Free providers have to make money somewhere — often from your bandwidth or data — and they rarely offer independently audited no-logs or speeds you'll actually tolerate. For something whose entire job is privacy and trust, paying a modest amount for an audited, fast provider is the honest trade. And if you genuinely never leave a trusted network, the right answer might be no VPN at all — be honest about your usage.

How hard is it to switch?

Trivial. Install the app, sign in, and you're protected — there's nothing to migrate and no data to move. The only real decisions are plan length (longer plans are far better value) and remembering to install it on every device you actually travel with.

My verdict

My default VPN recommendation. It's fast enough that you'll leave it on, audited enough to trust, and the Threat Protection earns its place. Grab a longer plan for the real value, install it on every device, and treat it as standard hygiene rather than a gadget.

Frequently asked questions

Is NordVPN trustworthy?

Its no-logs policy has been independently audited multiple times, which is the strongest assurance a VPN can offer. Combined with strong encryption and a long track record, it's among the more trustworthy options.

Will a VPN slow my connection?

Every VPN adds some overhead, but NordVPN's NordLynx protocol keeps the penalty small in real use — most people won't notice it for browsing, calls or streaming.

What does NordVPN cost?

It's subscription-based, with the best value on longer plans and higher tiers that add tools like a password manager and encrypted storage. Check current pricing on their site — the partner link applies the live offer.

Do I really need a VPN?

If you use public Wi-Fi, travel, or want to limit tracking, yes. On a trusted home network with no privacy concerns, the case is weaker. It protects your connection — it doesn't replace strong passwords and 2FA.
