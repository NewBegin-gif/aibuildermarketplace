Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 2 Oct 2026

(consumer plans re-verified 2026-10-02 in the browser; the page is geo-priced and served us euros from a Dutch address) Consumer two-year plans: Basic €3.49/mo, Complete €4.49/mo and Ultimate Max €8.49/mo, each with three extra months. They are charged as €94.23, €121.23 and €229.23 for the first 27 months, with a 30-day money-back guarantee. Read the renewal line before you buy: those same plans renew at €139.08, €219.48 and €338.28 a year, so Basic goes from about €3.49 a month to about €11.59 — roughly a threefold step.

The business product, NordLayer, is quoted in USD worldwide — read from a European address the page still prints dollars and states priceCurrency USD — and it publishes both billing cycles. Per user per month: Lite $8 on annual billing or $10 month-to-month, Core $11 or $14, Premium $14 or $18, which the toggle labels as savings of 20%, 21% and 22%.

There is a five-user minimum and a 14-day money-back guarantee, and Core and Premium require a dedicated-IP server on top: +$40 a month on annual billing, +$50 month-to-month (both toggles re-read on 3 October 2026). An enterprise offer starts at $6 per user per month on annual billing, or $10 month-to-month, from 200 seats and with Lite capabilities. Two endpoint add-ons sit beside the ladder: CrowdStrike Falcon Go at $2 per device a month and CrowdStrike Falcon Enterprise at $9.

Two things changed between our readings and neither is a price. The top tier was called Ultimate Pro on 17 August 2026 and is called Ultimate Max on 14 September 2026, with the same €8.49 introductory rate and the same €338.28 renewal; it is also the only consumer tier that includes a dedicated IP and the cyber insurance, which pays out up to €5,000 per covered claim.

The NordLayer ladder was re-read on 14 September 2026 on nordlayer.com/pricing and the three seat rates are unchanged from 17 August; what is new here is the month-to-month column and the two CrowdStrike add-ons, taken from the plan data the page serves itself. The consumer page was read again on 2 October 2026 with every intro rate, 27-month charge and renewal unchanged; nordlayer.com/pricing served us a bot check that day, so the NordLayer figures stand as read on 14 September 2026. When the paid plan is worth it

For the consumer product the tier difference is small and the term length is the real decision. Basic, Complete and Ultimate Max were €3.49, €4.49 and €8.49 a month on the two-year term when read from a Dutch address, charged as €94.23, €121.23 and €229.23 for the first 27 months. What upgrading does not change is the renewal: those same plans then bill €139.08, €219.48 and €338.28 a year, so Basic moves from roughly €3.49 to about €11.59 a month whichever tier you pick.

Choose the tier on what the extras are worth to you, then set a calendar reminder for the renewal date, because that is the number that decides the second year. NordLayer, Nord's separate business product, runs $8 to $14 per user a month across Lite, Core and Premium on annual billing, or $10 to $18 month-to-month, with a five-user minimum. The upgrade there carries a compulsory cost: the dedicated-IP server ($40 a month on annual billing, $50 month-to-month) is required on Core and Premium rather than optional. Enterprise starts at $6 a seat but needs 200+ seats.

How to actually use NordVPN

For the consumer app the route is short. Download the app for the platform in use, open it and sign in with an existing Nord Account rather than creating one inside the app. Then press Secure my connection to let it choose a server, or tap a country on the map for a specific exit. Linux users skip the interface and run nordvpn connect. The vendor sells 2-year, 1-year and 1-month terms, and the refund window is a 30-day money-back guarantee tied to the first purchase only, so evaluation must finish before the term renews.

Teams set up NordLayer instead, a separate product, and that is where people stall. The vendor's guide claims an admin can get the platform running in under 10 minutes, but seats are invited under Organization > Members and a licence only becomes active once the invitation is accepted. Teams must exist before a gateway or dedicated IP can be assigned to them, and reducing licence counts is not self-service: the documentation routes downscaling through an account manager.

Frequently asked questions

What does NordVPN cost per month, really?

The advertised figure and the figure you will pay are different, and the gap is on the page if you read the small print. On 17 August 2026, from a Dutch address, the two-year Basic plan was €3.49 a month — €94.23 for the first 27 months — and renews at €139.08 a year, which is about €11.59 a month. Complete runs €4.49 then €219.48 a year; Ultimate Max — renamed from Ultimate Pro since our August reading — €8.49 then €338.28. Every one of those figures was still on the page on 14 September and on 2 October 2026. Set a reminder before the renewal date on the day you sign up.

What if I need a VPN for a team rather than for myself?

Then this is not the product to buy. The NordVPN plans reviewed here are consumer plans; Nord sells teams a separate product, NordLayer, with its own pricing. Its seats are $8, $11 and $14 per user per month for Lite, Core and Premium on annual billing, with a five-user minimum, and on Core and Premium a dedicated-IP server at +$40 a month is required rather than optional, as their pricing page stated when we read it on 17 August 2026. A five-person team on Core is therefore $55 plus $40, not $55.

Is the 30-day money-back guarantee good for the renewal too?

No. It covers your first term. Once a renewal charge goes through it is outside the guarantee, which is exactly the situation behind most of the billing complaints on review platforms. The guarantee itself is reported to work when you use it in time, via live chat.

What should I weigh before buying IT & Productivity software?

Judge it on evidence rather than adjectives. Look for a published security page, single sign-on and role controls on the plan you would actually buy (they are often gated to higher tiers), a status page with real incident history, and a clear answer on where your data is stored. If the tool will hold anything sensitive, those four answers matter more than any feature comparison.
