What NordVPN does

Secure VPN with threat protection for business & remote teams

Pricing (2026)

Consumer plans from about $3-4/mo on long terms; the business product (NordLayer) runs roughly $8-11 per user/mo with a 5-user minimum, plus about $40/mo for a dedicated server and fixed IP (2026). Renewals rise from intro rates. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

For a business you're really buying NordLayer, priced per user with a seat minimum. It's solid and easy, but a VPN is one layer of security, not a whole strategy — don't mistake it for endpoint protection.

What buyers report about NordVPN

Recurring themes from Trustpilot, ComplaintsBoard, TechRadar and court filings, read in August 2026. We have not run NordVPN ourselves, so this is other buyers' experience rather than ours. We summarise it here because the complaints are usually the part a vendor page leaves out.

What they praise
  • • Speed, ease of use and support quality are the three things positive reviewers name, and independent testers back the speed. 4.1 on Trustpilot.
  • • A 30-day money-back guarantee that reviewers report actually working, via live chat.
What they complain about
  • • Auto-renewal is the subject of active litigation, not just complaints. A class action was filed on 10 April 2026 in Virginia alleging the cancellation flow is deliberately difficult; Nord Security faced at least four similar complaints in 2024-2025 and at least two more class actions in 2026.
  • • Once the renewal date passes, those charges are non-refundable. The 30-day guarantee covers your first term only.
  • • Renewal pricing is substantially higher than the introductory rate, and the plan structure has grown more complicated.
  • • Set a calendar reminder before the renewal date on the day you sign up. That single action avoids almost every complaint here.

Frequently asked questions

Is NordVPN secure and reliable enough to depend on?

Judge it on evidence rather than adjectives. Look for a published security page, single sign-on and role controls on the plan you would actually buy (they are often gated to higher tiers), a status page with real incident history, and a clear answer on where your data is stored. If the tool will hold anything sensitive, those four answers matter more than any feature comparison.

Who should not use NordVPN?

Skip it if you're a solo user chasing the cheapest personal VPN — the consumer plan, not the business tier, is what you want. Buying a tool to fix a problem you don't have yet just adds cost and another login to manage.
