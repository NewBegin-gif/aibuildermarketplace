Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 2 Sep 2026

Four tiers: Lite free, Kickstart $9.90/mo or $95/yr, Starter $19.90/mo or $191/yr, Standard $39.90/mo or $383/yr, with annual marked 20% off, plus an Enterprise tier arranged by meeting. Each paid tier runs a discounted first month ($3.96, $7.96 and $15.96). Pre-orders above the plan limit are $0.20 each and $0.15 on Standard; SMS and WhatsApp are priced per country. Kickstart is restricted to Basic Shopify stores in their first year. 7-day trial and a 30-day money-back guarantee. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

When the paid plan is worth it

The ladder has four rungs, not two: free Lite, Kickstart $9.90 a month, Starter $19.90 and Standard $39.90, or $95, $191 and $383 prepaid for a 20% saving, with a 7-day trial and a 30-day money-back guarantee. What decides whether any tier is worth it is a number from your own store, not theirs: how much revenue you lose to stock-outs. This app does one job — turning an out-of-stock moment into a recovered sale through back-in-stock and pre-order alerts — so its return is entirely a function of how often you actually sell out.

A catalogue that rarely goes out of stock has nothing for it to recover, and at $9.90 a month that is $95 prepaid a year for alerts nobody triggers. So run the estimate before the trial: count the SKUs that went out of stock in the last quarter, multiply by their average order value and by a conservative recovery rate. If that figure does not clear about $120 a year you are on Lite or on nothing; the tiers above earn their keep only once alert volume is the constraint, at 500 on Kickstart against 1,500 on Standard.

Three costs sit off the headline: pre-orders above the plan limit at $0.20 each ($0.15 on Standard), SMS and WhatsApp priced by country, and another script on your storefront. Weigh all of it against the revenue you are actually leaking rather than against the sticker. The 30-day money-back guarantee makes that measurement cheap to run for real.

How to actually use Notify Me

Measure the leak before you install anything, because this app's value is entirely a function of how often you sell out. Pull the last three months and count the times a product with real demand hit zero, and for how long. Multiply by your average order value and by a realistic recovery rate. If your catalogue rarely goes out of stock, that number is small and no tier changes it.

Then start on Lite and watch notification volume, since that is what the tiers scale on. Kickstart at $9.90 is limited to Basic Shopify stores in their first year, Starter $19.90, Standard $39.90. Your alert count decides which, and pre-orders past the limit bill on top. Check your page speed before and after. This is another script on your storefront as well as another monthly line, and both belong in the comparison against the revenue you are actually leaking to stock-outs.

There is a 7-day trial and a 30-day money-back guarantee, which together are long enough to see a real restock cycle.
