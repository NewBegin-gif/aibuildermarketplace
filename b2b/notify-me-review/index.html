What Notify Me does

Shopify app to recover lost sales on out-of-stock products: back-in-stock notifications, pre-orders and wishlists that keep customers buying instead of bouncing.

Pricing (2026)

(2026) A free plan to start, then Kickstart around $9.99/mo for new stores and a Standard tier around $39.90/mo, scaling by notification volume; 7-day trial and a 30-day money-back guarantee. Verify current tiers in the Shopify App Store. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

The honest knock

It does one job — turning out-of-stock moments into recovered sales via back-in-stock and pre-order alerts — and its value is entirely tied to how often you actually sell out. If your catalog rarely goes out of stock, you won't see much return. As with any Shopify app it's another monthly line item and another script on your storefront, so weigh it against the real revenue you're leaking to stock-outs.

Frequently asked questions

Is Notify Me worth it for a small team?

It depends on whether you have the bottleneck it solves. Small teams get the most out of this category when one clear problem is already costing real hours or revenue; buying ahead of that just adds cost and another login. Price it against the hours or lost deals it removes, not against its feature list, and start on the smallest plan that covers the job.

Who should not use Notify Me?

Skip it if you rarely run out of stock, or your store volume is too low for the recovered sales to outweigh the subscription. Buying a tool to fix a problem you don't have yet just adds cost and another login to manage.
