Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 23 Sep 2026

Four tiers: Lite, a free plan with mostly lifetime allowances, then Kickstart $9.90/mo or $95/yr, Starter $19.90/mo or $191/yr, Standard $39.90/mo or $383/yr, with annual marked 20% off, plus an Enterprise tier arranged by meeting. Each paid tier runs a discounted first month ($3.96, $7.96 and $15.96). Kickstart and Starter carry the same caps (500 alerts, 500 preorders); Standard raises them to 1,500 each, and the listing says unlimited plans are available inside the app.

Pre-orders above the plan limit are $0.20 each and $0.15 on Standard; SMS and WhatsApp are priced per country. Kickstart is restricted to Basic Shopify stores in their first year. Lite's allowances are mostly lifetime, not monthly: 100 back-in-stock requests, 5 pre-orders, 50 wishlist actions and 5 SMS and 5 WhatsApp messages over the life of the store, plus 10 notifications a month. 7-day trial and a 30-day money-back guarantee. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

When the paid plan is worth it

The ladder has four rungs, not two: free Lite, Kickstart $9.90 a month, Starter $19.90 and Standard $39.90, or $95, $191 and $383 prepaid for a 20% saving, with a 7-day trial and a 30-day money-back guarantee. What decides whether any tier is worth it is a number from your own store, not theirs: how much revenue you lose to stock-outs. This app does one job — turning an out-of-stock moment into a recovered sale through back-in-stock and pre-order alerts — so its return is entirely a function of how often you actually sell out.

A catalogue that rarely goes out of stock has nothing for it to recover, and at $9.90 a month that is $95 prepaid a year for alerts nobody triggers. So run the estimate before the trial: count the SKUs that went out of stock in the last quarter, multiply by their average order value and by a conservative recovery rate. If that figure does not clear about $120 a year you are on Lite or on nothing; the tiers above earn their keep only once alert volume is the constraint, at 500 on Kickstart and on Starter alike against 1,500 on Standard, so Starter's extra $10 a month buys branding, reports and page-builder support rather than more alerts.

Three costs sit off the headline: pre-orders above the plan limit at $0.20 each ($0.15 on Standard), SMS and WhatsApp priced by country, and another script on your storefront. Weigh all of it against the revenue you are actually leaking rather than against the sticker. The 30-day money-back guarantee makes that measurement cheap to run for real.

Frequently asked questions

Can I run Notify Me on the free Lite plan for good?

Only for a very small catalogue. Read 23 September 2026, Lite's limits on notify-me.io/pricing are mostly lifetime rather than monthly: 100 back-in-stock requests, 5 pre-orders, 50 wishlist actions and 5 SMS and 5 WhatsApp messages over the life of the store, with 10 notifications a month on top. Once a store has collected 100 sign-up requests in total, Lite is used up however slowly they arrived. It works as a way to see whether shoppers click the button at all; it is not a plan to stay on. The first paid rung, Kickstart at $9.90 a month, lifts that to 500 alerts and 500 pre-orders, but only for Basic Shopify stores in their first year.

What does Starter at $19.90 get me over Kickstart at $9.90?

Not more volume. Read 23 September 2026 on the Shopify App Store listing, both plans carry 500 alerts across email, SMS and push and 500 pre-orders without a transaction fee, and both charge $0.20 per pre-order above the limit. Starter adds brand-customised emails, a pre-order delivery fulfillment trigger, basic reports, low-stock alerts to you and page-builder integration. The practical reason most stores land on Starter is the other way round: Kickstart is only open to Basic Shopify stores in their first year, so an older store or one on a higher Shopify plan starts at $19.90. Volume only rises at Standard, $39.90, with 1,500 alerts and 1,500 pre-orders and a lower $0.15 pre-order overage.

What if I pay and it does not recover any sales?

You can get the money back. Read 23 September 2026, the FAQ on notify-me.io/pricing says Notify Me offers a 7-day trial and a 30-day money-back guarantee on top of refunds for subscriptions, and every paid tier on the App Store listing shows the 7-day free trial and a discounted first month ($3.96 on Kickstart, $7.96 on Starter, $15.96 on Standard). A month is long enough to see one real restock cycle. Count how many notified shoppers actually bought after the alert, and compare that revenue to the plan price before the guarantee runs out. SMS and WhatsApp messages are priced by country on top of the plan, so include them in that sum.

What should I weigh before buying Growth & Revenue software?

It depends on whether you have the bottleneck it solves. Small teams get the most out of this category when one clear problem is already costing real hours or revenue; buying ahead of that just adds cost and another login. Price it against the hours or lost deals it removes, not against its feature list, and start on the smallest plan that covers the job.
