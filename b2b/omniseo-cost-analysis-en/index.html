OmniSEO pricing in 2026 starts at $29/month for the Starter plan, $89/month for Professional, and $299/month for Enterprise, with annual discounts of 20%. Each tier includes keyword tracking, site auditing, and reporting features, but differs significantly in limits and advanced capabilities like competitor analysis and white-label options.

FAQ

Does OmniSEO offer a free trial or free plan?

OmniSEO provides a 14-day free trial with full access to Professional features, tracking up to 500 keywords and 5 competitor domains. No free plan exists, but the trial doesn't require a credit card and includes onboarding support to test all core functionality before committing.

What are the main differences between OmniSEO pricing tiers?

Starter ($29/month) tracks 1,000 keywords and 3 sites, Professional ($89/month) handles 5,000 keywords and 10 sites with competitor analysis, while Enterprise ($299/month) offers 25,000 keywords, unlimited sites, white-label reporting, and dedicated account management with custom integrations.

Are there setup fees or hidden costs with OmniSEO?

OmniSEO charges no setup fees for standard plans. However, keyword overage costs $0.05 per extra keyword monthly, premium support costs $49/month additional, and custom integrations start at $500 one-time. Enterprise plans include most add-ons but may have implementation fees for complex setups.

How much does it cost to scale from 10 to 100 users on OmniSEO?

OmniSEO pricing is per-account, not per-user, so adding team members costs nothing extra. However, scaling typically requires upgrading tiers due to increased keyword and site limits. Most growing agencies move from Professional ($89/month) to Enterprise ($299/month) when managing 50+ client sites.

Can you negotiate OmniSEO enterprise pricing and get discounts?

Yes, OmniSEO offers 15-30% discounts for annual commitments, multi-year contracts, and accounts exceeding $500/month. Enterprise clients can negotiate custom keyword limits, additional integrations, and dedicated support. Volume discounts start at 10% for $1,000+ monthly commitments with flexible payment terms.

What Each Plan Actually Includes

Starter Plan ($29/month) - Perfect for Small Business Owners

The Starter plan targets solo entrepreneurs and small business owners managing their own SEO. You get 1,000 keyword tracking slots, which sounds generous but fills up quickly when tracking long-tail variations. The 3-website limit works for businesses with a main site plus a couple landing pages or microsites.

Core features included:

  • Daily keyword rank tracking for 1,000 terms
  • Basic site audit covering 500 pages per site
  • Standard reporting templates (non-customizable)
  • Email support with 24-48 hour response times
  • 1 competitor tracking slot
  • Google Analytics and Search Console integration
  • Mobile and desktop rank tracking

What's missing: No backlink analysis, limited local SEO features, basic reporting only, no API access, and minimal competitor intelligence beyond basic rank comparisons.

Professional Plan ($89/month) - The Sweet Spot for Agencies

Most SEO agencies and consultants choose Professional because it balances features with cost-effectiveness. The 5,000 keyword limit accommodates 8-15 typical client accounts, while 10 websites covers most small-to-medium agency portfolios.

Everything from Starter, plus:

  • 5,000 keyword tracking capacity
  • 10 website monitoring
  • 5 competitor analysis slots
  • Backlink monitoring and analysis
  • Local SEO tracking for Google My Business
  • Basic white-label reports (logo customization)
  • Priority email support (12-24 hour response)
  • API access with 10,000 monthly calls
  • Advanced site auditing for technical SEO

Real-world usage: Agencies typically use 3,500-4,500 keywords monthly, leaving room for growth. The 5 competitor slots let you track main competitors for each client, providing valuable competitive intelligence for strategy development.

Enterprise Plan ($299/month) - Built for Scale

Enterprise serves larger agencies, in-house teams at mid-market companies, and SEO consultancies managing 20+ clients. The 25,000 keyword limit and unlimited websites remove most scaling constraints.

Everything from Professional, plus:

Hidden Costs Every Founder Should Know

Keyword Overage Fees

OmniSEO charges $0.05 per keyword monthly when you exceed plan limits. This adds up fast—going 1,000 keywords over costs an extra $50/month. Many users underestimate their needs and face surprise bills.

Real example: A Professional plan user tracking 6,500 keywords pays an additional $75/month (1,500 × $0.05), making their effective cost $164/month instead of $89.

Premium Support Costs

Standard support works for basic issues, but complex technical problems or urgent needs require premium support at $49/month additional. This includes:

  • 1-hour response time guarantee
  • Direct phone line access
  • Screen sharing troubleshooting sessions
  • Priority feature request consideration

Custom Integration Fees

While OmniSEO integrates with major platforms natively, custom integrations cost extra:

  • Custom API endpoints: $500 setup + $100/month maintenance
  • Proprietary CRM integrations: $1,000-3,000 depending on complexity
  • Custom dashboard development: $2,000-5,000 one-time

Data Export and Migration

Historical data exports beyond 12 months cost $199 per year of data. If you've been tracking keywords for 3 years and want complete historical exports, expect to pay $597 for the full dataset.

Cost Optimization Strategies

Right-sizing Your Plan

Most users over-purchase initially. Start with Professional and monitor actual usage for 2-3 months. Key metrics to track:

  • Average monthly keyword usage (aim for 80% of plan limit)
  • Number of active competitor tracking slots
  • Report generation frequency and customization needs
  • API call volume if using integrations

Annual Billing Savings

Annual plans save 20% across all tiers. For Professional users, this means $213 annual savings ($852 vs $1,068). The payback period is 10.7 months, making annual billing a no-brainer if you're committed to the platform.

Keyword Management Best Practices

Avoid overage fees by regularly auditing tracked keywords:

  • Remove keywords that haven't moved in 6+ months
  • Focus on revenue-generating terms rather than vanity metrics
  • Use keyword groups to track similar terms more efficiently
  • Pause tracking for seasonal keywords during off-periods
