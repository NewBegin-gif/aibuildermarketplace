FAQ

How much does OmniSEO cost per month?

OmniSEO's monthly pricing ranges from $49 for Starter (5 projects) to $399 for Agency (unlimited projects). Professional costs $149/month for 25 projects. Enterprise plans start at $999/month with custom scaling options.

Does OmniSEO offer a free trial?

Yes, OmniSEO provides a 14-day free trial with access to all Professional features. No credit card required for signup, and you get 3 project slots with full keyword tracking and reporting capabilities.

What's included in OmniSEO's cheapest plan?

The $49 Starter plan includes 5 projects, 500 keyword tracking, basic rank monitoring, competitor analysis for 3 competitors, monthly reports, and email support. API access and white-label features are not included.

Can I cancel OmniSEO anytime?

Yes, OmniSEO allows monthly cancellation with no contracts. Annual subscribers can cancel but won't receive refunds for unused months. All data remains accessible for 30 days after cancellation for export purposes.

Does OmniSEO charge setup fees?

No setup fees for Starter and Professional plans. Agency plans include free onboarding. Enterprise customers may have $500-2000 implementation fees depending on custom integrations and data migration requirements.

What Each Plan Actually Includes

Starter Plan ($49/month) - Best for Freelancers

The Starter plan targets solo consultants and small businesses managing fewer than 5 websites. You get basic rank tracking for 500 keywords, competitor analysis for up to 3 competitors, and monthly automated reports. The plan includes mobile and desktop tracking, local SEO features for 10 locations, and basic backlink monitoring (up to 1,000 backlinks tracked).

What's missing: No API access, no white-label reporting, no advanced competitor research, and limited historical data (6 months only). Customer support is email-only with 24-48 hour response times.

Professional Plan ($149/month) - Most Popular Choice

Professional unlocks 25 projects and 2,500 keywords, making it suitable for growing agencies or businesses with multiple brands. You gain access to API endpoints (1,000 calls/month), white-label reports with custom branding, advanced competitor analysis for 10 competitors, and 24-month historical data retention.

Additional features include content optimization suggestions, technical SEO audit tools, social media tracking integration, and priority email support with same-day responses. Local SEO expands to 50 locations, and backlink monitoring increases to 10,000 tracked links.

Agency Plan ($399/month) - For Scaling Operations

Agency tier supports 100 projects and 10,000 keywords with unlimited competitor tracking. You get dedicated account management, phone support, and enhanced API limits (10,000 calls/month). White-label features extend to custom domains and branded client portals.

Advanced features include automated client reporting, team collaboration tools, advanced rank tracking filters, international SEO monitoring across 50+ countries, and integration with popular tools like Google Data Studio, Slack, and Zapier.

Enterprise Plan ($999+ monthly) - Custom Solutions

Enterprise pricing varies significantly based on needs, starting around $999 for basic unlimited usage and scaling to $5,000+ monthly for Fortune 500 implementations. Custom features include dedicated servers, advanced security compliance (SOC 2, GDPR), custom integrations, and dedicated customer success management.

Hidden Costs You Need to Know About

Overage Charges

OmniSEO charges $0.50 per additional keyword beyond your plan limits and $10 per extra project slot. These overages can add up quickly - one client reported an extra $240 monthly after expanding from 500 to 980 keywords on the Starter plan without realizing the incremental costs.

Add-On Features

  • Advanced API Access: $99/month for unlimited API calls (vs. limited calls in standard plans)
  • Premium Support: $149/month for 24/7 phone support and 2-hour response SLA
  • Custom Integrations: $2,000-10,000 one-time setup depending on complexity
  • Additional User Seats: $25/month per user beyond plan limits
  • Extended Data Retention: $50/month to keep historical data beyond standard limits

Implementation Costs

While setup is free for most plans, larger implementations often require professional services. Data migration from competing tools costs $500-2,000. Custom dashboard development ranges from $1,500-5,000. Training sessions for teams of 10+ users typically cost $200/hour with a 4-hour minimum.

Cost Optimization Strategies

1. Annual Billing Savings

Always choose annual billing for the automatic 20% discount. This saves $1,180 annually on Professional plans and $960 on Starter plans. For agencies, the annual Agency plan saves $1,920 yearly.

2. Right-Size Your Plan

Audit your actual usage monthly. Many users overpay by 40-60% because they estimate usage incorrectly. Use OmniSEO's usage dashboard to track keyword consumption and project needs, then adjust accordingly.

3. Keyword Management

Focus on high-impact keywords rather than tracking everything. Remove low-priority, non-converting keywords to stay within plan limits. One agency reduced their keyword count from 3,200 to 1,800 without losing actionable insights, allowing them to downgrade from Agency to Professional plan.

4. Team Collaboration

Instead of buying multiple individual accounts, use shared logins where permissible and upgrade to plans with more user seats. Professional's 5-user limit often serves teams of 8-10 when properly coordinated.

Enterprise Negotiation Tips

Volume Discounts

Enterprise customers can negotiate 15-40% discounts on list prices. Key leverage points include multi-year commitments, case study participation, and reference opportunities. One customer secured 35% off by agreeing to a 3-year contract and quarterly success story participation.

Custom Contract Terms

Request flexible scaling provisions that allow plan changes without penalties. Negotiate data portability clauses and extended trial periods for team rollouts. Push for dedicated support SLAs and custom integration development as part of the package.

Payment Terms

Annual payments often unlock additional 5-10% discounts beyond the standard 20%. Quarterly payments may be negotiable for enterprise deals over $50,000 annually.

Free Tier Limitations Analysis

What Works in the Free Trial

The 14-day trial includes full Professional features, making it excellent for evaluating core functionality. You can set up 3 projects, track 100 keywords, and generate sample reports. This provides sufficient time to assess user interface, data accuracy, and integration capabilities.

What Doesn't Work

No long-term data trends (obvious limitation), limited keyword capacity makes it impossible to test with real client loads, and no API access prevents integration testing. The trial also lacks team collaboration features and white-label customization.

When to Upgrade

Upgrade when you need to track more than 100 keywords consistently, require historical data beyond the trial period, need white-label reports for clients, or want API access for custom dashboards. Most agencies find the trial sufficient for initial evaluation but need Professional within 30 days of serious usage.
