What Omniseo does

AI search visibility (GEO) tracking. Monitor and improve how your brand shows up in AI answers across ChatGPT, Google AI and other channels — track prompts, competitors and citations to grow AI-driven discovery.

Pricing (2026)

(2026) Essentials about $89/mo (4 AI channels, 50 prompts, 5 seats), Professional about $349/mo (10 channels, 100 prompts), Enterprise from about $899/mo (custom regions, unlimited seats, consultation). Often 50% off the first month; annual saves roughly two months. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

Two honest cautions. AI search visibility (GEO) is a young, fast-moving category — tracking how you appear in ChatGPT and other AI answers is genuinely useful signal, but the metrics are far less standardized than classic SEO, and you're paying to monitor a moving target you only partly influence. And the step from Essentials to Professional is a big jump for more channels and prompts. Treat it as an early-mover intelligence tool, not a guaranteed ranking lever.

When the paid plan is worth it

OmniSEO's split runs between paid tiers rather than free-vs-paid: there is no self-serve free tier for the core platform, only separate standalone tools. Essentials, at $89/month ($45 for the first month), suits a single-brand team tracking a handful of AI channels — it caps at 4 AI channels and 50 saved prompts per month, with no CSV/PDF export. Teams needing more channels or heavier prompt research push toward Professional at $349/month ($175 first month), which raises the caps to 10 AI channels, 100 saved prompts per month, and adds 5,000-row exports per month. What upgrading does not solve is competitor visibility: both Essentials and Professional track only 5 competitors — that ceiling only lifts on Enterprise, priced from $899+/month with custom quotes, which lists "5 per brand/serviceline" instead of a flat cap.

How to actually use Omniseo

There are two entry points: self-serve signup at app.omniseo.com/signup (Essentials or Professional, discounted to $45 or $175 for the first month), or a demo request, which requires name, email, website URL, and a message, with only the company field optional. OmniSEO states its team configures tracking on your behalf — "our team sets everything up for you" — rather than a self-service dashboard you configure yourself, so first value depends on their setup pace rather than an instant API connection. For the demo path, OmniSEO commits to following up within 24 hours to schedule the session, not to activating tracking immediately. Because setup is vendor-managed, there is no published documentation describing how domain or channel verification actually works, which makes it hard to judge activation time in advance. On Essentials, the 50 ad-hoc query lookups included per month can also be used up quickly while still testing which prompts and channels matter.
