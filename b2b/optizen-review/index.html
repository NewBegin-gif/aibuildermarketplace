The catch

Starter's $9.99 buys two generated clips a month and Pro's $29.99 buys five; after that you pay $2 or $1.50 per clip. A store with fifty products that wants a video on each is buying credits, not a plan, and should price it that way before signing up. The free tier is a demo rather than a usable floor — one campaign, five conversions, no AI at all. And there is almost no merchant track record to read: the app launched on 30 October 2025 and, read 5 October 2026, carries exactly one App Store review, a five-star note from 11 November 2025 by a store that had used it for two days, so nearly a year in, you would in effect be the test.

What the money actually buys

Going from free to Starter mostly buys the limits away rather than new features: unlimited campaigns and unlimited conversions instead of one campaign and five conversions, plus the right to use AI-generated video at all. Pro at $29.99 lifts the monthly AI allowance from two clips to five, adds priority support, and drops the per-clip price from $2 to $1.50. So the tiers are really a video budget. Two AI clips a month is one product a fortnight; five barely covers a collection.

Any store that wants generated video across a catalogue ends up in credit bundles — up to $40 at a time on Starter, $30 on Pro — and at that point the subscription is the small half of the bill. If you bring your own footage the sum inverts: only AI generation is metered, so merchant-supplied YouTube and Vimeo video runs on the flat $9.99 with nothing on top.

How to actually use Optizen

Optizen installs from the App Store, so setup begins inside your store rather than at a separate signup. The free tier is the honest first step: one active campaign, up to five conversions and bundles, YouTube or Vimeo video only, with a 7-day trial attached. That is enough to answer the question that actually matters — does a video upsell change what shoppers do on your product and post-purchase pages, or do they scroll past it?

Point it at one best-selling product with a video you already own, and read the two things the app reports separately: engagement and revenue attribution. Only move to a paid tier once you have that answer, because paid is where AI video generation starts and where the metering starts with it. Starter at $9.99 a month includes two AI video credits, Pro at $29.99 includes five, and everything beyond the allowance is bought per clip.

Before you upgrade, count the products you would genuinely want a generated video for and multiply by $2 on Starter or $1.50 on Pro. That number, not the subscription, is your real monthly cost. Read on apps.shopify.com/optizen-video-upsell, 31 August 2026.

Frequently asked questions

What does Optizen cost?

Three tiers, billed in USD every 30 days, read on apps.shopify.com/optizen-video-upsell on 5 October 2026 and unchanged since our first reading on 31 August. Free carries a 7-day trial, one active video upsell campaign, up to 5 conversions and bundles, and YouTube or Vimeo video only. Starter is $9.99 a month for unlimited campaigns, unlimited conversions and bundles, AI video alongside your own, and 2 AI video credits a month. Pro is $29.99 a month for five credits, every placement and priority support. The listing publishes no annual column at all, so the monthly figure is the figure.

How many AI videos does a plan actually include?

Two a month on Starter and five on Pro, and the listing prices everything above that separately: $2 per extra AI credit on Starter, sold in bundles of $2, $10, $20 and $40, or $1.50 per credit on Pro, in bundles of $1.50, $7.50, $15 and $30. Put a catalogue against those numbers before you pick a plan. Fifty generated clips on Pro is $75 of credits on top of the $29.99 subscription, and the subscription is the smaller half of that bill. What the page does not say is whether re-generating a clip costs another credit.

Is the free plan enough to test the thing it advertises?

Not quite, and the gap is the point of the app. Free gives you one active campaign, up to 5 conversions and bundles, and all the placement options, but the videos have to be your own on YouTube or Vimeo — AI-generated video begins on Starter at $9.99. So the free tier proves whether video upsells move your AOV at all, which is a fair question to answer first, but it cannot tell you whether the generated clips are good enough to put in front of buyers. How much of a track record can I check before installing?

Very little, and you should know that going in. The listing says the app launched on 30 October 2025 and it carries exactly one rating, a five-star one left on 11 November 2025 by a merchant who had used the app for two days; the developer is Optizenapp, in Macclesfield, South Australia. What should I weigh before buying Growth & Revenue software?

It depends on whether you have the bottleneck it solves. Small teams get the most out of this category when one clear problem is already costing real hours or revenue; buying ahead of that just adds cost and another login. Price it against the hours or lost deals it removes, not against its feature list, and start on the smallest plan that covers the job.
