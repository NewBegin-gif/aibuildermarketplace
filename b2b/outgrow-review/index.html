Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 6 Sep 2026

Freelancer Limited $22/month or $14 annually for 5 content pieces, 5 content types and 250 leads a month; Freelancer Pro $45/$25 for 7 pieces, 7 types and 1,000 leads; Essentials $115/$95 for unlimited pieces, 9 types, 3 users and 7,500 leads; Business $720/$600 for unlimited pieces, all types, 10 users and 50,000 leads. Annual billing is advertised as saving up to 44%.

Three tiers our earlier reading missed sit alongside these: a free Forms/Surveys plan at $0 with 100 leads a month, Startup Basic at $55/$35 ('only new companies can apply') with 750 leads, and Startup Special at $85/$55 ('only startups and small businesses can apply') with 8 content types, 6 pieces and the same 1,000-lead cap as Freelancer Pro. Outgrow branding cannot be removed below Essentials, where it comes off only under the small-business programme, and a custom domain via CNAME is Business-only.

A done-for-you build service is listed at 'starting from $250 per content piece'. Note that outgrow.co/llms.txt disagrees with the page: it lists Business at $225, mixes monthly and annual rates, and dates itself to 2025. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

What the price did

We capture Outgrow’s own pricing page every month and keep the figures, so this is our record of the page — not a forecast, and not someone else’s summary.

Outgrow's main ladder is unchanged across every reading we have: our July and August 2026 captures both record $22/$14, $45/$25, $115/$95 and $720/$600, and the page returned exactly those figures again on 6 September 2026. One correction to our own record belongs here, though. On 17 August we wrote that a 'Startup at $85/$55' tier did not appear on the page, and struck it from the review. It does exist. It is called Startup Special, it is $85 monthly or $55 annually, it is restricted to startups and small businesses, and it sits in a second row of eligibility-gated plans below the main four — which is why a reading of the main ladder alone misses it. The tier we removed was real.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

Read the caps rather than the prices — Freelancer Limited allows 250 leads a month, Freelancer Pro 1,000, Essentials 7,500 and Business 50,000 — because per lead, Essentials and Business cost almost the same and the real jump in value is from Pro to Essentials. Also note what money does not fix: Outgrow's branding stays on every plan below Essentials, comes off there only under the small-business programme, and a custom domain is Business-only at $600 a month.

Interactive content genuinely converts better than static forms, but only when the calculator or quiz gives real value; a thin quiz wrapped around a lead form fools nobody.

What Outgrow does

Interactive content builder for quizzes, calculators, polls and assessments. Generate and qualify leads with engaging, no-code experiences, then sync them straight into Klaviyo via OAuth for email/SMS follow-up, into Attio for CRM handoff, or into CleverTap, where each answer maps to a field you choose and the lead lands as a contact or in a segment. When the paid plan is worth it

Two meters run here and we had the second one wrong. Content types and pieces gate the lower tiers; monthly lead caps gate the upside — but the caps are not what our earlier note said. Read off the plan panels: Freelancer Limited allows 250 leads a month, Freelancer Pro 1,000, Essentials 7,500 and Business 50,000, with a free forms-and-surveys tier at 100. That changes the whole calculation. Paid annually, Freelancer Pro is $25 for 1,000 leads, or 2.5 cents each.

Essentials is $95 for 7,500, or 1.27 cents. Business is $600 for 50,000, or 1.2 cents. So Essentials and Business cost effectively the same per lead, and the eightfold gap we previously described between them does not exist. The real cliff is one rung lower: moving from Freelancer Pro to Essentials halves your cost per lead and lifts the cap from 1,000 to 7,500. What no amount of money below $600 a month removes is Outgrow's own branding.

It stays on Freelancer Limited, Freelancer Pro and Startup Special; on Essentials it comes off only under the small-business programme; a custom domain via CNAME is Business-only. Everything below that sits on yourcompany.outgrow.us.

How to actually use Outgrow

Forecast leads and then look one rung down the ladder, because the tier most people land on is not the one the price suggests. Essentials at $95 a month annually includes 7,500 leads; Freelancer Pro at $25 includes 1,000. Under a thousand leads a month, Pro is the plan, and at its ceiling the FAQ offers two routes: move up a tier, or take a lead add-on where you are.

Check the eligibility tiers before you pay list price. There is a $0 forms-and-surveys plan at 100 leads a month, a Startup Basic at $35 annually for new companies with 750, and a Startup Special at $55. Read that last one carefully: it costs $30 more than Freelancer Pro, carries the same 1,000-lead cap and allows one content piece fewer, so it buys charts, a chatbot type and webhooks rather than headroom.

If you do hit the cap, ask before upgrading — the pricing FAQ says the vendor will craft a lead add-on instead. And do not price from outgrow.co/llms.txt: it puts Business at $225 where the page says $720.

Frequently asked questions

What does Outgrow cost?

Billed annually: $14 a month for Freelancer Limited, $25 for Freelancer Pro, $95 for Essentials and $600 for Business. Billed monthly those become $22, $45, $115 and $720. Annual is advertised as saving up to 44%. There is also a free forms-and-surveys plan, and two eligibility-gated tiers — Startup Basic at $35 annually for new companies and Startup Special at $55 for startups and small businesses. Read on 6 September 2026.

Where is the real jump in the ladder?

Between Freelancer Pro and Essentials: $25 to $95 a month annually. That is where content pieces become unlimited, the lead cap goes from 1,000 to 7,500 a month and you get three users instead of one. It is also where the cost per lead halves, from 2.5 cents to 1.27 cents, which is the only rung on the ladder where the price per unit moves that far.

Is the top tier worth $600 a month?

Only on lead volume and branding. Business raises the allowance from 7,500 leads a month to 50,000 — about six times the volume for about six times the price, so the cost per lead barely moves. What it does buy outright is full removal of Outgrow branding and hosting on your own domain via CNAME, neither of which is available lower down except under the small-business programme.

What should I weigh before buying Growth & Revenue software?

It depends on whether you have the bottleneck it solves. Small teams get the most out of this category when one clear problem is already costing real hours or revenue; buying ahead of that just adds cost and another login. Price it against the hours or lost deals it removes, not against its feature list, and start on the smallest plan that covers the job.
