What Outgrow does

Interactive content builder for quizzes, calculators, polls and assessments. Generate and qualify leads with engaging, no-code experiences, then sync them straight into Klaviyo via OAuth for email/SMS follow-up, or into Attio for CRM handoff.

When the paid plan is worth it

Two meters run here and they gate different things. Content types and layouts limit the lower tiers; monthly LEAD limits cap the upside. It is the second one that decides your bill, because it bites exactly when things go well. Freelancer Limited is $22 a month ($14 annually) for five content pieces, Freelancer Pro $45 ($25), Essentials $115 ($95) with unlimited pieces and 1,000 leads a month, and Business $720 ($600) with 50,000 leads. Annual billing saves up to 44%, which is a large discount by any standard. Work out the cost per lead and the ladder inverts the usual advice. Essentials is about $0.095 a lead at its cap; Business is about $0.012 — nearly eight times better. So the expensive plan is dramatically cheaper per unit, and the question is not whether you can afford Business but whether you can fill it. A quiz that goes even mildly viral hits Essentials' thousand-lead cap in days, and that is precisely the moment you least want friction between a visitor and a form. The practical approach: start on Essentials, watch the lead curve for two months, and move the moment you are regularly clearing half the cap — not when you hit it, because hitting it costs you the leads themselves. One correction worth noting since it appears in older write-ups including ours: the second tier is Freelancer Pro at $45/$25, not a 'Startup at $85/$55' — that plan does not appear on the page or in either of our monthly captures. And the thing no tier buys: interactive content converts better than a static form only when the calculator or quiz gives real value. A thin quiz wrapped around a lead capture fools nobody, and it is the content rather than the plan that produces the leads you are paying per unit for.

How to actually use Outgrow

Forecast leads rather than content pieces, because the lead cap is what actually stops you. Essentials includes 1,000 leads a month, and a quiz that goes even mildly viral reaches that in days — precisely the moment you least want friction. Per lead, Essentials works out at about $0.095 and Business at $0.012, so the upside tier is nearly eight times better value on the meter that matters. Check the second meter too: content types and layouts gate the lower tiers, so Freelancer Limited's five pieces is a real constraint if you were planning a suite of calculators rather than one. Note the second rung is Freelancer Pro at $45 monthly or $25 annually — not the 'Startup at $85/$55' that older write-ups, ours included, carried; that plan does not appear on the page. Then build one genuinely useful calculator or quiz before you scale to several. Interactive content converts better than static forms only when it gives the visitor something real — a number they wanted, an answer they can act on. A thin quiz wrapped around a lead form fools nobody, and it will burn your monthly lead allowance to no purpose. Use the free trial to test the embed on your actual site rather than in their preview, and take annual billing once you know the tier: it saves up to 44%.

Frequently asked questions

What does Outgrow cost?

Billed annually: $14 a month for Freelancer Limited, $25 for Freelancer Pro, $95 for Essentials and $600 for Business. Billed monthly those become $22, $45, $115 and $720. Annual saves up to 44%. Read on 17 August 2026.

Where is the real jump in the ladder?

Between Freelancer Pro and Essentials: $25 to $95 a month annually. That is where content pieces become unlimited and lead capture scales to 1,000 a month. Below it you are limited to a handful of pieces, which is fine for testing one calculator or quiz and not for running them as a channel.

Is the top tier worth $600 a month?

Only on lead volume. Business raises the allowance to 50,000 leads a month against 1,000 on Essentials — a fiftyfold increase for roughly six times the price. If you are nowhere near the Essentials ceiling, the jump buys headroom you will not use.

Is Outgrow worth it for a small team?

It depends on whether you have the bottleneck it solves. Small teams get the most out of this category when one clear problem is already costing real hours or revenue; buying ahead of that just adds cost and another login. Price it against the hours or lost deals it removes, not against its feature list, and start on the smallest plan that covers the job.

Who should not use Outgrow?

Skip it if a simple form or survey does the job (Typeform-class is cheaper), or your traffic would blow through the lead caps. Buying a tool to fix a problem you don't have yet just adds cost and another login to manage.
