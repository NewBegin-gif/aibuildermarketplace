Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 23 Aug 2026

Sole proprietorship around CA$49; incorporation roughly CA$499 (federal) to CA$699 (BC), with government fees included; annual compliance about CA$199–599 a year, with the Online Minute Book Plan free for the first year. The RBC offer is not a cashback that arrives later: Ownr's help centre describes it as an instant discount taken at payment — CA$49 off a sole proprietorship, CA$300 off an incorporation — on the condition that you open an RBC business bank account at a branch within 60 days.

Miss that window and the rebated amount is charged back to the card you paid with; open the account later and it is refunded once you tell Ownr's team. Every figure here is in Canadian dollars; Ownr sells into Canada only. Amounts last verified 23 August 2026: on 22 September 2026 ownr.co/pricing rendered no amounts in its page source (a JavaScript shell) and the help centre points to that page rather than restating the prices, so the figures above could not be re-read. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

When the paid plan is worth it

The Online Minute Book Plan, at CA$199 a year, covers a single-shareholder corporation that just needs its annual return filed, resolutions generated, and documents stored — and Ownr's own subscription policy, updated 6 August 2026, states that this plan is free for the first year and renews each year after that, so the first real bill lands twelve months after you incorporate. Incorporation packages include up to 3 user accounts, enough for a founder plus an accountant or lawyer.

Upgrading to the Managed Corporation Plan (CA$599 a year) becomes worth it once a company outgrows that account cap or starts making frequent director, officer, or address changes, since those are unlimited on the higher tier along with the legal agreement library. What upgrading does not touch is government processing time. Both plans wait in the same queues: Ownr's timeline page, updated 12 August 2026, puts a GST/HST number and a payroll number at 10 business days each, BC name requests at 6 business days (5 express), and Quebec incorporation at 6 business days, none of which move with your plan.

Paying more buys account capacity and unlimited changes inside Ownr's own system, not faster incorporation and not faster CRA turnaround.

How to actually use Ownr

Getting started means choosing a business structure (sole proprietorship or corporation), picking a name, and filling out Ownr's online application, which the platform says takes minutes to complete. Before starting, have your directors' and officers' legal names and addresses ready, since anyone beyond the primary owner needs to e-sign consent. What follows depends on province and structure: on Ownr's own timeline page, updated 12 August 2026, Ontario incorporation and sole proprietorship both process in 1 business day, as does British Columbia; Alberta takes 1-3 business days for either; federal named incorporation takes 1-3 business days and numbered 1-2; and Quebec incorporation takes 6 business days.

The long wait comes after registration, not during it, and it is longer than we previously reported: the same page now puts both the GST/HST number and the payroll number at 10 business days each, where our August reading said 2-3. Those are CRA queues, outside Ownr's control. For Ontario sole proprietorships registered after the CRA's August 2025 cutoff, the business number won't even appear in the Ownr dashboard; it has to be requested directly from the CRA.

Frequently asked questions

Is the RBC rebate really CA$300 off, or does it come back to bite?

It is a real discount and it is conditional, and the condition has teeth. Ownr's help centre article on that unexplained charge describes the rebate as an instant discount applied at payment — CA$49 for a sole proprietorship, CA$300 for a corporation — taken off the price you pay on the day. In exchange you agree to open an RBC business bank account by visiting an RBC branch within 60 days of that payment. If no account is opened in that window, the rebated amount is charged to the card you originally paid with, which is why people find an unexplained CA$49 or CA$300 on their statement months later. It is recoverable: complete the branch setup afterwards and the charge is refunded to the same card, but only after you email Ownr's team to tell them it is done. A separate article confirms you can decline the offer up front and open an account later, in which case the rebate is deposited into the RBC account instead. So budget the full price unless a branch visit within two months is something you will actually do.

What am I locked into if I incorporate through Ownr?

A year at a time, with no refund for the unused part. Ownr's billing article states that all plans require a minimum subscription period of one year, and the subscription policy adds that corporate plans renew automatically on an annual basis, with an email ten days before each renewal. You can cancel at any time before renewal, but the cancellation only takes effect at the end of the current annual billing period and there is no credit or refund for the remaining portion. If a renewal has already been charged, Ownr does not refund it after 14 days from that charge, though your access runs to the end of the term you paid for. Two things soften this. Sole proprietors are not on a subscription at all — a one-time fee only — and the Online Minute Book Plan is free for its first year. One thing sharpens it: cancelling your Ownr account does not close your registration with the government, and Ownr explicitly recommends lining up another provider for annual returns, resolutions and cap-table work before you leave, or the corporation risks falling into default.

How fast will I actually be able to invoice a client?

Registration is the fast part and the tax numbers are not. On Ownr's own timeline page, updated 12 August 2026, the application takes minutes to fill in and the government step runs 1 business day for an Ontario or BC incorporation or sole proprietorship, 1-3 days in Alberta, 1-3 days for a federal named incorporation (1-2 numbered), and 6 business days in Quebec. Name work is same-day in Ontario, Alberta and federally, but a BC name request takes 6 business days, or 5 on express — so in BC the name, not the incorporation, sets your date. Then the CRA queue starts, and this is where our earlier figure was wrong: the same page now lists 10 business days for a GST/HST number and 10 for a payroll number, not the 2-3 days we reported in August. If you need to charge GST/HST or run payroll from day one, that is a fortnight to plan around, and none of it moves faster on a more expensive Ownr plan.

What should I weigh before buying Operations & Workflow software?

Usually it sits on top rather than replacing everything. Tools in this class tend to become the layer where work is tracked and handed off, while your existing systems stay the source of truth for their own data. Before you buy, map which tools it would replace outright and which it would merely sync with. If the answer is that it syncs with all of them, you are adding a layer rather than removing one.
