What Ownr does

Register or incorporate your business in Canada online and manage the legal admin: sole proprietorship or incorporation in minutes, plus contracts and documents — without a lawyer for every step. Canada-only, RBC-owned. Through our partner link, readers save 15% on registration or incorporation.

Pricing (2026)

Sole proprietorship around $49 CAD; incorporation roughly $499 (federal) to $699 (BC), with government fees included; annual compliance about $199–599/yr. If you bank with RBC, a cashback of up to ~$300 CAD can make the registration effectively free (2026). Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

Two honest limits. It's Canada-only — provincial incorporation covers Ontario, Alberta, BC and Quebec — so it's no use outside Canada. And it's built for straightforward setups: it won't handle complex share structures, multi-investor shareholder agreements, tax planning, partnerships or non-profits. For those you still want an accountant or lawyer.

When the paid plan is worth it

The Online Minute Book Plan, at $199/year, covers a single-shareholder corporation that just needs its annual return filed, resolutions generated, and documents stored — it includes up to 3 user accounts, enough for a founder plus an accountant or lawyer. Upgrading to the Managed Corporation Plan ($599/year) becomes worth it once a company outgrows that account cap or starts making frequent director, officer, or address changes, since those are unlimited on the higher tier along with the legal agreement library. What upgrading does not touch is government processing time: CRA business number assignment, GST/HST registration, and payroll number setup all run on the same 2-3 business day (or longer, for Ontario corporations) government queues regardless of plan, and BC name requests still take 5-6 business days. Paying more buys account capacity and unlimited changes inside Ownr's own system, not faster incorporation or faster CRA turnaround.

How to actually use Ownr

Getting started means choosing a business structure (sole proprietorship or corporation), picking a name, and filling out Ownr's online application, which the platform says takes minutes to complete. Before starting, have your directors' and officers' legal names and addresses ready, since anyone beyond the primary owner needs to e-sign consent. What follows depends on province and structure: Ontario incorporation and sole proprietorship registration process in 1 business day, federal named incorporation takes 1-3 business days, and Quebec incorporation takes 6 business days. The most common wait comes after registration, not during it: getting a CRA business number can take up to 4 business days for Ontario corporations, and GST/HST or payroll program numbers each take 2-3 business days to process — all CRA-side delays outside Ownr's control. For Ontario sole proprietorships registered after the CRA's August 2025 cutoff, the business number won't even appear in the Ownr dashboard; it has to be requested directly from the CRA.
