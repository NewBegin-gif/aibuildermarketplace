Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 23 Aug 2026

Five tiers, and the meter is pages plus AI insights rather than people. Free $0: 1 page, unlimited traffic, unlimited collaborators, 14 days of data retention and 15 AI insights a month. Starter $29: 2 pages, unlimited collaborators, 90 days retention, conversion tracking, 100 AI insights. Growth $79: 5 pages, unlimited collaborators, 2 heatmaps, a year of retention, 250 AI insights. Pro $129: 10 pages, unlimited collaborators, 5 heatmaps, three years of retention, cross-domain conversions, 750 AI insights.

Scale $199: 20 pages, 10 members, 15 heatmaps and 1,500 AI insights — the only tier that names a number of people. Annual billing is marked Save 20%. The footnote under the table reads 'Designed for real-world usage. Subject to fair use.', which is the asterisk on the unlimited-traffic promise. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

What PagePulse does

Per-page site analytics with heatmaps and AI-summarised insights. Free for one member and one page; Starter at $29/month buys one member and two pages. The tiers gate on members and tracked pages at once, and the 'unlimited traffic' promise carries a fair-use footnote.

When the paid plan is worth it

The ladder is priced on pages and members, and the striking thing is that it barely rewards volume. Per page: Starter is $29 for 2 pages ($14.50 each), Growth $79 for 5 ($15.80), Pro $129 for 10 ($12.90) and Scale $199 for 15 ($13.27). That is essentially flat -- the middle tier is actually the worst value per page. So there is no reason to buy headroom you will not use; take the tier that fits your current page count and move when you actually need to.

The free tier is genuinely useful rather than a demo: $0 for 1 member and 1 page with unlimited traffic. If you are watching a single landing page, that is the whole product. The first paid step doubles your pages to 2 for $29, which is the most expensive page you will ever add here -- worth knowing that the jump from free to Starter is the weakest value on the ladder, not the strongest.

Members scale alongside pages (1, 2, 5 and 10), so a small team with many pages and a large team with few pages both end up paying for the axis they do not need. Check which of the two binds first. One footnote to read literally: “unlimited” traffic is subject to fair use. If your page is genuinely high-traffic, ask what that means in numbers before you rely on it. Annual billing saves 20%.

How to actually use PagePulse

Count your pages, not your people. Every tier below Scale carries unlimited collaborators, so a team of one and a team of ten pay the same — what moves you up the ladder is how many pages you track: 1 on Free, 2 on Starter at $29, 5 on Growth at $79, 10 on Pro at $129 and 20 on Scale at $199. Scale is the only tier that names a headcount (10 members).

Then look at the second meter, because it is the one that bites quietly: AI insights are capped per month and the steps are steep — 15 on Free, 100 on Starter, 250 on Growth, 750 on Pro and 1,500 on Scale. If the insights are your reason for buying, size on that number rather than on the page count. Start on the free plan, which is a genuine one: one page with unlimited traffic and unlimited collaborators.

That is enough to instrument the single page that matters most — your checkout, your pricing page, your main landing page — and find out whether the insights change anything you do. If they do not on your most important page, they will not on your tenth. Read the fair-use footnote before you rely on 'unlimited' traffic. The line under the table says 'Designed for real-world usage. Subject to fair use.', so if the page you want to track is genuinely high-traffic, ask what the threshold is and what happens when you pass it. Annual billing saves 20%.
