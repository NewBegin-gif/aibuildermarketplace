Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 16 Sep 2026

The meter is credits, and there are two separate pools. Data credits are a different pool, bought once and never expiring, and they are the only part of the product priced per action: 1 data credit per lead or contact returned, 2 per matched enrichment lookup through the API or an agent, and a lookup that finds nothing is free. They run $25 for 500 down to $175 for 5,000, so between 5.0 and 3.5 cents a lead.

Frequently asked questions

What does Parallel AI cost?

Read on 16 September 2026 on parallellabs.app/pricing, which stamps itself “Last updated: 2026-08-18”. Pay As You Go is $0 with 50 credits and no card, Entrepreneur is $99 a month for 2,000 credits and 2 companies, Business is $297 for 9,000 credits and 10 companies, and Enterprise is quote-only. Lead data credits are a separate pool bought once: $25 for 500, $45 for 1,000, $100 for 2,500 and $175 for 5,000. Add-ons are priced on top — extra companies $20 a month, extra seats $15, an extra white-label instance $50, and anonymous website pixel identification from $75 to $500 a month depending on resolutions.

Do unused credits roll over?

No, and the vendor's own FAQ says so plainly: credits reset on the 1st of each month based on your plan allocation. A quiet month is burned rather than banked. The two pools behave differently, which is the part worth knowing before you choose between subscribing and topping up — purchased credit packs and lead data credits never expire. Every seat inside a company draws on the same monthly allocation, so adding people adds work without adding capacity, and the platform notifies you at 80% and 90% of that allocation.

How many credits does one action use?

The pricing page does not say, and that is the number your budget actually turns on. Content generation, agent conversations and outreach are all simply “credits” with no published per-action rate, so 2,000 a month on Entrepreneur is a quantity without a unit until you have run your own work through it. Only the lead side is priced per result: 1 data credit per lead or contact returned, 2 per matched enrichment lookup through the API or an agent, and a lookup that finds nothing is free. Spend the 50 free credits on the workflow you would actually buy this for, then read Usage & Billing, which tracks consumption by feature, model and timestamp.

What should I weigh before buying Growth & Revenue software?

It depends on whether you have the bottleneck it solves. Small teams get the most out of this category when one clear problem is already costing real hours or revenue; buying ahead of that just adds cost and another login. Price it against the hours or lost deals it removes, not against its feature list, and start on the smallest plan that covers the job.
