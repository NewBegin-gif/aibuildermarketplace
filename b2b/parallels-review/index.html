Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 8 Nov 2026

Three editions are sold as yearly subscriptions. Standard is €99.99 a year (8 GB virtual RAM and 4 virtual CPUs per machine), Pro is €119.99 a year (128 GB and 32 CPUs, command line tools and developer features) and Business is €149.99 a year (Pro plus central administration, volume licensing and deployment through Mac management tools). On the day we read the page a Black Friday offer cut these to €49.99, €59.99 and €97.49 a year; it is a promotion, so check the number at checkout.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Parallels Desktop cost?

Read on 8 November 2026 from a Netherlands connection: Standard €99.99, Pro €119.99 and Business €149.99 a year. A Black Friday offer on that day showed €49.99, €59.99 and €97.49. Check the final price at checkout.

Do I need a Windows licence?

Yes. The vendor says a Windows licence is not included with Parallels Desktop and must be bought separately.

How many Macs does one licence cover?

One. The vendor FAQ says each Parallels Desktop licence covers one Mac, so you need a separate licence for each Mac.

Is there a free trial?

The vendor advertises a 14-day free trial of Desktop on its trial page.

Can I get a refund?

The buy page promises a 30-day money-back guarantee on purchases. The vendor help centre says renewal transactions cannot be returned.
