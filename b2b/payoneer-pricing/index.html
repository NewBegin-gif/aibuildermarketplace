Payoneer Fees in 2026

PricingResearched assessment · by Daniel HaketUpdated 2026-08-02

Payoneer is free to open and the fees arrive in layers. Each one is small; the stack is not. Here they are in one place.

Visit Payoneer → Affiliate link — the rest of this page is the honest version, including who should skip it.

The fee stack

What happensFee
Receiving non-local currency1% (minimum $1)
Receiving by cardup to 3.99% + $0.49
Withdrawal, same country and currency$1.50 flat
Currency conversion1.2–4%
Annual account fee$29.95 — waived in year one, and whenever at least $6,000 arrives in 12 months

Verified 2 August 2026. Rates vary by country and account type — confirm yours in the Payoneer app before you plan around them.

The risk that costs more than any fee

Account freezes

Roughly 75% of negative reviewers name account freezes or blocked funds as their main complaint. Re-verification can freeze an account precisely while money is in transit, and escalations run for weeks.

The practical conclusion is not to avoid Payoneer — it is to never make it your only route to your own money. Keep a second account that can receive the same payments.

Early 2026 also brought reports of hacked accounts and unauthorised transfers, one of $4,300. Enable every security control the account offers.

Where the maths works

Payoneer earns its place when a marketplace or platform will not do a normal bank transfer to your country — then the 1% is the cost of getting paid at all, and reviewers say the transfers land quickly and reliably.

Where it does not: if your payer can send SEPA or local ACH, a normal business account beats this on every line above.
