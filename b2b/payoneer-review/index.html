What Payoneer is

Receive and manage cross-border payments from clients and marketplaces.

  • Receiving accounts — local-style account details in several major currencies to get paid like a local.
  • Marketplace & client payouts — widely supported by marketplaces and platforms for paying sellers and freelancers.
  • Mass payouts & billing — pay contractors/suppliers and send invoices internationally.
  • Card & withdrawals — a commercial card and withdrawal to local bank accounts.

Where it genuinely helps

Payoneer's strength is reach. If your income arrives from international marketplaces or clients who already support it, receiving in multiple currencies and withdrawing locally is far less painful than wrangling international wires through a traditional bank. For sellers on global platforms, it's often the path of least resistance.

Who it's for

Frequently asked questions

What is Payoneer used for?

Receiving cross-border payments from international clients and marketplaces, holding balances in multiple currencies, paying contractors via mass payouts, and withdrawing to a local bank account. It's popular with freelancers and e-commerce sellers.

Is Payoneer safe and legitimate?

Yes — it's an established, regulated payments company used by millions of businesses and major marketplaces. As always, confirm the specifics that apply to your country and account type.

What does Payoneer cost?

There are fees on certain transactions, currency conversion and withdrawals, which vary by country and payment type. Model your actual payment flow rather than assuming it's free — check current fees on their site.

Payoneer vs Wise or Airwallex?

Payoneer wins on marketplace acceptance and reach; specialists like Wise often win on transparent FX. Many global sellers use more than one. If you also want cards, expenses and bill pay in one place, look at a finance stack like Aspire too.
