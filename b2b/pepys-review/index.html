Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 15 Nov 2026

Pepys has a free account and pay-as-you-go credits. The free account gives 60 minutes once, with no card, files up to 1 GB, transcription in 99 languages with timestamps, AI summary, chapters and chat, translation, exports to TXT, DOCX, PDF, SRT, VTT and JSON, and API access metered on your free minutes. Paid credits cost €1.1 an hour on purchases under €17.6, €0.88 an hour from €17.6 and €0.75 an hour from €74.8.

The smallest pack shown is 10 hours for €11 and the buy button offers 20 hours for €17.6. Any purchase gives you files up to 5 GB and 10 hours, unlimited Google Drive, OneDrive and Zoom imports, batch upload of up to 100 files, speaker labels at 3 credits per minute, word-level timestamp exports and removal of the Pepys mark. The page says credits never expire, personal use has no subscription and all sales are final.

Full pricing notes (2 more paragraphs)

Teams can add a flat monthly plan. We did not buy credits, so we do not know the tax added. Re-read on 15 November 2026 with a plain fetch of pepys.co/pricing: the hourly rates (€1.1, €0.88 and €0.75) are unchanged, and the page now lists packs of 10 hours for €11, 30 for €26.4, 50 for €44, 75 for €66, 100 for €74.8 and 200 for €149.6, plus a custom top-up from $5.

It also prices the team plans: Starter €16.72 a month for 20 shared hours, Team €43.12 for 60 and Scale €87.12 for 140, with no per-seat fee and unused hours kept. The terms say prices exclude tax, which Polar, the merchant of record, adds at checkout.

Plans change, so check the live price on their site before you buy.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

The team plan has no visible price. The page shows euros but bills in USD, and all sales are final. Pepys Dictate, the Mac dictation app, is a separate product. Word-level timing exports (JSON, SRT, VTT) come from transcripts made from audio; the founder wrote to us on 20 November 2026 that sources built only from existing captions may not contain that timing data. We did not test word-level export.

    Frequently asked questions

    Can you export your data from Pepys?

    Exit check 2026-11-19; route excerpt: No export or reimport was performed; came along (excerpts): not tested; lost (excerpts): not tested; re-import: not tested.

    How much does Pepys cost?

    Per the pricing page read on 13 November 2026, the first 60 minutes are free. Paid credits cost €1.1, €0.88 or €0.75 an hour depending on pack size, starting at 10 hours for €11, billed in USD.

    Is there a subscription?

    Not for personal use: the page says credits never expire. Teams can take a flat monthly plan, read on 15 November 2026 as Starter €16.72 for 20 shared hours, Team €43.12 for 60 and Scale €87.12 for 140, with no per-seat fee.

    Do speaker labels cost extra?

    Yes. They use 3 credits per minute, so a labelled hour uses three hours of credit.

    Does it need a bot in my meeting?

    No. You upload the recording afterward, and the home page says no bot joins the room.

    What do we not know?

    How accurate the transcripts are on real recordings and the tax added at checkout.

    Can I get my money back for unused credits?

    No. The terms (read 15 November 2026) say all credit purchases are final and credits, used or unused, are non-refundable except where the law requires a refund. Because credits never expire, unused hours stay in your account. Buy a small pack first if you are unsure.

    What happens to unused team hours if we cancel?

    They stay. The terms say hours added by a team plan never expire, are not erased when the subscription ends or a payment fails, and that a cancellation takes effect at the end of the paid month. Team features stop after that month; a price change only applies to renewals after notice.

    Can team members see each other's transcripts?

    By default, yes: every member of a team workspace sees all its transcripts. The terms say an owner or admin can switch to a mode where each member sees only their own transcripts plus what they share, and in that mode owners and admins cannot open other members' private transcripts. Personal workspaces stay private.
