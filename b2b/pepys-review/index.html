A quote appears here only after it matched the live page word for word on the date shown. “Being verified” means we have not matched it yet, not that it does not exist.

Pepys has a free account and pay-as-you-go credits. The free account gives 60 minutes once, with no card, files up to 1 GB, transcription in 99 languages with timestamps, AI summary, chapters and chat, translation, exports to TXT, DOCX, PDF, SRT, VTT and JSON, and API access metered on your free minutes. Paid credits cost €1.1 an hour on purchases under €17.6, €0.88 an hour from €17.6 and €0.75 an hour from €74.8.

The smallest pack shown is 10 hours for €11 and the next 20 hours for €17.6. Any purchase unlocks files up to 5 GB and 10 hours, unlimited Google Drive, OneDrive and Zoom imports, batch upload of up to 100 files, speaker labels at 3 credits per minute, word-level timestamp exports and removal of the Pepys mark. The page says credits never expire, personal use has no subscription and all sales are final.

Teams can add a flat monthly plan, but the page shows no price for it. We did not buy credits, so we do not know the tax added or how the team plan is priced. Plans change, so check the live price on their site before you buy.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

The team plan has no visible price. The page shows euros but bills in USD, and all sales are final. Pepys Dictate, the Mac dictation app, is a separate product.

What Pepys does

Audio and video transcription with timestamps in 99 languages, summaries and exports; pay per hour of audio with no subscription.

One pack unlocks the pro features for good

The page says any purchase, even 10 hours for €11, keeps speaker labels, batch upload, the API and word-level exports unlocked after the hours run out. That makes the smallest pack the real entry point to the paid tools. Speaker labels triple the credit use, so labelled interviews cost about three times the headline rate.

How to actually use Pepys

Create a free account, upload an audio or video file or paste a public link, and read the timestamped transcript in the editor. The free 60 minutes are enough to try one long interview. Connect Google Drive, OneDrive or Zoom if the recordings live there. We did not run our own recordings through Pepys, so we cannot say how accurate the transcripts are or how well speaker labels work.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Pepys cost?

Per the pricing page read on 13 November 2026, the first 60 minutes are free. Paid credits cost €1.1, €0.88 or €0.75 an hour depending on pack size, starting at 10 hours for €11, billed in USD.

Is there a subscription?

The page says personal use has no subscription and credits never expire. Teams can add a flat monthly plan, but no price is shown.

Do speaker labels cost extra?

Yes. They use 3 credits per minute, so a labelled hour uses three hours of credit.

Does it need a bot in my meeting?

No. You upload the recording afterward, and the home page says no bot joins the room.

What do we not know?

How accurate the transcripts are on real recordings, the team plan price and the tax added at checkout.
