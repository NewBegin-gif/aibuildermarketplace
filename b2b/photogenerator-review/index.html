Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 16 Sep 2026

Credit-based, in US dollars, with no free tier among the published plans — the site offers some free credits to try the generator, but every plan is paid. Billed monthly: Starter $12 for 360 credits a month, Pro $49 for 1,100 and Max $119 for 5,300. Billed annually: Starter $10 a month charged $120 a year for 4,320 credits, Pro $39 a month charged $468 for 24,000, and Max $89 a month charged $1,068 for 63,600.

There is also a Pay Once route with four packs that carry no subscription: Starter Pack 2,750 credits for $99, Plus Pack 5,600 for $199, Pro Pack 8,500 for $299 and Max Pack 19,900 for $499. Credits buy access to a single pool of models — images on Nano Banana, Nano Banana 2, Nano Banana Pro and GPT Image 2; video on Seedance 2.0, Seedance 2.0 Fast, Seedance Pro, Kling 2.5, Kling V3, Veo 3 Fast, Veo 3 Quality and Wan 2.5 — and the pricing page publishes a full table of how many generations each plan buys on each model, which is where the real prices are.

One caution before you buy: the page quotes Pro monthly twice and not at the same figure. The plan card says $49 a month; the comparison table directly beneath it heads the same column $29 a month, for the same 1,100 credits. Both were on screen together on 16 September 2026. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

The card says $49 a month; the table below it says $29 a month for the identical 1,100 credits. Only the tier badged Most Popular carries two prices — Starter and Max match at $12 and $119 in both places. The Subscribe button sits in the table, next to the lower figure. Take the $49 as the number to budget against and check the charge before you confirm it. That matters more because of what the arithmetic says about Pro.

Divide each monthly plan by its credits and Starter is $0.033 a credit, Max is $0.022, and Pro — the one the page recommends — is $0.045. The middle tier is the most expensive credit you can buy on monthly billing, 34% dearer than the cheapest plan on the page. It only makes sense annually, and then it makes a lot: Pro's annual plan carries 24,000 credits rather than the 13,200 you would get paying monthly for a year, so it is the one tier where you get 82% more credits as well as a lower rate, landing at $0.020 a credit.

The Yearly toggle advertises 30% OFF. Against the monthly headline, the actual reductions are 16.7% on Starter, 20.4% on Pro and 25.2% on Max — none of them 30%. Pro is the only plan where the saving genuinely beats the badge, and only because of those extra credits rather than the price. The deeper thing to understand is that a credit is not a picture. Starter's 360 monthly credits are 180 images on Nano Banana at 2 credits each, or 120 on GPT Image 2 at 3 — but on video the same allowance is 90 clips on Seedance Pro at 4 credits, and 3 clips on Veo 3 Quality at 120.

That is a thirtyfold spread inside one plan, so a plan that looks generous becomes a fortnight of work the moment you prefer the best video model. To the vendor's credit the headline counts do reconcile with its own table — 'up to 2,160 images' on annual Starter is exactly Nano Banana, and 'up to 1,080 videos' is exactly Seedance Pro — so these are the cheapest models rather than invented numbers.

They are still the cheapest models. One quirk to know before you pick: Seedance Pro costs 4 credits while plain Seedance 2.0 costs 20 and Seedance 2.0 Fast costs 15. The tier named Pro is the cheapest of the three, not the dearest, which is the opposite of what the naming implies. And know what you are buying: this is an aggregator, not a model. You are paying for one workspace, one balance and one prompt gallery across other companies' models, so the question is whether that convenience beats going to those providers directly at your volume.

The recommended tier is the worst rate, unless you pay yearly

Work out the price of a credit in each of the ten ways this page lets you buy one, and the ladder stops behaving like a ladder. On monthly billing: Starter is $12 for 360 credits, which is $0.033 each; Max is $119 for 5,300, which is $0.022; and Pro — badged Most Popular — is $49 for 1,100, which is $0.045. The tier in the middle is the most expensive credit on the page, 34% above the entry plan.

Whatever Pro is for on monthly billing, it is not value. Annual billing fixes it, and overfixes it. Starter becomes $120 a year for 4,320 credits ($0.028), Max $1,068 for 63,600 ($0.017), and Pro $468 for 24,000 ($0.020). Note what happened to Pro: 24,000 credits, where twelve monthly payments would have bought 13,200. Starter and Max carry exactly the same annual credits as twelve monthly cycles, so only Pro gets the extra 82%.

Paying for Pro annually rather than monthly is a 56% cut in the cost of a credit, which is why it is the only tier where the 30% OFF badge understates the saving instead of overstating it. The Pay Once packs sit in between and never expire: $99 for 2,750 credits ($0.036), $199 for 5,600 ($0.036), $299 for 8,500 ($0.035) and $499 for 19,900 ($0.025). The first three are all the same rate, so the only pack that buys anything cheaper is the largest.

Against annual Max at $0.017 a credit, a one-off pack costs roughly twice as much — the premium you pay for not committing, which is fair enough, but worth knowing you are paying. Then convert credits into the thing you actually wanted. At Starter's monthly rate a Nano Banana image is about 6.7 cents and a GPT Image 2 image is 10 cents, while a Seedance Pro clip is 13 cents and a Veo 3 Quality clip is $4.00.

Move to annual Max and that Veo clip falls to about $2.02. The model you choose changes your cost per asset by a factor of thirty; the plan you choose changes it by a factor of two. Pick the model first.

How to actually use PhotoGenerator

Work out your model before your plan, because the model is the bigger multiplier. The pricing page publishes a full table of how many generations each plan buys on each model, and the spread inside one plan is enormous: Starter's 360 monthly credits are 180 Nano Banana images, or 90 Seedance Pro clips, or 3 Veo 3 Quality clips. Decide what you will actually generate, find that row, and read the number rather than the headline.

Do not take the monthly Pro plan. At $49 for 1,100 credits it is the worst rate on the page; the same tier billed annually is $468 for 24,000 credits, which is less than half the cost per credit and 82% more credits than twelve monthly cycles would give you. If you are not ready to commit for a year, Starter monthly at $12 is a better rate than Pro monthly, and the Pay Once packs let you buy credits that never expire without a subscription at all.

Check the charge before you confirm it. The plan card and the comparison table quote Pro at two different prices ($49 and $29) on the same page, and the Subscribe button in the table sits beside the lower figure. Use the free credits and the prompt gallery first. The gallery carries thousands of prompts contributed by other users with the image each one produced, and any of them can be copied or used as a reference image — which is the fastest way to find out whether the models here give you the look you want before you spend anything.
