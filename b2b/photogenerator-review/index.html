Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 3 Oct 2026

Credit-based, in US dollars, with no free tier among the published plans — the site offers some free credits to try the generator, but every plan is paid. Starter has one size: $12 a month for 360 credits, or $120 a year ($10 a month) for 4,320. Pro and Max each carry a three-step credit selector. Pro monthly is $29 for 1,100 credits, $49 for 2,000 or $79 for 3,300; yearly it is $288, $468 or $744 for 13,200, 24,000 or 39,600 credits.

Max monthly is $119 for 5,300, $199 for 9,200 or $299 for 14,400; yearly $1,068, $1,716 or $2,508 for 63,600, 110,400 or 172,800. The yearly plans carry exactly twelve months of credits, so the saving is in the price alone: 17% on Starter, 17% to 22% on Pro and 25% to 30% on Max, under a toggle badged 30% OFF. Pay Once packs need no subscription: 2,750 credits for $99, 5,600 for $199, 8,500 for $299 and 19,900 for $499, valid for 360 days (the Pro Pack card says 369) and non-refundable.

Credits draw on one pool of image models (GPT Image 2 and 2.5, Nano Banana, Flux 2, Seedream, Midjourney, Recraft and others) and video models (Seedance, Kling, Veo 3.1, Wan, Minimax, Hailuo and others), and the page's per-model table is where the real prices are. Correction to our 16 September reading, which reported Pro at $49 on the card and $29 in the table for the same 1,100 credits: the card has a credit selector, $29 buys 1,100 credits and $49 buys 2,000, and the Pay Once credits expire after 360 days. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

Starter's 360 monthly credits are 360 images on GPT Image 2.5 Flare at 1 credit each, 180 on Nano Banana at 2, 120 on GPT Image 2 at 3 or 45 on Nano Banana Pro at 8 — and on video, 90 clips on Seedance Pro at 4 credits, 18 on Seedance 2.0 at 20, 7 on Veo 3.1 Fast and 3 on Veo 3.1 Quality. The headline counts ('Up to 360 images', 'Up to 90 videos') are the cheapest model in each column; they reconcile with the vendor's own table, but they are still the cheapest models.

The Yearly toggle advertises 30% OFF. Every yearly plan carries exactly twelve months of credits, so the saving is the price cut alone: 16.7% on Starter, 17.2% to 21.5% on Pro and 25.2% to 30.1% on Max, which means only the 14,400-credit Max reaches the badge. The Pay Once packs are dearer per credit than any subscription and, despite the 'pay once' framing, expire after 360 days and cannot be refunded.

One naming quirk: Seedance Pro costs 4 credits while Seedance 2.0 costs 20 and Seedance 2.0 Fast 15, so the model called Pro is the cheapest of the three, not the dearest. And know what you are buying: an aggregator, not a model. You pay for one workspace, one balance and one prompt gallery across other companies' models, so the question is whether that convenience beats going to those providers directly at your volume.

What PhotoGenerator does

AI image and video generator that puts a dozen third-party models behind one credit balance and one workspace: Nano Banana (three variants) and GPT Image 2 for stills, Seedance, Kling, Veo 3 and Wan 2.5 for video, plus face swap, background removal, upscaling, style transfer, lip-sync, motion sync, character creation and voice tools. A prompt gallery carries thousands of community prompts with the image each produced, reusable as prompt or reference.

The credit rate falls with size, and yearly beats monthly

Work out the price of a credit and the ladder behaves like a ladder. Billed monthly, Starter costs $0.033 a credit, Pro $0.026 at 1,100 credits, $0.025 at 2,000 and $0.024 at 3,300, and Max $0.022 at 5,300 down to $0.021 at 14,400. Billed yearly, every option gets cheaper: Starter $0.028, Pro $0.022 to $0.019, Max $0.017 to $0.015. Upgrading therefore buys a lower rate per credit, but the step between neighbours is small (Pro's three sizes sit within two-tenths of a cent of each other), so size the plan on what you will use, not on the discount.

The yearly switch is worth more than the tier: it takes 17% off Starter and up to 30% off the largest Max, though only that largest Max reaches the 30% the toggle advertises. The Pay Once packs cost $0.035 to $0.036 a credit for the first three and $0.025 for the 19,900-credit pack, more than any subscription, and they expire after 360 days, so they are a way to avoid a commitment, not a way to save.

What upgrading does not change is the price of a generation, which the model sets. A 1-credit GPT Image 2.5 Flare image costs 3.3 cents on monthly Starter; a Veo 3.1 Quality clip at about 115 credits costs roughly $3.80 there and still about $1.67 on the largest yearly Max. The model you pick moves your cost per asset by a factor of about a hundred; the plan moves it by about two. Pick the model first.

How to actually use PhotoGenerator

Work out your model before your plan, because the model is the bigger multiplier. The pricing page publishes a full table of how many generations each plan buys on each model, and the spread inside one plan is enormous: Starter's 360 monthly credits are 360 GPT Image 2.5 Flare images, 180 Nano Banana images, 90 Seedance Pro clips or 3 Veo 3.1 Quality clips. Decide what you will actually generate, find that row, and read the number rather than the headline.

Pay yearly if you will use the credits: every plan is cheaper per credit billed annually, from 17% off Starter to 30% off the largest Max. Within a tier, size by volume rather than discount, because Pro's 1,100, 2,000 and 3,300-credit options differ by about a tenth of a cent per credit. The Pay Once packs avoid a subscription but cost more per credit and expire after 360 days. Check which credit option is selected before you subscribe: the Pro card opens on its middle option and Max on its smallest, and the price changes with the selector.

Use the free credits and the prompt gallery first. The gallery carries thousands of prompts contributed by other users with the image each one produced, and any of them can be copied or used as a reference image — which is the fastest way to find out whether the models here give you the look you want before you spend anything. And if you are joining as an affiliate rather than a customer, note the rule on their own application form: paid promotion is not allowed, and you may not bid on their brand keywords.
