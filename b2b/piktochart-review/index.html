Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 6 Aug 2026

(2026-08-06, read in a live browser on their own pricing page, US dollar view) Free is $0, Pro is $15 per member per month billed monthly or $10 billed annually, Business is $20 monthly or $17 billed annually, and Enterprise is quote-only from 101 members. Free stops at 2 PNG downloads, 1 GB of storage, 60 AI credits and free templates only, and is capped at 1-4 members.

Pro adds unlimited PNG downloads, premium templates and icons, 500 AI credits and 100 GB, and the annual rate is what the page calls 33% off. Business, 15% off annually, adds PDF and PowerPoint export on top of PNG, the Brand Kit (brand colours, fonts and logos), 1,000 AI credits and 250 GB. Enterprise adds SSO/SAML, workspace management and a dedicated account manager.

Two separate tabs on the same page price other audiences. Education is $39 per member billed annually with 250 AI credits and unlimited PNG, PDF and PowerPoint downloads, and requires a valid educational email address, with Campus quote-only above 3,000 seats. A Nonprofits tab also exists, which we did not read. The page can be switched between USD, GBP, EUR, SGD, MXN and INR.

No free trial is offered on the paid tiers — the free plan is the trial — and the page states you can cancel anytime. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

Pro — the tier the page marks most popular — exports PNG only. PDF and PowerPoint are Business features, which the full feature table confirms in a single row most people scroll past. So if the reason you want this is to email a PDF handout or drop slides into a deck, Pro at $10 a member a month is not a cheaper Piktochart — it is the wrong product.

Your real entry price is $17 a member a month on annual billing, or $20 monthly. The free plan reads as generous and is not. Unlimited projects, two downloads — and not two a month: the comparison table lists the number of downloads as a flat 2. That is enough to find out whether you like the editor, which is a fair trial, but it is not the working free tier some competitors in this category offer, so do not plan around it.

The third thing to price is the AI credits: 60 on Free, 500 on Pro, 1,000 on Business. Nowhere on the pricing page does it say what one credit buys, or what happens when you run out — there is no top-up price anywhere on the page. If AI generation is the reason you are buying, ask what a credit costs and what it covers before you commit to a year, because that is a meter that can decide your bill and it is not published.

One last piece of arithmetic. The annual discount is 33% on Pro but only 15% on Business, so the saving shrinks exactly at the tier most teams that need PDF or PowerPoint end up on. And if you have an educational email address the picture changes completely: Education is $39 per member for a whole year, against $120 for a year of Pro, and it includes the PDF and PowerPoint exports that Pro does not have.

What Piktochart does

Infographic, report and poster maker with template libraries and AI credits — the free plan allows 2 downloads, Pro is $10 per member/month billed annually, and PDF/PowerPoint export starts at Business ($17).

What buyers report about Piktochart

Recurring themes from the buyer-review sites listed at the foot of this page, read in August 2026. We have not run Piktochart ourselves, so this is other buyers' experience rather than ours. We summarise it here because the complaints are usually the part a vendor page leaves out.

What they praise
  • • Template categorisation is the recurring reason people pick it over Canva: reviewers who switched say deciding which template to start from is faster here than in tools with far bigger libraries.
  • • Reviewers with no design background report producing infographics and reports that look professionally made, and the newer AI generation draws specific praise from people who came from Excel or Canva.
What they complain about
  • • Chart control is the sharpest complaint: a data analyst two-plus years in reports labels overlapping with no good way to fix them, which bites in exactly the job the tool is sold for.
  • • Lag on large or image-heavy designs, mentioned by several long-term users on tight deadlines.
  • • Trial reviewers call the Pro price high against competitors and the free-tier layouts too thin for business presentations.
  • • Accessibility, from a higher-education reviewer in May 2021: no accessibility checker, no proper way to add alt-text, hundreds of WAVE errors on templates, and charts and maps that screen readers could not read. It is the oldest gripe here and we could not confirm it still holds, but it lands on the education buyers this tool aims at.

Where the ratings disagree. Capterra shows 4.7 across 198 reviews and TrustRadius 7.7 out of 10 across 12, but the TrustRadius record is close to dormant: its most recent full review dates from 2021, so the lower score describes an older product rather than offering a second opinion on the current one. When the paid plan is worth it

The gate that decides this purchase is a file format, not a volume. Free and Pro both export PNG only -- Free stops at 2 downloads, Pro makes them unlimited -- and PDF and PowerPoint export do not appear until Business. So if your infographic has to land in a deck or go to a printer, the answer is Business at $17 a member billed annually, and no amount of Pro gets you there.

Establish which formats you need before comparing anything else; everything below is secondary to that. The annual discount is oddly uneven: Pro is $15 monthly against $10 annually, which the page calls 33% off, while Business is $20 against $17 -- only 15%. Paying yearly therefore narrows the gap between the tiers on a monthly basis ($5) and widens it annually ($7). Neither is large in absolute terms, which is another argument for choosing on export formats rather than on price.

Business also carries the Brand Kit -- brand colours, fonts and logos -- plus 1,000 AI credits against Pro’s 500 and 250 GB against 100 GB. Free is capped at 1 to 4 members, 1 GB and 60 AI credits. One route worth checking before paying business rates: the Education tab prices at $39 per member billed annually with 250 AI credits and unlimited PNG, PDF and PowerPoint downloads. That is the export capability of Business for roughly a fifth of the annual cost ($39 against $204), and it needs a valid educational email address.

There is no free trial on the paid tiers -- the free plan is the trial -- and you can cancel anytime.
