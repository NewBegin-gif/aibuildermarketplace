Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 26 Aug 2026

Free is $0 with daily credits and no card. Starter is $120 a year, down from $200; Plus $360 from $600; Pro $1,800 from $3,000. The tiers are really credit bundles — 2,100, 6,700 and 35,500 credits a month — and the page prints the exchange itself: a dollar buys 2.1 credits on Starter, 2.23 on Plus and 2.37 on Pro. So the discount for going up a tier is in the credit rate as much as in the headline, and generation speed is what separates the tiers otherwise. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

What buyers report about Pippit

What they praise

What they complain about
  • • Failed generations still consume credits: Trustpilot reviewers describe burning an entire allowance without one usable video.
  • • Voice synchronisation is the most-named quality failure on avatar and talking-photo output.
  • • Support is slow — one reviewer waited two and a half weeks on a promised 3-5 business-day response — and refunds are described as hard to get.
  • • Third-party AI models have been removed from the platform without notice, which reviewers cite as a trust issue.
  • • Some report charges after they believed they had cancelled.

When the paid plan is worth it

Credits are the meter here, not videos, so compare on those. Starter gives 2,100 a month at EUR 9 and Plus gives 6,700 at EUR 30 -- which works out at EUR 4.29 and EUR 4.48 per thousand credits. The middle tier is therefore not a volume discount at all; it costs marginally more per credit than the entry plan. Buy Plus because you need the throughput in a single month, not because it is better value, because it is not.

Pro at EUR 155 spans 17,600 to 59,000 credits depending on which step you take, so its per-credit cost ranges from worse than Starter to roughly half of it. That makes Pro the one tier where the specific step genuinely matters -- ask which allowance the price you are quoted actually buys. Every figure carries a struck-through list price (EUR 15, 50 and 255 monthly). A discount present on every read is a price, not an event.

Two cautions about the page itself rather than the product. Pippit prices in the visitor’s local currency -- our read returned euros, with one Dutch billing label on an otherwise English page -- so your figures may differ by country. And two reads on 3 August 2026 returned amounts that contradicted each other, which is why we re-checked on the 5th. Read the live page before you buy rather than trusting any quote, including this one.

The free tier gives recurring daily credits and needs no card, which is the safe way to calibrate your real credit burn.

The consistent buyer complaint on Trustpilot is that generations which failed still consumed credits — so run your hardest prompt, let it fail, and check whether the credits came back. That one test tells you more about your real cost per usable output than the price list does. Size on credits rather than videos, because credits are the meter: 2,100 a month on Starter, 6,700 on Plus, and 17,600 to 59,000 on Pro depending on which Pro step you buy.

A tool that charges per attempt rather than per result means your effective allowance is your success rate times the headline. And keep your own records from day one — screenshots of charges, dates, what failed and what it cost. Refunds are reported as hard to obtain and support replies are described in weeks, so the time to assemble evidence is before you need it rather than after.
