Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 6 Sep 2026

The page opens on the Yearly tab, labelled -20% off, and every card carries a struck-through list price. Yearly: Free is €0 with recurring daily credits and no card; Starter €105 a year (list €170) for 2,100 credits a month; Plus €310 (list €515) for 6,700 a month; Pro is a four-step ladder rather than one price — 17,600 / 23,500 / 35,500 / 59,000 credits a month at €770 / €1,025 / €1,540 / €2,565 a year, against list prices of €1,285 / €1,710 / €2,565 / €4,280, with the 35,500 step shown by default.

Monthly: Starter €14 (list €22, marked 36% off), Plus €39 (list €70, 44% off) and four Pro amounts of €100, €105, €155 and €255 against lists of €179, €170, €255 and €430 — note that on the monthly tab the list price does not rise in step with the allowance. Beside each card the page prints its own credits-per-euro ratio — EUR1=2.4 on Starter, 2.59 on Plus and 2.77 on Pro — while the same card's figures work out at 240, 259 and 277 credits per euro on yearly billing, so the printed unit is a hundredfold off the arithmetic beside it.

The ordering is the same either way: the rate improves as you go up. Against our 5 August 2026 euro reading the two lower tiers have gone up: Starter from €9 to €14 a month and Plus from €30 to €39, with their list prices raised from €15 to €22 and from €50 to €70. pippit.ai/llms.txt names the four plans and the credit allowances but no amounts, pointing to the pricing page for current figures. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

What the price did

Real movement between two readings a month apart, both from a Netherlands exit and both in euros. On 5 August 2026 Starter was €9 a month against a struck-through €15, and Plus €30 against €50. On 6 September 2026 Starter is €14 against €22 and Plus €39 against €70 — a rise of 56% on Starter and 30% on Plus, with the list prices raised alongside the discounted ones. What buyers report about Pippit

Recurring themes from the buyer-review sites listed at the foot of this page, read in August 2026. We have not run Pippit ourselves, so this is other buyers' experience rather than ours. We summarise it here because the complaints are usually the part a vendor page leaves out.

What they praise
  • • Turning a product link into ready-to-post marketing videos is the part reviewers consistently call genuinely fast.
  • • The free tier lets you try it without a card, with credits that refresh on a schedule.
  • • The breadth in one place — video, avatars, talking photos, posters, scheduling — is what keeps reviewers on it despite the complaints.
What they complain about
  • • Failed generations still consume credits: Trustpilot reviewers describe burning an entire allowance without one usable video.
  • • Voice synchronisation is the most-named quality failure on avatar and talking-photo output.
  • • Support is slow — one reviewer waited two and a half weeks on a promised 3-5 business-day response — and refunds are described as hard to get.
  • • Third-party AI models have been removed from the platform without notice, which reviewers cite as a trust issue.
  • • Some report charges after they believed they had cancelled.

When the paid plan is worth it

Credits are the meter here, not videos, so compare on those — but the comparison has flipped since our August reading, and older write-ups, ours among them, have it backwards. On the yearly tab, which is the one the page opens on, Starter is €105 a year for 2,100 credits a month, Plus €310 for 6,700 and Pro €1,540 for 35,500. That is €4.17, €3.86 and €3.61 per thousand credits: the rate now improves at every step up, and the page's own ratio beside the three cards moves the same way, printed as EUR1=2.4, 2.59 and 2.77 — a hundredfold off the 240, 259 and 277 credits per euro its own figures give, but pointing in the same direction.

On the monthly tab the same ordering holds, at €6.67 and €5.82 per thousand for Starter and Plus. The middle tier is no longer the bad deal it was on 5 August 2026, when Starter at €9 beat Plus at €30 on price per credit. What did not improve is the absolute level. Starter went from €9 to €14 a month between those two readings and Plus from €30 to €39, and the struck-through list prices rose with them, from €15 to €22 and from €50 to €70.

A discount present on two reads a month apart is a price rather than an event, and a list price that climbs alongside the discounted one is a price rise wearing the same clothes. Pro is where the specific step decides the bill, because it is four prices and not one: 17,600, 23,500, 35,500 and 59,000 credits a month at €770, €1,025, €1,540 and €2,565 a year. Ask which allowance the figure you are quoted actually buys.

On the monthly tab the four Pro list prices — €179, €170, €255 and €430 — do not rise in step with the allowance, so check the arithmetic on your own step rather than assuming the ladder is ordered. One caution about the page rather than the product: Pippit prices in the visitor's currency, so a reader outside the euro zone will see different figures. Two reads on 6 September 2026 returned identical amounts, unlike 3 August 2026 when two reads on the same day contradicted each other — but open the live page before you buy rather than trusting any quote, this one included.

The free tier gives recurring daily credits and needs no card, which is the safe way to calibrate your real credit burn.

How to actually use Pippit

Read the live page in your own country's currency before you buy, and trust no quoted figure including ours. Pippit prices in the visitor's local currency, and two reads on the same day in August returned amounts that contradicted each other. Two reads on 6 September 2026 agreed exactly — but by then the euro prices had risen since August, Starter from €9 to €14 a month and Plus from €30 to €39.

This is a page to open yourself. Then test the failure mode before you spend anything, using the free tier's recurring daily credits, which need no card. The consistent buyer complaint on Trustpilot is that generations which failed still consumed credits — so run your hardest prompt, let it fail, and check whether the credits came back. That one test tells you more about your real cost per usable output than the price list does.

Size on credits rather than videos, because credits are the meter: 2,100 a month on Starter, 6,700 on Plus, and 17,600 to 59,000 on Pro depending on which Pro step you buy. A tool that charges per attempt rather than per result means your effective allowance is your success rate times the headline. And keep your own records from day one — screenshots of charges, dates, what failed and what it cost.

Refunds are reported as hard to obtain and support replies are described in weeks, so the time to assemble evidence is before you need it rather than after.
