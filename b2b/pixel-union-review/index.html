What Pixel Union does

Premium Shopify themes and store apps for online retailers: professionally designed, conversion-focused storefronts without custom development.

Pricing (2026)

Premium Shopify themes at a one-time price — Pixel Union themes around $300–$340 and Out of the Sandbox themes (Turbo, Flex) roughly $350–$495, paid once rather than a subscription (2026). Promotional discounts appear often; verify current theme prices on their site. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

The honest knock

A premium theme is a strong starting point, not a finished store. The conversion lift comes from how you configure, populate and optimize it — a $350 theme on a store with weak product pages or slow images still converts weakly. Heavily customized premium themes can also be more work to keep updated and can add page weight if you switch on every feature. Buy it for the design system and speed-to-launch, not as a magic conversion switch.

What they praise
  • • One-time purchase with a lifetime licence — no subscription, which against app-based page builders is the whole argument. Flagship themes rate 94-99% positive.
  • • Trusted by over 100,000 merchants, with Klaviyo, Afterpay and Klarna compatibility built in. Empire is the pick for large catalogues, Atlantic for brand-led mid-size stores.
What they complain about
  • • One licence per store. Running several brands means buying it again each time, and at $220-$420 a theme that adds up fast.
  • • The newer themes sit at the top of that range — Pluma $420, Inova $400, Nook $390.
  • • A theme is a starting point, not a finished store. Budget for the customisation you will want on top.

Frequently asked questions

Is Pixel Union worth it for a small team?

It depends on whether you have the bottleneck it solves. Small teams get the most out of this category when one clear problem is already costing real hours or revenue; buying ahead of that just adds cost and another login. Price it against the hours or lost deals it removes, not against its feature list, and start on the smallest plan that covers the job.

Who should not use Pixel Union?

Skip it if a free or low-cost Shopify theme already covers your needs, or you have a developer building a bespoke storefront anyway. Buying a tool to fix a problem you don't have yet just adds cost and another login to manage.
