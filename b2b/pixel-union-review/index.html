What the price does not buy is a finished store, and that is where the money actually goes. How to actually use Pixel Union

Buy the theme for the design system and the speed to launch, not as a conversion switch. A $360 theme on a store with weak product photography, thin descriptions and slow images still converts weakly -- the lift comes from how you configure, populate and optimise it, and that work happens after the purchase rather than instead of it. So plan that work before you buy: real photography, real copy, and a page-speed check once the theme is populated with your actual catalogue rather than demo content.

Heavily customised premium themes can add page weight if you switch on every feature, so turn features on deliberately and measure after each one. Establish how updates work before you customise heavily. A theme you have edited extensively is more work to keep current, and that cost arrives months later when you have forgotten what you changed -- keep a written note of every customisation as you make it. Frequently asked questions

Do I pay again for Pixel Union theme updates?

No. The theme pages (read 27 September 2026) list free theme updates and free customer support with every Pixel Union theme, so the one-time price, $100 for Startup up to $360 for Empire or Tailor, is the whole theme cost. The optional $96-a-year Priority Support plan, read on 7 September, is the only recurring charge in the group. What updates do cost is time if you have edited the theme's code, since each update has to be reconciled with your changes.

Does a theme preset cost extra?

No. The store's product data (read 27 September 2026) prices every preset at the theme's own price: Empire, Circuit, Depot and Forma are all $360, and Startup's four presets are all $100. A preset is a starting style, not a separate product, so choose the theme for its structure and the preset for its look.

Can I try a Pixel Union theme before paying?

Yes. Each theme page (read 27 September 2026) carries a 'Try theme for free' button next to the price, alongside several demo presets to compare. Do that trial with your real catalogue and images rather than the demo content, since page weight and layout fit are what decide whether it works for you.

What should I weigh before buying Growth & Revenue software?

It depends on whether you have the bottleneck it solves. Small teams get the most out of this category when one clear problem is already costing real hours or revenue; buying ahead of that just adds cost and another login. Price it against the hours or lost deals it removes, not against its feature list, and start on the smallest plan that covers the job.
