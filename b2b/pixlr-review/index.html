Pixlr Review (2026)Cheap Plans, Credits Reset Each Renewal

By Daan Haket · Tested hands-on · written with an AI assistant · who does what

Pixlr does not refund a processed payment, and monthly AI credits do not accumulate: they are reset to the plan allowance at each renewal and fall to zero when you cancel.

Best for

people who edit photos and make quick social and marketing images in the browser and want a cheap ad-free editor, with optional AI image, video and voice generation on the same account across web, desktop and mobile.

Skip it if

you need a professional editor for print or colour-critical work, you want generative output on the best models in volume at a predictable cost, or you need a refund route if the tool does not suit you.

Pixlr has a free version, which its FAQ describes as usable without an account or charges, carrying ads and a limit on daily free saves whose number the site does not publish. Paid plans cover the web, desktop and mobile apps with one account. Plus is €2.49 a month, or €1.99 a month billed yearly: no ads, unlimited saves, one AI generation at a time and 80 AI credits a month (960 for the year on yearly billing).

Premium is €9.99 a month, or €7.99 billed yearly: all image, video and audio models, four concurrent generations, 1,000 AI credits a month (12,000 for the year), private mode and the full library of fonts, templates and elements. Ultra starts at €24.99 a month, or €19.99 yearly, with up to 10,000 credits a month (120,000 for the year), unlimited fast image generations subject to fair use, eight concurrent generations, a priority queue and extended high-resolution exports; Ultra MAX doubles the credits at €49.99 a month or €39.99 yearly.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

The credit estimates are generous for fast models and much thinner for the better ones; Premium's 1,000 credits are about 200 pro images by Pixlr's own table. Ultra's unlimited fast generation is subject to fair use, and the page says availability is not guaranteed. The free version carries ads and a daily save limit Pixlr does not quantify. Upgrading is not possible on a PayPal subscription. Prices here were read in euros; buyers elsewhere see their own currency.

We have not tested Pixlr hands-on. The pricing FAQ and the Terms of Use contradict each other on credits. The FAQ says credit packs never expire; the terms, last updated 13 May 2026, say purchased credits are valid for one year from purchase and then expire automatically, and are non-refundable. The pricing page says yearly subscribers get all their credits valid for a year; the terms say all plan credits are provided monthly and any unused at the end of the month expire.

Until Pixlr aligns the two, plan as if the stricter terms apply: use packs within a year and do not count on carrying yearly credits from one month to the next. The terms also let Pixlr change the price, package or features of a plan at any time, with or without notice. Publishing AI-generated content on Pixlr's platform grants Pixlr an irrevocable, royalty-free, worldwide licence to it, so keep client work you have not released off its community features. Disputes go to arbitration in Singapore, where Pixlr Pte Ltd is based.

What Pixlr does

Browser, desktop and mobile photo editor with AI image, video and voice generation; a free version with ads and paid plans that add ad-free editing, AI credits and a template library.

Plus buys no ads, Premium buys the AI

The two lower plans answer different needs. Plus at €2.49 a month removes the ads and the daily save limit and gives a token 80 AI credits, which the page reckons at about 80 fast images or 4 fast videos. If you mostly edit photos and make collages, that is the plan. Premium at €9.99 is the first plan with every image, video and audio model and 1,000 credits a month, about 200 images on the better pro models by the page's own estimate, plus the template and font library.

Ultra is for people generating in volume: from €24.99 a month, unlimited fast images under a fair-use clause, 8 parallel generations and a priority queue. Yearly billing takes about 20% off every tier and changes how credits behave: you get the whole year's credits valid for a year instead of a monthly allowance that is reset at each renewal. That suits uneven use. Do not overbuy on monthly billing, because unused monthly credits do not carry over and disappear entirely if you cancel; credit packs are the only credits that never expire.

Per credit, the ladder rewards volume. Plus works out at about 3.1 euro cents a credit (€2.49 for 80), Premium at 1 cent (€9.99 for 1,000) and Ultra at 0.5 cents (€24.99 for 5,000). Ultra MAX is €49.99 for 10,000, exactly the same price per credit as Ultra, so MAX buys volume, not a discount. The real jump is Premium to Ultra: 2.5 times the price for 5 times the credits, plus unlimited fast images under fair use, 8 parallel generations and the priority queue.

How to actually use Pixlr

Start on the free version in the browser; the FAQ says it needs no account. You will see ads and run into a daily save limit, which tells you quickly whether you use Pixlr often enough to pay. If you mostly need ad-free editing, Plus is the step. If you want the AI generators, estimate your month with the page's own table: Premium's 1,000 credits are about 1,000 fast images but only about 200 pro images or 50 fast videos.

Buy on the web with a card if you may want to upgrade later: the FAQ says upgrades are not possible on PayPal subscriptions, and app-store purchases are managed and refunded only by the app store. Pixlr itself does not refund a processed payment, so start with one month rather than a year. Schools can apply for free education access. Schools and education NGOs should not buy a plan first.

Pixlr for Education is free: register and verify a Pixlr account with an email on the school's own domain, have one staff representative fill in the application form, and allow 48 hours for the school to be registered. The knowledge base says the upgrade lasts 12 months, after which the school is offered 20% off Premium. The education page lists unlimited teacher and student registrations on a whitelisted domain, and the terms let teachers consent on behalf of students.

If you pay, use a Visa or Mastercard rather than PayPal when you might move up a tier, and cancel in your Subscriptions page at least a day before renewal: the terms confirm auto-renewal and no refunds for unused time.

Frequently asked questions

Can you export your data from Pixlr?

Do unused Pixlr AI credits roll over?

Not on monthly billing. Pixlr's FAQ, read 12 and 28 October 2026, says subscription credits are topped back up to the plan allowance at each renewal, so 863 left on Premium becomes 1,000, not 1,863, and they drop to zero if you cancel. Yearly plans are sold with the whole year's credits valid for a year, but the Terms of Use say plan credits expire monthly. Credit packs are used after the subscription credits; see the next question on how long they last.

Can I get a refund on a Pixlr subscription?

Pixlr says no. Its FAQ states it cannot process a refund once a payment has been processed, and subscriptions bought in a mobile app are refunded only through that app store. You can cancel at any time and keep premium features until the period ends. Read 12 October 2026.

Is Pixlr free to use?

Yes, with limits. The free version needs no account but shows ads and caps the number of saves per day; the site does not publish the number. Plus removes ads and the save limit from €2.49 a month (€1.99 yearly), read from a Netherlands connection on 12 October 2026; the English home page says plans start at $1.99.

Can I upgrade a Pixlr plan I bought with PayPal?

Not by yourself. The FAQ on pixlr.com/pricing, read on 24 October 2026, says plan upgrades are only available for credit or debit card purchases, not via PayPal; if you bought with PayPal, Pixlr asks you to email info@pixlr.com and its team will assist. If you expect to move up a tier later, paying by card avoids that support step.

Do Pixlr credit packs expire?

Pixlr's own pages disagree. The pricing FAQ (read 28 October 2026) says pack credits never expire and are used after subscription credits; the Terms of Use (13 May 2026) say purchased credits are valid for one year from purchase and then expire automatically. Use packs within a year to be safe.

Do yearly Pixlr credits last the whole year?

The pricing page says yes: on yearly billing all credits are valid for one year (12,000 on Premium). The Terms of Use say plan credits are provided monthly and expire at the end of each month. Ask Pixlr in writing before relying on a year-long balance.

Is Pixlr free for schools?

Yes, through Pixlr for Education: an ad-free upgrade for educators, students and education NGOs on a whitelisted school domain, approved within about 48 hours of the application. It lasts 12 months; after that the school is offered 20% off Premium (knowledge base, read 28 October 2026).

How can I pay for Pixlr?

On the web by Visa or Mastercard credit or debit card, or PayPal; in the mobile apps through the app store. Pixlr does not accept cash cards and arranges other methods via info@pixlr.com. Only card subscriptions can be upgraded in place.
