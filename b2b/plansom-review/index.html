What Plansom does

AI-assisted planning for tasks, goals and team collaboration, with a free Starter tier that keeps tasks unlimited. Pro is about $14.99/month or $149.99 a year. A smaller player next to Asana and ClickUp, with fewer integrations and a smaller ecosystem around it.

Pricing (2026)

Free Starter with unlimited tasks; Pro is about $14.99/mo or $149.99/yr (2026). Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

The honest knock

It's a smaller player next to Asana, ClickUp and Motion — strong value, but fewer integrations and a smaller ecosystem.

Recurring themes from Capterra, Tekpon and SoftwareWorld, read in August 2026. We have not run Plansom ourselves, so this is other buyers' experience rather than ours. We summarise it here because the complaints are usually the part a vendor page leaves out.

What they praise
  • • 99% of ratings are five stars, and reviewers describe the product itself as excellent with a genuinely good user experience.
  • • A free Starter plan with unlimited tasks and goal-setting, then Pro at $14.99/month or $149.99 a year.
What they complain about
  • • Plan management is where it falls down, and reviewers are blunt: subscription prices rose twice in two months, and the company keeps changing what each plan includes — removing features in a way that pushes users to upgrade.
  • • That instability is the complaint, not the software. If you need predictable cost, this is the risk to weigh.
  • • The free tier has a usage threshold rather than being genuinely unlimited.

Frequently asked questions

Does Plansom replace my current setup or sit on top of it?

Usually it sits on top rather than replacing everything. Tools in this class tend to become the layer where work is tracked and handed off, while your existing systems stay the source of truth for their own data. Before you buy, map which tools it would replace outright and which it would merely sync with. If the answer is that it syncs with all of them, you are adding a layer rather than removing one.

Who should not use Plansom?

Skip it if your team already lives in an established PM tool — switching costs outweigh the AI-planning extras. Buying a tool to fix a problem you don't have yet just adds cost and another login to manage.
