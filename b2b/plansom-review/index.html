How to actually use Plansom

Start by asking for the number, because the site does not have one. There is no pricing page on plansom.com, no pricing link in the navigation, and /pricing returns 404 — the only routes to a figure are signing up or asking. When you ask, split the question in two. A per-seat rate is the easy half. The half that decides the bill is the AI work: this is sold as agents that run tasks for you, so ask whether agent runs are included, capped or metered on top, and what a busy month costs once it goes over.

Get the security claims in writing at the same time. The homepage says SOC 2 Ready — which is not the same as SOC 2 certified — plus GDPR compliance and that your data is not used to train LLMs. Ask which of those are audited, and by whom. Then run it on real work before committing to a term. The product is now built around agents rather than task lists, and that is a change only your own material can judge.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Plansom cost?

Plansom does not publish a price. Re-checked 24 September 2026, plansom.com/pricing still returns a 404 and the site's own llms.txt lists no pricing page. Its terms (last updated 9 July 2025) say plan details and pricing are available on the website and that fees may change, with a change taking effect at the start of your next term and notice beforehand; in practice the figure is only visible after signing up or in a sales conversation. The Pro price of about $14.99 a month we recorded earlier no longer traces to the site, so do not budget on it. Ask for the monthly and annual figure, and what AI agent work costs, in writing.

Can I get my money back if Plansom does not fit?

Usually not. The terms, last updated 9 July 2025 and read 24 September 2026, say all payments are non-refundable, on monthly and annual plans alike. A monthly plan can be cancelled any time and stops at the end of the paid month, with no credit for the part-month. An annual plan is charged for the full year up front, and cancelling only stops the renewal; the terms say unused months are generally not refunded. Consumers in the EU or UK may have a 14-day cooling-off right on the first purchase, which can be lost once paid features are used. On a team monthly plan every active user licence is billed each month, used or not.

What happens to our data when we stop using Plansom?

It is deleted or returned, whichever you choose. Per the terms, read 24 September 2026, on termination or expiry, or on written request, Plansom will delete or return all customer personal data it processes for you and delete its copies, generally within 30 days. Data it must keep by law, or that sits in routine backups, is isolated from further processing until it can be deleted, and a formal certificate of deletion is available on request. The homepage, read 5 September 2026, also states that your data is not used to train LLMs, alongside SOC 2 Ready and GDPR compliance. Export your plans before the subscription ends.

What should I weigh before buying Operations & Workflow software?

Usually it sits on top rather than replacing everything. Tools in this class tend to become the layer where work is tracked and handed off, while your existing systems stay the source of truth for their own data. Before you buy, map which tools it would replace outright and which it would merely sync with. If the answer is that it syncs with all of them, you are adding a layer rather than removing one.
