What Postscript does

SMS marketing for Shopify stores: campaigns, automations and opt-in tools.

Pricing (2026)

(2026-08-01, read on their own pricing page) Starter is shown as $0 a month with a $49 monthly minimum spend, at $0.015 then $0.009 per SMS and $0.045 per MMS, plus carrier fees. Growth is $100 a month at $0.01 then $0.008 per SMS and $0.03 per MMS, plus carrier fees. Carrier fees are marked with an asterisk on every tier and are not quantified on the page. A free trial is offered. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

The honest knock

The $0 a month is not free — it carries a $49 monthly minimum spend, which means $49 is your floor whether you send anything or not. Read the tiers as what they are: a platform fee plus a per-message rate plus carrier fees that the page will not quantify. That third line matters more than it looks, because carrier fees are levied per message by the mobile networks and on a large send they are a real fraction of the bill. Before you compare this with anything, work out your monthly message volume and multiply it out on both tiers: Growth costs $100 more in platform fee but saves a fifth of a cent per message, so it pays back somewhere around 50,000 messages a month.

What buyers report about Postscript

Recurring themes from the Shopify App Store, G2, Capterra and Software Advice, read in August 2026. We have not run Postscript ourselves, so this is other buyers' experience rather than ours. We summarise it here because the complaints are usually the part a vendor page leaves out.

What they praise
  • • Built only for Shopify, and it shows — the data model and flows are native rather than adapted. Dr. Squatch, Brooklinen and True Classic run on it.
  • • The Starter plan is free with pay-per-message pricing, so you can start without a subscription.
What they complain about
  • • Support was replaced with AI, and reviewers say so directly — a January 2026 App Store review criticises exactly that, and a 2025 one describes nobody on chat and no email reply.
  • • Reviewers report being locked into long-term plans and charged after unsubscribing.
  • • Shopify only. If you add another storefront, this does not come with you.
  • • Growth is $25/month and Professional $500 on top of per-message costs.
Best for: Shopify stores with a list large enough that SMS beats email on response — the per-message economics only work once volume justifies the platform fee.
Who should skip it: if you are testing whether SMS works for you at all — the $49 minimum spend on the free tier means the experiment has a floor, and the carrier fees are an unknown you cannot price up front.

The natural comparison is Attentive or Klaviyo SMS — the direct SMS peers for ecommerce — compare total cost at your real monthly send volume, including carrier fees, not the platform fee. Decide by which one fits the job above, not by the louder brand.

Frequently asked questions

Is Postscript secure and reliable enough to depend on?

Judge it on evidence rather than adjectives. Look for a published security page, single sign-on and role controls on the plan you would actually buy (they are often gated to higher tiers), a status page with real incident history, and a clear answer on where your data is stored. If the tool will hold anything sensitive, those four answers matter more than any feature comparison.

Who should not use Postscript?

Skip it if you are testing whether SMS works for you at all — the $49 minimum spend on the free tier means the experiment has a floor, and the carrier fees are an unknown you cannot price up front. Buying a tool to fix a problem you don't have yet just adds cost and another login to manage.
