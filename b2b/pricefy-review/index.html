A genuinely free tier: $0 for up to 50 SKUs, 5 competitors, daily price updates and AI automatch. What buyers report about Pricefy

Hands-on: tested 2026-11-19Reported by buyers: Capterra, SourceForge, SoftwareReviews and hands-on tests, read August 2026

Themes from the buyer-review sites listed at the foot of this page, read in August 2026. We tested Pricefy ourselves on 2026-11-19, but one test is a narrow sample, so here is what other buyers report. We summarise it here because the complaints are usually the part a vendor page leaves out.

What they praise
  • • A free-forever tier at 50 SKUs means you can check matching accuracy on your own catalogue before paying anything. In this category that is rare and genuinely useful.
  • • From $49/month against $99+ for most competitors, and live in minutes rather than a setup project.
What they complain about
  • • The step from free to paid is steep in what you get: 50 SKUs free, then 100 SKUs for $49. The tiers run to $499 for 25,000.
  • • Dynamic repricing and MAP monitoring, which many retailers consider the point, come only with higher tiers.
  • • MAP enforcement and multi-region pricing governance hit a ceiling fast. Enterprise buyers should look elsewhere.

When the paid plan is worth it

Pricefy's Free plan covers up to 50 SKUs, 5 tracked competitors, and one price check per day, which suits a small store that just wants basic competitor visibility without repricing automation. The concrete push toward paid comes fast: Starter, at $49/month ($37/month billed annually, a 25% discount), only raises the catalog cap to 100 SKUs, though it does add unlimited competitors and doubles price checks to two per day. Merchants who actually need automated repricing have to go further still, Dynamic Repricing sits on Pro ($99/month, up to 2,000 SKUs), and Autopilot Repricing with MAP/MSRP monitoring only appears on Business ($189/month, up to 15,000 SKUs).

What upgrading does not solve is match quality. The AI Automatch engine that pairs your SKUs to competitor listings is the same across every tier. Variants, bundles, refurbished listings and multipacks still need manual confirmation in the dashboard before repricing rules are safe to activate, regardless of how much you pay.

    How to actually use Pricefy

    Setup starts with a free signup that needs no credit card, then connecting a store, Pricefy offers one-click integrations for Shopify, WooCommerce, Magento, BigCommerce, PrestaShop and Wix, after which the catalog imports automatically through the connection or via API. Pricefy's marketing claims you can be live within 2 minutes of signup, but that refers to account creation, not full catalog matching. AI Automatch pairs SKUs to competitor listings first by EAN, GTIN, UPC or SKU codes, falling back to an AI/ML comparison of names, brands, descriptions and images when codes are missing.

    The documentation cites 95%+ match accuracy and one case of 8,000 SKUs matched against six competitors overnight. Where users actually get stuck is after matching: variants, bundles, refurbished listings and multipacks are prone to mismatches and need manual confirmation in the dashboard before any repricing rule is activated, and catalogs above 50 SKUs will hit the Free plan's cap mid-setup.

    What does Pricefy cost?

    Free for up to 50 SKUs and 5 competitors. Paid runs $49, $99, $189 and $499 a month billed monthly, or $37, $74, $142 and $374 billed yearly, a 25% saving. Read on 17 August 2026 and unchanged on 28 September 2026. Note the $49 Starter tier, which our earlier write-up skipped entirely.

    Which tier do I need for my catalogue?

    SKU count is the meter: 50 free, 100 on Starter, 2,000 on Pro, 15,000 on Business and 25,000 on Enterprise. Competitors become unlimited from Starter up, together with twice-daily updates, email alerts and Excel/CSV reports; the free tier stays at 5 competitors and one daily update. If you are under 50 SKUs the free tier is a real plan rather than a trial, which is unusual in this category.

    What is the difference between dynamic and autopilot repricing?

    Dynamic repricing arrives on Pro and lets you set rules that adjust your prices against competitors. Autopilot, on Business, runs those adjustments without you approving each one, and that tier is also where MAP and MSRP monitoring lives, the compliance side, if you sell brands that police minimum pricing.

    Can I really cancel Pricefy anytime, as the pricing page says?

    Not quite. Pricefy's terms of service (read 16 November 2026) say an annual subscription needs 30 days' notice before the renewal date and a monthly one 7 days' notice before its renewal date, and that subscriptions renew automatically. The same terms call all fees nonrefundable except where the terms say otherwise or the law requires it, and promise at least 14 days' notice before a fee change. So put the cancellation in your calendar a month ahead on a yearly plan; "cancel anytime" on the pricing page does not mean money back for the rest of the term.

    What happens if my catalogue outgrows the plan, or I switch plans mid-cycle?

    The pricing FAQ (read 16 November 2026) says Pricefy warns you as you near the SKU limit, then you either upgrade or remove products, and that monitoring is not interrupted. Upgrades charge only the prorated difference; on a downgrade the remaining credit goes to your next billing cycle rather than back to your card. Keep your own copy of the data as well: the terms say you own your data but that Pricefy is not responsible for backups or recovery, and Excel/email reports are listed from Starter up, not on the Free card.
