What Printify does

Global print-on-demand network. Create custom products and automate your e-commerce fulfillment without holding inventory.

Pricing (2026)

A free plan ($0 — you pay only product base cost plus shipping per order); Premium about $39/mo monthly or ~$24.99/mo annual (up to ~20% off catalog costs), plus custom Enterprise (2026). Premium breaks even around 16-17 orders/month. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

Printify makes nothing until you sell — the Premium fee only pays off past a break-even order volume, and because it's a middleman marketplace, print quality and shipping vary by the provider you pick. Order samples first.

What buyers report about Printify

Recurring themes from Trustpilot and print-on-demand practitioner reviews, read in August 2026. We have not run Printify ourselves, so this is other buyers' experience rather than ours. We summarise it here because the complaints are usually the part a vendor page leaves out.

What they praise
  • • The marketplace model means providers compete on price, and that is why the base costs undercut single-factory competitors.
  • • Wide catalogue and easy store integration — for testing designs without inventory it does the job.
What they complain about
  • • Quality depends entirely on which provider you pick, and it varies. Reviewers report faded colours, off-centre prints, rough hand feel and cracking after a few washes from the cheaper providers. Order samples from your specific provider, not from Printify generally.
  • • Delivery runs 5-10 business days in the US and 7-14 in Europe — slower than customers expect, and shipping delays are a top cause of negative reviews for POD sellers.
  • • Premium went from $29 to $39 a month in February 2026, a 35% rise.
  • • Reviewers report being charged $300 for annual plans they did not intend to renew, with refunds hard to obtain.
Best for: creators and stores testing print-on-demand with no upfront cost, upgrading to Premium once volume justifies it.

Frequently asked questions

Is Printify worth it for a small team?

It depends on whether you have the bottleneck it solves. Small teams get the most out of this category when one clear problem is already costing real hours or revenue; buying ahead of that just adds cost and another login. Price it against the hours or lost deals it removes, not against its feature list, and start on the smallest plan that covers the job.

Who should not use Printify?

Skip it if you sell only a handful of items a month — the free plan is right, and Premium's fee outweighs the discount. Buying a tool to fix a problem you don't have yet just adds cost and another login to manage.
