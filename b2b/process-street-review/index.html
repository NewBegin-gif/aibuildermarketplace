What Process Street does

Turn your recurring work into simple, trackable checklists and SOPs. AI-powered workflows, approvals and conditional logic — so your team runs smoothly even when you're not in the room.

Pricing (2026)

Startup from about $100/mo (up to 5 members, businesses under 15 staff), Pro custom (roughly the $1,500/mo range), plus Enterprise; example per-member rates run ~$12.50-15/member/mo, often with a 5-user minimum (2026). Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

Pricing is opaque and jumps steeply from Startup to Pro, with seat minimums that push the real cost above the headline per-member rate — you'll need a sales call to price it. Best once recurring checklists are core to how you operate.

How to actually use Process Street

The shortest route to real value is the AI Document Importer. Process Street's help docs describe clicking + New in the top right corner, choosing Workflow, then Import Document, dropping in an existing SOP and clicking Generate Workflow. The importer accepts PDF, DOCX, HTML, CSV and image files, with a file size limit of 25 MB and an image limit of 20 MB, and returns tasks with form fields, approvals, stop tasks and role assignments rather than a flat checklist. Every new account starts on a complimentary 14-day trial of the Pro plan with no credit card, so the sensible move is to import a process people run and push a live run through it inside that window; the vendor's own starter lesson budgets 10 minutes to create a workflow. The usual stall is seats and permissions: invitations sit under the profile menu at Settings, Users & Guests, the Startup tier includes 5 users and 10 guests, and the docs state that once a limit is reached each extra person is billed pro-rata at the moment of invitation. Distilled from Process Street pricing page and vendor help documentation (AI Document Importer, getting-started lesson, invitations), read 4 August 2026.

Frequently asked questions

Does Process Street replace my current setup or sit on top of it?

Usually it sits on top rather than replacing everything. Tools in this class tend to become the layer where work is tracked and handed off, while your existing systems stay the source of truth for their own data. Before you buy, map which tools it would replace outright and which it would merely sync with. If the answer is that it syncs with all of them, you are adding a layer rather than removing one.

Who should not use Process Street?

Skip it if you just need to write down a few SOPs — a doc or a cheaper tool captures them without the platform cost. Buying a tool to fix a problem you don't have yet just adds cost and another login to manage.
