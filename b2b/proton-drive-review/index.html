Proton Drive Review (2026)

IT & ProductivityResearched assessment · by Daniel HaketUpdated 2026-09-21

Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 26 Aug 2026

Proton Free gives 5 GB. Drive Plus is $3.99 a month for 200 GB, marked 20% off $4.99 and billed at $47.88 every twelve months. Proton Unlimited is $9.99 for 500 GB and folds in the VPN, Mail and Calendar, billed at $119.88 a year.

Frequently asked questions

What does Proton Drive cost?

Read on 17 August 2026 from a Dutch address, so in euros: Free gives 5 GB; Drive Plus is €3.99 a month on annual billing (€47.88 a year) for 200 GB; Proton Unlimited is €9.99 (€119.88) for 500 GB plus VPN, Mail, Calendar and Pass. The page switches between CHF, EUR and USD, so your figure depends on where you buy.

Which plan makes sense if two of us need the storage?

Proton Duo, which our previous write-up missed entirely: 2 TB shared across up to 2 users at €14.99 a month on annual billing (€179.88 a year), against €9.99 for Unlimited's 500 GB for one. For a household of more, Family gives 3 TB across up to 6 users at €23.99. For a business it is Drive Professional at €7.99 per user for 1 TB each.

Is the free tier usable?

It is 5 GB, which is enough to try the encryption and the document editor and not much else — a phone's photo roll will fill it. What you do get free is the full end-to-end encryption and the online document and spreadsheet editor, so it works as a genuine trial of the privacy model rather than a crippled demo.

What should I weigh before buying IT & Productivity software?

Judge it on evidence rather than adjectives. Look for a published security page, single sign-on and role controls on the plan you would actually buy (they are often gated to higher tiers), a status page with real incident history, and a clear answer on where your data is stored. If the tool will hold anything sensitive, those four answers matter more than any feature comparison.
