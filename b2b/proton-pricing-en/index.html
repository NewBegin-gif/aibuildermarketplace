Proton pricing starts at $3.99/month for Mail Plus, $9.99/month for Unlimited (most popular), and $19.99/month for Duo. Annual billing offers 20% savings. The free tier supports 1 user with 1GB storage, while paid plans include VPN, Drive, Calendar, and Pass with up to 500GB storage.

FAQ

How much does Proton cost per month?

Proton Mail Plus costs $3.99/month, Unlimited is $9.99/month, and Duo is $19.99/month when billed annually. Monthly billing increases costs by 25%. The free tier includes basic email with 1GB storage and limited features.

What's included in Proton Unlimited vs Mail Plus?

Mail Plus includes only email with 15GB storage for $3.99/month. Unlimited adds Proton VPN, Drive, Calendar, and Pass for $9.99/month with 500GB storage, making it better value for most users.

Is there a free version of Proton?

Yes, Proton offers a permanent free tier with 1GB email storage, basic encryption, and limited VPN access. However, it lacks custom domains, advanced features, and sufficient storage for business use.

How much does Proton Business cost?

Proton Business starts at $6.99 per user monthly when billed annually. It includes 15GB storage per user, custom domains, admin controls, and priority support. Volume discounts apply for 50+ users.

Can I upgrade or downgrade Proton plans anytime?

Yes, you can change Proton plans instantly through your account settings. Upgrades take effect immediately with prorated billing, while downgrades apply at your next billing cycle to prevent data loss.

What Each Plan Actually Includes

Free Tier - $0/month

The free plan provides basic encrypted email with serious limitations. You get 1GB storage (roughly 1,000 emails), 150 messages daily, and basic encryption. The VPN includes 1 connection to 3 countries with medium speed. No custom domains, limited folders (3), and Proton branding on emails.

Best for: Testing Proton's interface and basic privacy needs. Not suitable for business or heavy email users.

Mail Plus - $3.99/month Annual

Mail Plus focuses purely on email enhancement. You get 15GB storage (15,000+ emails), unlimited messages, 10 email addresses, and custom domain support. Advanced features include auto-reply, email scheduling, and unlimited folders/filters. Still no VPN, Drive, or other apps.

Best for: Users who only need secure email with professional features but use other services for VPN/storage.

Unlimited - $9.99/month Annual (Most Popular)

Unlimited provides the full Proton ecosystem. Email gets 500GB storage with all Mail Plus features. VPN includes 10 connections, 1,900+ servers worldwide, and streaming support. Proton Drive offers secure cloud storage, Calendar handles encrypted scheduling, and Pass manages passwords with unlimited logins.

Best for: Privacy-conscious individuals wanting complete digital security without managing multiple subscriptions.

Business - $6.99/user/month Annual

Business plans add administrative controls essential for companies. Each user gets 15GB email storage, custom domains, and centralized billing. Admin dashboard manages user permissions, enforces security policies, and provides usage analytics. Priority support includes phone assistance during business hours.

Best for: Companies needing centralized control, compliance features, and professional support.

Hidden Costs Analysis

Setup and Migration Costs

Proton doesn't charge setup fees, but migration costs exist. Custom domain setup is free, but DNS configuration might require technical expertise ($100-500 consulting). Email migration from Gmail/Outlook takes 2-8 hours of manual work or $200-800 for professional services.

Storage Overages

Unlike some competitors, Proton doesn't allow storage overages. When you hit limits, new emails bounce until you upgrade or delete content. This "hard limit" prevents surprise bills but can disrupt business operations.

Add-on Costs

  • Additional Storage: Not available as add-on; requires plan upgrade
  • Extra VPN Connections: Fixed per plan; no individual add-ons
  • Priority Support: Only available with Business plans
  • Advanced Features: Bundled in plans; no à la carte options

Annual Billing Requirements

Significant discounts require annual commitment. Monthly billing costs 25% more, and there's no quarterly option. Annual plans auto-renew at full price after promotional periods end.

Enterprise Negotiation Strategies

Volume Discounts

Proton offers tiered discounts: 10% for 50+ users, 15% for 100+ users, and 20%+ for 500+ users. Enterprise deals (1000+ users) can reach 30-40% off list price with multi-year commitments.

Contract Terms to Negotiate

  • Payment Terms: Request NET 30 instead of upfront annual payment
  • Price Protection: Lock rates for 2-3 years against increases
  • Pilot Programs: 30-60 day free trials for large deployments
  • Professional Services: Include migration assistance in large deals
  • SLA Guarantees: 99.9% uptime with service credits for violations

Timing Your Purchase

Best negotiation periods are end of quarter (March, June, September, December) and end of fiscal year. Sales teams have more flexibility to offer concessions during these periods.

Cost Optimization Strategies

Right-Size Your Plan

Start with Mail Plus if you only need email, then upgrade when requiring VPN/storage. Don't pay for unused features – Proton's modular approach lets you upgrade smoothly.

Annual Billing Savings

Annual billing saves 20% across all plans. For businesses, this represents $2,000+ savings annually on 100-user deployments.

Family Plan Optimization

Family plans cost less than 3 individual Unlimited subscriptions. Small businesses can use Family plans if they don't need administrative controls, saving $30-60 monthly.

Storage Management

Monitor storage usage monthly. Large attachments consume space quickly – use Proton Drive links instead of email attachments to preserve email storage.

Free Tier: When It Works vs When to Upgrade

Free Tier Works For:

  • Personal email with light usage (under 100 emails monthly)
  • Testing Proton's interface and features
  • Secondary/backup email account
  • Basic privacy needs without business requirements

Upgrade Triggers:

  • Storage: Hitting 1GB limit (approximately 1,000 emails)
  • Professional Use: Needing custom domain (@yourcompany.com)
  • Productivity: Requiring folders, filters, or auto-reply
  • Integration: Wanting VPN, cloud storage, or password manager
