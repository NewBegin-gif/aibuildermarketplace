Proton Review (2026)

By Daan Haket · Researched and written with an AI assistant · who does what

Storage is the axis, and the jump from Drive Plus to Unlimited is not really about storage at all: you go from 200 GB to 500 GB, but you also start paying for Mail, Calendar, Pass and VPN whether or not you wanted them.

Best for

teams and individuals for whom encryption is an actual requirement rather than a preference — handling client material, legal or medical files, or anything where a provider being able to read your data is itself the problem.

Skip it if

you mainly need cheap capacity or deep collaboration features, where the mainstream drives give more space and better co-editing per euro, or you would be paying for the Mail and VPN half of the bundle without using it.

Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 11 Oct 2026

Mail Plus is $3.99 a month, marked 20% off $4.99 and billed at $47.88 every twelve months, for 15 GB, ten addresses and one custom domain. Unlimited is $9.99 a month, 23% off $12.99, billed at $119.88 a year, for 500 GB, fifteen addresses, three domains and the VPN, password manager and Drive storage. Those are the default figures an American is shown; the page also has a currency selector, and picking euros gives euro amounts rather than a conversion of these.

Frequently asked questions

What happens if I downgrade with more data than the new plan allows?

Proton's support article 'What happens if my usage exceeds the limits of my plan?' (proton.me/support/free-plan-limits, read 11 October 2026) says your access is limited rather than your data removed straight away. In Mail you cannot receive new email except from Proton, cannot send attachments and cannot manage your calendar; Drive stops syncing and uploading. For a former business or family plan, added existing accounts are removed from the organisation, created members drop to 0 MB and a custom domain is disabled. If you are still over the limit after 12 months, the data in the affected apps can be deleted.

Can I take my mail with me if I leave?

Yes, on every plan. The Proton Mail Export Tool article (proton.me/support/proton-mail-export-tool, read 11 October 2026) says the tool is available for everyone and exports all your emails with metadata as EML and JSON files, which clients such as Thunderbird or Outlook can import. Using Proton in a desktop mail client over IMAP/SMTP is a paid feature: the Mail pricing table (read 11 October 2026) lists it for Mail Plus and Unlimited, not Free.

Does the free plan limit how much I can send?

Yes. The Mail pricing comparison (proton.me/mail/pricing, read 11 October 2026) lists 150 messages a day on Free against unlimited on Mail Plus and Unlimited, plus 3 folders, 3 labels and 1 custom filter on Free. Attachments are capped at 25 MB on all three plans.
