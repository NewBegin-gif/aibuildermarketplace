This is priced per channel and then added up, which is the part worth understanding before you compare it with anything. What PushOwl does

Shopify web-push and omnichannel marketing, now part of Brevo. One-click push lets stores re-engage shoppers who never leave an email — recovering abandoned carts and browsing sessions — then layer in email, SMS and WhatsApp on Brevo's deliverability stack. Usage-based pricing: you pay for what you send, not list size.

When the paid plan is worth it

PushOwl's free Basic Bundle covers stores sending up to 500 web push notifications and 500 emails a month with unlimited subscribers, which is enough for a small or early-stage Shopify store testing push and newsletter campaigns. The concrete trigger to upgrade is push volume: once notifications exceed 500/month you need the $19/month Plus Bundle (10,000-30,000 pushes; $15 a month billed yearly) or the $79/month Power Bundle ($71 billed yearly) for higher volume and features like browse abandonment and segmentation.

What upgrading the push bundle does not solve is email and SMS capacity. Those run through a separate Brevo account with its own scaling: emails climb toward $300 for 250,000 sends, and SMS starts at $7.50 for 750 credits. So paying more for Power buys bigger push limits and automation, not more email or SMS volume.

How to actually use PushOwl

Installation itself is fast. PushOwl's help center lists three steps to add the app from the Shopify App Store — go to the app listing, click Add app, then click Install App — with no verification needed to start collecting web push subscribers via the on-site opt-in prompt. The step that gates deliverable email and SMS campaigns is domain authentication: you connect a Brevo account, then add a Brevo code, DKIM record, and DMARC record to your domain's DNS.

Brevo warns this can take up to 48 hours to propagate; skipping it means mail goes out from a shared @brevosend.com address with worse deliverability, and PushOwl notes authentication has been mandatory for senders since February 1, 2024. SMS has a separate compliance step: toll-free numbers used to text US or Canadian recipients need carrier verification, which PushOwl's help center says can take up to 5 business days, and delivery is blocked until it clears.

In practice, the DNS wait and the SMS verification window are where new users stall between installing the app and sending their first fully authenticated campaign.

Frequently asked questions

What does PushOwl cost?

It is priced per channel and then added up, which is the part to understand before comparing it with anything. Read on 16 September and again on 4 October 2026 on pushowl.com/pricing, where each bundle card shows three channel prices and their sum. Plus totals $19.00 a month — email $0 for 500, web push $19 for 10,000, SMS $0 for none. Power totals $79.00 a month — email $0 for 500, web push $79 for 25,000, SMS $0 for none. Does that monthly price include email and SMS?

Barely, and this is where “omnichannel” gets expensive. In every bundle's default configuration — free, $19 and $79 alike — the included email allowance is 500 sends a month and the SMS allowance is zero credits. The number on the card is the web-push channel's price; email and SMS are separate rates that add to it. A store that wants push plus real email volume is therefore buying two channels, and the $19 or $79 is only the first of them. Push is what actually scales with the tier: up to 500 notifications a month on Basic, a 10,000–30,000 band on Plus, and 25,000 to unlimited on Power. Read 16 September 2026.

What does the free bundle actually do?

More than most free tiers. At $0 it carries 500 web push notifications and 500 emails a month with no cap on how many subscribers you collect, campaign sending and scheduling, price-drop and back-in-stock automation, and basic reports. What it does not carry is the recovery automation most people come here for: abandoned-cart automation starts on Plus, while flash-sale triggers, browse abandonment, segmentation, smart delivery and custom attribution windows start on Power. Read on pushowl.com/pricing, 16 September 2026. One structural caveat before you build on it: the app is Shopify-only, so none of this travels if you change platform.

What should I weigh before buying Growth & Revenue software?

It depends on whether you have the bottleneck it solves. Small teams get the most out of this category when one clear problem is already costing real hours or revenue; buying ahead of that just adds cost and another login. Price it against the hours or lost deals it removes, not against its feature list, and start on the smallest plan that covers the job.
