What PushOwl does

Shopify web-push and omnichannel marketing, now part of Brevo. One-click push lets stores re-engage shoppers who never leave an email — recovering abandoned carts and browsing sessions — then layer in email, SMS and WhatsApp on Brevo's deliverability stack. Usage-based pricing: you pay for what you send, not list size.

Pricing (2026)

Shopify-only, usage-based (2026): the Free Basic bundle at $0/mo covers 500 web-push notifications and 500 emails a month with price-drop and back-in-stock automation; Plus at $19/mo lifts web push to 10,000/mo and adds abandoned-cart automation; Power at $79/mo reaches 25,000 web push/mo with flash-sale and browse-abandonment automation, smart delivery and segmentation. Email above 500/mo is bought as credits (about $25 for 10,000). Annual billing saves 10-20%. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

The bundles are push-first: every paid tier still caps included email at 500 a month, so 'omnichannel' email at any real volume means buying separate credits — and consumption-based pricing gets harder to predict as your sends climb. It's also Shopify-exclusive, so it becomes a dead end the day you move platforms.

Recurring themes from the Shopify App Store and merchant reviews, read in August 2026. We have not run PushOwl ourselves, so this is other buyers' experience rather than ours. We summarise it here because the complaints are usually the part a vendor page leaves out.

What they complain about

  • • Results trail email and SMS. Treat it as an extra channel, not a replacement.

When the paid plan is worth it

PushOwl's free Basic Bundle covers stores sending up to 500 web push notifications and 500 emails a month with unlimited subscribers, which is enough for a small or early-stage Shopify store testing push and newsletter campaigns. The concrete trigger to upgrade is push volume: once notifications exceed 500/month you need the $19/month Plus Bundle (10,000-30,000 pushes) or the $79/month Power Bundle for higher volume and features like browse abandonment and segmentation. What upgrading the push bundle does not solve is email and SMS capacity: those run through a separate Brevo account with its own scaling (emails climb toward $300 for 250,000 sends, SMS starts at $7.50 for 750 credits), so paying more for Power buys bigger push limits and automation, not more email or SMS volume.

How to actually use PushOwl

Installation itself is fast: PushOwl's help center lists three steps to add the app from the Shopify App Store — go to the app listing, click Add app, then click Install App — with no verification needed to start collecting web push subscribers via the on-site opt-in prompt. The step that gates deliverable email and SMS campaigns is domain authentication: you connect a Brevo account, then add a Brevo code, DKIM record, and DMARC record to your domain's DNS. Brevo warns this can take up to 48 hours to propagate; skipping it means mail goes out from a shared @brevosend.com address with worse deliverability, and PushOwl notes authentication has been mandatory for senders since February 1, 2024. SMS has a separate compliance step: toll-free numbers used to text US or Canadian recipients need carrier verification, which PushOwl's help center says can take up to 5 business days, and delivery is blocked until it clears. In practice, the DNS wait and the SMS verification window are where new users stall between installing the app and sending their first fully authenticated campaign. Best for: Shopify stores that want to win back abandoned carts and browsing sessions with one-click web push — reaching shoppers who never handed over an email — and then layer in email and SMS, starting on a free tier that genuinely does something.
Who should skip it: if you're not on Shopify (it's built exclusively for it), or you need a full standalone email platform — the 500-email bundle cap means heavy senders keep paying extra per credit.

The natural comparison is Klaviyo or Omnisend — the deeper Shopify email and SMS marketing platforms — PushOwl's edge is web push and a free tier that actually works; Klaviyo goes further on email flows and segmentation but costs more as you grow. Decide by which one fits the job above, not by the louder brand.

Is PushOwl worth it for a small team?

It depends on whether you have the bottleneck it solves. Small teams get the most out of this category when one clear problem is already costing real hours or revenue; buying ahead of that just adds cost and another login. Price it against the hours or lost deals it removes, not against its feature list, and start on the smallest plan that covers the job.

Skip it if you're not on Shopify (it's built exclusively for it), or you need a full standalone email platform — the 500-email bundle cap means heavy senders keep paying extra per credit. Buying a tool to fix a problem you don't have yet just adds cost and another login to manage.
