PushOwl Review (2026)

Growth & RevenueResearched assessment · by Daniel HaketUpdated 2026-09-05

The bundles are push-first: every paid tier still caps included email at 500 a month, so 'omnichannel' email at any real volume means buying separate credits — and consumption-based pricing gets harder to predict as your sends climb. It's also Shopify-exclusive, so it becomes a dead end the day you move platforms.

Best for: Shopify stores that want to win back abandoned carts and browsing sessions with one-click web push — reaching shoppers who never handed over an email — and then layer in email and SMS, starting on a free tier that genuinely does something.
Who should skip it: if you're not on Shopify (it's built exclusively for it), or you need a full standalone email platform — the 500-email bundle cap means heavy senders keep paying extra per credit.

Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 17 Aug 2026

This is priced per channel and then added up, which is the part worth understanding before you compare it with anything. Each bundle sets a separate rate for email, web push and SMS, and the monthly figure is their sum. Basic is $0 and covers 500 emails and 500 web push notifications a month with no SMS credits. Plus is $19/month for 10,000 web push (10,000-30,000 band) and adds abandoned-cart automation.

Power is $79/month for 25,000 up to unlimited web push, with flash-sale and browse-abandonment triggers, segmentation and custom attribution windows. Yearly billing is advertised at 10% off, and 20% on the web push side of Plus. It is Shopify-only. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

What PushOwl does

Shopify web-push and omnichannel marketing, now part of Brevo. One-click push lets stores re-engage shoppers who never leave an email — recovering abandoned carts and browsing sessions — then layer in email, SMS and WhatsApp on Brevo's deliverability stack. Usage-based pricing: you pay for what you send, not list size.

When the paid plan is worth it

PushOwl's free Basic Bundle covers stores sending up to 500 web push notifications and 500 emails a month with unlimited subscribers, which is enough for a small or early-stage Shopify store testing push and newsletter campaigns. The concrete trigger to upgrade is push volume: once notifications exceed 500/month you need the $19/month Plus Bundle (10,000-30,000 pushes) or the $79/month Power Bundle for higher volume and features like browse abandonment and segmentation.

What upgrading the push bundle does not solve is email and SMS capacity. Those run through a separate Brevo account with its own scaling: emails climb toward $300 for 250,000 sends, and SMS starts at $7.50 for 750 credits. So paying more for Power buys bigger push limits and automation, not more email or SMS volume.

How to actually use PushOwl

Installation itself is fast. PushOwl's help center lists three steps to add the app from the Shopify App Store — go to the app listing, click Add app, then click Install App — with no verification needed to start collecting web push subscribers via the on-site opt-in prompt. The step that gates deliverable email and SMS campaigns is domain authentication: you connect a Brevo account, then add a Brevo code, DKIM record, and DMARC record to your domain's DNS.

Brevo warns this can take up to 48 hours to propagate; skipping it means mail goes out from a shared @brevosend.com address with worse deliverability, and PushOwl notes authentication has been mandatory for senders since February 1, 2024. SMS has a separate compliance step: toll-free numbers used to text US or Canadian recipients need carrier verification, which PushOwl's help center says can take up to 5 business days, and delivery is blocked until it clears.

In practice, the DNS wait and the SMS verification window are where new users stall between installing the app and sending their first fully authenticated campaign.
