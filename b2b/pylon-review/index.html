What Pylon does

B2B customer support platform built for Slack and Microsoft Teams. Manage tickets, track issues, and deliver white-glove support directly where your clients chat.

Pricing (2026)

(2026) Per-seat with minimums: Starter about $59/seat/mo (3-seat min ≈ $177/mo), Professional ~$89 (3-seat min), Enterprise ~$139 (7-seat min ≈ $973/mo), billed annually. AI modules are separate: AI agents from ~$100/mo scaling with volume, premium AI assistant ~+$50/seat, phone ~+$35/seat. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

The honest knock

The per-seat sticker is the entry ticket; the AI that makes the platform sing is metered separately, so a realistic modern deployment (seats + AI agent + phone) lands well above the tier price — model your full basket. That said, its B2B-native design (Slack/Teams-first support, account-based views) is a genuine fit gap that classic ticket tools fake with workarounds.

What buyers report about Pylon

Recurring themes from G2 and practitioner reviews, read in August 2026. We have not run Pylon ourselves, so this is other buyers' experience rather than ours. We summarise it here because the complaints are usually the part a vendor page leaves out.

What they praise
  • • 4.9 on G2 — the Slack-native workflow is what B2B teams supporting enterprise customers in shared channels actually need, and 150+ organisations have migrated off Zendesk, Intercom and Service Cloud to it.
  • • AI summarisation and unified channels are named as genuinely time-saving rather than decorative.
What they complain about
  • • There is no free plan and no free trial. You sign a paid contract before you can test the automations, AI or Account Intelligence properly.
  • • The $59-$139 per seat headline is not the bill — AI add-ons, seat minimums and annual-only billing sit on top. Monthly billing costs $70 and $118 against $59 and $89 annual.
  • • The Slack-first design does not fit every customer base, and reviewers turn critical at scale over channel gating.
  • • API and automation capabilities are weaker than the incumbents it replaces.
Best for: B2B SaaS teams supporting customers in shared Slack/Teams channels who want tickets, accounts and AI in one B2B-native platform.
Who should skip it: if you run classic B2C email/chat support (Zendesk-class tools are cheaper at volume), or the seat minimums exceed your team.

Frequently asked questions

Will Pylon work for a remote or distributed team?

Generally yes, since this category is built for it, but the details that bite are timezone handling, how per-seat cost scales as the team spreads, and whether numbers, routing or coverage exist in the countries your people actually sit in. Availability varies far more by country than the marketing pages suggest, so check your specific regions before committing.

Who should not use Pylon?

Skip it if you run classic B2C email/chat support (Zendesk-class tools are cheaper at volume), or the seat minimums exceed your team. Buying a tool to fix a problem you don't have yet just adds cost and another login to manage.
