Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 5 Oct 2026

Annual pricing runs four tiers. Basic is 1,200 SAR (~$320) for 1 user and 1 location, covering ZATCA Phase 1, invoices, payments and credit notes. Pro is 1,800 SAR (~$480) for 3 users and 3 locations, adding ZATCA Phase 2, quotations and the full procurement cycle.

Frequently asked questions

Which Qoyod tier do I actually need for ZATCA?

That depends on your phase, and it is the only question that should pick your tier. As last verified on 5 October 2026 (prices read in-browser on app.qoyod.com, because qoyod.com/en/pricing refuses plain requests), Basic at 1,200 SAR a year covers ZATCA Phase 1 for one user and one location. Phase 2 (the integration phase, where invoices are cleared with the tax authority) starts at Pro, 1,800 SAR a year for three users and three locations, which also brings quotations and the full procurement cycle. Advanced at 2,211 SAR adds fixed assets, depreciation and accounting dimensions for five users and five locations; Enterprise is quote-only. So if Phase 2 applies to your business, your real floor is 1,800 SAR and not the 1,200 on the front of the price list. The riyal is pegged to the dollar at 3.75, so those are roughly $320, $480 and $590 exactly rather than approximately.

What do extra users, locations and payroll cost on top?

Enough to overtake the plan itself, which is the trap in this price list. The add-ons are billed per year on top of the tier, as verified on 5 September and again on 5 October 2026: extra system users 240 SAR each (about $64), extra locations 480 SAR each (about $128), point of sale 600 SAR per user (about $160) and payroll 120 SAR per active employee (about $32). Do the arithmetic before you pick a tier rather than after. A four-person shop on Basic pays 1,200 plus three seats at 240, which is 1,920 SAR: more than Pro costs while still sitting on Phase 1. And because payroll is charged per head, a business with twenty people on the books adds 2,400 SAR for that one module alone. Put it together and a twenty-person business on Pro with payroll and two POS users lands near 5,400 SAR, about $1,440 a year, against the $480 the tier headline suggests. Count seats, locations and terminals first.

Is the discount on Advanced a real saving?

It is a real reduction and a three-year decision, which are not the same thing. Verified 5 September and again 5 October 2026: Advanced shows 2,211 SAR struck through from 3,300 SAR under a banner reading 33% off for a three-year term. So the lower number is not this year’s price for a yearly commitment: it is the rate for signing up to three years of an accounting platform, which is the software you are least likely to want to move mid-term and most likely to regret being locked into. Price the alternative honestly: Pro at 1,800 SAR a year with no multi-year term, against Advanced at 2,211 with one. The monthly toggle costs more in any case, the site advertises up to 20% off for paying annually, and a 14-day free trial is offered without a credit card, so use it on your own data before either commitment. Migration is the step where accounting switches actually fail.

Can I take my accounting records with me if I close my Qoyod account?

Yes, before you close it. Qoyod's FAQ, read on 23 October 2026, says you can export your full records before closing. Do the export first; the FAQ does not say whether anything can still be retrieved after the account is closed, so that is not confirmed.

Can I limit what an individual employee can see?

Yes. The same FAQ, read on 23 October 2026, says data access permissions are managed at the user level. Keep in mind that every extra system user is a paid add-on on top of the tier (see the add-on prices above), so restricting access by giving each person their own login has a yearly cost.
