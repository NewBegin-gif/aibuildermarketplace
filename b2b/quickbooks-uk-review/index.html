The honest knock

The page says six months, not a year: Sole Trader Plus shows £1 and renews at £10 a month, Plus shows £5.60 and renews at £56. That tenfold step arrives in month seven, on a tool you will by then have your entire bookkeeping inside — which is precisely when switching is hardest. Add 20% VAT on top of every figure, and add payroll separately if you need it. Budget from the list price and treat the discount as a half-year rebate, not as the price.

What QuickBooks UK does

Intuit's accounting platform for the UK market — bookkeeping, VAT and Making Tax Digital submissions, invoicing and payroll as a paid add-on. Plans run from £10 a month for sole traders to £123 for the twenty-five-user tier, excluding VAT, with a 30-day trial.

Plus is cheaper per user than Essentials

Read on their own UK pricing page on 28 August 2026, in pounds before VAT: Sole Trader Plus £10 a month, Simple Start £16, Essentials £38 for three users, Plus £56 for five, Advanced £123 for twenty-five. Divide the middle two by their seats and the ladder inverts. Essentials is £12.67 per user a month; Plus, though £18 more, is £11.20 — 12% cheaper per head. That £18 buys two extra seats at £9 each, below both published per-seat rates: if a fourth person needs access, upgrading is the cheapest seat on the page.

Then the number that should decide this, which is not a tier. A 90% discount was running and the page states its length plainly: six months, not a year. Sole Trader Plus shows £1 and becomes £10; Plus shows £5.60 and becomes £56 — a tenfold step landing in month seven. What upgrading does not solve: VAT at 20% sits on every figure, payroll stays a paid add-on on every tier including Advanced, and the Intuit Intelligence chat carries a monthly query limit at the top of the range too. A larger plan buys seats and reports, not a lower renewal.

How to actually use QuickBooks UK

Write down the month-seven price before you start, because that is when the offer ends. It is £10 a month for Sole Trader Plus, £16 Simple Start, £38 Essentials, £56 Plus and £123 Advanced, all excluding VAT and all roughly ten times what the first six months show. Add 20%, and add payroll if you employ anyone. Count your users next, not your features. Essentials caps at three and Plus at five, and because Plus is cheaper per head the seat count settles the tier more cleanly than the feature list does.

At three users and growing, buying Plus outright costs £9 per extra seat. Use the 30 days on the work you actually dread — the VAT return, the bank reconciliation, the invoice chase — rather than on the setup wizard. And do the import during the trial, not after: the cost of this decision is not the subscription, it is that your books end up inside it, which is exactly what makes the step at month seven so hard to walk away from.

Frequently asked questions

What should I weigh before buying Finance & Accounting software?

Ask your accountant before you buy; it is the cheapest step in this whole decision. What matters is whether it exports in the format they already work in, whether the ledger mapping survives that export, and whether they can access it directly. A tool your accountant has to re-key by hand costs more than it saves.
