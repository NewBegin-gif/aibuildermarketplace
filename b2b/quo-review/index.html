Ditching old-school business phones: Why Quo (OpenPhone) is the modern hub

Tested & used by DaanVisit Quo →

By Daan — originally shared on ZeroTouchSupport, my own workflow hub.

If you've ever managed a growing customer operations team, you know the absolute nightmare of traditional business telephony. Passing around physical SIM cards, losing track of text conversations on private personal numbers, or having zero visibility into whether a customer request was actually answered—it's a massive operational risk that halts scaling.

To scale a modern front-office without manual friction, you need a shared, collaborative command center for calling and messaging. That is precisely why I brought Quo (OpenPhone) into our software registry. It completely re-imagines how business numbers function for digital teams.

What exactly is Quo (OpenPhone)?

OpenPhone is a modern, cloud-based business phone system built directly for remote teams, startups, and client-centric companies. It completely replaces clunky desktop hardware and outdated enterprise VoIP setups by turning your phone lines into shared workspaces that run seamlessly via a web browser, desktop app, or mobile device.

Why I consider this a must-have

Coming from an operations and risk management perspective, transparency and data centralization are everything. OpenPhone solves the hidden leaks in your operational support model with a few standout features:

  • Shared Team Inboxes: Multiple team members can call, text, and manage the exact same business line simultaneously. You can instantly see who is responding to a customer in real-time, eliminating double-work.
  • AI-Powered Summaries & Transcripts: Stop wasting billable hours manually summarizing support calls. OpenPhone automatically generates high-accuracy audio transcriptions and action-oriented summaries for every conversation.
  • Internal Threads & Collaboration: You can tag team members directly inside a customer's text history. This lets you resolve issues behind the scenes without starting a separate Slack thread or email chain.
  • Automated Workflows: Set up precise business hour routings, conditional auto-replies for missed texts, and snippet templates to answer recurring inquiries instantly.

For operations managers looking to maintain total visibility over client relations, moving your communication infrastructure to a collaborative cloud hub is a complete game-changer.
