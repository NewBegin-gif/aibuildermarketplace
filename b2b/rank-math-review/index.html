The free version is so generous that many sites never need to upgrade — PRO mainly earns its keep once you want built-in rank tracking, Content AI credits or to manage many sites. Don't pay until you hit those. Throughout this review we weigh it against Yoast SEO.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Rank Math and WP Rocket bundle worth it?

It is the single most-searched thing about Rank Math on our pages, so here is the arithmetic as read on 17 August 2026: the bundle is €11.99 a month billed annually, renewing at €13.99, against €7.99 (renewing €8.99) for Rank Math PRO alone. You are therefore paying about €4 a month for the WP Rocket side. Whether that beats buying WP Rocket separately depends on how many sites you run — check WP Rocket's own licence count before you commit, since that is where the value sits or does not.

What does Rank Math cost in year two?

More than year one, and the page says so plainly: PRO renews at €8.99 a month plus taxes against a first-year €7.99, Business at €27.99 against €24.99, and Agency at €64.99 against €54.99. All are billed annually. This is the most common complaint about Rank Math in reviews, and it is not hidden — it is on the pricing page, under the price.

Does PRO really cover unlimited websites?

Unlimited websites that you own. Client websites are explicitly excluded, and that is the line that decides which tier a freelancer needs: Business at €24.99 covers 100 client sites and Agency at €54.99 covers 500. If you build for other people, PRO is not the plan regardless of how few sites you have.

What should I weigh before buying Growth & Revenue software?

It depends on whether you have the bottleneck it solves. Small teams get the most out of this category when one clear problem is already costing real hours or revenue; buying ahead of that just adds cost and another login. Price it against the hours or lost deals it removes, not against its feature list, and start on the smallest plan that covers the job.
