Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 10 Sep 2026

The plans
  • Free — $0 a month
    100 export credits a month (1,200 a year on annual billing), 2,400 email views a year, unlimited contact and company views, 1 seat, no card required
  • Starter — $39 a month, or $29 on annual billing
    5,000 export credits a month (60,000 a year), unlimited email views, data enrichment, Chrome extension, ABM list builder, advanced filtering, 1 seat
  • Pro — $59 a month, or $49 on annual billing
    10,000 export credits a month (120,000 a year), everything in Starter, 1 seat
  • Business — $99 a month, or $79 on annual billing
    20,000 export credits a month, credits granted upfront, 3 seats
  • Enterprise — custom — Talk to Sales
    custom credit grant and user count

Five tiers in USD. Free is $0 with 100 export credits a month and 2,400 email views a year. Starter is $39 a month, or $29 on annual billing, for 5,000 export credits a month. Pro is $59 or $49 for 10,000. Business is $99 or $79 for 20,000 credits and, for the first time, three seats instead of one. Enterprise is quote-only.

The annual discount is a flat 20% on the price and nothing else: the credit allowance is identical either way (5,000 a month is billed as 60,000 a year). Note that the main /pricing/ page carries no figures at all — it only routes you to Prospect or Email Verifier — so the prices above come from the Prospect pricing page one level down. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

The honest knock

Contact and company views are unlimited on every plan including the free one, so the demo will feel generous; what you actually pay for is pulling those records out. Free gives 100 exports a month, which is a sample rather than a workflow. The second thing to check is seats: Starter and Pro both include exactly one, so a two-person sales team is on Business at $99 a month before it needs the extra credits.

And two figures on the page are not explained anywhere — Starter and Pro promise to "unlock an extra $250 / $500 worth of data", which is priced in dollars rather than in credits or records, and the cost of a credit once you run out is not published at all.

What ReachStream does

B2B contact database with a published price instead of a quote: unlimited contact and company views on every tier, with the meter running on exports rather than on looking. Free is $0 for 100 export credits a month; Starter $39 a month ($29 annually) for 5,000, Pro $59/$49 for 10,000, Business $99/$79 for 20,000 and three seats. Annual billing takes 20% off the price and changes nothing about the allowance. Read on their own pricing page on 10 September 2026.

The natural comparison is RocketReach — RocketReach sells the same shape of thing — a contact database with self-serve tiers — and both publish their prices, which already puts them in a different bracket from the quote-only end of this market. The difference is what the meter counts. If your team browses widely and exports rarely, ReachStream is the cheaper shape. Frequently asked questions

What should I weigh before buying Sales & CRM software?

Most tools in this category plug in rather than replace. Check which direction the data flows and whether the sync is genuinely two-way, because a one-way push creates a second source of truth that your reps will quietly stop trusting. If it does aim to replace your CRM, price the migration honestly: history, custom fields and reporting rarely move across cleanly.
