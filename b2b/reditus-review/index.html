What Reditus does

Affiliate-program software plus marketplace for B2B SaaS: launch, manage and scale a partner program with tracking, automated payouts and affiliate discovery.

Pricing (2026)

(2026) 14-day free trial, no credit card. Startup $149/mo month-to-month or $99/mo billed annually ($1,188); Growth $299/mo or $179/mo annually ($2,148); Scale Up $399/mo annual-only ($4,788/yr); Enterprise from $799/mo annual-only. Payout processing adds 5% (card) or 2% (invoice) on top. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

The advertised $99 is the annual-commitment rate — month-to-month is $149. The affiliate-marketplace listing, often the very reason teams pick Reditus, only unlocks at Scale Up: $4,788 per year, annual-only. And payout processing fees of 5% (card) or 2% (invoice) sit on top of every plan.

When the paid plan is worth it

Reditus has no free tier; the entry point is the $99/month Growth plan, offered with a 14-day trial that requires no credit card. Growth covers affiliate programs tracking up to $60K in ARR, includes one AI-assisted affiliate search per month, and gives access to what the pricing page calls a "limited" affiliate database — workable for a single company running one program without constant partner sourcing. The concrete push toward Scale Up ($399/month, billed annually) is that $60K ARR cap and the one-search-per-month limit: once tracked revenue or affiliate-discovery needs grow past those numbers, Growth stops covering the account. What upgrading does not fix: Scale Up adds a marketplace listing and full access to the affiliate database, which raises exposure to Reditus's pool of affiliates, but it does not make affiliates apply, get approved, or drive sales. That still depends on the commission structure and recruiting effort a company puts in, neither of which is a paid-tier feature.

How to actually use Reditus

The first real step after signing up for the 14-day free trial is entering company details, which Reditus uses to generate a custom affiliate landing page and sign-up page. Next comes installation: a tracking script placed on every page of the main domain and on the sign-up page itself, plus a sign-up snippet (gr('track', 'conversion', {email: ...})) that must fire exactly once, on a successful new sign-up. Reditus's own installation guide flags the main failure point directly: placing the tracking script behind a cookie consent wall, which stops conversions from being recorded. Payouts need Stripe connected, or manual handling through the Reditus API if Stripe isn't used. On the Growth plan, affiliate discovery is capped at one AI-assisted search per month against a database the pricing page calls "limited," so building out a partner roster beyond that search can stall until a Scale Up upgrade. Best for: B2B SaaS teams with a product-led motion that want to launch an affiliate program and tap an affiliate-discovery network, and are comfortable committing annually.
Who should skip it: if you are pre-product-market-fit, need month-to-month flexibility on a small budget, or expected marketplace exposure on the entry tiers.

The natural comparison is PartnerStack — PartnerStack is the bigger, enterprise-depth network; Reditus is leaner and cheaper to start, but its marketplace exposure gates behind the annual-only Scale Up tier. Decide by which one fits the job above, not by the louder brand.
