What Reply.io does

AI-driven sales engagement platform. Automate your cold outreach across email, LinkedIn, and calls while keeping the human touch. Now includes a mobile app (Jason AI) for iOS and Android to run outreach on the go.

Pricing (2026)

(2026) Email Volume from about $49/mo (1,000 active contacts, 5,000 emails); Multichannel about $89/user/mo — but LinkedIn automation (~$69/user) and calling (~$29/user) are separate add-ons, so real multichannel lands around $150-187/user; the Jason AI SDR tier starts around $500/mo. Agency plan from ~$210/mo. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

The advertised $89 is the lobby, not the building: use LinkedIn and calling — the whole point of multichannel — and the real per-seat price roughly doubles. Budget 1.5-2x the sticker, plus sending infrastructure (domains, warmup) that any cold-outreach stack needs. The platform is genuinely capable; the pricing just rewards reading this paragraph before the demo call.

What buyers report about Reply.io

Recurring themes from G2, Trustpilot and Reddit, read in August 2026. We have not run Reply.io ourselves, so this is other buyers' experience rather than ours. We summarise it here because the complaints are usually the part a vendor page leaves out.

What they praise
  • • Automation depth and multichannel sequences are what SDR teams rate — 4.6 on G2 across 1,542+ reviews, with support quality at 9.3 out of 10.
  • • Once workflows are configured properly, reviewers report it genuinely saving time.
What they complain about
  • • Billing is the most documented complaint on every platform. A Reddit PSA thread details a promo price of about $499/month for Jason AI becoming $1,200+ in-product, with no self-serve cancellation.
  • • Multiple reviewers use scam-alert language about the free trial specifically. Read the trial terms and set a reminder.
  • • The $89 per user Professional plan reaches about $292/month for a two-rep team once enrichment and CRM sync are added.
  • • Chrome-extension LinkedIn automation carries an estimated 3-5% account restriction rate per active user over 90 days.
  • • Support response times of 24-72 hours are reported alongside the high G2 support score — the two coexist.
Best for: sales teams running structured multichannel outbound (email + LinkedIn + calls) who want sequences, warmup and a B2B database in one platform.
Who should skip it: if you only send plain email sequences (cheaper single-channel tools exist), or your list is too small to justify per-seat-plus-add-ons math.

It depends on whether you have the bottleneck it solves. Small teams get the most out of this category when one clear problem is already costing real hours or revenue; buying ahead of that just adds cost and another login. Price it against the hours or lost deals it removes, not against its feature list, and start on the smallest plan that covers the job.

Skip it if you only send plain email sequences (cheaper single-channel tools exist), or your list is too small to justify per-seat-plus-add-ons math. Buying a tool to fix a problem you don't have yet just adds cost and another login to manage.
