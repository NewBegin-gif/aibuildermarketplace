What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

One plugin forever at $145 is honest value — but a working portrait stack means four plugins at $145-$179 each, $648 in total, while the Start subscription hands you the entire set for $169 a year with 2,400 retouches included. The catch cuts the other way too: own-forever versus keep-paying is the real decision, since the credits' 5-year rollover only holds 'while the subscription is active' — stop paying and the suite and the stockpile are gone.

Low volume? Cloud retouching without a subscription is sold in packs, $24 for 100 retouches, and at $0.15 to $0.24 a photo it is the dearest lane per image, not the cheapest.

Owning beats renting after 3.8 years

Three ways to pay, and they price the same photograph so differently that picking the lane matters more than picking the tier (read on their pricing and product pages on 7 September 2026). Buy outright and the plugins are one-time. The photo collection is fifteen plugins: thirteen at $145 — Heal, Crop, Face Make, Portrait Volumes, Skin Tone, Skin Mask, Mattifier, White Teeth, Eyes Bundle, Stray Hairs, Fabric, Dust and Color Match — with Dodge & Burn and Clean Backdrop at $179 each.

A working portrait stack of Heal, Crop, Dodge & Burn and Clean Backdrop is $648. Subscribe and you get the entire plugin set plus a yearly retouch allowance. Start is $169 a year, Pro $299 and Business $759, a limited offer against list rates of $20, $35 and $90 a month that the page also restates as $14.08, $24.92 and $63.25 a month. They carry 2,400, 6,000 and 18,000 retouching credits a year, 108,000 to 1,200,000 culling credits, and 20/40/100 GB of storage.

One photo is one credit however many plugins you run on it. That sets up the comparison that matters. Four plugins cost $648 to own; Start costs $169 a year and includes all fifteen plus 2,400 retouches. Renting is cheaper than owning for the first 3.8 years, and ownership only wins after that — and only if you would still want the same four plugins in year four. In a category where the models are being retrained, that is the bet you are placing.

The third lane has moved, and it moved the wrong way for light users. Cloud retouching without a subscription is now sold in packs: $24 for 100 credits, $59 for 300 and $179 for 1,200, which is $0.24, $0.20 and $0.15 a retouch against $0.070 on Start, $0.050 on Pro and $0.042 on Business. The best pack rate is more than twice the cheapest subscription's, and the $179 Extended Pack costs $10 more than a whole year of Start while delivering half the retouches and none of the plugins.

Packs only win below roughly 800 retouches a year: two Advanced Packs plus two Starter Packs cover 800 retouches for $166, while 900 takes three Advanced Packs at $177 — more than a full year of Start. What no tier buys is permanence, and the exit clause is sharper than it reads. Unused credits roll over for up to five years, but only 'while the subscription is active', so a photographer who banks two quiet years and then pauses for a season loses the stockpile and the plugin set together.

The 14-day money-back guarantee carries its own condition: it holds while you have used up to 500 retouches.

How to actually use Retouch4me

Use the free route first, and use it properly. Frequency Separation and Color Match Free are permanently free plugins, Apex, the Photoshop Panel and Arams are free applications, and every paid plugin has a Try Demo button. Spend that trial on your most difficult work — the skin you would normally spend twenty minutes on, not the shot that was already clean — because that is the only thing that tells you whether the models suit your subjects.

Then count retouches over a year, honestly, across a real season rather than a busy week. Below roughly 800 a year the credit packs cost less in total than a subscription — 800 retouches is $166 in packs against $169 for a year of Start — and above that Start wins on total cost as well as per retouch, and hands you the whole plugin set with it. The packs are the expensive lane per photo, not the cheap one — $0.15 at best against $0.070 on Start.

Decide the ownership question before you subscribe rather than after. Four plugins outright is $648 and yours forever; Start is $169 a year, and everything, including any credits you have banked, disappears when you stop paying. Renting is cheaper until year four. If you take a subscription, use the money-back window while you are still under 500 retouches, because that is the condition attached to it.
