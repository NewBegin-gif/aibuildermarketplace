What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

Work out which lane you are in before you pay, because the three lanes price the same photo very differently. One plugin forever at $145 is honest value — but a working portrait stack means four or five plugins at $145–$179 each, over $600, while the Start subscription hands you the entire plugin set for $169 a year with 2,400 retouches included. The catch cuts the other way too: own-forever versus keep-paying is the real decision, since the credits' 5-year rollover only holds 'while the subscription is active' — stop paying and the suite and stockpile are gone. Low volume? Price the $0.10-per-photo route first: 100 photos a month is $10.

Best for: high-volume portrait, school and wedding photographers who already live in Photoshop and want repetitive retouching (skin, stray hairs, dust, teeth) automated — owning the plugins outright or renting the whole suite on credits, whichever their volume favours.
Who should skip it: if occasional editors — the free tools plus $0.10/photo pay-as-you-go cover light use; or anyone unwilling to do the three-lane maths, because picking the wrong lane here easily doubles what the same workflow costs.

The natural comparison is Evoto — the credit-based AI retouching desktop app — Evoto bundles everything in one interface paid per export credit, Retouch4me offers lifetime plugins, a credit subscription or pay-per-photo; high volume favours owning the plugins, low volume favours credits. Verify both current price lists yourself.. Decide by which one fits the job above, not by the louder brand.
