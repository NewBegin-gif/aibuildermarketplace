What Salesflare does

CRM for small B2B teams that auto-logs emails, meetings and contact details from your inbox and calendar. $29–99/user/month with no caps on contacts or pipelines. Deliberately lean: no built-in dialer, no quoting or invoicing, and basic reporting unless you export.

The catch

It's deliberately lean: no built-in dialer, no quoting or invoicing, and reporting feels basic unless you export. Integrations cover the essentials (Gmail, Outlook, Zapier) but niche tools lean on Zapier — and at higher seat counts the price overlaps fuller CRMs.

When the paid plan is worth it

Salesflare's plans start after a 30-day trial that requires no credit card; there is no permanently free tier. Solo users or small teams evaluating basic contact and email tracking can run the entire trial on any plan since core CRM functions (automated data capture, email tracking, mobile and LinkedIn access) are already present in the entry-level Growth plan ($39/month, or $29/month billed annually). The concrete push toward Pro ($64/month, $49 annual) is the lead credit allotment: Growth includes 5 lead credits per month, versus 100 on Pro and 250 on Enterprise, and multi-step email sequences and user permission controls are Pro-only.

What upgrading does not solve is capacity: Salesflare states there are no hidden limits on contacts, users, templates, or pipelines on any paid plan, so moving to a higher tier does not buy more storage or seats. Running out of lead credits is addressed by purchasing separate credit packs (from $39 for 250), not by the plan tier itself beyond the monthly allotment.

How to actually use Salesflare

Initial setup means installing the Salesflare sidebar in your email inbox and, optionally, the Chrome extension for LinkedIn. Then connect your email account under Settings > Email Settings so contact capture, email tracking and signature sync start working automatically; the mobile app is a separate install. Existing contacts come in through Settings > Import Data as a CSV or from another database. Because Salesflare merges any re-imported contact with a matching email address into the existing record instead of creating a duplicate, messy or duplicate-heavy source files need cleaning before import or contacts silently merge into the wrong record.

A common stall point is expecting full functionality from the trial. Multi-step email sequences and user permission controls are gated to the Pro plan. So a team trialling or buying the entry-level Growth plan will not see those features, even though the rest of the CRM is fully automated from day one.

Frequently asked questions

Is Salesflare worth it for a small team?

It depends on whether you have the bottleneck it solves. Small teams get the most out of this category when one clear problem is already costing real hours or revenue; buying ahead of that just adds cost and another login. Price it against the hours or lost deals it removes, not against its feature list, and start on the smallest plan that covers the job.

Who should not use Salesflare?

Skip it if you need deep customisation, a big integration marketplace, or built-in calling and invoicing — Pipedrive or a fuller CRM fit better. Buying a tool to fix a problem you don't have yet just adds cost and another login to manage.
