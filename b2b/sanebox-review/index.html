What SaneBox does

Intelligent email triage. SaneBox uses AI to silently filter out the noise, so you only see the emails that actually matter.

Pricing (2026)

Tiered by accounts and features: Snack about $3.49/mo (1 account), Lunch ~$5.99, Dinner ~$16.99 (4 accounts, all features); annual billing ~20%+ off, 14-day trial (2026). Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

It works with any email provider (a plus over built-in tools), but you're paying monthly to filter mail that modern clients increasingly sort for free — the value is real only if your inbox is genuinely overwhelming and native filters aren't cutting it.

What buyers report about SaneBox

Recurring themes from G2 and Trustpilot, read in August 2026. We have not run SaneBox ourselves, so this is other buyers' experience rather than ours. We summarise it here because the complaints are usually the part a vendor page leaves out.

What they praise
  • • It works with the mail client you already use rather than replacing it, which reviewers rate as the main advantage over switching to a new inbox app.
  • • The privacy model is unusually clean — it reads headers and metadata, not message bodies — and privacy-conscious reviewers call this out specifically.
What they complain about
  • • No free tier, and the 14-day trial is the only way to judge it.
  • • The features people actually want — deep cleaning, attachment handling — sit on the top tier, not the entry one.
  • • Cancellation friction comes up repeatedly. Check how you cancel before you start the trial.

When the paid plan is worth it

SaneBox has no free tier — a 14-day trial and then a paid plan — and the tiers split on two axes that are easy to miss. Read in their own help centre on 2 August 2026: Snack covers 1 email account, Lunch covers 2, Dinner covers 4. The second axis is the one that catches people. Lunch does not give you every feature — it lets you "choose 6 Sane features" from a list of eleven: SaneNews, SaneLater, SaneNoReplies, SaneDoNotDisturb, SaneCC, SaneNotSpam, DIY Training Folders, SaneBlackHole, SaneTomorrow & SaneNextWeek, DIY Snooze Folders and SaneReminders. Only Dinner gives all of them. So if SaneBlackHole and SaneReminders are the two you came for, check they fit inside your six. One thing we could not verify: the plan prices are rendered in the browser on their pricing page and were not readable in the page we could fetch, so we are not quoting figures. Check the live page — and note that they bill monthly, yearly or two-yearly, so the headline you see depends on which term is selected.

How to actually use SaneBox

SaneBox works by moving mail between folders on your existing account, so the first decision is which account to connect — and on Snack you only get one. Connect the inbox that actually hurts. Then leave it alone for a few days. The filtering learns from where you move things, so the useful signal is you dragging a message out of SaneLater back into the inbox, not you reading a settings page. Judge it at the end of the trial, not on day two. Pick your feature set deliberately if you land on Lunch. Six of eleven is a real constraint, and the two most people end up wanting are SaneBlackHole, which permanently kills a sender, and SaneNoReplies, which tracks mail you sent that nobody answered. Frequently asked questions

Does SaneBox replace my current setup or sit on top of it?

Usually it sits on top rather than replacing everything. Tools in this class tend to become the layer where work is tracked and handed off, while your existing systems stay the source of truth for their own data. Before you buy, map which tools it would replace outright and which it would merely sync with. If the answer is that it syncs with all of them, you are adding a layer rather than removing one.

Who should not use SaneBox?

Skip it if your inbox is manageable or your mail client's built-in filtering already handles it — you don't need to pay for this. Buying a tool to fix a problem you don't have yet just adds cost and another login to manage.
