Scarcity.AI Review (2026)Real Data, Tunable Truth

E-commerceResearched assessment · by Daniel HaketUpdated 2026-09-25

All three read real data, which is worth saying before anything else — the low stock counter shows inventory levels and the visitors counter reads unique visitors, live traffic and product views.

Best for: Shopify merchants who genuinely run low stock or timed promotions and want to surface that on the product page — the low stock counter is tied to real inventory, which makes it the honest one of the three when your stock actually is low.
Who should skip it: if your stock is not low and your sale does not end, because a claim a shopper would read as false is one the FTC treats as deceptive; or your brand sells on trust, where a blinking counter costs more in credibility than it returns.
