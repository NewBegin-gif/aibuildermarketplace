Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 30 Sep 2026

The pricing page no longer shows plans: seamless.ai/pricing now forwards the browser to seamless.ai/pricing-inquiry, a demo-request form headed 'Perfect Pricing for Startups to the Fortune 500' that offers special pricing for unlimited users, bulk credits, prospector, job changes, buyer intent and enrichment, and carries no amount, no plan table and no FAQ. Still no published price above the free plan, in a fifth reading.

Seamless's help centre now states the allowances: Free is 50 Universal Credits that do not replenish; a Basic licence gets 250 Universal Credits a month and a Pro licence 10,000 a month, both refreshing on the contract date; bulk pool credits are bought up front and do not replenish. Legacy Pro accounts created before 1 April 2026 keep 1,000 credits a day, no longer sold.

Researching a contact costs 1 credit for the whole profile, a Data Enrich row 1, an Autopilot contact 1, and an AI Assistant interaction or AI-written message 0.2. Buyer Intent runs on separate Intent Topics, not credits. Credit Back Protection refunds the credit when Seamless's own check flags an email as invalid, but not for soft bounces, role addresses like info@, or phone numbers.

Until 7 September the pricing page's FAQ said Pro starts at 10,000 annual credits; the help article says 10,000 a month, so ask which one your quote uses. Third-party guides report an entry around $147 a month for roughly 250 credits; that matches the Basic allowance, but the dollar figure is not Seamless's number. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

What buyers report about Seamless.ai

Recurring themes from the buyer-review sites listed at the foot of this page, read in August 2026. We have not run Seamless.ai ourselves, so this is other buyers' experience rather than ours. We summarise it here because the complaints are usually the part a vendor page leaves out.

What they praise
  • • Real-time search rather than a static database, and the Chrome extension pulls contacts while you browse — 4.4 on G2 across 5,000+ reviews, 4.5 on Capterra.
  • • Reviewers who work within their credits report it finding contacts other tools miss.
What they complain about
  • • Cancellation is the number one complaint across every platform — ahead of data quality, pricing and features. Contracts are annual with 60 days' notice required before renewal; miss it by a fortnight and you have another twelve months.

Where the ratings disagree. 4.4 on G2 against 1.4 on Trustpilot, and the BBB logged 79 complaints in three years with 42 never answered. That is the widest and best-documented split in this whole set. The G2 reviews are about finding contacts; the rest are about getting out.

Price the usable contacts, not the credits

There is still no published price above the free plan, and since late September 2026 there is not even a pricing page: seamless.ai/pricing now forwards to a demo-request form that promises special pricing and shows no amount (read 30 September 2026). Five readings across July, August and September, five blanks. Free is one user and 50 credits that do not replenish; everything above it is Contact sales. What Seamless does publish is the allowance, in its help centre rather than on a sales page: a Basic licence gets 250 Universal Credits a month and a Pro licence 10,000 a month, both refreshing on the contract date.

That explains the figure everyone else quotes. Third-party guides report an entry around $147 a month for roughly 250 credits, which is the Basic allowance, not Pro. Moving from Basic to Pro is a 40-fold jump in credits, so the real question is whether you will research more than 250 contacts a month. One conflict remains: the old pricing page's FAQ, read on 7 September, put the Pro floor at 10,000 annual credits, not monthly.

That is a twelve-fold difference; get it in writing. What a bigger tier does not buy is the extras. On 7 September the old page marked AI Assistant, Data Enrichment, Autopilot and Buyer Intent Data as Add-on in every column, Enterprise included. The help centre now says the first three draw on the same Universal Credits, while Buyer Intent needs separate Intent Topics. Either way, upgrading the tier is not what puts intent data in the contract.

What the contract rations is credits, not seats, and a credit here buys the whole profile, phone number and email together. So the number to compare is not the price per credit but the price per contact you can actually reach. Independent tests put email deliverability here around 74%, meaning 20-30% bounce, against ZoomInfo's roughly 2-3%. Credit Back Protection refunds the credit when Seamless's own check flags an email as invalid, but not soft bounces, role addresses or phone numbers, so it trims that gap rather than closing it.

Divide any per-credit rate you are quoted by your own measured hit rate before you compare it with anything, and take that measurement during the free 50 rather than trusting either figure. The other half of this purchase is the paperwork, and it is where the complaints cluster. The contract auto-renews and requires 30 to 60 days' written cancellation; buyers report the renewal conversation being harder than the first sale, and there are reports of charges landing after a cancellation the customer believed was confirmed.

None of that is a price, but all of it is a cost. Get the notice period, the cancellation method and the renewal price in writing, and put a calendar reminder 90 days before renewal on the day you sign — not 30, because 30 is the window itself.

How to actually use Seamless.ai

Spend the 50 free credits as a measurement rather than a trial. Pull 50 contacts you can verify independently — people at companies you already sell to, or addresses you have mailed before — and check two things: how many resolve at all, and how many actually deliver. Independent tests put deliverability here around 74%, and your own 50 will tell you whether your segment is better or worse than that.

Take that bounce rate into the sales conversation, because it is the only number that converts their price into your cost. Divide any quoted per-credit rate by your measured hit rate and compare that figure — not the quoted one — against the alternatives. Go into that conversation with the one quantity they do publish. Their FAQ says Pro starts at 10,000 annual credits, so ask what those 10,000 cost before you discuss anything bigger, and ask separately about the AI Assistant, Data Enrichment, Autopilot and Buyer Intent Data, which are add-ons on every tier including Enterprise.

Then handle the contract before the product. Ask for the renewal price, the notice period and the cancellation method in writing, and set a calendar reminder 90 days before each renewal date at the moment you sign. The notice window is 30 to 60 days, so a reminder set at 30 days is already too late, and buyers report the exit being harder than the entry.

Frequently asked questions

What does Seamless.ai cost?

It is not published, and the pricing page now forwards to a demo-request form. There is a free plan with one user and 50 credits that do not replenish; everything above it is quoted by sales. Seamless's help centre gives the allowances: 250 Universal Credits a month on a Basic licence, 10,000 a month on Pro. Third-party guides cite roughly $147 a month for about 250 credits, which fits Basic, but we could not verify any amount against Seamless's own pages in five readings.

What should I check before signing?

The credit allowance and what happens when it runs out, since that is what the contract rations, and which of the four add-ons you need — AI Assistant, Data Enrichment, Autopilot and Buyer Intent Data were marked Add-on on every tier, Enterprise included, when the pricing page still listed plans (7 September 2026); Buyer Intent also runs on separate Intent Topics rather than credits. Buyers also consistently report friction at renewal rather than at purchase, so get the cancellation terms and the renewal price in writing at the start.

Is the free plan useful?

Fifty credits is enough to test data accuracy on a handful of your own target accounts, which is exactly what you should do — accuracy varies sharply by territory and industry, and it is the only thing that matters in this category. Check names you already know the answers for.

What should I weigh before buying Growth & Revenue software?

It depends on whether you have the bottleneck it solves. Small teams get the most out of this category when one clear problem is already costing real hours or revenue; buying ahead of that just adds cost and another login. Price it against the hours or lost deals it removes, not against its feature list, and start on the smallest plan that covers the job.
