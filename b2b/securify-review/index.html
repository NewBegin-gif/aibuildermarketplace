Securify Review (2026)

E-commerceResearched assessment · by Daniel HaketUpdated 2026-09-05

Blocking by country and IP is a blunt instrument and it cuts both ways: every rule that keeps out a fraudster also keeps out a customer travelling, using a corporate VPN, or living behind a carrier NAT.

Best for: stores seeing real fraud or bot traffic that want country, IP, VPN and email-risk rules plus automatic holds on suspicious orders, and who will review the block log rather than set and forget.
Who should skip it: if you are blocking on a hunch rather than on measured fraud — start by reading your order data, because a blocked legitimate customer never tells you they were blocked.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

The app is honest enough to give you a live log of what it blocked, and that log is the part to actually read — a rule set nobody reviews quietly costs sales. Also worth separating in your head: blocking bots from your analytics is a reporting fix, while blocking fraud at checkout is a money fix, and the two need different rules.

What Securify does

Country blocker and fraud filter for Shopify: blocks countries, IP ranges, bots, VPNs and risky emails at checkout, and holds suspicious orders automatically. Free plan; paid $5.99 to $49 a month.

What buyers report about Securify

Recurring themes from the buyer-review sites listed at the foot of this page, read in August 2026. We have not run Securify ourselves, so this is other buyers' experience rather than ours. We summarise it here because the complaints are usually the part a vendor page leaves out.

What they praise
  • • When it is set up, merchants say it does the job: blocking high-risk traffic, VPNs and bots, with one April 2026 reviewer naming the real-time visitor log as the part they actually use.
  • • Support is fast and reaches beyond English — a Japanese merchant in May 2026 says staff responded and solved the trouble very quickly.
What they complain about
  • • The most common one-star story is the same one, and it is a setup trap rather than a broken product: the app runs as a theme app extension, and if that is not switched on it blocks nothing while still billing. The vendor's own replies say so — to a merchant who says it charged for two years and did not work (November 2025), and to one whose country rule did nothing (June 2025). A Shopify theme update can switch it off again.
  • • IPv6 is not supported. A January 2025 reviewer could not block even their own address; the vendor confirmed in reply that the app is IPv4-only.
  • • Bot blocking is not configurable. A May 2026 reviewer says you cannot control which bots you allow, and the vendor answered that custom bot blocklists are still being built.
  • • Rules that do not hold: abandoned checkouts from a blocked country continued for four days (December 2025), a Germany block did not take (June 2025), and a VPN test still reached the store (May 2026).
  • • Blocking too widely cuts the other way. One merchant reports being locked out of their own site (August 2024) — the same risk the block log exists to catch.
  • • Support response times are called too slow by several of the one-star reviewers (December 2025).

Where the ratings disagree. The rating splits rather than averages: 78% five stars and 14% one star, with almost nothing in between — a shape that usually means the product either works or was never running. Reading the one-stars, that is close to literal. Most of them trace to the theme app extension being off, which the vendor points out in its replies. So treat the 4.4 as conditional on a setup step, verify on day one that blocking actually fires, and check again after every theme change.

When the paid plan is worth it

Three steps, and only the last one is a real product change. Free blocks unlimited countries and IPs already, but stops at 500 blocked visitors a month and keeps 1,000 records -- for a small store under attack that cap is the thing that bites first. Pro at $5.99 lifts the cap, adds VPN detection, a custom block message and bulk IP import. Advanced at $9.49 adds bot protection and unlimited logs, which is $3.50 for the one thing most people install this app for.

Growth is the jump: $49 a month, five times Advanced, and it buys a different category of tool. That is machine-learning bot blocking, blocking by email address or domain, an allowlist for trusted buyers, a risk scan for fake email addresses, and one-click tagging of risky customers. That is fraud screening rather than access blocking, so treat Growth as a separate purchase decision instead of an upgrade. Annual billing saves 30% on Pro and Advanced but only 17% on Growth.

The trial is 3 days, which is short for judging a blocking tool. Read on the App Store listing on 19 August 2026; rating 4.4 from 67 reviews.

How to actually use Securify

Run it in log-only mode first if the plan allows, or start with the narrowest rule you can defend, and watch the live log for a week before you widen anything. The order in which to add rules matters: bot and scraper blocking is low-risk and cleans up your analytics, IP and VPN blocking is medium-risk, and country blocking is the one most likely to cost you real orders. Check the block log against your actual fraud cases monthly — if the rules are not catching anything, they are only costing you.
