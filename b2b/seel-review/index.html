What Seel does

Shopper-paid returns and shipping protection for Shopify, BigCommerce and Adobe Commerce. Customers add Worry-Free Purchase at checkout and Seel pays the refund instead of you — free for the merchant.

Pricing (2026)

Free for the merchant, because Seel is shopper-paid — the fee lands on your customer's checkout rather than your invoice. Customers who add Worry-Free Purchase (formerly Return Assurance) pay roughly $2-$5, reported elsewhere as 3-5% of order value; Seel sets that figure with its own algorithm and publishes no rate card. No merchant subscription and no platform fee (verified 2026-08-02). Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

Two things to weigh, and neither is the price you pay — because you do not pay one. First, you cannot set the fee. Seel's algorithm prices it, so if it lands too high for your niche and shoppers stop opting in, your only lever is switching the feature off entirely. Second, the charge appears on your checkout, not Seel's: shopper-side complaints filed with the BBB describe the fee arriving in the cart with the opt-out easy to miss, and that lands on your brand. Test it as a customer before you enable it. Also check the platform list — Shopify, BigCommerce and Adobe Commerce only, with no WooCommerce, Wix or custom storefront support.

What buyers report about Seel

Recurring themes from the Shopify App Store, SmartCustomer and BBB complaint filings, read in August 2026. We have not run Seel ourselves, so this is other buyers' experience rather than ours. We summarise it here because the complaints are usually the part a vendor page leaves out.

What they praise
  • • Merchants report conversion lifting by roughly 5% on average once Return Assurance is offered, and shoppers take it 24% of the time — far above the usual attach rate for a B2C add-on.
  • • 4.8 out of 5 on the Shopify App Store across 213+ reviews, 92% of them five stars.
What they complain about
  • • You cannot set the fee. Seel's algorithm prices it, so if the fee lands too high for your niche and customers stop opting in, there is no lever to pull.
  • • Shopper-side complaints filed with the BBB describe the charge appearing in the cart with the opt-out easy to miss. That lands on your checkout, not Seel's.
  • • Direct customer ratings run much lower than the merchant-side ones — around 3.3 out of 5 across 63 reviews on SmartCustomer.

Where the ratings disagree. The Shopify App Store rating is merchants rating a revenue tool; the consumer-facing ratings are shoppers rating a charge they did not expect. Both are real, and only one of them is your customer.

Best for: stores selling final-sale, high-consideration or hard-to-size products, where return anxiety is the thing killing the order — merchants report conversion lifting around 5% and shoppers taking the option roughly 24% of the time.
Who should skip it: if your margin already absorbs returns comfortably, or you need control over what appears on your own checkout — the fee is set by Seel and shown to your customer either way.

The natural comparison is Redo — the other shopper-paid model, but with merchant-side workflow control Seel does not offer. Decide by which one fits the job above, not by the louder brand.

Frequently asked questions

Does Seel work with my store platform?

Check your platform and version specifically. Shopify support is close to universal in this category, while WooCommerce, BigCommerce, Magento and headless setups vary a lot, and 'supported' sometimes means a generic API rather than a maintained app. A listing in your own platform's app store, with recent reviews there, is a better signal than the vendor's logo wall.

Who should not use Seel?

Skip it if your margin already absorbs returns comfortably, or you need control over what appears on your own checkout — the fee is set by Seel and shown to your customer either way. Buying a tool to fix a problem you don't have yet just adds cost and another login to manage.
