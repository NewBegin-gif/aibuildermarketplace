We tested it · 2026-11-17

We created the account and our AI assistant ran the test in our browser. On 17 November 2026 the session tested Seele in our free account: one prompt for a small cat game, which gave a game brief, 9 concept assets and a playable prototype in about 10 minutes for 45 Koins, the Pro badge on the button for the full game, the plans window on the Monthly and Annually tabs, and the cancel text in the FAQ. The full game, the checkout and the coupon were not reached. Nothing was paid. Start and account: the test started at 16:21:53 (Dutch time) with a prompt sent at the workspace of the vendor site, in the free Seele our account (plan Free, 360 Koins), already logged in, in our own tab. The app was already in English. The model was Seele02-flash (Fast, affordable, perfect for basic games, Current); the Pro and Ultra models showed Unlock this model and were locked. The balance before was 360 Koins. Core action, a small game from one prompt: the prompt was A simple 2D game where a cat jumps over boxes; arrow keys to move, space to jump, score counter. After about 1 minute the app asked 3 questions: Engine (Three.js, Godot, Unity or Unreal, where Unreal was locked), Art style and Run length; we chose Three.js, Pixel arcade and Endless score chase (the recommended default), which took 5 extra clicks. Seele then made a Game brief (Concept, Full game not built yet), 9 concept assets (covers, three cats and three scenes) and a playable prototype, with the message Worked for 9m 34s. The result stood at the latest at 16:32:41, about 10 minutes after the start. The balance after was 315 Koins, so 45 Koins were used; the page Usage and Koin said 29 spent this month and 315 current balance. The prototype (TRY THE BOXES) says that the shapes are drawn in code, so the art is not final. We clicked Start and pressed Space: the cat jumped and the score rose (SCORE 11, BOXES 1 / 26, COMBO 1.1x); after that the screen said THE BOX WON. with a button RUN AGAIN. So the result is playable, but it is a prototype with shapes and not a finished game. The full game: the button Make the full game with AI carries a Pro badge and so does the line LET AI BUILD THE FULL GAME in the brief; the text says the full game is built to commercial quality, with upgraded visuals, levels and audio. Summarised from the pricing notes below; the notes carry the conditions.

Standard is $20 a month for 2,000 Koins, Pro $100 for 12,000 and Ultra $200 for 25,000; billed annually they show as $17, $78 and $167 a month. The Pro allowance is shown as 10,000 plus 2,000 Koins and Ultra as 20,000 plus 5,000. We read the monthly, annual and Koin Pack tabs in a browser. On 2 October 2026 the same page showed Pro at half that price with half the Koins, so Pro now costs twice as much for twice the Koins; Standard and Ultra are unchanged.

Everything is paid for in Koins, the platform's credit, deducted per task according to the model, cloud time and third-party tools used, so there is no fixed price per game. A new account gets 360 Koins on sign-up, with no credit card. The toggle says annual saves 22%, which is true for Pro only; the plan cards themselves say 15% on Standard and 17% on Ultra.

Full pricing notes (2 more paragraphs)

Standard unlocks full game generation, the Pro model and project downloads; Pro adds the Seele02-Ultra model and a commercial licence; Ultra adds all-channel commercial use including resale, 200GB of storage, a private cloud host, mini-game publishing and dedicated support. Koin Packs without a plan cost $20 for 2,000, $50 for 5,000, $200 for 20,000 and $500 for 50,000, and the pack page says bought Koins never expire.

The plan cards say unused Koins roll over and never expire. On 2 October the upgrade page applied a 'New User 50% Off First Month' coupon by default; in our session on 17 November we saw no such offer on the plans. Payment by card through Stripe. The terms name SEELE AI, Inc. and the usage policy SEELE Technology Limited. Seele's llms.txt (read 27 October 2026) says a free tier is available and that commercial-use rights need an active Pro plan or above, and the terms (dated 28 July 2026) say prices are in US dollars and that a price change on a subscription takes effect at the next renewal.

Plans change, so check the live price on their site before you buy.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

Koins are deducted by the AI time, cloud time and third-party tools a task uses, and the help centre says every change you ask for, from renaming a character to fixing a bug, is a new generation that costs Koins again. It also says Unity projects cost significantly more than lightweight Three.js web games. The 360 free Koins are meant for a first build, and one public review we read, from a 16-year-old, says he ran out before finishing his first game.

The cheapest plan does not let you sell what you make. Commercial use is listed on Pro and Ultra only, and the terms (section 4.2, updated 28 July 2026) say the right to publish, sell or advertise in your games comes with an active subscription at Pro or above, which is $100 a month or $78 billed annually (read on seeles.ai/upgrade on 17 November 2026 in our own test session).

On Standard at $20 you can build and share, not earn. The pricing page and the terms disagree on the two things that protect your money. The upgrade page FAQ says unused Koins roll over and the help centre says they never expire, while the terms say unused subscription Koins do not carry over to the next billing period. The upgrade page advertises a 7-day free trial and a 7-day money-back guarantee, while the terms say all payments are final and non-refundable, and that refunds may be refused for anything bought with a coupon or discount, which would cover the 50% first-month offer the page applied by default on 2 October 2026; we did not see that offer on 17 November.

The terms also say a chargeback raised without first emailing support will be treated as fraud. Finally, the homepage promises you can take your Unity project with you, while the help centre's article on Koins says source code download is currently not available. We did not try to export a project ourselves. The export picture has moved since we first read it. Seele's guide, read on 27 October 2026, tells you to switch to the files view to review, download or export the project, and lists Three.js, Unity, Godot and Unreal as target engines, with typical output ranging from a playable web build to a Unity project with a WebGL or mobile build.

Its llms.txt draws the line for Unreal: the browser-playable 3D build is not a native Unreal project or .uproject, and Blueprint, C++, plugins and packaging must be built and validated separately. Seele's upgrade page, read on 17 November 2026 in our own test session, lists project downloads from Standard upward and not on the free plan, so do not count on one with the sign-up Koins. The terms also contradict themselves on Koins.

Section 5.3 says Koin does not expire and stays in your account until used; section 5.5 says unused subscription Koin does not carry over to the next billing period. The likely reading is that bought Koin Packs stay and the monthly plan allowance resets, but the terms do not say so in those words. On termination, unused Koin and subscription time are forfeited without refund. Disputes go to binding arbitration in Hong Kong, the minimum age is 13 (with parental consent under 18), and section 8.3 gives Seele a licence to use your games, screenshots and gameplay footage in its own marketing, next to a note that the AI may generate similar or identical output for other users.

What Seele does

AI game-studio agent that turns a text prompt, image or drawing into a playable 2D or 3D game in the browser, with Three.js, Unity and Unreal targets and generated 3D assets, images, video and audio, billed in Koin credits.

Pro is the real entry price if you want to sell

Use the 360 free Koins to see how far one game gets you, and build it in Three.js first, since the help centre says web games use fewer Koins than Unity. If the game is for fun or for a class, Standard at $20 a month is enough; its 2,000 Koins are the same amount a $20 Koin Pack buys, but the plan also gives you the better model, asset downloads and priority queue that a pack does not.

If you plan to put the game on a store, run ads in it or sell it, the terms make Pro the minimum: $100 a month, or $78 a month billed annually (read on seeles.ai/upgrade on 17 November 2026 in our own test session). That is the one plan where the advertised 22% annual saving is accurate; on Standard it is 15% ($17 against $20) and on Ultra the card says 17% ($167 against $200).

Ultra at $200 is for heavy users who need 25,000 Koins a month and publishing support. If a coupon or discount is offered at checkout, think twice before taking it if a refund might matter to you, because the terms allow refunds to be refused on anything bought with a discount. The Koin table changes the plan maths. On the Pro model one game generation of about 1,400 to 1,500 Koins uses most of Standard's 2,000 for the month, so Standard is in practice a Flash-model plan.

Pro's 12,000 Koins cover about eight Pro-model game generations, or about three games with four revisions each. The guide also says Koins can be earned for free through first login, events, community tasks, sharing games and plays on your published games, and that revenue from ads or in-app purchases added to a published game can be withdrawn; selling or advertising still needs Pro or above under the terms.

    How to actually use Seele

    Sign up at seeles.ai with an email or Google account to receive the 360 sign-up Koins; no card is needed. Start a new workspace from one of the ready-made skills, such as a 2D or web game, rather than a blank prompt, and keep the first request small: every follow-up change costs Koins again, so describe the game fully up front instead of iterating line by line. Check the usage dashboard after each step to learn what a typical change costs.

    If a generation fails because of a server error, email service@seeles.ai with a screenshot and the page address; the help centre says Koins for failed tasks are refunded to your balance after a manual check, but not for results you simply dislike. You can log in on only one device at a time. Before you subscribe, decide whether you will sell the game, because that decides between Standard and Pro, and keep a copy of the terms page from the day you pay, since it and the pricing page do not say the same thing about refunds and unused Koins.

    Cancel from your account settings; access continues to the end of the billing period. The guide's own Koin table is the best budgeting tool we found. Generating a playable game costs roughly 120 to 140 Koins with the Flash model and 1,400 to 1,500 with Pro; each revision about 77 to 81 on Flash and 520 to 710 on Pro; an image about 20, a 3D asset or scene about 18 and a video about 186, all given as estimated averages.

    Frequently asked questions

    How much does Seele cost?

    Standard is $20 a month for 2,000 Koins, Pro $100 for 12,000 and Ultra $200 for 25,000, or $17, $78 and $167 a month billed annually. New accounts get 360 free Koins. Read on seeles.ai/upgrade on 17 November 2026 in our own test session.

    Can I sell games I make with Seele?

    Only on Pro or Ultra. The terms say commercial use of what you create requires an active subscription at Pro or above; the $20 Standard plan does not include it.

    How many Koins does a game cost?

    There is no fixed price, but Seele's guide (read 27 October 2026) gives estimated averages: about 120 to 140 Koins to generate a playable game on the Flash model and 1,400 to 1,500 on Pro, and about 77 to 81 (Flash) or 520 to 710 (Pro) per revision. Every change is charged as a new generation, and the help centre said in October that Unity projects cost significantly more than Three.js web games.

    Does Seele run on my computer or in the cloud?

    In the cloud. Seele's homepage, read on 22 October 2026, describes a single cloud environment with files, preview, agent chat and runtime in one tab. Which export formats you get and whether a project runs locally outside Seele is not confirmed in the pages we read, so check that before you build something you need to take elsewhere.

    Is Seele only for games?

    No. The same homepage (read 22 October 2026) says the agent generates games, images, video, 3D assets and code. All of it is paid from the same Koin balance, so asset work draws on the credits you budgeted for games.

    Can I download my Seele project?

    The guide (read 27 October 2026) says the files view lets you review, download or export the project, and that you can name a target engine (Three.js, Unity, Godot or Unreal) when you need files to continue developing. Two limits: Seele's llms.txt says the Unreal-style build is browser-playable 3D, not a native Unreal project, and a help-centre article we read on 2 October said source code download was not available. Seele's upgrade page, read on 17 November 2026 in our own test session, lists project downloads from the Standard plan upward; the free plan does not include them.

    Do unused Seele Koins roll over?

    The terms (dated 28 July 2026) say both: Koin does not expire (section 5.3), but unused subscription Koin does not carry over to the next billing period (section 5.5). Plan for the monthly allowance to reset, and buy a Koin Pack only for Koins you want to keep. If the account is terminated, unused Koin and subscription time are forfeited without refund.

    Can Seele use the games I make?

    Yes, for its own promotion. You own your content and the AI output, but section 8.3 of the terms gives Seele a worldwide, royalty-free licence to use it, including games, screenshots and gameplay footage, for promotional and marketing purposes. The terms also say the AI may give other users similar or identical output, and do not guarantee that your game can be protected by intellectual property rights.

    Where are disputes with Seele settled?

    In binding arbitration in Hong Kong, according to the terms read 27 October 2026. The contracting company is SEELE Technology Limited, while the refund section names SEELE AI, Inc. and directs every billing question to service@seeles.ai before any bank dispute.
