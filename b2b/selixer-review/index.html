The entry tier is not an entry tier

Three plans, no free tier, a 14-day trial on each, and no annual rate published anywhere — so everything here is monthly, billed in USD every 30 days (re-verified 10 September 2026 on the Shopify listing). Essence is $149, Catalyst $299 and Aether $399. Read the feature lists rather than the prices and the bottom rung shrinks. Essence gives two seats, one connected ad platform, a year of history, the ad dashboard and an inventory health score.

The full True Profit waterfall, the per-product P&L and SKU action matrix, POAS, CAC and product-level ad revenue — which is the entire reason anyone installs a profit dashboard — all begin on Catalyst. The real price of the product this app is sold on is $299, exactly double the headline. What Essence does give you is history: a year of it, where Catalyst drops the date cap altogether. So the entry tier can answer whether this quarter beat last quarter.

What it cannot do is tell you which campaign or which SKU took the margin, and that is the question a profit dashboard exists to answer. Above that, the ad-platform caps decide the tier as firmly as the features do — one, then two, then three. If you run Meta, Google and TikTok, you are on Aether at $399 for connectivity alone, whatever your revenue looks like, and with no annual option that is $4,788 a year at full rate.

The per-seat maths runs oddly: $74.50 a seat on Essence, $74.75 on Catalyst and $49.88 on Aether, so the middle tier is the one place seats get no cheaper. Then there is the part of the bill that is not a tier at all. Every plan carries an AI credit allowance — 500, 2,000, 3,000 — and top-ups start at $25. The listing files those as usage-based charges on the same 30-day cycle, which makes the monthly figure a floor rather than a ceiling.

Ask what consumes a credit before you plan around it. Then weigh the thing that is not on the price list. This app launched on 19 May 2026 and carries a single public review. Use the 14 days to reconcile its numbers against a month you have already closed in your own books. If the profit figure it reports does not match the one you already know to be true, you have learned the most important thing about it for free.

How to actually use Selixer

Pick the trial tier by what you need to test, not by price. Essence's single ad platform cannot demonstrate the profit waterfall or the campaign POAS tracking, because neither exists on that plan — so a trial on Essence tells you little about the product you would actually buy. Spend the 14 days on one job: reconcile Selixer against a month you have already closed in your own books. Load the same period, compare its profit figure to your accounts, and find out where the two disagree — shipping, refunds, transaction fees and cost of goods are the usual culprits.

A dashboard you cannot audit is worse than no dashboard, and this one has one public review to go on. Count your ad platforms before you count your revenue. The caps are one, two and three, so running Meta, Google and TikTok puts you on the $399 plan regardless of how small the store is. And note two things the headline price does not carry: there is no annual rate published, so budget twelve months at the monthly figure, and every plan comes with a fixed AI credit allowance on top, with top-ups from $25.
