The entry tier is not an entry tier

Three plans, no free tier, a 14-day trial on each, and no annual rate published anywhere — so everything here is monthly, billed in USD every 30 days (re-verified 10 September 2026 on the Shopify listing). Essence is $149, Catalyst $299 and Aether $399. Read the feature lists rather than the prices and the bottom rung shrinks. Essence gives two seats, one connected ad platform, a year of history, the ad dashboard and an inventory health score.

The full True Profit waterfall, the per-product P&L and SKU action matrix, POAS, CAC and product-level ad revenue — which is the entire reason anyone installs a profit dashboard — all begin on Catalyst. The real price of the product this app is sold on is $299, exactly double the headline. What Essence does give you is history: a year of it, where Catalyst drops the date cap altogether. So the entry tier can answer whether this quarter beat last quarter.

What it cannot do is tell you which campaign or which SKU took the margin, and that is the question a profit dashboard exists to answer. Above that, the ad-platform caps decide the tier as firmly as the features do — one, then two, then three. If you run Meta, Google and TikTok, you are on Aether at $399 for connectivity alone, whatever your revenue looks like, and with no annual option that is $4,788 a year at full rate.

The per-seat maths runs oddly: $74.50 a seat on Essence, $74.75 on Catalyst and $49.88 on Aether, so the middle tier is the one place seats get no cheaper. Then there is the part of the bill that is not a tier at all. Every plan carries an AI credit allowance — 500, 2,000, 3,000 — and top-ups start at $25. The listing files those as usage-based charges on the same 30-day cycle, which makes the monthly figure a floor rather than a ceiling.

Ask what consumes a credit before you plan around it. Then weigh the thing that is not on the price list. This app launched on 19 May 2026 and carries a single public review. Use the 14 days to reconcile its numbers against a month you have already closed in your own books. If the profit figure it reports does not match the one you already know to be true, you have learned the most important thing about it for free.

Frequently asked questions

What does Selixer get access to in my store?

More than most analytics apps, and it is worth reading the grant before the price. The listing's data-access block, read 19 September 2026, asks to view customer data including names, email addresses, phone numbers and physical addresses, plus device and activity data — geolocation, IP address, browser and operating system, browsing behaviour and the client ID cookie. It also asks to edit orders, including all order history, returns and shipping information, to view discounts and gift cards, to edit store analytics and web pixels, and to view your Shopify Payments account and payouts. Most of that is what a true-profit dashboard genuinely needs: it cannot compute what you keep without fees, refunds and payouts. The part to weigh is who is holding it — a developer in Ahmedabad whose app launched in May 2026 — and whether your own privacy notice covers a processor with this reach. Read their privacy policy before you connect, not after.

What are the AI credits, and what happens when they run out?

They are the usage half of the bill, and the listing prices the top-up without ever saying what a credit buys. Each plan carries a fixed monthly allowance — 500 on Essence, 2,000 on Catalyst, 3,000 on Aether — with top-ups starting at $25, and Shopify files them as usage-based charges on the same 30-day cycle as the subscription. What no plan card explains is the exchange rate: how many credits an AI insight, a WhatsApp agent reply or a forecast consumes, or whether those features simply stop when the allowance is gone. On a $149 plan a $25 top-up is a sixth of the subscription, so a store that leans on the AI features could quietly move up a price bracket without changing plans. Ask for the credit cost of the two or three actions you expect to use daily, and check during the trial whether the counter moves faster than you expected.

One public review and a May 2026 launch — how do I de-risk this?

By spending the 14-day trial on reconciliation rather than exploration. Every tier carries the trial, so pick the one whose features you would actually buy — on Essence the profit waterfall and the campaign-level POAS do not exist, so a trial there tells you little — and use it on a month you have already closed in your own books. Load the period, compare Selixer's profit figure against your accounts, and find where they disagree: shipping, refunds, transaction fees and cost of goods are the usual culprits. That single exercise tells you more than the App Store record can, which on 19 September 2026 is one five-star review from a merchant who had used the app a fortnight. That reviewer also wrote that it is worth getting in touch for better offers, which is a hint rather than a rate card, but on a $149 to $399 subscription from a young vendor the conversation costs you nothing.
