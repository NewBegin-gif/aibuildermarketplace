The meter is your whole store, not the app.

Best for: shopify stores that want bundles, add-ons, cart funnels and post-purchase offers out of one app and would rather be billed on order volume than hand over a share of the upsell revenue — and small stores in particular, since under 50 orders a month the full strategy set costs nothing.

Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 19 Sep 2026

Billed in USD. The App Store listing shows one ladder, Starter, and it is priced by your store's total monthly order count rather than by anything the app does: free up to 50 orders a month, $9 from 51 to 500, $19 from 501 to 1,000, and $29 above 1,000. All four steps carry the same 18 upsell and cross-sell strategies, 50 free AI recommendations, free setup on a call and chat, email and Zoom support, with a 30-day free trial on the paid steps. Logbase's own pricing page adds two tiers the listing does not show.

Frequently asked questions

What is Selleasy actually metering?

Your store's total monthly orders, not the orders its upsells influenced. Read on apps.shopify.com/upsell-cross-sell-kit-1 on 19 September 2026: Starter Tier I is free to install up to 50 orders a month, Tier II is $9 from 51 to 500, Tier III is $19 from 501 to 1,000 and Tier IV is $29 above that — all four carrying the same 18 upsell strategies and the same 50 free AI recommendations. That cuts both ways. Two orders a day exhausts the free tier whether or not a single upsell ever fired, and a 5,000-order store running one quiet bundle still pays the top step. It stays cheap in either direction, but it is not the meter most people assume they are signing up to, and a merchant review on the same listing names exactly this as the reason they left.

Is $29 the most I can pay?

No. The App Store listing shows only the Starter ladder, which stops at $29, but Logbase's own site carries two tiers above it. Pro is $49 a month or $499 a year and removes the order limit entirely, adding 1,000 AI recommendations a month, an AI campaign optimiser, checkout upsells for Shopify Plus and advanced targeting. Max is $99 a month or $1,009 a year, adding 2,500 recommendations, advanced customisation, a rules engine, an AOV advisor and priority support. Enterprise is quoted on request. The two upgrades a growing store is most likely to want — AI recommendations at volume, and checkout upsells on Plus — both sit above the ceiling the listing shows you.

If the price goes up, am I moved onto the new one?

On the evidence of Logbase's own public answer, no. When a merchant complained on the App Store in November 2025 that the fee had climbed from $8.99 to $16.99 to $19, Logbase replied in public with the account history: that store installed in September 2024, paid $8.99 while it sat in the 51-500 order band, moved to $16.99 once it passed 500 orders a month, and would stay at a $16.99 ceiling — the $19 rate applied only to stores installing from that point on. So two different things were being felt at once: the order band doing what it says, and a list price that new installs pay and existing ones do not. It is a support reply rather than a published policy, so ask for it in writing if the grandfathering is part of your decision.

What should I weigh before buying Growth & Revenue software?

It depends on whether you have the bottleneck it solves. Small teams get the most out of this category when one clear problem is already costing real hours or revenue; buying ahead of that just adds cost and another login. Price it against the hours or lost deals it removes, not against its feature list, and start on the smallest plan that covers the job.
