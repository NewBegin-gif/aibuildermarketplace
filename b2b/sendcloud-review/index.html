What Sendcloud does

European e-commerce shipping platform: connect 160+ carriers, print labels in batches, show delivery options at checkout, send branded tracking (email, WhatsApp, SMS) and run a self-service returns portal — with rules-based shipping automation. Available to online retailers in 8 countries (Netherlands, Belgium, UK, France, Germany, Austria, Italy, Spain). Free plan available.

Pricing (2026)

(2026, tiers renamed) A genuine Free plan (€0/mo, no courier contract, no subscription — you pay only carrier costs, with limited scope). Paid tiers, billed monthly or yearly (-20%): Lite €28/mo + €0.10 per label, Growth €87/mo + €0.09, Premium €175/mo + €0.08, Pro €639/mo + €0.07; Enterprise is bespoke. Note the double meter: a subscription AND a per-label fee on every paid plan. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

The pricing has layers — a per-label fee on top of the subscription, plus carrier surcharges that can land on invoices weeks later. It's built out-of-the-box, so deep customisation is limited, there's no inventory management (ShipStation has it), and high-volume shippers (€12k+/mo) plus pickup-reliability complaints suggest it's happiest with small-to-mid EU stores.

What buyers report about Sendcloud

Recurring themes from G2, Capterra and Trustpilot, read in August 2026. We have not run Sendcloud ourselves, so this is other buyers' experience rather than ours. We summarise it here because the complaints are usually the part a vendor page leaves out.

What they praise
  • • Multiple carriers, automated labels and returns in one place — for a store shipping across Europe that consolidation is the whole value.
  • • You can bring your own carrier contracts on paid plans rather than being locked into their rates.
What they complain about
  • • The bill has three layers: subscription, per-label fee (€0.12 on Lite down to €0.08 on Pro, plus €0.15 for each label over your included volume), and the actual postage. Carrier surcharges can land weeks later.
  • • One reviewer reports rates invoiced around 30% above public prices for certain carriers, and questions how those can be called discounted. Check a sample of your own invoices against published rates.
  • • Bugs are reported, notably in Royal Mail rate selection.
  • • A merchant spending over €12,000 a month describes 18 months of DPD pick-up failures needing weekly support contact. Support consistency is the recurring theme.
Best for: European e-commerce stores that want easy multi-carrier shipping (DHL, PostNL, Colissimo, GLS, DPD), branded tracking and a returns portal live in under 30 minutes.
Who should skip it: if you ship mainly in the US, need inventory management and complex fulfilment, or you're a very high-volume shipper wanting deep customisation — ShipStation fits that better.

Frequently asked questions

Does Sendcloud replace my current setup or sit on top of it?

Usually it sits on top rather than replacing everything. Tools in this class tend to become the layer where work is tracked and handed off, while your existing systems stay the source of truth for their own data. Before you buy, map which tools it would replace outright and which it would merely sync with. If the answer is that it syncs with all of them, you are adding a layer rather than removing one.

Who should not use Sendcloud?

Skip it if you ship mainly in the US, need inventory management and complex fulfilment, or you're a very high-volume shipper wanting deep customisation — ShipStation fits that better. Buying a tool to fix a problem you don't have yet just adds cost and another login to manage.
