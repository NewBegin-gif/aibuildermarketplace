Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 12 Sep 2026

Four tiers on apps.shopify.com/seokart, billed in USD and recurring every 30 days through Shopify. Free covers 50 pages of one-click AI optimisation, 10 tracked keywords checked monthly, 50 image optimisations and 50 pages of bulk editing, with limited rich snippets. Pro is $20 a month or $216 a year and covers 1,000 products with 100 tracked keywords checked weekly, unlimited image and bulk optimisation, full rich snippets, a 404 fixer and Cruise Control auto-SEO.

Plus is $30 a month or $324 a year for 5,000 products, Max $40 or $432 for 10,000, and the annual price saves 10% on all three. Read the ladder for what it does not change: from Pro upward the keyword tracking stays at 100 and the feature list is word for word identical, so every step above $20 buys catalogue size and nothing else.

The vendor's own page, seokart.com/pricing read the same day, publishes only two app tiers — Free and Pro at $20 — so for this app the Shopify listing is the fuller source and the vendor site the shorter one, which is the reverse of the usual pattern. What that page does publish on its own is a managed SEO service at $799, $1,299 and $1,949 a month.

That is a different product from the app, and it is where the free SEO consultancy in the plan table leads. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

seokart.com sells managed SEO at $799, $1,299 and $1,949 a month next to the app (read 12 September 2026), so the consultancy is also the front of an agency retainer. The evidence base is decent rather than deep: 69 reviews on 12 September 2026, launched 4 June 2020, developer Activant Solutions Pvt Ltd in Jaipur. The 5.0 average hides the shape — 67 five-star reviews and two one-star, with nothing in between.

Then the two limits that decide whether this is the right tool. The rank tracker checks monthly on Free and weekly on Pro and above, so it is a trend line, not a daily rank check, and 100 keywords is the ceiling on every paid tier. And one-click AI optimisation writes meta titles and descriptions in bulk: that is the whole appeal, but generic AI metadata on a thousand products is a real outcome, so the pass you have to budget for is reading what it wrote on your best-selling pages before you let Cruise Control run unattended.

The app also needs access to products, store analytics and the Online Store, plus blog contributor data.

What SEOKart does

Shopify SEO app with one-click AI optimisation: bulk meta tags, image alt text and file names, URL edits, JSON-LD schema and rich snippets, 404 fixing and 301 redirects, image compression for page speed, and a built-in keyword rank tracker with a Cruise Control auto-SEO mode. Free plan covers 50 pages and 10 keywords; Pro $20 a month or $216 a year for 1,000 products, Plus $30 or $324 for 5,000, Max $40 or $432 for 10,000 — above Pro the ladder buys catalogue size and nothing else. Frequently asked questions

What should I weigh before buying SEO & Marketing software?

Judge it on evidence rather than adjectives. Look for a published security page, single sign-on and role controls on the plan you would actually buy (they are often gated to higher tiers), a status page with real incident history, and a clear answer on where your data is stored. If the tool will hold anything sensitive, those four answers matter more than any feature comparison.
