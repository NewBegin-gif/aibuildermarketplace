Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 15 Sep 2026

Two publishers, and they do not publish the same ladder. The Shopify listing, https://apps.shopify.com/seokart, bills in USD and recurs every 30 days through Shopify, with four fixed tiers. Free covers 50 pages of one-click AI optimisation, 10 tracked keywords checked monthly, 50 image optimisations and 50 pages of bulk editing, with limited rich snippets. Pro is $20 a month or $216 a year for 1,000 products with 100 tracked keywords checked weekly, unlimited image and bulk optimisation, full rich snippets, a 404 fixer and Cruise Control auto-SEO.

Plus is $30 a month or $324 a year for 5,000 products, Max $40 or $432 for 10,000, and annual billing saves 10% on all three. On those four cards the tracked-keyword line never moves off 100 and the feature lists above Pro are word for word identical. The vendor's own page sells the same app as one configurable Pro plan and runs much further.

Under the APP tab on https://seokart.com/pricing (read 15 September 2026) the card carries two dropdowns. Catalogue size, priced at the base 100 keywords: 1,000 products $20 a month, 5,000 $30, 10,000 $40, 25,000 $75, 50,000 $100, 100,000 $150, 500,000 $300, 1,000,000 $500 and 2,000,000 $800. Tracked keywords are the second dropdown and run 100, 200, 300, 400, 500. That step is charged, and it costs more the larger the catalogue: on 1,000 products the five choices are $20, $30, $40, $50 and $60 a month, and on 10,000 products they are $40, $60, $80, $100 and $120.

So 500 keywords on a 10,000-product store is $120 a month, three times the $40 the Shopify card presents as the top of the range. The first three catalogue rungs match the Shopify tiers exactly; what the listing leaves out is everything above 10,000 products and the keyword dropdown altogether. The same vendor page also sells a managed SEO service at $799, $1,299 and $1,949 a month (read 14 September 2026).

That is a separate, optional service alongside the app, and it is what the free SEO consultancy in the plan table opens the door to. The app is sold for BigCommerce too: that listing, https://www.bigcommerce.com/apps/seokart/ (read 14 September 2026, last updated there 2 September 2026), publishes a $20 a month recurring fee, no upfront charge and a 10-day free trial. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

seokart.com sells a managed SEO service at $799, $1,299 and $1,949 a month (read 14 September 2026). That is a separate, optional service rather than a tier of the app, and a free consultation is the natural place for a conversation about it to start. The evidence base is decent rather than deep: 69 reviews on 12 September 2026, launched 4 June 2020, developer Activant Solutions Pvt Ltd in Jaipur.

The 5.0 average hides the shape — 67 five-star reviews and two one-star, with nothing in between. Then the two limits that decide whether this is the right tool. The rank tracker checks monthly on Free and weekly on Pro and above, so it is a trend line, not a daily rank check, and 100 tracked keywords is what every Shopify plan card publishes, but that is not the ceiling: the vendor's own configurator at seokart.com/pricing sells 200 to 500 tracked keywords as a priced step, $10 a month per extra 100 on a 1,000-product store and $20 a step on a 10,000-product one (read 15 September 2026).

And one-click AI optimisation writes meta titles and descriptions in bulk: that is the whole appeal. This review is researched rather than hands-on, so take the next line as general practice with any bulk AI writer and not as something we watched SEOKart produce -- budget a pass for reading what it wrote on your best-selling pages before you let Cruise Control run unattended. The app also needs access to products, store analytics and the Online Store, plus blog contributor data.

When the paid plan is worth it

The free plan covers 50 pages of AI optimisation, 10 keywords checked monthly, and limited image, bulk and schema tools. The Shopify card labels it a trial, but seokart.com is explicit that there is no time limit and you can keep using it (read 14 September 2026), so a store that fits inside those 50 pages has a real free option rather than a countdown. Pro at $20 a month is where a larger catalogue becomes workable: 1,000 products, unlimited image and bulk optimisation, full schemas, the 404 fixer and Cruise Control auto-SEO.

Above Pro the ladder is longer than the Shopify listing lets on. Those cards stop at Max, $40 for 10,000 products, and the feature lists on Pro, Plus and Max are word for word identical. The vendor's own configurator at seokart.com/pricing keeps going on the same plan: $75 for 25,000 products, $100 for 50,000, $150 for 100,000, and on up to $800 a month for two million (read 15 September 2026).

Annual billing saves 10% on the three tiers Shopify publishes. Rank tracking is the limit worth budgeting for, and it is a priced extra rather than a fixed ceiling. Every Shopify card publishes 100 tracked keywords; the vendor's configurator adds a second dropdown running 100 to 500, and each step costs real money -- $10 a month per extra 100 keywords on a 1,000-product store, $20 a month per step on a 10,000-product one, so 500 keywords there is $120 a month against the $40 the listing implies is the ceiling.

Nothing checks more often than weekly at any price, so this stays a trend line rather than a daily rank check. And no tier at any price adds keyword research or backlink data: what the money buys is catalogue size and how many keywords you may watch. The free SEO consultancy on every plan sits next to the vendor's managed service at $799 to $1,949 a month: a separate, optional service, not a higher tier.

How to actually use SEOKart

The vendor publishes an order of operations and it is a sensible one. Their guide says to start with the SEO Optimizer on your top 25 best-sellers rather than the whole catalogue, using the AI assist for meta tags and product descriptions. Then run the 404 Fixer over broken links, add rich snippets, and only then let the Bulk Optimizer fill blank meta tags across the store. Image optimisation, size and alt text, comes next; the rank tracker last, once the optimisations are done and there is something to measure.

Two things are worth knowing first. There is a Report/Restore feature with its own tutorial, and that is the undo you want within reach before a bulk run touches a thousand products. And Cruise Control, the auto-SEO on Pro and above, is the switch to leave off until you have read what the AI wrote on the pages that actually earn money. Google Analytics 4 is connected separately, with a walkthrough of its own.

Distilled from Setup steps and the recommended order come from SEOKart's own tutorial hub, https://seokart.com/how-to-guide/ (read 12 September 2026), which lists video walkthroughs for the SEO Optimizer, Image Optimizer, Bulk Optimizer, page-speed, rich snippets, GA4, Report/Restore, rank tracker, 404 fixer and URL editor, and closes with a written recap of the sequence. Plans, limits and the feature lists were read the same day on the Shopify listing, https://apps.shopify.com/seokart, and the managed-service prices on https://seokart.com/. The app also ships for BigCommerce: the vendor's pricing page links out to https://www.bigcommerce.com/apps/seokart/, and that listing (read 14 September 2026, last updated there 2 September 2026) publishes the same $20 a month recurring fee, no upfront charge and a 10-day free trial. The vendor's own pricing page was read again on 15 September 2026: under its APP tab the Pro card is a configurator with a catalogue dropdown (1,000 to 2,000,000 products, $20 to $800 a month) and a tracked-keyword dropdown (100 to 500), neither of which appears on the Shopify or BigCommerce listing. No independent third-party walkthrough was found worth citing.
