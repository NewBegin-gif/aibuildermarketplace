What Shiftie does

Online staff scheduling and team management. Build rotas in minutes, handle clock-ins, leave and shift swaps — scheduling without the spreadsheet headache.

Pricing (2026)

Per active employee, per month (2026): roughly £2.75/employee, dropping to about £1.50 once you pass ~30 staff; entry plans from about £39/mo. You only pay for active users, and all plans include a 28-day free trial. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

It's focused, affordable rota software — which means it does scheduling, clock-ins, leave and swaps well, but it isn't a full HR or payroll platform, so a growing business will still bolt on payroll and HR tools around it. Pricing is genuinely low, but the per-active-employee model means a large seasonal team in a busy month costs more than the headline suggests. For shift-based scheduling specifically, it's a clean, cheap fit.

Frequently asked questions

Does Shiftie replace my current setup or sit on top of it?

Usually it sits on top rather than replacing everything. Tools in this class tend to become the layer where work is tracked and handed off, while your existing systems stay the source of truth for their own data. Before you buy, map which tools it would replace outright and which it would merely sync with. If the answer is that it syncs with all of them, you are adding a layer rather than removing one.

Who should not use Shiftie?

Skip it if you need full HR, payroll and performance management in one system, or your team doesn't work in shifts at all. Buying a tool to fix a problem you don't have yet just adds cost and another login to manage.
