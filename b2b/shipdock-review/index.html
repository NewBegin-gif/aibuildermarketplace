Shipdock Review (2026)

E-commerceResearched assessment · by Daniel HaketUpdated 2026-09-25

Read the carrier list against your own country before anything else.

Best for: Shopify merchants shipping in Central and Eastern Europe who want pickup points, multi-carrier label printing and cash-on-delivery tracking in one app rather than one integration per carrier — and who are on Shopify Plus, where the pickup-point picker sits inside checkout instead of after it.
Who should skip it: if your carriers are not in its list, which is the first thing to check, since the app's centre of gravity is Packeta and the Central European networks; or you need a label only occasionally, when a CSV export and your carrier's portal cost nothing.
Price: From $9.99/mo (Lite, CSV export only); label printing starts at $14.99/mo on Basic · full pricing as we read it on 18 Sep 2026 ↓

Three tiers, no free plan, every amount unchanged since 22 August 2026, and the entry tier still names its own limitation. Lite is $9.99 a month or $107.88 a year and is labelled 'only CSV': pickup point and home delivery, an email reminder, the pickup-point widget on the thank-you and order-status pages, unlimited carriers across more than twenty, and an order export to custom CSV.

Basic is $14.99 or $161.89 a year and is the first tier that prints shipping labels, adding parcels created automatically after fulfilment and cash-on-delivery payment tracking. Shopify Plus is $24.99 or $269.89 and moves the pickup-point widget into the checkout itself rather than the pages after it. Annual billing saves a stated 10% on each. Trials are five days on Lite and Basic and ten on Plus.

Charges are in USD and recur every 30 days. The listing now names its carriers rather than counting them: Packeta, Balíkovna, DPD, Magyar Posta, InPost, DHL24, Posti, GLS and myGLS, Omniva, Foxpost, PPL, One, Telli, Eserviss, Esiunt, Slovenská pošta, Česká pošta, Fan Courier, Easyship, Econt and PostNord. Developer happyapps.cz; the app sits in Shopify's delivery and pickup category, is offered in English and Czech, and carries 42 reviews at a 5.0 rating — the same total as in August, but now with every one of the 42 at five stars where our earlier reading recorded one at four. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

The listing slug is 'packeta', the developer is happyapps.cz, and cash-on-delivery tracking is a headline feature — all three point at Central and Eastern Europe, where Packeta and Zásilkovna are everyday carriers and COD is a normal way to pay. More than twenty carriers are supported, but a US or UK merchant should confirm theirs is among them before the five-day trial starts. The second thing is the trial length against the job.

Five days on Lite and Basic is short for a shipping integration, because the test that matters is a real order going out with a real label to a real address. Install it on a day you are actually shipping, not on a quiet Sunday.

What Shipdock does

Shopify pickup points, multi-carrier label printing and cash-on-delivery tracking, strongest on the Central European carrier networks. Listed in the Shopify App Store as "ShipD Labels & Pickup points" (apps.shopify.com/packeta) - same app by happyapps.cz, Shipdock is the earlier name (checked 18 September 2026).

Where the ratings disagree. The geography is the finding, and it is unanimous. Across the three pages read here — thirty reviews — every single reviewer is in Czechia, Slovakia or Hungary, and roughly half write in Czech or Slovak. Not one merchant in Western Europe, the United Kingdom or the United States appears anywhere in the record. That is not a mark against the app: what it does have is a deeper, longer-running set of reviews than most shipping apps manage. It does mean the 5.0 is evidence about Zásilkovna, Packeta, PPL and cash on delivery, and evidence about nothing else. A merchant outside Central Europe is reading an average that has never been tested on their carriers, which makes checking the carrier list first, and then spending the five-day trial on a day you are genuinely shipping, the whole of the due diligence.

When the paid plan is worth it

The five dollars between Lite and Basic is the largest step on this ladder, whatever the numbers suggest. Lite exports a CSV; Basic prints labels, creates parcels automatically once you fulfil, and tracks cash-on-delivery payments. If you were going to hand a spreadsheet to a carrier portal anyway, Lite is a reasonable saving. If you expected to press print, Lite is the wrong product rather than the cheap one. The step to Shopify Plus at $24.99 is not a volume tier and does not read like one.

What it buys is placement: the pickup-point picker appears inside checkout instead of on the thank-you and order-status pages afterwards. That matters, because a shopper who chooses a pickup point after paying has already made a delivery decision without seeing the option. Worth knowing before you weigh that: checkout itself is only editable on Shopify Plus, so this is a limit of the platform rather than something the vendor chose to charge for.

If you are not on Plus, the tier is not available to you at any price, and the post-purchase placement is the only one there is.

How to actually use Shipdock

Check your carrier before you install. The app supports more than twenty, but it grew out of a Packeta integration and its strength is the Central European networks, so a merchant elsewhere should confirm the specific carrier rather than trust the number. Then pick the tier on one question: do you want to press print? Lite is labelled 'only CSV' and means it — it hands you a spreadsheet and you take it from there.

Basic is the first tier that prints labels and creates parcels automatically after fulfilment, and for most merchants that is the whole reason to install a shipping app at all. Start the trial on a shipping day. Five days is short, and the only test worth running is a genuine order: fulfil it, print the label, and confirm the pickup point the customer chose actually reaches the carrier. Reviewers describe setup as entering a customer ID and API credentials and then configuring the shipping profile, which is quick — the shipping profile is where the time goes.

Frequently asked questions

Will it work with my carrier?

Check the names rather than the count, because the listing now prints them. As read on 18 September 2026 it names Packeta, Balíkovna, DPD, Magyar Posta, InPost, DHL24, Posti, GLS and myGLS, Omniva, Foxpost, PPL, One, Telli, Eserviss, Esiunt, Slovenská pošta, Česká pošta, Fan Courier, Easyship, Econt and PostNord, with a trailing ellipsis that suggests more. Every one of those is a European network, and the ones a Czech, Slovak, Hungarian, Polish, Baltic, Romanian, Bulgarian or Nordic merchant uses daily are on it. What is not on it is as informative: no USPS, UPS, FedEx or Royal Mail. Full integration — the vendor's own word — is claimed for Packeta, DPD, GLS, Magyar Posta, Foxpost and Fan Courier, so even within Europe there is a first tier of support and a longer tail. If your carrier is in the tail, ask what 'supported' means for it before the five-day trial starts.

Do I need Basic just to track cash on delivery?

Yes, and it is worth knowing before you pick Lite on price. Cash-on-delivery payment tracking appears in the Basic plan text at $14.99 and in Shopify Plus at $24.99, not in Lite at $9.99. That matters more here than it would for most shipping apps, because this app's home markets are the ones where COD is an ordinary way to pay rather than an exception — the same $5 step also buys label printing and parcels created automatically after fulfilment. So if a meaningful share of your orders are paid at the door, Lite is not a cheaper version of what you need; it exports a spreadsheet and leaves both the labels and the reconciliation to you. The five dollars is the price of the app doing the work rather than describing it.

Is a 5.0 from 42 reviews something I can lean on?

Lean on what the reviews say, not on the average. Forty-two is a small record and, read on 18 September 2026, all forty-two sit at five stars — an unbroken run that tells you nobody has had a bad enough experience to write it down, but not much about how the app behaves at scale. What is useful is that several reviewers describe the same setup in the same terms: enter a customer ID and API credentials, then configure the shipping profile, with support resolving a configuration error quickly. Reviewers write from Czechia, Slovakia and elsewhere in the region, several of them in Czech, which corroborates where this app's strength actually is better than the rating does. Treat the 5.0 as the absence of complaints from a regional user base rather than as evidence the app fits a market its reviewers do not ship to.

What should I weigh before buying E-commerce software?

Check your platform and version specifically. Shopify support is close to universal in this category, while WooCommerce, BigCommerce, Magento and headless setups vary a lot, and 'supported' sometimes means a generic API rather than a maintained app. A listing in your own platform's app store, with recent reviews there, is a better signal than the vendor's logo wall.
