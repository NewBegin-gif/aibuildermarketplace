What Shippo does

Multi-carrier shipping software for e-commerce. Compare rates, print discounted labels and automate tracking across carriers — shipping that scales with your store.

Pricing (2026)

(2026) Starter is free (discounted carrier rates, but $0.05/label when connecting your OWN carrier accounts); Pro from ~$19/mo (200 labels, fee removed, branded tracking) stepping up with volume ($29/mo for 201-500…); Premier custom above ~10k labels/mo. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

The pricing is honest but layered: the free tier's per-label fee on your own carrier accounts quietly adds up, and Pro's volume steps mean your subscription auto-climbs with a good sales month. The real money is elsewhere anyway — in the carrier rate discounts, which vary by lane and weight. Run your five most common shipments through it against your current rates; that comparison decides, not the subscription price.

Best for: e-commerce sellers shipping across multiple carriers who want rate comparison, label printing and tracking in one place with genuine rate discounts.
Who should skip it: if you ship a handful of packages monthly through one carrier (their native tools are fine), or you're big enough for direct carrier contracts to beat aggregated rates.

The natural comparison is ShipStation or EasyPost — the direct shipping-software peers — compare discounted rates on your actual lanes and the workflow features your volume needs. Weigh the honest alternatives on our ShipStation alternatives page.

Frequently asked questions

Does Shippo replace my current setup or sit on top of it?

Usually it sits on top rather than replacing everything. Tools in this class tend to become the layer where work is tracked and handed off, while your existing systems stay the source of truth for their own data. Before you buy, map which tools it would replace outright and which it would merely sync with. If the answer is that it syncs with all of them, you are adding a layer rather than removing one.

Who should not use Shippo?

Skip it if you ship a handful of packages monthly through one carrier (their native tools are fine), or you're big enough for direct carrier contracts to beat aggregated rates. Buying a tool to fix a problem you don't have yet just adds cost and another login to manage.
