Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 11 Aug 2026

App plans. Starter is free: up to 30 labels a month, discounted USPS, UPS, FedEx and DHL Express rates, 1 user login, and 5¢ per label if you connect your own carrier account. Pro is listed as "Starts at $17/mo", with an annual option quoted at $205/year (a 10% saving); it covers 1-200 labels a month, makes your own carrier accounts free, and adds branded tracking pages, chat and phone support and up to 5 user logins.

The comparison table puts Pro at up to 10,000 shipments a month with $0.08 per label beyond that. Premier is contact-us: unlimited shipments, 15+ user logins and a dedicated account manager. Non-US address validation is billed separately at 9¢ per validation on Starter, 8¢ on Pro and 6¢ on Premier. API calls not tied to a Shippo label are billed at API Starter rates. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

The free tier stops at 30 labels a month, and its 5¢ per label on your own carrier accounts quietly adds up. Pro looks like a flat $17 a month until you read the comparison table: it covers up to 10,000 shipments and then bills $0.08 for every label beyond that, so a good sales month adds a variable line to a subscription that reads as fixed. Address validation outside the US is metered too, at 9¢, 8¢ or 6¢ depending on plan.

The real money is elsewhere anyway — in the carrier rate discounts, which vary by lane and weight. Run your five most common shipments through it against your current rates; that comparison decides, not the subscription price.

The $17 is the annual rate, and two numbers disagree

Start with two things on Shippo's own page that cannot both be true, because you will meet them at checkout. Pro is listed as 'Starts at $17/mo', with an annual option quoted at $205 a year described as a 10% saving. But $17 times twelve is $204, so the annual plan would cost a dollar more than paying monthly, and there would be no saving at all. The arithmetic only reconciles one way: the $17 is the annual rate expressed monthly, and the true month-to-month price is about $19.

That is an inference rather than a quoted figure, so check it on the checkout screen — but budget $19 if you intend to pay monthly. The second disagreement is about volume. The plan description says Pro covers 1 to 200 labels a month; the comparison table on the same page says up to 10,000 shipments a month with $0.08 per label beyond that. Those differ by a factor of fifty, and which one applies changes whether Pro is a small-merchant plan or a serious one.

Ask before you commit, especially if your volume sits between the two figures. Take the 10,000 reading and the marginal maths is stark: $17 spread across 10,000 labels is $0.0017 each, and the very next label costs $0.08 — forty-seven times as much. A subscription that reads as flat acquires a variable line the moment a good month arrives. Then the meters beside the plan. Starter is free for up to 30 labels a month with one user login, but charges 5¢ a label if you connect your own carrier account — which Pro makes free, along with branded tracking pages, chat and phone support and up to five logins.

Non-US address validation is billed separately at 9¢ on Starter, 8¢ on Pro and 6¢ on Premier: an international shipper validating a thousand addresses a month pays $80 on Pro, which is 4.7 times the subscription. API calls not tied to a Shippo label bill at API Starter rates. Premier is contact-us for unlimited shipments, 15+ logins and a dedicated account manager. None of which is the real money. That sits in the carrier rate discounts, which vary by lane and by weight and are the reason to use a platform like this at all.

Run your five most common shipments through it against the rates you pay today. That comparison decides this purchase; the subscription is rounding.

How to actually use Shippo

Price five real shipments before you look at a plan. Take your five most common parcels — the actual weights, the actual destinations — and compare Shippo's quoted rates against what you pay your carriers today. Discounts vary by lane and weight, so this is the only comparison that predicts your bill, and it dwarfs the subscription either way. Use the free Starter plan to do it. Thirty labels a month is enough to ship real orders and see real rates, though note the 5¢ per label if you connect your own carrier account rather than using Shippo's negotiated ones.

Then get two things clarified in writing before you upgrade. What Pro's included volume actually is — the plan description says 200 labels a month and the comparison table says 10,000, and the difference decides whether the $0.08 overage applies to you. And what you will pay monthly: $17 times twelve is a dollar more than the $205 annual price that claims a 10% saving, so the monthly rate is almost certainly about $19.

If you ship internationally, add address validation at 8¢ on Pro — a thousand a month is $80, several times the plan itself.

The natural comparison is ShipStation or EasyPost — the direct shipping-software peers — compare discounted rates on your actual lanes and the workflow features your volume needs. Weigh it against ShipStation on the job you actually need done.

What should I weigh before buying Operations & Workflow software?

Usually it sits on top rather than replacing everything.
