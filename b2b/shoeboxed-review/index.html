What Shoeboxed does

Turn paper and digital receipts into organized, IRS-ready expense data with OCR plus human verification — syncs with QuickBooks and Xero.

Pricing (2026)

(price re-verified 2026-08-02 against the vendor's own pricing page) (2026) Starter about $97/yr (30 digital scans/mo, 1 Magic Envelope/yr); Pro ~$297/yr (200 scans/mo, monthly paper scans, QuickBooks/Xero sync); Plus ~$79/mo; Paper Plus ~$179/mo for heavy mail-in volume. 30-day money-back guarantee. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

Its unique trick — the Magic Envelope, where you literally mail a stuffed envelope of paper receipts and humans digitize them — is also the meter to watch: envelope frequency defines the tiers, and heavy paper businesses land in the $179/mo bracket quickly. If your receipts are already digital, cheaper scan-only apps exist; Shoeboxed earns its price precisely when physical paper is the problem.

What buyers report about Shoeboxed

Recurring themes from GetApp, Capterra, Software Advice and SelectHub, read in August 2026. We have not run Shoeboxed ourselves, so this is other buyers' experience rather than ours. We summarise it here because the complaints are usually the part a vendor page leaves out.

What they praise
  • • The Magic Envelope is the reason people choose it specifically: post a full envelope of receipts and it is all in your account a few days later, ready for the accountant. Nothing else does that. 88% satisfaction across 259 reviews.
  • • For a business with a shoebox rather than a workflow, it removes the whole problem.
What they complain about
  • • Data extraction is not always accurate, categorisation is clunky and bulk actions are limited — reviewers report spending time correcting rather than filing.
  • • Automatic categorisation cannot be customised to match your chart of accounts.
  • • No automatic GPS mileage tracking; it is manual entry, unlike competing apps.
  • • $23/month annually or $29 monthly, with overage fees on top. For a low receipt volume reviewers say it does not pay.
Best for: businesses drowning in PAPER receipts — trades, field crews, owners with shoeboxes of history — who want them humanly digitized, verified and IRS-ready.
Who should skip it: if your receipts are mostly digital already (lighter OCR apps cost less), or your accountant's tooling ingests receipts natively.

Frequently asked questions

Is Shoeboxed safe to run my finances through?

The bar here is higher than features. Check who actually holds the money and under what licence, whether client funds are segregated, what audit trail and permission controls you get, and how support behaves when something goes wrong mid-transaction. Anything touching payments should be able to answer those in writing; if it cannot, that is your answer.

Who should not use Shoeboxed?

Skip it if your receipts are mostly digital already (lighter OCR apps cost less), or your accountant's tooling ingests receipts natively. Buying a tool to fix a problem you don't have yet just adds cost and another login to manage.
