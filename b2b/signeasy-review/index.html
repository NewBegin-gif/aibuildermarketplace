What Signeasy does

E-signature and contract management for SMBs — unlimited signing and sending, templates, audit trails and integrations, priced per seat.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

The $10 Personal plan is annual-billed and single-seat — and note that sign-unlimited is not send-unlimited: proper unlimited sending starts at Business, $20 per seat on a yearly commitment, while contract-management features gate at Business Pro, $30 per seat.

What buyers report about Signeasy

Recurring themes from G2 and Capterra, read in August 2026. We have not run Signeasy ourselves, so this is other buyers' experience rather than ours. We summarise it here because the complaints are usually the part a vendor page leaves out.

What they praise
  • • Signers do not need an account, and the drag-and-drop fields make what to fill in obvious. That is the whole friction point in e-signature, and reviewers say it is solved. 4.7 on both G2 (762 reviews) and Capterra.
  • • Offline mobile signing and unlimited templates from $20/month, with a $10 personal tier.
What they complain about
  • • Support is the one thing even positive reviewers flag as needing work.
  • • Reviewers do not find the pricing competitive against the cheaper alternatives, and small businesses call it expensive for what they use.
  • • There are region-based restrictions — check yours is covered before you commit.

When the paid plan is worth it

Signeasy's pricing page lists no free plan, only a 14-day free trial, and four tiers. Personal ($10/month/seat annual, $120 yearly; $15/seat monthly) covers 1 user and no templates. Business ($20/month/seat annual, $240 yearly; $30/seat monthly) adds 5 templates, automatic reminders and sequential signing. Business Pro ($30/month/seat annual, $360 yearly; $40/seat monthly) adds roles and permissions, team usage reports, unlimited templates, SharePoint and HubSpot. The fourth tier, Build Your Plan, is custom-priced for 5+ seats or high document volume.

The compare table's “Send for signature via email” row reads 5/month for Personal and Unlimited for the other three; the Business plan card's own feature “Send unlimited documents for signature” carries a tooltip ending “Fair usage policy applies.” API, SSO, HIPAA compliance and bulk send are ticked only in Build Your Plan's column; across the three seat-priced tiers those rows are blank or Add-on, and the API row carries the footnote “*Comes at an additional cost”.

Signature APIs are priced separately across four cards: a $0 Starter sandbox for 100 requests, Intermediate at $125/month ($1,499 billed annually) for 1000, Advanced at $250/month ($2,999 billed annually) for 2000 — both asterisked to “Fair usage restrictions applicable” — and a custom-quote tier.

How to actually use Signeasy

Start inside the 14-day trial with one real document and one template: upload, place fields, send. Templates are the tier trip-wire, since Personal has none, Business has 5 and Business Pro unlimited, so pick the tier before building a library. The bigger snag is Signeasy's own documentation. Its public FAQ describes a free account with 3 documents to sign, a “Pro plan” at 39.99 US$ per year and pay-as-you-go credits of 10 documents for 4.99 US$, none of which appear on the pricing page.

The FAQ calls that plan “for individual professionals only”. Mobile buyers writing public App Store reviews report the app prompting them to subscribe or renew while a paid subscription is active. The API pricing page's Starter card carries a Get Started button linking straight to developer.signeasy.com/signup for a free sandbox; the same page's FAQ answers “How can I try out the Signeasy APIs?” with contact us, after which a solution expert reaches out within 24–48 business hours with details on how to provision a sandbox account.

Frequently asked questions

Will Signeasy still be in your workflow after week two?

That is the real test, and it comes down to whether it removes a step or adds one. Tools that live inside something you already open daily tend to survive; tools that require a brand-new habit usually do not. Give it a fortnight on real work before paying annually. Who should not use Signeasy?
