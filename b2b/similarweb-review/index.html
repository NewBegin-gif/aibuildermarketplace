Frequently asked questions

What does Similarweb cost?

Nothing is published, and we re-checked on 14 August 2026 to be sure. The pricing page shows a feature matrix across the Competitive Intel, Strategic SEO, Ads Intel, Gen AI Intel and AI Studio suites, and every business tier routes to 'contact our sales team to discuss a custom package'. The practical consequence is that the number you are quoted is set by what a salesperson thinks you will pay, with no public anchor to argue from.

What should I pin down before the sales call?

Three things that are priced apart from the seats. Data history is an add-on — the page offers up to 37 months as an extra, not an inclusion. API access is sold standalone or inside the custom package. And the trial has no stated length on the page. Ask for the rate card in writing and ask what happens at renewal, because with no list price there is nothing to hold a renewal quote against.

Is the free trial safe to start?

Start it deliberately rather than casually. The page offers a free trial without stating how long it runs, so the length you get is whatever the signup flow gives you. Set your own cancellation reminder on the day you sign up instead of relying on the page to tell you when it ends.

Is Similarweb secure and reliable enough to depend on?

Judge it on evidence rather than adjectives. Look for a published security page, single sign-on and role controls on the plan you would actually buy (they are often gated to higher tiers), a status page with real incident history, and a clear answer on where your data is stored. If the tool will hold anything sensitive, those four answers matter more than any feature comparison.

Who should not use Similarweb?

Skip it if you want a quick check on one competitor — the free tier and public estimates cover that, and the paid product is an annual commitment agreed by phone. Buying a tool to fix a problem you don't have yet just adds cost and another login to manage.
