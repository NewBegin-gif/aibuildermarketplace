When the paid plan is worth it

There is no permanent free plan, so the paid floor has to earn itself back — and the vendor quotes that floor two ways. A solution page advertises "$29/month for 500 outgoing SMS messages," while the pricing page charges $39.00 for the same 500 credits, or $398.40 billed yearly; budget against the pricing page. Tiers scale on volume beside one shared feature list — two-way messaging, auto-replies and drip campaigns, unlimited contacts — so paying more buys volume, not capability.

The spend justifies itself for a business with an opted-in list that texts on a predictable rhythm: incoming SMS replies cost nothing (incoming MMS still costs 1 credit each), and a bigger bundle beats overage. Sporadic sending is the bad fit: unused credits roll over only until the end of the following month, and overage runs 5.5 cents per credit, so guessing high and guessing low both leak money. Annual billing saves 20% but freezes that guess for a year.

Upgrading does not remove the carrier layer: a local number is still $10 monthly plus a $4 registration, extra numbers $10 each, extra seats $20 monthly, and a dedicated short code begins at $1,000/mo. A 30-day refund covers a bad guess.

How to actually use SimpleTexting

Signup takes no credit card, and the 14-day trial is the whole evaluation window, so the number decision comes first. A local 10DLC number costs $10 per month plus a $4 one-time carrier registration, and the form asks for a legal company name, an EIN, a website, a customer support email and number, an opt-in method and two sample text messages. The usual stall is a mismatch between the stated use case and the submitted business details; the vendor says registration is often done in a day or less, and once the registry accepts it, activation takes minutes.

A toll-free number carries no additional monthly fee, but verification typically takes three to five business days and carriers cap unverified toll-free traffic until it clears. Once a number is live, the fastest route to something real is a keyword opt-in with a welcome message and a compliance auto-reply. Then go to the Campaigns tab, New campaign, name it for internal reference, paste the message, choose a contact list, and send now, schedule it, or set it to repeat.

Frequently asked questions

What does SimpleTexting cost?

From $39 a month for 500 credits, billed yearly at $398.40, up to about $909 a month at 50,000 credits. An SMS spends one credit and an MMS three, extra credits beyond the plan are 5.5 cents each, and unused credits roll over. Read on 17 August 2026.

What does it cost for a team, and for extra numbers?

Every paid plan already includes three user seats, which is more generous than most; beyond that it is $20 a month per seat. Numbers are separate: $10 a month each, with a local number carrying a $4 activation fee on top and a one-off $4 carrier registration for 10DLC compliance. Short codes are a different product entirely, starting at $1,000 a month.

Is there a discount for nonprofits?

Yes, and it is stated on the page: nonprofits, churches and other tax-exempt organisations receive 15% off any monthly plan. That stacks against the 20% yearly discount as an either/or rather than both, so ask which applies before committing to a year.
