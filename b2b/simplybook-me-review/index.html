SimplyBook.me Review (2026)Two Caps: Bookings and Features

By Daan Haket · Tested hands-on · written with an AI assistant · who does what

The plan ladder is set by bookings per month and by how many custom features you may switch on, and those two ceilings move independently — so you can outgrow a plan on features while nowhere near the booking cap, or the reverse.

Best for

service businesses that take appointments and want a booking page, reminders and online payment without building one.

Skip it if

you only need a simple calendar link for one person — a free scheduling link does that without a booking system on top.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

Free and Basic also carry SimplyBook.me branding on your booking page; white-label starts at Premium. Two integration limits sit in their own documentation. Calendar Sync works with web calendars only, and the help page warns it may not work with Microsoft Exchange calendars. The Zapier connector, which gained a create-booking action in June 2026, stops working once HIPAA or Google Authenticator is switched on — so the practices most likely to need HIPAA lose the Zapier route.

When the paid plan is worth it

The free plan is genuinely usable and that makes the decision unusually clear: 50 bookings a month, one provider, one custom feature, no card. If you take fewer than 50 appointments a month and work alone, you may never need to pay. What pushes you up is rarely the booking count — it is the custom features. Free gives you one, Basic three, Standard eight, and only Premium makes them unlimited.

Intake Forms, Accept Payments, Coupons & Gift Cards and Membership are each one entry on SimplyBook.me's own custom-features list; ordinary reminders are not on that list at all. So it is the paid extras that move you, and a business wanting all four is at Basic's ceiling of three before it starts, which means Standard at EUR 24.90. The other trigger is your own branding. Free and Basic show SimplyBook.me on your booking page; the white-label footer starts at Premium (EUR 49.90 annual), and full branding with a branded favicon only at quote-only Enterprise.

If you are sending clients to that page from your own site, price that in before you compare with the cheaper tiers.

    How to actually use SimplyBook.me

    Count two numbers before you pick a plan, because they cap you independently: appointments a month, and how many custom features you actually need switched on. Most people size on bookings, hit the feature ceiling first, and upgrade twice. Start on Free rather than the trial. The 14-day trial runs on premium features, so it tells you what the top plan feels like and not whether the tier you intend to buy is enough.

    Free is permanent and renews monthly — build the real booking page there. Build the booking page first, then count what you actually need switched on, because their custom-features list is where the plan ladder bites: Intake Forms, Accept Payments, Coupons & Gift Cards and Membership are each one entry on it. Connect your payment processor after that, remembering their fees are charged by Stripe, PayPal or Square directly and not by SimplyBook.me.

    Use the trial knowing what it leaves out: most custom features are included, but Coupons & Gift Cards, HIPAA, Import Clients and Point of Sale are not, so a clinic that needs HIPAA or a business moving an existing client list cannot test either before paying. The Free plan has no time limit but, per the pricing FAQ, has to be renewed monthly — put that date in your calendar so the booking page does not lapse.

    If your team runs on Outlook with Microsoft Exchange, test Calendar Sync on day one.

    Why did the pricing page quote Brazilian reais?

    On 20 September 2026 SimplyBook.me served its pricing page in reais to us from two European networks, with BRL in the page's own markup and no currency switcher. When we re-read it on 16 October 2026 the ladder was back in euros (EUR 11.9, 24.9 and 49.9 a month billed annually). The page picks the currency for you, so check which one the plan card shows before you compare it with another tool.

    Which limit will I hit first?

    Whichever of the three moves fastest for you, because they move independently. Every plan is bounded by monthly bookings (50, 100, 500, 2,000), by how many premium custom features you may have switched on at once (1, 3, 8, then unlimited) and by staff providers (1, 5, 15, 30). A two-chair salon with 300 bookings a month is nowhere near the Standard booking cap but can outgrow its feature count; a solo therapist who wants HIPAA and intake forms hits the feature wall while using a fraction of the bookings. Their FAQ, read 20 September 2026, is clear that free custom features never count against the limit — only premium ones do — and that you can toggle premium features on and off without losing data, which makes the feature ceiling a scheduling problem rather than a hard stop.

    What happens when I run out of bookings mid-month?

    Nothing breaks, which is the useful part. Their FAQ, read 20 September 2026, says the account stays active, your data stays safe and the booking page stays online; you get a notification with the choice to upgrade or buy additional bookings, and existing bookings are unaffected — customers can still view, reschedule or cancel. What you cannot do is take new ones until you pay. Worth knowing alongside the fee structure: SimplyBook.me itself charges no transaction fee, no setup fee and no per-user fee, but SMS credits, AI Booking credits, WhatsApp credits and extra bookings are metered add-ons, and your payment processor (Stripe, PayPal, Square, Authorize.net) bills its own rate on top.

    What do SimplyBook.me's add-ons cost?

    The add-on list on the pricing page (read 20 October 2026) is in dollars even when the plans show euros: 100 SMS credits $8, 100 WhatsApp credits $8, 100 AI Voice Booking credits $8, 100 extra bookings $4, Social Media & Ads $11 a month, a custom domain with three years of SSL $119, your own SMS gateway $149, and account-manager help at $100 an hour. The page notes that custom domains, SMS gateways and SMS credits are never part of the subscription fee.

    Is the paid Full Setup worth considering?

    It is a fixed package rather than consulting time: from €450, SimplyBook.me's account managers gather your company details in a meeting, then give four hours of setup, one hour with a designer, a recorded one-hour training session, a tutorial playlist and two months of premium subscription (pricing page, read 20 October 2026). Compare that with the $100 an hour listed for ad-hoc account-manager help.

    Which features sit on which SimplyBook.me plan?

    From the pricing FAQ, read 20 October 2026: Standard adds Client App branding and the HIPAA and SAML SSO custom features; Premium unlocks Payments PRO, the QuickBooks, FreshBooks and Xero integrations and the white-label page footer; Enterprise adds the Cluster Interface for multi-location access, a dedicated account manager, Queue Manager, a High Load API, full white label, a branded favicon and a choice of data location. Upgrades apply instantly with prorated billing, and a cancelled paid plan stays active until the end of the period.

    How does SimplyBook.me compare with Shore?

    Shore's own page (read 26 September 2026, euros excluding VAT) prices its Booking package at EUR 49.90 a month billed yearly for up to 5 staff, and its All-in-One with a till at EUR 119.90. SimplyBook.me's Basic covers 5 providers at €11.9 billed annually but caps you at 100 bookings and 3 custom features a month, and Premium reaches 30 providers at €49.9. If you need appointments and point of sale in one contract, Shore is the comparison; for booking alone SimplyBook.me is the cheaper way in.
