Smartli pricing in 2026 runs on a freemium model: a limited free tier for testing AI-generated Etsy listings, and paid subscription tiers that scale with the number of listings, credits, or shops you manage. For a typical small Etsy team, realistic monthly spend lands between roughly $9 and $49 depending on volume needs. The single biggest gotcha is credit/listing-based overage — teams that scale past their tier's monthly generation limit get throttled or forced to upgrade mid-cycle, which quietly inflates real costs beyond the sticker price.

FAQ

How much does Smartli actually cost per month?

Smartli publishes tiered monthly and annual pricing on its official pricing page, with a free entry tier and paid plans that typically fall in the sub-$50/month range for small sellers. Exact current figures change periodically, so always confirm live pricing on Smartli's official site before budgeting, since we cannot guarantee the numbers below remain unchanged after publication.

Does Smartli have a free plan?

Yes, Smartli offers a free tier aimed at letting new users test AI listing generation before committing to a paid plan. Free tiers on tools like this are almost always capped by a monthly generation/credit limit and reduced feature access (fewer templates, no bulk tools, limited SEO keyword depth), which is enough for testing but not for running an active multi-listing Etsy shop.

Are there hidden fees with Smartli?

The main hidden costs are not "fees" in the traditional sense but usage-based friction: credit/listing overage charges or forced upgrades once you exceed your tier's monthly cap, plus the opportunity cost of time spent editing AI output. Smartli does not publicly list setup fees or onboarding charges, but always check the checkout screen for annual-vs-monthly price differences before subscribing.

Is Smartli cheaper than hiring a copywriter or using competitor tools?

For a single small shop, Smartli's paid tiers are almost certainly cheaper than hiring a freelance Etsy copywriter per listing, and it's competitively priced against other AI Etsy-listing tools. Whether it's the cheapest option depends on your listing volume — high-volume sellers should compare cost-per-listing across Smartli and alternatives rather than flat monthly price alone.

What happens if I outgrow my Smartli plan?

When you exceed your tier's listing or credit allowance, Smartli generally requires an upgrade to a higher tier or an add-on credit purchase to continue generating listings that month. This is the most common cause of unexpected cost increases, so sellers who list frequently or manage multiple shops should size their plan based on peak-month volume, not average-month volume.

What Each Plan Includes

  • Free tier: A capped number of AI-generated listings per month, access to basic title/tag/description generation, and no or minimal SEO keyword research depth. Good for testing the tool's writing quality before committing spend.
  • Starter/paid entry tier: Typically raises the monthly generation cap, unlocks more complete SEO tag suggestions, and may add basic analytics on listing performance.
  • Growth/Pro tier: Usually adds bulk listing generation, deeper keyword research tools, multiple shop connections, and priority processing — aimed at active sellers managing dozens of SKUs.
  • Enterprise/Agency tier: Positioned for agencies or high-volume sellers managing many shops; pricing is quote-based and not published, which is standard practice for enterprise SaaS tiers across the industry.

Hidden Costs Founders Should Know About

  • Credit/generation overage: The most common real-world cost surprise — exceeding your monthly cap forces an upgrade or add-on purchase mid-cycle.
  • Annual lock-in discount trade-off: Annual billing usually looks cheaper per month but requires upfront cash commitment; if you cancel early, refund policies vary and should be checked directly with Smartli's terms.
  • Multi-shop/multi-seat costs: Agencies managing several Etsy shops may need multiple accounts or an Enterprise quote, since lower tiers are typically scoped to a single shop.
  • Editing/QA labor cost: AI-generated copy still requires human review for brand voice and accuracy — factor in staff time, which isn't reflected in the subscription price itself.
  • Support tier gating: Faster or dedicated support is commonly reserved for higher tiers; free and entry-tier users may rely on email/self-serve help only.

Enterprise Negotiation Tips

  • Ask directly for a custom quote rather than assuming list pricing applies — enterprise/agency tiers are quote-based and negotiable by design.
  • Request a multi-shop or multi-seat discount if managing several Etsy stores under one agency umbrella.
  • Negotiate an extended free trial or pilot period before committing to annual billing at scale.
  • Ask about contract length flexibility — quarterly commitments may be available even if not advertised publicly.

Free Tier Limitations

The free tier is suitable for testing AI writing quality and generating a handful of listings, but it is not built for active, scaling sellers. Expect a hard monthly generation cap, limited or no bulk tools, and reduced access to deeper keyword/SEO research. Upgrade once you're listing more than a few products monthly or need consistent, repeatable optimization across an active shop.
