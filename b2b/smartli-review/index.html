What Smartli does

AI writing suite for e-commerce: product listings, ad copy, SEO text and brand assets. Monthly runs $39 (Starter) to $299; on annual billing Starter disappears and Pro drops to about $24/month. Each of the nine tools is competent rather than deep, so leaning hard on one job means outgrowing it.

Pricing (2026)

(re-verified 5 Aug 2026, on smartli.ai) Monthly: Starter $39, Pro $59, Empire $99, Unicorn $299, each with a 7-day free trial. Switch to annual billing and Starter disappears from the table; the rest become Pro $24/mo (billed annually at $288), Empire $57/mo ($684) and Unicorn $79/mo ($948). Starter was $29/mo when we first checked, so the entry price has gone up. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

The catch

Smartli bundles nine AI tools — copy, SEO, images, logos — into one suite, and that breadth is also the honest catch: each individual tool is competent rather than best-in-class, so a team that leans hard on one job may outgrow it toward a specialist. And like all AI copy, its product descriptions and ad copy need a human edit and a fact-check — never publish specs or claims it invents about your product.

For a store that wants many content jobs handled cheaply in one place, it's efficient.

What buyers report about Smartli

Recurring themes from Trustpilot, the Shopify App Store and hands-on reviews, read in August 2026. We have not run Smartli ourselves, so this is other buyers' experience rather than ours. We summarise it here because the complaints are usually the part a vendor page leaves out.

What they praise
  • • Nine tools in one subscription — product descriptions, blog copy, ad copy, image generation, background and watermark removal, logos and video. For a high-SKU dropshipping store that convenience is real.
  • • The Shopify app holds around 4.0 stars for its core use.
What they complain about
  • • Billing is the serious problem. Trustpilot sits around 1.9 out of 5, with users reporting charges continuing after cancellation — $109 to $152 month after month, and double charges after cancelling within an hour of installing.
  • • There is no money-back guarantee and refunds are refused.
  • • The 7-day trial has no free plan behind it. $39 to $299 a month.
  • • Output needs editing to match a brand voice, and basic-plan support is slow.

Where the ratings disagree. 4.0 on the Shopify App Store against 1.9 on Trustpilot. The app rating is people using the tools; the Trustpilot rating is people trying to stop paying for them.

When the paid plan is worth it

The pricing page lists a 7-day free trial on all four monthly tiers and no free plan in either tab state. Starter ($39/month) lists only "20 SEO optimized descriptions" and "Premium AI generated keywords"; Pro ($59/month, 100 SEO optimized descriptions) is the first column carrying "10 AI blog writer", "AI Ads writer" and "VIP chat support", none of which appear under Starter. Above Pro the added lines are quantity: Empire $99/month for 1,000 SEO optimized descriptions and a line reading "50 AI blog writer"; Unicorn $299/month for "Unlimited SEO optimized descriptions", "Unlimited AI Blog writer" and "Unlimited Ads writer".

Annual billing changes the arithmetic sharply — Pro $24/month billed annually at $288, Empire $57/month at $684, Unicorn $79/month at $948 — but Starter is absent from the yearly view, and the refund policy names the trade-off: annual plans include no trial period and are strictly non-refundable once payment processing is complete, while monthly plans get no refunds once the seven-day trial has expired. Whichever term you pick, the refund policy requires cancellation through account settings at least 24 hours before the renewal date, and states that uninstalling smartli.ai from your store will not cancel the subscription.

How to actually use Smartli

Setup starts either from the Shopify App Store listing, titled "ChatGPT: 9 AI Tools, 1 App!", or from the smartli.ai site. The listing's pricing line reads "From $39/month. Free trial available.", and its Starter card names "20 SEO Optimized Descriptions per month", "Premium AI Generated Keywords" and "Chat and email support"; check that quota before you bulk-import. The generator page asks for "PRODUCT NAME" plus "Product Information And Specs", and puts output behind a button labelled "Generate Product Description"; the calls to action there read "Try for free" and "Get Started Today".

Smartli's product page advertises a bulk product description generator that "lets you automate product copy creation at scale", and its FAQ names Shopify, WooCommerce and Wix as example platforms. A Shopify App Store review by OLB LASHES (Canada), labelled "Edited November 14, 2025", reports an attempted payment of "152.00 USD" after cancelling on the install day, and unanswered mail to support@felex.co and support@smartli.ai.

Best for: e-commerce sellers who want an affordable all-in-one AI suite to draft product listings, ad copy, SEO text and basic brand assets in one place.
Who should skip it: if you need best-in-class depth in one area (a specialist copy or image tool), or you won't edit and fact-check AI output before it ships.

Frequently asked questions

Is Smartli worth it for a small team?

It depends on whether you have the bottleneck it solves. Small teams get the most out of this category when one clear problem is already costing real hours or revenue; buying ahead of that just adds cost and another login. Price it against the hours or lost deals it removes, not against its feature list, and start on the smallest plan that covers the job.

Who should not use Smartli?

Skip it if you need best-in-class depth in one area (a specialist copy or image tool), or you won't edit and fact-check AI output before it ships. Buying a tool to fix a problem you don't have yet just adds cost and another login to manage.
