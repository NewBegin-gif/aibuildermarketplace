Smarty Discounts Review (2026)

E-commerceResearched assessment · by Daniel HaketUpdated 2026-09-25

The evidence base is thin.

Best for: Shopify merchants whose promotions have outgrown the native discount engine — capped amounts, a fixed amount that behaves correctly in several currencies, tiers by quantity, subtotal or weight, BXGY, gifts, mix-and-match — and who want to build them with rules rather than with a developer, targeting by product, customer, cart, market, country or company.
Who should skip it: if your promotions are ordinary percentage or fixed-amount codes, which Shopify's built-in discounts already do for free; or you want a long support and reliability record before a young app gets write access to the discounts in your checkout.
