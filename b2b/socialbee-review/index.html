Add-on profiles undercut almost every plan

SocialBee bills per social profile, one brand account on one network, and that is the number that decides your tier, not your headcount. Bootstrap is $29 a month ($290 a year) for 5 profiles, 1 workspace and 1 user. Accelerate is $49 ($490) for 10 profiles. Pro is $99 ($990) for 25 profiles, 5 workspaces and 3 users per workspace. The agency tiers run $179, $329 and $449 for 50, 100 and 150 profiles.

AI generation is unlimited on every tier (2026-08-01, read on their own pricing page). First the trap the vendor names honestly: one business posting to Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, X and TikTok has used the entire Bootstrap allowance before a second brand exists. Five networks is five profiles. Then the arbitrage, which the page does not point out. Extra profiles are sold in packs of five for $15 a month: $3.00 a profile, and that is cheaper than the per-profile rate of every named plan except the largest.

Bootstrap is $5.80 a profile, Accelerate $4.90, Pro $3.96, Pro50 $3.58, Pro100 $3.29, and only Pro150 at $2.99 beats the add-on. Work the consequences through: Bootstrap plus one pack is $44 for 10 profiles against Accelerate's $49. Bootstrap plus four packs is $89 for 25 against Pro's $99. Bootstrap plus nine packs is $164 for 50 against Pro50's $179. The cheap plan with add-ons undercuts the named plan at every rung until 150 profiles.

The catch is structural rather than financial, and it is worth being precise about. Add-on packs buy profiles and nothing else. Bootstrap stays one workspace and one user, so this route works for a single operator running many accounts and breaks the moment you need colleagues in separate workspaces, which is exactly what an agency needs and exactly what the agency tiers are for. Extra users are $10 a month and extra workspaces $10, so you can bolt some of it back on: a two-person team on 8 profiles is $54 built from Bootstrap plus a pack plus a user, against $59 on Accelerate plus a user.

Annual billing is printed as 16% off: Bootstrap is $290 a year against $348 paid monthly, so roughly two months free. The add-ons have annual prices too ($150 a year for five profiles, $100 for an extra user, $100 for an extra workspace) and that is the one place the arbitrage finally runs out. On annual billing a pack works out at $2.50 a profile a month against Pro150's $2.49, so the largest named plan wins by a single cent, and every rung below it still does not.

Count your profiles across every network first, then check whether the plan or the packs get you there for less: on current prices, it is usually the packs. One caveat on the arbitrage above, added 22 September 2026. Every named-plan price still holds to the cent, but the add-on rates the argument rests on, $15 a month for five profiles, $10 for a user, $10 for a workspace, are no longer printed on the pricing page.

It now says only that to add extra users, workspaces and profiles you go into the SocialBee app and buy the add-ons there. Nothing here is known to be wrong, and the plan prices around it are unchanged, but the packs can no longer be priced from outside an account. Confirm the in-app rate before you commit to a route built on it.

    How to actually use SocialBee

    Count profiles before anything else, across every network and every brand. A profile is one account on one network, so a single business on Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, X and TikTok is five: the whole Bootstrap allowance. That number decides your plan; your team size does not. Then check the add-on route before you buy the plan the profile count points to. Extra profiles come in packs of five for $15 a month, which is $3.00 each: cheaper than the per-profile rate of every plan except the biggest.

    Bootstrap plus one pack is $44 for 10 profiles where Accelerate is $49; Bootstrap plus four packs is $89 for 25 where Pro is $99. The exception is structure. Bootstrap is one workspace and one user however many profiles you bolt on, so if you need colleagues working in separate client workspaces, buy the tier rather than the packs: that separation is what the agency plans are actually selling. Extra users and workspaces are $10 a month each if you only need one or two.

    There is a discount code on the pricing page. Can I still use it?

    Probably not, and the page will not tell you. Read 22 September 2026, the agency section carries a banner offering 50% off monthly agency plans for three months, 'prices start at just $89.50/month instead of $179', plus 50% off yearly plans and 'save up to $2694 per year', with the code SBDAY2026 to be used 'before June 10'. Try it at checkout by all means, but budget the undiscounted rate: a birthday sale still advertised in September is decoration until the checkout says otherwise.

    What does the 14-day trial actually let me test?

    Pro, whichever plan you are considering, and that is the catch. Read 22 September 2026: the page header promises a free 14-day trial, no contracts and a 30-day money-back guarantee, and a separate block, 'Try It Risk-Free: Full Pro Access for 14 Days', states you get complete unrestricted access to every Pro feature for two weeks with no credit card. Pro is 25 profiles across 5 workspaces at 3 users per workspace, with unlimited content categories and sources. So if you are evaluating Bootstrap at $29, the fortnight hands you five times the profiles, five times the workspaces and unlimited categories where Bootstrap gives ten, and the things you will come to lean on, advanced analytics, exportable reports, internal notes and the post approval system, are precisely what Bootstrap does not include. Test the workflow you will actually own, and treat the 30-day money-back guarantee, not the trial, as the safety net on the plan you buy.

    How far back does the analytics go, and what else is rationed on the entry plan?

    Three months on Bootstrap against two years on everything above it, which is the limit most likely to catch you out. Read 22 September 2026, the plan cards state 'Analytics up to 3 months of data' on Bootstrap and 'Analytics up to 2 years of data' on Accelerate, Pro and all three agency tiers; advanced analytics starts at Accelerate and exportable reports at Pro. On a plan aimed at freelancers that cuts in a specific way; you cannot compare this quarter with the same quarter last year, so any seasonal case you want to make to a client needs a record kept outside SocialBee from the start. Two more counters move with the tier: content categories run 10, 50 then unlimited, and content sources 10, 30 then unlimited. Categories matter more than the number suggests, because category-based recycling is the mechanism the whole product is built on, so hold ten against your posting plan before you count profiles.

    Which plan lets clients or colleagues work in separate workspaces?

    Only Pro. On socialbee.com/pricing (read 3 November 2026) Bootstrap and Accelerate each show '1 user/ workspace | 1 workspace', while Pro ($82.50 a month billed annually) shows '3 user/ workspace | 5 workspaces' and up to 25 profiles. So an agency that wants each client in its own space, or a reviewer next to the person posting, has to buy Pro; Accelerate's post approval system does not add a second user. What a guest or client login costs beyond three users per workspace is not stated on the page.
