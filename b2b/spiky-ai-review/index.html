What Spiky.ai does

AI meeting intelligence for revenue teams — records and analyses sales calls, tracks keywords and talk patterns, and coaches reps. Free tier at 3 meetings per user, then $15 to $40 per user per month on annual billing.

Pricing (2026)

Read in a live browser on their own pricing page, 31 Jul 2026, across both billing toggles. There is a genuine free tier, capped at 3 meetings per user per month. On annual billing Plus is $15, Pro $24 and Premium $40 per user per month; switch to monthly and the same three read $18, $29 and $49. Annual saves up to 18%. Trials need no card. Meeting allowances: 3 on Free, 30 on both Plus and Pro, unlimited on Premium. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

The number to check first is the meeting cap, not the price. Plus and Pro both stop at 30 meetings per user per month, so the step from $15 to $24 buys features, not capacity. A rep taking two calls a day passes 30 in three weeks, which pushes you to Premium at $40 — nearly triple the entry price — for volume rather than capability. Price this from your team's actual call count and you will land on the right tier; price it from the feature table and you will not.

What buyers report about Spiky.ai

Recurring themes from G2, Capterra, GetApp and Software Advice, read in August 2026. We have not run Spiky.ai ourselves, so this is other buyers' experience rather than ours. We summarise it here because the complaints are usually the part a vendor page leaves out.

What they praise
  • • 4.9 on G2 across 115 reviews, with time saved on note-taking and automatic action items the consistent theme.
  • • SOC 2 Type II, GDPR, KVKK and ISO 27001 infrastructure — the compliance position is genuinely solid for a tool this size.
What they complain about
  • • Transcript accuracy drops for non-native English speakers and strong accents. It is the top negative theme on G2 and a dealbreaker for a multilingual team — test with your actual callers.
  • • The pricing page contradicts itself: $15 or $18, $24 or $29, $40 or $49 for the same tiers depending on which block you read. Confirm what you are signing up for.
  • • Free covers 10 meetings per user, Pro $29 for 30, Premium $49 unlimited. Getting a whole team on it is where the cost bites.
  • • Customising the coaching and feedback settings takes learning.
Best for: sales and customer-facing teams that already run most conversations on video and want the coaching layer — talk-time, keyword tracking and call review — without buying a full revenue-intelligence platform.
Who should skip it: if your team's calls are mostly ad hoc or on the phone rather than video, or the meeting volume per rep sits well above 30 a month, in which case only the top tier fits and the cheaper plans are a false economy.

Frequently asked questions

Does Spiky.ai replace my CRM or plug into it?

Most tools in this category plug in rather than replace. Check which direction the data flows and whether the sync is genuinely two-way, because a one-way push creates a second source of truth that your reps will quietly stop trusting. If it does aim to replace your CRM, price the migration honestly: history, custom fields and reporting rarely move across cleanly.

Who should not use Spiky.ai?

Skip it if your team's calls are mostly ad hoc or on the phone rather than video, or the meeting volume per rep sits well above 30 a month, in which case only the top tier fits and the cheaper plans are a false economy. Buying a tool to fix a problem you don't have yet just adds cost and another login to manage.
