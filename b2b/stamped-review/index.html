Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 12 Sep 2026

There is no free plan, and what you can actually buy depends on your monthly order volume. Below 5,000 orders a month the page sells only one product: Reviews, at $23/month at the 200-order step and $99/month at 1,000 orders. Repeat and Loyalty do not appear at those volumes at all. At the 5,000-order step the full line-up shows up — Repeat $499/month, Loyalty $299/month, Reviews $199/month — and the Multi-Product Bundle, which advertises 20% off, is a flat $798/month.

The bundle tab locks the 200- and 1,000-order steps, so $798 is the entry price for taking more than one product, not a figure from the top of a ladder. From 20,000 orders upward, Unlimited included, every card turns into "Custom plan — Talk to Sales". Their FAQ says the same thing in words: plans start at $199/month for a single product and $798/month for multi-product access, and smaller brands are pointed at the self-serve plans in Stamped's Shopify App listings instead.

Plans include up to two domains, each additional store gets its first month free, and additional domains are $100/month each. Sales tax is added as a separate line item. The redesigned page no longer says anything about overage charges. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

The $798/month figure that circulates for the full suite is real, but it is a floor rather than a ceiling: that is what multi-product access costs from the 5,000-order step, and below that step you cannot buy the bundle at all — the pricing page sells Reviews on its own, $23/month at 200 orders and $99 at 1,000, and does not show Repeat or Loyalty. Crossing 5,000 orders doubles Reviews from $99 to $199, and wanting a second product moves you to $798.

A second storefront is another $100 a month on top, and past 20,000 orders every card turns into "Talk to Sales". Start with one product and price the step above the volume you actually expect.

How to actually use Stamped

Install the app from the Shopify app store, then import first: existing reviews go in as a CSV through Settings, Importer. Then set store name, store email, the From address reviews are sent from, and the deliverability settings beside them. Display is a separate job. On Shopify 2.0 the main widget and star-rating badge are added from the theme customiser as a section and a block; on vintage themes you paste Stamped's liquid snippets into product.liquid or main-product.liquid, and into product-card.liquid for collection pages.

The tracking script goes into theme.liquid before the closing body tag, with your own public API key and store hash.

Distilled from Stamped's own help centre, chiefly 'Shopify: Getting Started' and 'Shopify: Installing the app, main widget and script' at stampedsupport.stamped.io, read 20 August 2026.

Common Stamped setup mistakes

Where Stamped setups actually go wrong, and what it costs you when they do. Same sources as the setup notes above — not from a walkthrough we ran ourselves.

The step teams underestimate is the theme code.

  • Review requests only generate once the Enable Review Requests toggle is saved; installing the app starts nothing.
  • Stamped documents that any change or update of your theme may remove its widget codes, so a redesign can silently end review display.
  • On non-product pages the main widget loses its Write a Review functionality.
  • Support for this installation is limited and, in Stamped's wording, offered to paid users only.

The natural comparison is Yotpo or Judge.me — other reviews and loyalty apps, some far cheaper.

Frequently asked questions

Is Stamped really around $800 a month?

It is, as soon as you want more than one product. $798/month is the Multi-Product Bundle at the 5,000-order step, and the bundle cannot be selected below that step at all — at 200 and 1,000 monthly orders the pricing page sells Reviews only, at $23 and $99 a month. So the honest answer turns on two things: your monthly order volume, and whether one product is enough. Stamped's FAQ gives the same floor in words, $199 for a single product and $798 for multi-product access. Read in a browser on 12 September 2026; an earlier reading of ours quoted about $18/month for the bundle at 200 orders, and the page as it stands today does not support that.

Is there still a free plan?

No. Each product — Repeat, Loyalty and Reviews — is its own subscription, and below 5,000 monthly orders the pricing page offers only Reviews, from $23 a month. If you are below that, the answer Stamped gives is its Shopify App Store listings, where it sells self-serve plans aimed at smaller and growing brands. What each product costs depends entirely on your order band, and the pricing section above has those figures with the date we read them. The redesigned page says nothing about overage charges either way.

What does a second storefront cost?

$100 a month per additional domain. Their pricing page states that plans include up to two domains and that each additional store gets a first month free before that charge starts — wording that survived the September 2026 rebuild of the page. Worth pricing in early if you run regional storefronts, because it lands on top of the per-product subscriptions rather than inside them.

What should I weigh before buying Growth & Revenue software?

It depends on whether you have the bottleneck it solves. Small teams get the most out of this category when one clear problem is already costing real hours or revenue; buying ahead of that just adds cost and another login. Price it against the hours or lost deals it removes, not against its feature list, and start on the smallest plan that covers the job.
