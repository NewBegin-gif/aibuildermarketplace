What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

The category is honest and proven (digital stamp cards beat lost paper cards), and the platform is young with limited public pricing and review coverage — so start on the free plan, run one real campaign, and measure repeat-visit lift before paying. The deeper truth of loyalty programs: the mechanics are 10% of the outcome; a reward worth returning for is the other 90%.

When the paid plan is worth it

The paid tiers mostly buy scale, not capability. StampEzee's comparison table has four columns, and what changes across them is branches (1, 2, 3, 10), active stamp cards (1, 3, 6, 12) and push notifications (0, 1, 4, 8). Stamp Lite, the only tier with a price on a public page we could open, is $29.99 a month for 2 branches, 3 stamp cards and 1 push notification. It pays back in two cases: you run more than one location, or you want to send a re-engagement push at all, since the entry column allows zero. It does not pay back if you want a better-looking card: custom stamp card designs appear only in the fourth column, and even there the allowance is 1. What upgrading does not solve: the pricing page carries no plan names and no prices, so the third and fourth tiers cannot be costed before you talk to sales.

How to actually use Stampezee

First value is a completed stamp card, not a created one. The vendor's route is short: build your first stamp card, assign staff, start rewarding customers, which the how-it-works page frames as launching "in minutes". The friction sits on the other side of the counter. StampEzee's own customer page describes a four-step flow that starts with the customer downloading the app and creating an account; wallet passes come after joining, not instead of it. So your first weeks are an install-rate problem, and staff have to ask at checkout every time. Second snag: scanning is a separate staff role, the Loyalty Executive, with its own app, so nothing moves until someone is assigned. Third: self-serve help is thin. On 24 July 2026 the help centre, guides and case-studies pages each returned no articles, so setup questions route to a booking call rather than documentation. Distilled from vendor pages only — stampezee.com/en/how-it-works, /en/for-customers, /en/faq and /en/help-center; we looked for an independent walkthrough and found none, so nothing third-party is used here.

Best for: local businesses (cafés, salons, retail) that want a simple digital stamp-card loyalty program starting free.
Who should skip it: if you need POS-integrated loyalty with CRM depth (bigger platforms exist), or your offer isn't repeat-visit shaped.

The natural comparison is Stamp Me or Loopy Loyalty — the digital-stamp peer set — compare per-location pricing and whether Apple/Google Wallet passes are included. Decide by which one fits the job above, not by the louder brand.
