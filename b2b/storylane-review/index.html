Storylane Review (2026)$50 to $625, Nothing Between

Sales & CRMResearched assessment · by Daniel HaketContent updated 2026-09-29Prices checked 2026-09-29View Storylane in the Atlas →

Transparency: we earn nothing from Storylane; the links to it are plain links. How we make money →

The jump from Starter to Growth is $50 to $625 a month — twelve and a half times — and the free tier is capped at one live demo, so the moment you want a second demo published you are on the ladder.

Best for: sales and marketing teams who show a product before a call and want to know which prospects clicked what — the free tier is genuinely usable for one demo.
Who should skip it: if you need more than one live demo but nowhere near 2,500 identified visitors a month, because that is exactly the gap the price ladder does not serve; a screen recording and a calendar link may do the job for less.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

What is actually being sold at each step is visitor de-anonymisation: 250 a month on Starter, 2,500 on Growth, 10,000 on Premium. Price that against what you already pay a separate tool to do, because it is the meter that decides your tier, not the number of demos. Extra seats are charged on top at $40 to $100 each, which means a five-person sales team on Starter is not $50 a month, it is $210.

You are buying de-anonymised visitors, not demos

Free is $0 for one seat and one published demo live at a time, with unlimited views. Starter is $50 a month ($40 annual) for one seat and unlimited demos. Growth is $625 ($500 annual) for five seats, and Premium $1,500 ($1,200) for ten. Enterprise is quoted, and their RepX AI sales-agent product is a separate subscription starting at $2,000 a month for up to 20,000 monthly visitors (first read 1 August 2026 on their own pricing page; the Demo Suite amounts were unchanged on 29 September 2026).

The step from Starter to Growth is twelve and a half times, which is the largest single jump on this site's price ladders — so it is worth being precise about what causes it. It is not demos: those are unlimited from Starter up. It is visitor de-anonymisation. Starter identifies 250 visitors a month, Growth 2,500 and Premium 10,000, and that meter is the product being sold. Price it as such, because the per-unit maths has an inversion in it.

On annual billing you pay $0.16 per identified visitor on Starter, $0.20 on Growth and $0.12 on Premium — so Growth is 25% worse value per visitor than the entry tier. You are buying the five seats and the headroom, not a better rate. Set that $0.16 to $0.20 against what you already pay a separate de-anonymisation tool, since many teams are paying twice without noticing. Seats are charged on top and they are not cheap: $40 each on Starter and $100 on Growth.

A five-person sales team on Starter is therefore $210 a month, not $50 — and at that point the $500 Growth plan with five seats included and ten times the visitor allowance is a different conversation. The free tier's real constraint is the single live demo. One published demo at a time is enough to prove the format works for your product and not enough to run a campaign, so treat it as a genuine trial with a hard edge rather than a starting plan.

How to actually use Storylane

Work out what you are actually buying before you look at the tiers, because it is not demos — those are unlimited from Starter up. It is visitor de-anonymisation: 250 a month on Starter, 2,500 on Growth, 10,000 on Premium. Estimate how many demo visitors you would want identified in a month and let that pick the plan. Check whether you are already paying for that elsewhere. Plenty of teams run a separate de-anonymisation or visitor-identification tool, and buying it twice is the most common way this subscription becomes poor value.

At $0.16 to $0.20 an identified visitor here, that comparison is straightforward. Add seats to the plan price before you compare anything: $40 each on Starter, $100 on Growth. A five-person sales team on Starter is $210 a month rather than $50, which changes the case for Growth considerably. Use the free tier to answer one question — does an interactive demo work for your product? One published demo at a time is enough for that and nothing more.

Build it for your hardest use case rather than your slickest, because the products that suit this format are the ones that are hard to explain in a screenshot.

Distilled from Storylane's pricing page (storylane.io/pricing), read 29 August 2026 on both the monthly and yearly toggle — the plan cards, the per-additional-seat lines, the Account Reveal feature rows that carry the de-anonymisation limits, and the separate RepX tiers further down the same page. Re-read 29 September 2026 on storylane.io/plans (both toggles and the RepX tab): Demo Suite prices, seats and Account Reveal limits unchanged; RepX now Growth from $2,000 for up to 20,000 visitors and Premium from $3,000 for up to 60,000, each including the Demo Suite, with a 30-day trial.

The natural comparison is Navattic or Reprise — the direct interactive-demo peers — compare the visitor-identification allowance at your tier, since that is what you are really buying.

Frequently asked questions

What does Storylane cost, and where is the cliff?

Free is $0 with 1 seat and exactly one published demo live at a time. Starter is $50 a month, or $40 billed annually, for 1 seat with unlimited published demos and extra seats at $40. Growth is $625 a month, or $500 annually, for 5 seats with extra seats at $100. Premium is $1,500, or $1,200 annually, for 10 seats. Re-read on their plans page on 29 September 2026, unchanged from our readings on 1 and 15 August. The cliff is Starter to Growth: $50 to $625 a month, twelve and a half times, and what it buys is visitor de-anonymisation going from 250 a month to 2,500.

Is the RepX product included?

No. RepX is Storylane's AI sales-agent product and it is priced separately: Growth starts at $2,000 a month for up to 20,000 monthly visitors, Premium at $3,000 for up to 60,000 and adds visitor de-anonymisation, and Enterprise is custom above that. It works the other way round: each RepX tier includes the Demo Suite's interactive demos, and RepX comes with a 30-day free trial. Read on their plans page on 29 September 2026; in August the same two prices were tied to 10,000 and 40,000 visitors.

What should I weigh before buying Sales & CRM software?

Most tools in this category plug in rather than replace. Check which direction the data flows and whether the sync is genuinely two-way, because a one-way push creates a second source of truth that your reps will quietly stop trusting. If it does aim to replace your CRM, price the migration honestly: history, custom fields and reporting rarely move across cleanly.
