What Streak does

CRM built directly inside Gmail: manage pipelines, leads and deals from your inbox with email tracking, mail merge and shared views — ideal for teams that live in Google Workspace.

Pricing (2026)

(price re-verified 2026-08-02 against the vendor's own pricing page) (2026) Pro about $49/user/mo annual ($59 monthly), Pro+ ~$69, Enterprise ~$129; ~20% annual discount. The free CRM tier was eliminated in 2024-25, though free email power tools (tracking, mail merge 50/day, snippets) remain. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

The pitch — CRM living inside Gmail — is also the ceiling: everything happens in the inbox, which power users love and growing teams outgrow (reporting, permissions, cross-team process). And note the trajectory: the free tier is gone and per-seat prices sit at real-CRM levels now, so the 'lightweight Gmail add-on' framing no longer matches the invoice. If the inbox is truly where your deals live, few tools feel this natural.

What buyers report about Streak

Recurring themes from Capterra, Reddit and CRM reviews, read in August 2026. We have not run Streak ourselves, so this is other buyers' experience rather than ours. We summarise it here because the complaints are usually the part a vendor page leaves out.

What they praise
  • • It lives inside Gmail, so adoption is instant — reviewers describe being up and running in seconds.
  • • Email tracking is well rated and is what many users stay for even without the CRM.
What they complain about
  • • The free CRM and Solo tiers were phased out across 2024-2025. What remains free is email power tools only, and the Reddit reaction was not kind.
  • • Gmail only. No Outlook, no Hotmail, no other client — if your team moves, Streak does not come with you.
  • • Shared pipelines and team email sharing sit on the expensive tiers. Pro is $49 per user annually, Pro+ $69, Enterprise $129.
  • • Bugs causing crashes and slowdowns are reported, and in a mail client that is disruptive.
Best for: solo founders and small Gmail-native teams whose pipeline genuinely lives in email threads and who want zero context-switching.
Who should skip it: if your team needs shared process outside the inbox (a standalone CRM scales better), or you're price-sensitive — the Gmail convenience now costs full CRM money.

Frequently asked questions

Is Streak worth it for a small team?

It depends on whether you have the bottleneck it solves. Small teams get the most out of this category when one clear problem is already costing real hours or revenue; buying ahead of that just adds cost and another login. Price it against the hours or lost deals it removes, not against its feature list, and start on the smallest plan that covers the job.

Who should not use Streak?

Skip it if your team needs shared process outside the inbox (a standalone CRM scales better), or you're price-sensitive — the Gmail convenience now costs full CRM money. Buying a tool to fix a problem you don't have yet just adds cost and another login to manage.
