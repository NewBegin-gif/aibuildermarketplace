Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 28 Aug 2026

A free plan plus three paid monthly tiers, each with a 7-day trial. The catch is in the free plan: it works only on a development store, not a live one. Premium is $49 a month ($470 a year), Advanced is $99 a month ($950 a year) and Ultimate is $399 a month ($3,830 a year). The headline feature — the AI Functions Generator, which writes a function from a plain-language description — does not appear until Advanced at $99, alongside the Scripts Editor and Functions Wizard.

So using SupaEasy the way it markets itself, on a live store, realistically starts at $99 a month. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

The catch

First, the 'free' plan is development-stores only, so any live use is paid, and the AI generator that headlines the app is not in Premium ($49) but in Advanced ($99), possibly plus metered AI credits as an add-on — so the AI promise realistically costs $99+ a month. Second, the rating is suspiciously spotless — 5.0 across 205 reviews, all five-star — and the praise dwells on fast human support (Andrea, Fabio) more than on the no-code AI running unattended, which hints the tool can need hand-holding.

What SupaEasy does

No-code Shopify Functions generator (by Nextools): build and migrate custom discount, checkout, payment, shipping and validation logic from the Shopify Admin, with a migrator for deprecated Scripts and an AI generator that writes functions from a plain-language brief.

What buyers report about SupaEasy

Recurring themes from the buyer-review sites listed at the foot of this page, read in August 2026. We have not run SupaEasy ourselves, so this is other buyers' experience rather than ours. We summarise it here because the complaints are usually the part a vendor page leaves out.

What they praise
  • • The clearest recurring job is migrating off Shopify's deprecated Scripts Editor: one merchant describes re-creating 'unique and complicated' discounting logic that had lived in Scripts, and names that migration as the reason to install.
  • • Reviewers who need conditional logic get specific about it — reading cart attributes, checkout validations, and custom pricing rules that Shopify's native discount tools cannot express.
  • • Several reviews describe the vendor building the function for the merchant rather than the merchant building it: one store needed pricing logic for a bundle app it had built itself, was told the app could do it, and had the function written for them; another got a walk-through call until they understood how it worked.
  • • Tenure on the reviews is unusually long for an app store — multiple reviewers write at 'about 1 year', 'almost 2 years' and 'about 2 years' with the app still in place.
  • • One reviewer credits the help docs and setup videos, the only praise in the sample aimed at self-service material rather than at staff.
What they complain about
  • • The sharpest criticism sits inside a five-star review: a US merchant writes plainly that they do not find creating functions 'supaeasy', and credits the support team for making it possible anyway.
  • • Getting one Black Friday discount script right took a ninety-message email chain, by the reviewer's own count — offered as praise for the support person who stayed with it, but it is also a measure of the effort a complex rule can take.
  • • 205 reviews and not one below five stars, with an identical one-line vendor reply under each. A record with no dissent in it cannot tell you what the failure modes are; read it as evidence of an attentive team, not as evidence of an app that never disappoints.

Where the ratings disagree. Across both pages the object of the praise is almost always a named person — Andrea, Fabio, Yuvraj, Abhi — rather than the software. Taken as a whole the reviews describe a done-for-you function-building service with an app attached, which matters if you are buying it expecting to configure discounts yourself.

What the money actually buys

SupaEasy's tiers are really a ladder of where the automation may run and how much of it is AI-assisted. The free plan is a sandbox — a development store, not your real one. Premium at $49 opens live use with presets and the migrator; Advanced at $99 is where the app's actual pitch lives, adding the AI Functions Generator, the Scripts Editor and the Functions Wizard; Ultimate at $399 is for the heaviest use.

The honest frame is that the value proposition — 'describe the function in plain language and we'll build it' — is an Advanced-tier feature, so the interesting version of SupaEasy starts at $99 a month, not the $49 the tier list leads with. What you buy at that price is the migration path off Shopify Scripts, which Shopify is retiring, and the ability to ship checkout and discount logic without a developer.

For a store that genuinely has Scripts to move or recurring custom-logic needs, that can pay for itself against a freelance developer; for a one-off simple discount, it is an expensive way in, and a focused discount app does the job for less.

How to actually use SupaEasy

SupaEasy installs from the Shopify App Store (apps.shopify.com/supaeasy) and works inside the Shopify Admin, so there is no separate account to create. The natural first move depends on why you are there. If you are migrating away from Shopify Scripts — the app's core use case now that Scripts is deprecated — you start with the migrator, which reads your existing Scripts and recreates them as Shopify Functions. If you are building fresh, you can start from a preset for a common case (a discount, a payment-method or shipping rule, a checkout validation) or, on the Advanced plan, describe what you want in plain language and let the AI Functions Generator write it.

The practical gate is the plan: the free plan only runs on a development store, so testing on your live shop and using the AI generator both require a paid tier ($99 for the AI). Budget the first real session around whichever paid plan unlocks the feature you actually came for. Read on apps.shopify.com/supaeasy and supaeasy.com/pricing, 28 August 2026.

The natural comparison is Shopify Functions — SupaEasy is a no-code layer over Shopify Functions — the same platform capability a developer would code by hand. With engineering time, native Functions cost nothing but your developer's hours; SupaEasy is for merchants who would rather describe the logic than write it, and who need the Scripts migrator. Frequently asked questions

What does SupaEasy cost?

A free plan (development stores only) plus Premium $49, Advanced $99 and Ultimate $399 a month, each with a 7-day trial (read 28 August 2026). The AI Functions Generator that headlines the app is an Advanced-tier feature, so live AI use starts at $99 a month.

Is the free plan actually usable?

Only for testing — it runs on a development store, not a live shop. Any production use is paid, so treat the free plan as a sandbox rather than a real free tier.

Which plan has the AI generator?

Advanced, at $99 a month — not Premium. Advanced also adds the Scripts Editor and Functions Wizard. If the plain-language AI builder is why you want SupaEasy, $99 is the real entry price, possibly plus AI credits as an add-on.

Why use it instead of coding Functions myself?

To skip the developer. SupaEasy generates and migrates Shopify Functions from the Admin without code, which matters most if you are moving off deprecated Shopify Scripts. With engineering time, native Shopify Functions cost nothing extra; the app is paying not to write them.

What should I weigh before buying E-commerce software?

Check your platform and version specifically. Shopify support is close to universal in this category, while WooCommerce, BigCommerce, Magento and headless setups vary a lot, and 'supported' sometimes means a generic API rather than a maintained app. A listing in your own platform's app store, with recent reviews there, is a better signal than the vendor's logo wall.
