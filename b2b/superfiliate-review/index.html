The honest knock

Their pricing page returns 404 with and without www, the homepage carries no figure at all, and the only route to a number is a sales call — so the honest warning is not that it is expensive, but that you cannot compare it against anything until you have spoken to them. Ask for the figure in writing on that call, and ask what separates the ends of whatever range they quote.

On fit rather than price: this is built for brands already running creator and affiliate programs, and it is overkill if you are testing your first affiliate offer.

What the entry tier has to earn back

The price is still the part you cannot check, but one thing about it is now settled. Superfiliate publishes no figure: /pricing returns 404 with and without www, there is no pricing item in the navigation, and the only commercial route is Book Demo (re-checked 5 September 2026). Their Shopify App Store listing publishes the shape instead — one plan called Custom, 'Free to install. Additional charges may apply', a note that the app needs an active Superfiliate subscription to work at all, and the one hard fact that all charges are billed in USD.

Our range of roughly $299 to $999 a month traces to no source we can date. What you can do without a number is work out the hurdle. At $299 a month you commit $3,588 a year; on a 40% gross margin the programme must produce about $8,970 of incremental revenue — roughly $750 a month — purely to cover the software. At $999 the same maths needs about $2,500 a month.

Then add what gets left out: creator commission comes out of the same margin. Pay 15% and an effective 40% margin becomes 25%, pushing the entry hurdle from $750 to about $1,200 a month.

How to actually use Superfiliate

Work out the hurdle before the demo, because it turns a vague question into one you can answer in six months. Use their number, not ours: nothing on their site publishes a price. If the entry really is $299 a month, that is $3,588 a year, which on a 40% gross margin needs roughly $750 a month of incremental revenue just to cover the software; $999 needs about $2,500. Then add creator commission out of the same margin — at 15% the entry hurdle moves to roughly $1,200 a month.

Ask three things in writing on that call: what each tier includes, whether the quote is annual or month-to-month, and what sits beneath the Shopify listing's 'Free to install', since the listing itself says the app needs an active subscription to run. Charges are billed in USD, which is at least one variable settled. Agree how you will measure 'incremental' before you launch. A customer who was going to buy anyway and clicked a creator link on the way shows up as programme revenue and costs you commission; the dashboard will not separate the two. Decide on a holdout or a pre-period baseline now.

Frequently asked questions

Does Superfiliate work with Klaviyo, ReCharge or our subscription app?

The Shopify listing says so for a named set. Read 24 September 2026, apps.shopify.com/superfiliate lists Checkout and Customer accounts plus Attentive, Klaviyo, Postscript, ReCharge, Skio and Stay.ai under 'Works with', gives ACH payments as the payout method and English as the only language. If your email, SMS or subscription tool is not on that list, ask on the demo call how data would reach it rather than assuming it does.

Is the Shopify app free?

Installing it is; using it is not. The listing, read 24 September 2026, shows a single plan called Custom, 'Free to install', and states that the app requires an active Superfiliate subscription for activation and use, with plan details from Superfiliate directly. All charges are billed in USD. So the free label covers the install, not the platform, and the real price only arrives on the sales call.

Is it only for Shopify stores?

The sold product is a Shopify app, and that is the only listing we found. Superfiliate's homepage, read 24 September 2026, describes it as unifying influencer marketing across Shopify, Meta and TikTok Shop, but the storefront and affiliate features are sold through the Shopify App Store, and the site publishes no plan for another store platform. If you sell on something else, ask on the demo what works without Shopify before you spend time on a pilot.

What should I weigh before buying Growth & Revenue software?

It depends on whether you have the bottleneck it solves. Small teams get the most out of this category when one clear problem is already costing real hours or revenue; buying ahead of that just adds cost and another login. Price it against the hours or lost deals it removes, not against its feature list, and start on the smallest plan that covers the job.
