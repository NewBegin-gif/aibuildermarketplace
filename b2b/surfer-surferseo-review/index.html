What Surfer (SurferSEO) does

Content optimization against live SERP data, and since 2026 a tracker for how often a brand is cited in AI answers like ChatGPT and Google AI Overviews. Billed yearly at four tiers.

Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 26 Aug 2026

The plans
  • Discovery — $49/mo billed yearly
    120 documents, 10 pages tracked, no AI prompt tracking
  • Standard — $99/mo billed yearly
    360 documents, 25 AI prompts refreshed weekly, 3 seats
  • Pro — $182/mo billed yearly
    50 AI prompts refreshed daily, 5 brand workspaces, 5 seats
  • Peace of Mind — $299/mo billed yearly
    unlimited documents, 100 prompts daily, API access, 10 seats
  • Enterprise — $999/mo
    tailored packages, SSO, white-label, advisory programme
  • AI Search Analytics — $158/mo billed yearly
    sold on its own — 100 prompts daily without the SEO suite

Billed yearly: Discovery $49/mo, Standard $99/mo, Pro $182/mo and Peace of Mind $299/mo, with Enterprise at $999/mo and AI Search Analytics sold on its own at $158/mo. Every listed rate assumes the annual commitment. The page publishes what that commitment saves you — $120, $240, $444, $720 and $324 a year — and working those back gives monthly-billing rates of about $59, $119, $219, $359 and $185; Surfer does not print those figures itself, so treat them as arithmetic on theirs rather than a reading.

Worth knowing if you are outside the US: we read this page from both sides and the numbers do not convert. A European is quoted €49 to €299 where an American is quoted $49 to $299, which at today's rate makes the same subscription materially more expensive in Europe. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

The meter that actually forces an upgrade is documents: 120 to 360 optimizations depending on tier, which a working content team passes faster than it expects. And the AI-visibility tracking that the product now leads with is capped by prompt count per tier, so watching more of your market costs a step up rather than an add-on.
