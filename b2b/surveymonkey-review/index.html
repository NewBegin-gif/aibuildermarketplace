We tested it · 2026-11-05

Tested by our team on 5 November 2026 on the free Basic plan: Daan created the account and our AI assistant ran the test in Daan's browser. We drafted a small quiz survey, looked for an export, and opened the checkout for Standard Monthly without paying. We collected no responses and sent nothing.

How we tested

  1. Read the account summary and the Basic plan limits
  2. Built a draft survey with one scored question and one paid question type
  3. Opened Analyze results and the export menu
  4. Read both pricing tabs and stopped at the last checkout screen
  • Sign-up asked four profile questions and then dropped us on the home screen. No card, no sales call and no linked account was required.
  • The account page shows 'Basic (Free) Account', '10 responses per survey' and 'Survey data storage: United States'. 'Access to Benchmarks' was already switched on, so your survey results feed SurveyMonkey's benchmarks unless you turn it off.
  • Quiz scoring worked on Basic: we marked one answer as worth one point. A 'Multiple textboxes' question raised the toast '1 paid feature added! Upgrade your plan or remove paid features to send this survey.' Basic lets you preview paid question types, but not send a survey that uses one.

Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 12 Nov 2026

Team plans are billed annually and need three or more users; pricing.md no longer lists any monthly Team variant. Every Team seat costs the same, the first one included: in the US table Team Advantage Annual is $360 a user a year and Team Premier Annual $1,104, the $30 and $92 a month the SurveyMonkey partner team quoted by email on 24 September 2026, so three seats come to $1,080 or $3,312 a year. The euro pricing page states the included responses: 50,000 a year on Team Advantage and 100,000 on Team Premier, counted across all surveys and users; Enterprise (five or more users, custom quote) has a custom limit.

Frequently asked questions

Can you export your data from SurveyMonkey?

What does SurveyMonkey cost for a team?

$360 a user a year on Team Advantage and $1,104 on Team Premier in the US, with a three-user minimum and annual billing only, so $1,080 or $3,312 a year to start; the first seat costs the same as the rest (pricing.md dated 22 September 2026, read 1 October). Euro storefronts charge €360 and €900 a seat a year, shown on the page as €30 and €75 a month. An earlier version of this answer added a dearer 'core seat'; that was our misreading of the individual-plan column and is withdrawn.

What happens if I go over my response limit?

On the self-serve plans you are charged per response above your plan's included limit, at a rate that depends on your storefront: $0.15 in dollar countries, €0.10 in euro ones, £0.15 in sterling. Two things make that bigger than it looks: on paid plans the limit is counted across all surveys and all users rather than per survey, so a colleague's popular survey can consume the whole team's allowance, and SurveyMonkey Audience responses are sold separately on top. Enterprise plans work differently: custom response limits and contract pricing. The pricing page states the included limit for Team plans (50,000 responses a year on Team Advantage and 100,000 on Team Premier, read on the euro storefront), while pricing.md still marks responses "Unlimited" on every paid plan, so ask for your own storefront's number in writing.

Is the free plan enough to test the product?

For the interface, yes; for a real survey, no. Basic allows ten questions per survey, and the 22 September 2026 edition of pricing.md shows its response limit as ten outside Canada (Canadian Basic users keep twenty-five). It keeps collecting, but responses past the limit stay hidden until you upgrade. For a genuine pilot, the only monthly plan is the individual Standard Monthly: $149 a month in the US table of pricing.md (still $149 in the edition updated 27 October 2026, read 12 November 2026), although the partner team quoted $99 by email on 24 September 2026 and no published table shows it. Euro storefronts differ (€39 in Germany, €35 in Ireland), so check the price at checkout. Team plans are billed annually from three users.

Can I bring an existing questionnaire into SurveyMonkey without retyping it?

SurveyMonkey's AI features page (surveymonkey.com/product/features/ai, read 4 November 2026) describes AI-powered survey import: you copy and paste a survey written elsewhere and the AI turns it into a structured survey with question types and formatting. The page marks this as available on all plans, but the same page also says feature availability can differ by region and by plan, so treat the free-plan import as the vendor's statement, not a guarantee for your account. How well it maps complex logic (skip logic, matrices) is not confirmed.
