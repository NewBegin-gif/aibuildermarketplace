What SurveyMonkey does

Survey and feedback platform: build surveys, collect responses at scale and analyze results for market research, CX and employee feedback. A native Salesforce integration pushes responses straight into Salesforce, triggers workflows off the answers themselves (churn-risk follow-up or an NPS detractor landing on a rep's desk, for instance) and ties feedback back to accounts and pipeline, so sales and CS read it inside the tool they already work in rather than in a separate reporting dashboard.

Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 17 Aug 2026

(re-verified 2026-08-17, served in euros from an EU address) The free tier is nearly useless for real research (10 questions, 25 responses). Team plans are €30 and €75 per user per month, both starting at a minimum of 3 users and billed annually, so the real entry point for a team is €90 a month rather than €30. Individual paid plans sit alongside those.

Overage is charged at €0.10 per additional response once you pass your plan's limit, and on paid plans response limits are counted across all surveys and all users rather than per survey. SurveyMonkey Audience responses are sold separately. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

The catch

The free tier is a teaser, response caps trigger $0.15-per-response overages that become a hidden cost on big studies, branding and customisation are limited, and a lot of the good analytics and logic sit behind higher tiers.

What buyers report about SurveyMonkey

Recurring themes from G2, Trustpilot, the BBB and PissedConsumer, read in August 2026. We have not run SurveyMonkey ourselves, so this is other buyers' experience rather than ours. We summarise it here because the complaints are usually the part a vendor page leaves out.

What they praise
  • • Everyone knows what a SurveyMonkey link is, and response rates benefit from that familiarity — it remains the default for a reason.
  • • Question logic and templates cover most research needs without training.
What they complain about
  • • Auto-renewal is on by default and accounts renew indefinitely whether used or not. An August 2024 BBB complaint documents a $276 renewal the customer says they never authorised.
  • • Paid fees are essentially never refunded. Annual plans bill the full year up front and are non-refundable — cancel before the renewal date or you have paid for the year.
  • • Cost dominates the complaints: expensive appears 116 times on G2 alone, with 91 mentions of limitations. Advantage is $39/month, Premier $139, and team plans need three users minimum.
  • • Support below Premier is email only — no phone, no chat — and reviewers report unreachable numbers when chasing a charge.

Where the ratings disagree. 3.2 on Trustpilot and 1.6 on PissedConsumer with 83% unfavourable, against solid scores on the software directories. The directories rate the survey tool; the consumer sites rate the subscription.

Best for: teams that need research-grade survey logic (skip logic, randomisation, quotas) with compliance (HIPAA, GDPR, SOC 2) and 200+ integrations.
Who should skip it: if you just need a simple form (Google Forms is free) or you're cost-sensitive with high response volumes — the overage fees add up.

The natural comparison is Google Forms or Typeform — the free-basic and the design-led form tools. Decide by which one fits the job above, not by the louder brand.

Frequently asked questions

What does SurveyMonkey cost for a team?

€30 or €75 per user per month billed annually, but with a three-user minimum on both — so the entry point is €90 a month, not €30. Read on 17 August 2026 from an EU address; your storefront may quote dollars.

What happens if I go over my response limit?

You are charged €0.10 for each additional response. Two things make that bigger than it looks: on paid plans the limit is counted across all surveys and all users rather than per survey, and SurveyMonkey Audience responses are sold separately on top. A single popular survey can therefore consume the whole team's allowance.

Is the free plan enough to test the product?

For the interface, yes; for a real survey, no. Ten questions and twenty-five responses will not complete a usable study, so treat the free tier as a demo. If you need a genuine pilot, budget for one month of a paid plan and check the response ceiling against your list size first.

Does SurveyMonkey replace my current setup or sit on top of it?

Usually it sits on top rather than replacing everything. Tools in this class tend to become the layer where work is tracked and handed off, while your existing systems stay the source of truth for their own data. Before you buy, map which tools it would replace outright and which it would merely sync with. If the answer is that it syncs with all of them, you are adding a layer rather than removing one.

Who should not use SurveyMonkey?

Skip it if you just need a simple form (Google Forms is free) or you're cost-sensitive with high response volumes — the overage fees add up. Buying a tool to fix a problem you don't have yet just adds cost and another login to manage.
