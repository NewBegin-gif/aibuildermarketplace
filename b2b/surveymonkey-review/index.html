Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 8 Sep 2026

Team plans are annual-only in practice: pricing.md lists Team Advantage Monthly and Team Premier Monthly as Canada-only and "currently not available to any user". The geography tables separate a core seat from each additional seat, and the per-user figure the pricing page advertises is the additional one. United States: Team Advantage Annual is $468 a year for the core seat plus $360 for every seat after it; Team Premier Annual is $1,668 plus $1,104.

Euro storefronts carry the same shape at €432 plus €360 and €1,188 plus €900. Team plans require three or more users, Enterprise five or more on a custom quote. Overage above the included response limit is priced per storefront: $0.15 a response in dollar countries, €0.10 in euro countries, £0.15 in sterling. That included limit is stated nowhere in pricing.md, whose feature matrix marks responses "Unlimited" on every paid plan. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

The first seat costs more than the rest

The advertised team price is a per-user figure, and nobody pays only that. SurveyMonkey's own pricing.md separates a core seat from each additional seat, and the figure the pricing page advertises is the additional one. In the US table, Team Advantage Annual is $468 a year for the core seat and $360 for every seat after it, so the three-seat minimum comes to $1,188 a year rather than the $1,080 you get by multiplying the advertised $30 a month by three.

Team Premier is $1,668 plus $1,104 a seat, so three seats is $3,876. Team plans are annual-only in practice: the monthly variants are listed as Canada-only and unavailable to any user. Individual paid plans sit alongside those, and the free tier — 10 questions and 25 responses — is not a research instrument by any definition; 25 responses cannot support a conclusion about anything. The mechanic that costs real money is the response meter, and it has an unusual shape.

On paid plans, response limits are counted across all surveys and all users rather than per survey. A three-person team therefore shares one pool, which means a colleague's large study quietly consumes the allowance everyone else was planning to use. Additional responses are charged at €0.10 each once you pass the limit. Put a real study through that and the subscription stops being the main number. Five thousand responses beyond your allowance is €500 in euro countries and $750 in dollar ones — between two fifths and two thirds of what three seats cost for an entire year.

On any serious piece of research the overage dominates, which makes the included response count the single most important figure on the page. SurveyMonkey's own pricing.md makes that worse rather than better: its feature matrix marks responses "Unlimited" on every paid plan, while the same document charges an overage "above the plan's included response limit" and never states that limit. Get the number in writing before you sign, alongside confirmation that the pool is shared.

One correction while we are here: we previously treated our own $0.15 overage figure as a misreading against a €0.10 truth. It was not. The overage is priced per storefront — $0.15 a response in dollar countries, €0.10 in euro ones, £0.15 in sterling — so both were right, and which one applies to you depends on where you buy. Two more costs sit outside all of it. SurveyMonkey Audience — respondents supplied by SurveyMonkey rather than from your own list — is sold separately, so if you do not have an audience the research cost is a third line item entirely.

And a good deal of the useful analytics and question logic sits on higher tiers, with branding and customisation limited below them. Price this from your response volume and your analysis needs, not from the advertised per-seat price.

How to actually use SurveyMonkey

Ask two questions before you buy: how many responses are included on the tier you are considering, and whether that pool is shared across every user and every survey on the account. Both matter more than the subscription. Responses beyond the limit cost €0.10 each in euro countries and $0.15 in dollar ones, so a 5,000-response study adds €500 or $750 on top of a three-seat plan that costs €1,152 or $1,188 for the whole year — and on a shared pool, a colleague's project can spend your allowance.

Budget the real entry price, not the advertised one. Team plans start at three users and the first seat costs more than the rest: in the US that is $468 plus two seats at $360, so $1,188 for the year, and the higher team tier is $1,668 plus $1,104 a seat. Euro storefronts read €432 plus €360. If you are one person, look at the individual plans instead rather than paying for two colleagues you do not have.

Pilot on the free tier only to check the question types and the logic you need, never to test a study — 10 questions and 25 responses cannot tell you anything about your research question. And if you do not have your own list to survey, price SurveyMonkey Audience separately before you commit; supplied respondents are a third cost on top of the plan and the overage.

Frequently asked questions

What does SurveyMonkey cost for a team?

More than three times the advertised per-user price. SurveyMonkey's own pricing.md charges a core seat and then a lower rate for each additional seat, and the figure the pricing page advertises is the additional one. In the US that is $468 a year for the core seat plus $360 for each seat after it, so the three-seat minimum is $1,188 a year, not the $1,080 you get by multiplying $30 by three. Team Premier is $1,668 plus $1,104 a seat. Euro storefronts read €432 plus €360, and €1,188 plus €900. Team plans need three users and are annual-only in practice (read 2026-09-08).

What happens if I go over my response limit?

You are charged per response above your plan's included limit, at a rate that depends on your storefront: $0.15 in dollar countries, €0.10 in euro ones, £0.15 in sterling. Two things make that bigger than it looks: on paid plans the limit is counted across all surveys and all users rather than per survey, so a colleague's popular survey can consume the whole team's allowance, and SurveyMonkey Audience responses are sold separately on top. The included limit itself is not published in the vendor's pricing.md, which marks responses "Unlimited" on every paid plan while still pricing the overage — so ask for the number in writing.

Is the free plan enough to test the product?

For the interface, yes; for a real survey, no. Ten questions and twenty-five responses will not complete a usable study, so treat the free tier as a demo. If you need a genuine pilot, budget for one month of a paid plan and check the response ceiling against your list size first.

What should I weigh before buying Operations & Workflow software?

Usually it sits on top rather than replacing everything. Tools in this class tend to become the layer where work is tracked and handed off, while your existing systems stay the source of truth for their own data. Before you buy, map which tools it would replace outright and which it would merely sync with. If the answer is that it syncs with all of them, you are adding a layer rather than removing one.
