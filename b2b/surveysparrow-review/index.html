What SurveySparrow does

Omnichannel experience management platform. Create highly engaging, conversational surveys that drive higher completion rates and actionable customer feedback.

Pricing (2026)

A free plan (limited); paid survey plans from about $19-39/mo (Basic) through Starter ~$59 and Business ~$149, with higher CX and reputation suites custom-quoted; billed quarterly or annually, not monthly (2026). Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

Correction after re-checking: Under the pricing page's yearly billing option the plan data lists $19 with 2,500 responses (Basic), $39 with 15,000 (Starter) and $79 with 36,000 (Business), the last two carrying a sale flag; under quarterly billing the same page lists $39 with 600 responses, $59 with 3,600 and $149 with 9,000. The page's sticky header separately lists Basic $19, Starter $49 and Business $99 with no billing period attached, so no per-month or annual-commitment rate should be asserted from those figures. Add-ons: extra users $49 per user per month, additional responses and API calls quote-only, Market Research from $125/month on Business or higher. Free plan: 75 responses per quarter, 3 active surveys, 10 questions per survey. (re-verified 2026-08-04)

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

The conversational-survey UX is the draw, but response limits on lower tiers and the 'contact sales' wall on advanced features (API, higher volume) make budgeting fiddly — and there's no true monthly billing.

What buyers report about SurveySparrow

Recurring themes from G2, Software Advice and Gartner Peer Insights, read in August 2026. We have not run SurveySparrow ourselves, so this is other buyers' experience rather than ours. We summarise it here because the complaints are usually the part a vendor page leaves out.

What they praise
  • • The conversational survey format measurably lifts response rates — that is the reason reviewers choose it over a standard form builder.
  • • Easy to pick up and use without training.
What they complain about
  • • White-labelling, custom CSS, HIPAA compliance and the Salesforce integration all sit on higher tiers. If any of those is why you are looking, price the tier that has it, not the $19 entry.
  • • Reporting is repeatedly called limited — "poor reporting" and "limited reporting" are both recurring G2 tags.
  • • From $19 to $249 a month, and reviewers from small organisations, nonprofits and occasional users consistently call it steep.

When the paid plan is worth it

SurveySparrow's forever-free tier is documented as 75 responses per quarter, 3 active surveys and 10 questions per survey: enough to prove the conversational format works, not enough to run an ongoing programme. The feature comparison marks 20+ common question types as available on every plan, so the free ceiling is volume and automation, not question design: display logic and skip logic, web embeds and QR codes all begin at Basic. Paying makes sense once collection is continuous. Under the yearly billing option the pricing page lists $19 with 2,500 responses, $39 with 15,000 and $79 with 36,000, the upper two sale-flagged; under quarterly billing it lists $39 with 600 responses, $59 with 3,600 and $149 with 9,000. Upgrading explicitly does not fix seat cost or volume overruns. Extra users cost $49 per user per month on top of the plan, Business includes only 3 users, and additional responses and API calls are "Contact us" items rather than self-serve top-ups. Market research is a separate add-on starting at $125/month, on Business or higher. A single annual survey rarely justifies any of it: the 14-day trial already opens Business features for up to 50 responses.

How to actually use SurveySparrow

The shortest route to value is short on paper. After signing up without a credit card, the Home page carries a New survey button; browse the classic templates or start from scratch, enter the Survey Title, choose the survey type and pick a folder, then click Create Survey to land in the builder. Changes are saved automatically, so the only real step left is sharing. Common SurveySparrow setup mistakes

Where SurveySparrow setups actually go wrong, and what it costs you when they do. Same sources as the setup notes above — not from a walkthrough we ran ourselves.

The friction sits in delivery rather than building.

  • A survey link works on every plan, but web embeds and QR codes are marked unavailable on the free plan, and sending from your own address means Settings, then Email Settings, then Email: add the DNS records SurveySparrow generates at your domain host, return and click Verify against the address.
  • The vendor documentation warns that domain verification may take up to 24 hours from the time of the request, and that deleting those records afterwards prevents emails from triggering.
  • Anyone with a launch date should book that day in advance, and remember the trial caps collection at 50 responses.

Frequently asked questions

Is SurveySparrow worth it for a small team?

It depends on whether you have the bottleneck it solves. Small teams get the most out of this category when one clear problem is already costing real hours or revenue; buying ahead of that just adds cost and another login. Price it against the hours or lost deals it removes, not against its feature list, and start on the smallest plan that covers the job.

Who should not use SurveySparrow?

Skip it if you just need a quick, occasional form — a free tool like Google Forms or Tally covers it without a subscription. Buying a tool to fix a problem you don't have yet just adds cost and another login to manage.
