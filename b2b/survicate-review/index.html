We tested it · 2026-10-29

Tested by our team on 29 October 2026 on the free trial: we created the account and our AI assistant ran the test in our browser. We built a two-question link survey, answered it twice ourselves, exported the responses and opened the Starter checkout without paying.

How we tested

  1. Built a two-question link survey from scratch
  2. Answered it twice ourselves with made-up test text
  3. Exported the responses as CSV via the emailed link
  4. Opened the Starter checkout and stopped before paying
  • The account opened on a 'Free 10-Day Trial' with '25 responses remaining', so the trial is limited by responses as well as days. No card was asked.
  • A new link survey is live from the start: 'Enable survey' is switched on and the anonymous link works before you press anything like Launch.
  • Answering a second time from the same browser showed 'You've already responded to this survey'. Retaking is off by default; we switched it on to add our second response.

Frequently asked questions

What happens when I hit my Survicate response limit?

It depends on the plan. The pricing FAQ (read 26 September 2026) says Starter lets you switch on overages and keep collecting for an extra cost - $0.89, $0.59 or $0.39 per additional response depending on the rung. On Growth, Pro and Enterprise your surveys stop collecting once the limit is reached, and you have to upgrade or buy additional responses, at a price the page does not print. Size Growth on your peak month, not your average.

Can I get a refund if I cancel Survicate?

No. The FAQ (read 26 September 2026) says Survicate does not offer refunds: you can cancel at any time, the subscription runs to the end of the billing cycle, and the account then drops to the Free plan - 25 responses a month, and archived surveys can be reopened only up to 300 responses. Upgrades take effect immediately at the prorated difference. On an annual Growth commitment, use the 10-day trial, which needs no card, before paying.

Does Survicate have a discount for education or non-profits?

Yes: an extra 25% off any yearly plan for educational institutions and non-profits, arranged by emailing sales@survicate.com, according to the pricing FAQ (read 26 September 2026). It applies to yearly plans only, so it does not help on the monthly-only Starter plan. On Growth's 250-response rung at $114 a month billed annually, 25% off would bring it to $85.50.

Can Survicate analyse feedback that does not come from its own surveys?

Survicate's homepage (survicate.com, read 4 November 2026) says it automatically analyses feedback from your surveys, support tickets, call recordings, reviews and more. The page does not say which plan includes this, which sources connect natively or whether connecting them costs extra, so those points are not confirmed; check the plan cards before counting on it to replace a separate feedback-analysis tool.

Can I export my data from Survicate, and does Survicate train AI on it?

Survicate support told us in writing on 29 October 2026 that survey data can be exported on a paid plan, from Analyze, Export, as XLSX or CSV (or PDF and PNG for reports), for all responses or only filtered ones, and that earlier exports stay available in Settings. On AI, support said Survicate does not use your data to train AI models: responses sent to OpenAI for AI follow-up questions may not be used for training, and text insights and Research Hub use OpenAI's Enterprise API, which does not train on customer data. This is the vendor's statement; we did not test it.

Does Survicate have a Data Processing Agreement for EU customers?

Support said on 29 October 2026 that the DPA, terms and privacy policy can be downloaded from Survicate's help centre, that data is hosted on AWS in Ireland, and that a custom DPA is available on some plans. We have not read the DPA itself, so check its terms before you sign.
