Platform Overview: What Synthesia Actually Does

Synthesia transforms text into video content using AI-generated avatars that speak in natural-sounding voices. The platform eliminates traditional video production bottlenecks by providing a browser-based editor where users input scripts and select from 140+ AI avatars speaking in 120+ languages.

The technology works by combining advanced text-to-speech synthesis with realistic avatar animation. Users create videos by typing scripts, choosing avatars, selecting backgrounds, and adding visual elements like slides, images, and logos. The AI handles lip-syncing, facial expressions, and natural speech patterns automatically.

For B2B applications, Synthesia excels in scenarios requiring frequent content updates, multilingual distribution, or consistent messaging across teams. Common use cases include employee training modules, product update announcements, customer onboarding sequences, and internal communications.

The platform processes videos in the cloud, typically delivering finished content within 10-15 minutes depending on length and complexity. This speed advantage becomes crucial when dealing with time-sensitive communications or rapid product iteration cycles.

Detailed Pricing Breakdown and Hidden Costs

Starter Plan: $30/month

The entry-level plan includes 10 video credits monthly, with each credit representing one minute of video content. Additional features include access to 70+ avatars, basic templates, and standard video quality (720p). This plan targets individual creators or small teams testing the platform.

Key limitations include no custom avatars, limited template access, and basic customer support. Users cannot download videos in bulk or access advanced editing features. The 10-credit limit restricts usage to short-form content or infrequent video creation.

Creator Plan: $90/month

The mid-tier option provides 30 video credits monthly with enhanced features. Users gain access to all 140+ avatars, premium templates, HD video quality (1080p), and priority customer support. This plan suits growing businesses with regular video content needs.

Additional benefits include basic brand customization, team collaboration for up to 3 users, and extended video length capabilities. However, custom avatar creation and API access remain unavailable. The 30-credit allocation supports moderate video production volumes.

Corporate Plan: $1,000/month

Enterprise-focused pricing includes 100 video credits monthly with comprehensive feature access. Corporate plans provide custom avatar creation, full brand kit integration, advanced collaboration tools, and dedicated account management. API access enables workflow automation and system integrations.

This tier includes priority processing, reducing video generation time to 5-10 minutes. Enhanced security features meet enterprise compliance requirements. The plan supports unlimited team members with granular permission controls.

Hidden Costs and Additional Fees

Credit overages cost $3-5 per additional minute depending on the plan level. Custom avatar creation incurs one-time fees ranging from $1,000-3,000 based on complexity and turnaround time. Professional services for implementation and training start at $5,000.

Annual billing provides 10-20% discounts across all plans. However, unused credits don't roll over monthly, potentially wasting budget on inconsistent usage patterns. Enterprise customers can negotiate custom pricing for high-volume usage or specific feature requirements.
