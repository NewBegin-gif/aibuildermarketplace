What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

It generates posts quickly and schedules them daily or weekly in bulk. And a Shopify blog filled with unedited AI articles is precisely the pattern search engines have spent the last two years demoting: the app lowers the cost of production, not the bar for ranking. Used the other way round, as a drafting and structuring tool for posts you actually edit, it is sensible and cheap. Two practical points on the price: the $9.99 headline holds only on Shopify Basic, and the surcharge takes it to $69.99 a month on Shopify Plus, which is the sort of detail that belongs in a headline rather than a footnote.

And the listing never says how many of the 250 monthly credits a single post costs, so the allowance cannot be planned against until you ask.

What Tapita AI SEO Blog Builder does

AI blog builder for Shopify: generates SEO-oriented posts with titles, images and keyword suggestions, embeds your products, and schedules bulk publishing from a drag-and-drop editor. Free with 50 one-off credits; Pro is $9.99 a month on Shopify Basic but rises with your Shopify plan to $14.99, $39.99 or $69.99.

When the paid plan is worth it

The number to check is not $9.99, it is which Shopify plan you are on. Pro is $9.99 a month on Basic, $14.99 on Grow, $39.99 on Advanced and $69.99 on Shopify Plus — the same app, the same 250 credits, a seven-fold spread. That surcharge is one line on the listing and it is the single largest factor in what this costs you, so read it before you compare Tapita to anything else.

On Shopify Plus this stops being a ten-dollar utility and starts competing with proper content tools. On Basic the annual rate is straightforward: $101.89 against $119.88 paid monthly, a genuine 15% off, and worth taking only if you already know you will still be publishing in six months. The free tier is more useful than most because the editor, the SEO audit and unlimited posts are all in it — what you do not get is recurring credits, only 50 one-off, and none of the scheduling, translation, keyword research or content-strategy features.

So free is the place to judge the writing quality on your own products, and Pro is what you buy when you have decided to publish on a cadence. Credits are the second meter: 250 a month on Pro, $0.06 each beyond that. The listing does not state how many credits a post consumes, and that is the number that decides whether 250 is generous or tight, so ask before you plan a calendar around it.

How to actually use Tapita AI SEO Blog Builder

Use the 50 free credits on one real post and take it all the way to publication, edits included, because what you are measuring is how much work the draft still needs — that number, not the subscription, is the true cost of this tool. Before you subscribe, check what Pro actually costs on your Shopify plan: $9.99 is the Basic price and the surcharge runs to $69.99 on Plus.

Ask support how many credits a full-length post consumes so you can tell whether 250 a month is two posts or twenty. When you do publish on a schedule, put a human editing pass in the calendar next to the publishing slot rather than after it. And link every post to the products it is meant to sell. The product-embedding blocks are the part of this app that a general AI writer cannot replicate. And watch Search Console rather than the post count.
