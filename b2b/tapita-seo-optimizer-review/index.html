What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

The price on the listing is a Shopify-Basic price, and almost nobody reads the line underneath it. The same Pro plan is $39.99 on Shopify Basic, $44.99 on Grow, $69.99 on Advanced and $99.99 on Shopify Plus, because Tapita adds $5, $30 or $60 on top depending on your Shopify plan. That is a two-and-a-half-fold spread on identical software, and it is the single largest factor in what this app costs you. Work out which row you are on before you compare it to anything else.

The second meter has no published rate at all. Basic gives you 100 AI metadata credits a month and Pro gives you 500, and beyond that the listing says only that more audit pages and more credits carry an extra charge. It does not say how much, and it does not say how many credits one product's metadata consumes. Tapita's own sister app publishes $0.06 a credit on its listing; this one publishes nothing, so you cannot price a month of AI metadata before you install.

Your Shopify plan sets the price, not the tier

The number on the listing is a Shopify-Basic number, and the line underneath it is what actually decides your bill. Tapita adds a surcharge tied to your Shopify plan: +$5 on Grow, +$30 on Advanced and +$60 on Shopify Plus. So Basic is $9.99, $14.99, $39.99 or $69.99, and Pro is $39.99, $44.99, $69.99 or $99.99, for identical software (read 2026-08-21 on the Shopify listing).

Two consequences follow, and the second one is a genuine buying rule. First, the spread is two and a half fold: Pro on Shopify Plus costs $99.99 against $39.99 on Shopify Basic. A Plus merchant on Tapita's *cheapest* tier pays $69.99 — more than a Shopify Basic merchant pays for Tapita's most expensive one.

Second, because the surcharge is charged per install rather than per tier, the gap between Basic and Pro is a flat $30 whatever Shopify plan you are on. That changes the upgrade decision completely depending on where you sit. On Shopify Basic, Pro costs four times what Basic does and needs real justification. On Shopify Plus, Pro costs only 43% more than Basic — $99.99 against $69.99 — for five times the AI credits, five times the daily image allowance, unlimited keyword research and hands-on help with Search Console issues. If you are on Plus or Advanced, take Pro; the relative cost of the upgrade has already been paid.
