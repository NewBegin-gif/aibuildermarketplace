Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 9 Sep 2026

Tellent publishes no prices. Its pricing page (read in a live browser on 9 September 2026, and separately in the served HTML) lists three ATS tiers for Recruitee — Start, Advance and Optimize — and the two Tellent HR modules, Manage (HRIS) and Grow (performance), and every one of them ends in 'Get a demo' rather than a figure; agencies are told to ask for a custom quote.

Neither the rendered page nor the served HTML contains a single currency amount, and recruitee.com/pricing carries none either. What the page does state is scope: Start covers 5 active job posts and a single-page CareersHub; Advance adds unlimited posts, a multi-page branded careers site, ReferralsHub, 100 AI screening credits a month, 3 AI matching searches, workflow automations, candidate self-scheduling, offer-letter generation and up to 3 custom job fields; Optimize adds SSO, API support, a testing environment, requisition approval workflows, unlimited custom fields, 65 matching searches, onboarding support, a dedicated CSM and BI integrations.

SSO, Texting, Journeys, AgencyHub and WhatsApp Hiring are optional add-ons on the lower tiers. A free trial is offered on every tier and needs only a company account; the page states no trial length. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

The honest knock

Ask for Start, Advance and Optimize quoted together with their renewal prices, because you have no anchor to check a single number against. And as with every ATS, workflow fit — approval chains, careers-site needs, integrations with your assessment tools — matters more than the feature list; the free trial is enough to run one real vacancy through, so do that.

No price is published, so scope is the only lever you have

Tellent publishes no prices. Read in a live browser on 9 September 2026, tellent.com/pricing lists three Recruitee ATS tiers — Start, Advance and Optimize — and two Tellent HR modules, Manage for HRIS and Grow for performance, and every one of them ends in 'Get a demo' rather than a figure. The served HTML contains no currency amount at all, and neither does recruitee.com/pricing. The euro figures this review used to quote were reviewers' recollections rather than anything Tellent publishes, and we have taken them out of the pricing section rather than treat a recollection as a rate card.

So the only lever you have before the call is scope, and the scope is stated plainly. Start is five active job posts and a single-page careers site. Advance adds unlimited posts, a multi-page branded careers site, employee referrals, 100 AI screening credits a month, workflow automations, candidate self-scheduling and offer-letter generation. Optimize adds SSO, API support, a testing environment, requisition approval workflows, unlimited custom fields and a dedicated CSM.

Read that list as a price map, because it is one. Nothing in Advance is a feature you grow into by accident — it is where a team that hires continuously ends up — and Optimize is bought by whoever needs single sign-on and an approval chain, which is a procurement requirement rather than a hiring one. Decide which of the three descriptions is actually you before anyone quotes you.

What upgrading will not fix is the thing you are most likely to be sold on. Moving up buys you unlimited job posts, more AI credits and more governance; it does not buy you a better fit between the tool and how your hiring managers already work. Approval chains, offer workflow and the integrations with your existing assessment tools are the parts that decide whether an ATS is used or worked around, and no tier upgrade changes them.

Ask for all three tiers quoted side by side with their renewal prices, because with nothing published you have no anchor to check a single number against — three numbers at least give you the shape of the ladder.

How to actually use Tellent

There is no public price, so do the two things that stand in for one. First, decide which tier you are before the call — five job posts and a one-page careers site, or unlimited posts with referrals and screening credits, or the SSO-and-approval-chain tier — then ask for all three quoted side by side with renewal prices. Three numbers at least give you the shape of the ladder. Second, run one real vacancy through the free trial rather than a test one.

It is offered on every tier and needs only a company account; neither the pricing page nor the sign-up form states a length, so ask what yours is first. Post the job, let candidates apply, and watch what goes wrong: how the careers page looks on a phone, whether applications reach your hiring manager, how much manual work a rejection takes. Then ask for a walkthrough of what a trial cannot reach — approval chains, offer workflow, assessment-tool integrations — and check which of SSO, Texting, Journeys, AgencyHub and WhatsApp Hiring are billed separately on your tier.

Distilled from tellent.com/pricing (which resolves to tellent.com/pricing-platforms) read in a live browser on 9 September 2026 — the three Recruitee ATS cards, the two Tellent HR module cards, their add-on lists and the full feature-comparison table, none of which carries a figure — checked against the served HTML of the same page and of recruitee.com/pricing the same day, neither of which contains a single currency amount, and against auth.tellent.com/sign-up, which states no trial length.

The natural comparison is Greenhouse or Personio — Greenhouse is the ATS benchmark at higher cost; Personio bundles HR-first — Tellent sits between on price with EU roots. Weigh it against BambooHR on the job you actually need done.

Frequently asked questions

What should I weigh before buying Operations & Workflow software?

Usually it sits on top rather than replacing everything. Tools in this class tend to become the layer where work is tracked and handed off, while your existing systems stay the source of truth for their own data. Before you buy, map which tools it would replace outright and which it would merely sync with. If the answer is that it syncs with all of them, you are adding a layer rather than removing one.
