What Tenable does

Exposure and vulnerability management (makers of Nessus): scan, prioritize and remediate security weaknesses across IT, cloud and OT — for security teams of any size.

Pricing (2026)

Nessus Professional runs about $4,390-$4,790/yr and Nessus Expert about $6,390-$6,790/yr; cloud Tenable Vulnerability Management starts around $3,500+/yr for ~100 assets and scales with asset count (2026). Frequently asked questions

Is Tenable secure and reliable enough to depend on?

Judge it on evidence rather than adjectives. Look for a published security page, single sign-on and role controls on the plan you would actually buy (they are often gated to higher tiers), a status page with real incident history, and a clear answer on where your data is stored. If the tool will hold anything sensitive, those four answers matter more than any feature comparison.

Who should not use Tenable?

Skip it if you have no one to triage and fix findings — a managed security service or a lighter scanner fits a small team better. Buying a tool to fix a problem you don't have yet just adds cost and another login to manage.
