Communication & VoiceResearched assessment · by Daniel HaketUpdated 2026-09-25

Two costs are easy to miss and both are monthly.

Best for: support and sales teams that already text customers from personal phones and need a shared, compliant inbox with a record of who said what.
Who should skip it: if you send marketing blasts rather than conversations — that is a different product and priced per message elsewhere; also skip it if your volume is low enough that a shared inbox on your existing helpdesk covers it.
