What TicketTailor does

Low-fee event ticketing and box-office software: sell tickets online, manage check-in and keep more of your revenue without per-ticket booking fees.

Pricing (2026)

No subscription (2026): pay-as-you-sell about $0.85/ticket (+VAT), or prepaid credits down to ~$0.30/ticket; free events are genuinely free for most organizers (under ~5k tickets/yr); charities get 50% off forever. Stripe/PayPal/Square processing fees apply separately. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

The honest knock

The fee honesty is the product — flat cents per ticket instead of percentage skims makes it dramatically cheaper than the big platforms on higher-priced tickets (a $0.30-0.85 fee on a $50 ticket vs a percentage is no contest). The trade: you bring your own audience. There's no discovery marketplace pushing your event, so if you rely on a platform's browsing traffic to sell out, the cheap fees won't fill seats.

Best for: event organizers with their own audience (venues, communities, creators) who want flat low per-ticket fees, full branding and no subscription.
Who should skip it: if you depend on marketplace discovery to sell tickets (Eventbrite's traffic is the fee you're paying for), or you run two events a year at tiny volume.

The natural comparison is Eventbrite or Humanitix — Eventbrite trades higher percentage fees for discovery traffic; Humanitix routes fees to charity — Ticket Tailor wins on raw cost per ticket. Weigh the honest alternatives on our Eventbrite alternatives page.
