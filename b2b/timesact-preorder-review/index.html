Timesact Preorder Review (2026)Free to Start, Fixed Price Past 100 Orders

E-commerceResearched assessment · by Daniel HaketUpdated 2026-09-25

The Fixed price depends on your Shopify plan, not on what you use.

Best for: Shopify stores that take pre-orders, deposits or back-in-stock sign-ups on a modest or seasonal volume and want to start free, pay a few dollars a month in quiet months and switch to a fixed price when a launch runs past a hundred or so orders.
Who should skip it: if you run Shopify Plus with steady high pre-order volume and object to a price set by your Shopify plan; most of your checkout traffic goes through Shop Pay or other accelerated buttons and you cannot test that path first; or you only need back-in-stock emails, which lighter apps cover.

Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 25 Sep 2026

Two families of plans, all billed in USD through Shopify every 30 days with no transaction fees. Flexible plans are usage-based: CORE Flexible starts at $5 a month for 10 orders and adds deposits, split payments, $0-down pre-orders, CSV upload, pre-order by product tag, SMS, 30 days of analytics and branding removal, at $0.20 per order past the volume you pick; PRO Flexible starts at $7 for 10 orders and adds market and location targeting, storewide automatic back-in-stock and low-stock, charge on fulfilment and analytics without a time window, at $0.30 per order past your volume. The volume selector on the pricing page lists CORE Flexible at $11 for 50 orders, $17 for 100, $37 for 250, $73 for 500 and $107 for 1,000, and PRO Flexible at $17, $27, $57, $87 and $127 for the same volumes.

Frequently asked questions

What does Timesact Preorder cost?

Free covers 20 orders a month. CORE Flexible starts at $5 a month for 10 orders with $0.20 per extra order; PRO Flexible starts at $7 with $0.30 per extra order. Fixed plans cover unlimited orders from $23 a month (CORE) or $27 (PRO) on Basic Shopify, rising with your Shopify plan to $117 and $147 on Plus. Billed in USD through Shopify, read on 25 September 2026.

When is Fixed cheaper than Flexible?

For a Basic Shopify store, somewhere between 100 and 250 orders a month: CORE Flexible is $17 for 100 orders and $37 for 250, against $23 for CORE Fixed. For a Plus store the Fixed price is $117, so Flexible stays cheaper up to about 1,000 orders ($107). You can switch plans in the app at any time.

Does Timesact take a cut of sales?

No. The pricing page and FAQ say it never takes a percentage of a sale on any plan. The only usage charges are the per-order amount on Flexible plans past the volume you chose and SMS messages, from $0.02 each for US numbers, if you use them.

What should I weigh before buying E-commerce software?

Check your platform and version specifically. Shopify support is close to universal in this category, while WooCommerce, BigCommerce, Magento and headless setups vary a lot, and 'supported' sometimes means a generic API rather than a maintained app. A listing in your own platform's app store, with recent reviews there, is a better signal than the vendor's logo wall.
