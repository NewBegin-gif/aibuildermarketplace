We tested it · 2026-10-20

Tested by our team on 20 October 2026 on a new free Beginner account: Daan created the account and our AI assistant ran the test in his browser. We built one project with tasks, a sub-task, a section, a priority and a date typed in plain words, then created projects until the free plan's limit appeared.

How we tested

  1. Created a project from My Projects
  2. Added three tasks in succession
  3. Entered a task containing the word today
  4. Added a subtask through the parent task details
  5. Created a Later section in the project menu
  6. Set the second task to priority 1 and displayed the completed third task
  7. Created five projects and attempted a sixth
  • A new account started at '0 projects' with a 'Free' label; the Inbox does not count as a project.
  • Typing 'Review today' was recognised while typing: Todoist highlighted 'today', removed it from the task name and saved a task called 'Review' due Today, and the Today view counted 1.
  • A sub-task added through the task details sat indented under its parent, which then showed a '0/1' counter. A section 'Later' came from the project menu with its own Add task.

Frequently asked questions

Is paying monthly for Todoist Pro much more expensive?

It comes to $24 a year more. Read 25 September 2026 with the page set to USD: Pro is $5 a month on annual billing, charged as $60 up front, or $7 on monthly billing, which is $84 over twelve months. Business is $8 per user on annual billing ($96) against $10 monthly ($120), plus local tax on both. Monthly costs more but binds you for one month at a time, which makes it the sensible way to find out whether the habit sticks before paying for a year.

What exactly does the free plan cap?

Five personal projects, three filter views, 5 MB per uploaded file and one week of activity history (plan comparison on the pricing page, read 25 September 2026). Archived projects do not count towards the project limit, according to the page's own tooltip, so finished work can be parked instead of deleted. In our test on 20 October 2026 the sixth project was blocked with a 7-day Pro trial offer, not created. Pro lifts those limits to 300 projects, 150 filters, 100 MB uploads and full history, and adds the calendar layout, task duration, deadlines and custom reminders. Free also gets limited Ramble sessions where Pro and Business get unlimited ones.

Can I share projects with other people without paying for Business?

Yes, within limits. The comparison table (read 25 September 2026) allows 5 people per personal project on every plan, the free one included. Business adds something different: a shared team workspace with up to 500 team projects, 250 people per team project, up to 1,000 members and guests, team roles and permissions, and centralised billing. A team created without paying gets 5 projects, per the page's tooltip. A household list shared by three people fits in personal-project sharing; permissions and one invoice for a team are what Business is for.

Do I need Todoist Pro to connect Claude, ChatGPT or the API?

No, according to Todoist's AI page (todoist.com/ai-tools, read 4 November 2026): its FAQ says connecting Todoist to Claude, ChatGPT, its MCP server, CLI or API "is available to everyone". The same answer says some basic Todoist Assist features, such as Filter Assist, are free, that more advanced Assist capabilities come with limited monthly use on free plans, and that paid plans get full access. The size of that free monthly allowance is not stated there, and the AI assistant you connect is billed by its own provider.
