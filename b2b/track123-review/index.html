Track123 Review (2026)$9 for 300 Orders, 5 Cents for Every One Over

E-commerceResearched assessment · by Daniel HaketUpdated 2026-09-27View Track123 in the Atlas →

The overage is the most expensive order on the ladder.

Best for: Shopify stores shipping from a few dozen to tens of thousands of orders a month, dropshippers included, that want a branded tracking page and order lookup on their own store, PayPal and Stripe tracking sync, and a free tier to start on.
Who should skip it: if you need estimated delivery dates, an unbranded page or Klaviyo triggers but ship far fewer than 2,000 orders a month, because all three start on the $49 Advanced tier; your volume swings well past your plan in peak months and you will not set a usage cap; or your fulfilment app rewrites tracking links and you cannot switch that permission off.

Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 27 Sep 2026

Starter is free for 50 orders a month, and orders past that do not sync until the next billing cycle. Growth starts at $9 a month or $84 a year for 300 orders, with 700 orders at $19 ($180 a year) and 1,000 at $29 ($276 a year). Advanced starts at $49 a month or $468 a year for 2,000 orders and climbs through 3,000 ($69), 5,000 ($99), 7,000 ($129), 10,000 ($179), 15,000 ($249) and 20,000 ($299), then in $50 steps to $599 a month, or $5,748 a year, for 50,000 orders.

Frequently asked questions

What does Track123 cost?

Starter is free for 50 synced orders a month. Growth is $9 a month for 300 orders, $19 for 700 and $29 for 1,000. Advanced is $49 for 2,000 and climbs to $599 for 50,000; Enterprise above that is quoted on request. Annual billing saves 20 to 22%. Read on 27 September 2026 in the vendor's pricing article.

What happens when I go over my orders?

On monthly billing each extra order costs $0.05, and the app keeps syncing. On annual billing there is no overage: new orders stop syncing until you upgrade. On the free plan orders past 50 do not sync until the next cycle. If you bill monthly, set the capped amount for usage charges; the vendor itself recommended this to a merchant billed $46 on the $9 plan.

Which features need the Advanced plan?

Estimated delivery dates, automatic translation of carrier scans, removing the Track123 branding, and the Klaviyo, Omnisend, Slack and Gorgias integrations. Growth adds custom email notifications, PayPal and Stripe tracking sync, data export and analytics to the free features.

Is there a free trial?

Growth and Advanced have a 7-day free trial on monthly and annual billing. Each store can claim it once; upgrading again later does not restart it.

Does it charge for customer lookups?

No. Plans count synced orders; the pricing article says lookups on the tracking page are unlimited on every plan.

What should I weigh before buying E-commerce software?

Check your platform and version specifically. Shopify support is close to universal in this category, while WooCommerce, BigCommerce, Magento and headless setups vary a lot, and 'supported' sometimes means a generic API rather than a maintained app. A listing in your own platform's app store, with recent reviews there, is a better signal than the vendor's logo wall.
