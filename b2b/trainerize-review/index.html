Trainerize Review (2026)Add-Ons Cost More Than the Plan

By Daan Haket · Tested hands-on · written with an AI assistant · who does what

The client-count ladder looks gentle, but most add-ons are flat and they land hardest on the smallest coach: at five seats a $23 Pro plan dressed with nutrition, payments and video is $63, while the same three turn the $225 two-hundred-client row into $290.

Best for

personal trainers and studios delivering structured workout/nutrition coaching through a polished client app, scaling by client count.

Skip it if

you coach a handful of clients over WhatsApp and sheets happily, or you want one flat price without add-on shopping.

What Trainerize does

Personal training and fitness-coaching platform. Deliver workout and nutrition plans, message clients and take payments from one branded app, so trainers can coach more clients online.

What buyers report about Trainerize

Hands-on: tested 2026-10-30Reported by buyers: Capterra, GetApp and Software Advice, read August 2026

Themes from the buyer-review sites listed at the foot of this page, read in August 2026. We tested Trainerize ourselves on 2026-10-30, but one test is a narrow sample, so here is what other buyers report. We summarise it here because the complaints are usually the part a vendor page leaves out.

What they praise
  • • The most well-rounded coaching platform in the category — AI workout builder, meal planner and video coaching in one place.
  • • Clients get an app that looks like a real product rather than a spreadsheet.
What they complain about
  • • The $23 headline is not what solo coaches pay. Real setups land at $100-$200 a month once custom branding ($169 one-off on Pro), Stripe Payments ($10/month) and Advanced Nutrition ($20-45) are added.
  • • Price increases trigger automatically when you cross a client threshold — growth costs you before it pays you.
  • • Building and editing workouts is confusing and slow, and the exercise library is thin with some outdated or unsafe entries.
  • • Crashes and glitches are reported.

The add-ons weigh most on the smallest coach

The client ladder looks gentle: free for one client, Grow $9 a month for two, and Pro from $23 at five seats to $225 at two hundred, all billed annually; month-to-month is about 10% more ($10, and $25 to $250). Studio Plus is $248 a month per location for 500 client seats. Prices are in USD before tax (re-verified 2026-09-27). A correction first. We earlier put the 200-client end of Pro at $275 and nutrition at a flat $45 on every Pro plan.

Operating each seat selector in both billing modes, Pro 200 is $225 annually or $250 monthly; $275 is Studio Plus billed monthly. And nutrition is now $20 a month on Grow, Pro 5 and Pro 15, rising to $45 only from Pro 30. The add-ons are still where the real bill lives. Business is $25 a month, Stripe payments $10 and video coaching $10, the same at five clients as at two hundred; the branded app is a one-time $169 plus your own $99-a-year Apple Developer Account.

At five seats, $23 plus nutrition, payments and video is $63: $40 of add-ons on a $23 plan. At two hundred the same three turn $225 into $290, about 29% more. Watch the step between 15 and 30 clients: the plan goes from $45 to $71 and nutrition from $20 to $45, so that stack jumps from $85 to $136. Measure it against what you bill. A coach charging $100 a month per client bills $500 at five clients and pays $63, about 13%; at two hundred the $290 sits inside $20,000, under 2%.

Early on, run without the add-ons you do not yet charge for. What no upgrade changes: add-ons are not included in the 30-day trial, and there are no refunds for the unused part of a subscription, so a year paid up front is spent. Studio Plus is the one tier where all add-ons are included.

    How to actually use Trainerize

    Price the stack, not the tier. The plan scales with client count, but most add-ons are flat: Business $25, payments $10 and video $10 a month cost the same at five clients as at two hundred, and nutrition is $20 up to 15 clients and $45 from 30. At five seats a $23 plan with nutrition, payments and video on it is $63, nearly three times the sticker. Turn on only what you charge for.

    If nutrition planning is not part of your package yet, do not subscribe to it; add each module the month it starts appearing on a client invoice. That single discipline is the difference between about 13% of revenue and a few per cent. Use the free single-client tier or Grow to test the part that actually keeps clients: the app they see. Put one real client through a full week — programme, check-in, message, progress photo — and watch it from their phone.

    Leave the branded app until the numbers justify $169 to Trainerize plus the $99-a-year Apple Developer Account it requires, which Apple bills you for directly.

    Frequently asked questions

    Can you export your data from Trainerize?

    Exit check 2026-10-30; route excerpt: No exercise export or import control found in the library and inspected Business menu …; came along (excerpts): not_tested; lost (excerpts): not_tested; re-import: not tested.

    What happens when my Trainerize free trial ends?

    Nothing is charged. The pricing FAQ (read 27 September 2026) says the 30-day trial, which has unlimited clients and needs no card, reverts automatically to the free plan with one coaching client, and you do not need to cancel. Add-ons are not included in the trial, so you cannot test nutrition, payments or video before paying; upgrading is possible at any time, before or after the trial ends.

    Do clients who only book sessions count toward my Trainerize seats?

    No. The FAQ (read 27 September 2026) distinguishes coaching clients, who get programs, tracking and messaging, from Basic clients, who can only book appointments and make purchases. Basic clients do not count against your plan's client limit, so every paid plan can carry an unlimited number of them; only coaching clients push you up the Pro ladder from $23 at five seats to $225 at two hundred, billed annually.

    Can I get a refund if I downgrade or cancel Trainerize?

    Not for time already paid. The FAQ (read 27 September 2026) says you can upgrade, downgrade or cancel at any time, but downgrades apply at the end of the current billing cycle and Trainerize does not refund unused portions of a subscription. Annual billing is about 10% cheaper - Pro 5 is $23 a month against $25 - so it pays only if you expect to stay on that seat count for the year.
