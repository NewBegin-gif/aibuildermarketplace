Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 9 Sep 2026

Basic is free for 1 client; Grow is $9/mo for 2 clients; Pro starts at $23/mo for 5 client seats and steps up through 15, 30, 50, 75, 100 and 200 seats, with the comparison table showing $275/mo at the 200-client end; Studio Plus is $248/mo for each location added, with 5+ locations quoted. Add-ons are priced per tier: on Pro, Advanced Nutrition Coaching $45/mo, Business $25/mo, Stripe Integrated Payments $10/mo, Video Coaching $10/mo and a Pro-level Custom Branded App at a one-time $169.

On Grow, nutrition is $20/mo (struck through from $45) with Business, payments and video at the same $25/$10/$10 and no branded app. Studio Plus includes all five. The branded app also requires your own Apple Developer Account at $99/year, charged directly by Apple. FitMetrics is a separate add-on from $6 per client. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

The add-ons are flat, so they hurt small coaches most

The client ladder looks gentle: free for one client, Grow $9 a month for two, Pro from $23 a month at five seats and stepping up to 200, and Studio Plus $248 a month per location. The comparison table puts the 200-client end at $275. Prices are in USD before tax. The add-ons are where the real bill lives. On Pro they are nutrition $45 a month, the Business module $25, Stripe payments $10 and video coaching $10, plus a one-time $169 for a branded app — and that app also needs your own Apple Developer Account, which is $99 a year charged by Apple rather than by Trainerize.

Crucially the add-ons are flat: they cost the same whether you coach five people or two hundred. Run that through the ladder and the distortion is severe at the bottom and mild at the top. A coach on Pro at five seats pays $23 for the plan; switch on nutrition, payments and video and the bill is $88, so the add-ons are nearly three times the subscription. The same three at the 200-client end turn $275 into $340, about a quarter more.

The sticker price is most misleading for exactly the coach with the least money. Note where the cheap nutrition price lives, too: $20 a month is a Grow price, and Grow stops at two clients — the moment you have five, nutrition is $45. The version that makes it decidable is software cost as a share of what you bill. A coach charging $100 a month per client bills $500 at five clients and pays $88 for a dressed platform — 18% of revenue, which is a lot.

The identical three add-ons sit inside a $20,000 book at two hundred clients. So this is expensive while you are building and cheap once you have built: early on, run without the add-ons you are not yet charging for. Against that, the client experience and the wider ABC ecosystem are genuinely mature, and that matters more than the arithmetic suggests: churn-prone coaches lose far more revenue to a clunky client app than to a fair subscription.

Add the branded app only when your brand is worth $169 to Trainerize plus $99 a year to Apple. Studio Plus is the one tier where all five add-ons are included.

How to actually use Trainerize

Price the stack, not the tier. The plan scales with client count, but the add-ons are flat — on Pro that is nutrition $45, Business $25, payments $10 and video $10 a month, the same at five clients as at two hundred. At five seats a $23 plan with nutrition, payments and video on it is $88, nearly four times the sticker. Turn on only what you charge for. If nutrition planning is not part of your package yet, do not subscribe to it; add each module the month it starts appearing on a client invoice.

That single discipline is the difference between 18% of revenue and a few per cent. Use the free single-client tier or Grow to test the part that actually keeps clients: the app they see. Put one real client through a full week — programme, check-in, message, progress photo — and watch it from their phone. Leave the branded app until the numbers justify $169 to Trainerize plus the $99-a-year Apple Developer Account it requires, which Apple bills you for directly.

What should I weigh before buying Operations & Workflow software?
