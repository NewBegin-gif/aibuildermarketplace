What Tresorit is

Zero-knowledge, end-to-end encrypted cloud storage and secure file sharing.

  • Zero-knowledge encryption — files encrypted end-to-end; only you hold the keys.
  • Secure sharing — links with passwords, expiry and revocation.
  • Team admin — policies, access controls and audit on business plans.
  • Swiss/EU data protection — a strong privacy jurisdiction and compliance focus.

What makes it stand out

Encryption you don't have to trust the provider for. With zero-knowledge architecture, Tresorit can't decrypt your files even if compelled — a categorically stronger guarantee than standard cloud storage. Paired with EU/Swiss data protection and proper team admin, it's a serious choice for confidential work.

Who it's for

Best for businesses, professional-services firms and anyone handling confidential client or company data who needs encryption they don't have to take on faith. If your files aren't sensitive and price is the priority, commodity storage will be cheaper.

Why switch from Dropbox

Dropbox is convenient, cheap and well-integrated — and not end-to-end encrypted by default, which is the whole reason to consider Tresorit. The honest line: if your files aren't sensitive, Dropbox is fine and cheaper. But if you handle confidential client or company data, zero-knowledge encryption means a server breach or a legal demand on the provider still can't expose your files — a guarantee standard cloud storage can't make.

How hard is it to switch?

Low, and best done in stages. Move your most confidential workspace first rather than everything at once, set up sharing controls and team policies, and keep your recovery keys handled carefully (zero-knowledge means there's no provider back door). Run it alongside Dropbox for the non-sensitive stuff until you're settled.
