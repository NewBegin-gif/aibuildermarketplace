The bill has two parts and both are published: a base fee from $99 and a resolution rate from $2 (pay as you go) down to $1 with volume packages.

Best for: customer-service teams with real ticket volume that want AI agents to resolve requests end to end and act inside the systems they already run — CRM, ERP, billing, commerce — with approval steps and human handover as they raise the automation level.

A base plan plus a per-resolution rate. Growth is $599 a month or $5,999 a year with 5 users, all digital channels, a $100 AI Operator budget and volume packages that bring resolutions down to $1. Business is $2,000 a month billed annually with 5 users, 3 workspaces (multi-brand), a $500 AI Operator budget, two custom integration days a year and a success consultant.

Frequently asked questions

What happens if my ticket volume goes over what I planned for?

Nothing stops. The pricing FAQ (read 27 September 2026) says you keep paying per resolution at your plan's rate, $2.00 pay as you go on Starter, and higher volumes move you into prepaid packages that bring it down to $1.00. Only full resolutions count as one; a hand-off to a person counts as half, and tickets passed straight to a human or flagged are free, so a busy month costs more only where the AI actually closed tickets.

Does Typewise have a startup deal?

Yes, through partner accelerators and VCs. The startup page (read 27 September 2026) says any code starts the free trial, and if it matches a partner and the company is under 5 years old, under $2M revenue, under $10M raised, under 50 employees and independent, Typewise adds 1,000 resolutions and 3 months free after a review of one to two weeks. Without a partner code you get the standard 14-day trial of 100 resolution credits.

Do I have to leave Zendesk to use Typewise?

No. Typewise's own Zendesk comparison (read 27 September 2026) recommends keeping Zendesk as the system of record and running Typewise on top of it, so nothing is migrated and it can be removed again. You would then pay Zendesk's seats plus Typewise's base plan from $99 a month and its resolution fees. The same page says Typewise hosts in the EU with zero data retention for AI processing.

What should I weigh before buying Customer Support software?

Judge it on evidence rather than adjectives. Look for a published security page, single sign-on and role controls on the plan you would actually buy (they are often gated to higher tiers), a status page with real incident history, and a clear answer on where your data is stored. If the tool will hold anything sensitive, those four answers matter more than any feature comparison.
