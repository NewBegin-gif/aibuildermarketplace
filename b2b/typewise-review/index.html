What Typewise does

AI agents for customer service that resolve requests end to end and take action in the systems you already run — CRM, ERP, billing, commerce — configured in plain language instead of flowcharts. Pricing starts at $1 per resolution on their own site (read 2026-08-17) and there is no published plan table, so what counts as a resolution is the number to pin down before a pilot.

Pricing (2026)

(2026-08-17, read on their own site) One number and no plan table: "Starting from $1 per resolution", stated on their home page next to a free trial, a demo booking and an ROI calculator. There is no pricing page — /pricing returns a 404 and their sitemap lists none — so every rate above that floor is quote-based. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

The honest knock

The bill scales with resolutions rather than seats, and "from $1" is a floor, not a rate card. That makes one unpublished definition the number that decides your invoice: what counts as a resolution? Ask whether a reopened ticket counts twice, whether a handover to a human still bills, and what the rate actually is at your volume — in writing, before the trial. The ROI calculator on their site is the vendor's own arithmetic; the pilot is where you replace it with yours.

What they complain about

  • • The company is small — around 12 people — with a thin public track record. Weigh that if you are betting a support operation on it.
  • • Voice is on the roadmap but not available. If your channel mix includes calls, this covers only part of it.
  • • The review base is 15 reviews. That is enthusiasm from early adopters, not a broad verdict.

Frequently asked questions

Does Typewise replace my current setup or sit on top of it?

Usually it sits on top rather than replacing everything. Tools in this class tend to become the layer where work is tracked and handed off, while your existing systems stay the source of truth for their own data. Before you buy, map which tools it would replace outright and which it would merely sync with. If the answer is that it syncs with all of them, you are adding a layer rather than removing one.

Who should not use Typewise?

Skip it if your helpdesk's built-in AI answers already absorb the volume, or your channel mix leans on phone support — their home page lists voice among the channels while their own SMB page still marks it "coming soon", so pin that down before you sign. Buying a tool to fix a problem you don't have yet just adds cost and another login to manage.
