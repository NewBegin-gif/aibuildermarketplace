Automating the inbox: My honest take on Typewise

Tested & used by DaanVisit Typewise →

By Daan — originally shared on ZeroTouchSupport, my own workflow hub.

If there's one thing I've learned from optimizing large-scale risk and banking operations, it's that human talent is completely wasted on repetitive tasks. Having your support team manually type out responses to the exact same customer inquiries day after day is a fast track to burnout and high overhead costs.

When the heavy lifting is handled by autonomous systems, you earn the freedom to step away from the screen.

You need a system that handles the bulk of the volume autonomously, while ensuring the quality of communication remains razor-sharp. When I started looking for a robust solution that goes beyond basic chatbots, I found Typewise.

What exactly is Typewise?

Typewise is an enterprise-grade AI customer service platform. It functions as an orchestration layer that sits above your existing tools (like Zendesk, Salesforce, or your email inbox). Instead of just offering spell-checks, it uses specialized AI agents to independently classify cases, retrieve data from your CRM, and fully resolve customer requests end-to-end.

Why I consider this a must-have for scaling support

A true "Zero Touch" setup doesn't mean compromising on quality; it means utilizing intelligent automation to do the heavy lifting safely. Here is why Typewise is a core recommendation:

  • End-to-End Resolution: Through natural language instructions (no coding required), you can train Typewise to handle entire workflows—like processing a return, generating a quote, or updating billing info—completely autonomously.
  • Hybrid Intelligence (Human-in-the-Loop): You are always in control. For highly complex or sensitive issues, Typewise drafts the perfect response and flags it for human review. Your team simply approves or tweaks it, drastically cutting down ticket handling time.
  • Native Integration: It reads and writes across the systems you already use. It pulls order data from your ERP and updates customer records in your CRM without any manual copy-pasting.
  • Consistent Brand Voice: Because the AI model is customized with your company's specific vocabulary and knowledge base, the output is highly accurate and always perfectly aligned with your brand's tone.

By deploying a system like Typewise, companies are seeing a 2-3x boost in productivity. It transforms your customer support from a manual cost center into a streamlined, highly efficient operation.
