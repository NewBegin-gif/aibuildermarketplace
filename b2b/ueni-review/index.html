What UENI does

Done-for-you websites and online presence for small and local businesses. Get a professional site, bookings and listings live fast — without building it yourself.

Pricing (2026)

(re-verified 2026-07-20) A done-for-you website built in 7 days: a one-time build fee (currently promoted at $79, a $520 saving off the usual ~$599) plus a $24.99/mo subscription; includes domain, email, editor and a 1-on-1 launch call, 0 transaction fees and a 30-day refund guarantee. An optional Growth plan adds marketing services. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

It's done-for-you, which is the appeal and the catch. DIY editing and customisation are limited, e-commerce and integrations are basic, and the most common complaint is the gap between the 7-day promise and reality — slow design turnaround and patchy communication with the team. Expect some upselling.

What buyers report about UENI

Recurring themes from G2, Capterra, GetApp and Tekpon, read in August 2026. We have not run UENI ourselves, so this is other buyers' experience rather than ours. We summarise it here because the complaints are usually the part a vendor page leaves out.

What they praise
  • • Done-for-you build with hosting, domain and email bundled, usually delivered within a week — for a non-technical owner that removes the whole project. 4.2 out of 5 across 29 reviewers.
  • • Affordable against hiring anyone to build the same thing.
What they complain about
  • • Every plan carries a $149 setup fee on top of the monthly, which is easy to miss.
  • • G2 reviewers flag hidden add-on costs — a blog, a subscribe button and basic ecommerce pushed one reviewer well past the advertised tier.
  • • Design control and the more useful tools sit on higher plans: $12.99 Website Launch, $59 Plus, $99 Growth.
  • • It is a managed service. If you want to change things yourself frequently, this is the wrong shape.
Best for: small-business owners with no time or tech skills who want a professional site built for them, not another tool to learn.
Who should skip it: if you want full control over design, run a real e-commerce store, or need deep customisation and integrations — a DIY builder gives you more freedom.

The natural comparison is a DIY builder like Wix or Squarespace — the cheaper do-it-yourself route. Decide by which one fits the job above, not by the louder brand.

Frequently asked questions

Is UENI worth it for a small team?

It depends on whether you have the bottleneck it solves. Small teams get the most out of this category when one clear problem is already costing real hours or revenue; buying ahead of that just adds cost and another login. Price it against the hours or lost deals it removes, not against its feature list, and start on the smallest plan that covers the job.

Who should not use UENI?

Skip it if you want full control over design, run a real e-commerce store, or need deep customisation and integrations — a DIY builder gives you more freedom. Buying a tool to fix a problem you don't have yet just adds cost and another login to manage.
