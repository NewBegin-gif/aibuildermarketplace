That price is a three-year commitment

The term toggles are Monthly, Yearly at -30%, 2 Years at -40% and 3 Years at -50%. Shared Starter shows $3.44 a month against a $6.89 list, for one domain and roughly 10,000 visits a month; the wider shared range runs about $3.29 to $11.95 and VPS starts around $10.99. Do the arithmetic on that headline before you accept which term it assumes. $3.44 is exactly half of $6.89, and half is the three-year discount — the two-year rate on the published ladder would be $4.13.

So the figure on the card is a thirty-six-month commitment under their own discount structure. Check which term is selected on the page you are looking at, because two years and three years are materially different promises to make to a smaller host. The renewal is the second thing to read, and our sources disagree about it, which is itself the finding. Our August capture records plans renewing at $4.39 and $6.89 a month rather than at the promotional rate; the plan cards state they renew at the same rate for another two years.

Both cannot be true of the same plan, so read the renewal line on the specific card you are buying — it is quoted per plan — and take a screenshot of it. On the less favourable reading, six years of Starter costs $123.84 for the first term and $248.04 for the second, which averages $5.17 a month rather than $3.44. Note also that the headline rates sit under a running 40%-off sale, so the crossed-out price is marketing rather than a baseline, and paying monthly gives back the entire discount ladder — the advertised figure is not a monthly rate in any sense.

Take the 10,000 visits a month on Starter seriously as well. That is a real ceiling, not a soft guideline, and a single post that does well will pass it. So the honest summary is the classic budget-host trade with one improvement. The improvement is that the renewal terms are now written on the card rather than discovered on an invoice. The trade is unchanged: you are locking up two or three years with a smaller, less established brand whose support is generally fast but inconsistent depending on which agent picks up your ticket.

This is a value play, not a premium-SLA one, and it should be priced as one.

Frequently asked questions

What does UltaHost cost?

The advertised rates are term-dependent: monthly, yearly at 30% off, two years at 40% and three years at 50%, and the pages default to the two-year figure. Shared hosting starts around $3.29 to $3.44 a month on that basis, with VPS from about $10.99. Read on 17 August 2026.

What happens at renewal?

The price goes up, and this is the number to plan around rather than the headline. Our readings record plans renewing at $4.39 and $6.89 a month against promotional rates near $3.29. Budget the second term, and check the renewal line on the specific plan card before buying — it differs between products.

Which term should I pick?

The discount ladder rewards length steeply (30%, 40%, 50% for one, two and three years), so the arithmetic favours the longest term you are confident about. The counterweight is that hosting is easy to outgrow or regret, and three years is a long time to be wrong — the two-year rate is where most of the discount already sits.
