We tested it · 2026-11-02

Tested by our team on 2 November 2026 on the free trial: Daan created the account and our AI assistant ran the test in Daan's browser. We created two dynamic website QR codes, exported the list to CSV, downloaded a QR image and read the analytics page. We did not open the checkout.

How we tested

  1. Created two dynamic website QR codes with test text
  2. Exported both codes to CSV and downloaded one PNG
  3. Compared the analytics page with the exported scan counts
  • The sidebar counted the 14-day trial down ('Your QR Codes will expire in 13d : 23hr : 58m') and warned that the codes expire when the trial ends: something to plan for before you print anything.
  • The analytics page showed 1,800 total scans and 1,296 unique scanners on a brand-new account, under the banner 'You're exploring a preview of your future insights'. These are sample figures; our CSV export showed 0 real scans for both codes.
  • The note field took one line; the second line of our note was lost while typing, and the saved note did not appear in the CSV export.

Frequently asked questions

Can you export your data from Uniqode?

Exit check 2026-11-02; route excerpt: QR Codes > select two > menu > Export; came along (excerpts): Two QR rows and destination IDs / Unicode in the second QR title; lost (excerpts): Note text absent from CSV; re-import: not tested.

What is the cheapest way into dynamic QR codes?

Uniqode's own pricing FAQ puts the floor at $9 a month (re-read 13 September 2026): static codes are free indefinitely with no limit on how many, and paying starts the moment you want a code whose destination you can change later. The same FAQ now also prices a Core plan at $49 a month and calls it the best value, without saying where Core sits in the tier ladder it uses elsewhere. There is a 14-day free trial, but codes created in it go inactive when it ends. You will see a $5 entry quoted on third-party listing sites -- that is not what the vendor's own page says, so price it against the $9 figure and check what you are actually offered at checkout.

Can I pay monthly for a one-off campaign?

No. Every paid plan is annual, which reviewers on TrustRadius and OMR Reviews name as the main friction: the smallest commitment is a year paid up front, which is awkward for a seasonal test or a single event. Also budget for the data expiring: reviewers report scan history being deleted after 60 to 90 days depending on plan, so export on a schedule if you want year-on-year comparison.

Will one upgrade be enough?

Not necessarily, and this is the complaint worth reading before you buy. A reviewer on Product Hunt (read August 2026) describes hitting the 50-code ceiling, paying to upgrade, and then still being unable to upload without a further upgrade, though they note the company refunded quickly. The steps between tiers are steep and features sit behind them, so map what you need against the tier list rather than starting at the bottom and climbing.

Does cancelling Uniqode delete my QR codes and data?

No. Uniqode's help article on cancelling (read 25 October 2026) says cancelling stops the renewal and does not delete your account, QR codes or campaigns. Dynamic QR codes and campaigns stop working on the renewal date; static codes you already made keep working, but you cannot create new ones. The same article contradicts itself on dashboard access: one section says you keep it until the renewal date, a tip at the end says cancelling removes it right away. That timing is not confirmed, so export your data before you cancel.
