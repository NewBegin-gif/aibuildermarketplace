What Unleashed Software does

Inventory and MRP software for manufacturers and wholesalers — real-time stock across warehouses, purchasing, sales orders and batch or serial tracking. The entry price covers 3 users and 100 sales orders a month; past that the bill stacks up.

Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 17 Aug 2026

(prices read 2026-08-17 on unleashedsoftware.com/pricing) Two bundles, in USD and excluding tax. Unleashed Core is $399 a month, or $4,389 a year which the page itself puts at $366 equivalent monthly. Unleashed Pro is $729 a month, or $8,019 a year at $668 equivalent monthly. The toggle promises “Save up to 10%”; the actual saving works out at 8.3% on Core and 8.4% on Pro.

Both bundles come with a 14-day free trial with all features enabled. What the headline covers is the part worth reading twice: Core includes 3 user licences, Pro 5, and both include just 100 sales orders a month and 250,000 or 500,000 API calls. Everything past that is priced separately — extra users at $69 a month on Core and $89 on Pro, sales-order upgrades at $70 (up to 500), $200 (up to 1,500), $320 (up to 3,000) or $490 (unlimited) a month, and support as a paid add-on at $99 a month for Standard or $239 for Premier.

Onboarding is a one-off fee on top: $449 self-service, $799 standard, $5,549 full. Optional modules start at $119 a month for Advanced Inventory Manager, $262 for Marketing Accelerator and $450 for CRM. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

The honest knock

The 100 sales orders a month included on both bundles is the number that decides your bill, not the $399. A wholesaler running 1,500 orders with six users who wants phone support pays $399 for Core, $200 for the order tier, $207 for three extra seats and $99 for Standard support — $905 a month, or 2.3 times the headline, before a one-off onboarding fee of at least $449. Support being a paid add-on rather than part of the plan is the detail most comparison tables miss. Price the bundle you will actually be on: count your busiest month's orders, your real seat count, and decide whether you can live without phone support, then compare that total against the alternatives rather than comparing entry prices.

When the paid plan is worth it

Compare the two bundles on the figures the pricing page actually publishes and the step looks expensive. Core is $399 a month for 3 user licences, 100 sales orders and 250,000 API calls. Pro is $729 for 5 licences, the same 100 sales orders, and 500,000 API calls. Since extra users cost $69 a month on Core, buying the two additional seats there brings you to $537 for five people -- $192 a month less than Pro, and on the published lines the only thing that $192 buys is 250,000 more API calls. If you are not making those calls, ask what else Pro includes before you take it, because the page does not say. The bigger point is that neither headline is the bill. Both bundles include just 100 sales orders a month, and orders past that are a separate line: $70 up to 500, $200 up to 1,500, $320 up to 3,000, $490 for unlimited. Support is also an add-on rather than an inclusion, at $99 a month for Standard or $239 for Premier. A modest business doing 400 orders with standard support is therefore at $399 plus $70 plus $99, which is $568 -- forty per cent above the advertised price. Onboarding is a one-off on top: $449 self-service, $799 standard, $5,549 full. One small honesty check on their own page: the annual toggle promises “save up to 10%”, and the published annual figures work out at 8.3% on Core and 8.4% on Pro.

How to actually use Unleashed Software

Start with the 14-day trial, which runs with all features enabled, and use it to answer one question rather than to tour the product: how many sales orders does your busiest month actually produce? That number picks your tier and it is where the bill moves, because both bundles include only 100 a month and the upgrades run $70 to $490. Do the same for seats — Core covers 3 and Pro 5, and each extra one is $69 or $89 a month, so a six-person team on Core is $207 a month on top before anything else. Decide about support during the trial too: phone and live chat sit in a paid tier at $99 or $239 a month, and it is easier to price that in now than to discover it after go-live. Budget for onboarding as a separate one-off line of $449 to $5,549 depending on how much hand-holding you want. Unleashed is part of The Access Group. Frequently asked questions

Does Unleashed Software replace my current setup or sit on top of it?

Usually it sits on top rather than replacing everything. Tools in this class tend to become the layer where work is tracked and handed off, while your existing systems stay the source of truth for their own data. Before you buy, map which tools it would replace outright and which it would merely sync with. If the answer is that it syncs with all of them, you are adding a layer rather than removing one.

Who should not use Unleashed Software?

Skip it if your order volume is low or steady enough that the included 100 a month covers it and a lighter tool would do — at that size the bundle price is buying capacity you are not using, and the per-order tiers only start paying back once volume is genuinely there. Buying a tool to fix a problem you don't have yet just adds cost and another login to manage.
