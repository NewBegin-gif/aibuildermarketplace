What Velora does

Unified operations platform for nonprofits: Keela (donor CRM), Aplos (fund accounting) and Raisely (online fundraising) as one connected suite — donor management, campaigns and finances built for charities instead of adapted from business software.

Pricing (2026)

Priced per product (2026): Keela (CRM) from about $134/mo annual for up to 1,000 contacts, scaling to ~$379/mo at 10k contacts; Aplos (accounting) Lite ~$79/mo and Advanced from ~$189/mo; Raisely's core fundraising tools have historically been free with paid tiers. Verify current bundles on their site. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

The honest knock

Velora is three acquired products (Keela, Aplos, Raisely) being unified into one platform, and that's the honest watch-out: integration between them is newer than the marketing suggests, so check how deeply the pieces actually connect for your workflow before committing. Costs also stack per product and per contact count — a growing donor base raises the CRM bill. For nonprofits though, purpose-built beats adapted business software in fund accounting specifically.

Frequently asked questions

Does Velora replace my current setup or sit on top of it?

Usually it sits on top rather than replacing everything. Tools in this class tend to become the layer where work is tracked and handed off, while your existing systems stay the source of truth for their own data. Before you buy, map which tools it would replace outright and which it would merely sync with. If the answer is that it syncs with all of them, you are adding a layer rather than removing one.

Who should not use Velora?

Skip it if you're a tiny charity that only needs a donation page (free tools cover that), or you're deeply invested in one best-of-breed piece already. Buying a tool to fix a problem you don't have yet just adds cost and another login to manage.
