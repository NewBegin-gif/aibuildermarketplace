How to actually use Vida AI

Take the discovery call with your worst call types in hand, not your best. Vida's published process is four steps: a discovery call of twenty to thirty minutes; scoping and a proof of concept in one to two business days, returning a plan, a timeline and an investment starting at $1,500; a build and live pilot over two to ten business days; then production. You sign off before anything is built.

Use that scoping step to settle the two things the pricing page does not state: what production costs per month, and what a call or a minute costs beyond whatever the quote includes. Both are quoted rather than published, so both are negotiable now and neither is afterwards. Then measure containment during the pilot — the share of calls fully handled without a human — per call type rather than as a single average, because one good category hides a bad one.

And if texting is part of the plan, allow the two weeks the page itself flags for SMS campaign registration.
