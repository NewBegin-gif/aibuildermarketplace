What Vida AI does

Deploy autonomous frontline voice agents. Let AI answer calls, book appointments, and handle FAQs 24/7 without sounding robotic.

Pricing (2026)

Quote-oriented (2026): agency/reseller plans from about $750/mo (a starter tier carries 5 agents and ~2,500 AI voice minutes; growth tiers advertise unlimited agents and minutes) and platform/service-provider plans from ~$2.5k/mo. Verify current tiers with sales. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

This is priced for agencies and telecoms reselling AI phone agents, not for a single small business wanting one receptionist bot — if that's you, the entry price buys far more platform than you need. And the universal truth of voice AI: demo calls are rehearsed, your callers aren't. Pilot it on your real call types (accents, interruptions, edge requests) and measure containment rate before betting the front desk on it.
