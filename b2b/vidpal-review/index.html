What VidPal does

Autonomous content engine for faceless and topic-based channels: it discovers trending stories in your niche, auto-generates short-form videos and carousels with AI voiceovers, and auto-publishes them across Instagram, TikTok, YouTube and more.

When the paid plan is worth it

Vidpal's free plan is a demo, not a tier: 4 lifetime credits and a "Made with Vidpal.ai" watermark, no card required. The real choice is Starter versus Pro. Starter ($12/month billed annually, $24 monthly) gives you 15 credits a month, 1080p exports and no watermark, but manual sharing only. Auto-publish and scheduling start on Pro ($23/month annually, $46 monthly), which also brings 40 credits, 2K exports and twice-daily auto-generated content.

If you came for hands-free posting, Starter will not deliver it. Pro's own math is the catch: at 1 credit per Reel or carousel, twice-daily posting spends 40 credits in 20 days. That bites on monthly billing, where credits reset each cycle and do not carry over; annual plans receive the credits as a yearly pool with flexibility across the 12-month window.

How to actually use VidPal

Vidpal's homepage promises setup in under 5 minutes; its own cross-posting guide budgets about 15 minutes once several platforms are involved, so plan for the longer figure. You pick niche topics, set a brand voice and connect a social account, and the free plan's 4 credits require no card, so you can see output before paying, though free renders carry the "Made with Vidpal.ai" watermark. Check what your plan allows before you build a workflow around posting: the pricing comparison table leaves manual sharing to Instagram, YouTube and TikTok blank on Free and lists it from Starter up, and auto-publish and scheduling only start on Pro ($23/month annually, $46 monthly).

No Vidpal page states whether connecting an account is possible at all on the free plan.
