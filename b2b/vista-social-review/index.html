Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 1 Sep 2026

(2026) Professional $79/mo ($758/yr) — 15 profiles, 2 users, 10,000 DM contacts, 1,000 AI credits/mo; Advanced $149/mo ($1,430/yr) — 30 profiles, 4 users, 25,000 contacts, 2,000 credits, Zapier/Make/MCP; Scale $349/mo ($3,638/yr) — 70 profiles, 8 users, 100,000 contacts, 3,500 credits, white label and client connect; Enterprise custom with unlimited profiles and users. Yearly billing is 20% off on Professional and Advanced but 13% on Scale.

Add-ons on every tier: listening across social, web and news from $75/mo, X (Twitter) $29/mo, Employee Advocacy free for 3 advocates then from $199/mo for 25. 14-day trial, no card. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

The honest check is coverage depth: platform APIs change constantly, so verify the specific networks and features YOUR clients need (particularly newer TikTok/threads capabilities and approval workflows) inside the trial rather than trusting the comparison table. Agencies stepping up from Professional hit the Scale tier for white-labeling — price that step into client contracts from day one.

What Vista Social does

Unified social media management and publishing. Schedule posts, manage conversations, and track analytics across all your brand accounts.

What buyers report about Vista Social

Recurring themes from the buyer-review sites listed at the foot of this page, read in August 2026. We have not run Vista Social ourselves, so this is other buyers' experience rather than ours. We summarise it here because the complaints are usually the part a vendor page leaves out.

What they praise
  • • 4.8 on G2 across 1,071 reviews, ranked number one Easiest to Use in 2026, and 4.1 on Trustpilot — an unusually consistent picture.
  • • Social listening is included where competitors charge premium rates for it, and one inbox genuinely covers Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, Threads and LinkedIn.
What they complain about
  • • User seats are capped per plan (3, 6, 10), so growing the team means upgrading rather than adding a seat. Three paid tiers, and the step up between them is steep.
  • • Heavy AI users run out of credits, and the AI suggests hashtags reviewers describe as unrelated to the content.
  • • The mobile app reformats content and is harder to check things in.
  • • There is so much in it that features like social listening, approval workflows and review management are hard to find if you do not know they exist.

White-labelling costs 4.4 times the entry plan

Professional is $79 a month ($758 a year) for 15 profiles and two users, Advanced $149 ($1,430) for 30 profiles and four, and Scale $349 ($3,638) for 70 profiles, eight users, white-labelling and client connect. Enterprise is custom, with unlimited profiles and users. The 14-day trial needs no card. Three things the tier price does not include, on any tier. Listening is free only on your own profiles; listening across social, web and news starts at $75 a month.

X (Twitter) is $29 a month. Employee advocacy is free for three advocates and $199 a month for 25. A Professional account that needs listening and X is $183 a month, not $79. AI is a meter rather than an assistant you own: 1,000 credits a month on Professional, 2,000 on Advanced, 3,500 on Scale, unlimited only on Enterprise. Check the yearly discount instead of trusting the badge. It is exactly 20% on Professional and Advanced, but Scale's $3,638 is 13% off $4,188 — some $288 a year more than the badge implies, which makes Scale 4.8 times Professional on annual billing rather than 4.4.

What no upgrade fixes: whether the networks and post formats your clients use are supported this month. Only the trial answers that.

How to actually use Vista Social

Connect your clients' real accounts on day one of the 14-day trial, not sample ones. Platform APIs change constantly, so publish an actual post in each format you use, on each network you use, and run your real approval chain through it. That is the only test that means anything. Count users before profiles. Professional includes two users and Advanced four, so a three-person agency hits the user ceiling long before it fills 15 profiles — the boundary that bites first is not the one the plan is named after.

Add the add-ons to your quote before you compare anything. Listening beyond your own profiles is $75 a month, X is $29, and employee advocacy is $199 for 25 people, on every tier. If you are an agency, price the Scale tier into your retainers before your first client. White-labelling only exists there at $349 a month, and it is triggered by a client wanting your brand on the dashboard rather than by your volume.

Note that yearly billing takes 13% off Scale rather than the advertised 20%: $3,638, not $3,350.

The natural comparison is Sprout Social or Hootsuite — the premium incumbents — Vista's pitch is 80-90% of the feature set at a fraction of the seat price; verify your critical 10% in the trial. Weigh it against Hootsuite on the job you actually need done.
