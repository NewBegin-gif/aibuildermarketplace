Volza Review (2026)

Growth & RevenueResearched assessment · by Daniel HaketUpdated 2026-07-02

Growth tools only earn their keep if they move a real number — leads, conversions or revenue — not just activity. So where does Volza actually fit? It's most often picked as a leaner, cheaper alternative to ImportGenius — so the real question is whether it does the job you actually need without the bloat.

What Volza does

Global export-import trade intelligence. Search real shipment data to find verified buyers, suppliers and market trends across 80+ countries — prospecting built on actual trade records.

Pricing (2026)

From about $1,500/year for 3B+ shipment records across 200+ countries, bundling verified buyer/supplier contacts — a fraction of Panjiva or ImportGenius, which historically ran $15k–50k+/year (2026). Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

Best for: import-export businesses and sourcing teams that want global customs-level trade data plus verified buyer/supplier contacts at a fraction of enterprise pricing.
Who should skip it: if you only need US shipment data with fast daily updates (ImportGenius refreshes daily), or you're not doing import/export — this is a niche intelligence tool.

The honest knock

Two honest catches: downloads run on a point system where the cost varies by country (US or India shipments are 1 point, thinner markets up to 10), and the update cycle is slow — some datasets take up to two months to refresh. There's a learning curve too; it rewards analysts, not casual users.

The natural comparison is ImportGenius or Panjiva — the US-focused faster-updating one and the pricey enterprise incumbent. Weigh the honest alternatives on our ImportGenius alternatives page.
