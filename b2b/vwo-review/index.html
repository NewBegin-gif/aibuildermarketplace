What VWO does

A/B testing and conversion optimization platform. Run experiments, heatmaps and personalization to lift conversions without developers.

Pricing (2026)

Published from ~$314/mo (annual) for up to 10k MTUs; higher tiers add Insights (heatmaps/recordings) and Personalize. Usage-based, scaling with traffic (2026). Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

The honest knock

The jump from Growth to Pro is steep — small businesses get priced out of MVT and complex targeting — and the UI is dense with options. Some users note the reporting breaks more often than pricier tools (though it doesn't affect the tests themselves).

Best for: teams that want an all-in-one experience-optimisation platform (A/B testing + heatmaps + personalisation) with friendlier Bayesian stats and transparent pricing.
Who should skip it: if you're a small business wanting advanced targeting/MVT on the entry tier (you'll be priced up), or an enterprise running massive concurrent experiments — Optimizely handles that scale.
