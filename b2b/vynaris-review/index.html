Vynaris is an API gateway, so what you pay is mostly the price of the models it calls for you. Every routed request is billed at the original provider list price of the model that actually served it, plus a Vynaris fee of 3% on your first $500 of usage in a month and 1% on the rest of that month; a $500 top-up switches to the 1% rate at once for that calendar month.

You fund it with prepaid top-ups from $20 by card or stablecoins; credit does not expire, and requests stop when the balance reaches zero, so there are no overages. Instead of top-ups you can take a fixed plan whose payment becomes API credit: S at $20 a month, L at $50 a month and XL at $150 a month, with unused credit rolling over.

Full pricing notes (2 more paragraphs)

L and XL also give access to Vynaris' own hosted uncensored models, priced per token from $1 per million input tokens, and allow payment in Monero at a 20% surcharge. On 10 November 2026 the pricing page listed four of those hosted “reduced-refusal” models, all with 128K context: Qwen3.6-35B-A3B and Qwen3.8-27B at $1 per million input tokens ($5 and $7 output), DeepSeek-V4-Flash at $2 / $11 and GLM-5.3 at $5 / $20.

The page promises “no prompt or output retention” for them; that is the vendor's statement, which we have not tested. The site labels the service an early beta.

Plans change, so check the live price on their site before you buy.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

The routing fee comes on top of the model price, so if routing rarely picks a cheaper model you pay slightly more than calling the provider directly. Your traffic passes through a third party, which matters for compliance. The hosted uncensored models are aimed at authorised security testing only.

What Vynaris does

OpenAI- and Anthropic-compatible API gateway that routes each request to a cheaper model when evals allow it, with a receipt per request showing the model and cost. Prepaid credit, no overages.

Top-ups first, a plan only for steady use

The fixed plans are prepaid credit, not a discount: S, L and XL add exactly what you pay as credit. Start with a top-up and look at your ledger; a plan only helps if you want a predictable monthly charge. L and XL matter only if you need the hosted uncensored models for authorised security testing. Above $500 a month the routing fee drops to 1% either way.

How to actually use Vynaris

Create an API key on vynaris.com and add credit with a top-up from $20, or pick a fixed plan. In your existing OpenAI or Anthropic client, change the base URL to Vynaris' endpoint, keep your SDK, prompts and tools, and set the model to auto. Send a few of your real requests and read the response headers: each one names the model that served it, the cost and the price you would have paid at list price.

Export the ledger after a day or two and check whether the cheaper models it chose are good enough for your tasks before you move production traffic.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Vynaris cost?

Read on 4 November 2026: the provider list price of the model that served your request, plus a routing fee of 3% on your first $500 of monthly usage and 1% after that. You prepay with top-ups from $20, or take a fixed plan of $20, $50 or $150 a month that becomes API credit.

Can I run up a big bill by accident?

Not according to the pricing page: there are no overages, and requests stop when your available balance reaches zero.

Does unused credit expire?

No. The pricing page says top-up credit does not expire and unused plan credit rolls over.

Do I have to change my code?

Vynaris says you only change the base URL in your OpenAI or Anthropic client and set the model to auto; your SDK, prompts and tool definitions stay the same.

Am I charged when the upstream model fails?

Not according to Vynaris' API documentation (vynaris.com/docs, read 5 November 2026). For a 502 upstream error it says the served model's provider failed or timed out, "You were not charged for a failed upstream call", and that the request is safe to retry. The same page says a 402 out-of-credits response means nothing was sent upstream and nothing was charged. The docs also say that requests naming one of Vynaris' own hosted models explicitly "bypass automatic model selection", so the auto-routing the routing fee is built around does not apply to those calls. Whether this holds on every plan is not stated separately; we have not tested it.
