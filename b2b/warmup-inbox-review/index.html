What Warmup Inbox does

Standalone email warm-up service — automated inbox warming, deliverability monitoring and integrations for Google, Outlook, Zoho and SendGrid.

Pricing (2026)

(2026) Free trial available. At research time the public pricing page listed no prices at all — plan details sit behind a signup or demo request. The model has historically been per-inbox subscription pricing; verify current rates in-app before committing. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

A pricing page without prices: at research time every number sat behind a signup or demo request. For a tool that sells transparency about your inbox health, opaque pricing is an odd look — budgeting before signup is guesswork.

What buyers report about Warmup Inbox

Recurring themes from G2, Capterra, Trustpilot and Software Advice, read in August 2026. We have not run Warmup Inbox ourselves, so this is other buyers' experience rather than ours. We summarise it here because the complaints are usually the part a vendor page leaves out.

What they praise
  • • Connect a Gmail account and warmup starts within ten minutes with no technical knowledge — that ease is what reviewers name first. 4.6 across G2, Trustpilot and Capterra.
  • • A 30,000+ inbox network, and support is consistently called fast and responsive. $15/month annually for a single inbox is fair.
What they complain about
  • • A handful of Trustpilot and G2 reviews flag Google Workspace accounts being suspended after connecting. That is the risk to weigh — your domain is worth more than the tool.
  • • The network leans heavily on Gmail accounts, so Outlook and Microsoft 365 inboxes see slower and less consistent warmup volume.
  • • Cost scales badly: Pro is $49-$59 per inbox, so ten inboxes is $590 a month. It does not fit an agency without a different plan.
  • • Billing complaints, and limited visibility into whether it is actually working.
Best for: outbound teams warming new sending domains and inboxes before campaigns, with integrations across Google, Outlook, Zoho and SendGrid.
Who should skip it: if you want to compare costs before creating an account — the public page will not let you — or your sending volume is too low to need dedicated warm-up.

The natural comparison is lemwarm — lemwarm comes bundled with lemlist outreach seats; Warmup Inbox is the standalone warm-up specialist with broader inbox integrations — but check its in-app pricing first, the public page shows none. Decide by which one fits the job above, not by the louder brand.
