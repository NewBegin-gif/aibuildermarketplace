Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 20 Sep 2026

Billed in USD, every 30 days, and the ladder has three steps rather than the two the app's own summary suggests. Free gives you one textual warning across unlimited products, with the styling, per-product targeting and per-country targeting included. Premium is $6.99 a month or $41.94 a year, and the annual figure really is half of twelve monthly payments. It lifts the cap to unlimited warnings, notes and pop-ups, adds cart and checkout warnings, targeting by product or collection, and US-state geo-targeting on top of country targeting — but the plan is labelled for all stores EXCEPT Shopify Plus.

Plus Plan is $12.99 a month or $77.94 a year, again exactly half of twelve monthly payments, and it is the Shopify Plus plan: the same feature list, plus warnings shown on the Checkout itself and the rule that lets a warning block checkout when configured. Both paid plans carry a 7-day free trial. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

Checkout blocking, and showing a warning on the Checkout page at all, sits on the Plus Plan at $12.99 a month — and that plan says, in the App Store's own words, that it is for Shopify Plus stores. The $6.99 Premium plan says the opposite: for all stores except Shopify Plus. So the two tiers are not a small ladder and a big one, they are two different audiences, and which one you may buy is decided by your Shopify subscription rather than by how much warning you need.

If you are on a standard Shopify plan and came here to stop a checkout, read the plan labels before you install. The second thing to be clear about is what the app does and does not take off your hands. It renders the notice, targets it by product, collection or country, and styles it to your store. It does not tell you what Proposition 65, a GDPR withdrawal notice or an age-verification disclaimer has to say in your jurisdiction, and it does not keep that wording current when the rule changes.

The compliance language stays your problem and your liability; this is the display layer for it.

What Warn does

Shopify app from Effective Apps that adds customizable warnings — Prop 65 labels, shipping-delay alerts, age-verification and withdrawal disclaimers — to product, cart and checkout pages, as text or pop-ups, targeted globally or by product, collection, country or US state. Free covers one textual warning; $6.99 a month lifts that to unlimited warnings for every store except Shopify Plus. Showing a warning on the Checkout page and blocking checkout are Plus Plan features at $12.99 a month, and that plan is for Shopify Plus stores. On the App Store since December 2019.
