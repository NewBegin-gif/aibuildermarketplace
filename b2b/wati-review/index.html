Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 14 Aug 2026

Users are capped per tier: Growth includes 3 and lets you add none at all; Pro and Business include 5, with extra seats at €39 and €89 per user per month. Add-ons are separate again — 1,000 automation triggers €35/mo, 1,000 AI co-pilot credits €25/mo, Shopify $4.99/mo, each extra WhatsApp number €29/mo. Our dollar reading of the same page on 23 July 2026 gave Growth $39, Pro $79 and Business $199 annually, so which currency and which figure you see depends on where Wati places you — check your own checkout before you budget.

Frequently asked questions

What does an extra user on Wati cost?

It depends which tier, and Growth is the trap: it includes 3 users and sells no fourth at any price, so a fourth person forces the jump to Pro. On the dollar page we captured in July and August 2026 the two extra-seat lines read $24 and $69 per user per month; on the euro page we were served on 14 August 2026 they read €39 and €89. Pro is the cheaper seat, Business the expensive one.

Am I being quoted in the right currency?

Worth checking before you budget. Our July and August 2026 captures of wati.io/pricing came back in dollars; on 14 August 2026 the same URL served us euros — Growth €59, Pro €119, Business €279 a month on annual billing — while the Shopify add-on stayed at $4.99. We cannot see from here what drives that, so take the figure from your own checkout, in your own currency, rather than from anyone's summary including ours.

Does paying Wati more reduce the per-message cost?

No. Meta's per-conversation fees are charged the same on every tier and Wati bills them through on its own rate card, so the metered half of the bill does not shrink when you upgrade. Upgrading buys seats, automation triggers, AI credits and extra WhatsApp numbers — and those are separately metered too.

What should I weigh before buying Communication & Voice software?

Generally yes, since this category is built for it, but the details that bite are timezone handling, how per-seat cost scales as the team spreads, and whether numbers, routing or coverage exist in the countries your people actually sit in. Availability varies far more by country than the marketing pages suggest, so check your specific regions before committing.
