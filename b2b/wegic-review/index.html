What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

The cheaper per-credit rate only kicks in on the higher annual tier, and premium AI media draws down a second 'Cloud' balance on top of your plan.

What Wegic does

AI website builder that generates and hosts sites from a conversation — credit-based, with custom domains and code export on higher tiers, and built-in image generation powered by ChatGPT Image-2 (upload a reference or prompt, drop the result straight into your design). It can also turn a single product photo or mockup into a ready-to-sell product page. A one-click optimize pass audits a published site for SEO and site-health problems and applies the fixes itself — vendor-announced in July 2026, and not something we have tested.

What buyers report about Wegic

Recurring themes from the buyer-review sites listed at the foot of this page, read in August 2026. We have not run Wegic ourselves, so this is other buyers' experience rather than ours. We summarise it here because the complaints are usually the part a vendor page leaves out.

What they praise
  • • Building a site by conversation genuinely works for someone who has never made one — clean interface, fast setup, and 2.5M people have used it.
  • • There is a free plan to see whether the approach suits you at all.
What they complain about
  • • There is no free trial of the paid plans and no refund policy — reviewers report being unable to get money back even after 15 minutes. Test everything you can on the free tier first.
  • • Credits are consumed even when an edit fails. If you revise constantly, the headline $11.90 or $19.90 a month is not what you will pay.
  • • Slow performance and crashes are reported.
  • • No code export and limited control — you cannot take the site elsewhere.

When the paid plan is worth it

There is no free build tier. New users get a small amount of free credits, but Wegic's documentation describes those as credits to chat with the AI before generating your website; the first generation itself runs through a $2.99 trial package that covers one full website generation. Starter is the real floor: $39.90/month, or $286.80/year ($23.90/month), for 600 credits a month, publishing to wegic.net and removal of Wegic branding. The pricing page rates 600 credits at up to two simple sites or one complex one — well under the roughly 15 websites Wegic's own blog once claimed for the same allowance.

Premium — $69.90/month, or $502.80/year ($41.90/month) — buys the thing most businesses need: a custom domain. Its allowance is 2,000 credits a month, which the pricing page now states outright — settling an old contradiction with a Wegic blog page that said unlimited. Two constraints to price in: an upgrade "cannot be undone or downgraded", and the 14-day money-back guarantee covers only your first annual subscription, excludes third-party fees, taxes and any domain you bought, and shrinks in proportion to the AI generations you already ran.

How to actually use Wegic

Create an account at wegic.ai, open the Workspace and select [+Create Website]. Describe the site you want to Wegic's AI assistant, Kimmy, or upload a sketch — hand-drawn, photographed or digital — which it converts into a working site. The AI then produces a draft you refine with plain-language instructions such as "change the button color to blue" or "put About Us first". Budget for that: each AI-generated edit uses credits, and the amount varies with the size and complexity of the request.

Product Hunt reviewers report credits being consumed even when the AI does not make the change they asked for, so keep instructions specific. Publishing to a your-business-name.wegic.net address is a Starter feature, not a free one; a custom domain requires Premium and needs an A record plus a www CNAME, where DNS propagation can take up to 48 hours.
