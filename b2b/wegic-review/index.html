What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

The cheaper per-credit rate only kicks in on the higher annual tier, and premium AI media draws down a second 'Cloud' balance on top of your plan.

What Wegic does

AI website builder that generates and hosts sites from a conversation — credit-based, with custom domains and code export on higher tiers. Wegic's own help center documents images only by upload or external URL; the built-in image generation the vendor promotes, including a product-photo-to-product-page pass, is not in that documentation and we have not tested it. A one-click optimize pass audits a published site for SEO and site-health problems and applies the fixes itself — vendor-announced in July 2026, and not something we have tested.

When the paid plan is worth it

There is no free build tier. New users get a small amount of free credits, but Wegic's documentation describes those as credits to chat with the AI before generating your website; the first generation itself runs through a $2.99 trial package that covers one full website generation. Starter is the real floor: $39.90/month, or $286.80/year ($23.90/month), for 600 credits a month, publishing to wegic.net and removal of Wegic branding. The pricing page rates 600 credits at up to two simple sites or one complex one — well under the roughly 15 websites Wegic's own blog once claimed for the same allowance.

Premium — $69.90/month, or $502.80/year ($41.90/month) — buys the thing most businesses need: a custom domain. Its allowance is 2,000 credits a month, which the pricing page now states outright — settling an old contradiction with a Wegic blog page that said unlimited. Two constraints to price in: Wegic's help centre says an upgrade "cannot be undone or downgraded", and as of 19 September 2026 there is no money-back guarantee left to fall back on — the pricing FAQ calls plans, renewals and the $2.99 trial pack non-refundable, and credits you have already spent are never restored.

How to actually use Wegic

Create an account at wegic.ai, open the Workspace and select [+Create Website]. Describe the site you want to Wegic's AI assistant, Kimmy, or upload a sketch — hand-drawn, photographed or digital — which it converts into a working site. The AI then produces a draft you refine with plain-language instructions such as "change the button color to blue" or "put About Us first". Budget for that: each AI-generated edit uses credits, and the amount varies with the size and complexity of the request.

Product Hunt reviewers report credits being consumed even when the AI does not make the change they asked for, so keep instructions specific. Publishing to a your-business-name.wegic.net address is a Starter feature, not a free one; a custom domain requires Premium and needs an A record plus a www CNAME, where DNS propagation can take up to 48 hours.

Frequently asked questions

Can I build a site on the free credits?

No. Wegic gives a new account 100 credits at signup, and its own FAQ, read 19 September 2026, is explicit about what they are for: credits to chat with the AI and describe what you want, while website generation is a paid feature. The pack that turns that conversation into a site is $2.99, and the card for it carries Wegic's own warning that most generates end up costing over $10 in credits, with no edit credits included. So the free tier demonstrates the conversation, not the product. Read the paid allowances the same way: Wegic rates 600 credits a month (Starter) at up to two simple sites or one complex one, and 2,000 (Premium) at up to four or two. Editing draws on the same balance, so a site you keep iterating on is a site you pay for more than once.

Do unused credits carry over to next month?

They do not. Wegic's FAQ, read 19 September 2026: at the end of each monthly billing cycle all remaining credits are cleared, and a fresh allowance is issued when the plan renews. Put that beside the refund answer on the same page — plans, renewals and the $2.99 trial pack are non-refundable, credits cannot be exchanged for cash, and credits spent on AI features count as consumed — and the money is spend-it-or-lose-it in both directions. The practical move is to batch the work. A quiet month on Starter costs $39.90 for 600 credits that evaporate, so if the site is finished and you only touch it occasionally, compare that honestly against the $286.80 annual rate and against running no subscription at all between rounds of changes.

What happens to my site if I stop paying?

It does not vanish at once, but it does change. Wegic's FAQ, read 19 September 2026, lists four consequences when a subscription ends: published sites stay live for up to 7 more days, the Wegic badge reappears on them, you receive no further credits, and a site on your own custom domain is unpublished — to get it back you log in and republish on a Wegic address. The page is not consistent about which address that is: the plan cards promise a permanent .wegic.net domain for the life of the subscription, while the FAQ says to republish using a Wegic.app domain. Either way the custom domain is the first thing you lose, and it is only on Premium. If the site matters to the business, take the code export — a Starter feature — before that week runs out.

What should I weigh before buying Design & Content software?

Up to a point, and that point is usually set by your plan. Check whether brand kits, custom fonts, colour locking and template controls sit on the tier you would actually buy, since these are common upsells. Test it with your own assets during the trial rather than the demo content, which is always chosen to flatter the output.
