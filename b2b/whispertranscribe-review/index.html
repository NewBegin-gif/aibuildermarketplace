Two products. Transcription plans, priced by minutes of uploaded audio a month, with unlimited users: Starter $19.99 a month billed annually ($240 a year) or $39.99 monthly for 320 minutes; Pro $29.99 annually ($360) or $59.99 monthly for 800 minutes; Grow $69.99 ($840) or $139.99 for 2,500 minutes; Scale $139.99 ($1,680) or $279.99 for 6,000 minutes. The annual toggle is labelled save 50%.

Each plan includes one free Meeting Assistant seat, files up to 10 hours long (5 GB on Starter, 10 GB above), AI clips, podcast search, import from web links, exports to Word, PDF, TXT, SRT, VTT, CSV, XLSX, JSON and ELAN, and priority support on Grow and Scale. Pay-as-you-go minutes start at $8 an hour, bought in the app, and never expire.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

Monthly billing costs twice the annual rate on every plan, and fees and unused credits are non-refundable under the terms. Transcripts are not kept on the vendor's servers after delivery, which is good for privacy but means a lost local copy is gone. Some AI processing runs in the United States. The pricing page says translation into 99 languages while its comparison table says 65+. The terms of 20 October 2026 add a 14-day withdrawal right for EU, EEA and UK consumers on subscriptions, refunded minus the service already used; buying a credit package gives up that right once the credits are in your account.

Subscription minutes expire every cycle, and the terms cap liability at the fees paid in the previous 12 months, or €0 for a user who paid nothing.

What WhisperTranscribe does

AI transcription and translation for podcasts, videos and interviews with speaker detection, content generation and many export formats, plus a bot-free meeting assistant; plans by minutes a month with unlimited users.

Buy minutes, then check the per-hour line

The four transcription plans are minute buckets, and the per-hour rate falls as the bucket grows: on annual billing Pro works out at $2.25 an hour, Grow at $1.68 and Scale at $1.40, matching the figures on the cards. Starter is the exception. Its card says $2.80 an hour, but $19.99 for 320 minutes is about $3.75 an hour, our arithmetic, so the smallest plan is dearer per hour than it looks.

Monthly billing doubles every price, which the save 50% badge states honestly. If your volume is irregular, the pay-as-you-go hours from $8 never expire and may beat a monthly plan. Users are free on transcription plans, so a team pays for minutes, not seats. The Meeting Assistant is the opposite: $16 a seat a month on annual billing, and each transcription plan includes only one seat. Minutes do not carry over.

The terms of 20 October 2026 say subscription credits reset at the start of each billing cycle, do not roll over and expire at the end of each period, and that processing stops when the limit is reached. Upgrades are charged pro rata at once; downgrades wait for the end of the cycle. So size the plan to a normal busy month and keep pay-as-you-go hours, which never expire, as the buffer for the odd heavy month, instead of buying a bigger bucket that sits half unused.

Price changes need at least 30 days' notice by email and apply from the next billing cycle, so a yearly plan is protected for its term. New subscriptions are billed through Stripe; subscriptions from before the move to Stripe stay on LemonSqueezy, which matters when you look for an invoice or a cancel button.

How to actually use WhisperTranscribe

Sign up without a card and transcribe one real file, ideally a recording with several speakers and the jargon of your field, then check the speaker labels and add problem words to the dictionary that reviewers mention. Save your transcripts. The terms describe a zero-retention design: uploaded audio is deleted within minutes after processing and transcripts are not kept on WhisperTranscribe's servers after delivery, so the copy on your device is the copy.

Export to the format you need, from SRT and VTT for subtitles to Word or JSON, as part of the routine. Pick a plan by counting a normal month's minutes, then compare the per-hour cost with pay-as-you-go at $8 an hour. Remember that processing runs partly on AI services in the United States, according to the terms, if that matters for your recordings. WhisperTranscribe is a desktop app for Windows and Mac (download page, read 25 October 2026), and the transcripts live in that app on that computer.

The terms say transcripts, AI outputs and chat history are stored only on your device, so a colleague or a second computer gets nothing unless you export it. If your recordings contain personal data, decide on processing before the first upload. Standard processing transcribes in the EU but sends AI features such as summaries and chat to Google Cloud Vertex AI's global endpoint, which may process them in the United States, with the Gemini API and Mistral AI as fallbacks.

EU processing, with Vertex AI EU endpoints and Mistral AI as fallback, is available on request by email to support. For business users the terms include processor clauses; an audit beyond what the law requires is at your cost, with 30 days' written notice. For recordings that may not leave your own systems at all, the custom-solutions page offers custom templates and processing on your own infrastructure, with no published price.

Frequently asked questions

How much does WhisperTranscribe cost?

Read 14 October 2026: Starter $19.99 a month billed annually ($240 a year) or $39.99 monthly for 320 minutes; Pro $29.99 or $59.99 for 800 minutes; Grow $69.99 or $139.99 for 2,500 minutes; Scale $139.99 or $279.99 for 6,000 minutes. Pay-as-you-go starts at $8 an hour. The Meeting Assistant is $16 a seat a month yearly or $20 monthly.

Does WhisperTranscribe keep my recordings?

Not by design. The terms of 20 October 2026 say uploaded audio and video are deleted 3 to 10 minutes after processing and that transcripts, AI outputs and chat history are stored only on your device, so save them yourself. Providers may keep short-term caches, generally deleted within 24 hours, and abuse-monitoring logs for up to about 60 days. Personal data linked to a deleted account goes within 24 hours. Your content is not used to train AI models.

Can I get a refund from WhisperTranscribe?

Usually not, with one exception. The terms of 20 October 2026 give consumers in the EU, EEA and UK 14 days to withdraw from a subscription, refunded minus a proportionate amount for the service and credits already used; buying a credit package waives that right once the credits appear. Otherwise fees and unused credits are non-refundable, except at the company's discretion for service failure, billing errors or duplicate charges reported within 30 days. After cancelling, the account stays on the free tier.

Do unused WhisperTranscribe minutes roll over?

No. The terms of 20 October 2026 say subscription credits reset at the start of each billing cycle, do not roll over and expire at the end of each billing period, and that processing stops at the plan limit. You can then upgrade, buy extra credits or wait for the next cycle. Pay-as-you-go hours, from $8 an hour, are the exception: the pricing page says they never expire.

Can all processing stay inside the EU?

On request, yes. Under standard processing transcription runs in the EU (Frankfurt servers, AssemblyAI or Soniox), but AI features go to Google Cloud Vertex AI's global endpoint and may be processed in the United States. The terms of 20 October 2026 offer EU processing, with all transcription and AI in the EU (Vertex AI EU endpoints, Mistral AI as fallback), if you email support@whispertranscribe.com. The privacy policy of 19 October 2026 lists the providers and their locations.

Who bills a WhisperTranscribe subscription?

Stripe, for subscriptions created now. The terms of 20 October 2026 say subscriptions created before the migration to Stripe continue to be billed through LemonSqueezy. Fees are prepaid, exclusive of VAT or sales tax, and price changes need at least 30 days' notice by email. The contracting company is WhisperTranscribe B.V. in Rotterdam.
