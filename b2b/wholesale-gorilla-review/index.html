Wholesale Gorilla Review (2026)

E-commerceResearched assessment · by Daniel HaketUpdated 2026-09-08

Wholesale Gorilla is a feature ladder, not a volume ladder, and the features most people picture when they say wholesale sit high on it.

Best for: Shopify merchants who already have wholesale buyers and want them ordering from the same store as retail customers — custom price groups, a signup and approval flow, net terms and wholesale-only shipping rules — without moving up to Shopify Plus for native B2B..
Who should skip it: if you need one flat wholesale percentage and nothing else, which customer tags and a far cheaper app already cover; you are on Shopify Plus, where native B2B gives you price lists and payment terms without a second subscription; or you want volume-break pricing on a small budget, because that single feature costs $149.95 a month here..

Three tiers, all billed in USD every 30 days: Lite $34.95 a month, Advanced $69.95 and Premium $149.95. There is no free plan. Every tier carries a 21-day free trial and nothing beyond it, so budget from day 22. Nothing here is metered — pricing rules and customer price groups are unlimited on all three tiers — so what you are buying as you climb is features, not volume.

The app has been on the Shopify App Store since 26 September 2018 and shows 4.7 stars over 313 reviews. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

The honest knock

Volume discounts — buy ten, pay less each — are Premium only, at $149.95 a month, more than four times the entry price. Discount codes, net payment terms, wholesale shipping rules and theme integration all start at Advanced, $69.95. Lite at $34.95 buys custom prices per customer group and a basic signup form, which is a price list rather than a wholesale channel. Work out which rung you actually need before you read $34.95 as your price, because the honest entry point for most wholesale operations is the middle one.

What Wholesale Gorilla does

B2B wholesale inside an ordinary Shopify store: custom prices per customer group, a signup and approval flow, net payment terms, wholesale-only shipping rules and a quick-order form, so trade buyers order from the same storefront as retail customers. Nothing is metered — the three tiers gate features, not volume. Lite $34.95 a month, Advanced $69.95, Premium $149.95, no free plan and a 21-day trial. Read on the App Store and the vendor's own comparison chart on 8 September 2026.

The natural comparison is Shopify Plus native B2B — Shopify's own B2B is included once you are on Plus, and it puts company accounts, price lists and payment terms in the platform itself rather than in an app that sits on top of your theme. Wholesale Gorilla exists for the shops below that line: it brings the same shape of wholesale to a standard Shopify plan for $34.95 to $149.95 a month, which is a fraction of the step up to Plus. The trade is where the logic lives. Native B2B cannot break when a theme updates; an app that integrates with your theme can, and the recurring complaint in this app's own reviews is exactly that — updates and theme changes that interrupt checkout. If you are already on Plus, paying again for this is hard to justify..
