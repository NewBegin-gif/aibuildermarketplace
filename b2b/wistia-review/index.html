Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 19 Sep 2026

Four tiers, and the headline numbers are the ANNUAL rates: the billing toggle on the page is set to Annual with a “Save 20%” chip, so Business at $79 a month and Business + Lead gen at $329 a month are what you pay when you commit for a year. Switch that toggle to Monthly and the same three cards read $0, $99 and $412 a month, with the per-extra-user rate moving from $25 to $31 — so the 20% the chip promises is real, and paying month to month costs about a fifth more.

Those monthly figures are rendered by the page rather than written in its source, which is why they can only be read with a browser. Free $0: 25 GB storage, 1 user, 200 GB bandwidth, basic analytics, Wistia branding on the player. Business $79: 250 GB+ storage, 3 users, 1 TB bandwidth, branding removed, collections and playlists, in-video CTAs and forms, advanced analytics.

Business + Lead gen $329 is Business plus the Automation Suite — marketing-platform connection (HubSpot, Adobe Marketo Engage, Pardot), lead capture and scoring, pipeline measurement — and the FAQ describes that suite as something you add to a Business plan rather than a separate product. Enterprise is quote-only: from 1 TB storage, 2 TB bandwidth, custom users, unlimited view-only access, team accounts, SSO, video playback protection, a dedicated customer success manager.

Price per additional user $25. Three rows in the feature table carry a “$” instead of a tick in every column, Enterprise included: Webinars, AI audio descriptions, and video dubbing with lip syncing. The overage rules sit in the FAQ rather than on the cards. Bandwidth is included with every plan and resets every month; free accounts cannot exceed their limit, while paid plans are billed for usage beyond what is included or prepaid.

Storage works the same way and can be bought upfront to avoid usage charges. Users are billed for usage beyond the seats the plan includes, as you add and remove them. Webinar and live-event allowances are per event and not cumulative across events; additional attendees are charged at $0.20 each and additional duration minutes at $2.00 each, billed on the next monthly billing date.

You can cancel at any time unless you are on Enterprise or a signed order form, either at the end of the billing cycle or immediately, and no refund is issued either way. Webinar plans have their own tab on the same page and carry no price in the source. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

When the paid plan is worth it

Business is what removes both — 250 GB+, three users included, branding off, collections and playlists, CTAs and forms, advanced analytics. That is the upgrade most people are actually buying. Check what the $79 is before you budget it. The pricing page's toggle is set to Annual with a “Save 20%” chip, so that figure is a per-month rate for a yearly commitment.

Switch the toggle to Monthly and Business is $99 and Business + Lead gen $412, with extra users at $31 instead of $25 — so a monthly trial is a known $20 more per month, not an unknown. What $79 does not settle is everything the table marks separately. Additional users are $25 each beyond the three, so a six-person team is $154 before anything else. How to actually use Wistia

Upload, then embed, then read the analytics — the order matters less than knowing embedding is where the choices are. Their help centre walks the embed flow: open a video's manage page, click Embed, pick a style and options, then Copy Code and paste it into your site. The recommended default for most platforms is an inline embed with responsive sizing, and inline is also what they recommend for accessibility and video SEO because the player is prominent on the page.

Popover is the alternative, showing a thumbnail that opens a player over the page, launchable from an image, link or button. Channels embeds a whole gallery and can carry a podcast. One thing that saves rework later: customisations made in Wistia propagate to embeds already live, so the player settings are not baked into the code you pasted.

Frequently asked questions

Is the $79 Business price monthly or annual?

Annual, and the page does not say so in words. Read on wistia.com/pricing, the billing toggle is set to Annual and carries a “Save 20%” chip, so $79 a month for Business and $329 a month for Business + Lead gen are the rates for a yearly commitment. Flip the toggle to Monthly — which takes a browser, because the figures are rendered rather than written into the page — and the same cards read $99 and $412 a month, with additional users going from $25 to $31. Read that way on 19 September 2026. So month-to-month costs about a fifth more, exactly as the chip promises, and starting monthly to test a bandwidth-metered product costs you $20 a month on Business rather than an unknown.

What gets billed on top of the plan?

Four things, and three of them are usage. Additional users are $25 each beyond the three Business includes, so a six-person team is $154 a month before anything else. Bandwidth resets monthly and paid plans are billed for usage past what the plan includes or what you have prepaid — 1 TB on Business, 200 GB on Free, and Free is hard-capped instead of billed. Storage behaves identically and can be bought upfront to avoid the usage charge. Webinars are the sharpest: the attendee and duration allowances are per event rather than cumulative, and past them it is $0.20 per extra attendee and $2.00 per extra minute, billed on your next monthly date. Webinars, AI audio descriptions and video dubbing also carry a “$” in every column of the feature table, Enterprise included, so no tier includes them outright.

Do I need the $329 plan to get the lead-generation features?

Not necessarily, and the FAQ is clearer about this than the pricing cards. Business + Lead gen at $329 is Business plus what Wistia calls the Automation Suite: connecting video engagement to HubSpot, Adobe Marketo Engage or Pardot, capturing and scoring leads, and measuring pipeline impact. The FAQ describes that suite as something that “can be added to your Business plan”, and says it comes included with the webinar product without being added separately. So the honest way to read the ladder is one core plan with an add-on, not four tiers. In-video CTAs and forms are already on Business, so if all you need is lead capture inside the player, $79 covers it; the $250 step is for routing that data into a marketing automation platform.

Can I cancel, and do I get money back?

You can cancel at any time unless you are on an Enterprise plan or have signed an order form, and you choose between stopping at the end of the billing cycle or stopping immediately. Either way, their FAQ states plainly that no refund is issued. Combined with the annual-by-default pricing that is worth planning around: a year committed at $79 a month is a year paid whatever happens in month three. Upgrades and add-ons can be applied later and are managed as subscriptions in Billing Settings, though on Enterprise some changes need a customer success manager. The free plan needs no credit card and keeps 25 GB indefinitely, so the low-risk order is to prove the workflow there, then buy the shortest term you can negotiate.

What should I weigh before buying Video & Audio software?

Judge it on evidence rather than adjectives. Look for a published security page, single sign-on and role controls on the plan you would actually buy (they are often gated to higher tiers), a status page with real incident history, and a clear answer on where your data is stored. If the tool will hold anything sensitive, those four answers matter more than any feature comparison.
