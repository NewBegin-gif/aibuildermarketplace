Wistia Review (2026)Free to 25 GB, Then $79/mo

By Daan Haket · Tested hands-on · written with an AI assistant · who does what

Bandwidth is the meter that decides your bill, and it is the one number you cannot predict before you publish.

Best for

marketing teams who want video on their own site with real analytics and lead capture, and who care that the player does not recommend a competitor's video afterwards.

Skip it if

you just need somewhere to put videos — an unlisted upload to a free platform does that, and the reason to pay here is the analytics and the branding control, not the hosting.

Four tiers, and the headline numbers are the ANNUAL rates: the billing toggle on the page is set to Annual with a “Save 20%” chip, so Business at $79 a month and Business + Signals at $329 a month are what you pay when you commit for a year. On 19 September 2026, with the toggle set to Monthly, the same three cards read $0, $99 and $412 a month, with the per-extra-user rate moving from $25 to $31.

Since that reading Wistia has renamed the $329 tier from Business + Lead gen to Business + Signals and added an AI credit allowance to every card; the prices, storage, users and bandwidth did not change. Free $0: 25 GB storage, 1 user, 200 GB bandwidth, 3,000+ lifetime credits, basic analytics, Wistia branding on the player. Business $79: 250 GB+ storage, 3+ users, 1 TB bandwidth, 5,000+ monthly credits, branding removed, collections and playlists, CTAs and forms, advanced analytics.

Full pricing notes (3 more paragraphs)

Business + Signals $329 is Business plus Signals — marketing-automation integrations (HubSpot, Marketo, Pardot), video engagement data on contact records, automated lead scoring, audience segmentation, triggered email nurtures, sales alerts and pipeline and revenue attribution — and the FAQ calls Signals an add-on for the Business plan that is included with every webinar plan. Enterprise is quote-only: from 1 TB storage, 2 TB bandwidth, custom users, 12,500+ monthly credits, Signals, team accounts, SSO, video playback protection, a dedicated customer success manager.

Price per additional user $25. Dubbing and AI audio descriptions are an add-on on both Business cards and included only on Enterprise. The page does not say what a credit buys. Storage beyond the plan is prepaid upfront or billed as usage. The FAQ as read on 19 September 2026 also said bandwidth resets monthly and is billed as usage past the allowance on paid plans (free accounts are hard-capped instead), and priced webinar overages at $0.20 per extra attendee and $2.00 per extra minute, per event; the shortened FAQ of 19 October 2026 repeats neither, so treat both as the last confirmed reading rather than current page text.

You can cancel at any time unless you are on Enterprise or a signed order form, either at the end of the billing cycle or immediately, and no refund is issued either way. Webinar plans have their own tab on the same page and carry no price in the source.

Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

A single video that does well can move you from Free to Business in a month, which is arguably the product working — but budget for it rather than being surprised. Their FAQ, as read on 19 September 2026, was precise about which way it cuts: free accounts cannot exceed their bandwidth limit, while paid plans are billed for usage beyond what is included or prepaid. The free tier carries Wistia branding on the player, so it is a trial with no time limit rather than a plan you can ship a brand on.

The published prices are the annual ones; the page's toggle sits on Annual with a 20% saving and the month-to-month figures are not in its source, so a monthly subscriber cannot price the plan from the pricing page. The webinar overages were the sharpest edge on that reading: $2.00 per extra minute means a session that runs long costs real money, the allowance is per event rather than cumulative, and the shortened FAQ of 19 October 2026 no longer prints either figure, so confirm them before you book a long session.

What they complain about

When the paid plan is worth it

Free is a real workspace with two hard edges: 25 GB, one user only, and Wistia branding on the player. Business is what removes both — 250 GB+, three users included, branding off, collections and playlists, CTAs and forms, advanced analytics. That is the upgrade most people are actually buying. Check what the $79 is before you budget it. The pricing page's toggle is set to Annual with a “Save 20%” chip, so that figure is a per-month rate for a yearly commitment.

Switched to Monthly on 19 September 2026, Business read $99 and what is now Business + Signals $412, with extra users at $31 instead of $25 — so a monthly trial is a known $20 more per month, not an unknown. What $79 does not settle is everything the table marks separately. Additional users are $25 each beyond the three, so a six-person team is $154 before anything else. SSO, video playback protection, team accounts and a dedicated customer success manager sit only on Enterprise, which is quote-only.

AI audio descriptions and video dubbing are an add-on on Business and included only on Enterprise, and webinars are priced on a separate tab. And bandwidth is the meter behind all of it: 1 TB on Business, billed as usage once you pass it according to the FAQ as read on 19 September 2026, and the number you cannot forecast before the video is published.

How to actually use Wistia

Upload, then embed, then read the analytics — the order matters less than knowing embedding is where the choices are. Their help centre walks the embed flow: open a video's manage page, click Embed, pick a style and options, then Copy Code and paste it into your site. The recommended default for most platforms is an inline embed with responsive sizing, and inline is also what they recommend for accessibility and video SEO because the player is prominent on the page.

Popover is the alternative, showing a thumbnail that opens a player over the page, launchable from an image, link or button. Channels embeds a whole gallery and can carry a podcast. One thing that saves rework later: customisations made in Wistia propagate to embeds already live, so the player settings are not baked into the code you pasted.

Is the $79 Business price monthly or annual?

Annual, and the page does not say so in words. Read on wistia.com/pricing, the billing toggle is set to Annual and carries a “Save 20%” chip, so $79 a month for Business and $329 a month for Business + Signals are the rates for a yearly commitment. Flip the toggle to Monthly — which takes a browser, because the figures are rendered rather than written into the page — and the same cards read $99 and $412 a month, with additional users going from $25 to $31. Read that way on 19 September 2026. So month-to-month costs about a fifth more, exactly as the chip promises, and starting monthly to test a bandwidth-metered product costs you $20 a month on Business rather than an unknown.

What gets billed on top of the plan?

Four things, and three of them are usage. Additional users are $25 each beyond the three Business includes, so a six-person team is $154 a month before anything else. Per the pricing FAQ as read on 19 September 2026 (the shortened FAQ of 19 October no longer repeats it), bandwidth resets monthly and paid plans are billed for usage past what the plan includes or what you have prepaid — 1 TB on Business, 200 GB on Free, and Free is hard-capped instead of billed. Storage behaves identically and can be bought upfront to avoid the usage charge. Webinars were the sharpest on that same reading: the attendee and duration allowances were per event rather than cumulative, and past them it was $0.20 per extra attendee and $2.00 per extra minute, billed on your next monthly date. AI audio descriptions and video dubbing are an add-on on both Business cards and included only on Enterprise, per the feature table read 19 October 2026.

Do I need the $329 plan to get the lead-generation features?

Not necessarily, and the FAQ is clearer about this than the pricing cards. Business + Signals at $329 (it was called Business + Lead gen until a rename we first saw on 19 October 2026) is Business plus Signals: marketing-automation integrations with HubSpot, Marketo or Pardot, engagement data on contact records, automated lead scoring, audience segmentation, triggered nurtures, sales alerts and pipeline attribution. The FAQ, read 19 October 2026, calls Signals an add-on for the Business plan and says it is included with every webinar plan. So the honest way to read the ladder is one core plan with an add-on, not four tiers. CTAs and forms are already on Business, so if all you need is lead capture inside the player, $79 covers it; the $250 step is for routing that data into a marketing automation platform.

Can I cancel, and do I get money back?

You can cancel at any time unless you are on an Enterprise plan or have signed an order form, and you choose between stopping at the end of the billing cycle or stopping immediately. Either way, their FAQ states plainly that no refund is issued. Combined with the annual-by-default pricing that is worth planning around: a year committed at $79 a month is a year paid whatever happens in month three. Upgrades and add-ons can be applied later and are managed as subscriptions in Billing Settings, though on Enterprise some changes need a customer success manager. The free plan needs no credit card and keeps 25 GB indefinitely, so the low-risk order is to prove the workflow there, then buy the shortest term you can negotiate.

Who counts as a paid seat, and what happens to a leaver's videos?

Wistia's User Roles article, read 19 October 2026, says Viewer seats are free and do not count towards account seat limits, and that Viewer seats are not available on the Free plan. A Viewer can watch, browse and comment on what has been shared with them, but cannot upload, record, edit, embed, open the analytics dashboards or use integrations — so on Business the $25 per extra user applies to the people who make and measure video, not to colleagues who only watch. The Manage Team article, read the same day, says deleting a user does not delete any content they uploaded and that it keeps playing, so removing a leaver to free a paid seat does not take their videos with them.
