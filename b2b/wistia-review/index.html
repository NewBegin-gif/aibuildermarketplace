What Wistia does

Video hosting built for marketing — player customisation, viewer analytics and lead capture.

Pricing (2026)

(2026-08-01, read on their own pricing page) Free $0: 25 GB storage, 1 user, 200 GB bandwidth, and Wistia branding on the player. Business $79/month: 250 GB storage, 3 users, 1 TB bandwidth. Business + Lead Gen $329/month. Enterprise quote-only: 1 TB+ storage, 2 TB bandwidth. Extra users $25 each. Two overages sit in the FAQ rather than on the price card: webinar attendees beyond the allowance cost $0.20 each and extra webinar minutes $2.00 each. Some AI features (video dubbing, audio descriptions) are marked as costing extra without a price. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

Bandwidth is the meter that decides your bill, and it is the one number you cannot predict before you publish. A single video that does well can move you from Free to Business in a month, which is arguably the product working — but budget for it rather than being surprised. The free tier carries Wistia branding on the player, so it is a trial with no time limit rather than a plan you can ship a brand on. The webinar overages are the sharpest edge: $2.00 per extra minute means a session that runs long costs real money, and that rate is in the FAQ, not on the pricing page.

What buyers report about Wistia

Recurring themes from GetApp, Capterra and pricing analyses, read in August 2026. We have not run Wistia ourselves, so this is other buyers' experience rather than ours. We summarise it here because the complaints are usually the part a vendor page leaves out.

What they praise
  • • Video hosting built for marketers rather than viewers — player customisation, lead capture and analytics that tie back to the funnel.
  • • Business at $79/month annually includes 3 users, 250 GB storage and 1 TB bandwidth.
What they complain about
  • • Exceed your storage and Wistia adds a block automatically to cover what you used — and the charge appears at the end of the billing cycle, not when you upload. Teams upload freely all month and find out on the invoice.
  • • There is no published per-GB overage rate for bandwidth. High-traffic sites cannot model the cost; you find out via an upgrade conversation.
  • • Extra Business users are $25/month each.
  • • Reviewers report steep jumps as usage grows, and integration charges reducing transparency.
Best for: marketing teams who want video on their own site with real analytics and lead capture, and who care that the player does not recommend a competitor's video afterwards.
Who should skip it: if you just need somewhere to put videos — an unlisted upload to a free platform does that, and the reason to pay here is the analytics and the branding control, not the hosting.

The natural comparison is Vidyard or Loom — the closest marketing-video peers — compare on bandwidth included and what the free tier puts on your player. Decide by which one fits the job above, not by the louder brand.

Frequently asked questions

Is Wistia secure and reliable enough to depend on?

Judge it on evidence rather than adjectives. Look for a published security page, single sign-on and role controls on the plan you would actually buy (they are often gated to higher tiers), a status page with real incident history, and a clear answer on where your data is stored. If the tool will hold anything sensitive, those four answers matter more than any feature comparison.

Who should not use Wistia?

Skip it if you just need somewhere to put videos — an unlisted upload to a free platform does that, and the reason to pay here is the analytics and the branding control, not the hosting. Buying a tool to fix a problem you don't have yet just adds cost and another login to manage.
