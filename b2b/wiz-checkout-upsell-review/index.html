What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

That tier is post-purchase, thank-you page and order status page only, and the listing explicitly marks it as not applicable for Shopify Plus. Everything the app is named after — checkout upsells, checkout banners and trust badges, input fields, gift messages, free gifts at checkout — starts on the $99 Plus Growth plan. There is no free plan either, only a 14-day trial; as of 19 September that tier carries a BFCM offer of $1 for the first month, undated, so today the cheapest honest look at checkout upsells is a dollar and after this promotion it is $99 a month again.

And A/B testing across all extensions, the feature you would want precisely to prove that $99 was worth it, sits one rung higher again at $199 — even though the listing advertises built-in A/B testing in its own highlights.

When the paid plan is worth it

The only step that changes what the app can do is Essential to Plus Growth, and it takes the bill from $49 to $99. That $50 buys the checkout itself — upsells, banners, trust badges, input fields, gift messages, a discount list and free gifts. It also matters on Shopify Plus, because the listing marks Essential as not applicable there, so on Plus this is the rung where the ladder starts.

Commit to a year at that tier and you pay $999 instead of $1,188, a saving of $189. Concierge at $199 is where you stop buying software: custom onboarding, monthly performance review calls and custom extension development are people, and the one product line beside them is A/B testing and analysis across all extensions. What no upgrade fixes is the evaluation window. Every tier gets the same 14 days and there is no free plan underneath, so the length of the trial rather than the price decides how much you can prove before the first invoice.

How to actually use Wiz Checkout Upsell

Check compatibility before you plan the work. The listing opens with a notice that the app is only compatible with stores meeting certain criteria, and it lists Checkout Extensibility and Functions under Works with — so confirm your own checkout is on that footing before you budget for the tier. Open the demo store the listing offers before you install anything; seeing the blocks in position reads faster than the feature list.

Then spend the 14 days on one question. Switch on a single block — one checkout upsell, one trust badge or one free-gift rule — and leave the rest off, because a checkout wearing six new elements cannot tell you which one moved the number. Skai Lama keeps a Checkout Wiz category in its help centre; the listing separately links a video tutorial playlist. If Klaviyo is in your stack, connect it at the start: it is on the listing's integration list, and retro-fitting the events is worse than wiring them once.

Frequently asked questions

What is the $1 first month on the listing, and can I plan around it?

It is a BFCM offer printed on the Plus Growth card — "Pay only $1 for first month!" — read on the listing on 19 September 2026. It sits on that $99 tier only: Essential at $49 and Checkout concierge at $199 show no such line. Nothing on the listing says when the offer ends, whether it stacks with the 14-day free trial that every plan carries, or whether it applies if you pay $999 for the year instead. What it does change is the cost of a real test: the checkout half of this app, the half it is named after, can be tried for a month for a dollar rather than $99. Confirm the terms in your admin before you count on it.

The listing advertises built-in A/B testing — is that on my plan?

Probably not, and the listing contradicts itself here. The highlights promise "Built-in A/B Testing: Run A/B tests on every block to see what actually converts", and A/B testing appears in the Analytics feature list. The plan table puts it in one place only: "A/B test & analyze all extensions" is a Checkout concierge feature, at $199 a month. Neither Essential nor Plus Growth lists it. So the feature that tells you whether the upsells earned their keep is priced above the upsells themselves. Assume it is not included below $199 until Skai Lama confirms otherwise in writing.

Will this even install on my store?

Check before you budget a tier, because this app has an eligibility gate. The listing carries a compatibility notice — "Wiz: Checkout Upsell is only compatible with stores that:" — and the criteria themselves only render once Shopify knows which store you are, so you will see them in your own admin rather than on the public page. Under Works with it lists Checkout, Customer accounts, Shopify Admin, Checkout Extensibility, Functions and Klaviyo, so checkout extensibility is the footing to confirm first. Two other things to weigh in the same sitting: Essential at $49 is marked not applicable for Shopify Plus, and the listing offers a demo store you can open before installing anything.

Is paying for the year worth it on this app?

Arithmetically, yes, modestly: Plus Growth is $999 a year against $1,188 paid monthly, a saving of $189, and Checkout concierge is $1,999 against $2,388, a saving of $389. The listing calls both 16%. Essential has no annual price at all. Two cautions before you commit. The evaluation window is 14 days on every tier with no free plan underneath, which is short for a checkout change that needs traffic to prove itself — and the $1 first month now printed on Plus Growth may be the cheaper way to buy that proof, though the listing does not say whether it survives an annual commitment. Charges are billed in USD every 30 days.

What should I weigh before buying Growth & Revenue software?

It depends on whether you have the bottleneck it solves. Small teams get the most out of this category when one clear problem is already costing real hours or revenue; buying ahead of that just adds cost and another login. Price it against the hours or lost deals it removes, not against its feature list, and start on the smallest plan that covers the job.
