What Woodpecker does

Cold email and LinkedIn outreach platform priced by contacted prospects — unlimited team seats and email accounts, built-in warm-ups, deliverability tooling and a lead finder.

Pricing (2026)

(price re-verified 2026-08-02 against the vendor's own pricing page) (2026) Free trial. Usage-priced: $7.00 per 100 contacted prospects; unlimited team members and connected email accounts included; 4 warm-ups free. Add-ons: LinkedIn account $29/mo each, managed sending addresses (Google/Microsoft) $6/mo each, extra warm-ups $5/mo per email account, Lead Finder credits from $10 per 500. The rate is real but the entry is not — the prospect slider starts at 500, so the smallest bill is 500 ÷ 100 × $7.00 = $35 a month (checked on their pricing page, 18 August 2026).

Contact 200 prospects and you still pay $35; the per-100 figure only starts describing your bill once you are past the minimum. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

The $7-per-100-prospects unit reads cheap, but a real outbound motion stacks add-ons fast: every connected LinkedIn account is $29 a month, managed sending addresses $6 a month apiece, extra warm-ups $5 per account — and contacted prospects are the meter, so scaling outreach scales the bill linearly.

What buyers report about Woodpecker

Recurring themes from G2 and cold-email practitioner reviews, read in August 2026. We have not run Woodpecker ourselves, so this is other buyers' experience rather than ours. We summarise it here because the complaints are usually the part a vendor page leaves out.

What they praise
  • • Deliverability out of the box is the reason agencies pick it, and it is the hardest thing to get right in this category. 4.3 on G2.
  • • Easy to use and support that reviewers call exceptional.
What they complain about
  • • No built-in prospect database — you source contacts elsewhere, which is a separate cost and a separate tool.
  • • Email only. No LinkedIn in the base product; that is a $29/month per account add-on.
  • • Contact volume caps force upgrades once a team is genuinely active. Base is $35/month for 500 contacted prospects, then $7 per 100.
  • • Sequence management has no drag-and-drop, and performance slows across multiple campaigns.

When the paid plan is worth it

Woodpecker has no free tier — only a 14-day trial capped at 100 sent emails — so 'upgrading' really means adding paid add-ons on top of the usage-priced base plan. That base plan already includes unlimited seats, unlimited connected mailboxes, catch-all verification, 4 free warm-ups, and 100 Lead Finder credits. The add-ons pay off when a specific capability, not volume, is the blocker. LinkedIn outreach costs $29/month per connected account, worth it once a campaign genuinely needs multichannel touches rather than email alone.

The $20/month integrations add-on (API, Webhooks, MCP Server, CLI) only matters if you're building custom syncs beyond the native CRM connectors. Extra warm-ups run $5/month per account once the 4 free slots are used. What none of these buy is speed: Woodpecker's own help center recommends warming up a new domain for about 3 months and a new mailbox on an already-established domain for 3-4 weeks before real sending — no add-on shortens that runway or lifts the 250-email daily cap on Woodpecker-bought Google/Microsoft mailboxes.

How to actually use Woodpecker

First real value means a sent campaign that actually lands: connect a mailbox, build a path (opening email plus follow-up steps), import prospects, set a daily limit, and hit run. What Woodpecker's own help center flags as the real bottleneck is deliverability timing, not the interface. A new domain needs about 3 months of warm-up, and even a new mailbox on an already-established domain needs 3-4 weeks, before automated sending is considered safe.

The free trial runs only 14 days or 100 emails, whichever comes first. Once sending, Google/Microsoft mailboxes bought through Woodpecker are capped at 250 emails per day, Maildoso/Infraforge ones at 100 per day, and the default recommended opening-email pace is 50 per day — limits that shape how fast a campaign scales even after setup is finished.

Frequently asked questions

Does Woodpecker replace my CRM or plug into it?

Most tools in this category plug in rather than replace. Check which direction the data flows and whether the sync is genuinely two-way, because a one-way push creates a second source of truth that your reps will quietly stop trusting. If it does aim to replace your CRM, price the migration honestly: history, custom fields and reporting rarely move across cleanly.

Who should not use Woodpecker?

Skip it if you send occasional one-off campaigns (usage pricing only wins when you actually use it), or your motion is LinkedIn-heavy — $29 per connected account adds up. Buying a tool to fix a problem you don't have yet just adds cost and another login to manage.
