What World Businesses for Sale does

International marketplace for buying and selling established businesses — restaurants, shops, services and manufacturers — free for buyers, with flat listing plans and 24/7 guidance for sellers.

Pricing (2026)

(2026) Browsing is free for buyers. Sellers pay a listing plan PLUS commission on sale: Basic £395 (3% commission, 6-month contract), Standard £749 (2% commission, 9 months), or Premium £1,500 flat — live until sold, no renewals, no commission stated. Free 24/7 chat guidance before you commit. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

Sellers pay twice on the lower tiers: a listing fee up front AND 2-3% commission when it sells — and the 6-9-month contracts mean an unsold business needs a paid renewal. Run the math: for slower-selling businesses the £1,500 until-sold Premium often beats paying £749 twice. As with any marketplace, the fee buys exposure, not a guaranteed sale.

Best for: owners selling established, often brick-and-mortar businesses — restaurants, shops, services, small manufacturers — who want international buyer exposure with hands-on guidance.
Who should skip it: if you're selling a small online asset (an auction-style online-business marketplace fits better), or you won't commit to a listing fee plus commission.

The natural comparison is Flippa — Flippa is the online-business marketplace (sites, apps, e-commerce) with auction dynamics; World Businesses for Sale leans brick-and-mortar and international with flat listing plans — pick by what you're actually selling. Decide by which one fits the job above, not by the louder brand.

Frequently asked questions

Is World Businesses for Sale safe to run my finances through?

The bar here is higher than features. Check who actually holds the money and under what licence, whether client funds are segregated, what audit trail and permission controls you get, and how support behaves when something goes wrong mid-transaction. Anything touching payments should be able to answer those in writing; if it cannot, that is your answer.

Who should not use World Businesses for Sale?

Skip it if you're selling a small online asset (an auction-style online-business marketplace fits better), or you won't commit to a listing fee plus commission. Buying a tool to fix a problem you don't have yet just adds cost and another login to manage.
