ZenBusiness Review (2026)

Operations & WorkflowResearched assessment · by Daniel HaketUpdated 2026-09-05

The $0 is real for the filing service and it is also the whole commercial idea: what you sign up for at zero has an annual carry that is not shown next to it.

Best for: first-time founders who want the paperwork handled and are willing to pay a yearly fee to keep it handled — the a-la-carte pricing means you can start at the state fee plus a registered agent and add only what you actually need.
Who should skip it: if you are comfortable filing with your state yourself and acting as your own registered agent. In that case the state fee is the only unavoidable cost, and the $199/year is buying convenience and a business address rather than anything you cannot do. Also skip it if you need legal advice rather than filing, since a formation service is not a law firm.

Pricing (2026)

✓ Pricing re-verified 27 Aug 2026

Formation itself is $0 plus the state's fee — that applies to LLC, S corp and C corp filings alike, and the state fee is separate, varies by state and is not shown until you pick one. Everything else is priced as its own line rather than bundled into plan tiers. The recurring items are registered agent at $199/year, Worry-Free Compliance Filing at $199/year, and Advanced Compliance Protection at $299/year.

One-time filings: operating agreement $99, EIN $99, business document templates $99, rush filing $79 and DBA registration $95 plus state fees. Then business licence report $149, amendment $100, certificate of good standing $100, annual report filing $100 per report, and New York publication $200 for the state that requires it. There is a second catalogue alongside the legal one: business website $129/year, eCommerce website $279/year, business email address $25/year, domain $25/year, domain privacy $10/year, logo kit $60, business banking $5/month and Money Pro with online payments $30/month. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

A registered agent is a legal requirement in every state, and theirs is $199/year — so the realistic first-year cost of 'the $0 LLC' is the state fee plus $199, before compliance filing at another $199/year. Price the second year, not the first. The second thing to hold in view is that the state fee is the largest and most variable number in the whole exercise, and ZenBusiness cannot change it.

It is set by your state, ranges widely, and their own page sends you to a lookup rather than quoting it. Anyone comparing formation services on the advertised price alone is comparing the small half of the bill.

What ZenBusiness does

US business-formation service — LLC, S corp and C corp filings at $0 plus the state's own fee, with the money in the recurring stack around it: registered agent $199/year, Worry-Free Compliance $199/year (Advanced $299/year), operating agreement $99, EIN $99. Add-ons are priced individually rather than bundled into tiers (site, verified 25 Jul 2026).

What buyers report about ZenBusiness

What they praise

  • • The dashboard keeps compliance dates visible, which is the part solo founders forget.
What they complain about
  • • Nearly 500 BBB complaints in three years, largely billing. They name deceptive billing, cancellations not being processed, and attempts to charge another full year without authorisation.
  • • Worry-Free Compliance is free the first year then auto-renews at $199. Registered agent is $99 the first year, $199 after. The first-year price is not the price.
  • • Upsells are frequent during and after checkout, and reviewers report a cancellation fee around $25.
  • • Filing delays and hard-to-reach support during busy periods.

When the paid plan is worth it

The upgrade question here is not a plan tier, it is which recurring line you keep. Worry-Free Compliance at $199/year buys the annual report and amendment filings being done for you. The same annual report filed individually is $100 per report, and an amendment is $100. So the maths only works if you expect more than one filing a year, or you value not tracking the deadline. Advanced Compliance Protection at $299/year adds unlimited amendment filings and a money-back guarantee on penalties for missed compliance events — that guarantee is the real difference between the two, so read what it covers before paying the extra $100.

Registered agent at $199/year is the one line most businesses genuinely cannot skip, unless you are willing to use your own address publicly and be available during business hours to receive legal documents.

How to actually use ZenBusiness

Look up your state's filing fee and processing time first — their own page has the lookup, and that number will usually be larger than anything ZenBusiness charges you. Decide the registered agent question before you file, because it is a required field on the filing itself: your own address is free but becomes public record and ties you to business hours, theirs is $199/year. Then file at $0 and add nothing else on day one.

The EIN at $99 is the add-on worth pausing on: it is required to open a business bank account or hire, and the IRS issues it directly at no charge, so you are paying to not deal with the IRS yourself. Standard filing is included; rush at $79 only matters if you have a launch date. If you are forming in New York, budget the $200 publication line separately — that state requires a newspaper announcement and it catches people out.
