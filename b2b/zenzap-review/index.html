What ZenZap does

Secure work-chat for teams — organized channels, built-in tasks and workflows, AI agents and admin controls, as easy to use as texting.

Pricing (2026)

(2026) Free plan with unlimited users, chats and built-in to-dos; Pro about $3/user/mo (integrations, storage); Business+ ~$8/user/mo (roles, audit trails, SLAs); Enterprise custom. Annual billing prices best. Plans change — always verify the live price on their site.

What the pricing page doesn’t say

The catch

The pricing genuinely undercuts Slack — the honest question is ecosystem: team chat lives or dies on integrations and habit, and ZenZap's app catalog is a fraction of Slack's, so teams glued to hundreds of integrations will feel the gap. For WhatsApp-refugee teams that mainly need organized channels, tasks and work/life separation, the free tier is a remarkably complete landing spot.

What buyers report about ZenZap

Recurring themes from G2, Capterra, GetApp and Software Advice, read in August 2026. We have not run ZenZap ourselves, so this is other buyers' experience rather than ours. We summarise it here because the complaints are usually the part a vendor page leaves out.

What they praise
  • • Keeping work messaging out of personal WhatsApp is the whole point, and reviewers say it achieves that without becoming complicated.
  • • $3 per user on Pro, $8 on Business+, with a free tier at 1 GB and 5 project chats.
What they complain about
  • • Reviewers report service interruptions with no proactive communication, and support emails going unanswered. For a tool a team depends on daily, that is the risk.
  • • Notifications and file uploads are glitchy.
  • • Integrations are limited and sit on Business+ along with API access.
Best for: small teams that run work over messy WhatsApp/personal chat today and want organized, professional chat with tasks — starting free.
Who should skip it: if your workflows depend on a deep integration ecosystem (Slack's moat), or you're already paid into Microsoft Teams via 365.

The natural comparison is Slack or Teams — the giants — richer ecosystems at higher per-seat costs; ZenZap wins on price and simplicity for chat-first small teams.

Will ZenZap work for a remote or distributed team?

Generally yes, since this category is built for it, but the details that bite are timezone handling, how per-seat cost scales as the team spreads, and whether numbers, routing or coverage exist in the countries your people actually sit in. Who should not use ZenZap?
