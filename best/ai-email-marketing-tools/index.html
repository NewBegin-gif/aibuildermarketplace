How to choose

Match the AI to your real bottleneck. Drowning in campaign creation and rewriting AI output? ActiveCampaign's brand-aware AI (it builds from your brand kit, session memory and past campaigns, per its own documentation) has the most complete feature set here. We have not run the same campaign brief through ActiveCampaign, Mailchimp, HubSpot and Klaviyo, so treat 'closest to send-ready' as a hypothesis to test in a trial, not a measured result. Watching cost as your list grows? Brevo bills per send. Creator newsletter? Kit. Don't pay for automation depth you'll never switch on.

Frequently asked questions

What's the best AI email marketing tool?

It depends on the job. For brand-aware AI that builds campaigns from your voice, past sends and custom instructions, ActiveCampaign documents the widest feature set (we have not tested output quality side by side); for cost control on a big list, Brevo bills per send; for creators, Kit. The best one is the one that fits how you actually send.

Does AI email marketing actually save time?

Only if the AI starts from your context — brand voice, past campaigns, audience. Generic AI just shifts the work to editing. The tools worth paying for feed on your own materials so the first draft lands closer to send-ready instead of needing a full rewrite.

What's the cheapest AI email tool for a growing list?

Tools that bill per email sent (like Brevo) are usually cheaper than per-contact pricing once your list is large but lightly engaged. Model your real send volume before committing — and check each provider's live pricing, since plans change.
