How to choose

Start with where your bottleneck actually is. No data? Apollo or Amplemarket. Drowning in manual research and personalisation? AISDR or Clay. Have data but no sending engine? Reply.io. Don't buy the whole category — one tool that fixes your real bottleneck beats five half-used subscriptions.

An all-in-one AI sales platform: an AI 'Searcher' that turns a plain-English ICP into verified contacts, 100+ contact-level buying-intent signals, and social prospecting from LinkedIn, plus multichannel outreach and deliverability tooling — drawing on a large multi-source contact database it refreshes continuously. Workflows, the automation layer, is included for every customer rather than sold as a premium add-on: triggers (a sequence finishing with no reply, a bounce, a meeting booked, a call logged, a CRM field changing), filters for who qualifies, branching paths and actions (enrol in another sequence, update CRM fields, create tasks, manage lists, enrich, add to exclusion lists), assembled on a visual canvas so the whole logic is readable at a glance. Frequently asked questions

What is an AI SDR tool?

An AI SDR (sales development rep) tool automates the top-of-funnel work a junior rep would do: finding prospects, researching them, writing personalised outreach and following up. The better ones genuinely book meetings; the weaker ones just send more generic email.

Can an AI SDR replace a human rep?

For the repetitive research-and-first-touch work, increasingly yes. For nuanced, high-value conversations, no — treat AI SDR tools as leverage for a founder or small team, not a full replacement.

What's the best AI SDR tool on a small budget?

Apollo has a free tier to start (900 credits per seat a year and 2 sequences, read 11 September 2026), and Reply.io is cost-effective if you already have data. Model your real volume first — pricing scales with contacts and sending, so check live plans before committing.
