Frequently asked questions

What is an AI SDR tool?

An AI SDR (sales development rep) tool automates the top-of-funnel work a junior rep would do: finding prospects, researching them, writing personalised outreach and following up. The better ones genuinely book meetings; the weaker ones just send more generic email.

Can an AI SDR replace a human rep?

For the repetitive research-and-first-touch work, increasingly yes. For nuanced, high-value conversations, no — treat AI SDR tools as leverage for a founder or small team, not a full replacement.

What's the best AI SDR tool on a small budget?

Apollo has a usable free tier to start, and Reply.io is cost-effective if you already have data. Model your real volume first — pricing scales with contacts and sending, so check live plans before committing.
