The best AI writing tools, by phase of the writing process (2026)

Short answer: Our picks for the best AI writing tools, by phase of the writing process: Frase for research and briefs that target what actually ranks; QuillBot for fast paraphrasing, summarising and tightening drafts; Rank Math for on-page SEO and schema inside WordPress. Below, what each is best for — and who should skip it.

Most AI writing tools are great at producing words and terrible at producing rankings. The difference is research, structure and genuine judgement — the parts AI is worst at. Used well, these tools take the boring 80% off your plate so you can spend your time on the 20% that actually wins: angle, accuracy and a point of view. This is a guide per phase — research, drafting, on-page SEO, product copy — not a head-to-head test of the same brief: the four tools do different jobs and are not ranked against each other.

How to choose

Use AI for the scaffolding — research, outlines, first drafts, on-page checks — and keep the judgement human. Frase for what to write, QuillBot to tighten it, Rank Math to optimise it on WordPress. The tool doesn't rank; a useful, opinionated page does.

Frequently asked questions

Can AI writing tools actually rank on Google?

AI helps with research, structure and speed, but pages rank on genuine usefulness and a real point of view — which still need a human. Use AI for the boring 80% and bring judgement to the 20% that wins.

What's the best AI tool for SEO content?

Frase is strong for research and briefs that target ranking intent; pair it with Rank Math for on-page optimisation if you're on WordPress, and QuillBot to tighten the draft.

Will Google penalise AI-written content?

Google rewards helpful content regardless of how it's made, and demotes unhelpful content — also regardless. AI-assisted is fine; AI-spam is not. The bar is usefulness.
