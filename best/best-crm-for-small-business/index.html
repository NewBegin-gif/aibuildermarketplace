The best CRM for a small business (2026)

Short answer: Our picks for the best CRM for a small business: Pipedrive for a simple, visual pipeline your team can use on day one, when marketing already lives in a separate tool; Close for teams that sell by phone and email all day, with calling built in; Nutshell for unlimited contacts and full API access on every tier, at a low per-user price. Below, what each is best for — and who should skip it.

This guide answers one question: which CRM gets a small sales team working fastest and pays for itself soonest. If you want email and SMS follow-up built into the CRM, read our guide to CRM software with marketing automation instead — that page ranks a different job and names a different winner. The best CRM for a small business isn't the most powerful one — it's the one your team actually keeps updated. Heavier CRMs earn their keep when sales, marketing and support data have to be joined up; a small team that mainly needs its deals moved along usually does better with a lighter tool it can use on day one. Here's what I'd pick, by how you actually sell.

How to choose

Don't buy the heaviest CRM you can find. Match the tool to how you sell: a visual pipeline you use on day one → Pipedrive; phone-and-email outbound → Close; marketing in the same tool → Nutshell; a CRM that logs emails and meetings for you → Salesflare; the lightest modern pick → folk. Then price the whole bill, not the seat: Close's call and SMS credits, Nutshell's marketing add-ons and Salesflare's five-seat minimum on Enterprise all sit on top of the headline price.

Frequently asked questions

What's the best CRM for a small business?

It depends on how you sell. For a team that mainly needs its deals moved along, Pipedrive's visual pipeline is the simplest to start on. If you sell mostly by phone, Close fits better (budget for call credits); if you want marketing in the same tool, Nutshell, or our guide to CRM with marketing automation.

Is Salesforce good for a small business?

Usually more than a small team needs at the start. It earns its keep when sales, marketing and service data have to be joined up across a larger organisation. We have no measured setup-time or payback figures to quote, so judge it on the setup work your team can realistically take on.

What's the cheapest CRM that's still good?

Tools with unlimited contacts and built-in marketing — like Nutshell — stretch a small budget further, and folk and Salesflare keep friction low. Compare each on current per-user pricing for your team size before deciding.
