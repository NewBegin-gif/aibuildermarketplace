Dashboard tools almost never charge you for the dashboard. They charge for what you plug into it, and every vendor picks a different dial. Read on their own pricing pages: Databox meters data sources — Free covers three sources and one dashboard, Analyst is $71 a month for five and Team Core $199 for ten on annual billing (12 September 2026). AgencyAnalytics meters clients: about $20 per client a month on annual billing with none included, and past 25 clients the vendor quotes instead of publishing (11 September 2026). Joiin meters legal entities, at $23 a month for one company on annual billing and $57 for five (17 August 2026). Diginius sells plan tiers — Essential $75, Pro $250, Core $500 a month — whose keyword, page and lead limits rise with the price; it no longer publishes seat counts (22 September 2026). Iconosquare meters social profiles: free for two, Starter EUR 9 a month billed yearly for three, Launch EUR 33 for five (15 September 2026). And Datarails meters nothing you can see: all three plans carry a Request Quote button where the number belongs (25 August 2026). So the first question is not which dashboard looks best in a demo. It is which job you are reporting on, and which dial that job is about to spin.

How to choose

Start with the job, then the dial. Reporting on your own business, reporting to clients, consolidating several legal entities, social media and Shopify margins are five different purchases, and each tool here meters a different thing: data sources, clients, entities, social profiles or a plan tier. Count your own number for that dial, then price a year out rather than today, because these tools are chosen when you have three sources and paid for when you have twelve. If only you read the dashboard, a free Looker Studio build costs a weekend and nothing a month; if clients read it, white-label reporting is the product you are buying, and it is priced per client. Before you sign, check the integration list against the tools you genuinely use: a missing connector turns a dashboard into a spreadsheet export with extra steps.

Frequently asked questions

What do dashboard tools actually charge for?

Rarely the dashboard itself. Databox prices data sources, AgencyAnalytics prices clients, Joiin prices the legal entities you consolidate, Iconosquare prices social profiles, and Diginius sells plan tiers with keyword, page and lead limits. Two tools with the same headline price can differ several-fold once your real numbers go in, so work out which job and which dial you are paying for before you compare plans.

Is there a genuinely free option?

Databox lists a free plan with three data sources, one user and one dashboard (pricing page, 12 September 2026). In our own test on 25 September the sign-up opened a 14-day Team Core trial rather than Free, the downgrade was blocked until we deleted Databox's sample data, and the one free dashboard slot stayed taken by their sample dashboard. EcomXray's analytics inside Shopify are free, with Pro at $29 a month (8 September 2026), and Iconosquare is free for two social profiles (15 September 2026). Outside this list, Google's Looker Studio is free and more flexible, but you build and maintain the connections yourself.

Why is one of these quote-only?

Datarails publishes three plans and puts a Request Quote button where every price should be, which we read on 25 August 2026. That is a legitimate model for enterprise FP&A, but it means you cannot compare it on cost without entering a sales process — worth knowing before you spend a week in one.
