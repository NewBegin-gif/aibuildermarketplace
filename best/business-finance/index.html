1. Aspire — Business account & cards — startups that want $0 monthly fees and built-in spend management; check country availability first

All-in-one finance stack for global businesses: multi-currency accounts (30+ currencies), corporate cards with 1.2% cashback, expense management, bill pay, payments and payroll — with AI fraud…

My take: A fintech account rather than a full bank, strongest in Southeast Asia. The Basic account is S$0 plus FX and transfer fees; confirm it supports your country and currencies first. Read our full Aspire review →

Visit Aspire →

2. Payoneer — Getting paid cross-border — international clients & marketplaces

Local receiving accounts for freelancers and sellers paid by international clients and marketplaces.

My take: Local receiving accounts for marketplace and client payouts. Conversion and withdrawal markups are where it costs you; for pure currency conversion a specialist FX account is usually cheaper. Read our full Payoneer review →

Visit Payoneer →

3. Bill.com — AP/AR automation — US businesses automating payables & receivables

Accounts-payable and receivable automation for finance teams with real approval chains.

My take: From $49 per user a month plus per-payment fees: built for real accounts-payable workflows, overkill for a handful of bills. Read our full Bill.com review →

Visit Bill.com →

4. Melio — Bill payments — simple B2B payments for small US teams

Bill payments for small businesses by bank transfer or card, syncing with your accounting tools. Card payments carry a flat 2.9%, which only pays off when you are deliberately floating cash.

My take: A free Go plan with 5 free ACH payments a month; the 2.9% card fee only makes sense when you deliberately float cash on a card. Read our full Melio review →

Visit Melio →

5. Gusto — Payroll — US payroll, benefits & HR in one; not a bank account

Payroll, benefits and HR in one friendly platform. Automated tax filings, employee onboarding and time tracking — run payroll for your whole team in just a few clicks.

My take: Simple is $49 a month plus $6 per person. US-only: since 1 May 2026 new international hires are referred to Remote's employer-of-record service. Read our full Gusto review →

Visit Gusto →

6. Navan — Travel & expense — booking and spend in one workflow

Business travel and expense management in one. Book trips, automate expense reports and control spend with real-time visibility.

My take: Free on Navan Business up to 300 employees; Navan earns commission on bookings, so check route prices against what you would pay elsewhere. Read our full Navan review →

Visit Navan →

7. doola — US company formation — founders forming a US LLC from anywhere; not a finance tool once you are set up

Business formation and compliance made easy. Launch your US LLC or C-Corp from anywhere globally, complete with banking and tax management.

My take: Starter is $297 a year plus state fees. Formation is the cheap part; the bookkeeping and tax tiers are where the recurring cost sits. Read our full doola review →

Visit doola →
