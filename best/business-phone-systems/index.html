1. Quo — Micro-teams of 2 to 5 working mostly on Wi-Fi

Shared business numbers, texting and AI call summaries for small teams, formerly OpenPhone. Call reliability off Wi-Fi is the recurring complaint, and there is no video conferencing.

My take: A modern phone app built around a shared inbox, with AI call summaries. Call reliability off Wi-Fi is the recurring complaint in our dossier, so test it on the networks you actually call from. Read our full Quo review →

2. CallHippo — Virtual numbers in 50+ countries on a budget

AI-driven business phone system. Virtual numbers in 50+ countries, power dialer, call analytics and AI agents — set up your sales and support calling in minutes.

My take: A free entry point and CRM integrations. 'Unlimited calling' covers the US and Canada only; international minutes meter separately, so price your real destinations first. Read our full CallHippo review →

Visit CallHippo →

3. Aircall — Sales and support teams of 3+ working in their CRM

Cloud phone system for sales and support teams: call routing, analytics and CRM integrations.

My take: A polished cloud phone system with routing, analytics and 250+ integrations. The 3-licence minimum triples the sticker price for a very small team. Read our full Aircall review →

Visit Aircall →

4. CloudTalk — Call-centre routing and analytics for support and sales

Cloud call centre with call routing, analytics and CRM integrations, priced per user; the Expert tier needs at least three licences.

My take: Strong CRM integrations and call analytics. Number rental, outbound minutes and AI add-ons stack on top of the seat price, so model your call volume first. Read our full CloudTalk review →

Visit CloudTalk →

5. KrispCall — Numbers in many countries at a budget seat price

Virtual numbers across 100+ countries with a shared dashboard, dialer and CRM hooks, priced per user.

My take: A shared dashboard, dialer and CRM hooks. Per-minute and per-SMS charges land on top of every seat, so compare call rates per country at your own volume. Read our full KrispCall review →

Visit KrispCall →

6. Calilio — Startups that want a simple cloud phone system

Cloud phone system with international numbers and call menus, priced per user.

My take: International numbers and clean dashboards. Numbers, minutes and SMS meter separately from a credit balance, so an active team pays above the per-seat price. Read our full Calilio review →

Visit Calilio →

7. Vida AI — Agencies reselling white-label AI voice agents

Deploy autonomous frontline voice agents. Let AI answer calls, book appointments, and handle FAQs 24/7 without sounding robotic.
