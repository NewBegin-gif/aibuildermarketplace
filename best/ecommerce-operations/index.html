1. Shippo — Multi-carrier rate comparison, labels and tracking

Multi-carrier shipping software for e-commerce. Compare rates, print discounted labels and automate tracking across carriers — shipping that scales with your store.

My take: Free up to 30 labels a month, with 5 cents a label on your own carrier accounts; read the Pro comparison table before assuming it is flat. Read our full Shippo review →

2. Katana MRP — Makers who need live inventory and production planning

Cloud manufacturing and inventory platform for product businesses. Live inventory control, production scheduling and order syncing across your sales channels — know exactly what to make and when.

My take: The $299 headline is the floor: sales orders, extra locations and the routing and traceability add-ons ($199 and $249 a month) sit on top. Read our full Katana MRP review →

Visit Katana MRP →

3. Printify — Testing print-on-demand with no upfront cost

Global print-on-demand network. Create custom products and automate your e-commerce fulfillment without holding inventory.

My take: Premium pays off only past a break-even order volume, and print quality varies by provider, so order samples first. Read our full Printify review →

Visit Printify →

4. Alidrop — Dropshipping automation with faster-shipping suppliers

Dropshipping automation — product sourcing, fulfilment and inventory sync, with access to faster-shipping US and EU suppliers.

My take: You pay the subscription plus wholesale product cost, so margins depend on product selection. Read our full Alidrop review →

Visit Alidrop →

5. Adwisely — Hands-off retargeting and prospecting ads for stores

Automated retargeting and prospecting ads on Meta and Google for Shopify, WooCommerce and BigCommerce stores.

My take: Charges 10% of ad spend on top of a monthly minimum: cheap at low spend, a real cut at higher budgets. Read our full Adwisely review →

6. Smartli — AI product listings, ad copy and SEO text in one suite

AI writing suite for e-commerce: product listings, ad copy, SEO text and brand assets. Each of the nine tools is competent rather than deep, so leaning hard on one job means outgrowing it.

My take: Nine AI tools in one; each is competent rather than best-in-class, so edit and fact-check before anything ships. Read our full Smartli review →
