1. ClickUp — One configurable workspace for tasks, docs and goals

The 'everything app' for project and work management. Centralize your tasks, docs, sprints, and team communication in one hub.

My take: Powerful and cheap per seat if you invest the setup time; for a light use case the depth turns into overwhelm. Read our full ClickUp review →

Visit ClickUp →

2. SmartSuite — Projects, CRM and operations in one database

One flexible work management platform to plan, track and automate any process. Projects, CRM and operations side by side — replace a stack of scattered tools with a single source of truth.

My take: A flexible, database-driven platform. The per-seat cost and learning curve pay off only if you commit to it as the single source of truth. Read our full SmartSuite review →

Visit SmartSuite →

3. Reclaim.ai — AI scheduling of tasks, habits and meetings

Auto-schedules tasks, habits and meetings around your stated priorities. Google Calendar-first, so Outlook-heavy teams are out, and the free tier is limited.

My take: Works on calendars it can write to: Google, Microsoft 365, Outlook.com and Exchange-backed accounts. Hosted or on-premise Exchange is listed as unsupported. Read our full Reclaim.ai review →

Visit Reclaim.ai →

4. SaneBox — AI email triage across any mail client

Email triage that moves low-priority mail out of the inbox and works with any provider. The honest catch: you are paying monthly to filter mail that modern email clients increasingly sort for free.

My take: Works with any email provider, which the built-in Gmail and Outlook filters do not; worth paying for only if your inbox is genuinely unmanageable. Read our full SaneBox review →

Visit SaneBox →

5. Process Street — Recurring work as trackable, auditable checklists

Recurring work as trackable checklists and SOPs, with approvals and conditional logic.

My take: Built for teams whose core work is recurring processes. Pricing is sales-led with seat minimums, so you need a call to price it. Read our full Process Street review →

Visit Process Street →

6. Trainual — SOPs and onboarding for teams of about ten or more

Documents SOPs and runs new-hire onboarding, with AI that drafts and updates training docs from your own account context.

My take: All four plans are quote-gated; the one published figure is a one-time implementation fee. A team of two or three does fine with a well-kept doc. Read our full Trainual review →

Visit Trainual →

7. WebCatalog — Many web apps and accounts as separate desktop apps

Turn web apps into tidy desktop apps and manage multiple accounts side by side. Less tab chaos, more focus — your whole stack organized in one place.

My take: Solves a real annoyance. The free plan's 2-app limit is tight, and browser profiles may already cover you. Read our full WebCatalog review →

Visit WebCatalog →
