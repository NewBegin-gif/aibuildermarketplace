3. Shorby — Turning one bio link into a sales hub

Link-in-bio pages and short links, with messenger deep-links into WhatsApp or Telegram and retargeting pixels firing on every click. It charges from about $15/month where Linktree does not charge at all, so the gap only pays off if messenger selling or ad retargeting is genuinely your funnel.

My take: A polished link-in-bio plus smart short links — point every channel at one page that actually drives clicks where you want them. Read our full Shorby review →

Visit Shorby →

4. Iconosquare — Knowing what your numbers actually mean

Social analytics, reporting and competitor benchmarking, deepest on Instagram and the core networks. Free for one user and two profiles, then Launch around €33/month and Scale €69. Tiers gate on seats and profiles, so agencies managing many brands climb faster than the entry price suggests.

My take: Deep analytics for Instagram, TikTok and more — the tool you add once you care about what's working, not just posting. Read our full Iconosquare review →

Visit Iconosquare →

5. ManyChat — Automating DMs & lead capture

Comment-to-DM and chat funnels for Instagram, Messenger, WhatsApp and SMS. Flows are button-and-script based, so an unexpected reply falls back. The free tier holds 25 active contacts and paid plans are banded by list size — $14/month at 250 contacts, $139 at 25,000.

My take: Turns Instagram and Messenger conversations into automated flows that capture leads and answer FAQs around the clock. Read our full ManyChat review →
