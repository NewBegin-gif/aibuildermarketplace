The best WP Engine alternatives (2026)

Short answer: Our picks for the best WP Engine alternatives: Kinsta for the closest like-for-like: managed WordPress where someone else owns the stack; Cloudways for control over the underlying server without administering it yourself; Hostinger for a very low entry price on a long prepaid term — $2.99 a month on the 48-month plan, $143.52 up front. Below, what each is best for — and who should skip it.

Most people leave WP Engine over one of two numbers. The first is the plan itself: read on their own plans page on 26 September 2026 from a European connection, Essential starts at €25.60 a month on annual billing, with €307.20 taken up front. That is a first-year price for new customers, and the page says renewal pricing is subject to change without naming the renewal rate. The entry configuration covers one site, 25,000 visits a month and 10 GB of storage. The second number is what happens when you grow: Core starts at €370.92 a month, and the add-ons that make the entry plan feel complete (extra security €19, NitroPack €19, read on 18 August 2026) took the entry plan from €27 to €65 a month, more than double, before a second site. And the meter is visits, not sites, which their own page hedges as “estimates only”. So the honest question is not which host is cheapest, but which meter you would rather be on. How to choose

Start by working out which meter binds you. If it is visits, a host that charges by sites or resources changes the shape of your bill entirely. If it is sites, the opposite. Then price two years rather than one: almost every host on this list advertises a term rate, and the renewal is where the real cost lives — that is true of WP Engine as well as of the cheaper options. Finally, price the add-ons you would actually switch on. A cheap plan plus security plus caching is often not cheap. This page does not work two like-for-like scenarios out to the cent; the per-host dossiers carry the prices we read, with the term and the renewal, so build the comparison for your own site count and traffic from those.

Frequently asked questions

Is Kinsta cheaper than WP Engine?

Not necessarily, and that is the wrong reason to move. Both sell managed WordPress at the premium end. Compare them on support responsiveness, the infrastructure underneath and how each one meters growth — and read both current price lists yourself, because both change.

What does WP Engine actually cost?

Read on their own plans page on 26 September 2026 from a European connection: Essential starts at €25.60 a month on annual billing, €307.20 up front, as a first-year price for new customers; the page does not name the renewal rate. The entry configuration covers one site, 25,000 visits a month and 10 GB of storage, and the plan scales up to 30 sites. Core starts at €370.92 a month. On 18 August 2026 the add-ons read €19 each for an additional site, extra security and NitroPack; we could not re-read them on 26 September because the plan builder did not load them.

Which alternative is genuinely cheapest?

On the headline, the long-term shared plans: ChemiCloud at $1.95 a month on a 36-month term (a campaign that runs to 30 September 2026; the renewal is $11.95) and Hostinger at $2.99 on a 48-month term with $143.52 up front. Both rates are tied to a long prepaid term and renew higher, so the honest comparison is the renewal price against the others, not the headline.
