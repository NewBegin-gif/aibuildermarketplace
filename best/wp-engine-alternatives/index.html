The best WP Engine alternatives (2026)

Short answer: The best wp engine alternatives are Kinsta for the closest like-for-like: managed WordPress where someone else owns the stack; Cloudways for control over the underlying server without administering it yourself; Hostinger for the lowest entry price by a wide margin. Below, what each is best for — and who should skip it.

Most people leave WP Engine over one of two numbers. The first is the plan itself: read on their own plans page on 18 August 2026, the entry Essential plan is €27 a month with €320 taken up front, for one site and 25,000 visits. The second is what happens when you grow — the step from Essential to Core is €27 to €371, and the add-ons that make the entry plan feel complete (extra security €19, NitroPack €19) nearly double it before you add a second site. And the meter is visits, not sites, which their own page hedges as “estimates only”. So the honest question is not which host is cheapest, but which meter you would rather be on. Here is what we would reach for, with prices we read ourselves.

The picks

Kinsta
Best for: the closest like-for-like: managed WordPress where someone else owns the stack

Managed WordPress hosting on Google Cloud, with free migrations and 24/7 support. From about $35/month for one site (25,000 visits, 10 GB), or roughly $29 billed annually. Exceeding the visit cap bills per 1,000 extra visits, and Kinsta confirms it offers no partner-only discount codes.

Skip it if you are leaving WP Engine mainly to save money — Kinsta is a premium host too, and the case for it is speed and support rather than price.

Cloudways
Best for: control over the underlying server without administering it yourself

Managed-hosting layer that rents you servers on DigitalOcean, Vultr, Linode, AWS or Google Cloud through one dashboard, with the stack, caching, staging, cloning, backups and 24/7 platform support pre-built on top. You buy a server and put as many sites on it as it holds — no per-site plans and no visit metering — billed hourly and invoiced monthly in arrears with no contract. The trade is control: Cloudways' own documentation states there is no root access and no bring-your-own cloud account, so the server lives in Cloudways' name. Owned by DigitalOcean since September 2022.

Skip it if you never want to think about a server at all, because this asks you to pick one.

How to choose

Start by working out which meter binds you. If it is visits, a host that charges by sites or resources changes the shape of your bill entirely. If it is sites, the opposite. Then price two years rather than one: almost every host on this list advertises a term rate, and the renewal is where the real cost lives — that is true of WP Engine as well as of the cheaper options. Finally, price the add-ons you would actually switch on. A cheap plan plus security plus caching is often not cheap.

Frequently asked questions

Is Kinsta cheaper than WP Engine?

Not necessarily, and that is the wrong reason to move. Both sell managed WordPress at the premium end. Compare them on support responsiveness, the infrastructure underneath and how each one meters growth — and read both current price lists yourself, because both change.

What does WP Engine actually cost?

Read on their own plans page on 18 August 2026: Essential starts at €27 a month with €320 taken up front, covering one site and 25,000 visits a month, with 10 GB of storage. Additional sites are €19 each, extra security €19 and NitroPack €19. Core starts at €371 a month.

Which alternative is genuinely cheapest?

Hostinger, by a wide margin — but the advertised rate is tied to a 48-month term and the renewal is roughly four times higher, so the honest comparison is the renewal price against the others, not the headline.
