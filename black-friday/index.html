Questions people ask

When do Black Friday SaaS deals 2026 start?

Most SaaS vendors launch Black Friday pricing in mid-to-late November 2026, though early-bird deals appear from October. This tracker verifies each claimed deal against our documented base prices, so you can see which discounts are real.

Are Black Friday software deals actually cheaper?

Often not: a common pattern is presenting the standard annual discount as a Black Friday special. Because we snapshot pricing pages monthly, we can compare the 'deal' against what the tool cost in September and October.
