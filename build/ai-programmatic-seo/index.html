AI + Programmatic SEO: Building a Multilingual Content Machine

Programmatic SEO — generating many pages from a template + data — got a lot easier with AI. I run content across dozens of languages without a dev team. But it's also the fastest way to get a site penalised if you do it lazily.

Here's the honest architecture and the pitfalls I learned the hard way.

The basic architecture

A data source (your tools, topics, comparisons) + a template + an AI writing layer = many targeted pages. The skill is in the data and the templates, not in cranking out volume. Frase → helps with SEO-driven briefs and optimisation.

The pitfalls that get you penalised

Thin, near-duplicate pages and machine-translated filler are exactly what Google's helpful-content systems target. I learned to prune ruthlessly, lead with one strong language, and add real first-hand judgement — not just generate-and-pray.

Quality still wins

Programmatic gives you reach; honest, opinionated content gives you rankings that stick. Use AI for the scaffolding and the boring parts — keep the judgement human. Not sure where to start with the tools? Try the Tool Finder →

Frequently asked questions

Is programmatic SEO against Google's rules?

Not inherently — templated pages built on real, useful data are fine. What gets penalised is thin, duplicative, low-value content at scale. Quality and genuine usefulness are the line.

Can you do programmatic SEO without coding?

Yes — with AI build tools and templates a non-coder can run it. The hard part is good data, strong templates and honest editing, not the code.

Does AI content rank?

AI-assisted content can rank well when it's genuinely useful and edited with real judgement. Pure generate-and-publish at scale usually doesn't, and can hurt the whole site.
