Step by step

  1. 1

    Pick a niche and a few pillars

    Three to five themes you can own. Depth beats spraying random topics.

  2. 2

    Build a briefs-first pipeline

    Research demand before you write — create content people are actually searching for.

  3. 3

    Create in batches

    Use AI for the scaffolding and the boring parts; keep the judgement and voice human.

  4. 4

  5. 5

    Capture an audience you own

    Send traffic to a newsletter signup; an owned list is worth more than any follower count.

FAQ

Does AI content rank in 2026?

AI-assisted content ranks when it's genuinely useful and edited with real judgement. Pure generate-and-publish at scale usually doesn't — and can hurt the whole site.

What's the most important part of a content engine?

Distribution and an owned audience. Great content nobody sees is wasted; capture readers into a newsletter so your reach compounds.

Do I need a whole team?

No. One person with this stack can research, create, schedule and grow a list — AI removes the grunt work, not the strategy.

Where should I start?

Research and an owned list first (Frase + a newsletter). Add creation and distribution tools once you have a repeatable rhythm.
