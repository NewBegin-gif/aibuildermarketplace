FAQ

Do I need inventory to start a Shopify store?

No. With print-on-demand (Printify) or dropshipping (Spocket), products are made or shipped only when you sell — you don't buy stock up front.

What actually improves Shopify conversion?

Speed and trust first (fast theme, real reviews), then ad-matched landing pages and post-purchase upsells. Most stores leave money on the table by skipping these.

How much does a Shopify stack cost?

Beyond Shopify's own plan, the apps here are mostly modest monthly fees, and print-on-demand has no inventory cost. Always check each tool's current pricing.

Print-on-demand or dropshipping?

Print-on-demand (custom products, your brand) suits original designs; dropshipping (existing products) suits faster catalogue tests. Many stores use both.
