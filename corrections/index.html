Corrections

42 mistakes in our own reviews that we found and fixed. Prices that a vendor changed are not mistakes and are not listed here; those are on the Price Watch. This page is for the times we had it wrong.

Each entry says what we got wrong and quotes the sentence that records the correction on the review itself, so you can check it there. The date is when that correction entered our records; entries marked “or earlier” predate our version history. Most of our research and writing is done by an AI assistant under fixed rules (who does what), and most of these mistakes were found by re-reading the vendor’s own page.

Found something wrong? Write to daniel@aibuildermarketplace.com. The same list is available as data: /api/v1/corrections.json.

  1. 8 Oct 2026Our YouTube videos

    25 review videos said a product had a free plan because a price text contained the word 'free' (for example 'free SSL' or 'toll-free'). Each of those videos now opens its description with a correction, and the video script no longer claims a free plan unless the pricing page lists one.
