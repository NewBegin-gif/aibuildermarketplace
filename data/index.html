Price changes, month over month

Since July 2026 we archive the pricing page of every tool we cover and compare each month against the last. Of the 122 pages we captured in both 2026-07 and 2026-08, 9 changed an amount and 113 did not — a monthly change rate of 7.4%.

The unchanged rows are in the file on purpose: a count without a denominator is not a statistic. Each changed row carries the price lines exactly as we captured them in both months, so the figure can be checked rather than taken on trust. The comparison flags candidates; a person reads the raw lines before anything is published. See the registry and how we review.
