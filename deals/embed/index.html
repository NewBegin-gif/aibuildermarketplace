Put three vendor-confirmed deals on your site

A small block with at most three reader offers in one niche, for a website or a newsletter. It is a pilot: we want to learn whether readers outside our own sites find it useful.

  • Only offers the vendor confirmed to us, with who confirmed it and when.
  • Newest confirmation first. Never ordered by commission.
  • Ended offers drop out on the next daily rebuild, and the block hides an offer past its end date on its own.
  • Links go to our deal page, where the terms and our affiliate disclosure are. Some purchase links there earn us a commission.
  • The block loads no third-party scripts and sets no cookies. The link tells us which site a visit came from, nothing about the reader.

How to add it

On a website, paste this code and swap crm for the niche you want. The small script lets the block set its own height.

<iframe src="https://aibuildermarketplace.com/deals/embed/crm/?host=YOUR-SITE" title="Deals" loading="lazy" style="width:100%;height:420px;border:0" id="aibm-deals"></iframe>
<script>addEventListener("message",function(e){if(e.origin==="https://aibuildermarketplace.com"&&e.data&&e.data.aibmDealsEmbed){document.getElementById("aibm-deals").style.height=e.data.aibmDealsEmbed+"px";}});</script>

Options: add &theme=dark or &theme=light to match your site, and &header=0 if your page already has a heading above the block.

In a newsletter, use the email HTML of the niche (linked below; tables and inline styles, no script). Replace YOUR-SITE in it with the name of your newsletter, so we can count visits from it.
