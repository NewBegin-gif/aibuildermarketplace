Needs confirmation means the vendor has not confirmed or reconfirmed it. Conditional means the vendor grants it case by case. A missing end date means none was given, not that the offer runs forever; an offer with a stated end disappears from this page at that moment.

Free plans, trials and demos come from the vendor’s own description. Partner links add no discount. Some links are affiliate links: we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you.
