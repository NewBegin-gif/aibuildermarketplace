🚀 What is Bitvavo?

Bitvavo is a Dutch cryptocurrency exchange founded in 2018 that has quickly grown to become one of Europe's leading crypto platforms. The company is based in Amsterdam and focuses on making cryptocurrency trading accessible and secure for both beginners and experienced traders.

Key Facts about Bitvavo

  • Founded: 2018 in Amsterdam, Netherlands
  • Regulation: Registered with De Nederlandsche Bank (DNB)
  • Supported Cryptocurrencies: 190+ digital assets
  • Users: Over 1 million registered users
  • Languages: Dutch, English, German, French, Spanish, Italian

What sets Bitvavo apart from other exchanges is its focus on the European market, competitive fees, and user-friendly interface. The platform offers both a web application and mobile apps for iOS and Android, making it easy to trade cryptocurrencies anytime, anywhere.
