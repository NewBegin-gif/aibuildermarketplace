What is InVideo?

InVideo is a powerful, cloud-based video editing platform that uses artificial intelligence to help users create professional videos quickly and easily. Whether you're a beginner or an experienced content creator, InVideo offers an intuitive interface with advanced features that make video production accessible to everyone.

In short: InVideo is an online video editor that combines AI technology with user-friendly tools to create engaging videos for social media, marketing, education, and more - all without requiring technical video editing skills.

How Does InVideo Work?

InVideo simplifies the video creation process through its intuitive workflow:

  1. Choose Your Starting Point: Select from templates, start from scratch, or use AI to generate a video from text
  2. Customize Your Content: Add your own media, edit text, adjust colors, and modify layouts
  3. Enhance with AI: Use AI features for voiceovers, automatic subtitles, and smart suggestions
  4. Preview and Edit: Review your video and make final adjustments using the timeline editor
  5. Export and Share: Download in various formats or directly share to social media platforms

Pro Tip: InVideo's AI can create an entire video from just a text prompt or article URL, making it incredibly fast to produce content for busy marketers and creators.
