What is Synthesia?

Synthesia is an innovative AI-powered video generation platform that allows users to create professional videos with AI avatars without the need for cameras, studios, or actors. The platform uses advanced artificial intelligence to generate realistic human-like presenters who can speak your text in over 120 languages.

🎯 Key Point

Synthesia transforms text into professional video presentations using AI avatars, making video production accessible to everyone regardless of technical skills or budget.

How Does Synthesia Work?

The technology behind Synthesia is based on deep learning and neural networks that have been trained on vast amounts of video data. Here's how the process works:

  1. Text Input: You provide the script or text you want the AI avatar to speak
  2. Avatar Selection: Choose from over 140 diverse AI avatars
  3. Language & Voice: Select from 120+ languages and various voice options
  4. Customization: Add backgrounds, music, and visual elements
  5. AI Processing: The AI generates lip-sync and natural movements
  6. Video Output: Receive a professional-quality video in minutes
