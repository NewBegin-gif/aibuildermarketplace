Hidden Costs and Additional Expenses

Important: Renewal Costs

WP Rocket licenses are annual subscriptions. After the first year, you'll need to renew your license to continue receiving updates and support. The renewal price is the same as the initial purchase price.

What Happens If You Don't Renew?

If you choose not to renew your WP Rocket license:

  • The plugin continues to work on your website
  • You won't receive new updates or security patches
  • No access to customer support
  • No access to new features

Additional Costs to Consider

  • CDN Services: While WP Rocket integrates with CDNs, you'll need to pay separately for CDN services (typically $5-50/month)
  • Hosting Requirements: Some features work best with specific hosting configurations
  • Developer Time: Initial setup and optimization may require technical expertise
