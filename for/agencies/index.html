Software for agencies, sorted by the problem it solves

Agency software gets expensive in a specific way: you pay per client, per seat or per site, and the bill grows every time you sign a new account. The tools below come from our reviews where we named agencies as a buyer, together with the pain we heard and whether the tool actually fixes it.

A green Yes means the vendor's own pages or buyer reports back it up. Partly means it works with a catch, and the catch is in the proof line. The plan named on each card is the one that fits an agency, with the price we read.

Most tools here are affiliate partners: the “Try” buttons may earn us a commission at no extra cost to you. Tools that do not fit this kind of business are left off, partners included.

Questions

Which tools here solve an agency problem outright?

34 of the 64 tools get a Yes for agencies in our reviews, backed by the vendor's own page or buyer reports. The others get Partly: they work, but with a catch on price, seats or limits that the card names.

Why is a tool I expected missing?

A tool only appears when our review names agencies as a buyer and says it solves their problem at least partly. If the honest answer for agencies was no, it stays off this page, even when it is a partner of ours.
