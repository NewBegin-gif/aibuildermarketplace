Software for founders and early-stage startups

Early on you buy tools for jobs you have never done before: forming a company, building a first version, hiring someone abroad, passing a security review. The risk is paying for the enterprise tier of a job you do once. These are the tools where our review named founders or startups as a buyer, with what each actually solves.

Each card shows the founder's problem, whether the tool solves it (Yes or Partly, with the source), and the plan that fits an early company at the price we read. Renewal and second-year costs are in the proof line where the vendor publishes them.

Most tools here are affiliate partners: the “Try” buttons may earn us a commission at no extra cost to you. Tools that do not fit this kind of business are left off, partners included.
