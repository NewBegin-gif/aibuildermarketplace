Software for freelancers and one-person businesses

When you work alone, every subscription comes out of your own pocket and every setup hour is unpaid. Most software is priced for teams, so the useful question is which plan fits one person and where the free or entry tier stops. These are the tools where our review named freelancers, sole traders or solo owners as a buyer.

Each card shows the problem in the words a solo owner would use, whether the tool solves it (Yes or Partly, with the proof), and the plan that fits one person at the price we read.

Most tools here are affiliate partners: the “Try” buttons may earn us a commission at no extra cost to you. Tools that do not fit this kind of business are left off, partners included.
