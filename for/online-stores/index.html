Software for Shopify and online store owners

Store apps are cheap one at a time and expensive together, and many charge more as your order count grows. Before adding another app, it helps to know which one fixes the problem you actually have. These are the tools where our review named a store owner or merchant as the buyer, grouped by the job they do.

Each card shows the merchant's problem, whether the app solves it (Yes or Partly, with the source and reading date), and the plan that fits a store of that size at the price we read.

Most tools here are affiliate partners: the “Try” buttons may earn us a commission at no extra cost to you. Tools that do not fit this kind of business are left off, partners included.
