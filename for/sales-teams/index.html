Software for small sales teams

A small sales team usually pays for four tools that overlap: a contact database, a sequencer, a dialer and a CRM. Credits run out mid-month and seats cost more than the data. These are the tools where our review named a sales team, rep or outbound team as the buyer, with what each fixes and where it stops.

Each card gives the team's problem, whether the tool solves it (Yes or Partly, with the source and reading date), and the plan that fits that team size at the price we read.

Most tools here are affiliate partners: the “Try” buttons may earn us a commission at no extra cost to you. Tools that do not fit this kind of business are left off, partners included.
