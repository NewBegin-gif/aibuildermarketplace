Software for therapists, clinics and salons

Appointment businesses lose money in small leaks: a missed call that books elsewhere, a no-show, an hour of admin after the last client. Therapists, clinics, studios and salons also deal with client records that need care. These are the tools where our review named a practice or salon as a buyer, and what we found about whether they fix the leak.

Each card shows the practice's problem, whether the tool solves it (Yes or Partly, with the source), and the plan that fits a practice of that size at the price we read. Check privacy and health-data rules for your country yourself: we note what the vendor states, not legal advice.

Most tools here are affiliate partners: the “Try” buttons may earn us a commission at no extra cost to you. Tools that do not fit this kind of business are left off, partners included.
