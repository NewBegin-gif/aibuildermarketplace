3 Honest Asana Alternatives (2026) — Project Management That Fits Your Team

Short answer: The best Asana alternatives are ClickUp for an all-in-one with tasks, docs and goals in one place; SmartSuite for no-code flexibility for custom workflows; Process Street for repeatable processes and checklists. Below, what each is best for — and the honest trade-offs.

Asana is clean and well-liked, but teams outgrow it in two directions: some want more power and customization, others want their docs, goals and processes in the same place rather than spread across apps. If Asana feels too rigid — or you're trying to consolidate — these alternatives are worth a look.

Why people leave Asana

  • Pricing climbs as you add seats and premium features.
  • Limited flexibility for teams with unusual or process-heavy workflows.
  • You may still need separate docs, goals or process tools alongside it.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best Asana alternative?

It depends on what you're missing: ClickUp if you want to consolidate tasks, docs and goals; SmartSuite if you need no-code flexibility for custom workflows; Process Street if your work is really a set of repeatable processes.

Is there a cheaper alternative to Asana?

Several of these can work out cheaper depending on team size and plan, and ClickUp in particular tends to offer more for the money. Compare current pricing for your seats and needs.

Which Asana alternative is best for custom workflows?

SmartSuite is built for no-code customization, so it bends to unusual processes more easily than Asana's more opinionated structure.
