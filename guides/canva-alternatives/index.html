Why people leave Canva

Frequently asked questions

What is the best Canva alternative?

There isn't one — it depends on the job. Gamma and Beautiful.ai beat Canva for presentations, AdCreative.ai for high-volume ad creative, and Logome.ai for logos. The point is to use a specialist for the thing you do most.

Is there a free Canva alternative?

Several of these offer free tiers or trials. Free design tools are always limited in exports or features — treat the free plan as a trial and check current limits on each site.

Which is best for presentations?

Gamma and Beautiful.ai both use AI to design slides for you, which makes them faster than building decks by hand in Canva — Gamma leans generative, Beautiful.ai leans structured templates.
