Replace a job, not an entire design workspace

A presentation, a social advertisement and an editable brand template have different acceptance criteria. Before choosing a Canva alternative, identify the output you produce repeatedly and the person who must edit it afterwards. A tool can be useful for that one output without replacing every design task in the business. This shortlist therefore compares Gamma, Beautiful.ai and AdCreative.ai by the work their dossiers describe, rather than declaring one the best general design platform.

There is no verified Canva price baseline in this comparison. We also do not have a measured speed comparison showing that these alternatives are faster than Canva. Start with your existing workflow: the last brief, the edits needed, the export requested and the person who approved it. That gives you a fair trial. Replacing an existing tool should solve a named problem such as deck creation, controlled layouts or producing ad variants, rather than merely offering another AI button.

Gamma: evaluate the draft and its handoff

Gamma's dossier describes turning a prompt or notes into a presentation, document or webpage draft. That makes it relevant when the starting point is information that needs a structure. Give it a fixed brief and retain a copy of the facts you supplied. Inspect the resulting story before polishing the design: the order of the argument, the relationship between a heading and its evidence, and whether a qualification disappeared in the draft.

Our team included Gamma in a presentation experiment on 28 September 2026 with the same investor-deck brief used across the compared tools. The linked review records what happened in that experiment. Treat it as a bounded observation, not proof that every subject or exported deck will behave the same way. In your own trial, use a representative brief and check both the on-screen result and the format your colleagues must receive.

The buying question is how much control the final handoff needs. If a customer expects a conventional deck with exact brand treatment, have the final editor inspect it before approving the subscription. If the output is shared as a presentation or webpage, test that sharing route instead. An attractive browser view does not settle export fidelity, and an export option does not settle how much cleanup your own template will require.

Beautiful.ai: decide whether layout structure helps

Beautiful.ai's dossier positions it around presentation design consistency, with less emphasis on unrestricted pixel-level layout control. That trade-off can be useful when several people need to build decks that look coherent. It can be frustrating when a designer needs an unusual composition or complex animation. Choose a trial deck that contains the awkward slide, rather than only the straightforward title and bullet slides.

Ask the eventual editor to make a realistic revision: replace a chart, add a qualification and change the order of a section. Watch whether the structured layout helps the work or forces a workaround. This is your acceptance exercise, not a result we claim to have measured for your organisation. The important comparison is the total editing journey, including whoever receives the exported presentation after the creator has finished.

Beautiful.ai was also part of our dated presentation experiment. Read that account for the actual scope and results. For buying, keep the commercial question separate: a short burst of deck work and an ongoing team workflow can call for different billing decisions. AdCreative.ai: a different shortlist for ad work

AdCreative.ai belongs here for performance-marketing creative volume, not as an interchangeable presentation maker. Its dossier describes creating variations across sizes and platforms and identifies brand and credit limits as purchasing considerations. Start by writing the campaign brief, required formats and the number of genuinely different concepts. A batch of resized assets is not necessarily a batch of different creative ideas, and more variants do not establish better advertising performance.

Decide how you will approve claims, brand details and visual rights before generating a large batch. Have a responsible person inspect each selected asset. If the campaign problem is the offer, audience or landing page, making more images may leave that problem untouched. The dossier explicitly cautions against buying creative volume when it is not the bottleneck. There are no conversion-lift measurements for AdCreative.ai in this guide, so no uplift is promised.

Use the trial to understand what consumes the allowance and how another brand or colleague would change the purchase. Record the usable assets you actually keep, alongside rejected drafts and the work needed to correct them. That record is an internal comparison for your campaign. Do not turn it into a general claim that one supplier produces better-performing advertisements without running an appropriate campaign test.

Use one brief and two different scorecards

For presentation tools, score factual coverage, narrative structure, revision effort and the final delivery format. For advertising creative, score compliance with the brief, relevant variation, required dimensions and the approval work left to do. Mixing these scorecards produces a misleading overall winner. A useful presentation draft and a useful ad-production workflow can both exist without one replacing the other.

Set pass conditions before opening the trial. A presentation might need all supplied facts, an editable chart and a file a colleague can revise. An ad batch might need the approved offer, specified formats and room for required qualifications. Use your own actual requirements, not a generic number of generated items. Keep the untouched input and the final accepted output so you can explain why the tool earned a place in the workflow.

Price the people and the repeated work

Check whether the proposed plan covers every person who must edit, rather than assuming a shared login is an acceptable team arrangement. Compare the billing term, the recorded allowance and any brand limits relevant to the product. A free allowance can be useful for evaluation without covering recurring production. Do not assume that a one-time credit grant renews every month, or that a trial is a permanent free plan.

Keep an exit example

Before making the tool part of a client workflow, keep a completed example outside the account and have the next person open it. Check what remains editable and what requires the original service. Decide who owns the source material and where approved assets live. If the tool is used only for drafts, make that role explicit so colleagues do not treat generated text or images as automatically approved.

Stay with your current arrangement if the trial does not remove the problem you identified. Choose Gamma for a trial centred on turning information into a structured draft, Beautiful.ai for a trial centred on presentation layout consistency, and AdCreative.ai for a trial centred on producing ad variants. Those are criteria for an evaluation, supported by the dossiers. Your own accepted output, review effort and billing fit decide whether switching is worthwhile.
