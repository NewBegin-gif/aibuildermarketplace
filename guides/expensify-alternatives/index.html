Short answer: The best Expensify alternatives are Navan for travel and expense together in one platform; Dext for receipt capture and clean data for bookkeeping and accountants; Shoeboxed for receipt scanning with human verification and tax-ready records. Below, what each is best for — and the honest trade-offs.

Expensify popularized scan-and-submit expenses, but teams increasingly want something cheaper, less fiddly, or part of a broader finance workflow. Whether you need receipt capture, travel-and-expense, or clean bookkeeping data, these alternatives are worth comparing.

Why people leave Expensify

  • Pricing and the per-user model can add up for small teams.
  • Some teams find the workflow fiddly for what they actually need.
  • You may want expenses tied into travel, cards or bookkeeping — not standalone.
