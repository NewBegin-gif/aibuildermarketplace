The Free Tier Index

153 of the 478 tools we hand-researched have a real free tier (32%). Here's every one, what you actually get free, and the ones where free is a doorway to a paid ceiling.

A tool is listed here only if our research found a genuinely free plan, version or tier. The sentence under each name is what that free plan includes, straight from our dossier. A ⚠ badge names what can start costing money: fees after a free allowance, usage meters, per-seat pricing or paid add-ons. No badge only means we found none of those in our notes, not that the free plan fits your volume: check the limit in the sentence.
