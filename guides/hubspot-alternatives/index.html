4 Honest HubSpot Alternatives (2026) — Lighter CRMs Your Team Will Actually Use

Short answer: The best HubSpot alternatives are folk for a lightweight, modern CRM that feels effortless; Keap for CRM plus built-in automation for small businesses; Salesflare for automatic data entry so the CRM stays current. Below, what each is best for — and the honest trade-offs.

HubSpot is genuinely powerful — but the bill climbs quickly once you need the paid hubs, and it's heavy for a small team. The best CRM is the one your team actually updates. If HubSpot feels like too much, these lighter options are easier to live in.

Why people leave HubSpot

  • Costs rise sharply as contacts grow and you add paid hubs.
  • It's complex — small teams use a fraction of it.
  • You can end up paying for capability you'll never switch on.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best simple alternative to HubSpot?

For a small team that wants something they'll actually maintain, folk and Salesflare are strong picks — both prioritise low friction over feature count. Keap is better if you also want marketing automation built in.

Is there a cheaper CRM than HubSpot?

Yes — most of the tools above cost less than HubSpot's paid hubs, especially as your contact count grows. Compare each on current pricing for your team size before deciding.

Which HubSpot alternative is best for automation?

Keap combines CRM with marketing and sales automation in one tool, which makes it a natural fit if automation is the reason you outgrew a basic CRM.
