5 Honest Mailchimp Alternatives (2026) — Cheaper Email

Short answer: The best Mailchimp alternatives are ActiveCampaign for advanced automation + light CRM; GetResponse for an all-in-one with landing pages and webinars; Brevo for paying per email sent instead of per contact. Below, what each is best for — and the honest trade-offs.

Mailchimp made email marketing easy for a generation of small businesses. But once your list grows, the bill climbs fast — and you pay for contacts even when they never open a thing. If the pricing or the feature paywalls have you looking around, these are the alternatives we'd actually move to.

Why people leave Mailchimp

  • Pricing scales steeply with list size — and counts unengaged contacts you'd rather not pay for.
  • Useful automation and reporting sit behind higher tiers.
  • Interface and plan changes have frustrated long-time users.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best free Mailchimp alternative?

Several alternatives offer a genuine free tier — Brevo (free monthly sends) and Kit are good starting points. Check each provider's current free limits before committing, as they change.

Is ActiveCampaign better than Mailchimp?

For automation and CRM-style follow-up, ActiveCampaign is generally more capable. Mailchimp is simpler for basic newsletters. The right pick depends on whether you need branching automations or just broadcasts.

Which Mailchimp alternative is cheapest?

Tools that bill per email sent (like Brevo) are often cheaper than per-contact pricing once you have a large but lightly-engaged list, though Brevo's send tiers cap stored contacts too. We hold no verified reading of Mailchimp's own price ladder, so there is no Mailchimp baseline on this page: take your list size, monthly sends and the automations you use, and price Mailchimp's current plan against each alternative's plan that actually covers that.
