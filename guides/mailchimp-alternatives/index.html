7 Honest Mailchimp Alternatives (2026) — Cheaper Email

Short answer: The best Mailchimp alternatives are ActiveCampaign for advanced automation + light CRM; GetResponse for an all-in-one with landing pages and webinars; Brevo for paying per email sent instead of per contact. Below, what each is best for — and the honest trade-offs.

Mailchimp made email marketing easy for a generation of small businesses. But once your list grows, the bill climbs fast — and you pay for contacts even when they never open a thing. If the pricing or the feature paywalls have you looking around, these are the alternatives we'd actually move to.

Why people leave Mailchimp

  • Pricing scales steeply with list size — and counts unengaged contacts you'd rather not pay for.
  • Useful automation and reporting sit behind higher tiers.
  • Interface and plan changes have frustrated long-time users.

The best Mailchimp alternatives

1A
ActiveCampaign
Best for: advanced automation + light CRM

Marketing automation and lifecycle email with a light CRM attached. Priced by contact count from about $15/month on Starter, with CRM pipelines, SMS and extra seats sold as add-ons — the sticker price sits well below what a real automation setup costs.

Visit ActiveCampaign →
2G
GetResponse
Best for: an all-in-one with landing pages and webinars

Email marketing with landing pages, automation and webinars in one suite, priced by contact count. Starter is €16/month (€13.12 billed annually) and allows a single automation workflow; unlimited workflows start on Marketer at €54. The better rates need long commitments.

Visit GetResponse →
3B
Brevo
Best for: paying per email sent instead of per contact

Email, SMS and a light CRM, priced by monthly send volume rather than contact count — cheaper for a large list you email selectively. Free forever at 300 emails a day with unlimited contacts; paid from about $9/month. Model your real send volume if you mail frequently.

Visit Brevo →
4K
Kit
Best for: creators and newsletter-first audiences

Email marketing built for creators (formerly ConvertKit). Grow your list with landing pages and forms, then automate newsletters and sell digital products from one place; a beta Kit MCP connects AI assistants like Claude directly to your account. Recent additions: a new landing-page editor, Dynamic Content, Newsletter Sponsorships, and App Store integrations on paid plans — SMS via SlickText, Patreon member sync (tier and support data in automations), Luma/Eventbrite event sync, and two-way HubSpot CRM sync on Pro.

Visit Kit →
5C
Campaign Monitor
Best for: design-led teams that care about templates

Email campaigns with strong templates and client-friendly management for brands and agencies. Priced per contact rather than per send: Essentials at 10,000 contacts runs about $89/month, so dormant subscribers bill like active ones. Automation depth is lighter than dedicated platforms.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best free Mailchimp alternative?

Several alternatives offer a genuine free tier — Brevo (free monthly sends) and Kit are good starting points. Check each provider's current free limits before committing, as they change.

Is ActiveCampaign better than Mailchimp?

For automation and CRM-style follow-up, ActiveCampaign is generally more capable. Mailchimp is simpler for basic newsletters. The right pick depends on whether you need branching automations or just broadcasts.

Which Mailchimp alternative is cheapest?

Tools that bill per email sent (like Brevo) are often cheaper than per-contact pricing once you have a large but lightly-engaged list. Model your real volume before deciding — we don't publish fixed prices because they change by region and plan.
