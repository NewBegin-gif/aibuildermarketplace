The Per-Seat Tax

126 of the tools we researched charge per seat, so your bill scales with headcount, not value. Estimate the real cost as your team grows, then see exactly which tools do it.

Cost today$75/mo
Cost at target size$375/mo
Annual difference at target team size$3,600/yr

A simple model: price per seat times seats. It compares one year at today's size with one year at your target size; it is not the total of the years in between. It leaves out base fees, minimum seat counts, seats included in a plan and tax, so it is not a quote for any of the tools below: read each tool's pricing note. Per-seat pricing isn't automatically bad (it's predictable), but the advertised price is a floor: plan for the seats you will need, not the seats you have.
