The Quote-Only Club

71 of the tools we researched hide their pricing behind a sales call. Here's who, why quote-only pricing exists, and how to handle it so you don't overpay.

How to handle quote-only pricing

  • Ask for the floor first. "What's the entry price for a team our size?" — before any demo.
  • Get it in writing, with the renewal rate. Quote-only often hides a steep year-two increase.
  • Name the alternatives. Mentioning a public-priced competitor is the fastest way to a real number.
  • Watch the annual lock. The quote usually assumes an annual (or multi-year) commitment.
  • Right-size the seats. Quote-only vendors price on your headcount — don't buy seats you won't use.
