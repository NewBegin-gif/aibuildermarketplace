5 Honest Salesforce Alternatives (2026) — Simpler CRMs Without the Enterprise Tax

Short answer: The best Salesforce alternatives are Close for fast inside-sales with calling, email and SMS built in; folk for a lightweight, modern CRM that feels effortless; Pipedrive for a visual pipeline that's easy to run day to day. Below, what each is best for — and the honest trade-offs.

Salesforce is the most powerful CRM on the market — and priced, configured and contracted like it. For a startup or SMB, that power often means paying to set up features you'll never use, on a tool too heavy for your team to keep updated. These alternatives give you a CRM that actually gets used, without the enterprise tax.

Why people leave Salesforce

  • Pricing and add-ons climb fast, and real setups often need a consultant.
  • It's built for large, complex sales orgs — overkill for most teams.
  • Adoption suffers when the tool is heavier than the job it's doing.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best simple Salesforce alternative?

For a small team that wants something they'll maintain, folk and Salesflare keep friction low, Pipedrive is the easiest visual pipeline, and Close is best if your team sells by phone and email daily. The right pick depends on how you actually sell.

Is there a cheaper CRM than Salesforce?

Yes — all of the above typically cost less than a configured Salesforce setup, especially once you factor in setup and admin. Compare each on current pricing for your team size.

Which Salesforce alternative is best for inside sales?

Close, because calling, email and SMS are built in, so reps spend time selling rather than logging activity — which is also what drives adoption.
