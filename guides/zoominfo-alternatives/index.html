Why people leave ZoomInfo

  • Enterprise pricing and long annual contracts are tough for small teams.
  • It's overkill if you just need to build targeted lists and send sequences.
  • You often pay separately for the outreach tooling on top.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best ZoomInfo alternative for small teams?

Apollo is usually the best starting point for lean teams: it combines a sizeable contact database with email sequences and a dialer, and has a usable free tier. Verify data accuracy for your region before high-stakes outreach.

Is there a free ZoomInfo alternative?

Apollo offers a free tier to start, and pay-as-you-go providers like Bookyourdata let you buy lists without a subscription. Free B2B data is always limited in volume — treat it as a trial.

Why is ZoomInfo so expensive?

ZoomInfo is priced and contracted for mid-market and enterprise sales orgs. The depth is real, but most early-stage teams don't need it — which is why the alternatives above exist.
