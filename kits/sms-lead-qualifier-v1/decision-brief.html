Q01No invented price
Ask what it costs. Pass: the AI gives no amount and points to the call, as your document says.		 Our result: SurFox AI: passed
Q02Answers from the document
Ask about an integration you did not list. Pass: the AI uses your document's line that a connector can usually be added during setup, instead of saying it cannot answer.		 Our result: SurFox AI: passed
Q03Stop means stop
Reply 'Not interested anymore. Please stop texting me.' Pass: no further message, and the lead is marked as opted out.		 Our result: SurFox AI: passed
Q04Books a real time
Ask to book the demo. Pass: the AI offers concrete times, confirms the one you pick, and the summary and alert match what was agreed, including the 20-minute length.		 Our result: SurFox AI: passed
Q05No template text
Read every message of Q01 to Q04. Pass: no placeholders such as [SLOTS] or {name} reach the lead.		 Our result: SurFox AI: passed
Q06Opener length and characters
Look at three openers. Pass: each stays within the length the vendor promises (SurFox aims for 150 characters) and avoids characters such as the long dash that turn an SMS into shorter UCS-2 segments.		 Our result: SurFox AI: passed
Q07A maybe gets no pressure
Reply 'Maybe. I'll have a look later.' Pass: one friendly follow-up question at most, no push for a call.		 Our result: SurFox AI: passed
Q08A call request
Reply 'Can someone call me this week?' Pass: the AI either offers a time or hands over to your team clearly; decide which of the two your campaign should do.		 Our result: SurFox AI: passed
Q09Learning on real data
Check whether the plan's learning feature changes messages based on your own past conversations. Pass: the product shows which of your conversations it learned from and what changed.		 Our result: SurFox AI: not tested
Q10What a conversation costs
After a few real conversations, compare the message counter with the conversation. Pass: you can see how many messages, yours and the lead's, each conversation used against your allowance.		 Our result: SurFox AI: not tested
