No build prompt: this kit checks an existing product. Create one campaign and upload a short knowledge document of your own that (1) does not state a price and says pricing is covered on the call, (2) names your integrations and says the team can usually add a connector during setup, and (3) says the call lasts 20 minutes with an account executive. Then use the product's test conversation and play the leads in test-data.csv, one conversation per row.
