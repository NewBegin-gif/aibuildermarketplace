List your SaaS

Free during beta. You write the listing. We publish our own research beside it — the same research behind the Honest Software Atlas. That is the whole idea: a page a buyer can trust because it was not written entirely by you.

What you get

  • Your pitch, with our independent research next to it. On the same page, under your own description: the pricing we read ourselves on your live pricing page with the date we read it, the cost-trap flags each carrying the sentence they came from, who your product is genuinely for and who should skip it. You cannot edit that part — which is exactly why it is worth having.
  • A page you can actually link to. Not a scraped directory entry, but a page that carries independent scrutiny. That is a credibility asset: it says you are willing to be looked at closely.
  • Reader reviews on your page — including critical ones. That is what makes a positive one mean something. Readers can write one straight from your listing.
  • Your product in front of a reviewer who actually reads it. We publish honest reviews and 300 hand-researched dossiers. Your listing is read by the same person who writes those.
  • An exclusive reader deal gets shown prominently — with its real conditions, including when the normal rate returns. Offers we cannot state precisely, we do not publish.

If we have not researched your product yet, your listing says so plainly rather than implying we have. The products we find genuinely interesting end up in a full independent review with a pricing dossier, and sometimes a review video. We do not promise that to every listing, because we would not be able to keep the promise.

Being straight with you

The audience is small and growing. If you are looking for a flood of traffic today, this is the wrong place and we would rather say so now than have you find out later.

Your link is nofollow — that is what Google requires for anything a vendor submits about themselves. So do not do this for SEO. Do it for a page that says clearly what your product does, on a site that also publishes what it does badly.

We check your pricing against your live page. If the numbers do not match, we ask you before publishing.

Why this is worth having: we say "skip it" when that is the honest answer, we never invent prices, and we do not run fake urgency. Listings that break those rules come down. That is exactly why being listed here carries more weight than a directory that accepts everyone. Featured placement may become paid later — the free tier stays free.

Our research on the page stays exactly as it is — you are adding your words next to it, not replacing it. Claiming also removes the noindex we keep on research-only pages.
