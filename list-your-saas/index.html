What a Founding partner page includes

Your full product page

Logo, up to five screenshots, your long description, a demo video and your reader deal, laid out the way a buyer reads.

Our pricing check beside it

We read your live pricing page and publish what we found with the date: plans, units, renewal and cost conditions. It is honest, so buyers trust it.

A hands-on paid review

We test your product with access you provide and publish it on your listing page, labelled. Or write your own, labelled “Written by the vendor”. Your paid review appears only on your own marketplace listing page. It is never added to our editorial reviews, best-of lists, comparisons or scores.

A sponsored YouTube video

A demo on our channel, marked “Includes paid promotion”. It links back to your page.

A Sponsored spot in your category

Your card at the top of your marketplace category, labelled Sponsored, with a spotlight we write from sources.

Live within one business day

Your full page goes live within one business day after payment; the pricing check, spotlight and paid review follow within 10 business days. Basic listings wait for our weekly Monday batch.

A client report

After 30 days and every quarter: visits, clicks and video views, dated and bot-filtered. What we cannot measure, we mark as “not measurable”.

Fixed price for a year

$100 for 12 months for the first 20 companies. After that the plan is $250 a year.

Being straight with you

Our audience is small and growing. We publish the real figures on the plans page, dated and bot-filtered, so you can judge it yourself. If you need a flood of traffic today, this is not the place.

A paid review can be critical. You can correct facts before publication, not the verdict. Once paid, it is published. We refund only if we cannot deliver.
