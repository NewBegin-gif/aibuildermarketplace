Bread & Butter

Lead intelligence with server-side tracking of the full visitor journey. · Marketing

⚠️ Listing written by AIBM at the vendor's request, not an independent review.

Also read: our independent Bread & Butter review — researched by AIBM, not the vendor.

Bread & Butter tracks the whole visitor journey server-side, starting from the first visit rather than from the moment someone converts. When an email does come in, it enriches and scores that profile and can start nurturing it, so the history before the form submission is not lost.

Pricing is per website, which is the detail to plan around if you run more than one: AI Enabled Pro is $49 a month per site, with 5,000 lead profiles. There is a fully functional 30-day trial that drops to a free tier afterwards rather than cutting you off — 200 lead profiles, unlimited traffic.

Our independent review covers where the per-site model starts to bite and what the profile ceilings mean in practice.
