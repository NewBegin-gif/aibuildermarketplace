Cloudways

Managed hosting on top of DigitalOcean, Vultr, Linode, AWS or Google Cloud. · IT & Productivity

⚠️ Listing written by AIBM at the vendor's request, not an independent review.

Also read: our independent Cloudways review — researched by AIBM, not the vendor.

Cloudways rents you a server at DigitalOcean, Vultr, Linode, AWS or Google Cloud and manages the layer on top: the stack, caching, staging, cloning, backups and 24/7 platform support, all from one dashboard. You get the control of a VPS without running the VPS yourself.

The billing model is the part worth understanding. It is hourly and post-paid — invoiced in the first week of the following month — with no annual contract to sign and no lock-in. That makes it unusually easy to test something for a week and stop. There is a 3-day free trial that does not ask for a card.

Our independent review sets out what the managed layer does and does not cover, and where the real cost sits once you add servers.
