Why we run this marketplace

Software, at its best, is quiet magic: it gives you your evening back, takes the boring part off your plate, and lets a two-person team do what used to take ten. We genuinely believe everyone should get to discover the tools that enrich their work and give them time back — not just the ones with the biggest ad budgets. That’s why listing is free during beta: small honest products deserve shelf space next to the giants. Our only conditions are the ones we hold ourselves to — real pricing, no invented claims, and readers get the last word. Critical reviews included.

What a listing here is, and what it is not

It is a permanent page that carries independent scrutiny, which is why it is worth linking to. It is not an SEO play: outbound listing links are nofollow ugc, because that is what Google requires for anything a vendor submits about themselves. And our audience is still modest and growing — we would rather say that here than have you find out afterwards.
