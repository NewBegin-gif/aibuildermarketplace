In Seller Snap’s own words

Seller Snap is an AI-powered repricing platform for Amazon & Walmart sellers built on Game Theory, not rules. Where rule-based repricers execute pre-written conditions, Seller Snap's algorithm models competitor behaviour patterns and selects the optimal price for each listing independently, in real time.

The core positioning is profit-first. Rather than chasing the lowest price to win the Buy Box, the AI is designed to avoid unnecessary price wars and actively look for opportunities to raise price when competitive dynamics allow. Sellers set minimum and maximum price boundaries; the algorithm handles the decision-making within those bounds, with no rule configuration required.

Seller Snap includes DataHub, a centralized analytics platform covering profitability by SKU, ASIN, brand, or fulfillment channel, with revenue, margin, fees, and Buy Box share in one view. The Competition Dashboard surfaces competitor behaviour across the catalogue: Buy Box win rates, FBA/FBM split, and repricing frequency.

The platform supports repricing presets with conditional triggers (inventory age, time of day, competitor presence), bulk strategy management via CSV, and integrations with InventoryLab, SkuVault, SellerCloud, and others.

Category: E-commerce / Amazon + Walmart Repricing Software. Built for new and established high-volume Amazon sellers. 15-day free trial, no credit card required.
