Seller Snap

AI repricer and seller analytics for Amazon and Walmart. · E-commerce

⚠️ Vendor-submitted listing — written by the vendor, not by AIBM. Our independent, researched dossiers live in the Honest Software Atlas. Reader reviews below are moderated for spam only; critical reviews get published too.

Also read: our independent Seller Snap review — researched by AIBM, not the vendor.

An AI repricer for Amazon and Walmart sellers, built around what the vendor calls game-theory repricing: instead of racing competitors to the lowest price, it aims at Buy Box share. It ships with min/max profit calculation, seller analytics, and imports from tools like InventoryLab and SkuVault.

Pricing follows capacity rather than features — every tier lists the same feature groups, and what you pay is set by your total SKU count in Seller Central and your number of Seller IDs. Starter is $100/month on annual billing (annual only); the cheapest month-to-month entry is $250. There is a 15-day trial that does not ask for a card.

Worth knowing before you sign: Amazon already includes a rule-based repricer with the Professional selling plan, so test that first if your need is simply matching the competitive price above your cost. Our review states the caps and the annual-commitment terms plainly.
