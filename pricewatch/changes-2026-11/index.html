Method and limits

We take monthly snapshots of public pricing pages. A change belongs here when the snapshots show a different amount for the same plan, currency and billing basis and the vendor’s live page confirms the new amount. We use regular prices and exclude introductory offers. The table is ordered by absolute percentage change.

Each price changed sometime between its dated captures. The captures show when we saw each price and cannot establish when a customer first paid the new amount. Open a row’s evidence for the exact excerpts, the live check time and any source that corroborates the old price.
