Pricing pages: what we compared in September 2026

Our monthly snapshot compared the pricing pages of 487 tools with last month's snapshot. 330 pages could be read in both months. On 98 of them the amounts shown differed. That is a candidate list, not a list of price changes: a currency switcher, a regional page, an expanded plan table or a changed example can move the amounts without any price changing.

  • 172 pages showed the same amounts both months (page text changed or not)
  • 98 pages showed different amounts (candidates, checked by hand before anything is published)
  • 1 pages stopped showing amounts (often a switch to "request a quote")
  • 13 pages started showing amounts
  • 46 pages showed no amounts in either month
  • 91 pages could not be compared (fetch failed or only the homepage was readable)
Method: monthly automated snapshots of every monitored pricing page; we compare the amounts found in the page text. A changed page text on its own is not counted as movement. No invented numbers: confirmed changes land in the Price Watch registry with evidence. How we review →
