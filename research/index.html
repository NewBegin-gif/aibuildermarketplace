AIBM RESEARCH

Original data studies, verifiable by design

Every report is computed from our hand-researched corpus and ships with the raw data (CC BY 4.0), so journalists and researchers can re-run the analysis instead of taking our word for it. The corpus grows month by month (238 tools in the July 2026 capture, 479 dossiers today), so each report names the population, period and denominator it was computed on rather than one shared corpus size.

Report 01 — first published July 2026, recomputed 2026-09-26. SaaS Pricing Transparency Report 2026: the sticker price is the whole story for only 20% of 479 researched tools. Usage meters, per-seat scaling, hidden price lists and renewal jumps — quantified, with evidence.
Hands-on test — run 2026-09-26. One booking app, three AI builders: Lovable, Replit and Emergent on the same prompt and free plans, 18 checks each. Two of three first versions let ten people book eight spots.
Hands-on test — run 2026-09-28. One pitch deck, four AI presentation tools: Gamma, Beautiful.ai, Prezi and Canva on the same eleven facts, 15 checks each. One deck added nine numbers we never gave it.
Hands-on test — run 2026-09-30. Active Intelligence 3.0: Wavelength on a brand-new ActiveCampaign account: ten tasks on a new trial account, each checked against our own pages. No invented numbers in the first campaign; one unsupported sentence in the second.
