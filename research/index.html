1 in 30 SaaS Pricing Pages Changes Price Each Month (2026)
We capture the pricing page of every tool we review on the 2nd of each month. Across 567 page-pairs from July 2026 to September 2026, 19 changed a price: 8 up, 3 down, 7 rebuilt, 1 withdrawn. Every one with the literal lines from both captures.
Report 02 · 2026-09-01
Who do AI engines cite for software pricing?
We asked ChatGPT and Perplexity what 20 business tools cost and logged every source they cited. 46% of citations point at the vendor's own page, and 0 of 40 answers cite nothing at all.
Report 03 · 2026-08-23
65 SaaS vendors ship MCP. 38 won't say which plan you need.
We checked 363 business-software vendors for a first-party MCP server. 65 have one. Of those, 38 state no plan or price for it anywhere in their own docs.
