How to cite

Cite the edition, so a reader lands on the numbers you quoted even after a newer edition appears. Each study card has a copy button; the form is:

AIBuilder Marketplace Research (Daniel Haket), “Panel: software terms, month by month”, edition 2026-10. https://aibuildermarketplace.com/research/software-terms-panel/ (data: https://aibuildermarketplace.com/research/software-terms-panel/data/2026-10/manifest.json). CC BY 4.0.

Reuse

CC BY 4.0: you may republish and adapt with attribution and a note of what you changed. Our own data, analysis, tables and charts. Quoted vendor text and screenshots of vendor pages remain the vendor's; we reproduce them as evidence.

Corrections and vendor responses

Write to the address above with the study, the edition and the claim. We check it against the evidence; if we were wrong, the study gets a dated correction note and the previous version stays available from its editions table. Vendors are welcome to correct facts about their own product, with a source; they do not see studies before publication and have no say over conclusions.

Independence

The research is self-funded from affiliate commissions on some of the tools we cover. Each study states which of its subjects pay us, whether we used free plans or trials, and any contest or campaign the page is entered in. Partners and non-partners are counted by the same rules.
