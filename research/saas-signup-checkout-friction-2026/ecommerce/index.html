E-commerce apps: Signup & Checkout Friction Scorecard 2026

This scorecard is part of the SaaS Signup & Checkout Friction Report 2026. It lists the 68 e-commerce apps we signed up for and tested hands-on, with what we met at each step from sign-up to cancelling.

Asked for a payment card before the free plan or trial would start8 of 55
Required a phone number at sign-up0 of 40
Showed nothing until we connected another account or verified a domain0 of 27
Showed a checkout total above the advertised price0 of 34
Had no cancel button we could find in the product1 of 9
Gave no usable result within our test session4 of 53
