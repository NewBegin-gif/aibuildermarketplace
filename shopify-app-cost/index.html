What Shopify apps actually cost at your volume

Almost none of these apps charge by the seat. They charge by the unit of work — and the unit is usually the thing the app exists to produce more of. Type your own numbers below and the ladder does the rest.

We keep dossiers on 60 Shopify apps and read every price ourselves. Counting them up: 35 of the 60 meter on a countable unit rather than a flat monthly fee, and only 22 of the 60 publish what an extra unit costs. That second number is the one that catches people. StoreFAQ charges $0.0159 for every FAQ page view past 500 — on an app whose entire job is to make those pages rank and get read. AskNest sells 500 AI replies for $4.99 and never says, anywhere on the listing, what happens to reply 501.

So this is not a pricing table. It is the ladder each vendor actually published, with your own volume run through it. Where a vendor does not publish an overage rate, the calculator says so and stops — it does not guess, because a derived number is how you end up quoting $66.67 for a plan that costs $70.

How this was built

Every ladder here was read on the vendor's own listing or pricing page on the date shown beside it, and typed in by hand rather than scraped — a pattern-match over our own price notes found 13 of the 35 metered apps, so the machine was not good enough. Nothing is derived: where a vendor publishes a monthly figure we use it, and where the ladder stops we say the ladder stops. Prices change, sometimes fast — one app on our list raised all three tiers between the 24th and the 25th of August with the same feature lists — so treat every figure here as dated evidence and confirm the current one before you commit.
