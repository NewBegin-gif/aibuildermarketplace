How the paid review works

We review it

You give us access, we test hands-on and write it. It appears only on your own listing page, under the label “Paid review: [your company] paid us for this review. They could correct facts, not the verdict.” Critical points stay in. Once paid, it is published.

You write it

Your own text about your product, shown on your listing page under the label “Written by the vendor” and marked Sponsored. We do not call it a review, because it is not one.

Your paid review appears only on your own marketplace listing page. It is never added to our editorial reviews, best-of lists, comparisons or scores. It never appears in our picks or the Atlas either, and never counts towards any of them. Listing pages carry noindex, links in paid blocks carry rel="sponsored", and paid reviews get no star rating or review markup.

Questions

How do I pay?

Choose Founding partner in the listing form. Right after submitting you pay with PayPal (card or PayPal balance, USD). Prefer an invoice? Email us and we send a PayPal invoice instead. Your page goes live within one business day after payment; the pricing check, spotlight and paid review follow within 10 business days.

Where does the paid review appear?

Your paid review appears only on your own marketplace listing page. It is never added to our editorial reviews, best-of lists, comparisons or scores.

Can the paid review be negative?

Yes, it can contain critical points. You can correct factual errors before publication, not the verdict. Once paid, it is published. We refund only if we cannot deliver.

Does paying change your editorial review or ranking?

No. Our editorial review is the same either way, and sponsors never appear in picks, best-of lists, comparisons or as a tie-break.

What happens after 12 months?

We ask whether you want to renew. If you do not, your page returns to a Basic listing; our research stays as it is.

I already have a free listing. What changes?

Nothing is taken away. Listings that went live before the new plans keep everything they show today. You can upgrade at any time.
