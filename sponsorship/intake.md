# Sponsorship intake checklist

Collect these items by email for the sponsored listing, spotlight and video.

- [ ] Product logo.
- [ ] Three to five product screenshots or other images we may publish.
- [ ] Optional demo video URL.
- [ ] Product facts to cover, with a source URL for each fact.
- [ ] Main contact’s name and email address.
- [ ] Desired go-live date (YYYY-MM-DD), if you have a launch or campaign date.

Send the complete intake at least five business days before your chosen date. We publish the vendor block and Sponsored spotlight on that date at no extra cost. Without a fixed date, the standard delivery window applies.
