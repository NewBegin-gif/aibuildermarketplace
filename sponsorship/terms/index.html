Terms for Founding partner and paid add-ons

Editorial independence

Payment has no influence on our editorial reviews, verdicts, scores, picks, best-of places or comparisons, and we do not sell links. We accept corrections only for factual errors and only with a source.

Paid review

The Founding partner plan includes one paid review, in one of two forms. It appears only on your own marketplace listing page, never in our editorial reviews, lists, comparisons, scores or the Atlas, and it never counts towards them.

We review it. You give us access and we test hands-on. The review is ours: it can contain critical points, and they stay in. Before publication you may point out factual errors with a source; you cannot change the verdict. It is published under the label “Paid review: [your company] paid us for this review. They could correct facts, not the verdict.” Once paid, it is published; we do not withdraw it because the verdict disappoints.

You write it. Your own text, published under the label “Written by the vendor” and marked Sponsored. We do not call it a review. We check stated facts and prices against your live pages and do not publish claims we cannot verify.

Links in paid blocks carry rel="sponsored", listing pages carry noindex, and paid content gets no star rating or review markup.

Delivery and reporting

Your Founding partner listing page (your logo, screenshots, description, demo video and reader deal) goes live within one business day after payment.

Our pricing check, your Sponsored spotlight and your paid review follow within 10 business days after payment and a complete intake (for a review we do: after we have working access). If you give us a launch or campaign date at least five business days ahead, the spotlight goes live on that date at no extra cost.

We publish one sponsored YouTube video, marked “Includes paid promotion”, within 30 days after payment and a complete intake.

Your plan runs for 12 months from publication. We send your first report 30 days after your page goes live, then every quarter. We report actual figures with dates and sources, filter out bot and invalid traffic where identifiable, and mark any figure we cannot measure “not measurable”.

We do not guarantee reach, rankings, leads or views.

Product audit report ($50 add-on)

A private PDF delivered by email within 5 business days after payment. It is never published and has no effect on any review, score or placement.

Price

Founding partner costs $100 for 12 months for the first 20 companies; the price is fixed for that year. After the first 20, the plan costs $250 a year. Basic listings are free.

Payment and refunds

Payment is due in advance in USD by PayPal payment link; on request by PayPal invoice or Wise bank transfer. Submitting the listing form does not charge you; your order starts when we have matched your payment. We refund your payment if we fail to deliver what is described here.

Eligibility and removal

Open to software products with a listing or review on AIBuilder Marketplace. We may decline an order (if you already paid, we refund in full), or remove a paid placement that breaks these rules (with a pro-rata refund).
