Sponsorship terms

Editorial independence

Sponsorship has no influence on reviews, verdicts, scores or editorial picks. We accept corrections only for factual errors and only with a source.

Delivery and reporting

  • Your vendor block and Sponsored spotlight go live on your launch or campaign date at no extra cost if you send the complete intake at least five business days beforehand.
  • Without a fixed date, we publish your premium listing and Sponsored spotlight within 5 business days after payment and a complete intake. Your premium listing runs for 12 months from publication.
  • We publish one sponsored YouTube video within 30 days after payment and a complete intake.
  • We send your first report 30 days after your premium listing goes live, then every quarter during the sponsorship. We report actual figures with dates and sources, filter out bot and invalid traffic where identifiable, and mark any figure we cannot measure “not measurable”.

We do not guarantee reach, rankings, leads or views.

Payment and refunds

Payment is due in advance in USD by PayPal invoice or Wise bank transfer. We will refund your payment if we fail to deliver the package as agreed.

Eligibility and removal

Sponsorship is open to tools that already have a listing or review on AIBuilder Marketplace. We may decline a sponsorship or remove a sponsored placement.
