🔄 After 2+ years with Bitvavo, I recently explored 5 major alternatives. Here's what I discovered:

**Why the switch?**
• API limitations hindering automated strategies
• Need for advanced trading features
• Seeking better institutional-grade tools

**Top findings:**

🚀 **Binance** - Superior API (1200 calls/min), extensive features, lower fees. Best for active traders.

🔒 **Kraken** - Exceptional security (95% cold storage), professional-grade tools. Ideal for large holdings.

🏢 **Coinbase Pro** - Enterprise infrastructure, regulatory compliance, seamless UX. Perfect for institutional needs.

🇪🇺 **Bitpanda** - European-focused, SEPA integration, multi-asset platform. Great for EU investors.

👥 **eToro** - Social trading innovation, copy trading features. Excellent for learning.

**Key lessons:**
✅ No single platform is perfect
✅ Diversifying across exchanges reduces risk
✅ API reliability matters more than low fees
✅ Security should never be compromised

70% Binance (active trading)
25% Kraken (secure storage)
5% Others (testing new features)
