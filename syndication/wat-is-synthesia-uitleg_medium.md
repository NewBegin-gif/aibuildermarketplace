## The Honest Truth: Limitations I've Encountered

Let me be transparent about the challenges:

**The Uncanny Valley Effect**: Sometimes, especially in longer videos, you notice the avatar isn't quite human. Subtle movements and expressions can feel mechanical.

**Emotional Range**: While the avatars are impressive, they can't convey complex emotions like a human actor. They work best for informational content rather than emotional storytelling.

**Cost Considerations**: Quality comes at a price. For small businesses or individual creators, the subscription cost might be prohibitive.

**Creative Limitations**: You're working within the platform's constraints. If you need highly creative or artistic videos, traditional production might still be better.
