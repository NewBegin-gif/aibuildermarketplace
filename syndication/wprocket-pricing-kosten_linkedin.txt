Spent time this week breaking down WP Rocket's pricing structure for anyone managing multiple WordPress sites professionally. A few things worth sharing 👇

💡 All three pricing tiers (Single, Plus, Infinite) include identical features — you're only paying for site license count, not functionality. No feature-gating tricks.

💰 Single: ~$59/yr (1 site) | Plus: ~$119/yr (3 sites) | Infinite: ~$299/yr (unlimited sites)

📊 The cost-per-site math shifts fast — if you're managing 5+ client sites, Infinite becomes significantly cheaper per site than buying individual licenses.

⏱️ The real ROI isn't just caching performance — it's setup time. Minimal configuration needed to get solid PageSpeed improvements, which matters if you bill by project rather than hour.

🔄 One thing to plan for: it's an annual subscription, not one-time. Renewal discounts often apply, but budget for full price as a safety net.

✅ 14-day refund window gives room to test real-world impact before fully committing.

For freelancers and agencies managing multiple WordPress builds, this is less "nice-to-have tool" and more of a scalable business cost that pays for itself quickly.
