Method and limitations

We pair vendor wording and its reading date with an observation on the named plan and test date. The unit is a recorded claim check, not a vendor; several checks may concern one tool. Contradicted means the claim and observation describe a comparable quantity but differ in this test; buyer direction is recorded separately. Not comparable and not testable are not contradictions. Wording events mean the original wording was absent after a successful source read; they do not establish intent. Failed reads are not changes. The watch event ledger counts detections, not necessarily unique claims. Price Watch uses paired monthly captures and human-reviewed changes, including restructuring and withdrawals. Dates mark observations or detection, not the exact time a vendor edited a page. We do not measure announcements, intent, all features, all plans, paid production use or the software market. Selection reflects our tool catalogue and available trials/free accounts; these tests use small synthetic datasets. Exit loss refers only to the tested route and records, not complete portability; without file inspection it is unknown. No percentages are reported, including for groups with n below 20. These results cannot be extrapolated to the market.
