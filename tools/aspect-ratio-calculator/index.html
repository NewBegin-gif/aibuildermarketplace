Common aspect ratios

16:9 is standard for YouTube and most video; 9:16 for Reels, TikTok and Shorts; 1:1 for square feed posts; 4:5 for portrait Instagram. Keeping the ratio when you resize stops images and video from stretching or squashing. Frequently asked questions

How do I keep an image's aspect ratio?

Scale width and height by the same factor. Enter your original dimensions and the new width here, and it calculates the height that preserves the ratio.

What aspect ratio should I use for video?

16:9 for YouTube and landscape video, 9:16 for vertical (Reels, TikTok, Shorts), 1:1 for square posts. Match the platform you're publishing to.

What does a ratio like 16:9 mean?

For every 16 units of width there are 9 of height. It's the simplified form of your dimensions — this tool calculates it for you.
