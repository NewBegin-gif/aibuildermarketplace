How to add your signature

Copy the generated signature, then paste it into your email client's signature settings — Gmail (Settings → General → Signature), Outlook, or Apple Mail. Keep it simple: name, role, one or two contact links, and a single accent colour. Avoid heavy images, which can get blocked or stripped. A clean text-and-link signature looks professional and renders reliably everywhere.

Will this signature work in Gmail and Outlook?
