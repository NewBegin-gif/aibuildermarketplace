What's a good engagement rate?

It's (likes + comments) ÷ followers, as a percentage. Rates fall as accounts grow, so context matters: 1–3% is solid for a mid-sized account, 3–6% is strong, and above 6% is excellent. Smaller accounts often see higher rates. Use it to judge which content resonates and to show brands real influence — a small, engaged audience often beats a large, passive one.

Frequently asked questions

How is engagement rate calculated?

Add up likes and comments on a post (or average across posts), divide by your follower count, and multiply by 100. This tool does it instantly.

What's a good engagement rate?

Roughly 1–3% is average, 3–6% is strong, and above 6% is excellent. Rates naturally drop as follower counts rise, so compare like-for-like.
