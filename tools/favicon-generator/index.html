How to add a favicon to your site

Download the PNG, upload it to your site's root (or assets folder), and add a line to your HTML <head>: <link rel="icon" href="/favicon.png">. Browsers show it in the tab, bookmarks and history. A 180px version doubles as an Apple touch icon. Keep it simple and high-contrast — at 16px, detail disappears, so a single bold letter reads best.

Frequently asked questions

What size should a favicon be?

Provide at least a 32×32px icon for the browser tab and a 180×180px version for Apple touch icons. This tool exports both sizes.

How do I install the favicon?

Upload the PNG to your site and add <link rel="icon" href="/favicon.png"> to your HTML head. Most site builders also have an upload field for it.
