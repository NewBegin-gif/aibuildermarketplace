What every invoice needs

A valid invoice has: your business name and contact, the client's details, a unique invoice number, the date, an itemised list of what you're charging for (description, quantity, price), any tax, and the total due. Add payment terms (e.g. 'due within 14 days') and how to pay. Keep your invoice numbers sequential — it makes bookkeeping and tax time far easier.

Is a PDF invoice legally valid?

Yes, a PDF invoice with your details, the client's, a number, date, itemised charges and total is perfectly valid in most countries. Check local tax rules for what else (like a VAT/tax number) you must include.
