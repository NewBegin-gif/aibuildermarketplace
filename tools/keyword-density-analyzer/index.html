Keyword density isn't a target

There's no magic percentage, and stuffing a keyword to hit one will hurt you. Use this to sanity-check: does your main term appear naturally, and are related terms present? Modern SEO rewards covering a topic thoroughly and readably, not repeating one phrase. If a word is wildly over-represented, that's your cue to vary the language.

Frequently asked questions

What is keyword density?

The percentage of times a word appears relative to the total word count. It's a rough indicator of what your content emphasises.

What's the ideal keyword density?

There isn't one. Aim for natural usage where your topic and related terms appear without forcing a number. Over-optimising looks spammy to Google.

How do I actually optimise content?

Cover the topic comprehensively and readably. Tools like Frase compare your draft to top-ranking pages and suggest what to add.
