Why processing fees matter

A 2.9% + fixed fee sounds small until you run volume or thin margins. On a 10 sale, a 0.30 fixed fee alone is 3% before the percentage. Factor fees into your pricing, and for international payments watch currency-conversion markups, which often cost more than the headline rate. Always check what lands in your account, not the sticker price.

Frequently asked questions

What are typical payment processing fees?

Around 2.9% + a fixed fee per transaction for cards (Stripe, Square), and ~3.49% + fixed for PayPal. International and currency conversion add more.

How do I reduce payment fees?

Negotiate rates at volume, pass small fixed fees into pricing, and for cross-border payments use a service like Payoneer with lower conversion costs.

Does this include currency conversion?

No — it calculates the processor's percentage and fixed fee. Conversion markups on international payments are extra, and often the bigger cost.
