1Password vs NordPass: what you pay for, seat by seat

Your staff pass logins around in chat, and nobody can tell which passwords are weak or leaked. You want shared vaults with an admin in control, but every plan has a seat rule, and the cheap one may not fit your team size. You are comparing 1Password and NordPass because one is the polished favourite and the other looks cheaper per seat.

Both tools are affiliate partners of ours: we may earn a commission if you sign up through a button on this page, at no extra cost to you. That is why we also say when to pick neither.

Short answerFor a company of five or more watching the budget, NordPass is cheaper per seat: Business is €3.99 per user a month billed yearly, excluding VAT (read 14 Oct 2026). 1Password costs more (Business $8.99 per user a month billed annually, read 1 Oct 2026), but it is the more polished pick, and its Teams Starter Pack covers up to 10 people for a flat $24.95 a month. If you need Entra ID or Okta sign-in, NordPass only offers it on Enterprise at €5.99 per user. For one person who wants something free, NordPass has a Free plan and 1Password has none.

Which one fits you

Pick 1Password if

  • You are a team of 10 to 20. The Teams Starter Pack is $24.95 a month flat for 10 members, plus up to 10 more seats at $4.99 each, billed annually (read 1 Oct 2026). Twelve people pay $24.95 + 2 × $4.99 = $34.93 a month.
  • Your staff also want vaults at home. Every 1Password Business user gets a free Families plan.
  • You want the most polished app and admin controls and accept paying for them. With 1Password the extra money buys the experience, the integrations and the business controls, not raw features.

Pick NordPass if

  • You want a free plan for yourself. NordPass Free stores and autofills passwords, though not across multiple devices. 1Password has no free tier.
  • You have 5 or more staff and want breach monitoring cheaply. NordPass Business is €3.99 per user a month billed yearly, excluding VAT, with group and folder sharing and data breach monitoring (read 14 Oct 2026).
  • You want a cheap first year of Premium on all your devices. Premium is €1.99 a month on the 1-year plan, €23.88 for the first 12 months, and the page shows a higher regular price for renewal.

When to pick neither

You are a team of two or three and do not want to pay for empty seats. NordPass sells Teams only as a 10-user pack and Business needs 5 users, and 1Password's cheapest team option is a flat fee for 10 members.

What it costs at a real size

A team of 12 on yearly billing. 1Password Teams Starter Pack: $24.95 + 2 × $4.99 = $34.93 a month. 1Password Business: 12 × $8.99 = $107.88 a month (both read 1 Oct 2026). Questions buyers ask

Is 1Password $2.99 or $3.99 a month?

Both appear. $3.99 billed annually is the Individual list price; $2.99 is a 25% discount for the first 12 months, for new customers only. Month to month it is $4.99 (read 1 Oct 2026).

Which NordPass plan supports Okta or Entra ID sign-in?

Only Enterprise, at €5.99 per user a month billed yearly, excluding VAT (read 14 Oct 2026). Teams and Business list SSO with Google Workspace only. We did not compare 1Password's SSO options.

Can I get my money back?

NordPass personal plans carry a 30-day money-back guarantee, and NordPass Business has a 14-day guarantee on the first purchase only. 1Password offers a 14-day free trial; our reading does not cover a refund policy.

What happens if I lose my 1Password password?

1Password keeps no record of your Secret Key and cannot recover it, so save your Emergency Kit before you store anything important. No plan changes that.
