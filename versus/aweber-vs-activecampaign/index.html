AWeber vs ActiveCampaign: do you need automation, or just a newsletter?

You want email to bring in customers, but a paid tool is hard to justify before you know how much automation you will use. If you are comparing these two, you are probably a small business or creator choosing between plain newsletters and emails that react to what subscribers do. Both bills climb as your list grows, so the choice also decides what you pay at 10,000 contacts.

Both tools are affiliate partners of ours: we may earn a commission if you sign up through a button on this page, at no extra cost to you. That is why we also say when to pick neither.

Short answerAWeber is the pick if you mainly send newsletters and want to start free: its free plan covers up to 500 subscribers and 3,000 emails a month. ActiveCampaign is the pick if behaviour-driven automation is why you are buying, since AWeber's comparison table excludes behavioural automation and webpage tracking from Lite. ActiveCampaign has no free plan and costs more as you grow: at 10,000 contacts Plus is $189 a month billed yearly (read 9 Oct 2026). The deciding factor is whether you will build automations or just send.

Which one fits you

Pick AWeber if

  • You are just starting: AWeber's free plan covers up to 500 subscribers and 3,000 emails a month, through its separate free signup page.
  • You only send newsletters: our ActiveCampaign dossier points newsletter-only senders to AWeber, since ActiveCampaign has no free plan and Starter climbs from $15 to $149 a month between 1,000 and 10,000 contacts.
  • Your list is mid-size and price matters: at up to 10,000 subscribers AWeber Plus is $112.50 a month billed yearly, against $189 for ActiveCampaign Plus at 10,000 contacts (both read 9 Oct 2026).

When to pick neither

Your list is large and mostly inactive, or growing faster than your budget. Both bills track list size, and buyers report that on ActiveCampaign accounts opened since 3 Nov 2025, unsubscribed and bounced contacts count toward the bill too.

Questions buyers ask

Does AWeber still have a free plan?

Yes, but through its separate free signup page, not the pricing page: up to 500 subscribers and 3,000 emails a month. Both limits were still there on 1 Oct 2026.

Does ActiveCampaign bill for contacts I cannot email?

SendX and buyer reports say that on accounts created on or after 3 November 2025, unsubscribed, unconfirmed and bounced contacts count toward the billed limit. The pricing page does not state it, so check your own invoice.

What happens when my ActiveCampaign list crosses a tier?

Reviewers say crossing a contact tier re-prices the whole plan, not just the extra contacts. Ask about it before you import a list.

What did our own tests show?

AWeber (29 Oct 2026): the free account opened, but our made-up example.com addresses were rejected on import, and the Lite trial checkout asked for a card first. ActiveCampaign (30 Sep 2026): the trial AI built a clean welcome automation, but image generation returned nothing in eight tries. We tested real sends on neither.
