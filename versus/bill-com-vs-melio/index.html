Bill.com vs Melio: a finance-team AP platform, or bill pay that starts free

Bills wait in someone's inbox for sign-off and suppliers get paid late. Or you only pay a few suppliers a month and do not want a subscription for it. If you are comparing these two, you are probably a small business owner or a finance lead working out how much accounts-payable software you really need. One charges per seat plus per payment. The other starts free.

Both tools are affiliate partners of ours: we may earn a commission if you sign up through a button on this page, at no extra cost to you. That is why we also say when to pick neither.

Short answerMelio wins for small businesses paying a handful of bills: the Go plan is free for one user, with 5 ACH payments a month and $0.50 for each one after (read 7 Oct 2026). Bill.com wins for finance teams with approval chains and real payment volume: Team is $65 per user a month with automatic 2-way sync to QuickBooks Online and Xero, plus fees such as $0.59 per ACH payment (read 30 Oct 2026). The deciding factor is seats times volume. Bill.com's cost climbs with every user and every payment, so it pays off only when the workflow is big enough to need it.

Which one fits you

Pick Bill.com if

  • Bills pass through several approvers: BILL's accounts-payable page says you can add as many rules, roles, steps and approval processes as you need. It does not say which plan includes custom workflows, so check before you buy Essentials.
  • You want two-way sync with QuickBooks Online, Pro, Premier or Xero: that starts on Team at $65 per user a month. Essentials only offers manual CSV import and export.
  • Most of your users only approve bills: Corporate at $89 per user adds lower-cost approver-only seats and procurement, though the pricing page gives no price for those seats.

When to pick neither

Your accounting software already has bill pay built in and you pay only a few bills a month. Bill.com's seat price is overkill at that volume, and Melio's own dossier says a second tool adds little.

The bill-pay feature inside the accounting software you already run covers that need without another subscription.

What it costs at a real size

Three people paying 30 suppliers a month by ACH, monthly billing. Bill.com Essentials: 3 × $49 = $147, plus 30 × $0.59 = $17.70, so $164.70 a month. Team, the first plan with automatic sync: 3 × $65 = $195 + $17.70 = $212.70 a month (read 30 Oct 2026). Melio Core: $25 + 2 × $10 = $45, with 20 ACH free and 10 more at $0.50: $45 + $5 = $50 a month. Boost: $55 + 2 × $10 = $75 a month, with 50 ACH free (read 7 Oct 2026).

Questions buyers ask

What does each payment cost?

Bill.com: $0.59 per ACH payment, $1.99 per mailed check, $19.99 per international USD wire and 2.9% when you pay by card (read 30 Oct 2026). Melio: ACH free up to your plan's allowance, then $0.50 each, $1.50 per paper check, $10 per wire and a flat 2.9% for card payments (read 7 Oct 2026).

What happens when Melio's free 90 days end?

If you have not subscribed, the account drops to the free Go plan, you lose the premium features, and data from those features is deleted. Note what you set up before day 90.

Do approvers need a paid seat?

On Bill.com every plan charges per user; Corporate adds lower-cost approver-only seats, but the page does not price them. On Melio, Unlimited includes unlimited users; whether an Approver or Viewer counts as a paid user on Core or Boost is not stated in its help centre.

Does either sync with my accounting software?

Bill.com syncs both ways with QuickBooks Online, Pro, Premier and Xero from the Team plan; NetSuite, Sage Intacct and others need Enterprise. On Melio's Go plan, Xero and QuickBooks Online sync is limited to the first 10 payments.
