Connecteam vs Deputy: which shift app costs less as your crew grows?

Shift changes go out by text, and at month end you cannot say who actually worked. If you are comparing these two, you probably run an hourly crew in hospitality, retail or field work and have just hired, or are about to hire, past ten people. The fear is that every new person turns into another seat on the bill.

Both tools are affiliate partners of ours: we may earn a commission if you sign up through a button on this page, at no extra cost to you. That is why we also say when to pick neither.

Short answerFor most small and mid-size shift teams Connecteam is cheaper: free for up to 10 users, then a flat $29 a month per hub for the first 30 users on yearly billing (read 22 Oct 2026). Deputy charges per user, from $5 a month on yearly Lite (read 19 Sep 2026), and earns that when you want auto-scheduling, demand forecasting and labour budgets, which sit in Core. The deciding factor is how many Connecteam hubs you need, because they are sold separately and stack.

Which one fits you

Pick Connecteam if

  • Your crew is 10 people or fewer: the Small Business Plan is free for life with all hubs, and in our 29 Oct 2026 test no card was asked.
  • You have 11 to 30 people and one hub is enough: Operations Basic is a flat $29 a month billed yearly, against 30 × $5 = $150 on Deputy Lite.
  • You can live with the essentials: the free Limited plan covers up to 30 users per hub, with a single time clock and up to 5 custom jobs on Operations.

Pick Deputy if

  • You want auto-scheduling, demand forecasting and labour budgets: those sit in Deputy Core at $6.50 per user a month billed yearly.
  • You would add Messaging+ and Analytics+: Core with both is $6.50 + $1.95 + $1.50 = $9.95 per user, more than Pro at $9, which includes them. Pro needs a sales call.
  • You want payroll in the same system in the US: Deputy Payroll by Paycor is $8 per user plus $49 a month on yearly Core or Pro, while Connecteam's free plan excludes payroll integration.

When to pick neither

Your whole team is salaried and works at desks with email and Slack. Connecteam is built for the frontline, and salaried staff rarely need Deputy's shift-level clocking.

A general project management tool fits an office team better than either shift app.

What it costs at a real size

100 shift staff on yearly billing, one hub. Connecteam Operations Basic: $29 + 70 × $0.80 = $29 + $56 = $85 a month (read 22 Oct 2026). Deputy Lite: 100 × $5 = $500 a month (read 19 Sep 2026). Add Connecteam's Communications hub on Basic and it becomes $85 + $29 + 70 × $0.50 = $85 + $29 + $35 = $149 a month, still below Deputy Lite.

Questions buyers ask

What happens at Connecteam's eleventh user?

You pick a paid plan per hub, or move to the free Limited plan for up to 30 users on each hub, which keeps only the essentials (pricing FAQ, read 22 Oct 2026).

Why can Deputy cost more than the list price for a tiny team?

Monthly plans carry a $30 minimum per invoice. Three users on monthly Lite would be 3 × $5.50 = $16.50, but you pay $30. Yearly billing has no such floor.

Can I get money back if it does not work out?

Connecteam refunds a yearly plan pro rata only within its first 30 days; monthly plans are not refunded. Deputy's billing FAQ says you may cancel a yearly plan early with no refund, and you cannot lower the number of employees during the term.

What did our own tests show?

Connecteam (29 Oct 2026): signup landed on the free plan but required a mobile number before the dashboard. Deputy (24 Oct 2026): the bulk import worked, but the base pay rates we pasted in came through empty. We did not test scheduling or the time clock on either.
