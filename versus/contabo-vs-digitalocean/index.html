Contabo vs DigitalOcean: more server per euro, or pay only for what you use

You need a Linux server for an app or an API, you can run it yourself, and you do not want to overpay for memory. You also do not want to be stuck paying for a server you stopped needing. If you are comparing these two, you are probably a developer or a small team on a budget. Contabo sells big specs cheap if you commit for two years. DigitalOcean sells smaller machines by the second.

Both tools are affiliate partners of ours: we may earn a commission if you sign up through a button on this page, at no extra cost to you. That is why we also say when to pick neither. Disclosure: we earn commissions when you shop through the links below.

Short answerContabo wins on hardware per euro: Cloud VPS 4 gives 4 vCPU and 8 GB of RAM for €4.40 a month, but that is the 24-month rate, excluding VAT (read 8 Oct 2026). DigitalOcean wins on flexibility: Droplets start at $4 a month for 512 MiB, billed per second with no term, with managed databases and app hosting next to them (read 3 Oct 2026). If the server will run for years and you can prepay, take Contabo. If the project may last months, or you want managed parts around the server, take DigitalOcean.

Which one fits you

Pick Contabo if

  • You want a lot of memory for little money and will keep the server: Cloud VPS 4 has 8 GB of RAM for €4.40 a month on 24 months, while DigitalOcean's $4 Droplet has 512 MiB.
  • Your app moves a lot of data: every Cloud VPS has unlimited traffic under fair use. The $4 Droplet includes 500 GiB of outbound transfer and charges $0.01 per GiB beyond that.
  • You expect to grow on the same machine: Contabo's VPS FAQ says RAM, vCores and storage scale within the Core line without rebuilding the server.

Pick DigitalOcean if

When to pick neither

You want someone else to look after the server and the software on it. Contabo leaves software upkeep and backups to you, and a Droplet is a server you install, secure and patch yourself.

Look at Cloudways instead. DigitalOcean owns Cloudways, which runs and patches the server for you on top of the same cloud. Read our Cloudways review.

What it costs at a real size

A small production server with backups, excluding VAT and overage. Contabo Cloud VPS 12 (12 vCPU, 48 GB RAM) on the monthly term: €25.00 + €6.70 Auto Backup = €31.70 a month. On the 24-month term: €20.00 + €6.70 = €26.70 a month, with 24 × €20.00 = €480.00 due at checkout (read 8 Oct 2026). DigitalOcean: a 2 GiB Droplet at $12 + daily backups at 30% ($3.60) + the smallest managed database at $15 = $30.60 a month (read 3 Oct 2026). These are different currencies and very different machines, so compare what your app needs, not the totals.

Questions buyers ask

Is Contabo's €4.40 really the monthly price?

It is the effective monthly rate on a 24-month term, labelled 'for the first 24 months' when we re-read it on 24 Oct 2026. The page does not say what the server costs from month 25. On Cloud VPS 12 the 24-month term was prepaid at €480.00.

Can I get a refund?

DigitalOcean's billing FAQ says it does not offer refunds. Contabo refunds only in set cases: a 14-day revocation for private accounts, 72 hours after an automatic renewal (not for customers in Germany), an accidental order cancelled straight away, or an account balance.

Are backups included?

Not on either. Contabo's Auto Backup was €6.70 a month on Cloud VPS 12. DigitalOcean backups cost 20% of the Droplet price for weekly or 30% for daily.

Can my app send email straight from the server?

Not from a Droplet: DigitalOcean blocks ports 25, 465 and 587 and points you to an email delivery service. Our Contabo dossier does not cover mail ports, so we did not compare this.
