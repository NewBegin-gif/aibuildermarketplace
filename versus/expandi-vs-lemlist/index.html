Expandi vs lemlist: should LinkedIn or cold email carry your outbound?

Your team sends connection requests and follow-ups by hand, and it eats the week. If you are comparing these two, you probably run outbound for a sales team or an agency and are deciding whether LinkedIn or cold email should carry it. You also worry about a bill that grows with every rep, and about LinkedIn restricting a profile your pipeline depends on.

Both tools are affiliate partners of ours: we may earn a commission if you sign up through a button on this page, at no extra cost to you. That is why we also say when to pick neither.

Short answerIf cold email is your main channel, lemlist is the cheaper start: Email is $69 a month, or $55 billed yearly, with unlimited users (read 5 Oct 2026). If LinkedIn is your main channel, Expandi is the specialist at $99 per seat a month, or $79 billed yearly (read 7 Oct 2026), and buyers say lemlist's LinkedIn side is thinner and its Chrome extension unstable. lemlist Multichannel adds LinkedIn, SMS, WhatsApp and calls to email at $109 per user ($87 yearly). The deciding factor is which channel your pipeline really comes from.

Which one fits you

Pick Expandi if

  • LinkedIn is where your pipeline comes from: Expandi is the LinkedIn-automation specialist, with a gradual warm-up that starts a new account at 5 actions a day and tops out at 21.
  • You run many client profiles: Agency pricing is quote-only for 10+ seats, and Expandi says the discount grows with the number of seats you buy.

When to pick neither

You send an occasional campaign or a few connection requests now and then. Neither has a free plan, lemlist's entry plan only pays off with steady volume, and for occasional connection requests LinkedIn's own tools are enough.

Look at Apollo instead. Our lemlist dossier names Apollo for teams that only send a one-off campaign now and then. Read our Apollo review.

What it costs at a real size

A team of three reps on yearly billing. lemlist Email: $55 a month, with unlimited users on that plan. lemlist Multichannel: 3 × $87 = $261 a month. Expandi: 3 × $79 = $237 a month, one LinkedIn account per seat, with taxes added by country. If you need contact data, lemlist's Lead data enrichment adds €16 a month on yearly billing for 2,000 credits; that add-on was priced in euros on the page we read. Expandi says its discount grows with seat count, so ask for a quote before you trust the $237.

Questions buyers ask

Will a second Expandi seat let me send more connection requests?

No. Warm-up tops out at 21 actions a day, and Expandi's own article notes LinkedIn enforces a weekly cap of 100 connection requests. A second seat buys a second account, not a faster one.

Does lemlist include contact data?

No. Lead data enrichment is a separate add-on at €20 a month, or €16 yearly, for 2,000 credits, about 400 emails or 100 phone numbers (read 5 Oct 2026).

Does cancelling stop my campaigns?

On lemlist, no: cancelling only stops renewal, running sequences keep sending until you stop them, and the enrichment add-on must be cancelled separately. On Expandi, a cancelled subscription cannot be reactivated, so export your leads and campaigns first.

Is Expandi's yearly deal really two months free?

The page says so, but $1,188 minus $948 is $240, which is closer to two and a half months of the $99 fee.
