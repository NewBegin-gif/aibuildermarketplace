Gusto vs Rippling: price your payroll today, or buy one employee record

You run a growing US team, and payroll, benefits and tax filings live in separate tools that eat hours every month. Or every new hire means setting up payroll, a laptop and app access by hand, and every leaver means undoing it all. You are comparing Gusto and Rippling because you need to know if payroll alone fixes this, or if you need the whole stack on one record.

Both tools are affiliate partners of ours: we may earn a commission if you sign up through a button on this page, at no extra cost to you. That is why we also say when to pick neither.

Short answerFor a US team that mainly needs payroll, benefits and tax filing, Gusto wins: its prices are on the page and you can cost it in minutes (Simple $49 a month plus $6 per person, read 16 Oct 2026). Rippling wins when the pain is onboarding and offboarding across HR, IT and Finance, because one employee record drives payroll, laptops and app access together. The deciding factor is how many systems you will move onto Rippling: run one module and you pay platform prices for a point tool. Rippling publishes only a floor ($8 per employee a month plus a $40 base fee, read 16 Oct 2026), so expect a sales call before you see a real number.

Which one fits you

Pick Gusto if

  • You only need US payroll done right and want a price today. Simple is $49 a month plus $6 per person (read 16 Oct 2026), with no contract and free setup.
  • Your staff work in several states. Plus adds multi-state payroll, next-day pay and time tracking for $80 a month plus $12 per person (read 16 Oct 2026).
  • You pay US contractors only some months. Gusto's pricing FAQ says they are billed at the per-person rate only in months you pay them.

Pick Rippling if

  • Hiring and leaving is your real time sink. On Rippling one hire can trigger payroll, a shipped and set-up laptop and app access, and offboarding reverses it. Capterra reviewers (Aug 2026) say this is where it earns its keep.
  • You plan to move several systems onto it, not one. Rippling sells HR, IT (devices and app access) and Finance (corporate cards and spend) as separate modules on one record, and the automation only pays off across several.
  • You are a US VC-backed startup. Eligible startups can get 6 months of free access through Rippling's startup program, depending on funding status and stage.

When to pick neither

Most of your team lives outside the US and you want a published price before talking to anyone. Gusto sends new international hires to Remote's employer-of-record service on a separate contract, and Rippling prices through sales.

Gusto's own FAQ advises teams that are mostly abroad to compare employer-of-record services directly.

What it costs at a real size

A US team of 20, on our 16 Oct 2026 reading. Gusto Simple: $49 + 20 × $6 = $169 a month. Plus: $80 + 20 × $12 = $320. Premium: $180 + 20 × $22 = $620. Rippling's published floor for the same 20 people is $40 + 20 × $8 = $200 a month, but that is only the HR-and-payroll entry and a floor, not a quote. Rippling does not state whether the $40 base applies to every module and company size, and IT and Finance modules are quoted on top.

Questions buyers ask

Which is cheaper for a small US team?

We can only fully price one side. Gusto Simple for 10 people is $49 + 10 × $6 = $109 a month (read 16 Oct 2026). Rippling's floor for 10 is $40 + 10 × $8 = $120, but its real price comes from a quote, so we did not compare final bills.

Can either pay staff outside the US?

Gusto runs US payroll only. Since 1 May 2026 it refers new international hires to Remote's employer-of-record service, from about $599 per employee a month on a separate contract. Rippling has a global payroll product, but it is priced by quote and we did not compare it.

Can I try Rippling before buying?

Not by yourself. Every route ends at 'Get a free quote' or 'Contact Sales', so your first look is a sales-led demo. Gusto has free setup and no contract, but our reading of its page mentions no trial.

Are Rippling's new AI tools ready to use?

Partly. On rippling.com (read 22 Sep 2026), Helpdesk is a shipped product, while most of AI Governance shows a waitlist or 'Talk to sales'. Only the AI Spend Console invites you to try it. We have not tested any of it.
