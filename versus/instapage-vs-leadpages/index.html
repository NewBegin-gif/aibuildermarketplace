Instapage vs Leadpages: which landing page tool fits your ad spend?

Your ads send clicks to a page that does not convert, and every wasted click is money gone. Or your ad traffic swings from month to month, and a visitor cap keeps pushing you up a plan. You are probably a marketer or a small business owner running paid ads, and you want to know whether you need a premium optimisation tool or a simpler page builder.

Both tools are affiliate partners of ours: we may earn a commission if you sign up through a button on this page, at no extra cost to you. That is why we also say when to pick neither.

Short answerFor a small business running a few ad-driven pages, Leadpages is the better buy: Pro is $23 a month billed annually ($29 monthly) and no Leadpages plan caps traffic (read 15 Oct 2026). For teams spending heavily on ads who want each ad to land on a matched page, Instapage goes further, and the Leadpages dossier itself says so; Create is $79 a month billed annually for 15,000 visitors (read 24 Sep 2026). The deciding factor is the size of your ad budget. We have not tested either tool ourselves.

Which one fits you

Pick Instapage if

  • You spend big on ads and want each ad to land on a page matched to it: Instapage offers AdMap ad-to-page personalisation, which our Leadpages dossier says goes further than Leadpages.
  • You get 30,000 visitors a month and want server-side A/B testing: Optimize is $159 a month billed annually, $199 monthly (read 24 Sep 2026), and adds it on top of Create.

When to pick neither

You need a landing page live without a card or a monthly bill. Neither has a free plan, and both trials ask for a card and roll into a paid plan.

If you cannot commit to paying from the first published page, a page on the website you already run is the cheaper route.

What it costs at a real size

One year on annual billing for a small advertiser under 15,000 visitors a month. Instapage Create: $79 x 12 = $948. Leadpages Pro: $23 x 12 = $276. If you want A/B testing, Instapage Optimize is $159 x 12 = $1,908 for 30,000 visitors, while Leadpages Grow is $79 x 12 = $948 with a manual two-way split, and Leadpages Optimize is also $159 x 12 = $1,908, with up to four variants, Smart Traffic, heatmaps and no traffic cap.

Questions buyers ask

Can I trial either one without being charged?

Both trials need a card. Instapage's 14-day trial ends when the 14 days run out or when you pass 2,500 unique monthly visitors, and then charges the plan you picked; you cancel under Account Management, Subscriptions, Cancel. Leadpages' 7-day trial converts to a paid plan unless you cancel, and reviewers report no refunds or proration after that, so decide before day seven.

Can I publish on my own domain?

On Instapage, Create and Optimize publish to subdomains such as go.yoursite.com through a CNAME: 2 on Create and the 30,000-visitor Optimize, 10 at 50,000. Your root domain is only on the quote-only Convert, and Instapage locks a domain to the first account that uses it. On Leadpages, Pro includes 1 custom domain, Business 2, Grow 4 and Optimize 6.

Are the prices the same in Europe?

Not for Leadpages. From a European connection on 15 October 2026 its page showed Pro at 29 euros and Business at 49 euros, but Grow at 115 euros and Optimize at 229 euros a month, against $99 and $199. Instapage quoted every amount in USD when we read it from the Netherlands.
