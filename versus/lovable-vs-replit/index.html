Lovable vs Replit: which AI builder fits an app you need this week?

You have an app idea that needs a database, you have no developer, and you want it online this week without setting anything up. Both tools promise that, and both bill the AI work by how much effort a request turns out to take, so you cannot tell in advance what a month will cost. You are probably a founder, a marketer or someone learning to build, and you want the one that gets a working app out with the fewest surprises.

Both tools are affiliate partners of ours: we may earn a commission if you sign up through a button on this page, at no extra cost to you. That is why we also say when to pick neither.

Short answerIf you want to describe an app and have it published the same day without touching code, pick Lovable. In our test of 26 September 2026 its first version was the only one of three builders that held an eight-spot limit when ten people booked at once, and it used 4 credits to build and publish. Pick Replit if you want a browser coding workspace where you read and change the code yourself: its Agent built a PostgreSQL API and web app in 8 minutes, but needed a repair prompt before it stopped overbooking. Neither gives you a fixed price for AI work, so set a budget on either.

Which one fits you

Pick Lovable if

  • You have no developer and need the app live this week: in our test (26 Sep 2026) Lovable built and published a booking app in about ten minutes and used 4 of 10 credits on a new account.
  • The app has a rule that must hold under pressure: Lovable's first version held an eight-spot limit when ten people booked at the same moment, with no repair round.
  • You want your own domain and no badge for a small fee: Pro at 25 euros a month for 100 credits (read 26 Sep 2026) adds a custom domain and removes the Lovable badge.

When to pick neither

Your team needs a predictable monthly bill for AI work and wants its developers to own and review every line. Both price AI requests by the work involved, and Lovable keeps the database key and admin password inside Lovable.

Our Replit dossier notes that a conventional code editor plus your own hosting removes the usage meter entirely, at the cost of doing the setup yourself.

What it costs at a real size

One builder for a year on the cheapest paid plan. Lovable Pro is 25 euros a month incl. VAT, and yearly billing is advertised as two months free: 25 x 10 = 250 euros a year. Replit Core is $18 a month billed annually: 18 x 12 = $216 a year, or 20 x 12 = $240 paid monthly. What that buys differs. Lovable's 100 monthly credits cover about 100 / 2.00 = 50 landing pages with images, or 100 / 0.50 = 200 styling changes. Replit has no fixed price per paid request, so its monthly credit cannot be turned into a number of tasks in advance. The currencies differ: Lovable was read in euros incl. VAT, Replit in dollars.

Questions buyers ask

Which one built the better app in your test?

On 26 September 2026 we gave both the same booking-app prompt on their free plans and checked the results against 18 tests. Lovable's first version held the limit when ten people booked an eight-spot class at once. Replit accepted all ten; after we described the problem, its Agent added a database lock in two minutes and the result was exactly eight. It was one run on one day, not a security audit.

Can I take my app with me?

The code, yes; the running app, not easily. Lovable created a GitHub repository with the full source and no secret keys, and it built on Node 22, but loaded no classes because the database key and admin password stay in Lovable. Replit's zip export built and ran on our own database, but the export and its Git history carried the generated admin password in plain text.

What happens when I run out of credits?

On Lovable the published pages stay up, but AI features stop and a backend on Lovable Cloud can pause once the workspace has no credits left at all (documentation read 27 Sep 2026). On Replit, paid requests need your approval, and you can set a budget and usage alerts under Account, Billing. On Replit's free Starter plan the usage page is closed, and in our test the daily allowance was gone after one build and one repair.

Do I pay in euros or dollars?

We read Lovable's pricing page from a European connection, where it shows euros including VAT. Replit's pricing page was read in US dollars. We did not compare both in one currency.
