Printify vs Tapstitch: more margin per sale, or a better garment?

You have designs and a store idea, but you cannot buy stock for designs that may never sell. If you are weighing these two, you are probably deciding whether your shop competes on price or on how the garment feels in the hand. Cheap blanks can feel thin, and buyers name faded or off-centre prints from some providers.

Both tools are affiliate partners of ours: we may earn a commission if you sign up through a button on this page, at no extra cost to you. That is why we also say when to pick neither.

Short answerPrintify wins on margin and range, Tapstitch on the garment. Lower product and shipping costs give Printify more profit per sale, and its free plan costs nothing until you sell. Tapstitch makes sense when your brand lives on heavyweight pieces, such as 345 to 430 gsm hoodies, and you accept a thinner margin for them. The deciding factor is what your customers pay you for: a low price or the fabric.

Which one fits you

Pick Printify if

  • You are testing designs: the free plan is $0 a month and you pay only product cost plus shipping per order (read 1 Oct 2026).
  • You ship enough items for Premium to pay off: at about $8.77 of cost per item, the $39 monthly fee breaks even between 14 and 23 items a month, or 9 to 15 on the $24.99 yearly rate.
  • You compete on price or product range: our notes on both tools say Printify costs less per sale and has a far bigger catalogue.

When to pick neither

Your samples from both fail your quality bar: Printify's print quality varies by provider, with faded colours and off-centre prints named on Trustpilot, and Tapstitch buyers report sizes running about one smaller.

Order samples from another print-on-demand network before you commit; our Printify research lists Printful and Gelato among the networks to compare.

What it costs at a real size

Printify Premium at the $8.77 item cost the pricing page's calculator uses: a 33% discount saves $8.77 × 0.33 = $2.89 an item, so $39 ÷ $2.89 = 13.5, about 14 items a month to break even. At the 20% line it saves $1.75 an item, so $39 ÷ $1.75 = 22.3, rounded up to 23 items. On the $24.99 yearly rate that is 9 and 15 items. Tapstitch has no fee to break even on: 20 heavyweight hoodies at the $14.92 from-price are 20 × $14.92 = $298.40 before your print and shipping, with about 5% off at that quantity.

Questions buyers ask

Which one makes more profit per sale?

Printify. Both dossiers agree that its lower product and shipping costs mean more profit per sale. Tapstitch trades some of that margin for heavier blanks.

Does Printify Premium improve print quality?

No. Print and shipping vary by the provider you pick, not by your plan. Order samples from that specific provider before you think about Premium.

How long does a Tapstitch order take?

Its shipping policy puts print-on-demand production at 1 to 6 working days and international delivery at 5 to 15 working days. New stores default to International Fulfillment, which is cheaper but slower, until you change it under Order Settings.

Can I return Tapstitch items I no longer want?

Not if they were printed correctly. The Returns Policy (read 5 Nov 2026) excludes items made to your design that you later do not want, and samples. Return shipping is on you.

What did we see when we tried Printify?

On a free account on 30 Oct 2026 no card was asked, and catalog prices showed two figures, such as 'From USD 6.40' and 'From USD 4.71 with Printify Premium'. We did not connect a store or order a sample.
