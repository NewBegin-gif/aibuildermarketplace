Proton Drive vs pCloud: privacy by default, or big storage paid once

You send contracts and client files that nobody else should read, or you are tired of a storage bill that comes back every year. If you are comparing these two, you are probably a freelancer, a couple or a family that cares about privacy and about the total cost. Proton sells encrypted storage as a subscription. pCloud sells large storage you pay for once.

Both tools are affiliate partners of ours: we may earn a commission if you sign up through a button on this page, at no extra cost to you. That is why we also say when to pick neither.

Short answerPick Proton Drive if privacy is why you are shopping: every plan is end-to-end encrypted, and Drive Plus is $3.99 a month for 200 GB, billed $47.88 a year (read 16 Oct 2026). Pick pCloud if you want terabytes for years and hate renewals: Premium Plus is 2 TB for €499 once (read 26 Oct 2026). The deciding factor is encryption. On pCloud's personal plans, encryption on your own device is a separate €150 one-time purchase, and pCloud may refuse refunds on discounted purchases, which is how its lifetime prices are shown.

Which one fits you

Pick Proton Drive if

  • You send client files that must stay private: every Proton plan is end-to-end encrypted. In our 9 Oct 2026 test four files came back byte for byte identical, and a public link opened for a visitor with no Proton account.
  • Two of you share one storage bill: Proton Duo is 2 TB for up to two users at $14.99 a month, billed $179.88 a year (read 16 Oct 2026). Two Unlimited accounts would cost $19.98 a month for 1 TB between you.
  • You want an easy way out: every Proton plan has a 30-day money-back guarantee.

When to pick neither

Your work depends on deep integrations, shared-drive collaboration and searching inside documents. Proton cannot search inside your files by design, and pCloud's shared folders only work between accounts in the same data region.

Our Proton dossier says mainstream cloud drives do integrations, collaboration and full-text search better.

What it costs at a real size

2 TB with encryption on your device, in euros. pCloud: Premium Plus €499 + Encryption add-on €150 = €649 once (read 26 Oct 2026). Proton's only 2 TB plan is Duo at €179.88 a year (read 10 Sep 2026 from the Netherlands). Three years of Duo: 3 × €179.88 = €539.64. Four years: 4 × €179.88 = €719.52. So pCloud pulls ahead in the fourth year and stays ahead after that, if Proton's price stays the same, and Duo shares its 2 TB between up to two users. Questions buyers ask

Is pCloud end-to-end encrypted like Proton Drive?

Only with the pCloud Encryption add-on, which is €150 once on personal plans (read 26 Oct 2026). Business plans include it. Proton encrypts end to end on every plan, the free one included.

How much free storage do I really get?

Proton says 5 GB. Our new account on 9 Oct 2026 started at 2 GB and reached 5 GB through a 3 GB welcome bonus. pCloud starts at 2 GB. In our 2 Nov 2026 test the first upload added 1 GB, and pCloud says you can raise the free account to 10 GB.

Can I get my money back?

Proton gives 30 days' money back on every plan. pCloud gives 14 days from payment, but its terms let it refuse refunds on promotional or discounted purchases, and the lifetime prices are shown as discounts.

What does lifetime mean at pCloud?

pCloud's terms define it as the account owner's lifetime or 99 years, whichever is shorter. You also cannot downgrade a one-time purchase, so buy the size you will need later.
