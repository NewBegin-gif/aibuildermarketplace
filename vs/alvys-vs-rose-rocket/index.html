The verdict

Pick Alvys if you’re FTL trucking carriers and asset-based (hybrid) brokers with roughly 5–300 trucks — especially compliance-heavy freight like hazmat, reefer and cross-border — plus shippers running private fleets, with real back-office staff to put dispatch, billing and DOT/IFTA compliance in one system. Walk away if you're a pure-play broker without assets, you run LTL/multi-modal loads billing multiple customers per load, you need rail, air or ocean tracking, or you're a single-truck operator without back-office staff — in that case the comparison above tells you where to look instead.
