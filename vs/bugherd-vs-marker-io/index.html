The verdict

Pick BugHerd if you’re web agencies and site teams that want clients to point-and-click feedback directly on the page — and, new in 2026, want AI agents (Claude Code, Cursor and others) to read, triage and action that feedback via BugHerd's MCP server, free in beta. Walk away if you don't work with external clients or website revisions (a plain issue tracker is enough), or you need deep dev-workflow tracking that belongs in Jira — in that case the comparison above tells you where to look instead.
