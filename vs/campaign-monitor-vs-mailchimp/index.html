There is no universal winner — it depends on the job. Mailchimp is the closest general peer, ActiveCampaign wins on automation depth — compare at YOUR list size, since contact pricing diverges fast

Is Campaign Monitor worth it in 2026?

Campaign Monitor is genuinely best for brands and agencies that want beautiful, reliable email campaigns with strong templates and client-friendly management, priced per contact. Skip it if you need deep behavioral automation and CRM-grade journeys (ActiveCampaign-class tools fit better), or your list is large but low-engagement — prune first.

What is the honest downside of Campaign Monitor?

You pay for contacts, not sends — so a large, quiet list costs the same as a large, active one, and dormant subscribers are literally a line item.
