Pick folk if you’re small sales teams, agencies and VC/partnership teams who want a clean, fast-to-set-up CRM with a great LinkedIn enrichment extension. Walk away if you're a solo founder who needs your network on your phone, or you need real workflow automation and reporting — folk is lightweight by design — in that case the comparison above tells you where to look instead.
