The verdict

Pick Oneflow if you’re sales and legal teams with real contract volume who want the full lifecycle — creating, negotiating, e-signing and archiving interactive contracts — in one platform with CRM integrations. Walk away if you just need signatures (a per-seat e-sign tool costs a fraction), or you cannot commit to annual billing at €250 a month — in that case the comparison above tells you where to look instead.

Questions people actually ask

Which is better, Oneflow or Signeasy?

There is no universal winner — it depends on the job. Signeasy is the lightweight per-seat e-signing route from about $10 a month; Oneflow is the full contract-lifecycle platform at €250/month annual — pick by whether you manage contracts or just sign them

Is Oneflow worth it in 2026?

Oneflow is genuinely best for sales and legal teams with real contract volume who want the full lifecycle — creating, negotiating, e-signing and archiving interactive contracts — in one platform with CRM integrations. Skip it if you just need signatures (a per-seat e-sign tool costs a fraction), or you cannot commit to annual billing at €250 a month.

What is the honest downside of Oneflow?

The entry is €250 a month on annual billing — €3,000 a year committed before you've sent your first contract — and the rest of the ladder lives in a sales conversation.
