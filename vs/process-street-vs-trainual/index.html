The verdict

Pick Process Street if you’re operations-driven teams that run recurring, checklist-based processes and want them tracked and auditable. Walk away if you just need to write down a few SOPs — a doc or a cheaper tool captures them without the platform cost — in that case the comparison above tells you where to look instead.
