The verdict

Pick Quo if you’re micro-teams of 2–5 people working mostly on Wi-Fi who want a clean, modern phone app built around a shared inbox, with AI call summaries (Sona). Walk away if you're scaling past ~10 users and need rock-solid call infrastructure, you make lots of calls off Wi-Fi, or you need video — Nextiva or RingCentral fit better — in that case the comparison above tells you where to look instead.
