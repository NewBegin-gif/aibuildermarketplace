The verdict

Pick Sendcloud if you’re European e-commerce stores that want easy multi-carrier shipping (DHL, PostNL, Colissimo, GLS, DPD), branded tracking and a returns portal live in under 30 minutes. Walk away if you ship mainly in the US, need inventory management and complex fulfilment, or you're a very high-volume shipper wanting deep customisation — ShipStation fits that better — in that case the comparison above tells you where to look instead.
