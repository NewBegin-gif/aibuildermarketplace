The verdict

Pick Serpstat if you’re solo SEOs and small in-house teams who want the whole workflow — keywords, rankings, audit, competitors — under one subscription at roughly a third of what the incumbents charge, and who will judge the data on their own market during the trial rather than on published database sizes. Walk away if your work depends on deep backlink analysis, since the 10,000 results per report ceiling does not move no matter which tier you buy; or you need more than three seats, because seats are not sold separately and the next tier roughly doubles the bill — in that case the comparison above tells you where to look instead.
