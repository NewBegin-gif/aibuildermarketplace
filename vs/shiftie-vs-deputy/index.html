Is Shiftie worth it in 2026?

Shiftie is genuinely best for shift-based businesses (hospitality, retail, leisure) that want quick rota building, clock-ins and leave management without spreadsheet pain. Skip it if you need full HR, payroll and performance management in one system, or your team doesn't work in shifts at all.

What is the honest downside of Shiftie?

It's focused, affordable rota software — which means it does scheduling, clock-ins, leave and swaps well, but it isn't a full HR or payroll platform, so a growing business will still bolt on payroll and HR tools around it.
