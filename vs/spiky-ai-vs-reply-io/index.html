The verdict

Pick Spiky.ai if you’re sales and customer-facing teams that already run most conversations on video and want the coaching layer — talk-time, keyword tracking and call review — without buying a full revenue-intelligence platform. Walk away if your team's calls are mostly ad hoc or on the phone rather than video, or the meeting volume per rep sits well above 30 a month, in which case only the top tier fits and the cheaper plans are a false economy — in that case the comparison above tells you where to look instead.
