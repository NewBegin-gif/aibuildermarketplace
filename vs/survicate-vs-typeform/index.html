The verdict

Pick Survicate if you’re product and CX teams running always-on feedback — NPS, CSAT and in-app surveys — piped into the CRM and analytics stack they already use. Walk away if you just need an occasional one-off questionnaire (a simpler form tool covers that) or your response volume is huge — response-metered pricing punishes scale — in that case the comparison above tells you where to look instead.
