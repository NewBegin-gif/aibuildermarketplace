The verdict

Pick Wegic if you’re non-technical founders who want an AI to build and host a simple site fast, and are comfortable with usage-based credit pricing. Walk away if you want predictable flat pricing, or you'll iterate heavily — credits make redesigns expensive, and real use needs a paid plan quickly — in that case the comparison above tells you where to look instead.

Wegic is genuinely best for non-technical founders who want an AI to build and host a simple site fast, and are comfortable with usage-based credit pricing.
